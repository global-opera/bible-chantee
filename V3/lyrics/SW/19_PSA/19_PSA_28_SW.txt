Verse 1:  
Ee Bwana, nitakuita,  
Uwe mwamba wangu,  
Usiwe kama kiziwi,  
Nisije nikashindwa.  
Nikipainulia mikono,  
Patakatifu pako,  
Nisamehe, nisaidie,  
Uisikie sauti yangu.

Chorus:  
Bwana, ni nguvu zangu,  
Moyo wangu umemtumaini,  
Nami nimesaidiwa,  
Ushangilie, ee roho yangu.  
Uwaokoe watu wako,  
Ubariki urithi wako,  
Chunga milele, ee Bwana,  
Uwe mwamba wangu.

Na kwa kadiri ya ubaya wao,  
Atawavunja, wala hatawajenga,  
Mimi nitakutukuza.
