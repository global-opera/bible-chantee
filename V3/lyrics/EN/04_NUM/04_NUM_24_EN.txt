[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the stillness of the evening,  
When the sun begins to fade,  
Balaam turned his heart to heaven,  
Seeking truth, not spells to trade.  
He beheld the tents of Jacob,  
Like gardens by the stream,  
The Spirit whispered softly,  
In a vision, he could dream.

(Chorus)  
Oh, how goodly are Your people,  
In the land where You have led,  
With a promise of Your blessing,  
In the wilderness, we tread.  
Pour Your waters from the heavens,  
Let Your kingdom be our song,  
In Your grace, we'll stand together,  
In Your love, we all belong.

(Verse 2)  
From the shadows of our battles,  
You have brought us from the night,  
With the strength of mighty lions,  
We will rise and shine Your light.  
Though the nations may be raging,  
And the foes may seek our fall,  
Blessed is he who blesses us,  
You are Lord of all.

In the hope of Your salvation,  
We find strength to take our flight.
