[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the plains of Moab, the children stand,  
A mighty nation, guided by Your hand.  
Balak trembles, fear grips his heart,  
For he knows, O Lord, You set them apart.

Chorus  
Blessed are Your people, O God most high,  
No curse can prevail, no darkness deny.  
In the face of giants, we'll lift up our song,  
For Your love is our fortress, where we belong.

Verse 2  
Balak sought Balaam, with silver and gold,  
"Curse this people," his anxious heart told.  
But Your voice spoke clearly, "They are blessed,  
No weapon formed against them shall ever rest."

Chorus  
Blessed are Your people, O God most high,  
No curse can prevail, no darkness deny.  
In the face of giants, we'll lift up our song,  
For Your love is our fortress, where we belong.

Bridge  
When the path seems twisted, and shadows arise,  
Open our eyes, Lord, to see through Your skies.  
Even through trials, Your promise stands true,  
In every battle, we trust and pursue.

Verse 3  
Balaam’s heart trembled, he saw the divine,  
The angel before him, with sword that would shine.  
“Speak only My words,” the Lord did declare,  
In faith and obedience, we’re held in Your care.

Chorus  
Blessed are Your people, O God most high,  
No curse can prevail, no darkness deny.  
In the face of giants, we'll lift up our song,  
For Your love is our fortress, where we belong.

Outro  
So we stand together, with hearts full of grace,  
In Your holy presence, we seek Your embrace.  
Blessed are Your people, forever we'll sing,  
To the One who is faithful, our Savior, our King.
