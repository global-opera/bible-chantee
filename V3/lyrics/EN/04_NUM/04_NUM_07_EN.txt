[TITLE]
The Offering of Our Hearts

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of this sacred place,  
Moses set the tabernacle, filled with grace.  
He anointed all the vessels, laid them down,  
A holy offering, our praises sound.  

Chorus  
Oh, we come to You, Lord, with hearts laid bare,  
In the shadow of Your glory, we find our prayer.  
With every gift we bring, with every song we raise,  
We dedicate our lives, to You we give our days.  

Verse 2  
Princes of Israel brought forth their gifts,  
Wagons and oxen, as the Spirit lifts.  
Each one in their service, they offered with delight,  
A fragrant sacrifice, a pleasing sight.  

Chorus  
Oh, we come to You, Lord, with hearts laid bare,  
In the shadow of Your glory, we find our prayer.  
With every gift we bring, with every song we raise,  
We dedicate our lives, to You we give our days.  

Bridge  
From every tribe and every land,  
We unite in faith, together we stand.  
Lord, speak to us from mercy's throne,  
In Your presence, God, we are never alone.  

Verse 3  
As the altar was anointed, offerings made,  
Twelve silver chargers, Your love displayed.  
A bullock for the burden, a lamb for peace,  
In our hearts, Lord, let our worship never cease.  

Chorus  
Oh, we come to You, Lord, with hearts laid bare,  
In the shadow of Your glory, we find our prayer.  
With every gift we bring, with every song we raise,  
We dedicate our lives, to You we give our days.  

Outro  
So here we stand, in reverence we bow,  
With hearts of worship, Lord, we seek You now.  
In the sacred silence, let our praises flow,  
We offer all we are, to You, we will go.
