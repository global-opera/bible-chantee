[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the morning light, we come with praise,  
Two lambs of the first year, our hearts ablaze.  
Without a spot, pure and true,  
A continual offering, Lord, we bring to You.  

Chorus  
Oh, sweet savor, rise to the skies,  
Our hearts in worship, no need for disguise.  
In every season, we'll lift Your name,  
With our sacrifices, forever the same.  

Verse 2  
At evening’s glow, we gather in prayer,  
With flour mingled, our love we share.  
A drink offering poured, our hearts laid bare,  
For in Your presence, we find our care.  

Chorus  
Oh, sweet savor, rise to the skies,  
Our hearts in worship, no need for disguise.  
In every season, we'll lift Your name,  
With our sacrifices, forever the same.  

Bridge  
On the Sabbath day, we find our rest,  
Two young bullocks, we give our best.  
In holy convocation, our voices unite,  
For You are our joy, our guiding light.  

Verse 3  
The Passover comes, with bread unleavened,  
In remembrance of grace, we gather, heaven-borne.  
A ram and seven lambs, all without blemish,  
In this sacred moment, our hearts we relinquish.  

Chorus  
Oh, sweet savor, rise to the skies,  
Our hearts in worship, no need for disguise.  
In every season, we'll lift Your name,  
With our sacrifices, forever the same.  

Outro  
So let our offerings rise, day by day,  
In every breath, Lord, we long to stay.  
With joy and reverence, our hearts we implore,  
Sweet offerings to You, now and evermore.
