[TITLE]
In His Appointed Season

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the wilderness, the Lord did speak,  
To Moses and His people, strong and meek.  
"Keep the Passover, in the night so still,  
At the appointed time, obey My will."  

[Chorus]  
In His appointed season, we gather 'round,  
With hearts of gratitude, His love profound.  
From the shadows of darkness, we rise anew,  
In the light of His promise, our hope shines through.  

[Verse 2]  
Though some may be weary, and others unclean,  
The Lord speaks grace, in the midst of the scene.  
“Stand still, I will guide you, through trials and fears,  
A second chance given, for all who draw near.”  

[Chorus]  
In His appointed season, we gather 'round,  
With hearts of gratitude, His love profound.  
From the shadows of darkness, we rise anew,  
In the light of His promise, our hope shines through.  

[Bridge]  
As the cloud leads the way, by day and by night,  
We journey together, in His steadfast light.  
Obeying the call, where He bids us stay,  
In tents of His mercy, we trust and obey.  

[Verse 3]  
So let us remember the sacrifice made,  
With unleavened bread, our hearts laid bare.  
No bone left broken, as we lift our song,  
In this holy moment, we truly belong.  

[Chorus]  
In His appointed season, we gather 'round,  
With hearts of gratitude, His love profound.  
From the shadows of darkness, we rise anew,  
In the light of His promise, our hope shines through.  

[Outro]  
In the wilderness, we hear His call,  
Together we stand, united we fall.  
For in His appointed season, we find our peace,  
In the arms of our Savior, our fears release.
