Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, Lord, we seek Your face,  
You healed the broken-hearted, brought us to Your grace.  
The blind now see, the lame can walk,  
Your love, our hope, in every talk.  

Chorus  
Come to me, all who are weary,  
Lay your burdens down, find rest in Me.  
For my yoke is easy, my heart is lowly,  
In Your arms, Lord, I’m finally free.  

Verse 2  
John called from the shadows, asking for the truth,  
"Are You the One, Lord? Show us what to do."  
You answered with the works of love,  
The lost are found, we lift our eyes above.  

Chorus  
Come to me, all who are weary,  
Lay your burdens down, find rest in Me.  
For my yoke is easy, my heart is lowly,  
In Your arms, Lord, I’m finally free.  

Bridge  
Oh, Father, thank You for revealing grace,  
To the weary hearts, and the childlike face.  
In the midst of trials, we find our song,  
In Your gentle whispers, we know we belong.  

Chorus

Outro  
In the stillness, Lord, we hear Your call,

In Your presence, Lord, we are truly blessed.
