[TITLE]
In the Shadow of the Cross

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When morning breaks, the skies turn grey,  
The innocent is led away.  
With bound hands and a silent plea,  
He bears the weight of sin for me.  

Chorus  
Oh, Jesus, King, upon that tree,  
Your blood speaks love, it sets me free.  
In anguish deep, You took my place,  
In the shadow of the cross, I find grace.  

Verse 2  
Judas weeps, his heart is torn,  
For thirty pieces, the price was borne.  
He cast away the silver's gleam,  
And in the night, lost all his dreams.  

Chorus  
Oh, Jesus, King, upon that tree,  
Your blood speaks love, it sets me free.  
In anguish deep, You took my place,  
In the shadow of the cross, I find grace.  

Bridge  
From Golgotha’s hill, the darkness fell,  
“Eli, Eli,” the Savior's yell.  
Yet through the pain, Your love remained,  
In every wound, my hope retained.  

Verse 3  
The soldiers mocked, they bowed their knee,  
Yet in their scorn, You set me free.  
You bore the shame, You wore the crown,  
With every breath, the world turned down.  

Chorus  
Oh, Jesus, King, upon that tree,  
Your blood speaks love, it sets me free.  
In anguish deep, You took my place,  
In the shadow of the cross, I find grace.  

Outro  
And when the stone was rolled away,  
The grave could not hold the Light of Day.  
With every tear, with every cost,  
I stand redeemed, forever lost in You,  
In the shadow of the cross.
