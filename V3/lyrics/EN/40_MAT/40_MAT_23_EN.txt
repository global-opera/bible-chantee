[TITLE]
Humble Our Hearts

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the law, we gather here,  
Listening to the call of love, so crystal clear.  
You say to serve, not to be seen,  
To walk in light, where hearts are clean.  

Chorus:  
Humble our hearts, Lord, lift us high,  
In the quiet, we seek Your sky.  
For the greatest among us, must be a friend,  
In the love of Christ, our souls will mend.  

Verse 2:  
Woe to those who wear a mask,  
In pride they dwell, in shadows they bask.  
Yet You call us to be true and free,  
To love our neighbors, as You love me.  

Chorus:  
Humble our hearts, Lord, lift us high,  
In the quiet, we seek Your sky.  
For the greatest among us, must be a friend,  
In the love of Christ, our souls will mend.  

Bridge:  
O Jerusalem, how He longs for you,  
Gather us close, in all that we do.  
With mercy and grace, we come to Your throne,  
In humility, Lord, make our hearts Your own.  

Chorus:  
Humble our hearts, Lord, lift us high,  
In the quiet, we seek Your sky.  
For the greatest among us, must be a friend,  
In the love of Christ, our souls will mend.  

Outro:  
So we lay down our pride, and rise in grace,  
In the arms of the Savior, we find our place.  
Humble our hearts, Lord, forever we pray,  
In Your love and light, we choose to stay.
