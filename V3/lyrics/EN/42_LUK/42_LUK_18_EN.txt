[TITLE]
A Cry for Mercy

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In a world where shadows fall,  
I come to You, my Savior's call,  
The widow's heart, her voice so clear,  
A cry for justice, Your love draws near.  
You hear my prayer, though I may faint,  
Teach me to trust, Lord, and to wait.

Chorus  
Oh, hear my cry, Son of David,  
Have mercy, Lord, I’m desperate for Your grace.  
In humble faith, I seek Your face,  
A heart once blind, now sees Your embrace.  
Oh, hear my cry, Son of David,  
Your love restores, it lifts me from despair.  
With every breath, in You I rest,  
My Savior, my hope, my heart's true quest.

Verse 2  
Two men bowed low in the temple’s light,  
One stood proud, the other in plight.  
The Pharisee spoke of all he'd done,  
While the publican’s heart weighed a ton.  
Lord, I am nothing, a sinner in need,  
Your mercy, my lifeline, my soul’s true creed.

Bridge  
For the things impossible, You make them true,  
The riches of heaven, I trade for You.  
With open hands, I lay down my pride,  
In the arms of mercy, Lord, I abide.  
You call the little ones, to You they come,  
In childlike faith, Your kingdom's begun.

So let my heart be ever near,  
To trust in You, my Savior dear.  
In every moment, lift my sight,  
To follow You, my guiding light.
