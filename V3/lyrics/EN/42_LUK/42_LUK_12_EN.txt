[TITLE]
Value Beyond Measure

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the silence, hear Him call,  
"Beware of hearts that hide it all.  
For nothing's covered that won’t be known,  
The whispers in the dark will be shown."  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, little flock, you are loved,  
More than sparrows, you rise above.  
In every trial, He holds your hand,  
Your worth defined by His great plan.  

[Verse 2]  
Consider the lilies, how they bloom,  
In grace adorned, dispelling gloom.  
If God so clothes the grass of the field,  
His promise of care will be revealed.  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, little flock, you are loved,  
More than sparrows, you rise above.  
In every trial, He holds your hand,  
Your worth defined by His great plan.  

[Bridge]  
Seek first His kingdom, trust in His ways,  
For all your needs, He'll provide each day.  
He knows your heart, He sees your strife,  
In Him, you find abundant life.  

[Verse 3]  
So lay aside your search for gain,  
Life’s not in wealth, but in His name.  
Confess Him boldly, let faith be heard,  
For every promise rests in His Word.  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, little flock, you are loved,  
More than sparrows, you rise above.  
In every trial, He holds your hand,  
Your worth defined by His great plan.  

[Outro]  
So let your heart be filled with grace,  
In every moment, seek His face.  
Fear not, dear ones, for in His light,  
You are treasured, you are His delight.
