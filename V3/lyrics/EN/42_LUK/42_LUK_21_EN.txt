[TITLE]
Lift Up Your Heads

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the temple,  
Rich and poor both give,  
A widow’s heart, so humble,  
In her two mites, she lives.  
He saw the weight of abundance,  
But her gift, a treasure rare,  
In the eyes of heaven’s justice,  
Love and faith laid bare.

Chorus:  
Lift up your heads, for redemption draws near,  
When the world is shaken, have no fear.  
With every trial, His promise we hold,  
In faith and patience, our hearts made bold.

Verse 2:  
Signs in the heavens, nations in distress,  
Yet in the midst of sorrow,  
He whispers, “You are blessed.”  
Though they may betray you,  
And darkness close around,  
Stand firm in His promise,  
In His love, we are found.

Chorus:  
Lift up your heads, for redemption draws near,  
When the world is shaken, have no fear.  
With every trial, His promise we hold,  
In faith and patience, our hearts made bold.

Bridge:  
Watch and pray, for the kingdom is at hand,  
When the fig tree blossoms, know His perfect plan.  
Heaven and earth may fade away,  
But His words endure, forever they stay.

Chorus:  
Lift up your heads, for redemption draws near,  
When the world is shaken, have no fear.  
With every trial, His promise we hold,  
In faith and patience, our hearts made bold.

Outro:  
So come, let us gather, in His name we stand,  
With hearts wide open, and joined hand in hand.  
For though the storms may rage and the night may fall,  
In His light we find hope, our Savior, our all.
