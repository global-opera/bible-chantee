[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Wisdom of Solomon

[Verse 1]  
In the land of promise, under the sun,  
Solomon reigned, the chosen one.  
With princes and scribes, he led with grace,  
A heart full of wisdom, in his rightful place.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord, we seek Your wisdom bright,  
Like the sand by the sea, Your truth is our light.  
In every season, we trust in Your plan,  
Guide our hearts, O Mighty Hand.  

[Verse 2]  
Judah and Israel, in peace they dwell,  
Under vines and fig trees, all is well.  
From the river to the sea, Your favor is near,  
In Your abundance, we have no fear.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord, we seek Your wisdom bright,  
Like the sand by the sea, Your truth is our light.  
In every season, we trust in Your plan,  
Guide our hearts, O Mighty Hand.  

[Bridge]  
Three thousand proverbs, songs of the soul,  
In every word, Your glory unfolds.  
From the heights of Lebanon to the depths of the sea,  
Your wisdom, O Lord, sets our spirits free.  

[Verse 3]  
Bring the nations, let them hear,  
The wisdom of Solomon, so pure and clear.  
For in every lesson, Your love we find,  
A heart that is humble, a spirit aligned.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord, we seek Your wisdom bright,  
Like the sand by the sea, Your truth is our light.  
In every season, we trust in Your plan,  
Guide our hearts, O Mighty Hand.  

[Outro]  
So we come together, united we stand,  
In the wisdom of Solomon, guided by Your hand.  
Forever we'll praise, in harmony sing,  
With hearts full of faith, to our Savior, our King.
