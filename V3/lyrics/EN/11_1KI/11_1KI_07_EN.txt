Verse 1  
In the heart of the land, Solomon rose,  
A temple of splendor, where worship flows.  
With pillars of cedar, reaching so high,  
A promise of heaven, beneath the vast sky.  

Chorus  
O come, let us gather, in this sacred place,  
Where the glory of God shines, full of grace.  
With hearts lifted high, we sing out His name,  
In the house of the Lord, forever the same.
