[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In the shadow of the city, where fear begins to rise,  
Benhadad, king of darkness, surrounds with his lies.  
He seeks to take our treasures, our hearts, and all we own,  
But You, O Lord, are mighty, on Your strength we stand alone.

Chorus:  
You are the God of the valleys, the hills and the seas,  
In every battle raging, You’re our victory.  
When foes come like a tempest, You calm the stormy seas,  
O Lord, our mighty fortress, our hope in times of need.

Verse 2:  
Ahab faced the challenge, with wisdom he did seek,  
The elders stood together, their hearts would not be weak.  
"Fear not the roar of armies, nor the king's cruel demand,  
For God has said He’ll save us, in His power we shall stand."

Bridge:  
When the mountains tremble, and the darkness falls,  
Your light will break the shadows, You hear our desperate calls.  
In the plains or the valleys, in the fight or in peace,  
Your promise stands forever, our fears and doubts released.

Verse 3:  
As the battle raged on, a prophet came to say,  
"Trust in the God of valleys; He’ll lead you on this day."  
With hearts now filled with courage, they marched in faithful song,  
For in the face of giants, it is You who makes us strong.

Outro:  
So we lift our voices high, in reverence and praise,  
For the God who fights beside us, through all our nights and days.  
O Lord, our mighty fortress, our hope in times of need,  
You are the God of the valleys, in Your love we are freed.
