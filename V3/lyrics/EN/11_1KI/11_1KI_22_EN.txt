[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Call to Battle

[Verse 1]  
In days of peace, the land stood still,  
But Ahab's heart sought war, against God's will.  
"Will you join me, Jehoshaphat, my friend?  
To Ramoth-Gilead, let our armies ascend."  

[Pre-Chorus]  
But wisdom cried from deep within,  
"Seek the Lord, before we begin."  
A prophet true, to speak His name,  
For only truth can guide us through the flame.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, we call on You,  
In battles fierce, our hearts stay true.  
With every word, Your Spirit leads,  
In the face of fear, Your promise feeds.  
We stand as one, united we fight,  
With faith as our shield, You are our light.  

[Verse 2]  
Four hundred voices sang in praise,  
“Go, for victory is Yours, we raise!”  
But Micaiah stood, with courage bold,  
He saw the truth, a tale foretold.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
“Israel scattered like sheep astray,  
Without a shepherd to show the way.”  
The king's heart hardened, his anger burned,  
But truth will conquer, and justice returned.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, we call on You,  
In battles fierce, our hearts stay true.  
With every word, Your Spirit leads,  
In the face of fear, Your promise feeds.  
We stand as one, united we fight,  
With faith as our shield, You are our light.  

[Bridge]  
So lead us forth, O mighty King,  
In every trial, let our praises ring.  
When darkness falls, and shadows loom,  
We trust in You, our hearts in bloom.  
Through every battle, through every strife,  
Your truth is our armor, our source of life.  

[Chorus]

[Outro]  
In the story of faith, where battles are won,  
We find our hope in the risen Son.  
Together we march, through valleys low,  
With You, O Lord, forever we’ll go.
