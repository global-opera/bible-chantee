Verse 1  
In Jezreel, a vineyard stands,  
An inheritance, a promise in His hands.  
But Ahab sought with greedful eyes,  
To take what’s pure, beneath the skies.  
Naboth stood firm, he would not yield,  
His heart was bound to the Lord’s field.  
“The Lord forbid,” his voice rang true,  
For faith and honor, he boldly grew.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, guard our hearts, keep us from strife,  
In the battles we face, be our guiding light.  
Let not the greed of the world take its toll,  
For the vineyard of our hearts belongs to Your soul.  

Verse 2  
Jezebel whispered, her plans were dark,  
With deceit and lies, she struck a spark.  
Two men arose, their hearts were cold,  
To silence the truth, their stories sold.  
Naboth was taken, the stones did fall,  
Yet Heaven wept, for justice called.  
Oh, how the wicked can twist and turn,  
But in the shadows, the faithful yearn.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, guard our hearts, keep us from strife,  
In the battles we face, be our guiding light.  
Let not the greed of the world take its toll,  
For the vineyard of our hearts belongs to Your soul.  

Bridge  
But the word of the Lord came down from above,  
To Elijah, a messenger of truth and love.  
“Have you killed for a piece of land?  
Your evil deeds will not withstand.”  
With sackcloth and tears, Ahab did cry,  
Humbled before the Lord on high.  
A glimpse of mercy, a chance to repent,  
In the heart of the wicked, His grace is sent.

Outro  
So let us stand firm, in faith we abide,  
With courage and love, let the truth be our guide.  
For the vineyard of our hearts, we will defend,  
In the light of His glory, our hope will not end.
