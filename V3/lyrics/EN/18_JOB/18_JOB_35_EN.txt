[TITLE]
Look to the Heavens

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
When the shadows close in tight,  
We search for answers, wandering hearts,  
Yet Your truth sets us apart.  
Oh, we cry out in our pain,  
Seeking solace in the rain,  
But in our pride, we often ask,  
"What profit is in righteousness?"

Chorus  
Look to the heavens, behold the skies,  
Higher than our thoughts, where Your glory lies.  
In our weakness, You are strong,  
Teach us, Lord, to sing Your song.  
You give us wisdom, more than we know,  
In our darkness, let Your light show.

Verse 2  
If I stumble, what do I gain?  
My transgressions, like a heavy chain.  
Yet You listen to the cries of the meek,  
In the silence, it’s Your voice we seek.  
Though the world may seem so vast,  
In Your love, our fears are cast.  
Help us trust when we can't see,  
Your judgment holds eternity.

Bridge  
Even in the night, You are here,  
With songs of hope, You draw us near.  
Though the pride of men may rise,  
We will seek Your heart and realize,  
That our righteousness is but a gift,  
In Your mercy, our spirits lift.

Outro  
So we lift our eyes to You,  
In our hearts, Your love is true.  
With every breath, we’ll trust and stand,  
In Your grace, we find our land.  
Look to the heavens, behold the skies,  
In You alone, our hope shall rise.
