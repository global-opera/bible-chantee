[TITLE]
In the Days of Your Light

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Oh, how I long for days gone by,  
When Your presence shone so bright,  
In the shadows, You were my guide,  
Walking with me through the night.  
In the secret place, I found my rest,  
With the Almighty, I was blessed.  
My children gathered, joy in their hearts,  
In Your love, we never drift apart.  

Chorus  
In the days of Your light, I was whole,  
Your grace like rivers, filling my soul.  
You were my strength when I stood tall,  
In the days of Your light, I had it all.  

Verse 2  
I was eyes to the blind, feet to the lame,  
Every whisper of hope carried Your name.  
To the poor and the weary, I gave my hand,  
In the midst of despair, You helped me stand.  
With righteousness wrapped around my heart,  
In Your mercy, my life took part.  
Oh, how the young would rise in Your name,  
And the wise would listen, never the same.  

Chorus  
In the days of Your light, I was whole,  
Your grace like rivers, filling my soul.  
You were my strength when I stood tall,  
In the days of Your light, I had it all.  

Bridge  
As I dwell in the promise of Your love,  
I’ll trust in the plan that’s written above.  
Though the seasons may change, my heart will remain,  
Rooted in hope, I’ll call on Your name.  

Chorus  
In the days of Your light, I am whole,  
Your grace like rivers, filling my soul.  
You are my strength when I stand tall,  
In the days of Your light, I have it all.  

Outro  
Let me walk, Lord, in Your radiant grace,  
In the days of Your light, I find my place.  
With every step, I’ll trust and believe,  
In the days of Your light, I shall receive.
