Verse 1  
In the silence of my heart, I come to You,  
With questions that I’ve carried, burdens deep and true.  
You speak from the whirlwind, Your voice thundered bright,  
Who am I to contend, in Your gracious light?

Chorus  
Gird me, Lord, with strength; let me stand in awe,  
In the shadow of Your wings, I know I’m never flawed.  
Though I tremble and I fall, I will trust in Your embrace,  
For in the whirlwind of my doubts, I find Your grace.
