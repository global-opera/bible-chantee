[TITLE]
The Thunder of Your Power

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence, we gather near,  
To the One who holds all fear,  
How can we speak of Your great might,  
When the heavens tremble at Your light?  
You stretch the skies, oh Lord, so wide,  
Holding the earth where shadows hide.  

Chorus:  
You are the strength of the weak,  
The hope for the lost,  
In Your presence, we seek,  
No matter the cost.  
Your power is thunder,  
Your wisdom divine,  
In awe of Your wonder,  
We give You our time.  

Verse 2:  
From the depths, the waters rise,  
You form the world, beneath the skies.  
Hell is bare before Your throne,  
In Your majesty, we find our home.  
You bind the clouds, with grace so pure,  
In every trial, Your love is sure.

Bridge:  
Who can comprehend,  
The whispers of Your hand?  
In the dark, You shine,  
With a light so divine.  
Oh, how little we know,  
Of the depths of Your grace,  
Yet we long to behold,  
Your glorious face.

So we gather in reverence,  
With hearts open wide,  
For the thunder of Your power,  
Is our faithful guide.
