Verse 1  
How long, O Lord, will my heart ache,  
Words like arrows, they pierce and break?  
My friends stand far, in silence they dwell,  
In my despair, I’ve no one to tell.  
Yet in my sorrow, I lift my cry,  
Though darkness surrounds, I know You are nigh.

Chorus  
For I know my Redeemer lives,  
In the midst of trials, His grace He gives.  
Though my flesh may fade, my hope shall remain,  
In the sight of my God, all sorrow is slain.

Verse 2  
The crown that was mine, stripped from my head,  
A path filled with darkness, where hope seems dead.  
Yet I will trust, though my spirit feels weak,  
In my deepest valley, it's Your voice I seek.  
Have mercy on me, O friends, I plead,  
For the touch of His hand is all that I need.

Bridge  
Oh, write my words, let them echo in time,  
Graven in the rock, a testament so fine.  
For I shall see Him, my eyes shall behold,  
In the promise of glory, my heart will be whole.

Outro  
So I lift my hands, in faith I will stand,  
For I know my Redeemer, holds me in His hand.  
With every breath, I will trust in His way,  
For my Redeemer lives, and He’s here today.
