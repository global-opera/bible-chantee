Verse 1  
In the stillness of a dry morsel,  
Where peace finds its place to dwell,  
Better is this sacred silence,  
Than a house where strife will swell.  
A wise heart shall find its treasure,  
With love that seeks to mend,  
For children’s laughter is a crown,  
In the grace that never ends.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, try our hearts, refine us,  
In Your love, let us abide.  
A gentle spirit leads us onward,  
In Your wisdom, we confide.  
Let peace flow like a river,  
In the quiet, we will thrive,  
With every breath, we’ll trust in You,  
In Your presence, we are alive.  

Verse 2  
A friend who loves through every season,  
Stands beside when storms arise,  
In the furnace, gold is shining,  
As You test our hearts and ties.  
Let us cover every transgression,  
With mercy’s gentle hand,  
For a merry heart is healing,  
In Your grace, we take our stand.
