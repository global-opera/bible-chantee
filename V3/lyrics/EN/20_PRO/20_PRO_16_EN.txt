[TITLE]
Direct My Steps

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of my heart, Lord,  
I seek Your guiding hand,  
Every thought and every word,  
From You alone I stand.  
Though my ways seem right to me,  
You weigh the spirits true,  
I commit my works to You,  
Establish all I do.  

Chorus  
Direct my steps, O Lord, I pray,  
In Your mercy, lead the way.  
With wisdom greater than gold's might,  
Help me walk in Your true light.  

Verse 2  
Pride may rise, but I will choose,  
The humble path to tread;  
For in the lowly spirit,  
Your grace is gently spread.  
When my ways delight Your heart,  
You bring peace to every foe,  
Better is a little gain,  
With righteousness to show.  

Chorus  
Direct my steps, O Lord, I pray,  
In Your mercy, lead the way.  
With wisdom greater than gold's might,  
Help me walk in Your true light.  

Bridge  
Sweet are the words of wisdom,  
A honeycomb to my soul,  
Teach me, Lord, to speak with grace,  
Make my heart and spirit whole.  
In every trial, in every storm,  
May I trust in You alone,  
For You, O Lord, are sovereign,  
In Your love, I find my home.  

Chorus  
Direct my steps, O Lord, I pray,  
In Your mercy, lead the way.  
With wisdom greater than gold's might,  
Help me walk in Your true light.  

Outro  
In the silence of my heart, Lord,  
I seek Your guiding hand,  
Though my paths may twist and turn,  
In You, alone I stand.
