[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the heart, where the whispers dwell,  
A soft answer turns away the strife we know too well.  
The eyes of the Lord, in every place they see,  
He beholds the good and evil, guiding you and me.

Chorus  
Oh, lead us on the path of wisdom,  
Where love and truth will grow.  
With hearts that seek understanding,  
In Your light, we long to go.  
Better is a humble feast,  
Where love is found, our souls released.

Verse 2  
A wholesome tongue, a tree of life, it speaks with grace,  
The lips of the wise, they share the knowledge in this place.  
Though folly calls, we’ll choose the way that’s right,  
With every word we offer, let it shine Your light.

Bridge  
In the fear of the Lord, we find our guiding light,  
With humility, we walk, away from prideful night.  
Reproof and gentle counsel, our hearts will learn to bear,  
As we seek the treasure found in the love and care.

Outro  
We’ll walk the path of life, where Your goodness leads the way,
