Verse 1:  
In a world where shadows loom,  
Evil men and hearts of gloom,  
I will not envy, I will not stray,  
For Your wisdom lights my way.  
Through understanding, my house is built,  
In Your knowledge, my fears are stilled.

Chorus:  
Oh, grant me wisdom, Lord, I pray,  
Fill my heart with truth today.  
In the battles I will face,  
Guide me with Your gentle grace.  
For in the counsel of the wise,  
Safety in Your love, I find.
