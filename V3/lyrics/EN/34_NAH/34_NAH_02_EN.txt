[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the fortress,  
We stand before Your gaze,  
Strengthen our hearts, O Lord,  
Guide us through these days.  
The valiant rise in scarlet,  
But their might will fade away,  
For You, O God, are greater,  
In Your truth, we’ll stay.

Chorus:  
O Lord, we cry, where do we go?  
When the chariots rage, and the rivers flow,  
Your mighty hand, our refuge and shield,  
In the day of reckoning, our hearts will yield.  
Stand, stand, we call on Your name,  
In our weakness, we seek Your flame.

Verse 2:  
The gates of sorrow tremble,  
As the lion’s roar is still,  
Empty are the treasures,  
Yet Your promise we fulfill.  
You have turned the hearts of nations,  
Empty vessels in despair,  
But in Your grace, we gather,  
With our burdens, we lay bare.

Bridge:  
Where is the dwelling of the lions?  
Where is the strength that once stood tall?  
You, O Lord, bring forth the silence,  
And in Your mercy, we heed the call.  
For You are against the proud and mighty,  
But to the humble, You draw near,  
In the ashes, we find our glory,  
In repentance, we will persevere.

In the day of reckoning,  
We will bow before Your throne,
