Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the morning, I ponder all I've gained,  
Riches, wealth, and honor, yet my soul feels uncontained.  
A feast laid out before me, but I cannot taste the grace,  
For the stranger eats my portion, and I'm left in empty space.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, teach me to find my rest,  
In the gifts that You provide,  
Not in what the world suggests,  
But in Your love, my heart abides.  
Though shadows fall and dreams may fade,  
Your light remains, my hope displayed.
