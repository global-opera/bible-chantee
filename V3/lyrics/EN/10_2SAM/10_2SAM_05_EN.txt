[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of promise, the tribes unite,  
“Here we stand as one, your flesh and bone,  
You’ve led us through shadows, through the darkest night,  
The Lord has spoken, you are His own.”  

Pre-Chorus  
In Hebron’s embrace, the elders proclaim,  
A covenant made before the Lord’s name,  
Anointing the king, with hearts full of praise,  
David, our captain, we lift up your ways.  

Chorus  
King David, arise, lead us in might,  
For the Lord our God is with you, shining bright.  
In the city of Zion, your reign will be true,  
With every battle won, we trust in You.  

Verse 2  
From the heart of Jerusalem, the Jebusites stand,  
But faith in our God gives us strength to command,  
With courage unyielding, we take back our land,  
For the blind and the lame, we will make a stand.  

Pre-Chorus  
In the fort of our fortress, your spirit ignites,  
Building a kingdom, as the day turns to night,  
You’ve grown ever greater, with the Lord by your side,  
A legacy forged in His love and His pride.  

Chorus  
King David, arise, lead us in might,  
For the Lord our God is with you, shining bright.  
In the city of Zion, your reign will be true,  
With every battle won, we trust in You.  

Bridge  
When the foes gather ‘round, and the valleys are dark,  
We seek You in prayer, Lord, ignite our spark.  
For every whisper of doubt, we raise up our sword,  
With the sound of the trees, Your victory’s stored.  

Chorus  
King David, arise, lead us in might,  
For the Lord our God is with you, shining bright.  
In the city of Zion, your reign will be true,  
With every battle won, we trust in You.  

Outro  
So let the banners fly, let our voices declare,  
In the heart of our king, we find hope and repair.  
With faith everlasting, our spirits renew,  
In the name of our Savior, we’ll follow You.
