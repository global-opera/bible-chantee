[TITLE]
In the Shadow of the King

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the night, a whisper calls,  
Ahithophel sought to rise and fall.  
Twelve thousand hearts in shadow’s grace,  
But a lion roars in the hidden place.  
Weary souls in battle’s breath,  
Fleeing darkness, facing death.  
Yet a voice from wisdom’s well,  
Hushai spoke, “He knows it well.”

[Chorus]  
Arise, O King, in the wilderness,  
Your faithful ones, they seek your rest.  
Though the enemy surrounds us here,  
We trust in You, we cast out fear.  
In the shadow of the King, we stand,  
With hearts united, hand in hand.  
For every battle, every strife,  
We find our refuge in Your life.

[Verse 2]  
To the river, swift they go,  
David’s heart, though weary, glows.  
The faithful bring their gifts of grace,  
In the wilderness, we seek Your face.  
Hushai’s words like morning light,  
A warning shared in the dead of night.  
“Pass quickly now, for time is short,  
The King shall rise, and we’ll support.”

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
In the valley low, we will not fear,  
For Your light, O Lord, is ever near.  
When the world is dark, and hope seems lost,  
We stand as one, we count the cost.  
For every tear and every fight,  
You lead us on, our guiding light.  
In the heart of night, we find our song,  
In the shadow of the King, where we belong.

[Outro]  
So let us rise, and let us sing,  
In the shadow, we praise our King.  
With every heartbeat, every breath,  
We stand in faith, we conquer death.  
In the shadow of the King, we stand,  
With hearts united, hand in hand.
