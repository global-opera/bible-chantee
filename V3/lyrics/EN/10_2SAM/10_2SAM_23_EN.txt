[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the valley, where the giants roam,  
David’s heart was lifted, in the battles he called home.  
Anointed by the Spirit, he spoke the words of grace,  
The Rock of Israel guiding, in every fear we face.  

Chorus  
Oh, rise up, mighty warriors,  
Stand firm in faith, behold!  
With hearts of courage, we will not falter,  
In the love of God, we are bold.  
Through the storms and through the trials,  
With our eyes fixed on the prize,  
We will fight for truth and justice,  
In the morning light, we rise.  

Verse 2  
Eleazar, brave and fearless, with a sword that would not yield,  
Defied the foes before him, on the battlefield he sealed.  
Shammah stood in the harvest, for the lentils he would fight,  
With the Lord’s strength beside him, he brought darkness into light.

Bridge  
David longed for water, from the well of Bethlehem,  
His friends broke through the darkness, for the love they held within.  
He poured it out before the Lord, a sacrifice so sweet,  
In every act of valor, His glory we repeat.

Outro  
So let us be like David, with a heart that seeks the Lord,  
In the battles that we face, we’ll trust in His accord.  
With the mighty men beside us, we will stand and we will fight,  
For our God is our salvation, in His love we find our light.
