[TITLE]
Return to the Heart of the King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of despair, a mother cries,  
Her sons torn apart, love's bond compromised.  
With a heart full of sorrow, she seeks the throne,  
“Help me, O King! I am all alone.”  

Chorus  
Return, O King, to the heart of the lost,  
Bring back the banished, no matter the cost.  
For as water spilled, our lives cannot be saved,  
Yet mercy flows deep, from the One who forgave.  

Verse 2  
“Remember, dear King, the Lord God above,  
Let not the blood avengers silence our love.  
You hold the wisdom, like angels on high,  
To discern the good, and the tears we cry.”  

Chorus  
Return, O King, to the heart of the lost,  
Bring back the banished, no matter the cost.  
For as water spilled, our lives cannot be saved,  
Yet mercy flows deep, from the One who forgave.  

Bridge  
You see our pain, You hear our plea,  
In the depths of our hearts, You set us free.  
Though we wander far, and our hope seems dim,  
Your love brings us back, to the light within.  

Verse 3  
With a heart full of grace, the King makes His stand,  
“Let Absalom come home, for I hold his hand.”  
No more shall he wander, lost in the night,  
For love covers all, and makes wrongs right.  

Chorus  
Return, O King, to the heart of the lost,  
Bring back the banished, no matter the cost.  
For as water spilled, our lives cannot be saved,  
Yet mercy flows deep, from the One who forgave.  

Outro  
So we gather in faith, with hearts open wide,  
To the King who restores, our Savior and guide.  
With every return, we find strength anew,  
In the arms of our King, forever true.
