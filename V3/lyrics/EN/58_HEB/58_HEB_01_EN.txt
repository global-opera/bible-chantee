[TITLE]
He Speaks Through His Son

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the ages, Your voice breaks through the night,  
Once You spoke through prophets, now You shine with holy light.  
Jesus, Son of glory, the heir of all we see,  
In Your love, our sins are purged, setting all the captives free.  

Chorus  
He speaks through His Son, the brightness of His grace,  
Forever on the throne, we seek Your holy face.  
O let the angels sing, proclaiming who You are,  
Our Savior, King of kings, our everlasting Star.  

Verse 2  
You are made much greater, than the angels ever known,  
A name above all names, in heaven’s great throne room shown.  
With a scepter of righteousness, You reign in majesty,  
In Your love, we find our hope, in Your heart, our destiny.  

Chorus  
He speaks through His Son, the brightness of His grace,  
Forever on the throne, we seek Your holy face.  
O let the angels sing, proclaiming who You are,  
Our Savior, King of kings, our everlasting Star.  

Bridge  
Lord, You laid the earth’s foundation, heavens crafted by Your hand,  
Though the world may fade and perish, in Your promise we will stand.  
You are the same forever, ever faithful, ever true,  
In the chaos, You are sovereign, through it all, we trust in You.  

Chorus  
He speaks through His Son, the brightness of His grace,  
Forever on the throne, we seek Your holy face.  
O let the angels sing, proclaiming who You are,  
Our Savior, King of kings, our everlasting Star.  

Outro  
He speaks through His Son, we lift our hearts in praise,  
For in Your love, O Savior, we find our hope always.  
He speaks through His Son, forever we will sing,  
To the glory of the Father, our Lord and risen King.
