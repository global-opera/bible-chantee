[TITLE]
The Cry of Your People

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the silence of our hearts, where sorrow dwells,  
A cry arises, echoing through the wells,  
The bones of those who’ve led us now lay bare,  
Before the sun and moon, in the open air.  
Oh, we’ve sought the fleeting, the shadows of night,  
But in our wandering, we’ve lost the light.  

[Chorus]  
Is there no balm in Gilead?  
Is there no healer near?  
We’ve turned our backs on Your promise,  
Yet Your voice is still clear.  
In the harvest past and summer's end,  
We cry out for hope, Lord, come and mend.  

[Verse 2]  
How long will we slumber, when judgment is near?  
The storks know their seasons, but we live in fear.  
With hearts that are hardened, we chase after lies,  
Yet Your love pursues us, beneath the dark skies.  
In the valley of ashes, we seek for Your grace,  
Restore us, O Lord, let us see Your face.  

[Chorus]  
Is there no balm in Gilead?  
Is there no healer near?  
We’ve turned our backs on Your promise,  
Yet Your voice is still clear.  
In the harvest past and summer's end,  
We cry out for hope, Lord, come and mend.  

[Bridge]  
We looked for peace, but trouble remains,  
In our affliction, Lord, break every chain.  
For the hurt of Your people, our hearts ache and break,  
Revive us, restore us, for Your mercy’s sake.  

[Chorus]  
Is there no balm in Gilead?  
Is there no healer near?  
We’ve turned our backs on Your promise,  
Yet Your voice is still clear.  
In the harvest past and summer's end,  
We cry out for hope, Lord, come and mend.  

[Outro]  
The cry of Your people, we lift it high,  
In the depths of our sorrow, Lord, hear our sigh.  
Though the world may tremble, and darkness descend,  
In the balm of Your presence, our hearts will mend.
