[TITLE]
The Sword of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Behold, the waters rise from the north,  
An overflowing flood, a mighty force,  
Cities cry out, their voices in pain,  
The land is trembling, yet You still reign.  
Fathers in fear, their hearts filled with dread,  
Not looking back, as the lost ones tread.  
O Lord, our refuge, our strength in the night,  
In the chaos around us, You are our light.  

Chorus  
O Sword of the Lord, how long will You stay?  
Bring forth Your justice, lead us in the way.  
We cry out for mercy, we plead for Your grace,  
In the midst of the storm, we seek Your face.  

Verse 2  
Ashkelon weeps, and Gaza stands bare,  
The remnants of hope linger in despair.  
Yet in the shadows, Your promise shines bright,  
Restoration and peace, You’ll bring to the night.  
The chariots thunder, the hoofbeats resound,  
But we find our shelter, where grace can be found.  
O Lord, You have spoken, Your word is our song,  
In the heart of the battle, we know we belong.  

Chorus  
O Sword of the Lord, how long will You stay?  
Bring forth Your justice, lead us in the way.  
We cry out for mercy, we plead for Your grace,  
In the midst of the storm, we seek Your face.  

Bridge  
How can You be quiet, when the charge is so clear?  
Your hand upon nations, we tremble in fear.  
Yet we lift our voices, our hearts will declare,  
That You are our fortress, forever we’ll share.  
Let the heavens resound, let the earth be still,  
For the Lord of all hosts, is working His will.  

Chorus

Outro  
Behold, the waters rise, but we shall not fall,  
In the arms of our Savior, we’re safe through it all.  
O Sword of the Lord, our voices we raise,  
In Your mighty presence, we offer our praise.
