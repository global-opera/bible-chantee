[TITLE]
Awaken Us, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Run through the streets, O Lord, we plead,  
Seek for the truth, in our hearts, plant the seed.  
Eyes blinded by lies, our faces like stone,  
In our rebellion, we’ve wandered alone.  

Chorus  
Awaken us, O Lord, in this hour of despair,  
Turn our hearts to You, show us how to care.  
For the broken and lost, let us hear their cry,  
In Your mercy, O Lord, let our hearts testify.  

Verse 2  
The mighty have fallen, their judgment is clear,  
In the silence of night, we can feel You near.  
Yet we’ve chased after idols, forsaken Your ways,  
Fill us with fire, Lord, ignite all our days.  

Chorus  
Awaken us, O Lord, in this hour of despair,  
Turn our hearts to You, show us how to care.  
For the broken and lost, let us hear their cry,  
In Your mercy, O Lord, let our hearts testify.  

Bridge  
You place the bounds of the sea, yet we fear not,  
Your hand holds the heavens, our hearts are distraught.  
Though the waves may roar, they cannot prevail,  
In Your strength, we stand, we will not fail.  

Verse 3  
Declare to the nations, let justice arise,  
As we humble our hearts, let truth be our guide.  
From the ashes of pride, Your glory will shine,  
In the midst of our darkness, let Your light be divine.  

Chorus  
Awaken us, O Lord, in this hour of despair,  
Turn our hearts to You, show us how to care.  
For the broken and lost, let us hear their cry,  
In Your mercy, O Lord, let our hearts testify.  

Outro  
O Lord, we are waiting, our souls long for You,  
In the depths of our hearts, make all things new.  
Awaken us, O Lord, we surrender this night,  
In Your grace, we rise, filled with Your light.
