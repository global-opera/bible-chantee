Verse 1  
In the shadows of a city lost,  
Where the walls once stood so high,  
A king in chains, his heart turned cold,  
Rebelled against the Lord most high.  
Famine struck, the cries rang out,  
Hope was lost beneath the weight,  
But Your mercy, Lord, it never fades,  
In our darkness, You still await.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear my cry,  
In the ruins, we lift our eyes,  
From ashes to glory, You will arise,  
Chains of silence, breaking in the night.  
Our hearts are weary, our spirits low,  
Yet You promise, You’ll never let go.  

Verse 2  
The captives weep by Babylon’s stream,  
Their dreams shattered, their hearts in pain,  
But in the night, a whisper calls,  
"Hold on, beloved, I’ve not forsaken."  
Nebuzaradan, with judgment fierce,  
Yet in the storm, Your love is clear,  
You lift the humble, restore the meek,  
In our brokenness, it’s You we seek.
