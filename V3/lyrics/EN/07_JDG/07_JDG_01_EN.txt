[TITLE]
Faithful in the Fight

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, the children cried,  
"Who shall go up, O Lord, as our guide?"  
He said, "Judah leads, for the battle is mine,  
I've given the land, it will surely be thine."  

Chorus:  
So we rise, with courage in our hearts,  
With faith in the promise, we won’t fall apart.  
Through valleys and mountains, we stand side by side,  
With You, Lord, our victory, our faithful guide.  

Verse 2:  
Judah called Simeon, "Come walk with me,  
Together we'll conquer, together we'll see."  
In Bezek they fought, ten thousand did fall,  
With the strength of the Lord, they answered the call.  

Chorus:  
So we rise, with courage in our hearts,  
With faith in the promise, we won’t fall apart.  
Through valleys and mountains, we stand side by side,  
With You, Lord, our victory, our faithful guide.  

Bridge:  
You’ve given us strength through every trial,  
You’ve shown us the way, each step, each mile.  
In the face of the giants, we’ll lift up our praise,  
For Your hand is upon us, our banners we’ll raise.  

Verse 3:  
Caleb sought courage, a land he desired,  
With springs of water, his heart was inspired.  
Together we’ll battle, no fear in our eyes,  
For our God goes before us, our faith will arise.  

Chorus:  
So we rise, with courage in our hearts,  
With faith in the promise, we won’t fall apart.  
Through valleys and mountains, we stand side by side,  
With You, Lord, our victory, our faithful guide.  

Outro:  
In every struggle, we’ll trust in Your might,  
With our eyes on the promise, we’ll walk in the light.  
For the Lord is our refuge, our hope and our song,  
In battles and trials, to You we belong.
