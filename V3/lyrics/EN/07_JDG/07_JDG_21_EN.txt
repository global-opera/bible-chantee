[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Mizpeh, we gathered in despair,  
Crying out to the heavens, our hearts laid bare.  
"O Lord, why this burden? Why must one tribe be lost?  
In the silence of the night, we count the heavy cost."

Chorus  
Restore, O Lord, what’s broken and lost,  
Bring hope to the weary, no matter the cost.  
Your mercy flows deeper than we can believe,  
In the face of our trials, help us to receive.  

Verse 2  
We built an altar, with offerings in hand,  
Seeking Your favor, united we stand.  
For Benjamin our brother, we long to see,  
A way to bring redemption, a path to unity.

Chorus

Bring hope to the weary, no matter the cost.

Bridge  
In the dance of the daughters, in the joy of the feast,  
We find our salvation, our hearts are released.  
From the depths of our sorrow, to the heights of Your grace,  
You gather the scattered, You lead us in place.

Verse 3  
As we journey in faith, we remember the call,  
To cherish each other, to stand tall.  
Though we falter and stumble, through the trials we face,  
Your love is our anchor, our strength, our embrace.

Chorus

Outro  
In those days, no king, but Your truth will abide,  
Every heart seeking, in You we confide.  
As we leave this place, let us carry the light,  
United in Your love, we will shine through the night.
