[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
From the stem of Jesse, a rod will arise,  
A Branch growing strong, reaching up to the skies.  
The Spirit of wisdom, understanding divine,  
In the fear of the Lord, His glory will shine.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, come, let us see, the promise unfold,  
In the heart of the meek, His righteousness told.  
He will judge with His grace, and the earth will be free,  
In the knowledge of the Lord, like waters, the sea.  

[Verse 2]  
The wolf and the lamb, together they’ll dwell,  
The lion will lie down, peace breaking the spell.  
A child leads them gently, no harm in His way,  
In His holy mountain, all fear will decay.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, come, let us see, the promise unfold,  
In the heart of the meek, His righteousness told.  
He will judge with His grace, and the earth will be free,  
In the knowledge of the Lord, like waters, the sea.  

[Bridge]  
He will gather the lost from the corners of earth,  
An ensign for nations, proclaiming His worth.  
From the ashes, He’ll raise us, in unity stand,  
All the outcasts will rise, held fast in His hand.

[Outro]  
Oh, the root of Jesse, in Him we find peace,  
In the love of our Savior, our troubles will cease.  
With faith as our girdle, let His light shine bright,  
In the glory of His presence, we walk in His light.
