Verse 1:  
In the night, the cries arise,  
From the ruins of Ar and Kir,  
Silent echoes filled with sighs,  
Moab weeps, their hearts in fear.  
Baldness marks the grief they bear,  
Sackcloth drapes the streets they roam,  
From high places, their despair,  
Calling out, they seek their home.

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear our cry,  
For the broken, for the lost,  
In the shadows, we will sigh,  
Count the burdens, know the cost.  
Lift our voices, we lament,  
For the land laid waste and torn,  
In our hearts, a deep lament,  
From the ashes, hope reborn.

Verse 2:  
Heshbon weeps, Elealeh calls,  
Echoes stretch to Jahaz near,  
Soldiers' hearts, like shattered walls,  
Grievous burdens, soaked in tears.  
Fugitives to Zoar flee,  
With the pain of Luhith’s rise,  
In Horonaim, their cries decree,  
Destruction’s shadow fills the skies.

Bridge:  
Desolate waters, where life once flowed,  
Fields lie barren, where dreams erode.  
Yet in mourning, we seek Your grace,  
Revive our hearts, O Lord, embrace.  
With every tear, our souls align,  
In the darkness, Your light will shine.
