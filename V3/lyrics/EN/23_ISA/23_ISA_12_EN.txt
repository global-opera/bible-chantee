[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the day of Your grace, I will lift my voice,  
Though storms may arise, in You I will rejoice.  
Your anger is turned, and Your comfort I find,  
O Lord, my salvation, forever entwined.

Chorus  
I will trust and not be afraid,  
For You are my strength, my song, my aid.  
From the wells of salvation, I draw with joy,  
Praise the Lord, my heart's true employ.

Verse 2  
Behold, You are near, my refuge and light,  
In the midst of the darkness, You shine ever bright.  
I will call on Your name, declare all Your ways,  
Exalted, O Savior, forever I’ll praise.

Chorus  
I will trust and not be afraid,  
For You are my strength, my song, my aid.  
From the wells of salvation, I draw with joy,  
Praise the Lord, my heart's true employ.

Bridge  
Cry out, O Zion, shout with delight,  
For great is the Holy One, our guiding light.  
Sing of His wonders, proclaim His great deeds,  
In the presence of Heaven, our spirits He leads.

Outro  
In the day of His love, we shall all proclaim,  
Great is our God, forever the same.  
With joy we draw near, with hearts open wide,  
In the wells of salvation, our hope will abide.
