[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the halls of the temple, where the banners rise,  
David called the faithful, with hearts open wide.  
Asaph and Jeduthun, Heman stood tall,  
With harps and with cymbals, they answered the call.  

Chorus  
O lift up your voices, let praises resound,  
With every heartbeat, let worship abound.  
For the sons of the faithful, united in song,  
We’ll sing of His glory, our hearts will belong.  

Verse 2  
Zaccur and Joseph, with Nethaniah’s grace,  
In the presence of the Lord, they found their place.  
Together they prophesied, with joy in their hearts,  
In the rhythms of heaven, each soul plays their part.  

Chorus  
O lift up your voices, let praises resound,  
With every heartbeat, let worship abound.  
For the sons of the faithful, united in song,  
We’ll sing of His glory, our hearts will belong.  

Bridge  
From the mighty to the humble, His love is the same,  
With every note we whisper, we call on His name.  
In the silence and the shouting, His presence we seek,  
As we gather together, His spirit we’ll speak.  

Verse 3  
For Heman’s descendants, with strength from above,  
They carried the promise, they carried His love.  
Two hundred and eighty, in harmony's grace,  
A chorus of worship, we seek His embrace.  

Chorus  
O lift up your voices, let praises resound,  
With every heartbeat, let worship abound.  
For the sons of the faithful, united in song,  
We’ll sing of His glory, our hearts will belong.  

Outro  
So let the harps ring out, let the cymbals play,  
With the sons of the faithful, we’ll worship and pray.  
In the house of the Lord, we will stand as one,  
With joy in our hearts, till His kingdom has come.
