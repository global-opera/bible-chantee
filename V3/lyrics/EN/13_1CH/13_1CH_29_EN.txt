[TITLE]
A Heart Prepared for You

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the kingdom, David stood so tall,  
He called upon the people, answered the great call.  
"Solomon, my son, with a tender heart,  
The work is vast, for God, we must take our part."  

(Chorus)  
So we offer, we lay down our gold,  
In praise and in worship, hearts brave and bold.  
All we have, Lord, it comes from Your hand,  
In joy we unite, as one we will stand.  

(Verse 2)  
With treasures from the mountains, and silver from the sea,  
We gather for Your temple, O Lord, with unity.  
Our hearts aligned in purpose, our hands ready to serve,  
For in this holy moment, we give our lives, we yearn.  

(Chorus)  
So we offer, we lay down our gold,  
In praise and in worship, hearts brave and bold.  
All we have, Lord, it comes from Your hand,  
In joy we unite, as one we will stand.  

(Bridge)  
Who are we, O Lord, that we should give so free?  
All things come from You, our hearts in harmony.  
Prepare our hearts, O God, for Your glorious name,  
As we build this house, we will never be the same.  

(Verse 3)  
David blessed the Father, in joy they bowed down,  
With sacrifices given, they wore the victor’s crown.  
And Solomon arose, in Your presence, he’d reign,  
With wisdom and with power, Your glory would sustain.  

(Chorus)  
So we offer, we lay down our gold,  
In praise and in worship, hearts brave and bold.  
All we have, Lord, it comes from Your hand,  
In joy we unite, as one we will stand.  

(Outro)  
O Lord, we seek Your face, in this sacred place,  
With every breath, we worship, in love, we find our grace.  
Forever we will praise You, our hearts forever true,  
In this house that we build, we’re united, Lord, with You.
