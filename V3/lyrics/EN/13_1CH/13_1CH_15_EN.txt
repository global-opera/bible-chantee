[TITLE]
Bring Up the Ark

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the city of David, a place prepared,  
A tent for the presence, a heart laid bare.  
David called the Levites, set apart for His name,  
To carry the glory, in joy we proclaim.  

Chorus  
Bring up the Ark, let our voices rise,  
With instruments of praise, lifting hearts to the skies.  
Shouting with joy, as the covenant comes,  
In the dance of surrender, we’re united as one.  

Verse 2  
Sanctified, we gather, holy and true,  
With joy in our hearts, we come to renew.  
The sound of the trumpets, the cymbals will play,  
Echoing our worship, in a glorious display.  

Chorus  
Bring up the Ark, let our voices rise,  
With instruments of praise, lifting hearts to the skies.  
Shouting with joy, as the covenant comes,  
In the dance of surrender, we’re united as one.  

Bridge  
God helped the Levites, His presence we seek,  
With offerings of love, we bow and we speak.  
Every heart is a vessel, His glory our aim,  
Together we’re singing, forever the same.  

Chorus  
Bring up the Ark, let our voices rise,  
With instruments of praise, lifting hearts to the skies.  
Shouting with joy, as the covenant comes,  
In the dance of surrender, we’re united as one.  

Outro  
In the city of David, let the music flow,  
With hearts full of worship, we’ll let the world know.  
Bring up the Ark, with praises we sing,  
In the presence of God, our souls take wing.
