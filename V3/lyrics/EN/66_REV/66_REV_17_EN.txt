[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the wilderness, I saw a sight,  
A woman clothed in scarlet light,  
With a golden cup, she held so high,  
Filled with abominations, her truth a lie.  
Kings of the earth, in her web they fall,  
Drunk with her wine, they heed her call.  
But the Lamb is here, He stands so tall,  
Lord of lords, He conquers all.

Chorus:  
O Great Mystery, O Babylon,  
Your reign shall end, your power gone.  
The Lamb is coming, with fire and might,  
In the darkness, He brings His light.  
Called and chosen, we stand in grace,  
With hearts ablaze, we seek His face.

Verse 2:  
Seven heads rise upon the beast,  
A tale of woe, a warning feast.  
The nations gather, the kings align,  
But with the Lamb, we shall not decline.  
For the beast that was, and is no more,  
Shall face the wrath of Heaven’s roar.  
In faith we walk, our spirits soar,  
With the faithful, we’ll stand and adore.

Bridge:  
And the waters flow, the nations cry,  
But the Lamb is King, He will not die.  
With ten horns raging, they seek to fight,  
But we are with Him, in the shining light.  
O mighty Savior, our hearts unite,  
In victory's hope, we lift our plight.

So let the heavens declare His fame,
