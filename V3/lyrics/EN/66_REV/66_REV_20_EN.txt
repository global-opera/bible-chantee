[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From heaven’s heights an angel came,  
With chains of light, he called His name,  
The serpent bound, the darkness ceased,  
A thousand years, our hearts released.  
In judgment thrones, the faithful stand,  
With Christ our Lord, forever grand,  
They bore the cross, they laid down all,  
In honor true, they answered the call.  

Chorus  
Hallelujah, sing His praise,  
In the light of eternal days.  
The dragon falls, his power spent,  
In Christ alone, our hearts content.  
Blessed are those who rise anew,  
In the Lamb’s embrace, our hope is true.  
Forever reign, we lift our song,  
In victory’s grace, we all belong.  

Verse 2  
The thousand years, a sacred reign,  
In peace we dwell, free from all pain.  
But lo, the serpent’s time shall come,  
To gather nations, the battle drum.  
Yet fire from heaven, swift and bright,  
Devours the foes in holy light.  
The devil cast where darkness dwells,  
For evermore, his tale repels.
