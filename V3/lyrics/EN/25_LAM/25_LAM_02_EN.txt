[TITLE]
Tears of Zion

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Daughter of Zion, where is your song?  
In the shadow of sorrow, how could this be wrong?  
The beauty of Israel, cast down from the skies,  
In the day of His anger, the heartache arises.  

Chorus  
Let the tears flow like rivers, night and day,  
Lament for the lost, as we bow and pray.  
O Lord, hear our cry, for the children we mourn,  
In the silence of sorrow, a new hope is born.  

Verse 2  
Your strongholds have fallen, the walls lay in shame,  
Our kings among nations, forgotten their name.  
The feasts now forgotten, the altar in dust,  
In the furnace of fury, we place all our trust.  

Chorus  
Let the tears flow like rivers, night and day,  
Lament for the lost, as we bow and pray.  
O Lord, hear our cry, for the children we mourn,  
In the silence of sorrow, a new hope is born.  

Bridge  
Arise, O Lord, and see our plight,  
We lift up our hands in the stillness of night.  
For the young and the old, with hearts filled with pain,  
We call on Your mercy, let grace fall like rain.  

Chorus  
Let the tears flow like rivers, night and day,

Outro  
O Daughter of Zion, your story's not done,  
In the valley of shadows, we look to the Son.  
For every lament, there’s a promise of grace,  
In the heart of the broken, we’ll find His embrace.
