[TITLE]
Covenant of the Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the night, David cried,  
"What have I done, O friend, why must I hide?  
Your father seeks my life, but I stand here,  
With grace in your eyes, banishing fear."  

Chorus:  
O Jonathan, my brother, in this sacred bond,  
We stand together, though the world goes on.  
With arrows swift, our fates entwined,  
In the name of the Lord, our hearts aligned.  

Verse 2:  
As the new moon rises, I must take my leave,  
To hide in the fields, my soul to grieve.  
If peace flows from the king, then all is well,  
But if wrath is kindled, then this tale I’ll tell.  

Chorus:  
O Jonathan, my brother, in this sacred bond,  
We stand together, though the world goes on.  
With arrows swift, our fates entwined,  
In the name of the Lord, our hearts aligned.  

Bridge:  
As you shoot the arrows, let the message fly,  
If they’re beyond you, then I must say goodbye.  
But if they’re near, then peace will reign,  
O Lord, be with us, through joy and pain.  

Verse 3:  
In the fields we stand, with sorrow and with grace,  
A covenant of love, no foe can erase.  
Though the world may turn, and darkness may loom,  
We hold to the promise, in His light, we bloom.  

Chorus:  
O Jonathan, my brother, in this sacred bond,  
We stand together, though the world goes on.  
With arrows swift, our fates entwined,  
In the name of the Lord, our hearts aligned.  

Outro:  
Go in peace, dear friend, our spirits intertwined,  
In the name of the Lord, forever we’ll find,  
Through trials and battles, His love will not part,  
For the covenant we share is the bond of the heart.
