[TITLE]
Wrestling with Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
On the path where shadows loom,  
Jacob walked in fear and gloom,  
But the angels met him there,  
In the night, a whispered prayer.  
"Lord, I'm not worthy of Your grace,  
Yet I seek to see Your face."  
Mahanaim, a place of peace,  
In the midst of doubt, Your love won't cease.  

Chorus  
Wrestling with grace, I hold on tight,  
In the darkness, You are my light.  
Though I stumble, my heart will sing,  
For in the struggle, my soul takes wing.  
You are my refuge, my guiding hand,  
In this journey, I take my stand.  

Verse 2  
With a heart that's filled with dread,  
Jacob prayed for mercy, he said,  
"I fear the wrath of Esau's might,  
Yet I trust in Your promise tonight."  
Two bands he formed, the flocks he laid,  
A humble gift, his fears portrayed.  
“Deliver me, O God of old,  
In Your arms, I find my bold.”
