[TITLE]
The Ladder to Heaven

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the night, beneath a starry sky,  
Jacob wandered, left his home behind,  
With a promise whispered in the dark,  
"Go to the land where your future starts."  
The blessing of a father, a charge to obey,  
He sought a bride in the ancient way.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, You stand above the night,  
A ladder reaching to the light,  
Angels ascending, descending free,  
In this place, Your presence speaks to me.  
You’re the God of Abraham, Isaac, and me,  
In my journey, Lord, I trust in Thee.

Verse 2  
In a dream, I saw the heavens wide,  
The dust of the earth, like stars, multiplied,  
You said, “I’m with you, wherever you roam,  
I will keep you safe, you’re never alone.”  
From the east to the west, my seed shall grow,  
In You, all the families of the earth will know.

Bridge  
Surely, the Lord is in this place,  
A holy ground, a sacred space.  
With stone and oil, my vow I raise,  
In every step, I’ll sing Your praise.  
If You will guide me on this way,  
I’ll trust in You, come what may.

Outro  
With every blessing, my heart will sing,  
To the God of covenant, my offering.  
In this house, I’ll call Your name,  
Forever changed, I’ll never be the same.  
Oh, Lord, You stand above the night,  
In Your arms, I find my light.
