### You, God, See Me

Verse 1  
In the silence of my heart, I wait,  
A promise lost, a dream laid bare,  
Sarai's sorrow, a bitter fate,  
Yet in the shadows, You are there.  
With a handmaid, a plan unfolds,  
In the quiet, doubts arise,  
But Your grace speaks through ages old,  
You hear our cries, You hear our cries.  

Chorus  
You, God, see me, in my despair,  
In the wilderness, I find You there.  
When I am broken, when hope has fled,  
You lift my spirit, You raise the dead.  
You, God, see me, my heart laid bare,  
In every struggle, You’re always near.  

Verse 2  
Hagar's journey, a path unknown,  
Fleeing from the weight of scorn,  
Yet in her flight, she finds a throne,  
The God who sees her, not forlorn.  
"Return," He says, "I will make you whole,  
Your seed shall flourish, like stars above."  
In midst of trials, He speaks to her soul,  
A promise whispered, a heart of love.

Bridge  
In the well of life, my fears subside,  
For You, O Lord, are my faithful guide.  
Ishmael's name, a story told,  
A legacy born, from trials bold.  
You’ve seen my pain, my longing heart,  
In every tear, You play a part.

Outro  
You, God, see me,  
You are my light,  
In every moment,  
You bring the night to light.  
You, God, see me,  
My soul’s refrain,  
In every sorrow,  
I call Your name.
