[TITLE]
In the Garden's Shade

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the garden, where pure light did shine,  
A whisper came from shadows, oh so fine.  
“Yea, hath God said?” the serpent's sly refrain,  
With eyes that saw the fruit, temptation’s gain.  

Chorus  
Oh, the heart that strays from love divine,  
In the depths of sorrow, how we intertwine.  
Yet in our fall, Your grace remains,  
In the garden’s shade, redemption reigns.  

Verse 2  
Eve beheld the beauty, wisdom’s sweet allure,  
Innocence surrendered, hearts were made unsure.  
“Surely not to die,” the serpent’s voice did call,  
In the taste of fruit, they lost it all.  

Chorus  
Oh, the heart that strays from love divine,  
In the depths of sorrow, how we intertwine.  
Yet in our fall, Your grace remains,  
In the garden’s shade, redemption reigns.  

Bridge  
Where are You, Lord? We hide in shame,  
Our eyes are opened, but our hearts feel blame.  
Yet You come walking, in the cool of day,  
With the promise of mercy, You guide our way.  

Verse 3  
To the serpent, You spoke, a curse to bear,  
But in the pain, there’s hope beyond despair.  
With coats of skins, You covered our shame,  
In our darkest hour, we still proclaim Your name.  

Chorus  
Oh, the heart that strays from love divine,  
In the depths of sorrow, how we intertwine.  
Yet in our fall, Your grace remains,  
In the garden’s shade, redemption reigns.  

Outro  
From dust we came, to dust we’ll return,  
Yet in Your love, our hearts will yearn.  
In the garden’s shade, we’ll stand once more,  
Restored in grace, forever to adore.
