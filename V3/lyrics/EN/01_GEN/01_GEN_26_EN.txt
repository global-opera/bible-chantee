[TITLE]
In the Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Gerar, a famine drew near,  
Isaac sought refuge, in the midst of his fear.  
But the Lord spoke gently, “Stay where you are,  
For I am your promise, your bright morning star.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, I will trust in Your ways,  
You’re the God of my fathers, through all of my days.  
In the valleys of struggle, Your blessings will flow,  
In the land of promise, Your love I will know.  

Verse 2  
Isaac sowed in the soil, though the drought was severe,  
One hundredfold harvest, the blessing was clear.  
With flocks overflowing, the nations would see,  
In the seed of Abraham, all hearts would be free.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, I will trust in Your ways,  
You’re the God of my fathers, through all of my days.  
In the valleys of struggle, Your blessings will flow,  
In the land of promise, Your love I will know.  

Bridge  
Digging wells of living water, calling out Your name,  
In the midst of contention, I will praise the same.  
Rehoboth, place of room, where You make me whole,  
Fear not, for I am with you, I’ll bless your soul.  

Verse 3  
When the kings came to Isaac, they saw Your light shine,  
Even in the darkness, Your love is divine.  
A covenant of peace, in the midst of our strife,  
You’re the God of my journey, the source of my life.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, I will trust in Your ways,  
You’re the God of my fathers, through all of my days.  
In the valleys of struggle, Your blessings will flow,

Outro  
So here in this valley, I’ll build an altar strong,  
To worship the One who’s been with me all along.  
In the land of promise, my heart will abide,  
With You, my Redeemer, forever my guide.
