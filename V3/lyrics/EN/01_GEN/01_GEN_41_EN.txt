[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the quiet night, Pharaoh dreamed alone,  
By the river’s edge, a message to be shown.  
Seven kine of grace, fat and full in their prime,  
Yet after them arose, lean ones in their time.  
They devoured the blessed, leaving nothing behind,  
A warning from Heaven, God’s will we must find.

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, You reveal Your plan,  
In dreams and in visions, we trust in Your hand.  
Through seasons of plenty, through famine’s cruel call,  
You guide us with mercy, Your wisdom for all.

[Verse 2]  
Then came ears of grain, ripe upon a stalk,  
But thin ones, withered, rose up, a bitter talk.  
They swallowed the fullness, a shadow of despair,  
Yet Joseph stood ready, to lead with Your care.  
He spoke of the years, of both plenty and need,  
In faith, we gather, for the harvest we heed.

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, You reveal Your plan,  
In dreams and in visions, we trust in Your hand.  
Through seasons of plenty, through famine’s cruel call,  
You guide us with mercy, Your wisdom for all.

[Bridge]  
From the dungeon to the throne, Joseph's rise was swift,  
With the Spirit of God, he was given the gift.  
Prepare for the future, let wisdom take flight,  
For Your hand is upon us, leading us in light.

[Verse 3]  
In the land of Egypt, the grain filled the store,  
With faith in Your promise, we’ll hunger no more.  
For You are our refuge, our hope in the night,  
In every season, Lord, You are our delight.

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, You reveal Your plan,  
In dreams and in visions, we trust in Your hand.  
Through seasons of plenty, through famine’s cruel call,  
You guide us with mercy, Your wisdom for all.

[Outro]  
So we lift our voices, in praise we will stand,  
For the dreams that You give us, are part of Your plan.  
In the midst of our struggles, we’ll call on Your name,  
For You are our Savior, forever the same.
