[TITLE]
Built on the Foundation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of our striving,  
We are called to rise and see,  
Milk no longer, we are yearning,  
For the solid truth to be.  
Divisions and the strife within us,  
Let us lay them down, be free,  
In Your grace, Lord, we are growing,  
In Your love, we find our peace.

Chorus:  
We are built on Christ, our Savior,  
The foundation strong and true,  
Every heart and every labor,  
Is a gift that comes from You.  
In the temple of Your Spirit,  
We are one, united, whole,  
With each stone, we build Your kingdom,  
For Your glory, Lord, our goal.

Verse 2:  
Who are we but servants, fleeting,  
In this world, we play our part,  
Planting seeds of hope and healing,  
Watering with love-filled hearts.  
But it’s You who brings the harvest,  
You alone give life to all,  
In Your hands, we find our purpose,  
In Your name, we heed the call.

Bridge:  
Let the fire reveal our hearts,  
What we’ve built will stand or fall,  
In Your presence, we are changed,  
You are our hope, our all in all.  
No more wisdom of this world,  
In Your truth, we find our way,  
All is Yours, and we are Yours,  
Forever, Lord, we’ll sing Your praise.
