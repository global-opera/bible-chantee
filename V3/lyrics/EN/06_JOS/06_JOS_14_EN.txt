Verse 1  
In the land where courage stood,  
The children gathered, wise and good,  
By the lot, the promise came,  
As the Lord declared His name.  
From the wilderness, they did arise,  
With hearts aflame, they touched the skies,  
Caleb stood, bold and free,  
Claiming what was meant to be.  

Chorus  
Give me this mountain, Lord, I pray,  
With strength renewed, I’ll walk Your way.  
For in Your promise, I will stand,  
With faith unshaken, I take this land.  
Oh, give me this mountain, my heart's desire,  
Your presence fuels this holy fire.  

Verse 2  
Forty years, the journey long,  
Yet in my heart, I’ve stayed so strong.

But I will rise, I will not fall.  
With every step, I’ll trust Your hand,  
For You have led me to this land.
