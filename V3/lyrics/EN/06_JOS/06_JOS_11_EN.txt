[TITLE]
Valley of Victory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, where the kings unite,  
Jabin sent his armies, gathering for the fight.  
But the Lord spoke to Joshua, “Do not fear the night,  
For tomorrow you will see My hand, bringing forth the light.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Valley of Victory, where the battles rage,  
With the sword of the Spirit, we will turn the page.  
Every fear defeated, every stronghold falls,  
In the name of our Savior, we will answer the call.  

Verse 2  
Like the sand upon the seashore, their numbers vast and wide,  
But the Lord stands beside us, as our faithful guide.  
With courage in our hearts, we rise to face the foe,  
For the promise of the heavens is the strength we know.  

Chorus

Bridge  
He houghs their horses, burns their chariots bright,  
As we march in His power, we will conquer the night.  
No enemy can stand against the truth we bear,  
For the Lord of all creation fights with us in prayer.  

Chorus

Outro  
So let the trumpets sound, let the banners fly,  
For the land is ours to claim, under heaven's sky.  
In the Valley of Victory, we will stand and sing,  
The promise of our Father, forever we will cling.
