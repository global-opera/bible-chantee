[TITLE]
Step into the Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the morning light, we rise anew,  
With hearts prepared, we seek Your truth.  
From Shittim’s land to Jordan’s shore,  
We gather as one, to trust You more.  

Pre-Chorus  
The ark of the covenant leads the way,  
A sign of Your presence, we will not stray.  
Sanctify our hearts, Lord, we prepare,  
For wonders await, Your love we’ll share.  

Chorus  
Step into the promise, where waters part,  
With every step, we follow Your heart.  
The living God is among us here,  
In faith we move, casting out fear.  
You guide us through, O Lord of all,  
With You beside us, we will not fall.  

Verse 2  
The priests stand firm on dry ground,  
As we remember, Your love abounds.

You lead us boldly into new life.
