[TITLE]
The Covenant of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of promise, where battles arise,  
All the kings gathered, with fierce battle cries.  
But the Gibeonites came, with wisdom so shrewd,  
In worn-out garments, their hearts were subdued.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, we trust in Your mighty hand,  
In every season, we will take our stand.  
When shadows loom and fear draws near,  
Your covenant of grace, we hold dear.  

Verse 2:  
They spoke of Your wonders, of Egypt's despair,  
Of kings overthrown, and the power You bear.  
"We come from afar," their voices declared,  
With bread that is moldy, their plight they shared.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, we trust in Your mighty hand,  
In every season, we will take our stand.  
When shadows loom and fear draws near,  
Your covenant of grace, we hold dear.  

Bridge:  
But Joshua faltered, he did not seek You,  
And the men took the spoils, unaware of the true.  
Yet still, in Your mercy, You guide our way,  
Your love everlasting, in night and in day.  

Verse 3:  
Three days into journey, the truth came to light,  
Neighbors they were, yet they chose the night.  
A vow had been spoken, in Your name, so true,  
For grace held them close, as they turned anew.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, we trust in Your mighty hand,  
In every season, we will take our stand.  
When shadows loom and fear draws near,  
Your covenant of grace, we hold dear.  

Outro:  
So let us remember, in battles we face,  
The power of mercy, the strength of Your grace.  
For we are Your people, called by Your name,  
In every endeavor, we’ll praise You the same.
