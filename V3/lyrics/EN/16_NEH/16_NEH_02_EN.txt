[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the month of Nisan, my heart was laid bare,  
Before the king, with sorrow, I stood in despair.  
He asked of my countenance, “Why do you grieve?”  
For the city of my fathers, in ruins, I believe.  

Chorus  
Let us rise up and build, with the hand of God upon us,  
In the shadow of the broken walls, we’ll find our strength in trust.  
Though the night may be dark and the foes may conspire,  
With faith as our foundation, we’ll set our hearts on fire.  

Verse 2  
I prayed to the heavens, my heart to the throne,  
“Send me to Judah, let this work be known.”  
With letters from the king, and favor in my sight,  
I journeyed through the night, guided by Your light.  

Chorus

Bridge  
Though they may laugh and scorn,  
Our hearts will not be torn,  
For the God of heaven leads us,  
In His love, we will be reborn.  
Together we will stand,  
In unity we’ll band,  
With courage in our souls,  
In His promise, we will hold.

Outro
