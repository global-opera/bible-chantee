[TITLE]
Restore Us, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the cry of the people, we hear their plea,  
Voices of sorrow, longing to be free.  
Their lands are in bondage, their hearts filled with pain,  
As they cry to the heavens, calling Your name.  

Chorus:  
Restore us, O Lord, to the grace of Your hand,  
Bring back our hope, as we stand on this land.  
With faith in our hearts, we unite in this fight,  
Your love is our strength, our beacon of light.  

Verse 2:  
We’ve mortgaged our futures, our families in chains,  
Yet still we find courage through the trials and pains.  
Like Nehemiah's heart, we rise to defend,  
For the sake of our brothers, let mercy extend.  

Chorus:

In the fear of our God, we will walk in Your way,  
No more shall we burden, no more shall we stray.  
We’ll lift up our voices, proclaiming Your name,  
With hearts full of promise, we’ll never be the same.

Think upon us, Lord, for the good that we've done,
