Verse 1  
In the stillness of the desert,  
Moses kept the flock so near,  
A burning bush before him,  
God’s voice whispered, calm and clear.  
“Draw near to this holy ground,  
Take off your shoes, for I am here.”  
The God of ages calls your name,  
His love will conquer every fear.

Chorus  
I AM, I AM, the God who sees,  
He hears the cries, He brings us peace.  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
We find our hope, we seek Your face.  
From chains of sorrow, You will lead,  
To a land of promise, flowing with seed.

Verse 2  
“I’ve seen the pain, the heavy load,  
The cries of anguish rise like smoke,  
I’ve come to save, to break the chains,  
From Egypt’s grasp, I’ll bring my folk.”  
Moses trembled at Your call,  
“Who am I to stand before the throne?”  
But You said, “I will be with you,  
Fear not, for you are not alone.”
