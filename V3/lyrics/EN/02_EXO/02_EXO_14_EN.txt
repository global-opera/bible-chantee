[TITLE]
The Lord Will Fight for You

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the sea, we stand in fear,  
With the chariots behind us, our hearts unclear.  
Pharaoh's army closing, our hope feels lost,  
But Your voice brings courage, no matter the cost.  

(Chorus)  
Fear not, for the Lord will fight for you,  
Stand still, watch His mighty hand break through.  
With waters divided, He leads us on dry ground,  
In the midst of the storm, His love will be found.  

(Verse 2)  
Moses raised the rod, the winds began to blow,  
The sea parted before us, a path we didn't know.  
Walls of water rising, we step into the light,  
With faith as our anchor, we’ll walk through the night.  

(Chorus)  
Fear not, for the Lord will fight for you,  
Stand still, watch His mighty hand break through.  
With waters divided, He leads us on dry ground,

(Bridge)  
No grave could hold us, no chains can bind,  
With every step forward, His power we find.  
The darkness behind us, the light in our sight,  
You are our salvation, our hope and our might.

(Outro)  
Thus He saved His people, on that fateful day,  
The Egyptians defeated, forever washed away.  
In reverent awe, we lift our voices high,  
For the Lord is our Savior, our hope will never die.
