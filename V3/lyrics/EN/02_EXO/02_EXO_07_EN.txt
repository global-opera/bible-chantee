[TITLE]
Let My People Go

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Egypt's night,  
The cries of Israel rise,  
Moses hears the call of God,  
With courage in his eyes.  
"Go to Pharaoh, speak My name,  
Let My people be set free,"  
With Aaron by his side,  
They stand in faith, they plead.

Chorus  
Let my people go, O Pharaoh,  
See the signs and wonders flow,  
The Lord our God is strong and true,  
He brings His light to break on through.  
In the heart of darkness, hope will grow,  
Let my people go.

Verse 2  
With the rod in Aaron's hand,  
A serpent bold was cast,  
And though the magicians tried,  
Their power could not last.  
Pharaoh's heart, it hardened still,  
He would not heed the plea,  
Yet God would stretch His mighty hand,  
And set His people free.

Chorus  
Let my people go, O Pharaoh,  
See the signs and wonders flow,  
The Lord our God is strong and true,  
He brings His light to break on through.

Bridge  
By rivers turned to blood,  
The Egyptians mourned their fate,  
But in the midst of judgment,  
God's mercy will not wait.  
He calls His children forth,  
To worship on the way,  
In the wilderness we’ll find,  
His promise every day.

Chorus  
Let my people go, O Pharaoh,  
See the signs and wonders flow,

Outro  
Let my people go, let my people sing,  
To the Lord who saves, the joy He brings.  
In the journey, in the fire,  
He is with us, our hearts inspire,  
Let my people go, let freedom flow,  
In His mighty love, we’ll forever know.
