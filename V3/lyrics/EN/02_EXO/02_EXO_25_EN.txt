[TITLE]  
A Sanctuary for You  

[Verse 1]  
Lord, You spoke to Moses, a call from the skies,  
"Gather hearts of the faithful, bring offerings, arise.  
Gold and silver, the finest of threads,  
Craft a place for Your glory, where Your Spirit weds."  

[Chorus]  
In the sanctuary, we gather,  
You dwell among us, O Lord, our banner.  
With hearts wide open, we come and we seek,  
A mercy seat where our souls can speak.  

[Verse 2]  
Shittim wood and gold, a table set so bright,  
Showbread placed before You, a symbol of Your light.  
Candlestick of pure gold, seven flames will burn,  
Illuminating Your promise, our hearts to return.  

[Chorus]  
In the sanctuary, we gather,  
You dwell among us, O Lord, our banner.  
With hearts wide open, we come and we seek,  
A mercy seat where our souls can speak.  

[Bridge]  
Cherubim, they hover, with wings spread in grace,  
In this holy presence, we find our place.  
You meet us at the altar, Your love is our guide,  
In the depths of Your mercy, we will abide.  

[Chorus]  
In the sanctuary, we gather,  
You dwell among us, O Lord, our banner.  
With hearts wide open, we come and we seek,  
A mercy seat where our souls can speak.  

[Outro]  
So let us build a dwelling, for Your name, we prepare,  
In every heart, a temple, Lord, we offer prayer.  
With every gift we bring, may our love shine through,  
Creating a sanctuary, a place just for You.
