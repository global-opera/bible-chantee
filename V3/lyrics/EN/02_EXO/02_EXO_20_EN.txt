[TITLE]
The Voice of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
You brought us out of darkness,  
From bondage set us free,  
Oh, Lord, our God, we worship,  
In reverence we will be.  
No other gods before You,  
Your name we will proclaim,  
With hearts that seek Your mercy,  
We lift up praise in Your name.

Chorus:  
Thunder and lightning, Your glory revealed,  
In the mountain Your presence, our hearts are healed.  
We stand in awe, in fear and grace,  
The voice of the Lord, our sacred space.  
You are our refuge, our rock, our guide,  
In covenant love, forever abide.

Verse 2:  
Remembering the Sabbath,  
In rest we find our peace,  
In six days You created,  
In stillness, let us cease.  
Honor those who guide us,  
With love that never fades,  
In truth, we walk together,  
In every choice we make.

Chorus:  
Thunder and lightning, Your glory revealed,  
In the mountain Your presence, our hearts are healed.  
We stand in awe, in fear and grace,  
The voice of the Lord, our sacred space.  
You are our refuge, our rock, our guide,  
In covenant love, forever abide.

Bridge:  
Oh, help us to remember,  
The words that You have said,  
In kindness and compassion,  
Let love be where we tread.  
No stealing, no false witness,  
In unity we stand,  
With hearts that seek Your justice,  
We follow Your command.

Chorus:  
Thunder and lightning, Your glory revealed,  
In the mountain Your presence, our hearts are healed.  
We stand in awe, in fear and grace,  
The voice of the Lord, our sacred space.  
You are our refuge, our rock, our guide,  
In covenant love, forever abide.

Outro:  
So let us walk in mercy,  
And keep Your ways so true,  
As we gather in Your presence,  
Our hearts belong to You.  
In the voice of the Lord, we find our song,  
In every breath of worship, we will carry on.
