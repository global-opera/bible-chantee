[TITLE]
Let My People Go

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Egypt's night,  
Where the shadows loom so tight,  
Moses stood before the throne,  
With a message, he was shown.  
"Pharaoh, hear the words of God,  
Let His people walk abroad,  
For the signs that I will bring,  
Show the power of our King."

Chorus  
Let my people go,  
That they may serve the Lord,  
In freedom's light, let them arise,  
With hearts restored.  
From the darkness, bring forth grace,  
In the promise of His face,  
Let my people go,  
To worship and embrace.

Verse 2  
The locusts came, a mighty swarm,  
Darkened skies, the earth was torn,  
Yet in Israel, a shining light,  
God’s protection, through the night.  
Pharaoh's heart, it turned to stone,  
He would not let the people roam,  
But in the struggle, truth will stand,  
The Lord will guide with mighty hand.

Bridge

God's hand moves mightily,  
Let His children walk in peace,  
In His love, let chains release.
