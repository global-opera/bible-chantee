[TITLE]
Holy Garments, Sacred Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In colors bright, the blue and gold,  
A tapestry of stories told,  
With scarlet threads, the work was spun,  
For Aaron's heart, the chosen one.  
As commanded, faith was sewn,  
In every stitch, His love was shown.  

Chorus:  
Holy garments, draped in grace,  
Cloaked in mercy, we seek Your face.  
With every thread, Your promise stands,  
We wear Your love, O Lord, in our hands.  

Verse 2:  
The onyx stones, engraved with care,  
A memorial for those who dare,  
To bear the names of lives redeemed,  
In Your presence, we are esteemed.  
With every row, the stones align,  
As blessings flow from heart to shrine.  

Chorus:  
Holy garments, draped in grace,  
Cloaked in mercy, we seek Your face.  
With every thread, Your promise stands,  
We wear Your love, O Lord, in our hands.  

Bridge:  
A bell and pomegranate ring,  
In sacred rhythm, praises sing.  
With linen fine, we stand restored,  
In unity, we seek our Lord.  
As Moses blessed, we lift our voice,  
In covenant love, we now rejoice.  

Chorus:  
Holy garments, draped in grace,  
Cloaked in mercy, we seek Your face.  
With every thread, Your promise stands,  
We wear Your love, O Lord, in our hands.  

Outro:  
Thus all was done, as You decreed,  
In faithfulness, we take our lead.  
With hearts aligned, we’ll serve and stand,  
In holy garments, hand in hand.
