[TITLE]
Rooted in Love

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the depths of our hearts, we gather near,  
Knit together in love, casting out fear.  
With treasures of wisdom, You fill our souls,  
In You, Lord Jesus, our spirit is whole.  

[Chorus]  
Rooted and built up, we walk in Your grace,  
Abounding in thankfulness, we seek Your face.  
Though trials may come, our faith will not sway,  
For in You, Christ Jesus, we find our way.  

[Verse 2]  
Though absent in flesh, still present in heart,  
Your joy and Your peace will never depart.  
Beware of the voices that lead us astray,  
For in You, all fullness, we find our stay.  

[Chorus]  
Rooted and built up, we walk in Your grace,  
Abounding in thankfulness, we seek Your face.  
Though trials may come, our faith will not sway,  
For in You, Christ Jesus, we find our way.  

[Bridge]  
You’ve blotted our sins, with love You have won,  
Triumphing over the darkness, the victory’s done.  
Let not judgments bind us, nor traditions confine,  
We’re complete in Your body, in Your love we shine.  

[Chorus]  
Rooted and built up, we walk in Your grace,  
Abounding in thankfulness, we seek Your face.  
Though trials may come, our faith will not sway,  
For in You, Christ Jesus, we find our way.  

[Outro]  
In the mystery of God, our hearts now believe,  
In love we are gathered, in You we receive.  
With each breath we take, we rise from the grave,  
Rooted in Your love, forever we’ll save.
