[TITLE]
Letters of the Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of our hearts,  
A story written deep within,  
Not by ink, but by Your grace,  
Lord, Your Spirit makes us whole again.  
We are known and read by all,  
Your love, our truth, we answer the call,  
Ministers of the new covenant light,  
In Your presence, darkness takes flight.

Chorus:  
Where the Spirit of the Lord is,  
There is liberty, we are free,  
From glory to glory, we behold,  
Your transforming power in me.  
Oh, let the veil be lifted high,  
As we turn our hearts to You,  
In Your love, we find our life,  
Letters of Christ, made anew.

Verse 2:  
No more chains of the old decree,  
In the shadow, we were blind,  
But now You shine, the truth revealed,  
In Your arms, our hearts unwind.  
With great boldness, we declare,  
The hope that dwells, forever near,  
For the glory that once was veiled,  
Is now our song, our hearts unveiled.

Chorus:  
Where the Spirit of the Lord is,

Bridge:  
Oh, Lord, we gaze upon Your face,  
Changed by grace, in this holy place,  
Open hearts, Your glory shines,  
In every moment, Your love defines.  
Here we stand, with open eyes,  
Transformed by You, we rise, we rise.

Outro:  
In the silence of our hearts,  
Your story lives, forevermore,  
Written not in stone, but love,  
In every life, Your grace restores.
