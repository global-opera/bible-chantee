[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the kingdom of shadows, a threat arose,  
Haman's dark plot, the enemy chose.  
Esther, with courage, fell at the king's feet,  
With tears in her eyes, she cried for our defeat.  

Chorus:  
Oh, King Ahasuerus, hear our plea,  
For the fate of our people, set us free!  
In your name, we rise, in your light we stand,  
With the seal of the king, we reclaim our land.  

Verse 2:  
The scepter is raised, a sign of Your grace,  
A promise of safety in this sacred place.  
Mordecai’s honor, the house of shame,  
Turned into a blessing, we’ll praise Your name.  

Chorus:  
Oh, King Ahasuerus, hear our plea,  
For the fate of our people, set us free!  
In your name, we rise, in your light we stand,  
With the seal of the king, we reclaim our land.  

Bridge:  
Joy and gladness, a feast in the night,  
The darkness is broken by Your holy light.  
From every province, together we'll sing,  
With a heart full of hope, we lift up our King!  

Chorus:  
Oh, King Ahasuerus, hear our plea,  
For the fate of our people, set us free!  
In your name, we rise, in your light we stand,  
With the seal of the king, we reclaim our land.  

Outro:  
So let the world witness, let all hear the sound,  
Of the joy of the righteous, in victory found.  
For in You, we find our strength and our song,  
In the heart of the battle, we know we belong.
