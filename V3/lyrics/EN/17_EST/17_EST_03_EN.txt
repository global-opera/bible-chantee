[TITLE]
Stand in Faith

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the halls of power, a decree was made,  
Haman rose in honor, while darkness cast its shade.  
But Mordecai stood firm, his heart held fast,  
For he knew his God, His promise shall outlast.  

Chorus  
Stand in faith, though the world may bow,  
We will rise, for our God is with us now.  
No fear can shake, no doubt can sway,  
In the face of the storm, we will trust and pray.  

Verse 2  
With anger in his heart, Haman sought to scheme,  
To destroy the chosen, to silence hope’s dream.  
But God’s hand is mighty, His plan will prevail,  
For through our trials, His love will never fail.
