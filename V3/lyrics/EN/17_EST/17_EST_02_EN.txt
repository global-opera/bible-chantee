[TITLE]
Esther's Call

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the palace of Shushan, a maiden so fair,  
Chosen among many, with beauty laid bare.  
Mordecai watched closely, his heart filled with care,  
For Esther, his daughter, God’s favor she’d share.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, rise up, dear Esther, with courage so bold,  
For such a time as this, your story unfolds.  
In trials and shadows, your faith will ignite,  
God’s hand is upon you, shining His light.  

[Verse 2]  
Twelve months of preparation, in oil and in grace,  
She entered the king’s chambers, to find her true place.  
With wisdom and kindness, she sought not her own,  
In silence she stood, as the queen was enthroned.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, rise up, dear Esther, with courage so bold,  
For such a time as this, your story unfolds.  
In trials and shadows, your faith will ignite,  
God’s hand is upon you, shining His light.  

[Bridge]  
When the whispers of danger arose in the night,  
You spoke for your people, bringing truth to the light.  
With Mordecai beside you, the plan was made clear,  
In the face of the battle, you conquered your fear.  

[Verse 3]  
A banquet of glory, a feast for the king,  
In the midst of the struggle, God’s favor did bring.  
With wisdom and courage, you stood firm and tall,  
For such a time as this, you answered the call.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, rise up, dear Esther, with courage so bold,  
For such a time as this, your story unfolds.  
In trials and shadows, your faith will ignite,  
God’s hand is upon you, shining His light.  

[Outro]  
So let us remember, in the battles we face,  
Like Esther, we’re chosen, by God’s boundless grace.  
With hearts full of courage, we’ll answer the call,  
For such a time as this, we stand strong for all.
