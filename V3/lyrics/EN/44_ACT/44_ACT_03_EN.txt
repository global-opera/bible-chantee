[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the temple, at the hour of prayer,  
A man was waiting, burdened by despair.  
Lame from birth, he sought a hand,  
But silver and gold, they could not stand.  

Pre-Chorus  
Then Peter and John, with eyes full of grace,  
Said, “Look upon us, behold His face.”  
In the name of Jesus, rise up and walk,  
With faith in His power, let your heart talk.  

Chorus  
Rise up and walk, leap with joy,  
Praising the One who came to restore.  
In every heart, let hope ignite,  
The God of miracles shines in the night.  

Verse 2  
He took him by the hand, lifted him high,  
Feet found their strength, his spirit did fly.  
Walking and leaping, he danced in the light,  
All who witnessed were filled with delight.  

Pre-Chorus  
“Why do you marvel? It’s not our might,  
It’s Jesus the Savior, the Truth, and the Light.  
Through faith in His name, this man stands whole,  
The Prince of Life heals every soul.”  

Chorus  
Rise up and walk, leap with joy,  
Praising the One who came to restore.  
In every heart, let hope ignite,  
The God of miracles shines in the night.  

Bridge  
Repent and be changed, let your burdens fall,  
In the presence of Jesus, we hear His call.  
Times of refreshing, He pours out His grace,  
Turning our hearts, we seek His face.  

Chorus  
Rise up and walk, leap with joy,  
Praising the One who came to restore.  
In every heart, let hope ignite,  
The God of miracles shines in the night.  

Outro  
So rise up and walk, let your spirit soar,  
In the name of Jesus, forevermore.  
We are witnesses to His great love,  
Our hearts united, we lift Him above.
