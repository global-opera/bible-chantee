[LYRICS]
[TITLE] "On the Shore We Pray"

[Verse 1]  
From the shores of Tyre we rise,  
With hearts prepared to sail the skies,  
Through the waves and winds we find,  
Your Spirit guiding, love entwined.  
Disciples gather, hands held high,  
For every tear, we lift a cry,  
In unity, our voices blend,  
Together, Lord, on You we depend.

[Chorus]  
On the shore we pray, let Your will be done,  
As we journey forth, united as one.  
With courage in our hearts, we will follow Your way,  
Oh Lord, we trust in You, on this blessed day.

[Verse 2]  
In the house of Philip, we abide,  
With prophesies, the truth our guide,  
Agabus speaks, a warning clear,  
Yet Paul stands strong, without a fear.  
For the name of Jesus, he’ll go forth,  
To Jerusalem, proclaiming Your worth,  
Though trials come, we stand as one,  
In Your embrace, our race is run.

[Chorus]  
On the shore we pray, let Your will be done,  
As we journey forth, united as one.  
With courage in our hearts, we will follow Your way,  
Oh Lord, we trust in You, on this blessed day.

[Bridge]  
Through the storms and every fight,  
We will shine Your love, bring forth the light.  
In every heart, let hope arise,  
For in Your name, we will not compromise.

[Chorus]  
On the shore we pray, let Your will be done,  
As we journey forth, united as one.  
With courage in our hearts, we will follow Your way,  
Oh Lord, we trust in You, on this blessed day.

[Outro]  
As we sail from shore to shore,  
With every step, we seek You more.  
In every nation, every land,  
We’ll carry forth, Your love we'll stand.  
On the shore we pray, let Your will be done,  
Oh Lord, we trust in You, our journey's just begun.
