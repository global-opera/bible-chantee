Verse 1:  
O Lord, we hear the whisper,  
Of judgment coming near,  
Against the pride of Edom,  
Their hearts consumed by fear.  
In the heights they built their fortress,  
But You will bring them low,  
For every act of violence,  
Your justice shall bestow.

Chorus:  
The day of the Lord is coming,

Your people will be free.  
No more shall they be scattered,  
In unity we stand,  
For the kingdom's Yours forever,  
With mercy in Your hand.
