[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
On the eighth day, a call was made,  
Moses spoke the words of grace,  
“Bring forth offerings, pure and whole,  
Before the Lord, we seek His face.  
A calf for sin, a ram for praise,  
In sacrifice, His love displayed.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the fire fall, let Your glory shine,  
We stand before You, Lord, our hearts entwined.  
With arms lifted high, we lift our song,  
In the presence of our God, where we belong.  

[Verse 2]  
The people gathered, hearts aligned,  
Before the altar, their faith combined.  
Aaron took the blood, the offering laid,  
In obedience, their sins were paid.  
With every breath, they sought His grace,  
For in His love, we find our place.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the fire fall, let Your glory shine,  
We stand before You, Lord, our hearts entwined.  
With arms lifted high, we lift our song,  
In the presence of our God, where we belong.  

[Bridge]  
And as the smoke rose, the heavens wept,  
The glory of the Lord, in silence kept.  
A fire from heaven consumed the night,  
The people fell down in holy light.  
In awe of the Lamb, our sins He bore,  
Forever we’ll praise, forever adore.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the fire fall, let Your glory shine,  
We stand before You, Lord, our hearts entwined.  
With arms lifted high, we lift our song,  
In the presence of our God, where we belong.  

[Outro]  
So let us gather, let our voices rise,  
In worship and wonder, beneath the skies.  
For in His presence, we are made whole,  
The glory of the Lord, our hearts console.
