[TITLE]
Atonement of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of my heart, I hear the call,  
A whisper of the truth, I’ve stumbled and I’ve fallen.  
With unclean hands, I stand before the light,  
Hidden flaws exposed, in the presence of Your might.  

Chorus  
Lord, I come, confessing all my sin,  
A sacrifice I bring, to be made whole again.  
In the shadow of Your grace, I find my place,  
With a humble heart, I seek Your face.  

Verse 2  
If I’ve touched the unclean, or strayed from Your way,  
I’ll bring my offering, and kneel to pray.  
A lamb or dove, for the wrongs that I have done,  
You see my heart, the battle I have won.  

Chorus  
Lord, I come, confessing all my sin,  
A sacrifice I bring, to be made whole again.  
In the shadow of Your grace, I find my place,  
With a humble heart, I seek Your face.  

Bridge  
You’ve promised mercy, in my ignorance I’ve strayed,  
Yet in Your kindness, my debts are all repaid.  
The blood of the Lamb, it washes me anew,  
Forever I am free, in the love that comes from You.  

Chorus  
Lord, I come, confessing all my sin,  
A sacrifice I bring, to be made whole again.  
In the shadow of Your grace, I find my place,  
With a humble heart, I seek Your face.  

Outro  
So I’ll lift my voice, and sing of what You’ve done,  
In Your arms of mercy, I am now Your son.  
With every breath I take, I’ll honor Your name,  
For in the atonement, I will never be the same.
