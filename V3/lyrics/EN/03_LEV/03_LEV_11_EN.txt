[TITLE]
Set Apart for You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
You spoke to Moses with Your light,  
“Tell My people, hear My call,  
Of clean and unclean, I guide them all.”  
The hoof that parts, the cud that chews,  
In Your wisdom, Lord, we choose.  

Chorus  
Set apart, O Lord, we long to be,  
In holiness and truth, we come to Thee.  
You are our God, we follow Your way,  
In every step, in all we say.  

Verse 2  
From the waters, You declare,  
Fins and scales, a sacred care,  
All that creeps, unclean to touch,  
Yet in Your grace, we are loved so much.  
Eagles soaring, ravens in flight,  
You teach us, Lord, to seek the light.  

Chorus  
Set apart, O Lord, we long to be,  
In holiness and truth, we come to Thee.  
You are our God, we follow Your way,  
In every step, in all we say.  

Bridge  
“For I am holy,” You speak to our hearts,  
From the land of Egypt, You’ve set us apart.  
Sanctify our lives, make us anew,  
In every moment, we turn to You.  

Verse 3  
Creeping things, they lead us astray,  
But Your love guides us, shows us the way.  
With every breath, we’ll praise Your name,  
In our hearts, we’ll never be the same.  
For You are holy; we lift our eyes,  
In Your presence, our spirits rise.  

Chorus  
Set apart, O Lord, we long to be,  
In holiness and truth, we come to Thee.  
You are our God, we follow Your way,  
In every step, in all we say.  

Outro  
So we stand in awe, in reverence true,  
In all our lives, we are set apart for You.  
In every choice, in every plea,  
We are Yours, Lord, eternally.
