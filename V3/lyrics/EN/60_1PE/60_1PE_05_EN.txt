[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Your grace, we gather here with humble hearts,  
Elders guiding, hands extended, in this love, we play our part.  
Feed the flock, we hear the call, with willing hearts, we stand so tall,  
Not for gain, but in Your name, we share Your truth, we share Your fame.

Chorus  
Oh, Chief Shepherd, hear our cry,  
In Your presence, we abide.  
With every care, we lay it down,  
In Your arms, we find our crown.  
Crown of glory, shining bright,  
In Your love, we find our light.

Verse 2  
You resist the proud, we come in meekness,  
Clothed in humility, we seek Your sweetness.  
Casting burdens, every fear,  
For You care, Lord, You are near.  
With vigilance, we guard our way,  
Strengthened in faith, we shall not sway.

Bridge  
After suffering, You perfect us,  
Establish, strengthen, in You we trust.  
To You be glory, forevermore,  
In this grace, we stand and soar.
