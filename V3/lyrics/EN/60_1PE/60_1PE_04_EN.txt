[TITLE]
Suffered for Our Gain

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, You bore our pain,  
Suffering in the flesh, You took our shame.  
No longer bound by sin, we rise again,  
To walk in Your will, our hearts You reclaim.  

Chorus  
We rejoice in trials, for we’re part of Your name,  
In the shadows of suffering, we find our praise.  
Glory revealed, joy in the flame,  
Lord, we trust in Your heart, through all of our days.  

Verse 2  
With fervent love, let our hearts entwine,  
Cover our sins, with grace that aligns.  
As we serve each other, let Your light shine,  
Good stewards of Your grace, in the sacred design.  

Chorus  
We rejoice in trials, for we’re part of Your name,  
In the shadows of suffering, we find our praise.  
Glory revealed, joy in the flame,  
Lord, we trust in Your heart, through all of our days.  

Bridge  
When the world calls us strange, we won’t be dismayed,  
For in every reproach, Your glory displayed.  
With the Spirit of grace, resting on us still,  
We glorify You, Lord, in surrendering will.  

Chorus  
We rejoice in trials, for we’re part of Your name,  
In the shadows of suffering, we find our praise.  
Glory revealed, joy in the flame,  
Lord, we trust in Your heart, through all of our days.  

Outro  
So let us not be ashamed, but lift high Your name,  
In suffering and grace, we are never the same.  
To You we commit, our souls in Your hands,  
Our faithful Creator, forever we stand.
