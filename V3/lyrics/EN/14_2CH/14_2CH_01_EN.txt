[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the throne, where mercy reigns,  
Solomon stood, his heart aflame,  
With a people vast, like grains of sand,  
He sought not gold, but Your guiding hand.  

Chorus  
Grant us wisdom, Lord, we pray,  
To lead our hearts in Your holy way.  
In the silence, hear our plea,  
Guide us, Lord, in unity.  

Verse 2  
At Gibeon’s heights, the altar stood,  
With offerings pure, as David’s would.  
Your promise whispered through the night,  
“Ask of Me, and I’ll bring forth light.”  

Chorus  
Grant us wisdom, Lord, we pray,  
To lead our hearts in Your holy way.  
In the silence, hear our plea,  
Guide us, Lord, in unity.  

Bridge  
For riches fade and honor's vain,  
But wisdom reigns through joy and pain.  
Let our hearts be ever true,  
In all we do, we seek You.  

Verse 3  
Oh, mighty King, with chariots bright,  
You bless the humble, and lead the fight.  
As silver flows like rivers wide,  
In Your presence, Lord, we abide.  

Chorus  
Grant us wisdom, Lord, we pray,  
To lead our hearts in Your holy way.  
In the silence, hear our plea,  
Guide us, Lord, in unity.  

Outro  
So here we stand, united strong,  
In Your wisdom, we belong.  
With every step, we'll trust and see,  
The path You’ve laid, our destiny.
