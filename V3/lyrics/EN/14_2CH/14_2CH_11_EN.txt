[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of Jerusalem,  
Where kings and warriors dwell,  
Rehoboam stood with strength in hand,  
To fight for what was his to tell.  
But the word of the Lord came down,  
From Shemaiah, strong and true,  
“Do not rise against your brethren now,  
For this battle is not for you.”

Chorus:  
Return to your home, oh faithful heart,  
In the arms of the Father, we shall not part.  
For the Lord is our refuge, our strength in the storm,  
With love that unites, His grace keeps us warm.

Verse 2:  
They obeyed the voice of God,  
Turned away from strife and pain,  
Fortified cities rose in Judah,  
With shields and spears to remain.  
Through valleys deep and mountains high,  
The Levites came from every land,  
To seek the Lord, to sacrifice,  
In unity, together we stand.

Chorus:  
Return to your home, oh faithful heart,  
In the arms of the Father, we shall not part.  
For the Lord is our refuge, our strength in the storm,  
With love that unites, His grace keeps us warm.

Bridge:  
Oh, Lord, we build our hearts on You,  
With every city, every praise we raise anew.  
In every trial, we find our way,  
In the light of Your presence, we shall stay.

Chorus:  
Return to your home, oh faithful heart,  
In the arms of the Father, we shall not part.  
For the Lord is our refuge, our strength in the storm,  
With love that unites, His grace keeps us warm.

Outro:  
So let us gather, let us sing,  
In harmony, let our praises ring.  
For He is our fortress, our guiding light,  
In the heart of His people, we find our might.  
Return to your home, we will not fight,  
In love and in faith, we walk in His light.
