Verse 1  
In the days of Solomon, a vision came to be,  
Twenty years of labor, the house built for Thee.  
Walls of strength and glory, gates that stand so tall,  
Cities raised in honor, answering Your call.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord of all creation, we lift our hands in praise,  
For in Your house we gather, our voices we will raise.  
From the mountains to the valleys, our hearts forever true,  
In the house of the Lord, we worship You.
