[TITLE]
Rest in Your Name

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, a king arose,  
Asa led with a heart that knows,  
He sought You, Lord, with all his might,  
Tore down the idols, brought back the light.  
In the city of David, peace was found,  
For ten years, Your grace did abound.  
With walls built strong, in faith they stood,  
Trusting in You, their God so good.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, we cry out, our hearts in Your name,  
In battles we face, You never change.  
With faith as our shield, we stand in Your might,  
Resting in You, our guiding light.  
O Lord, we trust, You are our defense,  
In every struggle, we find confidence.  
We seek You, Lord, our refuge and fame,  
In Your mighty name, we rise again.  

Verse 2:  
When Zerah came with numbers vast,  
Asa called out, his heart steadfast.  
“Lord, it’s nothing for You to save,  
With many or few, our souls You crave.”  
He faced the odds, with courage bold,  
In the valley of Zephathah, his story told.  
You smote the foe, made the mighty flee,  
For in Your power, we are set free.

Bridge:  
So let us build, as the land finds rest,  
With walls and gates, in You we’re blessed.  
In every city, Your glory we’ll share,  
For You are with us, we know You care.  
As we gather spoils from battles won,  
Our hearts will sing of the victory done.  
In every moment, in trials we face,  
We seek Your presence, we seek Your grace.
