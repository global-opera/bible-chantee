[TITLE]
The House of Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the heart of Moriah, where the ancients trod,  
Solomon built a temple, a dwelling place for God.  
With every stone and pillar, a vision came alive,  
A haven for the weary, where the spirit can thrive.  

Chorus  
Oh, we lift our hearts in praise,  
To the house of glory, where His presence stays.  
With gold and precious stones, in radiant light,  
We gather in His name, our souls ignited bright.  

Verse 2  
Cherubim in splendor, their wings spread wide,  
Guardians of the Holy, in His love we abide.  
The vail of blue and crimson, woven with care,  
Invites us to the sacred, to the depths of prayer.

Bridge  
Jachin and Boaz, the pillars stand tall,  
Strength and beauty in His house, we answer the call.  
With chains of grace surrounding, His love is our guide,  
In this temple of worship, forever we abide.

Outro  
So come to the altar, let your burdens be known,  
In the house of our Savior, you’re never alone.  
With every breath we offer, in reverence we sing,  
To the God of creation, our hearts forever cling.
