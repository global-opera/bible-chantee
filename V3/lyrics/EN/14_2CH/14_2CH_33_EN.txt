[TITLE]
From Darkness to Light

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Twelve years young, a king on the throne,  
Manasseh ruled where the light had shone.  
Yet he turned from the path of grace,  
Built high places, lost in disgrace.  
Idols rose in the sacred halls,  
As the echoes of heaven's call,  
Fell silent to the weight of sin,  
O Lord, where do we begin?

Chorus  
From darkness to light, we cry out in prayer,  
Restore us, O Lord, show us Your care.  
In our weakness, Your mercy will shine,  
Turn our hearts back, make us aligned.  
In the valley, we seek Your face,  
With humbled hearts, we find our place.

Verse 2  
Through the thorns, in chains he was bound,  
In his despair, Your mercy found.  
He called on You, with tears of regret,  
And You heard him, Your love was set.  
You brought him back from the depths of despair,  
To repair the altar, to lay his heart bare.  
Idols cast out, the worship restored,  
A nation united, to sing of the Lord.

Your grace abounds, Your love will fulfill.
