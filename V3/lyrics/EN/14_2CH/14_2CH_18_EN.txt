[TITLE]
The Word of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of kings, when shadows fell,  
Jehoshaphat sought the Lord, all would be well.  
With riches and honor, he stood side by side,  
But wisdom called out, “In God, you must abide.”  

Chorus  
Speak, O Lord, Your truth, we long to hear,  
In the battles we face, cast aside our fear.  
With every promise, we stand and proclaim,  
For Your Word is our strength, and forever the same.  

Verse 2  
Ahab summoned the prophets, four hundred strong,  
“Go, and prosper!” they sang, but was it wrong?  
Yet Jehoshaphat asked for a voice of the true,  
“Micaiah speaks wisdom; let us hear from You.”  

Chorus  
Speak, O Lord, Your truth, we long to hear,  
In the battles we face, cast aside our fear.  
With every promise, we stand and proclaim,  
For Your Word is our strength, and forever the same.  

Bridge  
Though the world may rise, with voices in deceit,  
The heart of the Lord, our souls will not retreat.  
For the shepherd knows, and calls us by name,  
In the fiercest of fights, we’ll never be the same.  

Verse 3  
Micaiah stood boldly, declaring the fate,  
“Israel scattered, their hearts would await.”  
Yet Ahab, in anger, cast him aside,  
But the truth of the Lord, forever will abide.  

Chorus  
Speak, O Lord, Your truth, we long to hear,  
In the battles we face, cast aside our fear.  
With every promise, we stand and proclaim,  
For Your Word is our strength, and forever the same.  

Outro  
So we march into battle, with faith as our guide,  
For the Lord is our shield, in Him we confide.  
With every step taken, we trust in His way,  
For the Word of the Lord will lead us each day.  
Amen, Amen, we’ll follow Your call,  
In the heart of the fight, we will stand tall.
