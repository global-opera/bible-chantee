[TITLE]
The Call to Return

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, a king did rise,  
Ahaz, he turned from truth, to earthly ties.  
He walked in shadows, made idols of stone,  
In the valley of Hinnom, he burned his own.  

Pre-Chorus:  
But the cries of the faithful, echoed through the land,  
A prophet stood up, with a righteous stand.  
“Return to the Lord, forsake your own way,  
For His fierce wrath is upon us today.”  

Chorus:  
O return, O return, to the God of our fathers,  
Lay down your burdens, let His mercy cover.  
He’s calling the captives, He’s healing the broken,  
In His arms of love, all His words are spoken.  

Verse 2:  
The kings of the nations brought trouble and strife,  
But the Lord was grieved by the taking of life.  
A remnant arose, they fought for the lost,  
“Do not add to our sins, count the cost.”  

Pre-Chorus:  
For God in His kindness, He hears our plea,  
He gathers the weary, restores our decree.  
“Clothe the naked, feed the weak,  
For in their kindness, it’s His grace we seek.”  

Chorus:  
O return, O return, to the God of our fathers,  
Lay down your burdens, let His mercy cover.  
He’s calling the captives, He’s healing the broken,  
In His arms of love, all His words are spoken.  

Bridge:  
Ahaz turned from the light, to darkness he fled,  
He sacrificed to false gods; in ruin, he tread.  
But there’s hope in the ashes, a promise to see,  
For God still reigns, setting captives free.  

Chorus:  
O return, O return, to the God of our fathers,  
Lay down your burdens, let His mercy cover.  
He’s calling the captives, He’s healing the broken,  
In His arms of love, all His words are spoken.  

Outro:  
So we lift up our voices, in worship and song,  
For the Lord is our refuge, to Him we belong.  
O return, O return, to the God of our fathers,  
In His grace and His mercy, our hearts He gathers.
