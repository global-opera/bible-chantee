[TITLE]
The Rise and Fall of Uzziah

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Judah, a young king arose,  
Sixteen years of age, with a heart that knows.  
He built up the cities, restored the land,  
With Zechariah’s wisdom, he took his stand.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
He sought the Lord, His face to find,  
In days of grace, his heart aligned.  
God made him prosper, his name spread wide,  
In battles fought, the Lord was his guide.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Uzziah, rise, in the strength of His might,  
But beware the pride that blinds the light.  
For glory and power can lead us astray,  
Seek Him in humility, walk in His way.  

[Verse 2]  
He fortified towers, built walls that stood tall,  
A mighty host of warriors, ready for the call.  
Yet in his success, his heart turned away,  
He entered the temple, where he should not stay.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
The priests stood firm, with courage and grace,  
“Uzziah, oh king, you must leave this place.”  
In anger he burned incense before the Lord,  
But leprosy struck, his pride was ignored.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Uzziah, rise, in the strength of His might,  
But beware the pride that blinds the light.  
For glory and power can lead us astray,  
Seek Him in humility, walk in His way.  

[Bridge]  
In the quiet of judgment, he was cast away,  
A leper in sorrow, lost in dismay.  
Yet even in darkness, a lesson remains,  
To walk with the humble, and know His reign.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Uzziah, rise, in the strength of His might,  
But beware the pride that blinds the light.  
For glory and power can lead us astray,  
Seek Him in humility, walk in His way.  

[Outro]  
So let us remember the tale of the king,  
In seeking the Lord, let our hearts take wing.  
For strength in His presence, a life set apart,  
In humility’s wisdom, we’ll guard our hearts.  
Amen.
