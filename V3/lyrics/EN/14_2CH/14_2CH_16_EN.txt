[TITLE]
Eyes of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the reign of Asa, a shadow fell,  
Baasha of Israel, a tale to tell.  
He built up Ramah, to keep us confined,  
But our God is faithful, His love is unlined.  

Chorus  
Oh, the eyes of the Lord, they roam through the earth,  
Seeking the hearts that know His true worth.  
In battles and struggles, in trials we face,  
We’ll trust in His promise, we’re held by His grace.  

Verse 2  
With silver and gold, Asa sought a plan,  
He called on the king, the Syrian man.  
But the seer spoke boldly, a message divine,  
“Trust not in the flesh, for the Lord is your sign.”  

Chorus  
Oh, the eyes of the Lord, they roam through the earth,  
Seeking the hearts that know His true worth.  
In battles and struggles, in trials we face,  
We’ll trust in His promise, we’re held by His grace.  

Bridge  
Were not the great hosts, the chariots vast?  
Yet with faith in the Lord, the foes were surpassed.  
Hearts turned to Him, in the midst of our fight,  
His power is present, our hope is in sight.  

Verse 3  
Asa grew weary, in the trials he faced,  
Yet sought not the Lord, but the healers in haste.  
His story is written, a lesson for all,  
To lean on our Savior, lest we stumble and fall.  

Chorus  
Oh, the eyes of the Lord, they roam through the earth,  
Seeking the hearts that know His true worth.  
In battles and struggles, in trials we face,  
We’ll trust in His promise, we’re held by His grace.  

Outro  
So let us remember, in all that we do,  
To seek the Lord’s heart, and His strength will renew.  
For the eyes of the Lord, they never depart,  
He’s with us forever, He knows every heart.
