[TITLE]
Jehoshaphat's Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Judah, where the faithful stand,  
A king arose, with a heart so grand.  
Jehoshaphat, he sought the Lord,  
Walking in the ways of David, his sword.  

Pre-Chorus:  
Strengthened by the Father, he took his stand,  
Casting out the idols, reclaiming the land.  
The fear of God fell on nations around,  
In His mighty presence, no enemy found.  

Chorus:  
Lifted high, our voices raise,  
In the light of His glorious ways.  
With every heart, we seek Your throne,  
In You, O Lord, we find our home.  

Verse 2:  
He taught the people with the Law in hand,  
Levites and priests, spreading truth across the land.  
Riches and honor were laid at his feet,  
In the love of the Father, his spirit complete.  

Pre-Chorus:  
The garrisons stood, strong in their might,  
For the Lord was with him, guiding the fight.  
From cities of store to castles so grand,  
He built up a kingdom, by God’s own hand.  

Chorus:  
Lifted high, our voices raise,  
In the light of His glorious ways.  
With every heart, we seek Your throne,  
In You, O Lord, we find our home.  

Bridge:  
Mighty men of valor, standing side by side,  
In faith we gather, with the Lord as our guide.  
The battles we face, we will not fear,  
For in You, O God, our victory's near.  

Chorus:  
Lifted high, our voices raise,  
In the light of His glorious ways.  
With every heart, we seek Your throne,  
In You, O Lord, we find our home.  

Outro:  
Jehoshaphat’s heart, a beacon so bright,  
In the love of the Father, we walk in His light.  
With every praise, we lift Him high,  
Our King forever, our battle cry.
