[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I lift my eyes to see the One,  
With measuring line in hand,  
He speaks of breadth and length to come,  
In this holy, promised land.  
"Jerusalem, my heart shall dwell,  
In towns without a wall,  
For I am fire around you,  
My glory over all."

Chorus  
Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion,  
For the Lord our God will come to you,  
He is rising, dwelling in the midst,  
His love will make us new.  
Come forth, O scattered flock,  
From lands that hold you down,  
The apple of His eye,  
In Him, we wear the crown.

Verse 2  
He calls us forth from north to south,  
With grace, He leads us home,  
In the midst of nations, we will shine,  
For we are never alone.  
A promise given, we shall know,  
The Lord of hosts is near,  
He shakes His hand against our foes,  
In faith, we have no fear.

Bridge  
Be silent, all flesh, before the Lord,  
He is raised in holy place,  
In Judah’s heart, He finds His rest,  
His mercy, full of grace.  
Join together, all His people,  
In unity, we stand,  
For He has chosen us forever,  
In the palm of His hand.
