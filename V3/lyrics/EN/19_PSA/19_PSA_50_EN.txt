[TITLE]
Gathered in Your Light

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
Mighty God, You’ve called the earth,  
From sunrise to the evening's birth.  
Out of Zion, beauty shines,  
In Your presence, love defines.  

(Chorus)  
We gather here, O Lord, in grace,  
With thankful hearts, we seek Your face.  
In the fire, Your voice we hear,  
Speak to us, our hearts draw near.  

(Verse 2)  
You are judge, and You are near,  
To Your saints, You draw them here.  
Every beast and bird, they sing,  
All creation praises King.  

(Chorus)  
We gather here, O Lord, in grace,  
With thankful hearts, we seek Your face.  
In the fire, Your voice we hear,  
Speak to us, our hearts draw near.  

(Bridge)  
In the day of trouble, we call Your name,  
You deliver us, You are the same.  
O, let our lives declare Your praise,  
In every moment, through all our days.  

(Verse 3)  
You see the heart, the hidden ways,  
You call us back when we betray.  
With every word, we choose to speak,  
In Your love, O Lord, we seek.  

(Chorus)  
We gather here, O Lord, in grace,  
With thankful hearts, we seek Your face.  
In the fire, Your voice we hear,  
Speak to us, our hearts draw near.  

(Outro)  
Whosoever offers praise,  
Glorifies You, in endless ways.  
Together, Lord, we stand as one,  
In Your light, our hearts undone.
