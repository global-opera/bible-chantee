[TITLE]
Our Help in the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
If it had not been for You, O Lord,  
When the storms of life were raging,  
If it had not been for Your great love,  
We would be lost, our hearts engaging.  
When the foes arise, their wrath like fire,  
We stand secure, our hope’s desire.

Chorus:  
Blessed be the Lord, our refuge and might,  
In the darkest hour, You shine so bright.  
Our souls escape like birds on the wing,  
From every snare, Your praises we sing.  
Our help is in the name of the Lord,  
Creator of heaven, our hearts restored.

Verse 2:  
The flood may rise, and the waters swell,  
But we trust in You, our Emmanuel.  
Though proud waters roar, we will not fear,  
For Your presence, Lord, is always near.  
You lift us up from the depths of despair,  
In Your arms of grace, we find repair.

Bridge:  
When the enemy surrounds, we will lift our eyes,  
To the hills where our help comes, in You we rise.  
No weapon formed against us will stand in Your way,  
For You are our fortress, our Savior, our stay.

Forever we’ll praise, forever adored.
