[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Praise the name of the Lord,  
All you servants, lift Him high,  
In His house we gather near,  
To worship and to testify.  
For the Lord is good, His love is true,  
Sing praises to His name, forever new.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, praise the Lord, for He is great,  
Above all gods, our hearts await.  
He reigns in heaven, earth, and sea,  
In every moment, He’s here with me.  

[Verse 2]
