[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear my prayer, O God, I cry to You,  
In the silence, Lord, I long for truth.  
Enemies surround, their voices rise,  
In the shadows, Lord, my spirit sighs.  

Chorus  
I will call upon You, Lord, my light,  
Evening, morning, noon, I seek Your sight.  
You deliver my soul, bring peace to my heart,  
In the storm, I will trust, You won’t depart.  

Verse 2  
My heart is pained, like the weight of night,  
Fear and trembling, Lord, You are my light.  
Oh, that I could fly on wings of grace,  
To the wilderness, to find my place.  

Chorus  
I will call upon You, Lord, my light,  
Evening, morning, noon, I seek Your sight.  
You deliver my soul, bring peace to my heart,  
In the storm, I will trust, You won’t depart.  

Bridge  
Cast my burdens, Lord, upon Your care,  
You sustain me, always, everywhere.  
Though the wicked rise, and the night seems long,  
In Your arms, I find my song.  

Chorus  
I will call upon You, Lord, my light,  
Evening, morning, noon, I seek Your sight.  
You deliver my soul, bring peace to my heart,  
In the storm, I will trust, You won’t depart.  

Outro  
I will trust in You, my steadfast King,  
In every trial, my heart will sing.  
Though the world may change, I will stand secure,  
For in Your love, Lord, I am pure.
