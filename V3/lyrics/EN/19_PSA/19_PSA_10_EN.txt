Verse 1  
Why do You stand so far away,  
In times of trouble, Lord, we pray?  
The wicked rise in prideful schemes,  
While innocent hearts shatter their dreams.  
In secret places, they lie in wait,  
But we cry out, "Lord, don't hesitate."

Chorus  
Arise, O Lord, lift up Your hand,  
Don’t forget the humble, take a stand.  
You see the pain, You know our plight,  
We trust in You, our guiding light.  
You are our hope, our strength, our song,  
In You alone, we still belong.

Verse 2  
The wicked boast, they turn away,  
They think that You will not repay.  
But You behold the cries of night,  
In shadows deep, You bring the light.  
Break the arm of those who harm,  
Protect the weak, and keep us warm.
