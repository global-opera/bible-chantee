[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Praise the Lord, O my soul,  
With every breath, I will extol.  
While I live, I’ll sing His grace,  
In His love, I find my place.  
Trust not in man, for he is frail,  
But in the Lord, my heart will sail.  
He made the heavens, earth, and sea,  
In Him alone, my hope shall be.  

Chorus:  
Praise ye the Lord, forever and ever,  
He reigns with love, His truth will not sever.  
In every trial, in every storm,  
He lifts the weary and keeps us warm.  
Praise ye the Lord, O my soul,  
In His embrace, I am made whole.  

Verse 2:  
He’s the help of the oppressed,  
In His arms, I find my rest.  
He feeds the hungry, breaks the chains,  
Opens blind eyes, and soothes our pains.  
He lifts the bowed, gives strength anew,  
In His kindness, we find what’s true.  
He cares for strangers, the fatherless,  
His love and mercy, we confess.
