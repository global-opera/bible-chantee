[TITLE]
Lord, Hear My Cry

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, in Your mercy, I come before Thee,  
Rebuke me not in Your wrath, please set my heart free.  
For Your arrows, they pierce me, Your hand weighs me down,  
In my weakness and sorrow, Your grace is my crown.  

Chorus  
Lord, hear my cry, my heart's heavy sigh,  
In my despair, I lift my prayer high.  
Though my sins weigh upon me, I seek Your embrace,  
O Lord, my salvation, I long for Your grace.  

Verse 2  
My wounds they are painful, my burdens too great,  
In my foolishness, Lord, I’ve sealed my own fate.  
Friends stand far from me, my heart feels alone,  
Yet in You, my Savior, I find my true home.

Bridge  
I will declare my iniquity,  
With a heart broken, I come to Thee.  
In my weakness, I trust in Your name,  
O Lord, be near, never let me be shamed.

In my brokenness, Lord, I find my peace in Thee.
