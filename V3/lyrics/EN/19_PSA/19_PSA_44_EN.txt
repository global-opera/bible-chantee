[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
We’ve heard of Your mighty hand,  
What You’ve done in days of old,  
You drove out the enemies,  
And planted us in lands of gold.  
Not by our swords, nor strength we claim,  
But by Your favor, we rise and stand,  
O Lord, our King, we lift Your name,  
In Your light, we hold Your hand.

(Chorus)  
Arise, O Lord, don’t hide Your face,  
In our weakness, show us grace.  
Though we fall and feel the shame,  
We remember, we call Your name.  
You are our hope, our battle cry,  
In You, O Lord, we will abide.  
Through every trial, we will sing,  
In God we boast, our mighty King.

(Verse 2)  
Though we walk through shadows deep,  
And the world may laugh and scorn,  
We will not forget the love You keep,  
In our hearts, we are reborn.  
Our steps may falter, our path may sway,  
Yet our hearts remain steadfast and true,  
In every sorrow, we’ll still proclaim,  
O Lord, we trust and wait for You.

(Bridge)  
Awake, arise, O Lord of love,  
Shine Your light from heaven above.  
In our anguish, hear our plea,  
For Your mercy, set us free.  
Though we’re scattered, lost, and worn,  
In Your arms, we are reborn.  
For Your sake, we lift our song,  
In Your presence, we belong.

Arise, O Lord, we wait for Thee,
