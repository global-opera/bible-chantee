[LYRICS]
### Title: Forever Good

Verse 1  
Praise the Lord, O my soul,  
Give thanks for His mercy, forever whole.  
Who can tell of His mighty deeds,  
His love and kindness, our hearts He frees.  

Chorus  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
Through every trial, His promise assures.  
Remember us, O Lord, with favor divine,  
In Your salvation, let our hearts align.  

Verse 2  
We have wandered, we have strayed,  
Yet Your grace, Lord, never fades.  
In the depths, You’ve led us through,  
With Your mighty hand, our hope renew.  

Chorus  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
Through every trial, His promise assures.  
Remember us, O Lord, with favor divine,  
In Your salvation, let our hearts align.  

Bridge  
When we forget Your wondrous ways,  
In our weakness, we lift our praise.  
You saved us from the depths of despair,  
With every breath, Lord, we declare.  

Verse 3  
You’ve heard our cries, You’ve seen our plight,  
You remembered us in darkest night.  
Gather us, O Lord, from every land,  
To give thanks for the love that stands.  

Chorus  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
Through every trial, His promise assures.  
Remember us, O Lord, with favor divine,  
In Your salvation, let our hearts align.  

Outro  
Blessed be the God of Israel,  
From everlasting, His truth we tell.  
Let all the people rise and say,  
Amen, we praise You, Lord today.
