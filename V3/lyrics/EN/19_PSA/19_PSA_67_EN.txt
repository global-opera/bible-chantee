[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Lord, we cry for mercy, hear our humble plea,  
Bless us with Your presence, let Your face shine free.  
Let Your way be known, across this weary land,  
Your saving grace, O God, in every heart and hand.  

Chorus  
Let the people praise You, O God, we lift our song,  
All the nations rejoicing, in Your love so strong.  
Oh, let the earth be glad, let joy rise from the deep,  
For You judge with righteousness, in You our hearts we keep.  

Verse 2  
From the mountains high, to the valleys low,  
All creation sings, of the truth we know.  
We gather here in faith, united as one voice,  
In Your light, we find our hope, in Your love, we rejoice.

Bridge  
God shall bless us, His mercy flows like streams,  
Filling every heart, fulfilling all our dreams.  
To the ends of the earth, may Your glory be revealed,  
As we stand together, in Your love, we are healed.

Outro  
God, You bless us, with every breath we take,

Let the nations praise You, now and evermore.
