[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night, I cry,  
With my voice, Lord, I lift my sigh.  
I pour my heart, my troubles laid bare,  
In this cave of shadows, You are there.  

Chorus  
You are my refuge, my portion in the land,  
When I am broken, Lord, I trust Your hand.  
Though the world may fail me, and darkness may loom,  
I will rise, I will sing, for You turn my gloom.  

Verse 2  
Overwhelmed in spirit, my heart feels so low,  
Yet You know my path, Lord, wherever I go.  
No one stands beside me, no one hears my plea,  
But in my desperation, I cling to Thee.

Bridge

In the depths of sorrow, You bring me peace.

Outro  
So I cry unto You, O Lord, hear my plea,
