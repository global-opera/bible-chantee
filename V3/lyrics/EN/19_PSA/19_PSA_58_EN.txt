[TITLE]
The Righteous Judge

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O congregation, hear the call,  
Do we speak of truth at all?  
In our hearts, we hide the shame,  
Yet You, O Lord, know every name.  
From the womb, we’ve gone astray,  
With our words, we twist and sway.  
But Your justice, like a light,  
Shines upon the wrong and right.  

Chorus  
Break the chains of wickedness,  
Melt away the heart's distress.  
In Your mercy, we find grace,  
Righteous Judge, we seek Your face.  
O Lord, we cry, we long to see,  
The day of justice, set us free.  

Verse 2  
Like the serpent in the night,  
They turn from truth, embrace the blight.  
But Your power, strong and true,  
Brings the dawn, makes all things new.  
Let their plans, like waters flow,  
Fade away, let justice grow.  
In the silence, hear our prayer,  
In Your presence, we lay bare.  

Chorus  
Break the chains of wickedness,  
Melt away the heart's distress.  
In Your mercy, we find grace,  
Righteous Judge, we seek Your face.  
O Lord, we cry, we long to see,

Bridge  
Before the thorns can pierce the night,  
You lift us from the depths of fright.  
The righteous stand, they will rejoice,  
In Your victory, we lift our voice.  
Verily, there's a reward,  
For those who trust in the Lord.

Outro  
So let us walk in Your light,  
Righteous Judge, our hearts ignite.  
With every step, we'll stand for truth,  
In Your love, we find our youth.  
Verily, there's a reward,  
In Your justice, we are restored.
