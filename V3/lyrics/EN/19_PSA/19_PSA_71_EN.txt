[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In Thee, O Lord, I place my trust,  
Let not my heart be filled with doubt.  
Deliver me in righteousness,  
Incline Your ear, hear my cry out.  

You are my rock, my fortress strong,  
In You, I find my safe retreat.  
Commanded to be saved, I long,  
In times of need, Your grace I meet.  

[Chorus]  
O God, be near, my refuge true,  
In every storm, my hope in You.  
When trials rise and fears surround,  
I’ll lift my praise, my voice resound.  

[Verse 2]  
From youth, my heart has known Your ways,  
Held by Your hand since I was born.  
Through every trial, through all my days,  
Your love’s my shelter, I’m reborn.  

Though enemies may seek my fall,  
And whisper lies with cruel intent,  
I trust in You, my Savior's call,  
In darkest nights, my light is sent.  

[Chorus]  
O God, be near, my refuge true,  
In every storm, my hope in You.  
When trials rise and fears surround,  
I’ll lift my praise, my voice resound.  

[Bridge]  
I will hope continually,  
Your righteousness my song will be.  
You’ve taught me well, through all my years,  
In every joy and through my tears.  

So when I’m old and gray, I’ll sing,  
Of all the wondrous works You bring.  
Forsake me not, till I proclaim,  
Your mighty power, and Your name.  

[Chorus]  
O God, be near, my refuge true,  
In every storm, my hope in You.  
When trials rise and fears surround,  
I’ll lift my praise, my voice resound.  

[Outro]  
With every breath, my heart will sing,  
Of Your great love, my Savior King.  
Let my soul rejoice, forever free,  
In You, O Lord, my victory.
