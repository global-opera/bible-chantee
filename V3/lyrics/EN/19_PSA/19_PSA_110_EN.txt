[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, You speak to my heart,  
“Sit beside Me, take your part,  
Until I make your foes,  
A footstool for your holy throne.”  
In Zion, Your strength will rise,  
In the shadows, we will not disguise,  
Your people gather, hearts ablaze,  
In the beauty of Your holy ways.

Chorus  
At Your right hand, we will stand,  
In the power of Your mighty hand,  
With the dew of youth, we sing,  
You are our Priest, our everlasting King.  
O Lord of grace, we lift our praise,  
In the dawn of Your holy days.

Verse 2  
You reign above, with a mighty rod,  
Judging nations, You are God,  
In the day of wrath, we find our peace,  
For in Your love, our fears will cease.  
From the womb of morning light,  
We rise in trust, and take our flight,  
Your promise stands, forever true,  
Melchizedek, we worship You.

Bridge  
In the brook of the way, we find our rest,  
You lift our heads, and we are blessed,  
With every tear, and every sigh,  
We trust in You, our One on high.  
You strike through kings, You lead the fight,  
In darkness, Lord, You are our light.

Outro
