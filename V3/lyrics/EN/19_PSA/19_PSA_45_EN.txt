[TITLE]
King of Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
My heart is full, I sing of love,  
To You, O King, my thoughts rise above.  
Fairer than all, in grace You shine,  
Blessed forever, my heart is Thine.  

Chorus  
O King of Glory, mighty and true,  
With righteousness and meekness, I worship You.  
In Your majesty, I find my rest,  
Forever, O Lord, You are the best.  

Verse 2  
Gird Your sword, O mighty One,  
With truth and power, the battle's won.  
Your arrows pierce the heart of strife,  
In Your embrace, I find new life.  

Chorus  
O King of Glory, mighty and true,

In Your majesty, I find my rest,

The King desires your beauty bright,  
In His presence, you're clothed in light.

Outro  
Your name we lift through every age,  
In every heart, we turn the page.
