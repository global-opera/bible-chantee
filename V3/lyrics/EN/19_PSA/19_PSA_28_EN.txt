[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, I cry to You, my Rock,  
In silence, Lord, don’t leave me here;  
I lift my hands, in prayer I walk,  
Hear my supplications, draw me near.  
The wicked speak, but hearts deceive,  
In Your truth, I find my plea,  
Don’t let me fall with those who grieve,  
Lord, I trust, my hope is Thee.

Chorus  
Blessed be the Lord, He hears my cry,  
My strength, my shield, in Him I rely.  
With all my heart, I will rejoice,  
I’ll sing of grace, I lift my voice.

Verse 2  
Give them according to their ways,  
For they disregard Your mighty hand.  
But I will trust in You always,  
You are my fortress, my solid stand.  
You hear my prayers, You lift me high,  
In every trial, You’re by my side,  
Your love and mercy never die,  
In Your embrace, I will abide.

Chorus  
Blessed be the Lord, He hears my cry,  
My strength, my shield, in Him I rely.

I’ll sing of grace, I lift my voice.

Bridge  
Save Your people, bless our land,  
Feed us, Lord, with Your gentle hand.  
Lift us up, we look to Thee,  
In our hearts, Your love shall be.

Chorus

Outro  
O Lord, my heart rejoices still,  
In every moment, I seek Your will.

Forever, Lord, I’ll sing the same.
