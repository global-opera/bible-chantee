[TITLE]
Dwell in Your Presence

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Who shall abide in Your holy place,  
In the shadow of Your grace?  
He who walks with truth in his heart,  
And from his ways will never part.  
With lips that speak no lies,  
His love in action never dies.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, we long to dwell,  
In the beauty of Your well,  
Where the righteous find their peace,  
In Your presence, we are free.  
We will stand, we will stay,  
Rooted in Your love each day.  

Verse 2:  
He who honors those who fear,  
In Your eyes, they draw near.  
No reproach will he take on,  
In Your light, our fears are gone.  
With a heart that’s pure and true,  
Lord, we place our trust in You.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, we long to dwell,  
In the beauty of Your well,  
Where the righteous find their peace,  
In Your presence, we are free.  
We will stand, we will stay,  
Rooted in Your love each day.  

Bridge:  
In the trials, we will hold,  
To Your promises of old.  
Though the world may shake and sway,  
In Your arms, we will stay.  
Never moved, we stand secure,  
In Your love, we are assured.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, we long to dwell,  
In the beauty of Your well,  
Where the righteous find their peace,  
In Your presence, we are free.  
We will stand, we will stay,  
Rooted in Your love each day.  

Outro:  
Forever in Your grace we’ll sing,  
In Your presence, our hearts will cling.  
Lord, who shall abide in this place?  
Those who seek Your holy face.
