[TITLE]
Oh, Give Thanks to the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O give thanks to the Lord, for He is good,  
His mercy endures through all time,  
The redeemed, let us rise, proclaim His grace,  
From the east and the west, His love we embrace.

Chorus:  
Oh, that men would praise the Lord,  
For His goodness and His wondrous works,  
He satisfies our longing souls,  
In His arms, we find our home.

Verse 2:  
In the wilderness, we wandered alone,  
Thirsting and hungry, our hearts turned to stone,  
Then we cried to the Lord in our despair,  
He delivered us, brought us forth with care.

Chorus:  
Oh, that men would praise the Lord,  
For His goodness and His wondrous works,  
He satisfies our longing souls,  
In His arms, we find our home.

Bridge:  
He breaks the chains that bind us in fear,  
Brings light to the darkness, His voice we hear,  
He calms the storms and leads us to peace,  
In His presence, our troubles cease.

Verse 3:  
To the sea in our ships, we sail the great deep,  
In the face of the waves, our souls start to weep,  
But we cry to the Lord, and He hears our plea,  
With a whisper, He calms the raging sea.

Chorus:  
Oh, that men would praise the Lord,  
For His goodness and His wondrous works,  
He satisfies our longing souls,  
In His arms, we find our home.

Outro:  
Let the wise see and know His kind embrace,  
In our hearts, we declare His grace,  
Oh, give thanks to the Lord, for He is good,  
Forever we’ll sing, forever we’ll stand,  
In the love of our God, united we’ll stand.
