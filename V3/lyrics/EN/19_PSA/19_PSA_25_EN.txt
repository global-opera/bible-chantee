[Verse 1]  
O Lord, I lift my soul to You,  
In faith, I find my peace anew.  
My heart, it trusts, though trials may rise,  
Let not my foes bring shame or lies.  

[Chorus]  
Show me Your ways, O Lord, I plead,  
In Your truth, I find my need.  
Lead me, teach me, guide my heart,  
In Your mercy, I’ll take part.

Show me Your ways, O Lord, I plead,
