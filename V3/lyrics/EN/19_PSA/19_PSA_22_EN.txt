[TITLE]
In the Depths, I Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
My God, my God, why have You turned away?  
In my cries by night, I search for Your embrace.  
Yet, You are holy, dwelling in our praise,  
Our fathers called on You, and You showed them Your ways.  

Chorus  
Be not far from me, O Lord, my strength,  
In my sorrow and my pain, I seek Your face.  
Deliver me from darkness, hold me near,  
In the depths, I call, You are my refuge here.  

Verse 2  
I’m a worm, despised, a shadow in the night,  
They mock and scorn me, but I cling to Your light.  
From my mother’s womb, You set my heart to trust,  
In the dust of death, You’ll rise, my hope’s a must.  

Chorus  
Be not far from me, O Lord, my strength,  
In my sorrow and my pain, I seek Your face.  
Deliver me from darkness, hold me near,  
In the depths, I call, You are my refuge here.  

Bridge  
You hear the cries of those who seek Your name,  
You’ve not despised our pain, You’re the same.  
I will praise You in the great assembly,  
For You have heard my heart, You’re my melody.  

Chorus  
Be not far from me, O Lord, my strength,  
In my sorrow and my pain, I seek Your face.  
Deliver me from darkness, hold me near,  
In the depths, I call, You are my refuge here.  

Outro  
All the ends of the earth will remember and turn,  
To worship before You, with hearts that yearn.  
Your kingdom reigns forever, Lord of all,  
In the depths, I call, Lord, I give You my all.
