[TITLE]
Deliver Me, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of my fears, I call Your name,  
Deliver me, O Lord, from the wicked’s flame.  
They gather for the battle, their hearts filled with strife,  
But You are my shelter, my strength, my life.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear my cry, hear my plea,  
Preserve my soul, set me free.  
You are my refuge in the storm,  
In Your presence, I am reborn.  

Verse 2:  
Their tongues are like serpents, they plot in the night,  
But You are my fortress, my guiding light.  
Let not their desires, take root in my way,  
For I trust in Your mercy, Lord, come what may.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear my cry, hear my plea,  
Preserve my soul, set me free.  
You are my refuge in the storm,  
In Your presence, I am reborn.  

Bridge:  
You cover my head in the day of the fight,  
With burning coals fall upon those who blight.  
I know You’ll maintain the cause of the meek,  
In Your arms of compassion, it’s Your face that I seek.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear my cry, hear my plea,  
Preserve my soul, set me free.  
You are my refuge in the storm,  
In Your presence, I am reborn.  

Outro:  
Surely the righteous will give thanks to Your name,  
In the arms of the Savior, we’ll never be the same.  
O Lord, my Deliverer, I lift my heart high,  
In Your love forever, I will abide.
