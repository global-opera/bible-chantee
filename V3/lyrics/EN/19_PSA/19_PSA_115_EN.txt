[TITLE]
Forever We Will Bless You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Not to us, O Lord, not to our name,  
But to Your glory, we lift our praise,  
For Your mercy flows and Your truth remains,  
In every moment, through all our days.  

Chorus:  
We will bless You, Lord, forevermore,  
From this time forth, we will sing and soar.  
Our help and shield, in You we trust,  
In every trial, Your love is just.  

Verse 2:  
The nations wonder, “Where is our God?”  
But You are in the heavens, doing as You please.  
Their idols fall, made of silver and gold,  
But we’ll stand firm, our hearts in peace.  

Chorus:  
We will bless You, Lord, forevermore,  
From this time forth, we will sing and soar.  
Our help and shield, in You we trust,  
In every trial, Your love is just.  

Bridge:  
O Israel, put your trust in the Lord,  
He’s mindful of us, His blessings poured.  
Both small and great, we gather near,  
In reverence and love, we know You’re here.  

Chorus:  
We will bless You, Lord, forevermore,  
From this time forth, we will sing and soar.  
Our help and shield, in You we trust,  
In every trial, Your love is just.  

Outro:  
The dead can’t praise, but we still sing,  
For You are our hope, our everything.  
Praise the Lord, in every breath we take,  
Forever we will bless You, for Your name's sake.
