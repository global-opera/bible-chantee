[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the stillness of this place,  
We gather to seek Your face,  
With hearts open wide, we come to adore,  
Your mighty acts, forever we’ll explore.  

(Chorus)  
Praise the Lord in His sanctuary,  
Lift our voices, let them rise,  
For in His power, we find our strength,  
All creation sings, to our Savior, we cry.  

(Verse 2)  
With the sound of the trumpet, let it ring,  
In harmony, our praises we bring,  
With harp and strings, let our hearts align,  
In joyful dance, we give You our time.  

(Chorus)  
Praise the Lord in His sanctuary,  
Lift our voices, let them rise,  
For in His power, we find our strength,  
All creation sings, to our Savior, we cry.  

(Bridge)  
Every breath within our lungs,  
Declares Your love, O Holy One,  
From the depths of our hearts, we sing,  
To the King of Kings, our offering.  

(Chorus)  
Praise the Lord in His sanctuary,  
Lift our voices, let them rise,  
For in His power, we find our strength,  
All creation sings, to our Savior, we cry.  

(Outro)  
Let everything that hath breath,  
Join the chorus, till the end,  
Praise the Lord, our hearts proclaim,  
Forever, we’ll exalt Your name.  
Praise ye the Lord!  
Praise ye the Lord!
