Verse 1:  
When You brought us to this land,  
A promise held in Your mighty hand,  
You cast out giants, strong and bold,  
In Your love, our hearts behold.  
We stand as one, a holy race,  
Chosen by grace, we seek Your face.  
No other gods shall claim our hearts,  
In Your embrace, our journey starts.

Chorus:  
For we are Your people, set apart,  
Loved by the Father, You know our hearts.  
With every covenant, You show Your might,  
In the shadows, You are our light.  
We’ll trust in You, O faithful God,  
Leading us forth, our path is broad.  
You’ve redeemed us from the night,  
In Your mercy, we find our sight.

Verse 2:  
You freed us from the chains of sin,  
Through the waters, we rise again.  
With every trial, our fears subside,  
In Your presence, we will abide.  
You bless the fruits of all our hands,  
In Your promise, our hope stands.  
No sickness or fear shall hold us down,  
In Your love, we wear the crown.

Bridge:
