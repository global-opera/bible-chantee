[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Journey Through the Wilderness  

[Verse 1]  
Through the wilderness, we tread,  
Following the path You've led,  
Mountains rise and shadows fall,  
Yet Your voice is our guiding call.  
You’ve spoken, Lord, “Rise and see,  
Turn your hearts, come follow Me.”  
For forty years, You’ve been our stay,  
In every night and every day.  

[Chorus]  
We will journey on, with faith in our hearts,  
Though the road is long, it’s Your love that imparts.  
Through the trials we face, we will not lose sight,  
For You’ve blessed our way, Lord, with Your guiding light.  

[Verse 2]  
Esau’s land, the Moabite’s shore,  
You’ve told us, “Do not fight, but seek for more.”  
In the face of giants, we stand strong,  
For You, O Lord, have made us belong.  
As we rise up, across the brook,  
In every promise, we take heart and look.  
With courage, we face what lies ahead,  
For in Your presence, our fears are shed.  

[Chorus]  
We will journey on, with faith in our hearts,  
Though the road is long, it’s Your love that imparts.  
Through the trials we face, we will not lose sight,  
For You’ve blessed our way, Lord, with Your guiding light.  

[Bridge]  
So we’ll pass through the lands that You have claimed,  
In Your name, Lord, we won’t be ashamed.  
Every battle fought, every city won,  
In Your hands, Lord, our victory’s begun.  
With every step, we’ll sing of Your grace,  
In this wilderness, we’ll seek Your face.  

[Chorus]  
We will journey on, with faith in our hearts,  
Though the road is long, it’s Your love that imparts.  
Through the trials we face, we will not lose sight,  
For You’ve blessed our way, Lord, with Your guiding light.  

[Outro]  
In the wilderness, we’ll find our way,  
With You beside us, we will not stray.  
For every promise, we stand and declare,  
Our God is with us, always near.
