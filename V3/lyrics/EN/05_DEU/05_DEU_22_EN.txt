Verse 1  
When a brother’s lost in the dark,  
And his heart feels heavy, torn apart,  
We will not hide, we will not flee,  
We’ll bring him back, as love decrees.  
With the strength to lift him high,  
In our hands, his hope shall rise,  
For in the fold, we find our way,  
To restore the lost, we will obey.

Chorus  
Oh Lord, let our hearts be true,  
In the love we share, we find You.  
Guide our hands, make us whole,  
To seek the lost, to heal the soul.  
In every burden that we bear,  
Let us show the love we share.  
Together we’ll rise, together we’ll stand,  
In Your grace, united we’ll band.

Verse 2  
In the fields where the shadows roam,  
We will not stray, we’ll lead them home.

We’ll honor life; we’ll speak instead.  
For the hurting and the weak,  
Your mercy flows, Your truth we seek.  
In the nest where love takes flight,  
We will cherish, we’ll do what’s right.
