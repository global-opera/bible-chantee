[TITLE]
In Your Covenant, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land that You have given,  
We gather in Your grace,  
With hearts laid bare before You,  
In this sacred, holy place.  
No blemish in our offering,  
Pure as gold before Your throne,  
We cast aside all wickedness,  
And seek Your face alone.

Chorus  
O Lord, we come to You in reverence,  
With every prayer, we stand in awe,  
In Your covenant, we find our refuge,  
Guide our hearts to follow Your law.  
We will heed Your voice, our hearts won't wander,  
In Your love, forever we'll abide,  
O Lord, in You we find our shelter,  
Our King and Savior, our faithful guide.

Verse 2  
When judgment calls, we seek Your wisdom,  
In matters heavy on our minds,  
We turn to those who serve before You,  
Your truth in them we find.  
Let the witness lead us forward,  
With clear hearts, we shall obey,  
For in the light of Your commandments,  
We shall walk, we shall stay.

Bridge  
From among us, You choose a leader,  
To shepherd us with love and care,  
May his heart be set upon Your word,  
In Your presence, may he always share.  
As we live and breathe in unity,  
Our voices rise, a faithful song,  
In the covenant of Your mercy,  
Together, we shall stand strong.
