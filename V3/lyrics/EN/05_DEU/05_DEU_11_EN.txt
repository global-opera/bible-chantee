[TITLE]
Blessings of the Promise

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
We gather in Your presence, Lord,  
With hearts that seek to know,  
Your mighty hand, Your faithfulness,  
In every place we go.  
You led us through the wilderness,  
You parted every sea,  
Your miracles surround us,  
Your love has set us free.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we will love You, Lord our God,  
With all our heart and soul,  
Your statutes guide our every step,  
In You we find our whole.  
The land of promise, flowing grace,  
You dwell within our hearts,  
In every trial, through every storm,  
Your blessings never part.  

(Verse 2)  
Remember all Your mighty deeds,  
The wonders that we’ve seen,  
Your eyes are on the land You give,  
A place where we can glean.  
From hills and valleys, rain descends,  
You nourish all we need,  
And as we walk in faithfulness,  
You plant a faithful seed.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we will love You, Lord our God,  
With all our heart and soul,  
Your statutes guide our every step,  
In You we find our whole.  
The land of promise, flowing grace,  
You dwell within our hearts,  
In every trial, through every storm,  
Your blessings never part.  

(Bridge)  
So let us teach our children well,  
Your words upon our hands,  
In every home, in every heart,  
Your truth forever stands.  
We choose to walk in Your great ways,  
To cleave unto Your name,  
And as we tread on holy ground,  
We’ll never be the same.

(Outro)  
We stand in awe of all You’ve done,  
Your faithfulness our song,  
In every blessing, every trial,  
With You, Lord, we belong.  
In every blessing, every trial,  
With You, Lord, we belong.
