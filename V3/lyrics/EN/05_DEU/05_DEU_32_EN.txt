[TITLE]
The Rock of Our Salvation

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Give ear, O heavens, hear the words I speak,  
As rain upon the earth, His truth we seek.  
He is our Rock, His ways are just and right,  
In every shadowed valley, He’s our guiding light.  

[Chorus]  
Ascribe greatness to our God,  
He leads us through the storm.  
In His arms, we find our rest,  
Our hearts forever warm.  
Oh, remember days of old,  
Our Father’s hand so true,  
He’s the Rock of our salvation,  
His promises renew.

[Verse 2]  
In the wilderness, He found us lost,  
With love and mercy, He paid the cost.  
He bore us on His wings, our burdens He will bear,  
In His grace, we stand unshaken, free from every care.

[Bridge]  
When we forget His love,  
And stray from His embrace,  
He calls us back to Him,  
To find our rightful place.  
Though nations may rise and fall,  
His truth will ever stand,  
The Rock of ages holds us close,  
In His mighty hand.

[Outro]  
So let the heavens hear our praise,  
And let the earth proclaim,  
The Rock of our salvation,  
Forever bless His name.
