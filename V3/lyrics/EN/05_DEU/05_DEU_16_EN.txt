[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the month of Abib, we gather 'round,  
To remember the night when our freedom was found.  
From Egypt we fled, in haste we did rise,  
The Lord brought us forth, beneath darkened skies.  

Chorus  
Oh, we will remember, the bread of affliction,  
No leaven among us, in humble submission.  
With hearts full of praise, we gather in grace,  
To rejoice in the Lord, our refuge, our place.  

Verse 2  
At the chosen altar, our sacrifice laid,  
With every offering, our gratitude displayed.  
We celebrate life, with family near,  
In the joy of His presence, we cast off all fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, we will remember, the bread of affliction,  
No leaven among us, in humble submission.  
With hearts full of praise, we gather in grace,  
To rejoice in the Lord, our refuge, our place.  

Bridge  
For three times a year, we stand before Thee,  
In the feasts of our harvest, in unity free.  
The widow, the stranger, all gathered as one,  
In the love of our Savior, our journey begun.  

Chorus  
Oh, we will remember, the bread of affliction,  
No leaven among us, in humble submission.  
With hearts full of praise, we gather in grace,  
To rejoice in the Lord, our refuge, our place.  

Outro  
So let us be just, in the ways that we walk,  
With kindness and mercy, in every sweet talk.  
For the Lord is our guide, in His love we abide,  
Forever we’ll lift up His name, our great pride.
