[TITLE]
Faithful to the Call

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
When prophets rise, with dreams they share,  
And wonders speak of gods not there,  
My heart, O Lord, shall stand so true,  
In You alone, my hope renew.  
For You have brought me from the night,  
Redeemed my soul, restored my light.  

(Chorus)  
I will walk in Your ways, O God,  
With all my heart, my soul, my rod.  
Your voice, I’ll follow, Your truth I’ll seek,  
In every promise, my spirit speaks.  
I will cleave to You, my heart’s desire,  
In Your embrace, I find my fire.  

(Verse 2)  
If friends or kin should lead astray,  
To serve the gods of yesterday,  
I’ll stand my ground, I won’t relent,  
For in Your love, my heart is bent.  
You pulled me from the chains of shame,  
In sacred trust, I bless Your name.  

(Chorus)  
I will walk in Your ways, O God,  
With all my heart, my soul, my rod.  
Your voice, I’ll follow, Your truth I’ll seek,  
In every promise, my spirit speaks.  
I will cleave to You, my heart’s desire,  
In Your embrace, I find my fire.  

(Bridge)  
In cities dark, where shadows dwell,  
Your light will shine, a holy well.  
With each command, I’ll stand as one,  
Declaring victory, Your will be done.  
Though trials come and voices rise,  
With faith unshaken, I’ll lift my eyes.  

(Chorus)  
I will walk in Your ways, O God,  
With all my heart, my soul, my rod.  
Your voice, I’ll follow, Your truth I’ll seek,  
In every promise, my spirit speaks.  
I will cleave to You, my heart’s desire,  
In Your embrace, I find my fire.  

(Outro)  
Faithful to the call, I stand with grace,  
In love eternal, I’ll seek Your face.  
My God, my King, forever near,  
In every moment, I’ll persevere.
