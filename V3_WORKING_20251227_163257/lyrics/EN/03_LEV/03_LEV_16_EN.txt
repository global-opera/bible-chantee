[TITLE]

[LYRICS]
 Atonement Day

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the veil,  
Where mercy meets our pain,  
The blood of sacrifice shall cleanse,  
And break our sin’s dark chain.  
As Aaron stands before the throne,  
In linen pure and bright,  
He offers up the bullock’s blood,  
To bring us back to light.

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord, we come with humble hearts,  
Your grace, our only plea;  
Through sacrifice, our sins depart,  
In You, we are set free.  
On this day of atonement,  
Our burdens laid to rest,  
In the shadow of Your mercy,  
We find our hope, our blessed.

[Verse 2]  
Two goats before the altar stand,  
One for sin, one sent away,  
With hands laid on the innocent,  
Our guilt upon it lay.  
Into the wilderness it goes,  
Far from our weary souls,  
In the depths of Your compassion,  
You heal and make us whole.

[Bridge]  
In the holy place, no man may tread,  
But through the Lamb we boldly come,  
With hearts ablaze, our spirits fed,  
O Holy One, Your will be done.  
Cleanse our hearts, renew our minds,  
As we stand in reverent awe,  
Forever bound in love divine,  
We worship You, our Lord and Law.

[Outro]  
With every tear and every prayer,  
Your love, our constant guide,  
In the light of Your forgiveness,  
With You we shall abide.  
Atonement day, forever true,  
Our hearts forever sing,  
In the shadow of Your mercy,  
We lift our voice and bring.
