[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness, You call my name,  
From the shadows, I rise unashamed,  
The priest stands ready, my heart in His hands,  
With two birds before Him, my hope understands.  

Chorus  
Cleansed by the blood, over running water,  
Sprinkled and washed, a son, a daughter.  
Seven times I’m made whole,  
In Your mercy, I find my soul.  
I am clean, forever free,  
Your grace has washed me, Lord, in Thee.  

Verse 2  
On the eighth day, I come to the door,  
With offerings of love, I bow and implore.  
The lamb is slain, and I feel the weight,  
In this sacred moment, I find my fate.

Bridge  
Oh, the hyssop and the cedar,  
Mark my heart, make me new.  
With each drop, I surrender,  
All I am, I give to You.  
Let the living bird take flight,  
Into the fields of endless light.

Outro  
As I stand in Your presence, I am renewed,  
The law of the leper, my heart now imbued.  
With the love of the Father, I rise and proclaim,  
Through the blood of the Lamb, I’m forever the same.  
Cleansed and made whole, in Your name I remain.
