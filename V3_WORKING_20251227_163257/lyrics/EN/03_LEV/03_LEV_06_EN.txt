[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the altar, where our sins are laid,  
We come with hearts contrite, for the debts we have made.  
If we lie to our brother, or take what is not ours,  
In the light of Your forgiveness, we find healing power.  

Chorus  
Lord, restore what we have taken,  
Bring us back into Your grace.  
In the fire of Your mercy,  
We find our rightful place.  
With a ram without blemish,  
We lay our burdens down,  
In the depths of Your compassion,  
Our hearts forever found.  

Verse 2  
As the fire ever burning, on the altar shall remain,  
We offer up our lives, surrendering our shame.  
With the flour and the oil, we bring a fragrant plea,  
In the holy place, O Lord, we seek to be set free.
