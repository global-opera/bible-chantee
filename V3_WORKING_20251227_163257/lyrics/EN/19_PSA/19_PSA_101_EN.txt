[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I will sing of mercy, Lord,  
To You, my heart will soar,  
In the quiet of my home,  
I seek Your face, I long for more.  
With a perfect heart, I walk,  
In Your light, my soul will talk,  
No wickedness before my eyes,  
In Your love, I find my rise.  

Chorus  
O Lord, come close to me,  
In the stillness, let me see,  
Your grace and truth that set me free,  
I will follow, faithfully.  
With every step, I choose the way,  
To walk in light, to seek and pray,  
O Lord, my heart will sing,  
In Your mercy, I take wing.  

Verse 2  
From the shadows, help me part,  
With a humble, tender heart,  
I will shun the prideful gaze,  
In Your presence, I am raised.  
The faithful dwell within my sight,  
In Your love, I find my light,  
To cut off all deceitful ways,  
Let my life bring You praise.
