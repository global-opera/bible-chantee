[TITLE]
My Soul Waits on You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In silence, Lord, my soul will wait,  
For You alone, my hope, my fate.  
You are my rock, my refuge strong,  
In Your embrace, Lord, I belong.  

Chorus  
O, my heart, trust in Him,  
Pour out your fears, let praises begin.  
For in the storm, He is my peace,  
My salvation, my sweet release.  

Verse 2  
Though voices rise to speak of strife,  
I lean on You, my source of life.  
They may conspire, but I will stand,  
For You, O God, hold all in hand.  

Chorus  
O, my heart, trust in Him,  
Pour out your fears, let praises begin.  
For in the storm, He is my peace,  
My salvation, my sweet release.  

Bridge  
Power belongs to You, O Lord,  
Mercy flows from Your heart's accord.  
In lowly hearts or high esteem,  
You're all we need, our hope, our dream.  

Chorus  
O, my heart, trust in Him,  
Pour out your fears, let praises begin.  
For in the storm, He is my peace,  
My salvation, my sweet release.  

Outro  
So I will wait, my soul at rest,  
In Your presence, I am blessed.  
You are my rock, my steadfast friend,  
Forever, Lord, on You depend.
