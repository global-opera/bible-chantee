[TITLE]
In Your Courts, We Flourish

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In the morning light, I lift my song,  
To give You thanks, O Lord, where I belong.  
Your lovingkindness, it calls my heart,  
From the break of dawn, You set me apart.  

Chorus:
O Most High, I sing Your praise,  
In the night, Your faithfulness stays.  
With ten strings, my heart will play,  
In Your courts, O Lord, I long to stay.  

Verse 2:
How great are You, O God, so deep,  
In the shadows where the weary weep.  
Though the wicked rise like grass in bloom,  
Your truth will scatter them, dispel the gloom.  

Chorus:
O Most High, I sing Your praise,  
In the night, Your faithfulness stays.  
With ten strings, my heart will play,  
In Your courts, O Lord, I long to stay.  

Bridge:
You lift me high, like the horn of the deer,  
Anointed fresh, Your presence drawing near.  
In the house of the Lord, I will stand and see,  
Your goodness flowing, a life set free.  

Chorus:
O Most High, I sing Your praise,  
In the night, Your faithfulness stays.  
With ten strings, my heart will play,  
In Your courts, O Lord, I long to stay.  

Outro:
Planted in You, I shall grow strong,  
Bearing fruit in age, forever I’ll belong.  
For You are upright, my Rock, my song,  
In Your courts, O Lord, where I am home.
