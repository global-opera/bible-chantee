[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Remember, Lord, the trials David faced,  
He swore an oath to seek Your holy place.  
With restless heart, he yearned to find,  
A dwelling for the One who’s so divine.  

Chorus:  
Arise, O Lord, in Your rest we wait,  
Come fill this place, our hearts, and elevate.  
Clothe us in righteousness, let joy resound,  
In Your presence, Lord, our hope is found.  

Verse 2:  
From Ephratah, we heard the call,  
In fields of wood, we’ve found it all.  
We’ll enter in, worship at Your feet,  
In Your tabernacle, our spirits meet.  

Chorus:  
Arise, O Lord, in Your rest we wait,  
Come fill this place, our hearts, and elevate.  
Clothe us in righteousness, let joy resound,  
In Your presence, Lord, our hope is found.  

Bridge:  
You’ve chosen Zion, our hearts You desire,  
A resting place, where we lift You higher.  
Abundantly bless, satisfy our need,  
Clothe us with salvation, in You we’re freed.  

Chorus:  
Arise, O Lord, in Your rest we wait,

Clothe us in righteousness, let joy resound,

Outro:  
His crown shall flourish, the horn of David rise,  
In Your love, O Lord, we find our prize.  
We lift our voices, forever we’ll sing,  
To the mighty God, our Savior and King.
