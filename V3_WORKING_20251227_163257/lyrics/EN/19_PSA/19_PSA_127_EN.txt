[TITLE]
Unless the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, we call upon Your name,  
Building dreams with tender hands, yet all feels just the same.  
Unless You, Lord, are the strength, our labors are in vain,  
We seek Your heart, Your guiding light, to shelter us from pain.  

Chorus  
Unless the Lord builds the house,  
We toil in vain, we toil in vain.  
In every tear, in every doubt,  
Your love remains, Your love remains.  
Sleep, O my soul, in His embrace,  
For He gives rest to those He loves,  
In His grace, we find our place,  
Our hearts rejoice, our hearts rejoice.  

Verse 2  
Children are a gift from You, a heritage so dear,  
Like arrows in the warrior's hand, we raise them up in cheer.  
Happy is the man who sees his quiver full and bright,  
In every laugh, in every tear, we find Your pure delight.
