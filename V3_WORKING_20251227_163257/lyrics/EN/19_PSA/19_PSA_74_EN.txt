[TITLE]
Plead Your Cause, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O God, why have You turned away,  
From the flock that You’ve called Your own?  
In the shadows, Your anger stays,  
While the enemy claims our home.  
Remember Your people, Lord, we cry,  
Purchased with love, we lift our sighs.  
In this desolation, hear our plea,  
Come restore what has been lost in Thee.

Chorus:  
Arise, O God, and plead Your cause,  
Let not the enemy blaspheme Your name.  
In the silence, we seek Your face,  
Reveal Your glory, and break the chains.  
You are our King, forever reign,  
In the midst of our trials, Your love remains.

Verse 2:  
Lift up Your hand from where it rests,  
Bring forth Your strength, our hearts confess.  
You divided seas with mighty breath,  
And silenced chaos with Your depth.  
The day is Yours, the night You keep,  
In every valley, You are our peace.  
O do not forget the cries of the meek,  
Let the poor and needy find what they seek.

Chorus:  
Arise, O God, and plead Your cause,  
Let not the enemy blaspheme Your name.  
In the silence, we seek Your face,  
Reveal Your glory, and break the chains.  
You are our King, forever reign,  
In the midst of our trials, Your love remains.

Bridge:  
Remember Your covenant, O Lord,  
In dark places, let Your light restore.  
Let not the oppressed return in shame,  
But let the humble praise Your name.  
With every tear, we bring to You,  
Hear our lament, make all things new.

Outro:  
O God, we trust in Your embrace,  
In every season, we seek Your grace.  
Come restore our hearts, O God, we pray,  
Plead Your cause, and light our way.
