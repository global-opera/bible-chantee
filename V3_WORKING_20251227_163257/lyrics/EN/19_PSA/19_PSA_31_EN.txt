Verse 1  
In You, O Lord, I place my trust,  
Never let me be ashamed.  
Deliver me, my righteous God,  
You are my rock, my fortress named.  
Bow down, O Lord, and hear my cry,  
Be my strength, my shelter high.  

Chorus  
Into Your hands, I commit my soul,  
You’ve redeemed me, Lord, You make me whole.  
In the shadows, You are my light,  
I will trust in You, my heart takes flight.

In Your mercy, I find my grace.
