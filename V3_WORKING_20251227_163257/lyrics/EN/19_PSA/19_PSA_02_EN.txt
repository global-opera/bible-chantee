[TITLE]
Kiss the Son

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Why do the nations rage,  
And plot in vain their schemes?  
The kings of earth unite,  
Against the Holy King.  
He sits upon the throne,  
In laughter He will reign,  
With wrath He will respond,  
Yet mercy still remains.  

Chorus:  
Kiss the Son, oh, bow before Him,  
In reverence, we seek Your face.  
Blessed are those who trust in You,  
Our refuge, our hiding place.  
With trembling hearts, we come to You,  
In awe of Your holy grace.  

Verse 2:  
He’s set His King on Zion,  
The promise stands today.  
He offers us the nations,  
To walk in His great way.  
So let us heed the warning,  
O rulers, hear the call,  
Serve the Lord with trembling,  
For He is Lord of all.  

Chorus:  
Kiss the Son, oh, bow before Him,  
In reverence, we seek Your face.  
Blessed are those who trust in You,

With trembling hearts, we come to You,

Bridge:  
You break the chains of darkness,  
You shatter every fear.  
Your rod of iron guides us,  
In love, You draw us near.  
We lift our hearts in worship,  
Our voices raised on high,  
In trust, we find our safety,  
You are our battle cry.

Outro:  
Kiss the Son, O kiss the Son,  
In His arms, our victory won.  
Blessed are they who call His name,  
Forever, we’ll proclaim His fame.
