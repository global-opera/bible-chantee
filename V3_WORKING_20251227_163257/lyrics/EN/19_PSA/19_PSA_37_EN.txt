[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the wicked, I will not be afraid,  
Though they rise like tall green trees, their roots will soon decay.  
I will trust in You, O Lord, my heart will find its rest,  
For You are my refuge, in Your arms I am blessed.  

Chorus:  
Delight in the Lord, and He will give,  
The desires of your heart, in Him we live.  
With every step I take, You guide my way,  
In Your love and grace, forever I will stay.  

Verse 2:  
When the storms of life are raging, and my soul feels so weak,  
I will cease from all my anger, in Your presence, Lord, I seek.  
For the meek shall inherit, the earth will be their home,  
In the peace that You provide, I am never alone.  

Chorus:  
Delight in the Lord, and He will give,

With every step I take, You guide my way,  
In Your love and grace, forever I will stay.  

Bridge:

In the days of trouble, You are always there.  
Your strength is my salvation, my fortress through the night,  
I will wait upon You, Lord, for You will bring the light.

Outro:  
So I’ll trust in You, O Lord, my heart will not be swayed,

With every breath I take, I’ll sing of all Your ways,  
In Your faithful love, forever I will praise.
