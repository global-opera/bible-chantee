Verse 1  
O Lord, in Your anger, do not rebuke me,  
In Your wrath, Lord, please show me grace.  
I come to You weak, my spirit is weary,  
Heal my bones, Lord, restore my place.  

Chorus  
Have mercy, O Lord, I lift my plea,  
In the silence, I wait; my heart longs for Thee.  
Deliver my soul, for Your love is my song,  
In the night, hear my cry, I know I belong.
