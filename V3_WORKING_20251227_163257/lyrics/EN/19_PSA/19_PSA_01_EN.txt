[TITLE]
Rooted in Your Love

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Blessed is the man who walks in Your ways,  
Not in the counsel where darkness stays.  
He finds his joy in Your holy law,  
In every word, he hears Your call.  

Chorus:  
Like a tree by the riverside,  
Bearing fruit in the season’s stride,  
His leaves will not wither, his heart will soar,  
In Your presence, Lord, forevermore.

Verse 2:  
The wicked are lost, like chaff in the breeze,  
Driven away, they’re brought to their knees.  
But You know the path of the righteous heart,  
Guiding us, Lord, never apart.  

Chorus:  
Like a tree by the riverside,  
Bearing fruit in the season’s stride,  
His leaves will not wither, his heart will soar,  
In Your presence, Lord, forevermore.

Bridge:  
Oh, the way of the righteous shines bright,  
In Your love, we find our light.  
Though trials may come, we shall not fear,  
For You are with us, always near.  

Chorus:  
Like a tree by the riverside,  
Bearing fruit in the season’s stride,  
His leaves will not wither, his heart will soar,  
In Your presence, Lord, forevermore.

Outro:  
Blessed is the man who walks with You,  
In every moment, Your grace is true.  
We’re rooted in love, we’ll stand tall and free,  
In the light of Your truth, we will be.
