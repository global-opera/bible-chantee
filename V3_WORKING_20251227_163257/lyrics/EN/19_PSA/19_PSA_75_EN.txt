[TITLE]
In Your Presence, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
To You, our God, we lift our praise,  
Your wondrous works, our hearts amaze.  
In every trial, we find our peace,  
For in Your name, all fears shall cease.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are near, our hearts declare,  
In Your presence, we find solace there.  
You judge with love, our hope is found,  
In Your mercy, we stand on holy ground.  

Verse 2:  
The earth may tremble, the pillars sway,  
Yet in Your hands, we’re safe each day.  
Fools may rise with a boastful pride,  
But in Your truth, we will abide.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are near, our hearts declare,  
In Your presence, we find solace there.  
You judge with love, our hope is found,  
In Your mercy, we stand on holy ground.  

Bridge:  
Promotion comes not from the east or west,  
But You alone, O God, know what is best.  
You lift the humble, bring down the proud,  
In Your faithfulness, we sing aloud.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are near, our hearts declare,  
In Your presence, we find solace there.  
You judge with love, our hope is found,  
In Your mercy, we stand on holy ground.  

Outro:  
Forever I’ll sing, to the God of grace,  
With thankful hearts, we seek Your face.  
The righteous rise, with voices strong,  
In Your presence, we belong.
