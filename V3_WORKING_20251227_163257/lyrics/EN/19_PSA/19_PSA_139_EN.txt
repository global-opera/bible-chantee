[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, You’ve searched my heart,  
You know my every thought,  
From my rising to my rest,  
In Your love, I am caught.  
You compass every path I take,  
Your hand is on my way,  
In the dark and in the light,  
You are with me every day.

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
In Your presence, all my fears will cease.  
No height or depth could ever keep me from Your love,  
You are my shelter, my strength from above.

Verse 2  
If I ascend to the skies,  
Or dwell in the deepest sea,  
Even there, Your hand will guide,  
Your right hand holds me free.  
You’ve woven me in secret,  
Fearfully made in grace,  
Your thoughts, oh God, are precious,  
In Your arms, I find my place.

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
In Your presence, all my fears will cease.  
No height or depth could ever keep me from Your love,  
You are my shelter, my strength from above.

Bridge  
Search me, O God, know my heart,  
Try me, reveal every part.  
Lead me in Your everlasting way,  
In Your light, I choose to stay.

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
In Your presence, all my fears will cease.  
No height or depth could ever keep me from Your love,  
You are my shelter, my strength from above.

Outro  
In Your presence, Lord, I am made whole,  
Forever in Your love, I give You my soul.  
In Your presence, Lord, forever I will be,  
Resting in Your arms, You are all I need.
