[TITLE]
In Joyful Harvest

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
When You turned our hearts, oh Lord,  
We were like dreams come alive,  
Our mouths filled with laughter,  
In Your grace, we will thrive.  
Among the nations, they will see,  
The wonders You’ve done for me,  
You’ve broken the chains that held us tight,  
In Your love, we find our light.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, You’ve done great things for us,  
We lift our hands, we sing in trust.  
Turn our hearts, let Your mercy flow,  
Like the streams that in the desert grow.  
In every tear, a promise sown,  
In joyful harvest, we are home.  

Verse 2:  
As we sow in tears, we know,  
Joy will rise, and hope will grow.  
With precious seed, we walk in faith,  
Bearing burdens, we seek Your grace.  
Though we weep, we will not fear,  
For in our sorrow, You are near.  
You’ve called us forth to share Your love,  
In every heartache, we rise above.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, You’ve done great things for us,  
We lift our hands, we sing in trust.  
Turn our hearts, let Your mercy flow,  
Like the streams that in the desert grow.

Bridge:  
He who goes forth, with tears in his eyes,  
Will return rejoicing, with joy that won’t die.  
Bringing sheaves of grace, we shall rejoice,  
In the love of the Father, we lift our voice.  

Chorus:

Outro:  
In joyful harvest, we are home,  
In Your embrace, we are never alone.  
You’ve done great things, we lift our song,  
In Your presence, Lord, we belong.
