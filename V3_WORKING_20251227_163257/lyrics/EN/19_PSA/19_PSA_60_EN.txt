[TITLE]
O God, Turn to Us Again

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O God, we call upon Your name,  
In valleys deep, we feel the shame.  
You've cast us off, our hearts in fear,  
Yet still we long for You, draw near.  
The earth has trembled, we are torn,  
In our despair, our hope reborn.  

Chorus:  
O God, turn to us again,  
Heal our hearts, mend every pain.  
With Your right hand, deliver us,  
In Your truth, we place our trust.  
O God, turn to us again,  
Let Your mercy be our friend.  

Verse 2:  
You’ve shown us hard things, we’ve drank our fill,  
The wine of astonishment, yet still,  
You give a banner for those who fear,  
In truth we stand, Your love is near.  
From Gilead to the shores of grace,  
You call us forth, we seek Your face.  

Chorus:  
O God, turn to us again,  
Heal our hearts, mend every pain.  
With Your right hand, deliver us,  
In Your truth, we place our trust.  
O God, turn to us again,  
Let Your mercy be our friend.  

Bridge:  
Who will lead us to the stronghold bright?  
Who will guide us through the dark of night?  
You have not left us, O God of might,  
Through You we stand, and in You we fight.  

Chorus:  
O God, turn to us again,  
Heal our hearts, mend every pain.  
With Your right hand, deliver us,

Outro:  
Through You we'll rise, with courage bold,  
In every trial, Your love we hold.  
O God, turn to us again,  
In our hearts, Your peace descend.
