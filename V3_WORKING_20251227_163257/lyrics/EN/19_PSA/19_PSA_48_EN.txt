Verse 1  
Great is the Lord, we lift His name,  
In the city where His glory reigns.  
Mount Zion stands, a beacon bright,  
The joy of all the earth, our guiding light.  
In every palace, His love is known,  
A refuge for the weary, we’re not alone.  

Chorus  
Oh, let Mount Zion rejoice,  
Daughters of Judah, lift your voice!  
For God is with us, forever we’ll sing,  
In the city of our God, our praises ring.

In the midst of His temple, we long to know.
