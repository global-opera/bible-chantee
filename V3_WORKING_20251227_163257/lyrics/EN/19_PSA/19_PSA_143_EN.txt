[TITLE]
Hear My Prayer, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Hear my prayer, O Lord, I cry,  
In Your faithfulness, answer me,  
In righteousness, draw near, I sigh,  
For no one lives, just You can see.  
The enemy has pressed me low,  
In darkness deep, my spirit's worn,  
Yet in my heart, Your love will grow,  
Through every trial, I'll be reborn.

Chorus:  
Oh, I thirst for You, my soul's desire,  
In the morning light, let kindness rise.  
Lead me on, O God, my heart's fire,  
Deliver me, my refuge, my prize.  
I lift my hands, to You I run,  
Quicken me, Lord, till the race is won.

Verse 2:  
I remember days, Your works I see,  
The wonders of Your mighty hand.  
Teach me, Lord, to follow Thee,  
In paths of truth, help me to stand.  
My spirit faileth, hear my plea,  
Hide not Your face, don’t let me fall.  
In Your embrace, let me be free,  
In You, O Lord, I trust through all.

Chorus:  
Oh, I thirst for You, my soul's desire,

Bridge:  
For Your name's sake, bring my soul out,  
Your mercy flows, my fears cast down.  
In the land of uprightness, I’ll shout,  
With grateful heart, I'll wear the crown.  
Cut off my foes, let peace abide,  
In Your embrace, I will reside.
