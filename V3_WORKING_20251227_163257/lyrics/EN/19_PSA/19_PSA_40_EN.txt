[TITLE]
From the Pit to the Praise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I waited patiently for You, Lord,  
You heard my cry, my heart restored.  
You lifted me from the depths of despair,  
Set my feet on solid ground, I'm standing there.  

Chorus  
You’ve put a new song in my heart,  
A melody of grace, where hope can start.  
Many will see, and they will fear,  
Trust in the Lord, for You are near.  

Verse 2  
Blessed is the man who makes You his trust,  
Not swayed by pride, but holding fast, You’re just.  
Your wondrous works, they cannot be told,  
Your thoughts toward me are treasures of gold.  

Chorus  
You’ve put a new song in my heart,  
A melody of grace, where hope can start.  
Many will see, and they will fear,  
Trust in the Lord, for You are near.  

Bridge  
O Lord, my God, Your mercy I seek,  
In my weakness, Your strength is unique.  
Let Your lovingkindness preserve my soul,  
I surrender to You, and I am made whole.  

Verse 3  
In the great assembly, I will proclaim,  
Your faithfulness, O Lord, I will not shame.  
Though I am poor and needy, You think of me,  
My help, my deliverer, forever I’ll be free.  

Chorus  
You’ve put a new song in my heart,  
A melody of grace, where hope can start.  
Many will see, and they will fear,  
Trust in the Lord, for You are near.  

Outro  
Let all who seek You rejoice and be glad,  
In Your salvation, Lord, we are so glad.  
The Lord be magnified, our voices raised high,  
Forever we’ll sing, our praises to the sky.
