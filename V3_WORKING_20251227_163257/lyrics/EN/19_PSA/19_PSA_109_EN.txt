[TITLE]
In Your Mercy, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Hold not Thy peace, O God of grace,  
When wicked words surround my place.  
They rise with hate, they speak untrue,  
Yet in my heart, my love is You.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, Lord, deliver me,  
For I am weak, my soul's in need.  
Let them curse, but bless Thou instead,  
For in Your hand, my hope is led.  

Verse 2:  
They’ve turned their hearts, their tongues like knives,  
Against the broken, where love thrives.  
But I will seek Your face in prayer,  
For You, O Lord, are always there.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, Lord, deliver me,  
For I am weak, my soul's in need.  
Let them curse, but bless Thou instead,  
For in Your hand, my hope is led.  

Bridge:  
As shadows fade and burdens grow,  
My heart cries out, my spirit knows.  
You stand with me in my despair,  
O Lord, my God, I lift my prayer.  

Verse 3:  
Let shame be theirs, let mercy flow,  
Your love transcends what man may show.  
I’ll praise Your name, lift high my voice,  
In You, my heart will always rejoice.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, Lord, deliver me,  
For I am weak, my soul's in need.  
Let them curse, but bless Thou instead,  
For in Your hand, my hope is led.  

Outro:  
I will greatly praise You, Lord,  
Among the multitudes adored.  
For You stand near the poor and meek,  
In Your embrace, my heart will seek.
