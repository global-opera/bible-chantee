[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O give thanks unto the Lord, for His mercy shines so bright,  
In the midst of every storm, He’s my refuge, my delight.  
Let Israel now lift their voice, let the house of Aaron sing,  
For His love endures forever, our salvation, He will bring.  

Chorus:  
His mercy endures, oh, forever,  
In every trial, He’s my song.  
With the Lord on my side, I will not fear,  
In His arms, I will belong.

I called upon the Lord in distress, He answered from above,

Though nations rise and gather ‘round, in His name, I stand tall,  
For the right hand of the Lord is strong, I will not fear at all.  

Chorus:

In His goodness, I will trust, for His mercy makes me whole, not sad.
