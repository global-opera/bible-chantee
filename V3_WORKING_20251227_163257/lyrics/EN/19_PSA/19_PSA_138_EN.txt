[TITLE]
With My Whole Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I will praise You with my whole heart,  
Before the heavens, I lift my song,  
In Your temple, I find my place,  
For Your lovingkindness leads me on.  
You’ve magnified Your Word, O Lord,  
Above all names, forever true,  
When I cried, You answered me,  
Strengthened my soul, my hope renewed.

Chorus  
Great is Your glory, O Lord, we sing,  
All the earth shall praise You, our King.  
In the midst of trouble, You revive my soul,  
Your mercy endures, You make me whole.

Verse 2  
Though You are high, You lift the low,  
You know my heart, You see my pain.  
When fear surrounds, I will not fear,  
Your right hand saves, I’ll call Your name.  
Kings will bow down and hear Your word,  
In humble awe, they sing Your praise,  
For Your promises are ever sure,  
In every moment, my heart will raise.

Chorus  
Great is Your glory, O Lord, we sing,  
All the earth shall praise You, our King.  
In the midst of trouble, You revive my soul,  
Your mercy endures, You make me whole.

Bridge  
You perfect all that concerns me, Lord,  
Your faithfulness remains, forevermore.  
Forsake not the works of Your hands, I pray,  
In every season, Your love will stay.

Chorus  
Great is Your glory, O Lord, we sing,  
All the earth shall praise You, our King.  
In the midst of trouble, You revive my soul,  
Your mercy endures, You make me whole.

Outro  
With my whole heart, I worship You,  
In every breath, I’ll sing what’s true.  
Your love surrounds, I’m never alone,  
Forever I’ll praise, my heart is Your home.
