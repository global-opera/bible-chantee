Verse 1  
Why do the wicked boast with pride,  
In their deceitful schemes they hide?  
Oh, mighty man, your words are sharp,  
Yet my heart clings to the truth, my hope, my spark.  
For the goodness of God endures forever,  
In every storm, You are my shelter.  

Chorus  
I will trust in Your mercy,  
In the house of the Lord, I'll stand.  
Like a green olive tree, I flourish,  
In Your presence, I find my land.  
You’ve done great things, I will praise You,  
Your name is good, I will wait on You.  

Verse 2  
Those who speak with a tongue of lies,  
Their end is near; they cannot rise.  
But I will laugh, I will not fear,  
For my strength is found in You, so near.  
The righteous see and know the way,  
In Your mercy, Lord, I choose to stay.
