[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the wilderness, I hear Your call,  
Pleading for hearts that have lost it all.  
You spoke to a mother, with mercy divine,  
"Come back to Me, for your love is mine."  

Chorus:  
Oh, return to your first love,  
Where the harvest flows from above.  
In Your arms, Lord, I find my rest,  
Betrothed in faith, forever blessed.  

Verse 2:  
You hedged my path with thorns of grace,  
To turn my heart back to Your embrace.  
In the echoes of longing, I hear You say,  
“Leave behind the lovers, come walk My way.”  

Chorus:  
Oh, return to your first love,  
Where the harvest flows from above.  
In Your arms, Lord, I find my rest,  
Betrothed in faith, forever blessed.  

Bridge:  
You give me corn and wine anew,  
In every season, I trust in You.  
With lovingkindness, You draw me near,  
In the valley of hope, I’ll cast away fear.  

Verse 3:  
No more Baalim, I’ll call You near,  
My heart rejoices, for You are here.  
In faithfulness, You make me whole,  
With Your Spirit, You’ve captured my soul.  

Chorus:  
Oh, return to your first love,  
Where the harvest flows from above.  
In Your arms, Lord, I find my rest,  
Betrothed in faith, forever blessed.  

Outro:  
You are my God, and I am Yours,  
In Your mercy, my heart restores.  
Forever, Lord, I will sing Your praise,  
In the light of Your love, my heart will stay.
