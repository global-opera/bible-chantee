[TITLE]
Return to Your First Love

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of Your mercy,  
You called me by my name,  
Though I wandered far from You,  
Your love remains the same.  
Like a bride who lost her way,  
I pursued the fleeting things,  
Yet Your heart is ever faithful,  
And to me, Your promise clings.

Chorus:  
Return to Your first love,  
O Israel, hear the call,  
In the silence and the longing,  
You will find me when you fall.  
For the King of all creation,  
Yearns to gather you again,  
In the arms of sweet redemption,  
You will know Your God, amen.

Verse 2:  
I remember all Your kindness,  
How you bought me with a price,  
Fifteen pieces for my freedom,  
And in grace, You made me rise.  
Though I strayed to other idols,  
In the night, I lost my way,  
But Your light breaks through the darkness,  
And Your truth will guide my day.

Bridge:

In the stillness, hear Him sing,

In His goodness, be inspired.  
David’s heart, a guiding light,  
In His presence, find your fight.  
Fear the Lord, and trust His grace,  
For in Him, you’ll find your place.
