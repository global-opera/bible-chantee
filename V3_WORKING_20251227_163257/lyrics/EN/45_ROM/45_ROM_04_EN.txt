[TITLE]
Faith Like Abraham

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
What shall we say of our father, Abraham?  
In his heart, he believed, and it was counted as grace.  
Not by works, for we stand before You, Lord,  
In the faith of his promise, our souls find their place.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed are those, whose sins are erased,  
Forgiven and free, in Your mercy embraced.  
Counted as righteous, not by what we do,  
But by faith in the One, who brings life anew.  

[Verse 2]  
He walked in the promise, against all the odds,  
In hope he believed, as he gazed at the stars.  
Though deadness surrounded, he never lost sight,  
For the God who brings life, He ignites our hearts.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed are those, whose sins are erased,  
Forgiven and free, in Your mercy embraced.  
Counted as righteous, not by what we do,  
But by faith in the One, who brings life anew.  

[Bridge]  
We stand on the promise, as children of grace,  
The faith of our father, our hearts now embrace.  
For it's not by the law, but by faith we are saved,  
In Christ, we are risen, in Him we are made.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed are those, whose sins are erased,  
Forgiven and free, in Your mercy embraced.  
Counted as righteous, not by what we do,  
But by faith in the One, who brings life anew.  

[Outro]  
So we lift up our voices, in worship we stand,  
In the faith of our father, united we’ll stand.  
For what He has promised, He’s faithful to keep,  
In the grace of our Savior, our souls He will reap.
