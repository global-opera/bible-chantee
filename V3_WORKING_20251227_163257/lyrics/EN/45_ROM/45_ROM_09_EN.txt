[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I speak the truth in Christ, my heart is heavy,  
For my kin, my people, in sorrow I stand,  
Oh, Lord, I long for mercy to flow,  
For all Your children, drawn close by Your hand.

Chorus:  
You call the weary, the lost and the broken,  
In the depths of our need, Your love will not fade.  
In Isaac’s promise, Your mercy is spoken,  
Children of the promise, forever displayed.

Verse 2:  
Not all who are called are the children of flesh,  
But those who believe, they walk in the light,  
As it was written, You choose and You bless,  
In Your perfect will, our hearts take flight.

Chorus:  
You call the weary, the lost and the broken,  
In the depths of our need, Your love will not fade.  
In Isaac’s promise, Your mercy is spoken,  
Children of the promise, forever displayed.

Bridge:  
Who are we, O Lord, to question Your ways?  
The Potter shapes clay with love and with grace.  
You’ve shown us Your power, Your glory revealed,  
In the vessels of mercy, our hearts are healed.

Chorus:  
You call the weary, the lost and the broken,  
In the depths of our need, Your love will not fade.  
In Isaac’s promise, Your mercy is spoken,  
Children of the promise, forever displayed.

Outro:  
Though the sands of the sea may seem like a throng,  
A remnant shall rise, to You we belong.  
With faith as our anchor, we trust in Your name,  
Children of the promise, forever the same.
