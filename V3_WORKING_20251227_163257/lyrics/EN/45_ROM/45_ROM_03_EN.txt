[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
What advantage, Lord, do we see,  
In Your word that sets us free?  
For the oracles, You’ve shared with grace,  
In every heart, You find a place.  
Though some may stray, doubt may rise,  
Your truth remains; we lift our eyes.  
Let every man be a liar, but You,  
In faithfulness, Your Word is true.

Chorus  
You are just, O God, our guide,  
In Your grace, we now abide.  
Though all have sinned, and none are right,  
In Christ, we find the light.  
Justified by faith alone,  
In Your love, we’ve found our home.

Verse 2  
No one seeks, no one understands,  
Lost in sin, we need Your hands.  
Our throats may speak with bitter lies,  
Yet still, You see through our disguise.  
Destruction waits for those who stray,  
But in Your mercy, lead the way.  
For all have fallen, yet all can rise,  
In Your embrace, we claim the prize.
