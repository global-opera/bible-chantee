[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In quiet whispers, I come to You,  
Seeking wisdom, my heart made new.  
Should I speak of vain thoughts, or lift my prayer,  
For Your truth, O Lord, is beyond compare.  
The weight of my sin, I lay at Your feet,  
In the shadow of grace, my soul finds defeat.  

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
You are the hope that will never cease.  
Though I am weak, Your strength is near,  
In the stillness, I cast off my fear.  

Verse 2  
What is man, that You would take notice?  
Yet in my darkness, You shine the brightest.  
I wander in search, but find no way,  
Until Your light guides me; show me the way.  
For the wicked may toil, and the oppressor fight,  
But in Your arms, Lord, I find my light.  

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
You are the hope that will never cease.  
Though I am weak, Your strength is near,  
In the stillness, I cast off my fear.  

Bridge  
So I lift my hands, surrender so sweet,  
In the silence, Your love is complete.  
Let not my heart stray, nor my spirit wane,  
For in Your grace, I’m forever changed.  

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
You are the hope that will never cease.  
Though I am weak, Your strength is near,  
In the stillness, I cast off my fear.  

Outro  
In Your presence, I will remain,  
Forever anchored, free from shame.  
As I seek Your face, my heart will soar,  
In Your presence, Lord, forevermore.
