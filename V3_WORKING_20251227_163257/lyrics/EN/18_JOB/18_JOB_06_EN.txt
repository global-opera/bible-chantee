Verse 1  
Oh, my grief, it weighs so heavy,  
Like the sands of the endless sea.  
The arrows of the Almighty,  
They pierce and swallow me.  
Where is the strength that I long for?  
What is this life that I bear?  
In the silence of my sorrow,  
Lord, I find You everywhere.

Chorus  
In my grief, I call to You,  
In the darkness, You shine through.  
When my spirit's worn and weak,  
Your love is the hope I seek.  
Though the trials may surround me,  
You’re the light that guides my way.  
In my grief, I call to You,  
Lord, my heart will choose to stay.

Verse 2  
Friends have turned like fleeting waters,  
Promises fade in the sun.  
Yet I seek Your understanding,  
In this battle, You are the One.  
Teach me, Lord, how to be silent,  
Help me see where I have erred.  
Though my words may seem like whispers,  
I know, Lord, You’ve always cared.
