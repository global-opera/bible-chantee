[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the silence, I am waiting,  
Longing for Your voice to speak,  
Oh, the secrets of Your wisdom,  
Deeper than the ocean's deep.  
You know my heart, You see my ways,  
In Your light, all shadows fade,  
If I prepare and stretch my hands,  
I find my hope in where You stand.  

(Chorus)  
In Your presence, Lord, I rise,  
My face unspotted, free from lies,  
I’ll cast my burdens far away,  
In Your embrace, I find my stay.  
You lift me high, as morning bright,  
My heart renewed in Your pure light.  

(Verse 2)  
Who can fathom all Your glory?  
Higher than the heavens above,  
Yet You whisper in my story,  
Filling me with perfect love.  
Let not wickedness dwell in me,  
I’ll lay it down, my heart will be,  
Steadfast in hope, I will not fear,  
You are my refuge, always near.  

(Chorus)  
In Your presence, Lord, I rise,  
My face unspotted, free from lies,  
I’ll cast my burdens far away,

My heart renewed in Your pure light.  

(Bridge)  
And when the storms of life surround,  
I’ll rest secure, my peace is found,  
For in Your arms, I will remain,  
No fear can touch me, I’ll proclaim.  
The eyes of the wicked shall not see,  
But I am safe, forever free.  

(Chorus)

(Outro)  
In Your presence, Lord, I stand,  
Holding tightly to Your hand,  
Forever changed, my spirit soars,  
In Your love, I’m home once more.
