[TITLE]
In Your Light, We Stand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of the night,  
Dominion rests in Your embrace,  
Fear and wonder fill the skies,  
You make peace in every place.  
Countless armies, all around,  
Your light upon us shines so bright,  
Yet in awe, we gather here,  
How can we stand before Your might?  

Chorus:  
In Your light, we stand,  
In Your grace, we find our way,  
Though we are but dust,  
You call us by name today.  
With hearts laid bare,  
We seek Your holy face,  
In Your light, O Lord,  
We find our resting place.  

Verse 2:  
Even moonlight fades away,  
Stars, they tremble in Your sight,  
How can we, mere fragile clay,  
Be justified in Your pure light?  
Yet You lift us from the ground,  
From the depths, You call us forth,  
Though we’re weak, in You we’re found,  
Your love reveals our true worth.  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
Oh, what is man that You care,  
That You would give Your heart to save?  
In our weakness, we are strong,  
In Your arms, we are brave.  
You shine Your light upon our path,  
Guiding us through every storm,  
In surrender, we find peace,  
In Your love, our hearts are warm.

Outro:  
In Your light, we find our home,  
In Your love, we are never alone.  
With every breath, we’ll praise Your name,  
Forever, Lord, we’re changed the same.
