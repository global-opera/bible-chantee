[TITLE]
In the Wildness of Your Creation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the wildness of Your creation,  
You know the time when life begins,  
The hinds in labor, casting sorrows,  
You guide their hearts, Your love it wins.  
From the mountains high to valleys low,  
You send the wild ones free to roam,  
In every heart, Your strength does show,  
You’ve built for them a sacred home.

Chorus:  
Oh, how great is our God,  
In majesty and might,  
You’ve filled the earth with wonder,  
Your wisdom shines so bright.  
From the eagle soaring high,  
To the horse that runs with pride,  
All creation sings Your glory,  
In You, we will abide.

Verse 2:  
The peacock’s wings, so bright and splendid,  
The ostrich leaves her eggs in dust,  
Yet in their care, You are defending,  
Your plans for them, we trust, we trust.  
The hawk in flight, the eagle rising,  
You command the skies above,  
In every creature, life surprising,  
Your heart reveals the depths of love.

Bridge:  
You know the paths we wander,  
You see our every care,  
In wilderness and wonder,  
Your presence lingers there.  
With every breath, we praise You,  
In every tear, we find,  
The strength that comes from knowing,  
Your love will never bind.

Outro:  
In the wildness of Your creation,  
We find our peace, our song, our place,  
Forever held in Your embrace,  
In the wildness of Your grace.
