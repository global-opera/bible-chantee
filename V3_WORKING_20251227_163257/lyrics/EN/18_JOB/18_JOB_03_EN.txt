[TITLE]
A Cry in the Darkness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In shadows deep, my heart does ache,  
The day I came, my soul did break.  
Let darkness veil the time I was born,  
In silence, I mourn, in sorrow, I mourn.  
Oh, let that night, with no voice sing,  
A solitary echo of forgotten things.  

Chorus  
Why is light given to the weary soul?  
In the depths of pain, I seek to be whole.  
When the troubles rise like a raging sea,  
Oh Lord, hear my cry, come set me free.  

Verse 2  
With kings and rulers, I long to rest,  
In a place of peace, where the heart is blessed.  
For the wicked cease, and the weary find peace,  
In the stillness of night, let my sorrows cease.  
But here I stand, with sighs like the rain,  
Seeking for comfort amidst all this pain.  

Chorus  
Why is light given to the weary soul?  
In the depths of pain, I seek to be whole.

Bridge  
Oh, I dig for hope, like treasures untold,  
In the silence, I find, Your love makes me bold.  
Though darkness surrounds, Your light still remains,  
In the midst of my fears, I'll trust in Your name.  

Chorus  
Why is light given to the weary soul?  
In the depths of pain, I seek to be whole.

Outro  
With every sigh, with every plea,  
I lift my heart, I trust in Thee.  
In the shadowed night, Your grace will unfold,  
In the midst of my pain, Your love I behold.
