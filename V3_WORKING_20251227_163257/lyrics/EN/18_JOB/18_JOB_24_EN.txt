[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Your Light

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence, Lord, we seek Your grace,  
When the world is lost, and hearts misplace.  
The needy cry, the widow's tears,  
Yet in our pain, You draw us near.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your light, we find our way,  
Though the darkness tries to steal the day.  
You are our hope, our refuge strong,  
In the depths of night, we sing our song.  

Verse 2  
Wild asses roam, in the wilderness,  
While the hungry long for Your righteousness.  
They pluck the fatherless from love's embrace,  
But You, O Lord, bring us to grace.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your light, we find our way,  
Though the darkness tries to steal the day.  
You are our hope, our refuge strong,  
In the depths of night, we sing our song.  

Bridge  
Though the wicked rise and the earth may quake,  
Your truth remains, no fear can shake.  
You see the heart, the wounds we hide,  
In Your mercy, we abide.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your light, we find our way,  
Though the darkness tries to steal the day.  
You are our hope, our refuge strong,  
In the depths of night, we sing our song.  

Outro  
So we lift our eyes, to the heavens above,  
In the arms of grace, we find Your love.  
Though trials come, we will not fall,  
For in Your light, we stand tall.
