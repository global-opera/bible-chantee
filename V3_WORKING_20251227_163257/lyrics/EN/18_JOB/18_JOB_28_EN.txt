Verse 1:  
In the depths where silver lies,  
And the gold is shaped with care,  
He sets an end to darkness,  
His light is everywhere.  
From the earth, our bread will rise,  
With fire burning bright,  
He knows the hidden treasures,  
The jewels of His might.

Chorus:  
Oh, where shall wisdom be found?  
In the heart of the Holy One,

To the wisdom that He gives,  
In His grace, we find our prayer,  
In His truth, forever live.
