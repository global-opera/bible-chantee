[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Your Hand

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the bitterness of my soul, I cry,  
Lord, hear my weary heart, don’t pass me by.  
Do you see my pain, the weight I bear?  
In the silence of the night, I seek your care.  

(Chorus)  
You made me from the dust, Lord, I am Yours,  
Fashioned by Your hands, my spirit soars.  
In the shadow of Your hand, I find my rest,  
Though my heart is heavy, I am truly blessed.  

(Verse 2)  
You know my heart, my every sin,  
Yet in Your mercy, new life begins.  
When darkness surrounds, and I feel alone,  
Your light will guide me; I am never on my own.  

(Chorus)  
You made me from the dust, Lord, I am Yours,  
Fashioned by Your hands, my spirit soars.  
In the shadow of Your hand, I find my rest,  
Though my heart is heavy, I am truly blessed.  

(Bridge)  
Every tear I shed, You hold them near,  
In my affliction, I know You hear.  
Though I may falter, Your grace remains,  
In the midst of my sorrow, I’ll sing Your name.  

(Chorus)  
You made me from the dust, Lord, I am Yours,  
Fashioned by Your hands, my spirit soars.  
In the shadow of Your hand, I find my rest,  
Though my heart is heavy, I am truly blessed.  

(Outro)  
So I lay my burdens down, before Your throne,  
In the quiet of Your love, I am not alone.  
Whisper peace to my soul, as I seek Your face,  
In the shadow of Your hand, I find my grace.
