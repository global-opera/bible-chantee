[TITLE]
In the Wilderness of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the night,  
Where the wilderness calls my name,  
David sought the heart of God,  
Amidst the fear, amidst the shame.  
With a heart that would not strike,  
He stood for what is true,  
For the Lord's anointed one,  
He chose to be faithful, too.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the grace that covers me,  
In the battles that I face,  
I will trust in Your decree,  
In the wilderness of grace.  
You deliver from my foes,  
You protect me with Your hand,  
In the silence, in the dark,  
I will follow where You stand.  

Verse 2:  
Saul lay sleeping by the fire,  
With a spear that held his pride,  
David whispered to his friend,  
“Let not vengeance be our guide.”  
For the Lord shall judge the heart,  
In His time, He'll bring to light,  
Trusting in the righteous path,  
We'll walk through the darkest night.

Bridge:  
And when the world surrounds me,  
And the giants roar their sound,  
I will lift my voice to heaven,  
In Your love, I am found.  
Your mercy flows like rivers,  
In the trials, I will see,  
That the Lord who fights my battles,  
Is forever close to me.
