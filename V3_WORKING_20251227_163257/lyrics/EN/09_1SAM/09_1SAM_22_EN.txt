Verse 1  
In the shadows of the cave, I find my place,  
Brothers gather 'round, in a time of grace.  
Distressed and weary, lost in the night,  
You are my captain, leading with Your light.  

Chorus  
O God, my refuge, I will trust in Thee,  
In the depths of sorrow, You set me free.  
When the storms surround me, I will stand my ground,  
In the heart of the battle, Your love will abound.  

Verse 2  
I called on the king, for my family’s care,  
In the land of Moab, found solace there.  
But the prophet spoke, “Go forth to Judah’s land,”  
With a heart of courage, I will take my stand.

Bridge  
Though the enemy rages, and darkness draws near,  
I will not falter, for I know You are here.  
With my brother Abiathar, we’ll face the night,  
For in Your presence, we will find our light.  

Verse 3  
From the depths of despair, we rise again,  
With faith as our armor, we’ll call on Your name.  
Though the world may crumble, and friends may flee,  
With You as my refuge, my heart will be free.
