[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Recover All

[Verse 1]  
When David returned to Ziklag's shore,  
His heart was heavy, for his home was no more.  
The flames were rising, the cries filled the night,  
Their loved ones taken, lost to the fight.  
He wept with his men, till their voices grew weak,  
In the midst of their sorrow, his courage would speak.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, we trust in Your faithful hand,  
You guide us through battles, through trials we stand.  
When hope seems lost, and our hearts feel small,  
We will rise up and pursue, for You will recover all.  

[Verse 2]  
David sought You, Lord, with the ephod near,  
"Shall I chase the enemy? Will victory appear?"  
You answered him boldly, “Pursue, you shall find,  
For I will deliver, I’ll strengthen your mind.”  
With four hundred men, through the night they pressed on,  
In the darkest of valleys, the dawn would soon dawn.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, we trust in Your faithful hand,  
You guide us through battles, through trials we stand.  
When hope seems lost, and our hearts feel small,  
We will rise up and pursue, for You will recover all.  

[Bridge]  
In the midst of despair, You bring forth a light,  
You turn our mourning into joy, our darkness to bright.  
When the enemy roars, and our spirits feel torn,  
We lift up our voices, for in You, we are reborn.  

[Verse 3]  
They found a young man, weak but alive,  
He spoke of the enemy, where they would thrive.  
Through the grace of Your hand, they found their way,  
With hearts full of courage, they fought through the fray.  
Not one was forgotten, all was restored,  
In the arms of the Father, they praised and adored.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, we trust in Your faithful hand,  
You guide us through battles, through trials we stand.  
When hope seems lost, and our hearts feel small,  
We will rise up and pursue, for You will recover all.  

[Outro]  
So let us remember, in shadows or light,  
Our God is with us, our hope burning bright.  
With faith as our shield, and love as our call,  
We will rise up and pursue, for You will recover all.
