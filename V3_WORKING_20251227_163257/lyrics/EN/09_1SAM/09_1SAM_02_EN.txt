[TITLE]
Exalted in the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hannah’s heart rejoices, in the Lord she sings,  
With a voice of triumph, her praises rise on wings.  
No one is like You, O Holy One,  
You break the bows of the mighty, and lift the lowly, Your will be done.  

Chorus  
There is none, none like You, Lord,  
In Your strength, we stand restored.  
You raise the humble from the dust,  
In Your salvation, Lord, we trust.  

Verse 2  
The barren shall bear fruit, the hungry filled with grace,  
You bring the dead to life, and the weary to their place.  
You judge the ends of the earth, and silence every foe,  
With a thunder from heaven, Your power we will know.  

Chorus  
There is none, none like You, Lord,  
In Your strength, we stand restored.  
You raise the humble from the dust,  
In Your salvation, Lord, we trust.  

Bridge  
Oh, God of knowledge, weigh our hearts,  
In our weakness, You are strong,  
You lift us high above the fray,  
In Your love, we belong.  

Verse 3  
Samuel grew in favor, ministering in light,  
While the world around him faltered, his heart remained so right.  
You call the faithful, and the righteous stand so tall,  
In the shadow of Your glory, O Lord, we heed Your call.  

Chorus  
There is none, none like You, Lord,  
In Your strength, we stand restored.  
You raise the humble from the dust,  
In Your salvation, Lord, we trust.  

Outro  
So we lift our hands in worship, and our hearts in prayer,  
For You are our foundation, in Your love, we find our care.  
Exalted is the Lord, our Rock, our guiding light,  
In Your grace, we find our hope, forever shining bright.
