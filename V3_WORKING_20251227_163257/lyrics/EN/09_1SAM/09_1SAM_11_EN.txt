[TITLE]
The Dawn of Our Deliverance

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of despair, the cries arose,  
From Jabeshgilead, the fear exposed.  
Nahash, the foe, with a heart so cold,  
Demanded their eyes, a tale of old.  
But hope was not lost, for the Spirit came,  
Upon Saul’s heart, igniting a flame.

Chorus:  
Oh, the dawn of our deliverance,  
When courage breaks the night.  
With the strength of the Almighty,  
We will stand and fight.  
For the Lord has won the battle,  
He’s our shield and guide,  
In His love, we find our refuge,  
In His grace, we abide.

Verse 2:  
The messengers ran, with tidings of grace,  
“Tomorrow, help will come, the Lord’s embrace.”  
With hearts now lifted, the people declared,  
“We’ll rise tomorrow, our faith prepared.”  
Three hundred thousand, united we stand,  
In the name of our Savior, we take our stand.

Chorus:

For the Lord has won the battle,

Bridge:  
And when the dawn broke, the battle was won,  
Not by our strength, but by the Son.  
No more fear, no more shame,  
For today, we’re free, we proclaim His name.  
“Salvation is here, let our voices rise,  
In unity we gather, beneath the skies!”

Outro:  
So let us renew our hearts in this place,  
In Gilgal we gather, to seek His face.  
With peace offerings, our praises we bring,  
For the Lord has made us, forever His King.  
Yes, the dawn of deliverance, our song we'll raise,  
In joyful surrender, we offer our days.
