[TITLE]
Anointed to Lead

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the sepulchre,  
Two men speak of hope anew,  
"Your father’s heart is heavy, son,  
He seeks for you, his love so true.  
From this day, you are called to rise,  
With oil poured out upon your head,  
Captain of His inheritance,  
A path of faith where angels tread.  

Chorus  
Anointed to lead, with the Spirit’s fire,  
Turned into another, lifted ever higher,  
In the presence of the prophets, we will sing,  
For the Lord has chosen you, oh, mighty king.  

Verse 2  
Upon the plain of Tabor’s grace,  
Three men bring bread and wine,  
They greet you with a holy kiss,  
A sign of love, divine design.  
The Spirit rushes like the wind,  
In unity with those who prophesy,  
A heart transformed, a voice restored,  
In the arms of God, you’ll surely fly.  

Chorus  
Anointed to lead, with the Spirit’s fire,  
Turned into another, lifted ever higher,  
In the presence of the prophets, we will sing,  
For the Lord has chosen you, oh, mighty king.  

Bridge  
Though voices whisper doubt and fear,  
In the silence, know He’s near,  
God’s own hand will guide your way,  
With courage rise, come what may.  
See the crown of grace bestowed,  
Walk in faith, your heart ignited,  
For the Lord who leads you on,  
Will shine His light where darkness roamed.  

Chorus  
Anointed to lead, with the Spirit’s fire,  
Turned into another, lifted ever higher,  
In the presence of the prophets, we will sing,  
For the Lord has chosen you, oh, mighty king.  

Outro  
So lift your voice, proclaim His name,  
With every step, you’ll never be the same,  
In the heart of battle, let His love resound,  
Anointed to lead, in Him you are found.  
Anointed to lead, in Him you are found.
