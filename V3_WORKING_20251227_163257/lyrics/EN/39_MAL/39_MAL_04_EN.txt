[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Behold, the day is coming, burning bright,  
The proud and wicked, shall face the night.  
But to those who fear, Your name we find,  
The Sun of righteousness, our hearts entwined.  

Chorus  
Arise, arise, O Sun of grace,  
With healing wings, we seek Your face.  
We tread the ashes of the past,  
In You, O Lord, our hope will last.  

Verse 2  
Remember the law, Your words so true,  
The prophets You send, to guide us through.  
Turn our hearts, O Father, we pray,  
From the children to fathers, lead us today.  

Chorus  
Arise, arise, O Sun of grace,  
With healing wings, we seek Your face.  
We tread the ashes of the past,  
In You, O Lord, our hope will last.  

Bridge  
The day is near, we feel the call,  
To stand in truth, to heed it all.  
With every step, we seek to grow,  
In love and mercy, let Your light show.  

Chorus  
Arise, arise, O Sun of grace,  
With healing wings, we seek Your face.  
We tread the ashes of the past,  
In You, O Lord, our hope will last.  

Outro  
O Lord of hosts, we lift our cry,  
In reverence, we come, as time draws nigh.  
Let hearts unite, in love we stand,  
Until that day, we trust Your hand.
