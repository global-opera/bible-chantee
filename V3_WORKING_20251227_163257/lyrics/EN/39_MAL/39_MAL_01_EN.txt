Verse 1  
You have loved us, O Lord, in ways we can't see,  
Yet we question Your heart, when we fail to believe.  
From the mountains of promise, to valleys of grace,  
In the shadow of Your love, we seek to find our place.  

Chorus  
You are a great King, in every land You reign,  
Your name shall be magnified, through joy and through pain.  
We bow in reverence, Lord, with hearts that are pure,  
In the light of Your mercy, our spirits endure.  

Verse 2  
Like a father to children, You call us to stand,  
But we offer You shadows, instead of our hands.  
In the fire of Your altar, we lay down our shame,  
Renew us, revive us, in Your holy name.

Bridge  
From the rising of the sun, to the setting of the sea,  
Let our lives be a fragrance, a sweet offering.  
For Your name is dreadful, yet filled with love’s grace,  
We long to see Your glory, and seek Your holy face.

Outro  
So we gather before You, with all that we are,  
In the depths of our worship, You shine like a star.  
O great King of glory, our hearts You restore,  
With every breath we offer, we will love You more.
