Verse 1  
From Gilgal's heights, an angel came,  
With words of promise, in His name.  
“I brought you forth from Egypt's land,  
To claim the promise, take your stand.  
But why, O Israel, do you stray?  
Forsaking vows, you’ve lost your way.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we weep at Bochim's call,  
Remembering the love that breaks our fall.  
Covenant keeper, hear our plea,  
Restore our hearts, set us free.  

Verse 2  
In days of Joshua, we stood so tall,  
We served our God, we gave our all.  
But when the elders passed away,  
A new generation lost its way.  
They turned to idols, the world’s embrace,  
Provoking anger, forsaking grace.
