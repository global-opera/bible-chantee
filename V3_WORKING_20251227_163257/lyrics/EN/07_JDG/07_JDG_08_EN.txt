[TITLE]
The Battle of Faith

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Ephraim, where voices raised in strife,  
Gideon stood with courage, leading by faith, not by life.  
He spoke of grace and victory, of hearts that intertwine,  
"Is not the gleaning of the grapes more precious than the vine?"  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, You fight our battles, our refuge in the storm,  
When we are faint and weary, Your strength will keep us warm.  
Through every trial and shadow, Your light will guide our way,  
In the battle of our faith, we will trust and not dismay.  

[Verse 2]  
Across the Jordan’s waters, with three hundred, he pressed on,  
Faint yet pursuing boldly, till the break of dawn.  
“Give bread to weary warriors,” he called to those in need,  
But they questioned his authority, doubting his noble creed.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, You fight our battles, our refuge in the storm,  
When we are faint and weary, Your strength will keep us warm.  
Through every trial and shadow, Your light will guide our way,  
In the battle of our faith, we will trust and not dismay.  

[Bridge]  
What shall we say of Gideon, who led with heart so true?  
He sought not rule or glory, but honored only You.  
With every sword that’s lifted, with every prayer that’s said,  
We remember Your deliverance, and the path that You have led.  

[Chorus]  
Oh Lord, You fight our battles, our refuge in the storm,  
When we are faint and weary, Your strength will keep us warm.  
Through every trial and shadow, Your light will guide our way,  
In the battle of our faith, we will trust and not dismay.  

[Outro]  
So let us stand together, in unity we’ll rise,  
For the God who led us forward, will lead us to the skies.  
In the echoes of the ages, Your faithfulness resounds,  
In the battle of our hearts, Your victory abounds.  
  
[Ending]  
Oh Lord, You fight our battles, forever we will sing,  
In the name of Jesus, our Savior, and our King.  
Amen.
