[TITLE]
Shibboleth of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of strife, where shadows loom,  
Ephraim's call came, a tempest's gloom.  
"Why did you fight without us near?  
We’ll burn your house, we’ll show our fear."  
But Jephthah stood with courage bold,  
In his heart a story told.  
He called for help, but they were gone,  
Yet the Lord delivered, He carried on.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the battles we face, the trials we tread,  
In the face of the storm, we’ll not be led.  
Through the fire and flood, our hearts proclaim,  
In the name of our God, we rise in His name.  
Shibboleth of grace, let our voices sing,  
Through the struggles of life, to our Savior, we cling.  

Verse 2:  
Gilead's men stood, fierce and strong,  
In the face of division, they pressed along.  
"Say Shibboleth," they called with might,  
Yet some could not speak in the truth of the light.  
Forty-two thousand fell that day,  
In the midst of conflict, hearts led astray.  
But Jephthah judged with wisdom’s grace,  
He led his people, sought God’s embrace.  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
From Ibzan’s reign to Elon’s call,  
Through every season, He’s God over all.  
Abdon’s legacy, a story divine,  
In every judge, His purpose we find.  
Oh, let our hearts carry His flame,  
In unity, we rise, forever the same.

So here we stand, in faith we unite,  
In the shadow of grace, we walk in His light.  
With Shibboleth spoken, our hearts intertwined,  
Together in love, God's purpose defined.
