[TITLE]
In the Days of No King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the mountain, a man named Micah stood,  
With silver in his pocket, he sought to do what’s good.  
He turned to his dear mother, in her heart a prayer,  
“Blessed be the Lord, my son,” she spoke with tender care.  

Chorus:  
Oh, in the days of no king,  
Every heart goes its own way,  
Yet You, Lord, reign supreme,  
Guide our steps, we humbly pray.  
In the days of no king,  
We seek Your light to shine,  
In every heart, let love take wing,  
Oh Lord, we are Yours, forever Thine.  

Verse 2:  
With silver turned to idols, in Micah’s house of gods,  
He sought a Levite’s blessing, amidst the broken odds.  
“Come dwell here with me, friend, be a father and priest,  
I’ll give you all you need, let our faith be increased.”
