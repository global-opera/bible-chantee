[TITLE]
Judges 16 - Remember Me, O Lord!
[LYRICS]
[Verse 1]
In the night of Gaza, Samson rose up tall,
A heart full of strength, but a heart destined to fall.
The gates of the city, he tore them away,
On his shoulders he carried his pride that day.

[Chorus]
O God, remember me,
In my weakness, I implore,
Restore my strength, O Lord,
That I may serve You once more.

[Verse 2]
Delilah, sweet deceiver, she charmed his heart,
Three times she tried to tear his strength apart.
But in his arrogance, he failed to see,
His power came from the sacred covenant's decree.

[Chorus]
O God, remember me,
In my weakness, I implore,
Restore my strength, O Lord,
That I may serve You once more.

[Verse 3]
The Philistines rejoice, they praise their god,
But Samson in the shadows, weeps for eyes now flawed.
He calls upon the heavens, his voice ascends,
In his final breath, his story ends.

[Bridge]
Between two pillars, he made his stand,
The temple crumbled by his hand.
More enemies fell in death than life,
A final victory through sacrifice.

[Chorus]
O God, remember me,
In my weakness, I implore,
Restore my strength, O Lord,
That I may serve You once more.
