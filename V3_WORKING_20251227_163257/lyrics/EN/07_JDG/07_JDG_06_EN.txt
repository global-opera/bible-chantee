[TITLE] Mighty in Our Midst

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the mountain, where the battle roars,  
Israel cried out to the heavens, longing for the Lord.  
In the dens and caves, they hid their fears,  
For the Midianites came like whispers in the years.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the Lord is with us, mighty in our midst,  
Hearts once broken, find hope in His kiss.  
From the ashes, He will rise,  
With courage we will stand, as the dawn breaks the skies.  

[Verse 2]  
The prophet spoke of freedom, from chains of old,  
“I brought you from the darkness, into light untold.”  
Yet they strayed from His promise, forgot His mighty hand,  
But the call of God is faithful, to restore this land.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the Lord is with us, mighty in our midst,  
Hearts once broken, find hope in His kiss.  
From the ashes, He will rise,  
With courage we will stand, as the dawn breaks the skies.  

[Bridge]  
Gideon, oh Gideon, you felt so small,  
But the Spirit of the Lord, will answer your call.  
Take heart, you are chosen, a warrior so brave,  
In your weakness, His power, will carry and save.  

[Verse 3]  
So he built an altar, called it Jehovah-shalom,  
In the face of the darkness, found strength to stand alone.  
With a fleece laid before Him, he sought a sign,  
And the Lord in His mercy, showed grace divine.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the Lord is with us, mighty in our midst,  
Hearts once broken, find hope in His kiss.  
From the ashes, He will rise,  
With courage we will stand, as the dawn breaks the skies.  

[Outro]  
In the battles that we face, we will not be dismayed,  
For the Lord is our fortress, and in Him we are saved.  
With our hearts aligned in faith, we’ll proclaim His name,  
Mighty in our midst, forever we’ll remain.
