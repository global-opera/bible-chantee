[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O that You were near, my Brother dear,  
In the warmth of love, I’d draw You near.  
In the garden’s shade, where our hearts are laid,  
I long to hold You close, my fears allayed.  

Chorus:  
Set me as a seal upon Your heart,  
Like a flame that will never depart.  
Though the waters rise, and the storms may roar,  
Your love is the anchor, forevermore.  

Verse 2:  
I come from the wilderness, leaning on You,  
In the shade of the apple tree, our love is true.  
With spiced wine to share, let Your kindness flow,  
In the embrace of Your arms, my spirit will grow.  

Chorus:  
Set me as a seal upon Your heart,  
Like a flame that will never depart.  
Though the waters rise, and the storms may roar,  
Your love is the anchor, forevermore.  

Bridge:  
No jealousy can dim this fire,  
For love is strong, our hearts conspire.  
In the depths of night, Your voice I hear,  
Whispering sweet, “My love, draw near.”  

Verse 3:  
O dwell in my heart, let the garden bloom,  
In the palace of silver, dispel all gloom.  
You’re the keeper of my soul, my treasure bright,  
With You, my Beloved, I find my light.  

Chorus:  
Set me as a seal upon Your heart,  
Like a flame that will never depart.  
Though the waters rise, and the storms may roar,  
Your love is the anchor, forevermore.  

Outro:  
Make haste, my Beloved, come swiftly to me,  
In the mountains of spices, our love will be free.  
With the world at our feet, and hope in our eyes,  
In Your arms, my Beloved, my spirit will rise.
