[TITLE]
In the King's Favor

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
On the third day, Esther stood,  
Clothed in royal grace,  
In the king’s court, she took her place,  
With courage in her heart, she sought His face.  
With shadows looming, her spirit bright,  
Trusting in the Lord, she walked in light.  

Chorus  
In the king's favor, I will stand,  
With hope and boldness, in His hand,  
For every battle, He is near,  
In His presence, I will not fear.  
In the king's favor, we find our song,  
For in His love, we will belong.  

Verse 2  
The scepter raised, a sign of grace,  
Esther touched it, embraced her place,  
What is your petition? The king did ask,  
A banquet prepared, a noble task.  
With faith unwavering, she made her plea,  
For her people’s freedom, she sought to be free.

Bridge  
Though enemies may rise, and shadows fall,  
With every trial, He hears our call,  
He turns the tides, He lifts the low,  
In every moment, His power will show.  
Oh, let us gather, in faith proclaim,  
The victory won, in Jesus’ name!
