[Verse 1]  
In the days of a mighty king,  
Ahasuerus on his throne did reign,  
From the lands of India to Ethiopia’s shore,  
His glory and riches, we can't ignore.  
With a feast that lasted many days,  
He showed his power in so many ways.  
The heart of the king, filled with delight,  
In the palace of Shushan, beneath starlit night.

[Chorus]  
Oh, hear the call, the king’s command,  
To honor the beauty, to take a stand.  
Yet Vashti stood firm, her voice was her own,  
In courage she walked, though she felt alone.  
For truth shall rise, and the heart shall be free,  
In the shadows of power, God's light we will see.

[Verse 2]  
On the seventh day, with spirits high,  
He sought out his queen, but she wouldn’t comply.  
In anger he turned to the wise men near,  
"What must we do? Let the law be clear!"  
Memucan spoke, with wisdom profound,  
“Her choice speaks for all, let her voice resound.”  
A command went forth, across the land,  
That honor and love in the home must stand.

[Bridge]  
In the heart of the kingdom, in the court of the great,

With a spirit of unity, let our voices soar,  
In the name of the King, we’ll love evermore.
