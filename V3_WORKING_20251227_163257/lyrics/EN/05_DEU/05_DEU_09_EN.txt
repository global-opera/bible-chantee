[TITLE]
Mighty Hand, Stretched Out

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear, O Israel, the promise spoken,  
Through the Jordan, we will move as one.  
Nations greater, walls like heaven,  
Yet in Your hands, our battles are won.  
A people tall, with fearsome might,  
But You, O Lord, are our guiding light.  

Chorus  
Mighty hand, stretched out, we will stand,  
In the fire, You go before us,  
Not by our strength, but by Your grace,  
You lead us on to our rightful place.  
We remember, though we fall,  
Your mercy carries through it all.  

Verse 2  
In the wilderness, we turned aside,  
Provoked Your wrath, yet You still abide.  
Forty days on the mountain high,  
You gave us words that never die.  
Though we are stiff-necked, lost in our ways,  
Your covenant love shines through our days.  

Chorus  
Mighty hand, stretched out, we will stand,  
In the fire, You go before us,  
Not by our strength, but by Your grace,

We remember, though we fall,

Bridge  
Oh, Lord, remember Your servants true,  
Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, we call on You.  
Though stubborn hearts may lead us astray,  
Your blood has redeemed, and we'll find our way.  
Your greatness shines through every trial,  
In our rebellion, You still reconcile.  

Chorus

Outro  
So here we gather, hearts laid bare,  
In Your presence, we find our prayer.  
Mighty God, in faith we trust,  
With every step, we rise, we must.
