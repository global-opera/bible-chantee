[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
If we hearken to Your voice, O Lord,  
And keep Your commands with hearts restored,  
You’ll lift us high above the earth,  
In cities and fields, we’ll see Your worth.  
Blessings flow like rivers wide,  
In our coming and going, You’re by our side.  

Chorus  
Blessed are we in the land You give,  
In Your love, we find how to live.  
Fruits of the body, harvest so bright,  
In Your presence, Lord, we walk in light.  

Verse 2  
When our enemies rise, we shall not fear,  
For You fight for us, You are so near.  
You’ll command blessings in all we do,  
In every task, we’ll find strength in You.  
You’ll make us holy, a people so bright,  
All nations will see, we are called by Your light.  

Chorus  
Blessed are we in the land You give,  
In Your love, we find how to live.  
Fruits of the body, harvest so bright,  
In Your presence, Lord, we walk in light.  

Bridge  
But if we turn, and stray from Your way,  
Curses will come, darkness will stay.  
Lord, help us to heed each word that You say,  
To walk in Your truth, not go astray.  

Verse 3  
Yet even in trials, we’ll trust in Your grace,  
For You are our refuge, our safe place.  
With trembling hearts, we’ll keep our eyes clear,  
In our darkest moments, You’ll draw us near.  
Let us rejoice in Your goodness and might,  
For Your love endures, and we’ll take flight.  

Chorus  
Blessed are we in the land You give,  
In Your love, we find how to live.  
Fruits of the body, harvest so bright,  
In Your presence, Lord, we walk in light.  

Outro  
So we’ll hearken to Your voice, O Lord,  
In faith and in love, we lift our chord.  
Forever we’ll sing of Your wondrous ways,  
In our hearts and lives, we’ll give You praise.
