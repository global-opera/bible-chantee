Verse 1  
When You brought us to this land,  
A promise fulfilled by Your mighty hand,  
We gather here, our hearts unite,  
With the firstfruits of our lives, we bring to light.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, we worship at Your altar,  
With grateful hearts, we lift our praise,  
For in the depths of our affliction,  
You heard our cries, You showed the way.  
A land of milk and honey flows,  
In every blessing, Your love bestows.
