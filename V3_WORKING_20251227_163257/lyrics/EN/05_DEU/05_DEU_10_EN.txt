[TITLE]
Covenant of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the mountain, You called me near,  
Hew two stones of promise, Your voice I hear.  
With Your words engraved, in my heart they reside,  
Ten commands of love, forever as my guide.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we fear You, with hearts full of grace,  
We walk in Your ways, in Your warm embrace.  
You are our treasure, our covenant true,  
We serve and we love, our lives are for You.  

Verse 2  
From the depths of the valley, to the heights of the skies,  
You chose us, O Israel, with compassionate eyes.  
Circumcise our hearts, let our spirits be free,  
In the land that You promised, we'll claim victory.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we fear You, with hearts full of grace,  
We walk in Your ways, in Your warm embrace.  
You are our treasure, our covenant true,  
We serve and we love, our lives are for You.  

Bridge  
For You are God of gods, Lord of all,  
You judge the fatherless, hear the stranger’s call.  
With every breath we take, we will sing Your praise,  
For the great things You've done, throughout all our days.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we fear You, with hearts full of grace,  
We walk in Your ways, in Your warm embrace.  
You are our treasure, our covenant true,  
We serve and we love, our lives are for You.  

Outro  
As stars fill the heavens, so shall our hearts be,  
A multitude of praises, forever set free.  
In the ark of Your love, our spirits align,  
With a covenant of grace, Lord, we are forever Thine.
