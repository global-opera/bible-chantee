Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, Lord, I call Your name,  
For the heart of man is fickle, yet You remain the same.  
You taught us love is sacred, a covenant so true,  
But when our hearts grow weary, how we stray from You.  

Chorus  
Remember, O my soul, the grace that sets us free,  
For we were once in bondage, now we walk in liberty.  
You called us to be righteous, to care for those in need,  
In the shadow of Your mercy, let us plant the seed.  

Verse 2  
When the harvest is forgotten, let it bless the weak,  
For the stranger and the widow, it’s their voice we seek.  
Let us not oppress the servant, but lift his weary hand,  
For in loving our neighbor, we fulfill Your command.
