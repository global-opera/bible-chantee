[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Land of Giants

[Verse 1]  
We journeyed forth, the path ahead,  
To Bashan's realm, where giants tread.  
King Og arose, fierce in might,  
But You, O Lord, said, "Fear him not; I fight."  
With faith in heart, we took our stand,  
For all the earth is in Your hand.  

[Chorus]  
You delivered us, O mighty King,  
Through valleys deep and mountains high, we sing.  
Your hand of power, our battle cry,  
In every struggle, Lord, You are nigh.  
Your strength is ours, forever true,  
In every giant, we trust in You.  

[Verse 2]  
We marched through cities, strong and high,  
With walls that touched the endless sky.  
Yet at Your word, we pressed on through,  
For every promise, we cling to You.  
Destruction fell on foes so grand,  
All was fulfilled by Your command.

[Bridge]  
O Lord, I see the greatness of Your ways,  
The mighty deeds, the endless praise.  
Though I may wander, my heart shall yearn,  
To glimpse the land, for which I long to learn.  
But I will trust in Joshua's might,  
For in his hands, You lead us right.

[Outro]  
So lift Your eyes, behold His grace,  
For every promise, we'll find our place.  
In the land of giants, we shall stand tall,  
With God before us, we shall not fall.
