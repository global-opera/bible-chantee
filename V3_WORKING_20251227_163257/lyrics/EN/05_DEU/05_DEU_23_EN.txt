[TITLE]
In Your Holy Camp

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the night, we come,  
To seek Your face, Lord, where we belong.  
With hearts that long for Your embrace,  
We gather here, in this sacred space.  

Chorus  
Oh, keep us holy, keep us true,  
With every step, we follow You.  
In Your presence, we find our peace,  
Lord, in Your love, our souls release.  

Verse 2  
Though the world may cast us aside,  
In Your grace, Lord, we will abide.  
You turned the curse into our song,  
Because You love us, we are strong.  

Chorus  
Oh, keep us holy, keep us true,  
With every step, we follow You.  
In Your presence, we find our peace,  
Lord, in Your love, our souls release.  

Bridge  
We vow to keep the words we speak,  
To lift the broken, to love the weak.  
In every promise, may we stand,  
Holding fast to Your guiding hand.  

Verse 3  
When we walk through the fields of grace,  
To share the bounty of Your embrace.  
May we remember, with grateful hearts,  
The love You give, and how it imparts.  

Chorus  
Oh, keep us holy, keep us true,  
With every step, we follow You.  
In Your presence, we find our peace,  
Lord, in Your love, our souls release.  

Outro  
In Your holy camp, we rise and sing,  
To the God of love, our offering.  
With every breath, our hearts proclaim,  
We are Yours, forever the same.
