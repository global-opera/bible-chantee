[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
We gather here, O Lord, in grace,  
To lift our hearts, to seek Your face.  
As we remember all You’ve done,  
Your body broken, our victory won.  
In unity, we stand as one,  
Under Your love, our hearts have sprung.  

Chorus:  
In remembrance of You, we break the bread,  
In the cup of Your blood, our hope is fed.  
With every prayer and every song,  
We honor You, Lord, where we belong.  

Verse 2:  
You called us forth, to love and serve,  
To walk in truth, and with every word.  
As we reflect on the path we tread,  
Let our hearts be pure, in the light You shed.  
For we are one, both man and wife,  
In Your great love, we find our life.  

Chorus:  
In remembrance of You, we break the bread,  
In the cup of Your blood, our hope is fed.  
With every prayer and every song,  
We honor You, Lord, where we belong.  

Bridge:  
Let us examine our hearts today,  
In Your presence, Lord, we humbly pray.  
Together we rise, as we seek Your face,  
In love and truth, we find our place.  

Chorus:  
In remembrance of You, we break the bread,  
In the cup of Your blood, our hope is fed.  
With every prayer and every song,  
We honor You, Lord, where we belong.  

Outro:  
So let us gather, in love and grace,  
In the spirit of Christ, we find our place.  
United in faith, we lift Your name,  
In remembrance of You, forever the same.
