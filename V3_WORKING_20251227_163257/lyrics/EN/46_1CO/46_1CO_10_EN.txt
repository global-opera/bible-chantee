[TITLE]
One Bread, One Body

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the cloud, our fathers walked with grace,  
Through the sea, we were set free, led to a holy place.  
Drinking from the Rock, that followed every day,  
In the wilderness, Your faithfulness, You guide us on our way.  

Chorus:  
One bread, one body, united in Your love,  
We gather ‘round the table, as we lift our hearts above.  
In temptation’s hour, Lord, we seek Your light,  
Fleeing from all idols, we stand in holy might.

Verse 2:  
Lessons from the past, a warning for today,  
Do not let our hearts grow faint, or lose our way.  
In the feast of blessings, we remember what You’ve done,  
With every cup and offering, we’re one in Christ, Your Son.  

Chorus:  
One bread, one body, united in Your love,  
We gather ‘round the table, as we lift our hearts above.  
In temptation’s hour, Lord, we seek Your light,  
Fleeing from all idols, we stand in holy might.

Bridge:  
For the earth is Yours, and all that’s in the land,  
In every meal and moment, help us understand.  
To glorify Your name, in all that we pursue,  
In love and unity, our lives reflect You.

Chorus:  
One bread, one body, united in Your love,  
We gather ‘round the table, as we lift our hearts above.  
In temptation’s hour, Lord, we seek Your light,  
Fleeing from all idols, we stand in holy might.

Outro:  
So let us hold each other, in grace and truth we stand,  
With every step and heartbeat, we walk with the Lamb.  
One bread, one body, together we will rise,  
In the light of Your salvation, we lift our hearts to the skies.
