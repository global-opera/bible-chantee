[TITLE]
Return to Me

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night, You call,  
To the hearts that wander far,  
Your voice, a whisper through the ages,  
"Turn to Me, I’ll heal your scars."  
Like the prophets of old, You plead,  
"Don’t be as your fathers were,  
In their ways they found no peace,  
But in My love, your hearts can stir."

Chorus  
Return to Me, O weary soul,  
Turn from darkness, let My light make you whole.  
I am jealous for your heart,  
With mercies new, I’ll never part.  
Return to Me, the Lord of Hosts,  
In Your presence, Lord, my spirit boasts.  
Your love, my strength, my guiding light,  
I’ll follow You through darkest night.

Verse 2  
I saw a vision, a rider bold,  
Among the myrtle trees so green,  
"Who rides for justice? Who rides with grace?"  
“Fear not,” the angel said, “You've seen."  
The earth is still, but hope will rise,  
For upon the cities, I’ll pour out grace,  
Jerusalem, in mercy, I’ll abide,  
In Zion, I’ll reveal My face.

Bridge  
Oh, hear the call, the gentle nudge,  
Leave behind your ways of old,  
In every promise, find your refuge,  
In every word, His truth unfolds.  
He’s returned with love, so rich, so free,  
Pouring out His grace, calling you and me.

I’ll lead you home, into My light.
