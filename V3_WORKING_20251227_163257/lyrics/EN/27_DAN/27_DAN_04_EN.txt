Verse 1  
In the shadow of the tree, I stood in pride,  
Built a kingdom strong, in my own might,  
But a whisper from the heavens shattered my throne,  
A watcher cried, “Your heart must be shown.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Most High, You reign forever,  
Your dominion stretches wide,  
From generation to generation,  
In Your hands, our hearts abide.  
You bring the humble low,  
And lift the meek in grace,  
Oh, Most High, in Your kingdom,  
We find our resting place.  

Verse 2

And I was on the ground.  
Seven times I wandered, where the beasts would roam,  
Until my eyes were lifted, to find my way back home.
