Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains,  
Hezekiah called the land,  
"Come, gather in His presence,  
To the house where we will stand.  
For our fathers turned away,  
But His mercy still remains,  
He will heal our hearts and guide us,  
As we lift His holy name."

Chorus  
Return to the Lord, O Israel,  
With hearts prepared to seek His face,  
He is gracious, He is merciful,  
In His arms, we find our place.  
Let us sing with joy and gladness,  
For His love will never cease,  
Return to the Lord, O Israel,  
Find your hope, your rest, your peace.

Verse 2  
From Beersheba to Dan,  
The message spread like fire,  
"Turn away from every altar,  
Come and seek your heart's desire.  
For the past may bring us shame,  
But His grace can make us whole,  
He will gather the forgotten,  
And revive our weary souls."

Bridge  
Hezekiah prayed for healing,  
For the ones who sought the light,  
"Though unclean, if hearts are yearning,  
Lord, forgive us in Your sight."  
And the heavens heard our voices,  
As the Levites sang in praise,  
In the joy of our redemption,  
We lift our hands in endless grace.
