Verse 1  
When Rehoboam stood in strength and pride,  
Forsook the law, let the truth subside,  
In the fifth year, the shadows loomed,  
Shishak came, and the kingdom gloomed.  
Oh, Israel, how they turned away,  
From the Lord’s light, into dismay.  

Chorus  
Humble hearts, we cry out to You,  
In our weakness, Lord, make us anew.  
Though the storms may rise, and the battles roar,  
We seek Your face, we long for more.  
Your mercy reigns, our hope restored,  
In humble hearts, we trust the Lord.  

Verse 2  
Then came Shemaiah, with a word so true,  
“Turn back to the Lord, He is here for you.”  
They bowed before Him, their hearts in remorse,  
The Lord is righteous, our only source.  
And the heavens listened, grace poured down,  
In the midst of trials, His love abounds.

Bridge  
Oh, let us not forget, the lessons learned,  
In every trial, His heart we yearn.  
For in our weakness, He makes us strong,  
With every step, we sing His song.  
From treasures lost, to treasures found,  
In humble service, His love abounds.
