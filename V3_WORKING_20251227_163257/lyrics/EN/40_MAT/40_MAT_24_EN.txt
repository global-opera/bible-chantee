[TITLE]
Watch and Pray

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Upon the mount, You spoke so clear,  
To hearts in question, You drew near.  
“Look not at stones that will decay,  
For greater things are on the way.  

Pre-Chorus  
Wars shall rise, and shadows loom,  
Yet in Your love, we find our room.  
Though many will stray, our hearts will stay,  
In faith we trust, come what may.  

Chorus  
Watch and pray, for the hour is near,  
The Son of Man will appear,  
In power and glory, with angels bright,  
Gather Your people, bring us to light.  

Verse 2  
False prophets rise, they speak Your name,  
But in our hearts, we know the flame.  
With every trial, we’ll stand our ground,  
For in Your truth, our hope is found.  

Pre-Chorus  
Though darkness falls, we will not fear,  
For You’ve prepared us, You are near.  
When nations clash, our voices raise,  
In steadfast love, we sing Your praise.  

Chorus  
Watch and pray, for the hour is near,  
The Son of Man will appear,  
In power and glory, with angels bright,  
Gather Your people, bring us to light.  

Bridge  
As lightning strikes from east to west,  
We’ll hold our hearts, in You we rest.  
No man knows the day or the hour,  
But we will wait, for You have power.  

Chorus  
Watch and pray, for the hour is near,  
The Son of Man will appear,  
In power and glory, with angels bright,  
Gather Your people, bring us to light.  

Outro  
So let us be ready, our lamps aglow,  
In every season, Your love we’ll show.  
For heaven and earth may fade away,  
But Your words, Lord, forever stay.
