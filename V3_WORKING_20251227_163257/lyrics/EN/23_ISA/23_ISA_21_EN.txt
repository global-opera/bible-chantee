Verse 1  
In the desert, shadows rise,  
Whirlwinds whisper, hear their cries.  
A grievous vision, burdens weigh,  
The treacherous heart leads us astray.  
My loins are filled with pain, I sigh,  
Like a mother, laboring, I cry.  
Fearfulness grips my trembling soul,  
In the night, my heart won't be whole.  

Chorus  
O watchman, what of the night?  
The morning comes, yet darkness fights.  
Prepare the table, stand and see,  
For the Lord is our victory.  
Babylon has fallen, the idols broken,  
In His presence, our hearts are woken.  

Verse 2  
From the land of Seir, a call rings clear,  
"Watchman, what of the night?" we hear.  
He answers softly, "The dawn will break,  
But the night still lingers, for our souls' sake."  
In Arabia's forest, we find our rest,  
For the weary traveler, the Lord is blessed.  
They brought him water, bread for the way,  
In the midst of chaos, He’s our stay.
