[TITLE]
Behold, I Create

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In shadows deep, where hearts have strayed,  
You called us forth, though we dismayed.  
With open arms, You drew us near,  
To a nation lost, You whispered clear.  

Chorus  
Behold, I create new heavens, new earth,  
A joy in Jerusalem, a song of rebirth.  
For every tear that we have cried,  
In Your love, Lord, we will abide.  
You answer before we even call,  
In Your presence, we stand, we won't fall.  

Verse 2  
You called us out from graves of shame,  
Yet we turned back, forgetting Your name.  
In gardens of idols, we burned our pain,  
But Your mercy flows, like gentle rain.  

Chorus

Bridge  
And the wolf will lie with the lamb,  
In peace, we rest in Your holy plan.  
No more sorrow, no more strife,  
In Your arms, we find new life.  
You gather us, Your chosen ones,  
In the light of the everlasting Son.

Outro  
So we lift our hands, in surrender, we sing,

In the light of Your love, we will follow You.
