[TITLE]
Inheritance of Praise

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the land of promise, the valleys wide,  
The tribe of Judah, with the Lord as their guide,  
From the shores of salt sea, to the mountains high,  
Their borders are drawn, under God’s watchful eye.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we sing of Your faithfulness,  
Inheritances crowned in grace,  
From the rivers to the mountains,  
We declare Your holy place.  
Every promise, every blessing,  
We stand on Your sacred ground,  
In the inheritance of praise,  
Your glory shall abound.  

(Verse 2)  
Caleb and Othniel, brave hearts took the land,  
With faith like a lion, they answered Your command.  
Springs of water flowing, in the desert’s embrace,  
You give us all we need, Your love, our resting place.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we sing of Your faithfulness,  
Inheritances crowned in grace,  
From the rivers to the mountains,  
We declare Your holy place.  
Every promise, every blessing,  
We stand on Your sacred ground,  
In the inheritance of praise,  
Your glory shall abound.  

(Bridge)  
Mountains tremble, giants fall,  
In the name of Jesus, we will stand tall.  
With every city, every heart,  
You are our fortress, we will not part.  
From the valleys of Achor to the heights above,  
We walk in Your power, we walk in Your love.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we sing of Your faithfulness,  
Inheritances crowned in grace,  
From the rivers to the mountains,  
We declare Your holy place.  
Every promise, every blessing,  
We stand on Your sacred ground,

(Outro)  
In the land of promise, united we raise,  
Our voices of worship, in an endless praise.  
For in Your great mercy, we’ve found our way home,  
In the inheritance of Judah, we are never alone.
