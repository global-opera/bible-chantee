[TITLE]
The Sun Stands Still

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land where shadows loom,  
Five kings gather, hearts consumed,  
Fear arose in Jerusalem's might,  
But Your promise, Lord, shines bright.  
Gibeon cried, "Come save us now,"  
Joshua, with courage, took his vow.  
From Gilgal’s heights, he came with pride,  
For You, O Lord, walk by our side.

[Chorus]  
Oh, the sun stands still, the moon obeys,  
When we call on You in our darkest days.  
With every battle, Lord, You fight for me,  
In the face of giants, I will stand free.  
Fear not, be strong, for the victory's near,  
The Lord of hosts is with us here.

[Verse 2]  
Great stones fell, from heaven above,  
Your hand, O Lord, showed endless love.  
With swords drawn, we took our stand,  
In faith we tread, led by Your hand.  
From city to city, we claimed the ground,  
In every promise, Your glory found.  
As Joshua spoke, the heavens sighed,  
For You, O Lord, were our guide.

[Chorus]  
Oh, the sun stands still, the moon obeys,  
When we call on You in our darkest days.  
With every battle, Lord, You fight for me,  
In the face of giants, I will stand free.  
Fear not, be strong, for the victory's near,  
The Lord of hosts is with us here.

[Bridge]  
And when the shadows rise,  
We lift our eyes,  
To the One who reigns,  
In every trial and pain.  
With feet upon the necks of foes,  
In Your strength, our faith grows.  
No weapon forged against us shall stand,  
For we are safe in Your mighty hand.

[Chorus]  
Oh, the sun stands still, the moon obeys,

[Outro]  
So let the heavens declare Your praise,  
For in Your presence, we find our ways.  
With every heartbeat, we sing Your name,  
Forever, Lord, we’ll proclaim Your fame.
