[TITLE]
Choose This Day

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where our fathers stood,  
Under skies of promise, God was good.  
He led us forth from bondage deep,  
With miracles and wonders, our hearts He keeps.  
From Terah’s home to Canaan's land,  
He called our name, and we took His hand.  
Through wilderness and trials faced,  
His faithfulness, forever embraced.

Chorus  
Choose this day whom you will serve,  
The living God, in whom we swerve.  
As for me and my house, we will stand,  
With lifted hearts, we’ll trust His plan.  
He has brought us through the night,  
His mighty hand our guiding light.  
With every breath, we sing His praise,  
Our hearts declare, we will obey.

Verse 2  
He parted seas and crushed our fears,  
With every battle, He draws us near.  
The Amorites fell before His name,  
With the hornet’s might, His power proclaimed.  
In cities built by others' toil,  
We dwell in grace, our hearts unspoiled.  
His covenant stands, forever wise,  
A witness stone, beneath the skies.

Bridge  
So put away the gods of old,  
With hearts inclined, let faith be bold.  
For He is holy, our God above,  
In sincerity, we choose His love.  
We are witnesses, our voices rise,  
Proclaiming truth beneath the skies.  
With every moment, we choose His way,  
Forevermore, we will not stray.
