### Title: "In the Dry Places"

Verse 1  
In the silence of the land, we mourn,  
Judah’s gates are heavy, our hearts are torn.  
Little ones seek water, but the wells are dry,  
With empty vessels, Lord, we lift our cry.  

Chorus  
O Lord, we call upon Your name,  
In our weakness, show Your strength again.  
Though our sins are many, Your mercy's near,  
In the dry places, Lord, we long for You here.  

Verse 2  
The ground is cracked, the plowmen hide their face,  
The hind forsakes her young in this barren place.  
O, hope of Israel, don’t leave us alone,  
In our darkest hour, be our cornerstone.  

Chorus  
O Lord, we call upon Your name,  
In our weakness, show Your strength again.  
Though our sins are many, Your mercy's near,  
In the dry places, Lord, we long for You here.  

Bridge  
We’ve wandered far, our hearts astray,  
But still, we seek You, Lord, day by day.  
Tears like rivers flow for our broken land,  
Restore us, Redeemer, hold us in Your hand.  

Verse 3  
If we turn our hearts, will You heal our pain?  
We acknowledge our weakness, O Lord, break the chain.  
Do not abhor us, remember Your grace,  
In the midst of judgment, let mercy embrace.  

Chorus  
O Lord, we call upon Your name,  
In our weakness, show Your strength again.  
Though our sins are many, Your mercy's near,  
In the dry places, Lord, we long for You here.  

Outro  
We will wait on You, our faithful guide,  
For You alone can bring the rain,  
In our hearts, in this land,  
We trust in Your holy name.
