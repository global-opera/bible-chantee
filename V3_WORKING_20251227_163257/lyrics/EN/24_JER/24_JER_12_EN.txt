[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Righteous, O Lord, when I plead for your grace,  
I ask of your judgments, in this weary place.  
The wicked seem happy, their roots growing deep,  
Yet you know my heart, in your presence I weep.  

Chorus  
Pull them out like sheep, O Lord, for the day,  
When your justice will shine, and the darkness will sway.  
In the land that is mourning, let your mercy descend,  
O God of our fathers, our Savior, our Friend.  

Verse 2  
The fields are now withering, the beasts cry in vain,  
The hearts of your people are burdened with pain.  
You’ve given us heritage, but it’s torn apart,  
In the hands of our foes, still you know my heart.

Bridge  
If we’ve run with the footmen, and they wearied our souls,  
How can we stand when the horsemen take hold?  
You’ve called us to rise, to stand firm in the fight,  
For you will restore us, bring us back to Your light.

Outro  
With compassion you’ll gather, the lost and the torn,  
You’ll give us our heritage, in the light of the morn.  
In the midst of your people, let your name be our song,  
Forever we’ll trust you, to you we belong.
