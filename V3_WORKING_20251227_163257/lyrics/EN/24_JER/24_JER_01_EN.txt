[TITLE]
I Have Called You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the night, You spoke my name,  
Before the dawn of time, I was known, not in vain.  
Through the trials and the fears, You’re my guiding light,  
In the womb, You set me apart, in Your love, I delight.  

Chorus  
I have called you, I have chosen,  
Fear not, for I am with you.  
Stand firm, rise up and speak,  
Your words, they carry truth.  
Though the nations rage and tremble,  
I will guard you, I will defend,  
In the face of every battle,  
My presence will not end.  

Verse 2  
As a child, I cried, “I cannot stand,  
Yet You touch my lips, by Your mighty hand.  
To root and to build, You’ve ordained my way,  
In every word I speak, Lord, let Your truth stay.

Bridge

You’ve made me a fortress, a city of grace,  
With iron walls around me, in this holy place.

Outro  
So I rise, I will not fear,  
For You are with me, Lord, so near.

I have called you, I have chosen,  
Forever, Lord, I’ll trust in You.
