Verse 1  
In the belly of my sorrow, I cried out in despair,  
From the depths of my affliction, Lord, I know You’re there.  
You heard my cries of anguish, as the waves crashed all around,  
Yet I lift my eyes to You, my hope in You is found.

Chorus  
Salvation comes from the Lord,  
You lift me from the sea,  
With a voice of gratitude,  
I will worship faithfully.  
Though I’m cast away from sight,  
I will look to You in light,  
Your mercy brings me back to life,  
Oh, Lord, my God, You are my guide.

Verse 2

Yet in my darkest moment, I remembered You, my King,  
You brought my soul from ruin, and to You my praises sing.
