[TITLE] The Call of the Holy

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the tents, they rose against the light,  
Korah and his brothers, in their pride, took flight.  
“Why do you stand above us, when we’re all made whole?  
The Lord is among us, He knows every soul.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the Lord will show who is holy,  
He’s the fire that burns in our hearts.  
In His presence, we find our story,  
From the depths, He calls us to partake,  
In the grace that He imparts.

[Verse 2]  
Moses fell on his face, cried out to the King,  
“Separate us from wickedness, let Your glory sing!”  
The earth split wide open, swallowed sin whole,  
With a sign of His power, He made known His role.

[Bridge]  
Stand between the living, make atonement for our sin,  
With incense rising up, let the healing begin.  
In the midst of our trials, let Your mercy be our plea,  
As we gather in Your name, Lord, we seek to be free.

[Verse 3]  
Now the cloud covers us, Your glory shines so bright,  
In the face of our weakness, You are our guiding light.  
Oh, we turn from our murmurs, to worship at Your throne,  
In the covenant of Your love, we find our true home.

In the stillness, we hear You,

Lord, in all that we do,
