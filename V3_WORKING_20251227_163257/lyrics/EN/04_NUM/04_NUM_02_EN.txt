[TITLE]
By Our Standards

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the morning light, we gather near,  
By our father's house, we hold so dear.  
Each tribe united, in You, we stand,  
Lord, guide our hearts, and lead our hand.  

Chorus:  
We will pitch our tents, as You command,  
In the shadow of Your love, we make our stand.  
With the banners raised, our voices sing,  
Together in Your presence, our praises ring.  

Verse 2:  
From the tribe of Judah, we rise with might,  
Oh, the sun is shining, our hearts ignite.  
Reuben, Simeon, Gad in their place,  
In harmony we march, by Your grace.  

Chorus:  
We will pitch our tents, as You command,  
In the shadow of Your love, we make our stand.  
With the banners raised, our voices sing,  
Together in Your presence, our praises ring.  

Bridge:  
From east to west, we hear Your call,  
With every standard raised, we won't fall.  
In the midst of the camp, Your glory shines,  
Among Your people, Your love aligns.  

Verse 3:  
Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin too,  
Together we journey, our hearts made new.  
Dan leads the way, with Asher and Naphtali,  
Each step we take, Lord, we trust in Thee.  

Chorus:  
We will pitch our tents, as You command,  
In the shadow of Your love, we make our stand.  
With the banners raised, our voices sing,  
Together in Your presence, our praises ring.  

Outro:  
So here we stand, by our standards high,  
In Your arms of mercy, we will rely.  
With hearts united, in covenant we bind,  
Forever in Your love, our souls aligned.
