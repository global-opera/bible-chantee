[TITLE]
A Sweet Sacrifice

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the seventh month, on holy ground,  
A trumpet calls, let the joy resound.  
We gather here, with hearts aligned,  
To offer praise, our love defined.  
Without blemish, the lambs we bring,  
To honor You, our voices sing.  

Chorus  
A sweet sacrifice, we lift our hands,  
In love and grace, we take our stand.  
With every offering, our hearts ignite,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our light.  

Verse 2  
On the tenth day, we afflict our souls,  
In humble hearts, Your mercy unfolds.  
A bullock, a ram, we lay at Your feet,  
In every gift, Your love we greet.  
With flour and oil, our thanks arise,  
In every whisper, our prayers entwined.  

Chorus  
A sweet sacrifice, we lift our hands,  
In love and grace, we take our stand.  
With every offering, our hearts ignite,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our light.  

Bridge  
On the fifteenth day, we gather in praise,  
For seven days, our hearts ablaze.  
Thirteen bullocks, the rams and lambs,  
In every moment, we seek Your plans.  
A solemn assembly, we bow in awe,  
In Your embrace, we feel the law.  

Chorus  
A sweet sacrifice, we lift our hands,  
In love and grace, we take our stand.  
With every offering, our hearts ignite,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our light.  

Verse 3  
As the days unfold, we bring our best,  
With each act of love, our souls find rest.  
A goat for sin, a heart made whole,  
In every sacrifice, we see Your goal.  
Through the feasts and vows, our spirits soar,  
In this covenant love, forevermore.  

Chorus  
A sweet sacrifice, we lift our hands,  
In love and grace, we take our stand.  
With every offering, our hearts ignite,

Outro  
Moses spoke, as You commanded,  
In sacred trust, our lives expanded.  
With grateful hearts, we sing this song,  
In sweet obedience, we all belong.
