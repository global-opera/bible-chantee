(Verse 1)  
In the wilderness, they stood so bold,  
Daughters of Zelophehad, a story to be told.  
With hearts united, they sought the land,  
For their father’s name, they made their stand.  
“No son to carry, his legacy we crave,  
In the sight of the Lord, our voices brave.”

(Chorus)  
Oh, give us our inheritance,  
A promise from the Lord,  
Among the brethren, let us stand,  
In His unchanging word.  
For every heart that's faithful,  
He hears our cries today,  
In His love, we find our place,  
In His light, we walk His way.

In the heart of the family, plant the faithful seed.”
