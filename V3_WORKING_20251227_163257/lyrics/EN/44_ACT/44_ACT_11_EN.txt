[TITLE]
Call Us to the Nations

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the city where we gather, hearts ignited by Your grace,  
Peter shared the vision, saw the truth we now embrace.  
What You’ve cleansed, Lord, we will not call unclean,  
Every tribe and every nation, Your love is our redeem.  

Chorus  
Call us to the nations, where Your Spirit leads the way,  
With a heart of compassion, we will share Your name today.  
In the light of salvation, let the whole world see,  
Your grace is for all people, every soul can be set free.  

Verse 2  
From Joppa to the nations, Your message spreads like fire,  
Gentiles filled with wonder, hearts lifted even higher.  
Baptized with the Spirit, as You promised from above,  
We’re united in Your purpose, sharing truth and love.
