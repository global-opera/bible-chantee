[TITLE]
Bound for Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the night, we gather here,  
Breaking bread together, drawing near.  
With the Spirit's whisper, hearts ignite,  
In the love of Jesus, we find our light.  

Chorus:  
We are bound for glory, on this faithful way,  
With every step we take, we’ll boldly say,  
“Lord, we’ll share Your kindness, we’ll spread Your grace,  
Together we will run, in this holy race.”  

Verse 2:  
Paul stood among us, sharing truth so clear,  
With tears and passion, he drew us near.  
In trials and temptations, he led the way,  
With faith unshakeable, he did not sway.  

Chorus:  
We are bound for glory, on this faithful way,  
With every step we take, we’ll boldly say,  
“Lord, we’ll share Your kindness, we’ll spread Your grace,  
Together we will run, in this holy race.”  

Bridge:  
So we watch and remember, the call to stand strong,  
For the flock that You’ve given, we sing this song.  
With love that is greater, we’ll heed Your command,  
In the power of Your Spirit, we’ll take our stand.  

Chorus:  
We are bound for glory, on this faithful way,  
With every step we take, we’ll boldly say,  
“Lord, we’ll share Your kindness, we’ll spread Your grace,

Outro:  
And as we journey onward, with hearts full of praise,  
We’ll carry forth Your message, through all our days.  
In the light of Your promise, we’ll never part,  
Bound for glory, Lord, forever in our hearts.
