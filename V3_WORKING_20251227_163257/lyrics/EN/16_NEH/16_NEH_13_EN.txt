[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Remember Me, O My God

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of Your temple, Lord,  
We gather as Your faithful ones,  
With hearts laid bare, we seek Your Word,  
In brokenness, our journey runs.  
The ones who turned from You, we mourn,  
The blessings turned to bitter strife,  
Yet even in their curse, we learn,  
You turn our trials into life.  

[Chorus]  
Remember me, O my God,  
For the deeds of my heart, I cry,  
In the chambers of Your love,  
Let not my labor pass You by.  
Oh, cleanse us from all that defiles,  
Restore the joy of holy days,  
In reverence, we seek Your face,  
Remember me, O God, always.  

[Verse 2]  
The Sabbath rests, a holy sign,  
Yet burdens brought upon the day,  
I call to those who tread the line,  
To guard this gift, to walk Your way.  
With every vow and every plea,  
We rise to cast the wrong aside,  
For in Your grace, we’re truly free,  
Our hearts, in You, are unified.  

[Chorus]  
Remember me, O my God,

[Bridge]  
From ashes, You bring forth our song,  
In unity, we stand as one,  
With faithful hearts, we will be strong,  
For every battle, You have won.  
Though we may falter, still we rise,  
In Your embrace, we find our fight,  
Through every tear, our spirits fly,  
In Your great mercy, we find light.

[Outro]  
Remember me, O my God,  
In all my ways, I seek Your grace,  
With every breath, my heart beats loud,  
Forever in Your holy place.  
Remember me, O God, always.
