[TITLE]
Hear Our Cry, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the palace of Shushan, I sat with a heart so torn,  
A word from my brethren, a burden borne.  
The walls of Jerusalem lie broken and bare,  
The remnant in sorrow, Lord, hear their despair.  

Chorus  
Hear our cry, O Lord, from the depths of our need,  
With Your eyes wide open, let Your mercy lead.  
Gather us back, from the shadows of shame,  
We are Your people, we call on Your name.  

Verse 2  
I confessed our transgressions, our hearts turned away,  
In the night and in fasting, we seek You, we pray.  
O great God of heaven, keep Your covenant true,  
For Your love and compassion, we long to renew.  

Chorus  
Hear our cry, O Lord, from the depths of our need,  
With Your eyes wide open, let Your mercy lead.  
Gather us back, from the shadows of shame,  
We are Your people, we call on Your name.  

Bridge  
Though scattered like dust, in the farthest of lands,  
You promise to gather with Your mighty hands.  
We turn to You, Lord, with hearts open wide,  
Bring us back, bring us home, be our faithful guide.  

Chorus  
Hear our cry, O Lord, from the depths of our need,  
With Your eyes wide open, let Your mercy lead.  
Gather us back, from the shadows of shame,  
We are Your people, we call on Your name.  

Outro  
O Lord, I beseech Thee, let mercy abound,  
In the sight of the king, let Your favor be found.  
For we are Your servants, redeemed by Your grace,  
We lift up our voices, in this holy place.
