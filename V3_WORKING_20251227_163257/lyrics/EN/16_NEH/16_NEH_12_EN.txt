[TITLE]
Songs of the Redeemed

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of old, when the walls stood tall,  
Zerubbabel led, with a heart for the call.  
Priests and Levites, united in grace,  
Gathered in Jerusalem, to seek God’s face.  

Chorus  
Lift your voices, let the praises rise,  
With thanksgiving, we behold the skies.  
For the joy of the Lord fills this sacred place,  
Songs of the redeemed, we sing of His grace.  

Verse 2  
From the fields of Geba to the plains of song,  
The Levites rejoiced, where our hearts belong.  
Jeshua and Ezra, with instruments in hand,  
Together we stand, in this promised land.  

Chorus  
Lift your voices, let the praises rise,  
With thanksgiving, we behold the skies.  
For the joy of the Lord fills this sacred place,  
Songs of the redeemed, we sing of His grace.  

Bridge  
Purify our hearts, O Lord, we pray,  
As we walk in Your light, guide us each day.  
With every trumpet, every cymbal's sound,  
In His presence, our hope is found.  

Verse 3  
Great sacrifices offered, with joy they came,  
Families rejoicing, lifting His name.  
From the towers to gates, we proclaim His might,  
In unity, we stand, in the warmth of His light.  

Chorus  
Lift your voices, let the praises rise,  
With thanksgiving, we behold the skies.  
For the joy of the Lord fills this sacred place,  
Songs of the redeemed, we sing of His grace.  

Outro  
So let the joy of Jerusalem be heard afar,  
In the heart of His people, shines our morning star.  
Together we sing, forever we'll praise,  
In the songs of the redeemed, our hearts ablaze.
