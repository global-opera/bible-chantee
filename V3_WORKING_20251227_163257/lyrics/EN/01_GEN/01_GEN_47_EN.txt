[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Canaan, where famine laid its claim,  
Joseph told of brothers, and called upon their name.  
"My father and my brethren, they come to seek their rest,  
In the land of Goshen, where they’ll be truly blessed."  

Chorus  
Oh, God of mercy, hear our cry,  
From the depths of sorrow, we look to the sky.  
In our pilgrimage, we find our way,  
In the land of Goshen, Your light will stay.  

Verse 2  
Pharaoh spoke to Joseph, “Let them dwell in peace,  
In the best of all the land, let their hunger cease.”  
With shepherd hearts, they ventured, their flocks in need of grace,  
In the arms of Egypt, they found a warm embrace.  

Chorus  
Oh, God of mercy, hear our cry,  
From the depths of sorrow, we look to the sky.  
In our pilgrimage, we find our way,  
In the land of Goshen, Your light will stay.  

Bridge  
When the money falters, and the cattle fade away,  
You provide the harvest, in Your faithful way.  
With every tear we've tasted, and every trial we've faced,  
You gather us together, our hearts in Your embrace.  

Verse 3  
Joseph, wise and caring, he nourished every soul,  
In the land of Goshen, our spirits are made whole.  
Though the years are fleeting, like shadows on the ground,  
In the arms of our Creator, our refuge will be found.  

Chorus  
Oh, God of mercy, hear our cry,  
From the depths of sorrow, we look to the sky.  
In our pilgrimage, we find our way,  
In the land of Goshen, Your light will stay.  

Outro  
So we dwell in promise, with hope that will not cease,  
In the land of Goshen, we find our perfect peace.  
With Jacob's blessing resting, upon each heart and hand,  
In the land of Goshen, we’ll forever stand.
