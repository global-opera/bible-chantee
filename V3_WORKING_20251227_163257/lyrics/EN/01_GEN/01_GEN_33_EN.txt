Verse 1:  
I lifted my eyes to the dawn,  
As I faced the past that I have drawn.  
With fear in my heart, I approached my kin,  
Knowing grace was the way I’d begin.  
Four hundred strong, yet love overcame,  
In the silence, I whispered Your name.  

Chorus:  
For I have seen Your face, like the sun in the skies,  
In the embrace of my brother, grace before my eyes.  
Your mercy flows like rivers, Your kindness, my guide,  
In the arms of forgiveness, I will abide.

Verse 2:  
I bowed low before him, my heart laid bare,  
All my children beside me, laid in Your care.  
“Who are these?” he asked, and I humbly replied,  
“The gifts from the Father, in whom I abide.”  
With trembling I offered, my heart on display,  
“Receive my blessing, let grace lead the way.”
