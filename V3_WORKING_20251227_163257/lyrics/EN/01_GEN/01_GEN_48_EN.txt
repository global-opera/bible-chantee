[TITLE]
Blessings in the Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Canaan, beneath the stars so bright,  
Jacob spoke of blessings, in the still of night.  
"God Almighty found me, in the place called Luz,  
He promised fruit and favor, a heritage for us."

Chorus  
Oh, let Your name be named on us,  
As we gather in Your grace.  
Lord, bless the sons of Joseph,  
As we seek Your holy face.  
In the midst of every trial,  
Your promise stands secure,  
Ephraim and Manasseh,  
In Your love we are assured.

Verse 2  
Joseph brought his children, to the bed of Israel,  
With a heart full of longing, love began to swell.  
Though his eyes were dimmed by age, his faith shone ever bright,  
He embraced the future, in the shadow of the night.

Bridge

With wisdom from above,  
The younger shall be greater,  
In the fullness of Your love.  
As we stand together,  
In Your covenant so true,  
We claim the blessings promised,  
Lord, we trust in You.

Outro  
So we sing of generations,  
In Your presence we will stand,  
With the blessings of our fathers,

As we walk in faith and trust,  
For in You, Lord, we find our hope,  
Forever in Your love, we must.
