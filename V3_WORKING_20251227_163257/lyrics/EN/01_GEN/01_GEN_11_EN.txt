[TITLE]
From Babel to Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Shinar, where the people stood,  
With one voice they gathered, united for good.  
"Come, let us build a tower, reach the heavens high,  
Make a name for ourselves, lest we scatter and die."  

Chorus:  
But the Lord came down to see their pride,  
He saw their hearts and their dreams collide.  
From one voice to many, He scattered their way,  
In the midst of their striving, they lost what to say.  

Verse 2:  
Brick by brick they labored, in unity strong,  
Yet God had a purpose; they could not belong.  
He confounded their language, confusion took flight,  
What was once a chorus turned into the night.  

Chorus:  
But the Lord came down to see their pride,  
He saw their hearts and their dreams collide.  
From one voice to many, He scattered their way,  
In the midst of their striving, they lost what to say.  

Bridge:  
Yet from these scattered nations, a promise would bloom,  
Through generations coming, a light in the gloom.  
From Terah to Abram, a covenant made,  
In the heart of the chaos, His purpose displayed.  

Verse 3:  
In the land of Canaan, a new journey starts,  
With faith as their anchor, and love in their hearts.  
Though Babel brought division, God’s plan would unfold,  
In the tapestry of ages, His story is told.  

Chorus:  
For the Lord looked down, and in love He sighed,  
He saw our need for hope, our dreams He would guide.  
From one voice to many, yet one heart we claim,  
In the midst of our striving, we'll lift up His name.  

Outro:  
From Babel to promise, our voices unite,  
In the grace of our Savior, we walk in His light.  
Though scattered and broken, His love brings us home,  
In the arms of the Father, we’re never alone.
