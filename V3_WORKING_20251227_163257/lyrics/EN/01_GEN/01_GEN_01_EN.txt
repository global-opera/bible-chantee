[TITLE]
In the Beginning

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the beginning, You spoke the Word,  
From the void, Your voice we heard.  
Light broke forth, dispelling night,  
You called it good, oh shining Light.  
Heaven and earth, Your hands did weave,  
In Your creation, we believe.  

[Chorus]  
You are the Maker, the Creator of all,  
From the skies above to the oceans that call.  
Every creature, every tree, sings Your praise,  
In the beginning, You laid the days.  
You are the Author, our story unfolds,  
In Your great love, our lives You hold.  

[Verse 2]  
You formed the firmament, divided the seas,  
With every whisper, the world found peace.  
Grass and flowers, trees so tall,  
You saw it good, the beauty of all.  
Stars in the night, the sun in the day,  
Guiding our steps, lighting our way.

[Bridge]  
Let us rise, in Your image we stand,  
Dominion and grace, given by Your hand.  
Be fruitful and multiply, we hear Your call,  
In every heartbeat, we worship it all.  

[Verse 3]  
Now we gather, in awe of Your might,  
Reflecting Your glory, shining so bright.  
Creation rejoices, the heavens proclaim,  
All of existence, glorifies Your name.

[Outro]  
In the beginning, You made it all,  
In every heartbeat, we answer Your call.  
From the heavens high to the earth below,  
In Your creation, Your love we know.
