[TITLE]
The Well of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Jacob journeyed to the land of grace,  
A well appeared, in that sacred place.  
Three flocks gathered, waiting for the day,  
To drink from the depths where the stone lay.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the stone, let the waters flow,  
In the heart of the land, where the blessings grow.  
From the well of promise, we’re nourished and free,  
In the arms of Your love, Lord, we come to Thee.  

Verse 2:  
Rachel came with her father’s sheep,  
A beauty in the fields, our hearts to keep.  
Jacob’s heart did leap at her sight,  
A love so deep, it shone like light.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the stone, let the waters flow,  
In the heart of the land, where the blessings grow.  
From the well of promise, we’re nourished and free,  
In the arms of Your love, Lord, we come to Thee.  

Bridge:  
In the struggles of life, when the nights grow long,  
You see our hearts and hear our song.  
Through every trial, You’re our guiding star,  
In the well of Your grace, we find who we are.  

Verse 3:  
Laban welcomed him with open arms,  
Two daughters, two paths, and their hidden charms.  
For Rachel’s love, Jacob would strive,  
Seven years felt like days, in love he’d thrive.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the stone, let the waters flow,  
In the heart of the land, where the blessings grow.  
From the well of promise, we’re nourished and free,  
In the arms of Your love, Lord, we come to Thee.  

Verse 4:  
Yet Leah bore the weight of her heart,  
Cried to the Lord, in her tears, a new start.  
Four sons were born, each name a plea,  
In her praise, she found her identity.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the stone, let the waters flow,  
In the heart of the land, where the blessings grow.  
From the well of promise, we’re nourished and free,  
In the arms of Your love, Lord, we come to Thee.  

Outro:  
So gather at the well, where love runs deep,  
In the arms of the Savior, our souls He’ll keep.  
With every heartbeat, let our praises rise,  
At the well of promise, we’ll lift our eyes.
