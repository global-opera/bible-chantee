[TITLE]
Journey to Goshen

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the still of the night, You called my name,  
“Jacob, rise up, for I am the same.  
Fear not the path that lies before thee,  
For in Egypt, I’ll set My people free.”  

Chorus  
Oh, we will follow, where You lead,  
With hearts united, in faith we plead.  
From Canaan's land to the fields of grace,  
In Your presence, Lord, we find our place.  

Verse 2  
With each step we take, we carry the load,  
Our families together, on this sacred road.  
The sons and daughters, a promise fulfilled,  
In the land of Goshen, Your love is revealed.  

Chorus  
Oh, we will follow, where You lead,  
With hearts united, in faith we plead.  
From Canaan's land to the fields of grace,  
In Your presence, Lord, we find our place.  

Bridge  
And Joseph weeps, as he meets his kin,  
A reunion of love, where hope begins.  
“No longer in sorrow, we stand as one,  
For the God of our fathers, His will be done.”  

Chorus  
Oh, we will follow, where You lead,  
With hearts united, in faith we plead.  
From Canaan's land to the fields of grace,  
In Your presence, Lord, we find our place.  

Outro  
In the journey ahead, we trust in Your plan,  
A great nation rising, under Your hand.  
With every breath, Lord, our hearts will sing,  
To the God of our fathers, our offering.  

(Repeat Chorus)  
Oh, we will follow, where You lead,

From Canaan's land to the fields of grace,
