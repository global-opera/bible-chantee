Verse 1  
In poverty, I walk with grace,  
Integrity my heart's embrace,  
The fool who speaks with twisted tongue,  
In shadows of his lies is strung.  
The knowledge sought is life indeed,  
In haste we fall, our hearts mislead.  

Chorus  
Oh, guide me, Lord, in wisdom's way,  
In truth and love, let me stay.  
Your favor, like the dew, brings life,  
In every trial, through joy and strife.

The hearts of men may turn away,  
Yet in Your light, I choose to stay.
