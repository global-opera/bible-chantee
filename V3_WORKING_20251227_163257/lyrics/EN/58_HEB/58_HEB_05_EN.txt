Verse 1  
You chose a priest from among men,  
To bear the weight of every sin.  
With gifts and prayers, he stands in grace,  
Compassion flows from this sacred space.  

Chorus  
Oh, You are the High Priest, forever we sing,  
In Your love, our salvation You bring.  
From the order of Melchizedek, You reign,  
In Your mercy, we find hope again.
