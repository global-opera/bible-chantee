[TITLE]
In the Shadow, Yet I Hope

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, I have seen my pain,  
By the rod of Your wrath, Lord, I call Your name.  
You’ve led me through the darkness, where shadows reside,  
In my brokenness, I cry, where can I hide?  

Pre-Chorus  
But this I recall to my heart, there’s hope in the strife,  
Your mercies are new each day, You breathe me to life.  

Chorus  
Great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, my soul will sing,  
In the depths of despair, Your light will bring,  
Though I walk through the valley, my heart will remain,  
In the shadow of sorrow, I’ll trust You again.  

Verse 2  
You’ve built walls around me, I feel the weight of my chains,  
Yet in the depths of my sorrow, I’ll praise through the pain.  
You’ve seen all my struggles, the tears that I’ve cried,  
With Your hand of compassion, Lord, be my guide.  

Pre-Chorus  
For You are the portion my heart longs to hold,  
In the silence, I’ll wait, for Your promise is bold.  

Chorus  
Great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, my soul will sing,  
In the depths of despair, Your light will bring,  
Though I walk through the valley, my heart will remain,  
In the shadow of sorrow, I’ll trust You again.  

Bridge  
You draw near to the broken, You lift up the weak,  
When I call on Your name, it’s Your love that I seek.  
Though the storms may surround me, I will rise from the dust,  
For in every affliction, Lord, in You I trust.  

Chorus  
Great is Your faithfulness, O Lord, my soul will sing,  
In the depths of despair, Your light will bring,  
Though I walk through the valley, my heart will remain,  
In the shadow of sorrow, I’ll trust You again.  

Outro  
So I lift up my heart, with my hands lifted high,  
In the midst of my trials, Lord, You’ll hear my cry.  
Great is Your faithfulness, my hope is in You,  
In shadows and sorrow, Your love will see me through.
