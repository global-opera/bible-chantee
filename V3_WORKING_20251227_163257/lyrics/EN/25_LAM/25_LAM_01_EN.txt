[TITLE]
In the Silence of Sorrow

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In the night, the city weeps,  
Once so grand, now silence keeps,  
Her beauty lost, her joy laid bare,  
O Lord, see the depths of despair.  
Among the nations, she stands alone,  
Her friends have turned, and love has flown,  
Once a princess, now she sighs,  
In the shadow of her cries.

Chorus:  
O Lord, behold my affliction,  
My heart is heavy, my soul in pain,  
No comfort comes, no hope remains,  
In the silence of sorrow, I call Your name.  
Hear my cries, as I seek Your face,  
In the depths of darkness, I long for grace.

Verse 2:  
Captive to the weight of sin,  
In the straits where grief begins,  
All her gates lie desolate,  
Her pleas unheard, her mournful fate.  
The adversary laughs in scorn,  
While her children, lost and worn,  
But still, my heart will not let go,  
For in the shadows, Your love can show.

Chorus:

Bridge:  
In the ashes, I remember,  
The days of old when joy was near,  
Yet even now, I lift my hands,  
In surrender to the One who understands.  
Your righteousness, O Lord, I see,  
In my weakness, You are my plea,  
Though the night is long and dark,  
Your light, O God, ignites my heart.

Outro:  
So I weep, but I will trust,  
In Your mercy, I find my rest,  
In the silence, You are near,  
O Lord, my hope, my strength, my cheer.  
In the silence of sorrow, I call Your name,  
In the silence of sorrow, I am not the same.
