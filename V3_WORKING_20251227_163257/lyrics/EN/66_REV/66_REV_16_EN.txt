[TITLE]
The Day of Reckoning

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From the temple, a voice resounds,  
To the angels, God’s wrath abounds.  
Pour your vials on the earth so bare,  
Judgment comes, a call to beware.  
The mark of the beast has led them astray,  
Grievous sores will find their way.  

Chorus  
O Lord, righteous in all Your ways,  
Your judgments true, we lift our praise.  
Even so, Almighty, we cry,  
Prepare our hearts for the day You draw nigh.  

Verse 2  
The seas turned to blood, the rivers dry,  
Every living soul, beneath the sky.  
From the altar, a voice cries out,  
For the blood of saints, we have no doubt.  
You are worthy, of all our fear,  
In Your justice, Lord, we draw near.  

Chorus  
O Lord, righteous in all Your ways,  
Your judgments true, we lift our praise.  
Even so, Almighty, we cry,  
Prepare our hearts for the day You draw nigh.  

Bridge  
Scorching sun, the darkness falls,  
Men gnaw their tongues, yet heed not Your calls.  
Three unclean spirits gather near,  
The battle awaits; let faith persevere.  
Behold, You come as a thief in the night,  
Blessed are those who walk in Your light.  

Chorus  
O Lord, righteous in all Your ways,  
Your judgments true, we lift our praise.  
Even so, Almighty, we cry,  
Prepare our hearts for the day You draw nigh.  

Outro  
It is done, the heavens shake,  
Voices thunder for Your sake.  
Great Babylon, a memory past,  
In Your fierce wrath, we find our cast.  
Every island, every mountain shaken,  
In Your glory, our hearts awaken.
