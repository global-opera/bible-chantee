[TITLE]
Awake and Overcome

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the night, we arise,  
With a name that speaks of life, yet we’re blind.  
Oh, Lord, we hear Your call, to be watchful,  
Strengthen what remains, awaken our hearts.

Chorus  
Awake and overcome,  
Clothed in white, we’ll run,  
For You hold the keys of grace,  
Jesus, we seek Your face.  
From the depths, You lift us high,  
We will rise, we will not die,  
In Your love, we find our worth,  
As we stand on holy ground.

Verse 2  
In Philadelphia, an open door,  
With little strength, we trust in You more.  
Though the world may rise against,  
We hold fast to Your promises.

Chorus

Bridge  
Oh, Laodicea, hear the voice,  
Hot or cold, make your choice.  
Gold refined in fire, we desire,  
Come, Lord Jesus, we need You here.  
Knock upon our hearts, we will respond,  
Sup with us, our Savior, come along.

Outro  
He that hath an ear, let him hear,  
Spirit of God, draw us near.  
Awake, arise, for the time has come,  
In Your name, we are one.  
Awake and overcome.
