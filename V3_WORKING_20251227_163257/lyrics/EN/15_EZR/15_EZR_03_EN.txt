[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the seventh month, as one we stand,  
Gathered in Your presence, Lord, hand in hand.  
Jeshua and Zerubbabel lead us forth,  
To build an altar, proclaim Your worth.  

Chorus  
You are good, Your mercy everlasting,  
With every offering, our hearts are casting,  
From morning light to evening's grace,  
We lift our voices, we seek Your face.  

Verse 2  
Fear surrounds us, but still we rise,  
With burnt offerings, we lift our cries.  
The feast of tabernacles, we remember well,  
In unity we gather, Your love we tell.  

Chorus  
You are good, Your mercy everlasting,  
With every offering, our hearts are casting,  
From morning light to evening's grace,  
We lift our voices, we seek Your face.  

Bridge  
Oh, the sound of joy, the weeping too,  
Ancient hearts remembering what You can do.  
Together we shout, together we weep,  
For the house of the Lord, our promises keep.  

Chorus  
You are good, Your mercy everlasting,  
With every offering, our hearts are casting,  
From morning light to evening's grace,  
We lift our voices, we seek Your face.  

Outro  
As the foundation is laid, we sing,  
With trumpets and cymbals, our praises ring.  
In the heart of Your people, forever stay,  
Lord, we build our lives on the foundations of praise.
