[TITLE]
In the House of God

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the house of God, let my heart be still,  
Ready to hear, Lord, Your voice, Your will.  
With humble steps, I come before Your throne,  
Let my words be few, in reverence I groan.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, teach me, Lord, to walk in Your ways,  
To cherish each moment, to count all my days.  
In the quiet, I’ll find my peace,  
In Your presence, my worries cease.  

(Verse 2)  
When I vow a vow, may I hold it true,  
For You find no pleasure in the heart unglued.  
Let not my mouth lead my soul astray,  
In the fear of God, I choose to stay.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, teach me, Lord, to walk in Your ways,  
To cherish each moment, to count all my days.  
In the quiet, I’ll find my peace,  
In Your presence, my worries cease.  

(Bridge)  
We toil for the wind, but You give us grace,  
In all our striving, You set the pace.  
The riches of earth may fade away,  
But the joy of the heart is here to stay.  

(Verse 3)  
To see the oppressed, and to lift them high,  
Your eyes watch over, You hear every cry.  
In the laboring heart, sweet rest is found,  
In the joy of our work, Your love abounds.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, teach me, Lord, to walk in Your ways,  
To cherish each moment, to count all my days.  
In the quiet, I’ll find my peace,  
In Your presence, my worries cease.  

(Outro)  
In the house of God, I will find my place,  
With a heart full of love, I’ll seek Your face.  
For every blessing, Lord, I give You praise,  
In the joy of my heart, I'll sing all my days.
