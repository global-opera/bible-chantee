[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of promise, the people cried,  
"Make our burdens lighter, we’re weary inside."  
Rehoboam stood, with wisdom to seek,  
Old men spoke softly, “Be humble, be meek.”  

Chorus  
But he turned to the voices of youth,  
Chose the path of pride, forsook the truth.  
Oh, the yoke of grace, we long to find,  
In every heart, let mercy bind.  

Verse 2  
“Your father’s heavy yoke, I’ll make it worse,”  
Words full of anger, a reckless curse.  
The kingdom divided, the people torn,  
From the house of David, a new way was born.  

Chorus  
But he turned to the voices of youth,  
Chose the path of pride, forsook the truth.  
Oh, the yoke of grace, we long to find,  
In every heart, let mercy bind.  

Bridge  
Lord, speak to our hearts, guide us today,  
Help us to listen, to follow Your way.  
In the silence, we hear Your call,  
To serve one another, to love one and all.  

Verse 3  
Jeroboam feared, the kingdom would sway,  
Built golden calves, led the hearts astray.  
Yet in every shadow, Your light will shine,  
You gather the lost, Lord, make them divine.  

Chorus  
But he turned to the voices of youth,  
Chose the path of pride, forsook the truth.  
Oh, the yoke of grace, we long to find,  
In every heart, let mercy bind.  

Outro  
So we lay down our burdens, our strife,  
In the arms of Jesus, we find new life.  
With every heartbeat, let love increase,  
In the yoke of grace, we find our peace.
