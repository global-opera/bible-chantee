[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the throne, a heart aches,  
Jeroboam’s son lies sick, the fear awakes.  
“Disguise yourself, my love, go seek the prophet,  
Ahijah waits in Shiloh, let his words be our profit.”  

Chorus:  
Oh, hear the cry of the broken,  
In the silence, Your truth spoken.  
The weight of kings, the tears of sorrow,  
Lead us, Lord, into tomorrow.  

Verse 2:  
With loaves and honey, she walks in disguise,  
But Ahijah sees through the veil of lies.  
“Why do you feign? The Lord sees your heart,  
Heavy tidings await, from Him you can’t depart.”  

Chorus:  
Oh, hear the cry of the broken,  
In the silence, Your truth spoken.  
The weight of kings, the tears of sorrow,  
Lead us, Lord, into tomorrow.  

Bridge:  
For the sins of the father, the children will bear,  
Yet in the midst of judgment, still, You care.  
A remnant of hope, a promise to keep,  
In our darkest hour, Your love runs deep.  

Verse 3:  
“Go tell Jeroboam,” the word of the Lord,  
“For I raised you up, yet you fell on your sword.  
You turned from my ways, made idols of gold,  
Now judgment is here, your fate will unfold.”  

Chorus:  
Oh, hear the cry of the broken,  
In the silence, Your truth spoken.  
The weight of kings, the tears of sorrow,  
Lead us, Lord, into tomorrow.  

Outro:  
As the child takes his rest, all Israel moans,  
For even in loss, Your mercy atones.  
Oh Lord of Israel, our hearts we renew,  
In the echoes of grace, we turn back to You.
