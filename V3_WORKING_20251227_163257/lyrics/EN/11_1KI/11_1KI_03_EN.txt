[TITLE]
Wisdom from Above

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, oh Lord, You came,  
To a heart so young, in a dream You called my name.  
With a heart of gratitude, I stand before Your throne,  
For the mercy shown to David, now I stand alone.  

Chorus  
Grant me wisdom, Lord, to lead this mighty throng,  
A discerning heart to know right from wrong.  
In Your ways, I will walk, as my father did before,  
With Your light guiding me, I will seek You evermore.  

Verse 2  
In the midst of my people, I find my place,  
A multitude, uncounted, in Your boundless grace.  
Not for riches, nor for power, do I seek to reign,  
But to shepherd with Your love, through joy and pain.  

Chorus  
Grant me wisdom, Lord, to lead this mighty throng,  
A discerning heart to know right from wrong.  
In Your ways, I will walk, as my father did before,  
With Your light guiding me, I will seek You evermore.  

Bridge  
When two hearts stand divided, in the shadows of their plight,  
Your wisdom brings the truth to the surface of the night.  
For love will seek the living, and mercy will prevail,  
In Your justice, Lord, we find our holy trail.  

Chorus  
Grant me wisdom, Lord, to lead this mighty throng,  
A discerning heart to know right from wrong.  
In Your ways, I will walk, as my father did before,  
With Your light guiding me, I will seek You evermore.  

Outro  
So I lift my voice in praise, to the King of all the earth,  
For the gift of understanding, for my calling and my worth.  
In Your presence, Lord, forever I will stay,  
With a heart of wisdom, lead me on my way.
