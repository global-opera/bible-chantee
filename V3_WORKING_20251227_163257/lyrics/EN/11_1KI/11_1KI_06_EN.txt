[TITLE]
The House of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of Solomon, a promise was made,  
Four hundred eighty years, from Egypt we’d strayed.  
With stone and with cedar, a temple was sought,  
A dwelling for the Lord, a home that He wrought.  

Chorus:  
Oh, build the house of the Lord,  
With every heart and every chord.  
In His presence we will stand,  
Our lives a sacrifice, our faith in His hands.  

Verse 2:  
The walls adorned with gold, cherubims in flight,  
In the holy of holies, we seek His light.  
He covers us with mercy, His love never fails,  
In the chambers of His heart, His grace prevails.  

Chorus:  
Oh, build the house of the Lord,  
With every heart and every chord.  
In His presence we will stand,  
Our lives a sacrifice, our faith in His hands.  

Bridge:  
"Walk in my statutes," the Lord did declare,  
"I'll dwell among my people, in love and in care."  
With every stone laid down, in sacred design,  
Together we will rise, as His glory shines.  

Chorus:  
Oh, build the house of the Lord,  
With every heart and every chord.  
In His presence we will stand,  
Our lives a sacrifice, our faith in His hands.  

Outro:  
So let us raise our voices, let our praises soar,  
In the house of the Lord, forever we adore.  
His temple in our hearts, His Spirit we embrace,  
In the beauty of His love, we find our resting place.
