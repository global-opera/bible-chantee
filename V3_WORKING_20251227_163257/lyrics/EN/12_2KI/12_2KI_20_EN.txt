[TITLE]
Hezekiah's Prayer

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In days of sorrow, Hezekiah cried,  
Faced with shadows, he turned to the side.  
“Remember, O Lord, the path I’ve trod,  
In truth and love, I’ve walked with God.”  

Chorus:  
You’ve heard my prayer, You’ve seen my tears,  
O Lord of mercy, calm all my fears.  
For in Your faithfulness, I find my way,  
You are my healer, my hope, my stay.  

Verse 2:  
The prophet came with words from on high,  
“Your days are numbered, but do not sigh.  
I will restore you, I’ll be your shield,  
Fifteen more years, your fate is sealed.”  

Chorus:  
You’ve heard my prayer, You’ve seen my tears,  
O Lord of mercy, calm all my fears.  
For in Your faithfulness, I find my way,  
You are my healer, my hope, my stay.  

Bridge:  
Let the shadow turn back, let the sun arise,  
You are the promise that never dies.  
In every trial, in every fight,  
Your light will guide me through the night.  

Verse 3:  
But then came whispers from Babylon's king,  
Hezekiah showed them all earthly things.  
Yet Isaiah warned of the days to come,  
What’s treasured here will be taken and done.  

Chorus:  
You’ve heard my prayer, You’ve seen my tears,  
O Lord of mercy, calm all my fears.  
For in Your faithfulness, I find my way,  
You are my healer, my hope, my stay.  

Outro:  
So let me walk in peace, let truth abide,  
For in Your presence, Lord, I will confide.  
Strengthen my spirit, my heart, my song,  
In Your sweet promise, I will belong.
