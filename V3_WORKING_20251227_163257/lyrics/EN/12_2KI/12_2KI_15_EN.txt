[TITLE]
In the Sight of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of kings, where the shadows fall,  
Azariah reigned, young yet standing tall.  
Fifty years in Jerusalem’s embrace,  
He sought the Lord, and walked in His grace.  
But high places stood, where the people strayed,  
Incense rising, in darkness they stayed.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we long to walk in Your light,  
To be found in the truth, to shine ever bright.  
In the sight of the Lord, let our hearts be pure,  
Guide us through battles, Your love will endure.

Verse 2  
Zachariah came, in the line of the sin,  
Evil in sight, where the darkness begins.  
Shallum conspired, the throne turned to dust,  
A kingdom divided, but in You we trust.  
Menahem ruled with a heart far from You,  
Yet still, Lord, Your mercy shines ever true.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we long to walk in Your light,  
To be found in the truth, to shine ever bright.  
In the sight of the Lord, let our hearts be pure,  
Guide us through battles, Your love will endure.

Bridge  
Jotham arose, with a heart right and bold,  
Yet high places lingered, the stories unfold.  
We cry out to You, in our brokenness call,  
Lord, lift us higher, through it all!  
For the chronicles tell of Your faithful embrace,  
In every story, Lord, we seek Your grace.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we long to walk in Your light,  
To be found in the truth, to shine ever bright.  
In the sight of the Lord, let our hearts be pure,  
Guide us through battles, Your love will endure.

Outro  
So here we stand, in the shadow of kings,  
With hearts wide open, to the hope that You bring.  
In the sight of the Lord, forever we’ll stay,  
Trusting in Your promise, each and every day.  
Amen, Amen, we lift up Your name!  
In the sight of the Lord, we’ll never be the same.
