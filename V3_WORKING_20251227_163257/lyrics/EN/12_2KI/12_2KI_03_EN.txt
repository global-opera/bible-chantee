[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land where kings arise,  
Jehoram faced the skies,  
With a heart that sought the light,  
Yet clung to shadows of the night.  
He called to Judah, side by side,  
Through the wilderness, they would ride,  
But the thirst was deep and wide,  
In their hearts, fear did abide.

Chorus:  
Oh, make this valley full of ditches,  
For the Lord will pour out His grace.  
In the silence, hear His whispers,  
With the dawn, He’ll fill this place.  
Hearts united, we will stand,  
With faith unbroken, hand in hand,  
For in the darkness, light will break,  
The waters flow, our souls awake.

Verse 2:  
Elisha came with words so bold,  
“Dig the ditches, be not cold.  
For you’ll see no wind, nor rain,  
Yet the waters will come again.”  
They obeyed, and morning’s light,  
Showed the earth so pure and bright,  
Blood-red rivers, Moab’s fear,  
In the face of God, we draw near.

Bridge:  
When battles rage and hope seems lost,  
We’ll dig the ditches, count the cost.  
With every step, we trust His plan,  
In every trial, we’ll take a stand.  
For He is faithful, He will provide,  
In the valley, He’s our guide.  
Let the waters rise and flow,  
In His mercy, we will grow.

Outro:  
So let us dig, let us prepare,  
For in this valley, He will care.  
With every ditch, we sow our trust,  
In the Lord, our hope is just.  
He will fill this land anew,  
In every heart, His promise true.  
Oh, make this valley full of ditches,  
For our God, He always enriches.
