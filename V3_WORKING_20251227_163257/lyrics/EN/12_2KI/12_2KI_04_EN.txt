[TITLE]
The Oil of Faith

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of despair, a woman cried aloud,  
Her heart was heavy, her hope was nearly drowned.  
“Lord, my husband’s gone, the creditor is near,  
What shall I do, O God? My soul is filled with fear.”  

[Chorus]  
But You see the empty vessels, waiting to be filled,  
With the oil of Your promise, Your love will be revealed.  
In every broken moment, when all seems lost and done,  
You pour out Your abundance, Your will is to be one.  

[Verse 2]  
Elisha spoke with wisdom, “What do you have in hand?”  
“A pot of oil only, Lord, I trust Your perfect plan.”  
“Go and gather vessels, not a few, but wide and deep,  
Shut the door behind you, with faith, His promise keep.”  

[Chorus]  
For You see the empty vessels, waiting to be filled,  
With the oil of Your promise, Your love will be revealed.  
In every broken moment, when all seems lost and done,  
You pour out Your abundance, Your will is to be one.  

[Bridge]  
When the child lay lifeless, her heart was full of dread,  
She sought the man of God, with tears of love she said,  
“Did I not ask for mercy? Did I not plead my case?  
Yet still, I trust in You, my Lord, I seek Your grace.”  

[Verse 3]  
Elisha prayed and fervently, he laid upon the child,  
And life returned in fullness, God’s promise reconciled.  
“Take up your son, dear mother, your faith has brought him near,  
In every darkened valley, Your hope will persevere.”  

[Chorus]  
For You see the empty vessels, waiting to be filled,  
With the oil of Your promise, Your love will be revealed.  
In every broken moment, when all seems lost and done,  
You pour out Your abundance, Your will is to be one.  

[Outro]  
So we gather in Your presence, with our hearts wide open wide,  
For in the midst of trials, we will trust and abide.  
With every empty vessel, we will call upon Your name,  
For our God of resurrection will forever be the same.  

[End]
