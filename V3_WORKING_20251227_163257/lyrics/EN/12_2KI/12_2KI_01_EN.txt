[TITLE]
No God But You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the throne,  
A king fell through the skies,  
Seeking answers from the dark,  
In whispers and in lies.  
But a voice from heaven spoke,  
Elijah, bold and true,  
"Why do you seek the false?  
There’s no God but you."

Chorus  
No God but You, our hearts declare,  
In every trial, in every prayer.  
Your fire ignites the night,  
You reign in power, You bring the light.  
No God but You, forever stay,  
Guide us back when we lose our way.

Verse 2  
Three captains sent in haste,  
To summon man of God,  
But fire fell from heaven's grace,  
Their pride was met with awe.  
The third one knelt, with trembling heart,  
"Let my life be spared,  
For I see the truth revealed,  
You’re the One who cares."

Bridge  
Where can we turn when hope is lost?  
In the valley of shadows, we count the cost.  
But Your word stands firm, a beacon bright,  
In the darkest of places, You are our light.  
We’ll trust in Your promise, we’ll stand on Your word,  
For every cry for help, we know we’ll be heard.
