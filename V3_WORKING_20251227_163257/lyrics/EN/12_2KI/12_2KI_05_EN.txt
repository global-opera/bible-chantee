[TITLE]
In the Waters of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Syria, a captain stood tall,  
Honored in battle, a hero to all.  
But a curse lay hidden, a shadow of pain,  
For he was a leper, in need of a name.  

Pre-Chorus:  
A maid from Israel spoke words of hope,  
“Go to the prophet, where healing can flow.”  
With silver and gold, he sought out the way,  
But pride held him back, as he turned to betray.  

Chorus:  
Wash in the waters, the waters of grace,  
Dipped in the river, find your rightful place.  
Seven times surrender, let the darkness flee,  
In the waters of grace, you will be free.  

Verse 2:  
At Elisha’s door, the captain stood still,  
Yet anger arose, for he sought a great thrill.  
“Are not rivers of Damascus so pure and so wide?  
Why should I obey? Why should I abide?”  

Pre-Chorus:  
But a whisper of wisdom from servants nearby,  
“If he asked for a mountain, would you not try?”  
So down to the Jordan, he humbled his heart,  
And in those waters, a brand new start.  

Chorus:  
Wash in the waters, the waters of grace,  
Dipped in the river, find your rightful place.  
Seven times surrender, let the darkness flee,  
In the waters of grace, you will be free.  

Bridge:  
Now Naaman returned, with a heart made anew,  
“No God like the Lord, His promises true.  
With earth from this land, I will honor His name,  
No more to the idols, I’ll never be the same.”  

Chorus:  
Wash in the waters, the waters of grace,  
Dipped in the river, find your rightful place.  
Seven times surrender, let the darkness flee,  
In the waters of grace, you will be free.  

Outro:  
So come to the river, where healing flows wide,  
In faith, we are cleansed, in Him we abide.  
With hearts open wide, we’ll sing of His ways,  
For in the waters of grace, we find endless praise.
