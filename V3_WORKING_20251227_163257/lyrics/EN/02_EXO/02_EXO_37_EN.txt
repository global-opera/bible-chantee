[LYRICS]

VERSE 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
Bezaleel’s hands did shine so bright,  
Crafting wood with purest gold,  
A story of our God retold.  
Two cherubims with wings spread wide,  
Over mercy, they abide,  
Their faces turned, in love they gaze,  
In this holy place, we raise.

CHORUS  
Come and see the Ark of promise,  
Where His presence rests in grace,  
Glory in the midst of chaos,  
Here we find our sacred space.  
Heaven's light, a guiding star,  
In His mercy, we are drawn near,  
In the Ark of His presence,  
All our burdens disappear.

VERSE 2  
On the table, bread of life,  
Golden vessels free from strife,  
Candlestick with light aglow,  
Illuminates the path we go.  
Each branch adorned with beauty rare,  
In this place, we find our prayer,  
Lift our voices, hearts entwined,  
In His presence, peace we find.

BRIDGE  
Holy anointing, fragrant and sweet,  
In the stillness, we bow at His feet.  
With every breath, we proclaim His name,  
In His glory, our hearts aflame.  
So we gather, hearts as one,  
In the shadow of the Holy Son,  
Every vessel, every prayer,  
In His presence, we find our share.
