[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Glory of the Lord

[Verse 1]  
In the stillness of the night,  
You spoke, O Lord, in might,  
Moses heard Your call so clear,  
Set the tabernacle here.  
Ark of testimony, pure and bright,  
Veil of holiness, shrouded in light.  
With every act, Your plan unfolds,  
In sacred spaces, our hearts behold.

[Chorus]  
Come, let us gather, behold His grace,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our place.  
Glory cloud descending, filling every space,  
With fire by night, His love we embrace.

[Verse 2]  
The altar stands, a sacred flame,  
Incense rising, lifting His name.  
Table set, the bread of life,  
Shining lamps through darkest strife.  
Wash us clean, we seek Your face,  
In this holy, hallowed place.  
As Moses moved with faithful heart,  
We long to follow, to play our part.

[Chorus]  
Come, let us gather, behold His grace,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our place.  
Glory cloud descending, filling every space,  
With fire by night, His love we embrace.

[Bridge]  
Oh, lead us in the journey, through the night and day,  
With Your glory as our compass, we will find our way.  
When the cloud is lifted, we will rise and go,  
For in every step we take, Your presence we will know.

[Chorus]  
Come, let us gather, behold His grace,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our place.  
Glory cloud descending, filling every space,  
With fire by night, His love we embrace.

[Outro]  
So here we stand, in awe of Thee,  
In the tabernacle, set us free.  
With every heart, we lift Your song,  
In the sacred space where we belong.  
Amen.
