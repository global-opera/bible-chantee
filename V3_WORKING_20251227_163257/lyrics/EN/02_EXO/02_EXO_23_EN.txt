[TITLE]
Walk in His Ways

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
When the world whispers lies,  
I will stand for what is true,  
With my heart set on the prize.  
No false witness on my lips,  
No wicked path to tread,  
I will love my enemy,  
With the mercy He has spread.  

Chorus  
Oh, walk in His ways,  
Follow the light of His grace,  
He will guide my heart,  
In every step, I’ll find my place.  
With the firstfruits of my labor,  
I give my all to Him,  
In the feast of His favor,  
My soul will never dim.

Verse 2  
Resting in the seventh year,  
Let the land breathe again,  
With the stranger, the poor, the meek,  
Together we will stand.  
For His judgment is so just,  
In His love we’ll find our peace,  
He will bless the bread we break,  
And our worries He’ll release.

Bridge  
He sent an Angel before me,  
To lead me in His way,  
No fear shall overcome me,  
In His arms, I’ll stay.  
He will fight for His people,  
With a promise ever true,  
In every battle, He’s with us,  
We’ll rise, we’ll see it through.

Outro  
So I’ll trust in His promise,  
And I’ll never let go,  
For in walking His ways,  
His love will ever flow.  
With each breath that I take,  
In His presence, I will stay,  
For my heart is forever,  
In the light of His way.
