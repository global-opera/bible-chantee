[TITLE]
Let My People Go

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of the night,  
God's voice came to Moses' heart,  
"Go to Pharaoh, speak my name,  
Let my people rise and stand apart.  
For their cries have reached the skies,  
I am the Lord, hear my plea,  
Let them go, for they are mine,  
To serve and worship, set them free."

[Chorus]  
Let my people go, O Pharaoh,  
Hear the call of the Lord today.  
With signs and wonders all around,  
His mighty hand will lead the way.  
From darkness into light,  
He breaks the chains of our despair,  
Let my people go, O Pharaoh,  
For the Lord is with us everywhere.

[Verse 2]  
Frogs upon the land did rise,  
A plague that filled the streets with dread,  
Yet Pharaoh’s heart was hard as stone,  
Ignoring every word God said.  
But Moses prayed, and God’s command,  
Brought relief to every soul,  
The frogs were gone by morning light,  
His people’s freedom was the goal.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
In the land of Goshen, His light will shine,  
A division made, His grace divine.  
Through trials and plagues, He holds us near,  
With every cry, He’s always here.  
So rise, O people, lift your voice,  
In faith we stand, and we rejoice.  
The Lord is mighty, His word is true,  
In every battle, He fights for you.

[Outro]  
Let my people go, let them be free,  
For in their hearts, His love shall be.  
With every promise, He will abide,  
In His embrace, we will abide.  
Let my people go, O let them go,  
For the Lord is with us, this we know.
