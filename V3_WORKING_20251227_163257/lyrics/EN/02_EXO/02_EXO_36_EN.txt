### Title: "For the Work of Your Sanctuary"

Verse 1  
In the heart of Your people, Lord,  
You placed wisdom to arise,  
Bezaleel and Aholiab,  
Crafting beauty in Your eyes.  
Every hand is joined in service,  
Each heart stirred with love so true,  
For the work of Your sanctuary,  
We offer all we have to You.

Chorus  
Oh, we bring our gifts before You,  
A tapestry of praise,  
With every thread of sacrifice,  
We weave our lives in grace.  
You are our foundation,  
In unity we stand,  
For the work of Your sanctuary,  
We trust in Your command.

Verse 2  
The curtains, woven fine and bright,  
In colors bold, a sacred sign,  
With loops of gold, we come together,  
A dwelling place, Your love entwined.  
Each board and bar, so skillfully made,  
A home for Your presence, pure and true,  
In the shadows of the tabernacle,  
Our hearts find refuge, Lord, in You.

Chorus  
Oh, we bring our gifts before You,  
A tapestry of praise,

We weave our lives in grace.

In unity we stand,

Bridge  
Let not our hands grow weary,  
As we labor in Your name,  
You are the strength within us,  
In this holy, sacred flame.  
Together we will build, Lord,  
A place where You can dwell,  
With every act of kindness,  
Your glory we will tell.

Chorus

Outro  
In the heart of Your people, Lord,  
We gather as one, renewed,  
For the work of Your sanctuary,  
Forever we will serve with You.
