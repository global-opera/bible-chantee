[TITLE]
Crafted by His Hand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of the mountain, the voice of God did call,  
To Bezaleel, He spoke with wisdom, filling him with all,  
With spirit of understanding, he was set apart,  
To weave the threads of glory, to reflect the Father's heart.  

Chorus:  
Crafted by His hand, our lives designed with care,  
In the shadow of the sacred, His presence we declare.  
Through the work of our hands, let His glory shine,  
In the beauty of creation, His love we intertwine.  

Verse 2:  
Aholiab stood beside him, wisdom in their hearts,  
Together they would build a place where worship never parts.  
The tabernacle shines with mercy, an altar made of grace,  
In every stone and timber, we find His holy space.

Bridge:  
Remembering the Sabbath, a sign of His embrace,  
In rest, we find our refuge, in His holy place.  
For six days He created, in awe we lift our praise,  
On the seventh He rested, in stillness we remain.

Outro:  
So let us build His kingdom, with hearts forever true,  
In the spirit of His calling, we find our strength anew.  
Crafted by His hand, we rise to take our stand,  
With every breath we offer, we’re woven by His plan.
