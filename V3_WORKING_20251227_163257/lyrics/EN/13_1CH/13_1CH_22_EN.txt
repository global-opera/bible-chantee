[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the heart of the land, where Your presence resides,  
David gathered the stones, as he sought to abide.  
With iron and brass, the finest of gold,  
He prepared for Your glory, a story retold.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, oh, rise up, let our hearts be aligned,  
In the house of the Lord, where His glory we find.  
With wisdom and courage, we follow His way,  
To build up the altar, to worship and pray.  

(Verse 2)  
Solomon, young, tender, called to lead the way,  
A man of great peace, for Your name he will sway.  
The nations will gather to witness Your might,  
In a temple of worship, Your love shining bright.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, oh, rise up, let our hearts be aligned,  
In the house of the Lord, where His glory we find.  
With wisdom and courage, we follow His way,  
To build up the altar, to worship and pray.  

(Bridge)  
For You are our refuge, our strength in the fight,  
With every stone laid, we surrender to light.  
The work of our hands, in Your name we commit,  
As we seek You in spirit, and in truth we submit.  

(Verse 3)  
Now set your hearts, let your souls seek His face,  
To build the sanctuary, a holy embrace.  
With every breath taken, we long to proclaim,  
In the house of the Lord, we lift up His name.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, oh, rise up, let our hearts be aligned,  
In the house of the Lord, where His glory we find.  
With wisdom and courage, we follow His way,  
To build up the altar, to worship and pray.  

(Outro)  
Yes, in the house of the Lord, our hearts will unite,  
As we build up His kingdom, shining ever so bright.  
With each stone that we lay, may His presence draw near,  
For in the house of the Lord, we will worship with cheer.
