[TITLE]
The Lord Goes Before Us

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the valley of shadows, where the giants stand tall,  
David sought Your wisdom, Lord, he answered Your call.  
With the strength of the cedars, and a heart full of praise,  
You lifted him high, in Your glorious ways.  

Chorus  
You go before us, O God of the fight,  
In the midst of our battles, You shine Your light.  
With the sound of the mulberry, guiding our way,  
We trust in Your promise, we’ll follow and stay.  

Verse 2  
The Philistines gathered, their armies in might,  
But David inquired, “Will You lead me to fight?”  
You said, “Go up boldly, for I am your shield,  
In the breaking of waters, the victory revealed.”  

Chorus  
You go before us, O God of the fight,  
In the midst of our battles, You shine Your light.  
With the sound of the mulberry, guiding our way,  
We trust in Your promise, we’ll follow and stay.  

Bridge  
When we fear the darkness and shadows surround,  
We’ll lift up our voices, in Your love we are found.  
For every stronghold, every fear laid to rest,  
You are our fortress, in You we are blessed.  

Chorus  
You go before us, O God of the fight,  
In the midst of our battles, You shine Your light.  
With the sound of the mulberry, guiding our way,  
We trust in Your promise, we’ll follow and stay.  

Outro  
So we stand in Your presence, our hearts open wide,  
With the fame of Your glory, let Your love be our guide.  
In the battles we face, we will sing of Your grace,  
For You go before us, our King, take Your place.  
Amen.
