[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
From the shadows, we gather near,  
In the heart of Hebron, we draw so near,  
Bone of my bone, in unity we stand,  
Your promise, O Lord, leads us by Your hand.  
In the days of old, when Saul was king,  
You called David forth, our hearts to sing.  
To shepherd Your people, to lead us home,  
In the city of David, we will not roam.

Chorus:  
Oh, the city of David, where Your glory shines,  
With courage like lions, in battle we rise.  
Your Word is our fortress, our hope and our guide,  
In the city of David, forever abide.

Verse 2:  
Through the fortress of Zion, where giants fall,

In the face of the foe, they stood so brave,  
For the Lord of Hosts, our hearts He'll save.  
Joab led the charge with valor and might,  
In the midst of the fight, we see Your light.  
Oh, the water from Bethlehem, sweet as a dream,  
David poured it out, a sacrifice supreme.
