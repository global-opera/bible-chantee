[TITLE]
Chosen to Build

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In Jerusalem, the people came,  
Princes gathered in His name,  
David stood, his heart ablaze,  
To build a house, to sing His praise.  
But God had said, "Not by your hand,  
For blood was shed, I’ve other plans."  
Yet from your line, a son will rise,  
To build My house, to lift My eyes.

(Chorus)  
Oh, Solomon, be strong and true,  
The Lord is with you, guiding through.  
With every stone, with every prayer,  
His presence dwells, forever there.  
For He has chosen you to stand,  
To build a house upon this land.

(Verse 2)  
The pattern given, the treasures weighed,  
For every vessel that must be made.  
Gold and silver, hearts aligned,  
In service pure, His love we'll find.  
Remember Him, seek Him each day,  
With perfect heart, in all you say.  
The Lord will search your thoughts within,  
For those who seek, will find Him there.

(Chorus)

(Bridge)  
Fear not, my son, for I am near,  
Through trials faced, I’ll calm your fear.  
With priests and Levites at your side,  
In every step, I will abide.  
The work is great, but do not tire,  
For I will fuel your heart with fire.

(Outro)  
In the shadow of His grace,  
We build a home, a holy place.  
With faith unshaken, hearts ablaze,  
We lift our voices, sing His praise.  
Oh, chosen one, arise and sing,  
For in this house, our praises ring.  
Forevermore, our hearts will dwell,  
In the love of God, Emmanuel.
