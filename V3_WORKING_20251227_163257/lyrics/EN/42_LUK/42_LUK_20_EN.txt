[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the temple where He taught,  
With authority that none could doubt,  
They questioned Him, the leaders sought,  
Yet Truth stood firm, He turned about.  
He shared a tale of a vineyard grown,  
Of servants sent, yet met with scorn,  
A son beloved, cast out alone,  
The heir rejected, our hearts now mourn.  

(Chorus)  
You are the stone the builders spurned,  
Yet You became the cornerstone,  
In every heart, Your love we've learned,  
In every life, Your grace has shone.  
Oh, Jesus, our Savior, our guiding light,  
We lay our hopes at Your feet tonight.  

(Verse 2)  
They plotted, schemed, with hearts of stone,

But to our God, our hearts we share.

Of resurrection, hope restored,  
In You alone, our souls are saved,  
The God of living, forever adored.
