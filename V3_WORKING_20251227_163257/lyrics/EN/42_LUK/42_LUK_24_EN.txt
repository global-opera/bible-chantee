[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
On the first day of the week, early dawn,  
The women came to seek, where hope had gone.  
They brought their spices, tears in their eyes,  
But the stone was rolled away, to their surprise.  

Chorus:  
He is risen! He is alive!  
In the darkness, He brings light.  
Why seek the living among the dead?  
Our Savior lives, just as He said.  

Verse 2:  
Two men in shining clothes, spoke with grace,  
"Why are you troubled? Look upon this place.  
Remember His words, in Galilee,  
The Son of Man must rise, for you and me."  

Chorus:  
He is risen! He is alive!  
In the darkness, He brings light.  
Why seek the living among the dead?  
Our Savior lives, just as He said.  

Bridge:  
O fools, slow to believe,  
He opened the Scriptures, our hearts received.  
The breaking of bread, their eyes were clear,  
In the midst of our doubt, He draws near.  

Verse 3:  
Now we proclaim, He’s risen indeed,  
With joy in our hearts, we share the good seed.  
Repentance and grace, we lift His name high,  
From Jerusalem’s streets, our praises will fly.  

Chorus:  
He is risen! He is alive!  
In the darkness, He brings light.  
Why seek the living among the dead?

Outro:  
He is risen! Oh, let us rejoice,  
In wonder and love, we lift up our voice.  
With hearts full of praise, we gather and sing,  
Our Savior is here, our risen King!
