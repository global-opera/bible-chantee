[TITLE]
Increase Our Faith

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In a world where trials come,  
We gather, Lord, in Your name,  
For the little ones we've wronged,  
Let our hearts feel the same.  
Teach us grace to forgive,  
When our brothers fall away,  
Help us turn our hearts to love,  
And walk in Your light each day.

Chorus:  
Increase our faith, like a mustard seed,  
Let us trust in You, O Lord, indeed.  
With every step, we’ll follow Your way,  
In Your mercy and love, we find our stay.

Verse 2:  
Ten lepers cried out for grace,  
“Jesus, Master, hear our plea,”  
As they went, they found their place,  
In Your healing, Lord, we see.  
But one returned to give You praise,  
With a heart so full of thanks,  
You said, “Rise, your faith has saved,”  
In Your presence, we find our ranks.

Chorus:  
Increase our faith, like a mustard seed,  
Let us trust in You, O Lord, indeed.  
With every step, we’ll follow Your way,  
In Your mercy and love, we find our stay.

Bridge:  
As the lightning shines from east to west,  
Your kingdom comes, within our chest.  
Help us remember, Lord, to be awake,  
In the days of Noah, we will not forsake.

Verse 3:  
In the fields and on the roofs,  
Let us not look back in doubt,  
For whoever seeks to save their life,  
Shall lose it, Lord, here’s our shout.  
We’re unworthy servants, at Your feet we lay,  
In Your arms of mercy, we choose to stay.

Chorus:  
Increase our faith, like a mustard seed,  
Let us trust in You, O Lord, indeed.  
With every step, we’ll follow Your way,  
In Your mercy and love, we find our stay.

Outro:  
So we lift our hearts in prayer,  
With a hope that’s strong and true,  
Increase our faith, O Lord, we declare,  
In Your love, we'll always pursue.
