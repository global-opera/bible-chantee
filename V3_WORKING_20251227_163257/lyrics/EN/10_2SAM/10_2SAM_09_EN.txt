[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the king, a question raised,  
“Is there anyone left from Saul’s house, to be praised?”  
Then Ziba stood, a servant true and bold,  
He spoke of Jonathan’s son, a story to be told.  
In Lodebar, Mephibosheth lay,  
His feet were lame, yet hope found a way.

Chorus:  
Come to the table, where grace abounds,  
With kindness flowing, love knows no bounds.  
Fear not, oh weary, you’re welcomed here,  
At the king’s table, there’s nothing to fear.  
From the ashes of shame, we rise and proclaim,  
In the kindness of God, we’re never the same.

Verse 2:  
David called him forth, with a heart full of grace,  
“Fear not, Mephibosheth, you have a place.”  
“I’ll restore what was lost, you’re my son now too,  
At my table you’ll feast, your spirit made new.”  
With humble heart, he bowed on the floor,  
“What am I, a dead dog, that You’d love me more?”

Bridge:  
Oh, the king’s heart is wide,  
His mercy our guide.  
In the feast of His love, we find our place,  
Restored and redeemed by His boundless grace.  
No longer forsaken, we’re cherished and free,  
At the table of mercy, we find our decree.

So let the world see, the love that we share,

Feasting on mercy, each and every day.
