[TITLE]  
"The Call of the King"

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the stone, a voice cried out,  
Sheba's trumpet rang, igniting doubt.  
"To your tents, O Israel, no part in David here,"  
But Judah stood unwavering, holding dear.  

[Chorus]  
O King, we lift our hearts to You,  
In battles fought, in faith so true.  
Through trials and storms, we won't depart,  
With You, our King, we'll never part.  

[Verse 2]  
Amasa gathered the men, but time slipped away,  
David sent his warriors, led them to the fray.  
Joab's sword in hand, his heart ablaze,  
Yet in the midst of chaos, wisdom guides our ways.  

[Chorus]  
O King, we lift our hearts to You,  
In battles fought, in faith so true.  
Through trials and storms, we won't depart,  
With You, our King, we'll never part.  

[Bridge]  
In the city of Abel, a wise woman stood,  
“Peace for our people, let’s act for good.”  
“Deliver Sheba, let our hearts be free,  
For the sake of our nation, let life be the plea.”  

[Verse 3]  
With courage and grace, the head was displayed,  
Joab’s trumpet sounded, the conflict swayed.  
Back to Jerusalem, a victory won,  
In the heart of our King, our battles are done.  

[Chorus]  
O King, we lift our hearts to You,  
In battles fought, in faith so true.  
Through trials and storms, we won't depart,  
With You, our King, we'll never part.  

[Outro]  
O King, our hearts, we bring anew,  
In every season, we trust in You.  
Through the storms of life, we’ll stand our ground,  
In the love of our King, our hope is found.
