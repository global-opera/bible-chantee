[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadows of the night, when the battles rage,  
A long war between two houses, a turning of the page.  
David waxed strong, while Saul's house grew weak,  
In the silence of the Lord, His promises we seek.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, David, in the strength of Your name,  
You are the chosen one, forever the same.  
From Hebron to the throne, Your heart leads the way,  
In the midst of the storm, Lord, we trust and obey.  

(Verse 2)  
Abner made his stand for the house of Saul,  
But a whisper in the wind, it beckons us all.  
"Make a league with David," the elders proclaim,  
For the Lord has spoken, His will remains the same.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, David, in the strength of Your name,  
You are the chosen one, forever the same.  
From Hebron to the throne, Your heart leads the way,  
In the midst of the storm, Lord, we trust and obey.  

(Bridge)  
In the grave of Abner, the king weeps and mourns,  
A lament for the fallen, in the silence that forms.  
Yet even through sorrow, Your purpose prevails,  
For the heart of the humble, in Your presence never fails.  

(Verse 3)  
So let the nations hear, let the tribes unite,  
In the light of Your glory, we’ll stand and fight.  
For the blood of the righteous cries out from the ground,  
In the name of Jesus, our victory is found.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, rise up, David, in the strength of Your name,  
You are the chosen one, forever the same.  
From Hebron to the throne, Your heart leads the way,  
In the midst of the storm, Lord, we trust and obey.  

(Outro)  
So we gather in Your presence, with hearts open wide,  
For in every battle, we’ll run to Your side.  
In the rise of David, Your promise shall reign,  
Through the trials and the triumphs, we’ll never be the same.
