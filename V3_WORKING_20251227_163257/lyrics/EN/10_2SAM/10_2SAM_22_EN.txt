[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the day of my distress, I called out to You,  
From the depths of my sorrow, You heard my cry true.  
You are my fortress, my shield in the night,  
The horn of my salvation, my guiding light.  

[Chorus]  
The Lord, my rock, I will praise Your name,  
In the battles I face, forever the same.  
You lift me high, through the storms I will stand,  
With Your strength, O God, I am led by Your hand.

I will give thanks, Lord, and sing of Your praise.  

[Verse 3]  
You brought me from darkness, into Your light,  
My refuge, my Savior, in You I find might.

The Lord lives, and blessed be my Rock,  
I’ll sing of Your mercy, my heart’s eternal clock.

For You are my strength, Lord, to You I belong.
