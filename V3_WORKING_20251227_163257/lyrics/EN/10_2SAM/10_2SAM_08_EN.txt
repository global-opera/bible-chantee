[TITLE]
Victory in His Name

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the valley, where the battles rage,  
David stood with courage, turning every page.  
With the strength of the Almighty, he faced the foe,  
Philistines and Moabites, brought low by his blow.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, our God, the mighty warrior,  
You fight for us, we stand in awe.  
With every victory, we lift Your name,  
In the heart of battle, You remain the same.  

[Verse 2]  
From the river Euphrates, to the lands of Zobah,  
He claimed the shields of gold, for the glory of Yahweh.  
Twenty thousand strong, the enemy fell,  
In the name of the Lord, we shall always dwell.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, our God, the mighty warrior,  
You fight for us, we stand in awe.  
With every victory, we lift Your name,  
In the heart of battle, You remain the same.  

[Bridge]  
King David reigned with justice and grace,  
Your hand upon him, in every place.  
In the midst of trials, we will not fear,  
For our God is with us, His presence is near.  

[Verse 3]  
Toi sent his blessings, vessels of gold,  
David dedicated all, his heart so bold.  
From every nation, we bring our praise,  
In the house of the Lord, forever we’ll raise.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, our God, the mighty warrior,  
You fight for us, we stand in awe.  
With every victory, we lift Your name,  
In the heart of battle, You remain the same.  

[Outro]  
So we march on forward, with faith in our hearts,  
In the name of Jesus, we’ll never depart.  
With each step we take, Your love is our guide,  
In the victory of Christ, we forever abide.
