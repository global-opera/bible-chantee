[LYRICS]
Kichwa: Hekima Yako, Ee Bwana

[Verse 1]  
Katika giza, unatuongoza,  
Moyo wa hekima, unatupeleka.  
Upumbavu hulala, mahali pa chini,  
Lakini wewe, Ee Bwana, ni mwanga wangu.

[Chorus]  
Hekima yako, Ee Bwana,  
Inatufundisha njia zako.  
Katika barabara zetu,  
Tuonyeshe, tuonyeshe, hekima yako.

[Verse 2]  
Maneno ya kinywa chako, yana neema,  
Mimi nikisikia, napata amani.  
Mpumbavu hujijenga, asijue hatari,  
Lakini wewe, Ee Bwana, unalinda moyo wangu.

[Chorus]  
Hekima yako, Ee Bwana,  
Inatufundisha njia zako.  
Katika barabara zetu,  
Tuonyeshe, tuonyeshe, hekima yako.

[Bridge]  
Wakati wa giza, na mvua inanyesha,  
Nitatembea, kwa njia yako.  
Hatuwezi kujua, yajayo yasemwayo,  
Lakini wewe, unajua, unajua yote.

[Chorus]  
Hekima yako, Ee Bwana,  
Inatufundisha njia zako.  
Katika barabara zetu,  
Tuonyeshe, tuonyeshe, hekima yako.

[Outro]  
Hekima Yako, Ee Bwana,  
Hekima Yako, Ee Bwana,  
Tuonyeshe, tuonyeshe, hekima yako.  
Amen.
