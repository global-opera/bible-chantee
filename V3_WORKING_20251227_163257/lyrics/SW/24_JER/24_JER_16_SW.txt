[TITLE]  
Mimi Ndiyo Bwana

[Verse 1]  
Neno lako, Ee Bwana, linanijia,  
Katika nchi hii, umesema wazi,  
Hawataliliwa, wala hawatazikwa,  
Uovu wa baba zetu, umewakosesha amani.

[Chorus]  
Mimi ndimi Bwana, nitatenda,  
Nitawaleta watu, wavuvi na wawindaji,  
Mato yangu yaziangalia,  
Hawafichiki, Ee Bwana, usikate tamaa.

[Verse 2]  
Watu wote, wakubwa na wadogo,  
Watakufa kwa njaa, na kwa upanga,  
Mizoga yao itakuwa, chakula cha ndege,  
Lakini wewe, unanipa tumaini, Baba.

[Chorus]  
Mimi ndimi Bwana, nitatenda,  
Nitawaleta watu, wavuvi na wawindaji,  
Mato yangu yaziangalia,  
Hawafichiki, Ee Bwana, usikate tamaa.

[Bridge]  
Tazama, siku zinakuja,  
Wakati wa kuleta amani,  
Nitawajulisha, mkono wangu,  
Nitatenda, jina langu litajwe.

[Chorus]  
Mimi ndimi Bwana, nitatenda,  
Nitawaleta watu, wavuvi na wawindaji,  
Mato yangu yaziangalia,  
Hawafichiki, Ee Bwana, usikate tamaa.

[Outro]  
Ee Bwana, nguvu zangu,  
Na kimbilio langu, siku ya taabu,  
Watu wa mataifa, wanakujia,  
Wanasema, “Baba zetu, hawakurithi chochote ila uongo.”
