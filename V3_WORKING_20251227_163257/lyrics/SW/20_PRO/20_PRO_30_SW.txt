[TITLE]  
Mwenyezi Mungu, Jina Lako

[Verse 1]  
Mimi ni kama mnyama, si mtu,  
Ningependa kujua hekima Yako.  
Ni nani aliye panda mbinguni?  
Ni nani atakayewezesha mvuvi?  

[Chorus]  
Mwenyezi Mungu, Jina Lako,  
Kila neno Lako, linanijenga.  
Unishike, nipe hekima,  
Nikiwa mnyonge, niwe na wewe.  

[Verse 2]  
Usininyime, Bwana, matatu,  
Uniondolee ubatili na uongo.  
Usiniletee umaskini, utajiri,  
Unilinde, unipe chakula.  

[Chorus]  
Mwenyezi Mungu, Jina Lako,  
Kila neno Lako, linanijenga.  
Unishike, nipe hekima,  
Nikiwa mnyonge, niwe na wewe.  

[Bridge]  
Kizazi kinatenda dhambi,  
Wakikataa baraka Zako.  
Lakini mimi nitainua,  
Mikono yangu kuelekea mbinguni.  

[Verse 3]  
Kwa maana dunia hutetemeka,  
Kwa sababu ya mambo haya.  
Bwana, niponye, nipe nguvu,  
Nisije nikakukana, nisije nikakosa.  

[Chorus]  
Mwenyezi Mungu, Jina Lako,  
Kila neno Lako, linanijenga.  
Unishike, nipe hekima,  
Nikiwa mnyonge, niwe na wewe.  

[Outro]  
Ninaomba, Bwana, usininyime,  
Ningependa kukujua,  
Ningependa kutembea katika ukweli,  
Mwenyezi Mungu, Jina Lako, ni takatifu.
