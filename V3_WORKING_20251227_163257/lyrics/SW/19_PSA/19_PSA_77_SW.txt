[TITLE]  
Mungu Wetu, Uliye Juu

[Verse 1]  
Nimpazie Mungu sauti yangu,  
Siku ya taabu, nalimtafuta,  
Mkono wangu, unanyoshwa usiku,  
Nafsi yangu, inakataa faraja.  

[Chorus]  
Ee Mungu, njia yako, utakatifu,  
Nani kama wewe, mfalme wa utukufu?  
Maji yalikuona, yakaogopa,  
Maajabu yako, tunayaona.  

[Verse 2]  
Nilipotaka, kumkumbuka,  
Roho yangu, ilizimia,  
Nafikiri, habari za kale,  
Nawaza, wimbo wa usiku.  

[Chorus]  
Ee Mungu, njia yako, utakatifu,  
Nani kama wewe, mfalme wa utukufu?  
Maji yalikuona, yakaogopa,  
Maajabu yako, tunayaona.  

[Bridge]  
Uliwaongoza, kama kundi,  
Kwa mkono wa Musa, na Haruni,  
Mawingu yalimwaga, sauti ikawa,  
Nchi ilitetemeka, utukufu wako.  

[Chorus]  
Ee Mungu, njia yako, utakatifu,  
Nani kama wewe, mfalme wa utukufu?  
Maji yalikuona, yakaogopa,  
Maajabu yako, tunayaona.  

[Outro]  
Mungu wetu, Uliye Juu,  
Katika giza, uongozi wetu,  
Tutakumbuka, fadhili zako,  
Milele na milele, ni wewe tu.
