[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Mungu na atufadhili,  
Kutubariki kwa uso wake,  
Njia yake ijulike,  
Katika mataifa, wokovu wake.  

[Chorus]  
Watu na wakushukuru, Ee Mungu,  
Watu wote na wakushukuru,  
Mataifa na washangilie,  
Waimbe kwa furaha,  
Kwa haki utawahukumu,  
Na kuwaongoza, Ee Baba.  

[Verse 2]  
Nchi imetoa mazao yake,  
Mungu wetu ametubariki,  
Miisho yote ya dunia,  
Itamcha Yeye, daima.  

[Chorus]  
Watu na wakushukuru, Ee Mungu,  
Watu wote na wakushukuru,  
Mataifa na washangilie,  
Waimbe kwa furaha,  
Kwa haki utawahukumu,  
Na kuwaongoza, Ee Baba.  

[Bridge]  
Tukufu ni jina lako,  
Katika kila taifa,  
Tutaimba na kushukuru,  
Kwa neema yako, Ee Mungu.  

[Chorus]  
Watu na wakushukuru, Ee Mungu,  
Watu wote na wakushukuru,

[Outro]  
Mungu atatubariki,  
Na tumshukuru daima,

Itamcha Yeye, daima.
