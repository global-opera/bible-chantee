[TITLE]  
Mfalme wa Amani

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Ninainua macho yangu,  
Nakuangalia, ewe Bwana,  
Matajiri wanatoa,  
Lakini mjane anatoa moyo.  
Katika umaskini wake,  
Anatoa vyote alivyokuwa navyo,  
Hakika, wewe ni Mfalme,  
Moyo wangu umekushikilia.

[Chorus]  
Mfalme wa Amani,  
Ninakuinua, ninakutukuza,  
Katika dhiki na huzuni,  
Wewe ni mfariji wangu.  
Ninaposhuhudia,  
Nitatenda kwa jina lako,  
Ufalme wako u karibu,  
Bwana, ni wewe pekee.

[Verse 2]  
Wakati wa vita na fitina,  
Nitatulia kwako, Bwana,  
Mimi siogopi,  
Maana wewe unaniinua.  
Mimi ni wa kwako,  
Nitatembea kwa nuru yako,  
Kila neno ulilonena,  
Litatimia, hakika ni kweli.

[Chorus]  
Mfalme wa Amani,  
Ninakuinua, ninakutukuza,

Ninaposhuhudia,

Ufalme wako u karibu,

[Bridge]  
Mioyo yetu isiwe na uzito,  
Katika ulafi na masumbufu,  
Kesheni, ombeni,  
Ukombozi unakaribia.  
Tazameni majira,  
Ufalme wa Mungu u karibu,  
Basi, tuinuke,  
Kwa maana, wewe ni Mfalme.

This worship song captures the essence of the biblical text, emphasizing themes of humility, faith, and the nearness of God's kingdom. The structure allows for an intimate and reflective worship experience, suitable for gentle instrumentation and a choir.
