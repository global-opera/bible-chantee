I take a moment here with You,
No noise, no rush.
Before I even ask,
I simply stop.

There are things I tend to forget,
Steps that You carried for me.
Even when I saw nothing,
You were already there.

I recognize
Your hand upon my path.

I give You thanks,
For every day I’m standing still.
I give You thanks,
For Your presence around my life.
Without understanding all,
Without seeing all,
I recognize
That You are faithful.

There were long and silent seasons,
Times with no reply.
But looking back,
I see Your patience at work.

What I thought was lost,
You kept along the way.
Even when I was distracted,
You never left.

I recognize
Your faithfulness.

I give You thanks,
Once again today.
I give You thanks,
Simply.
