This moment feels different inside,
I feel Your peace surrounding me.
My heart grows calm, my spirit opens,
You are here, faithful God.

When the road becomes uncertain,
When fear tries to take its place,
I remember every promise spoken,
You watch over every step I take.

I do not walk alone,
I will not retreat.
My trust is found in You,
My refuge, my King.

You are my protection,
My safe shelter, my strong fortress.
Nothing will reach me,
I am kept by Your hand.
You are my protection,
By day and through the night.
I stand without fear,
For You are watching over my life.

Even when the shadows draw near,
Even when the battle rises up,
Your faithfulness surrounds me,
Like a wall that cannot break.

I move forward unharmed,
I remain standing in faith.
For You command Your angels
To guard me wherever I go.

I do not walk alone,
I will not be shaken.
My trust is found in You,
My help, my King.

You are my protection,
My safe shelter, my strong fortress.
Nothing will reach me,
I am kept by Your hand.
You are my protection,
Today and forevermore.
I live within Your peace,
Under Your loving care.
