This morning feels different inside,
Something has shifted in the air.
My heart can tell, my spirit knows,
You led me here, Eternal Father.

Before the doors that stayed closed,
I knocked, I prayed, I stood in faith.
Time went on, the waiting weighed,
Yet You were working all along.

This is not the time to step back,
This is not the time to doubt.
Every promise You have spoken
Is coming true right now.

You open doors no one can close,
You break the chains, none can resist.
Your voice resounds, the night retreats,
Victory erupts, Jesus reigns.

Locks are falling,
Walls are shaking,
At Your name all things bow.

You open doors no one can close,
Your light advances, fear is gone,
Victory erupts, Jesus reigns.
