In the silence I sort my thoughts,
Between what rushes and what matters.
There are voices I hear too often,
And Yours I sometimes forget.

I learned to fill the empty spaces,
To hide the weight of my questions.
But today I change the order,
I come back to You.

You are waiting in my heart,
Present, faithful.

First I seek You,
Before I lose myself.
First I seek You,
To see things clear.
I put You first,
Even when I tremble,
And I can breathe again.

When I seek You in my thoughts,
Inner walls begin to move.
You speak where I used to run,
You light what I avoided.

I don’t ask for every answer,
I only ask for truth.
Even when it hurts,
I choose to trust You.

You are waiting in my heart,
Present, faithful.

First I seek You,
And something opens wide.
First I seek You,
And fear has no place.
I put You first,
Again today.
First I seek You,
And I feel alive.
