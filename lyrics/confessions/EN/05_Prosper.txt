[Verse 1]
Let this book not depart from my lips
I meditate on it day and night
Then I succeed in my ways
And blessing grows in me

[Chorus]
I prosper, by the hand of God
All that I do bears fruit
I prosper, under His eyes
My life flourishes day and night

[Verse 2]
I am like a tree planted by the water
That gives its fruit in season
All that I undertake succeeds
In the peace of His house
