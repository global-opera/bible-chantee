My Help Comes from the Lord

I lift my eyes unto the hills,
Where does my hope arise?
My help comes from the Maker,
Of heavens and the skies.
He will not let my foot be moved,
In Him I find my rest,
For He who watches over me,
Is faithful and is best.

Oh, Lord, You are my keeper,
In shadows, You’re my light.
Through every day and every night,
You guard my heart, my soul, my way,
Forever, Lord, I’ll trust and stay.
My help comes from the Lord,
In Him, I am secure.

Behold, He never slumbers,
In watchful love, He stands.
The sun shall not smite by day,
Nor moon with gentle hands.
He’ll guard my going out and in,
From now until the end,
With grace, You keep my weary heart,
My faithful, loving friend.

Oh, Lord, You are my keeper,
In shadows, You’re my light.
Through every day and every night,
You guard my heart, my soul, my way,
Forever, Lord, I’ll trust and stay.
My help comes from the Lord,
In Him, I am secure.

I will not fear the darkness,
You are my refuge strong.
In every storm, Your presence,
Is where my heart belongs.
You preserve my soul, O Lord,
With every breath I take,
In every moment, You are here,
My heart, You will not shake.

Oh, Lord, You are my keeper,
In shadows, You’re my light.
Through every day and every night,
You guard my heart, my soul, my way,
Forever, Lord, I’ll trust and stay.
My help comes from the Lord,
In Him, I am secure.

From this time forth, forevermore,
In You, I find my peace.
My help comes from the Lord,
In You, my fears will cease.
Forever, Lord, I’ll trust and stay,
My help comes from the Lord today.
