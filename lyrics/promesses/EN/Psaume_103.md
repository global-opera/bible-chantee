Bless the Lord, O My Soul

Bless the Lord, O my soul,
With all my heart, I lift His name.
He forgives my every sin,
And heals me with His grace again.
He redeems my life from the depths of despair,
Crowning me with love, tender mercies to share.

Oh, bless the Lord, my soul,
Remember all His benefits,
His mercy is forever whole,
He is my refuge, my hope, my rest.
As the heavens are high, His love reaches me,
From east to the west, He sets my spirit free.

He knows my frame, He sees my heart,
Like a father, He holds me near.
In every trial, He plays His part,
His kindness wipes away my fear.
The grass may wither, the flowers fade,
But His love endures, forever displayed.

Oh, bless the Lord, my soul,
Remember all His benefits,
His mercy is forever whole,
He is my refuge, my hope, my rest.
As the heavens are high, His love reaches me,
From east to the west, He sets my spirit free.

Angels, sing of His might,
All creation, lift Him high.
In His kingdom, we rejoice,
With every breath, we praise His voice.
His righteousness flows to our children’s days,
Forever and ever, we’ll sing His praise.

Oh, bless the Lord, my soul,
Remember all His benefits,
His mercy is forever whole,
He is my refuge, my hope, my rest.
As the heavens are high, His love reaches me,
From east to the west, He sets my spirit free.

Bless the Lord, O my soul,
In every moment, let His love unfold.
With tender hearts, we give Him praise,
Forever and ever, we sing His ways.
