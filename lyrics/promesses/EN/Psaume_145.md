I Will Extol You, My God

I will extol You, my God, my King,
Forever, my heart will sing.
Every day, I bless Your name,
Your greatness, Lord, will never change.
Unsearchable, Your mighty ways,
From generation, we lift our praise.

Great is the Lord, forever adored,
Your mercy flows like a mighty shore.
Gracious and kind, You hear our plea,
In every heart, Lord, set us free.

I speak of the glory of Your reign,
Your wondrous works, our souls proclaim.
You uphold the weak and give them grace,
In Your embrace, we find our place.
Your kingdom stands through endless days,
We sing of Your power and Your ways.

Great is the Lord, forever adored,
Your mercy flows like a mighty shore.
Gracious and kind, You hear our plea,
In every heart, Lord, set us free.

You open Your hand, satisfying all,
To those who call, You hear their call.
Your righteousness shines, holy and true,
In every moment, we turn to You.
Preserve us, Lord, as we seek Your face,
In truth, we find our resting place.

Great is the Lord, forever adored,
Your mercy flows like a mighty shore.
Gracious and kind, You hear our plea,
In every heart, Lord, set us free.

My mouth will speak Your praise, O Lord,
Let every heart sing, let every soul soar.
Forever and ever, Your name we'll bless,
In love and in faith, we find our rest.
