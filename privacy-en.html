Privacy Policy – Sung Bible (Bible Chantée)

Last updated: 29 July 2026

1. Introduction

The Sung Bible (Bible Chantée) application is developed as a Trusted Web Activity (TWA) and displays the secure content of the website https://biblechantee.com. It does not require the creation of a user account.

The application is published by Stéphane Cassani, Switzerland. This privacy policy explains what data may be processed when using the application, for what purpose, and what your rights are.

2. Data collected

The application does not request any direct personal identification information (name, postal address, telephone number or password) in order to function.

The website displayed in the application uses Google Analytics, which may collect certain technical information such as:

  • the pages viewed;
  • interactions with the application;
  • pseudonymised technical identifiers;
  • device information;
  • usage data intended for audience measurement.

This information is processed in accordance with the settings configured in Google Analytics.

The application also stores certain content locally on your device (cache of audio files and texts) in order to improve performance and offline operation. This data remains solely on your device and is never transmitted to the publisher.

3. Purposes

The technical data collected via Google Analytics is used solely in order to:

  • measure audience;
  • improve the application;
  • better understand how it is used.

Local storage is used solely in order to improve the performance and offline availability of the application.

4. Third-party services

The application uses the following services:

  • Google Analytics (audience measurement);
  • Google Play (application distribution and licence verification);
  • a hosting provider ensuring the availability of the website.

Google's privacy policy is available at the following address:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

The application also contains links to external platforms (PayPal and GoFundMe) allowing users to support the project. These platforms have their own privacy policies. The publisher neither receives nor processes any payment data.

5. Data retention

Data collected by Google Analytics is retained according to the retention settings configured in that service.

Data stored locally remains on your device and can be deleted by clearing the application cache or by uninstalling it.

6. Security

Access to the website and to the application takes place exclusively via an encrypted HTTPS connection.

The application has no user database and stores no personal data on the publisher's servers.

7. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have in particular the following rights:

  • right of access;
  • right to rectification;
  • right to erasure;
  • right to restriction of processing;
  • right to object;
  • right to data portability.

Processing carried out via Google Analytics is also subject to Google's privacy policy.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

8. Contact

For any question concerning this privacy policy or the processing of data:

info@biblechantee.com
