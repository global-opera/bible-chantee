[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the sight of the Lord, we stand as His own,  
Children of the promise, in grace we have grown.  
Set apart, a holy people, chosen with care,  
Above all nations, His love we declare.

Chorus  
We are the children of God,  
Living in His light,  
With every step we take,  
We walk in His might.  
In reverence and joy,  
We lift our hearts high,  
For the Lord is our guide,  
We shall never deny.

Verse 2  
With wisdom He leads us, in paths that are true,  
His laws and His blessings, forever renew.  
From every clean creature, we gather to feast,  
In His house we rejoice, as His love is released.

Bridge  
As we tithe with glad hearts, our offerings rise,  
In the place that He’s chosen, we lift our eyes.  
For the stranger, the widow, the lost, and the meek,  
In His love, we find strength, in His word, we speak.
