[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Moab, Moses spoke,  
Words of the covenant, a promise invoked.  
"Remember the wonders, the signs you have seen,  
In Egypt's shadows, where I set you free."  

Chorus  
We stand as one before You, Lord,  
In hearts united, we lift one accord.  
You are our God, our faithful guide,  
In Your covenant love, we will abide.  

Verse 2  
For forty years, through wilderness vast,  
Your hand has led us; Your love holds fast.  
Our shoes unbroken, our hearts You renew,  
In every struggle, we find strength in You.  

Chorus  
We stand as one before You, Lord,  
In hearts united, we lift one accord.  
You are our God, our faithful guide,  
In Your covenant love, we will abide.  

Bridge  
Keep our hearts close, lest we stray away,  
From the truth of Your promise, let us not delay.  
We choose to follow, to trust in Your name,  
For in Your presence, we’ll never be the same.  

Verse 3  
With little ones gathered, and strangers we greet,  
We enter Your promise, in faith we repeat.  
You swear to be with us, from ages before,  
To Abraham, Isaac, forevermore.  

Chorus  
We stand as one before You, Lord,  
In hearts united, we lift one accord.  
You are our God, our faithful guide,  
In Your covenant love, we will abide.  

Outro  
Oh, Lord of the covenant, hear our cry,  
In every generation, we lift our eyes high.  
For the secret things belong to You,  
But revealed to us, Your word we pursue.
