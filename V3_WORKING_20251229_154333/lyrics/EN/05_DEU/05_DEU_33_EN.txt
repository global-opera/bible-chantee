[TITLE]
Blessings from the Mountain

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From Sinai, the Lord descended,  
With fire and light, His love extended,  
Ten thousand saints around Him stand,  
He comes to bless, to guide our land.  
His law a fire, our hearts ignite,  
In every promise, we find our light.  

Chorus  
O Israel, rejoice and sing,  
For the God of blessings is our King.  
In His arms, we find our place,  
Forever held by His grace.  
Happy are we, the chosen ones,  
Saved by the Lord, His love never shuns.  

Verse 2  
Judah calls, hear his sweet voice,  
In battle’s midst, we lift our choice.  
Levi’s heart, with judgment true,  
Teaching us the ways anew.  
Benjamin, safe in the Lord’s embrace,  
Cloaked in mercy, we seek His face.

Bridge  
None can compare to the God we serve,  
In heaven’s heights, His strength preserves.  
The eternal refuge, our fortress strong,  
In every battle, we sing His song.  
O fountain of Jacob, flow from above,  
We dwell in safety, surrounded by love.

Outro  
So let us gather, our hearts entwined,  
In holy union, His truth defined.  
With every mountain, every sea,  
We stand as one, forever free.  
O Israel, rejoice and sing,  
For the God of blessings is our King.
