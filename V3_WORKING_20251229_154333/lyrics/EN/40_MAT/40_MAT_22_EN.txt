Verse 1  
In the quiet of the evening, the King prepares a feast,  
He sends His word to all the hearts, to come and find their peace.  
Yet many turn away, their eyes on fields and trade,  
But still He calls with mercy, His love will not be swayed.  

Chorus  
Come to the marriage, all are invited,  
The table is set, His grace ignited.  
Come with your burdens, lay them down,  
In the arms of the Father, love will abound.  

Verse 2  
He calls from every corner, both good and bad alike,  
With open hands, He welcomes us, to share in His delight.  
But when the King approaches, He seeks the wedding dress,  
With hearts adorned in righteousness, we find our true blessedness.

Bridge  
For many are called, yet few will choose,  
To heed the call of love, and not refuse.  
With every step of faith, we find our place,  
In the presence of the King, we’re wrapped in grace.

Outro  
So let us gather round Him, in unity we stand,  
With hearts that seek His kingdom, forever hand in hand.  
For in the joy of Jesus, our lives He will renew,  
In the wedding of the ages, we find our love so true.
