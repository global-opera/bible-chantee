[TITLE]
Faithful and True

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the latter days, we hear Your call,  
Spirit speaking to us, guiding through it all.  
Though some may wander, led by deceit,  
We stand in Your truth, our hearts at Your feet.  

Chorus  
For every creature is good,  
Sanctified by Your word,  
With thanksgiving, we lift our praise,  
In You, Lord, we are secured.  
We trust in the living God,  
Our Savior, true and kind,  
In faith, we rise, united strong,  
With love and hope aligned.  

Verse 2  
Let no one despise the youth we bear,  
In word and in spirit, we show we care.  
With godliness our aim, we seek Your face,  
In every moment, we find Your grace.
