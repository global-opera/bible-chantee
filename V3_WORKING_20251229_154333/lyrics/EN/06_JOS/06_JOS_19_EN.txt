[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Inheritance of Grace

[Verse 1]  
In the land of promise, where our fathers stood,  
Simeon, Zebulun, with faith they understood.  
From Beersheba’s valleys to the shores of the sea,  
You guide our inheritance, O Lord, faithfully.  

[Chorus]  
We stand in awe, Lord, of Your mighty hand,  
You carve our destinies in this holy land.  
From cities of refuge to mountains so high,  
In every inheritance, we lift our hearts, we cry.  

[Verse 2]  
Issachar, Asher, in Your presence we dwell,  
In the fields of harvest, where Your blessings swell.  
Through valleys of struggle, through battles we fight,  
Your promise our anchor, Your love our true light.  

[Chorus]  
We stand in awe, Lord, of Your mighty hand,  
You carve our destinies in this holy land.  
From cities of refuge to mountains so high,  
In every inheritance, we lift our hearts, we cry.  

[Bridge]  
With Joshua beside us, we claim every ground,  
Your word is our power, in You we are found.  
Through trials and triumphs, we trust in Your ways,  
Forever we’ll worship, through all of our days.  

[Verse 3]  
Dan to the battle, in faith they will rise,  
With courage and hope, they’ll claim their prize.  
O Lord, You have given, O Lord, we receive,  
In every moment, in You we believe.  

[Chorus]  
We stand in awe, Lord, of Your mighty hand,  
You carve our destinies in this holy land.  
From cities of refuge to mountains so high,  
In every inheritance, we lift our hearts, we cry.  

[Outro]  
With every inheritance, You’ve shown us the way,  
In faith, we will follow, and trust You each day.  
Your grace is our portion, Your love is our song,  
Together in worship, we’ll carry You along.
