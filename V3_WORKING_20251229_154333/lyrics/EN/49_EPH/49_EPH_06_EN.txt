[TITLE]
Stand in His Strength

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Children, hear the call, obey your parents' voice,  
Honor them in love, in the Lord rejoice.  
For this is right and true, a promise from above,  
That you may live in peace, surrounded by His love.  

Chorus  
So stand in His strength, put on His grace,  
With hearts united, we seek His face.  
In every struggle, we’re never alone,  
With the armor of God, we find our home.  

Verse 2  
Fathers, guide with care, raise your children right,  
Nurture them in truth, shine the Savior's light.  
Servants, work with heart, as unto the Lord,  
In every task you do, let His love be your sword.  

Chorus  
So stand in His strength, put on His grace,

Bridge  
With truth around our waist, righteousness our shield,  
Our feet are shod with peace, in Him we’re healed.  
Faith will quench the fire, His Word, our guiding light,  
In prayer, we find our strength, through the darkest night.

Outro  
Peace and love from Christ, our hearts forever free,  
Grace upon us all, as we walk in unity.  
Stand firm in His might, let His Spirit lead,  
In the strength of the Lord, we are all we need.
