[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the altar, a voice cried out so bold,  
The man of God from Judah, with a message to behold.  
“Jeroboam, hear the warning, for a child shall be born,  
Josiah, king of promise, to bring back the scorn.”  

Chorus  
Oh, the word of the Lord, a beacon in the night,  
Guiding the faithful, with truth and with light.  
In the midst of our battles, His voice we adore,  
We stand on His promise, we trust and we soar.  

Verse 2  
The altar split asunder, the ashes poured away,  
A sign of God’s judgment, a call for hearts to sway.  
But the king’s heart grew hardened, he sought to take command,  
Yet the hand that reached in anger, was dried by God’s own hand.

Bridge  
In the stillness of the moment, temptation drew him near,  
A prophet’s lie deceiving, he lost what was so dear.  
Disobedience brought sorrow, the lion on his way,  
A fate for those who wander from the path that God will lay.  

Verse 3  
Yet in the dust of mourning, the old prophet did not weep,  
He honored what was written, the promise he would keep.  
“Bury me beside him, for the word of truth shall stand,  
The altar shall be broken, and the high places, unplanned.”

Outro  
So let us heed the calling, let faith our hearts inspire,  
For the word of the Lord is a flame, a holy fire.  
In every trial and triumph, His voice will ever guide,  
We rise upon His promise, in His love we abide.
