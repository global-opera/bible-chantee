Verse 1  
Out of the dust, You called my name,  
Lifted me high, no longer the same.  
You showed me mercy, grace unconfined,  
Yet I wandered far, leaving You behind.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, my heart is aching,  
For the way we’ve turned from You.  
In shadows, we’ve been breaking,  
But Your light will lead us through.  
Turn our hearts back to Your ways,  
In Your arms, Lord, we will stay.  

Verse 2  
Kings have risen, kings have fallen,  
In the echoes of their pride,  
Baasha’s sins and Zimri’s calling,  
In their folly, they denied.  
Yet Your Word, it stands forever,  
Through the ages, true and bright,  
In the darkness, be our tether,  
Guide us with Your holy light.
