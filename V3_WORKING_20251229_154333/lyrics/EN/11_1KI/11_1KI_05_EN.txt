[TITLE]
A House for Your Name

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of peace, a king arose,  
Solomon, wise, with a heart to propose.  
To build a house for the Lord on high,  
Where His glory shines, and praises rise.  

Chorus  
O, bless the name of the Lord, we sing,  
For He’s given us rest, our hearts take wing.  
With every stone laid, and timber hewn,  
We build a house for Your name, O Lord, we’re renewed.  

Verse 2  
Hiram came forth, with a heart full of grace,  
To bring forth the cedars, in this sacred place.  
Together they toiled, as one in the fight,  
Building a temple, reflecting Your light.  

Chorus  
O, bless the name of the Lord, we sing,  
For He’s given us rest, our hearts take wing.  
With every stone laid, and timber hewn,  
We build a house for Your name, O Lord, we’re renewed.  

Bridge  
From Lebanon’s heights, the timber flows,  
In unity, love, the foundation grows.  
With wisdom bestowed, our hearts align,  
In the house of the Lord, Your presence divine.  

Chorus  
O, bless the name of the Lord, we sing,  
For He’s given us rest, our hearts take wing.  
With every stone laid, and timber hewn,  
We build a house for Your name, O Lord, we’re renewed.  

Outro  
So come, all the nations, let praises resound,  
In this holy dwelling, Your glory is found.  
With every heartbeat, our voices proclaim,  
We build a house for Your name, forever the same.
