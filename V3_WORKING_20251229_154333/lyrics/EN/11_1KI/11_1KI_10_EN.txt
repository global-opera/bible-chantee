[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where gold and spices flow,  
A queen journeyed far, her heart aglow.  
She sought the truth, the wisdom divine,  
To hear of the King, of glory so fine.  

Chorus  
Oh, blessed be the Lord our God,  
Who reigns in wisdom, who guides our hearts.  
From the heights of glory, He brings us peace,  
In the throne of Solomon, our fears release.  

Verse 2  
She stood in awe, her spirit laid bare,  
For all she had heard, now seen in the air.  
The splendor of his court, the wisdom profound,  
In the presence of the King, her heart was unbound.  

Chorus  
Oh, blessed be the Lord our God,  
Who reigns in wisdom, who guides our hearts.  
From the heights of glory, He brings us peace,  
In the throne of Solomon, our fears release.  

Bridge  
Happy are the servants, who dwell in His light,  
Listening to His counsel, morning till night.  
For Solomon shines with the grace of the Lord,  
In a kingdom of justice, His truth is our sword.  

Verse 3  
The gold and the treasures, the gifts that she bore,  
Poured out as a blessing, her heart wanted more.  
For wisdom exceeds what the world can bestow,  
In the arms of the Savior, His love overflows.  

Chorus  
Oh, blessed be the Lord our God,  
Who reigns in wisdom, who guides our hearts.  
From the heights of glory, He brings us peace,  
In the throne of Solomon, our fears release.  

Outro  
So let us come forth, with hearts open wide,  
To seek out His wisdom, our faithful guide.  
In the echoes of ages, His love will resound,  
In the throne of Solomon, true peace can be found.
