[TITLE]
A Legacy of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the heart of the kingdom, a tale unfolds,  
Of courage and honor, of faith that upholds.  
Mordecai stood, a beacon of light,  
In perilous times, he chose what was right.  

Pre-Chorus  
With wisdom he sought, the peace of his kin,  
In a world full of chaos, he let hope begin.  
For the king raised his name, in the annals of grace,  
A story of triumph, a love we embrace.  

Chorus  
Oh, let us rise, in the shadow of His might,  
With hearts intertwined, we will shine through the night.  
For the legacy lives, in the lives that we touch,  
In the wealth of His people, we find strength and such.  

Verse 2  
A tribute was laid on the land and the seas,  
Yet Mordecai’s wisdom brought forth hearts at ease.  
Next to the throne, but humble in heart,  
He sought the well-being, played his faithful part.  

Pre-Chorus  
With courage he spoke, for the sake of his kin,  
In the face of the storm, he let love begin.  
For the king raised his name, in the annals of grace,  
A story of triumph, a love we embrace.  

Chorus  
Oh, let us rise, in the shadow of His might,  
With hearts intertwined, we will shine through the night.  
For the legacy lives, in the lives that we touch,  
In the wealth of His people, we find strength and such.  

Bridge  
When the darkness surrounds, and the fear starts to creep,  
We’ll gather together, in the promises we keep.  
With Mordecai’s spirit, let us stand firm and bold,  
For the stories of faith, are the treasures we hold.  

Chorus  
Oh, let us rise, in the shadow of His might,  
With hearts intertwined, we will shine through the night.  
For the legacy lives, in the lives that we touch,  
In the wealth of His people, we find strength and such.  

Outro  
In the chronicles written, our voices will sing,  
Of the grace that unites us, and the hope that He brings.  
For Mordecai’s courage, in our hearts shall remain,  
A legacy of faith, forever proclaim.
