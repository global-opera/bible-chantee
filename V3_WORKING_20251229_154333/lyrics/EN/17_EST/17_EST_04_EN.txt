[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the city, cries arise,  
Mordecai's heart breaks, his spirit sighs.  
Clothed in ashes, he pleads for his kin,  
A voice of anguish, where do we begin?  

Pre-Chorus:  
Every heart is heavy, the decree brings despair,  
But a whisper of hope flows through the air.  
Esther, your courage, we call on you,  
For such a time as this, a purpose so true.  

Chorus:  
Rise up, O Queen, take your stand,  
For your people, extend your hand.  
In the shadows, courage ignites,  
With faith as your armor, step into the light.  

Verse 2:  
Haman's decree looms dark in the night,  
But in prayer and fasting, we seek the light.  
Gather together, we’ll cry out in one voice,  
In the face of the battle, our hearts will rejoice.  

Pre-Chorus:  
Mordecai calls, “Do not be afraid,  
For deliverance comes, though darkness invade.”  
Esther, remember, your call is divine,  
For such a time as this, our lives intertwine.  

Chorus:  
Rise up, O Queen, take your stand,  
For your people, extend your hand.  
In the shadows, courage ignites,  
With faith as your armor, step into the light.  

Bridge:  
If I perish, I perish, still I will go,  
In the face of the peril, Your presence I’ll know.  
With hearts united, we’ll fast and we’ll pray,  
For the hope of the nations, Your will leads the way.  

Chorus:  
Rise up, O Queen, take your stand,  
For your people, extend your hand.  
In the shadows, courage ignites,

Outro:  
For such a time as this, we gather in grace,  
Trusting in Your promise, we seek Your face.  
In moments of trial, when darkness seems near,  
We’ll rise as one body, in faith, we draw near.
