[TITLE]
In the House of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I was glad when they called my name,  
To gather in Your holy place,  
Where our hearts unite in praise,  
In the house of the Lord, we seek Your face.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Jerusalem, city of peace,  
Within Your walls, our hearts find ease,  
We lift our voices, give thanks to You,  
In the house of the Lord, our strength renew.  

Verse 2:  
Compacted together, we rise as one,  
The tribes of the Lord, our journey begun,  
To testify to Your endless grace,  
In the house of the Lord, we find our place.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Jerusalem, city of peace,  
Within Your walls, our hearts find ease,  
We lift our voices, give thanks to You,  
In the house of the Lord, our strength renew.  

Bridge:  
Pray for the peace, let love abound,  
In the heart of Your people, may hope be found,  
For my brethren’s sake, I will proclaim,  
Peace be within, in Jesus' name.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Jerusalem, city of peace,  
Within Your walls, our hearts find ease,  
We lift our voices, give thanks to You,  
In the house of the Lord, our strength renew.  

Outro:  
Because of the house of our God above,  
We seek Your good, we seek Your love,  
In the house of the Lord, forever we'll stay,  
In Your presence, Lord, we long to stay.
