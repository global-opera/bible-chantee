[TITLE]
From Zion, Our Salvation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of my heart, I whisper low,  
The fool says there is no God, yet I know,  
Corruption fills the earth, and darkness reigns,  
But Your eyes see all, through joy and pain.  

Chorus:  
Oh, from Zion, our salvation,  
In Your presence, we find grace,  
You look down upon creation,  
And gather us in Your embrace.  
Though we wander, lost and blind,  
In Your mercy, hope we find.  

Verse 2:  
You searched the hearts of men, but none are true,  
Each one turned away, oh, how we need You.  
The workers of iniquity feast on strife,  
Yet still, You call us to the light of life.  

Chorus:  
Oh, from Zion, our salvation,  
In Your presence, we find grace,  
You look down upon creation,  
And gather us in Your embrace.  
Though we wander, lost and blind,  
In Your mercy, hope we find.  

Bridge:  
When fear surrounds us, You are near,  
You scatter all our foes, we need not fear.  
Bring back our hearts, Lord, from the night,  
In the shadows, be our guiding light.  

Chorus:  
Oh, from Zion, our salvation,

You look down upon creation,  
And gather us in Your embrace.

Outro:  
Oh, that salvation comes, let joy arise,  
Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel sighs.  
In Your arms, we find our rest,  
Forever in Your love, we are blessed.
