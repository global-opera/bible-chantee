[TITLE]
In You, O Lord, I Trust

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord my God, I come to You,  
In the shadows where fears pursue,  
Save me from those who seek my fall,  
Be my refuge, my all in all.  

Chorus  
In You, O Lord, I trust my heart,  
Deliver me, though I feel apart.  
Your righteous hand, my guiding light,  
I’ll praise Your name, through darkest night.  

Verse 2  
If I have wronged, I lay it bare,  
Search my soul, Lord, hear my prayer.  
Let the wickedness find its end,  
In Your mercy, my soul defend.  

Chorus  
In You, O Lord, I trust my heart,  
Deliver me, though I feel apart.  
Your righteous hand, my guiding light,  
I’ll praise Your name, through darkest night.  

Bridge  
Arise, O Lord, in anger rise,  
Hear the cries of the broken sighs.  
Judge me, Lord, by Your decree,  
For in Your grace, I long to be.  

Verse 3  
You are my shield, my defense so true,  
In the trials, I’ll cling to You.  
For the wicked shall reap their own shame,  
While the righteous rejoice in Your name.  

Chorus  
In You, O Lord, I trust my heart,  
Deliver me, though I feel apart.  
Your righteous hand, my guiding light,  
I’ll praise Your name, through darkest night.  

Outro  
I will praise the Lord, Most High,  
With every breath, my spirit will fly.  
In Your righteousness, I find my song,  
Forever, Lord, to You I belong.
