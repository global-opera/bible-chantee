[TITLE]
In You I Trust

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O Lord, be merciful, hear my cry,  
For the weight of the world, it makes me sigh.  
Enemies gather, their hearts full of strife,  
Yet in You, O God, I find true life.  
When fear surrounds me, I will not shake,  
For in Your embrace, my spirit awake.

Chorus:  
In God, I will trust, I will not fear,  
What flesh can do, my heart draws near.  
Your vows upon me, I’ll lift my praise,  
In the light of Your love, my soul will stay.

Verse 2:  
They wrest my words, their thoughts filled with hate,  
But You know my wanderings, You hold my fate.  
Each tear that I cry, You keep in Your book,  
In moments of darkness, my heart You’ll soothe.  
When I call on Your name, my foes will retreat,  
For I am Your child, and in You, I’m complete.

Chorus:  
In God, I will trust, I will not fear,  
What flesh can do, my heart draws near.  
Your vows upon me, I’ll lift my praise,  
In the light of Your love, my soul will stay.

Bridge:  
O God, deliver my feet from the fall,  
I’ll walk in Your light, I’ll answer Your call.  
I praise Your word, for You are my song,  
In every shadow, I know I belong.

Chorus:  
In God, I will trust, I will not fear,  
What flesh can do, my heart draws near.  
Your vows upon me, I’ll lift my praise,  
In the light of Your love, my soul will stay.

Outro:  
I will trust in You, my Savior and King,  
In the depths of my heart, Your praises I’ll sing.  
For You’ve delivered my soul from the night,  
I walk before You, in the light of the living, so bright.
