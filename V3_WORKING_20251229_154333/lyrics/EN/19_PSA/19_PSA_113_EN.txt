[TITLE]
From Dawn to Dusk, We Praise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Praise the Lord, O servants, lift Your voice,  
In every heart, let His name rejoice.  
From dawn's first light to evening's grace,  
Blessed be His name, our hiding place.  

Chorus:  
Praise the Lord, His glory reigns,  
Above the heavens, through joyful strains.  
Forevermore, we lift Him high,  
From the rising sun to the evening sky.  

Verse 2:  
Who is like our God, so high above,  
Yet humbles Himself, our hearts to love?  
He lifts the poor from dust and shame,  
And calls them forth to share His name.  

Chorus:  
Praise the Lord, His glory reigns,  
Above the heavens, through joyful strains.  
Forevermore, we lift Him high,  
From the rising sun to the evening sky.  

Bridge:  
He makes the barren sing with joy,  
A mother’s heart, no grief to destroy.  
With princes, He seats the lowly ones,  
In His embrace, our hope has begun.  

Chorus:  
Praise the Lord, His glory reigns,  
Above the heavens, through joyful strains.  
Forevermore, we lift Him high,  
From the rising sun to the evening sky.  

Outro:  
Praise ye the Lord, let your hearts declare,  
His love endures, forever we share.  
From this time forth, we'll sing His praise,  
Our faithful God, we bless Your ways.
