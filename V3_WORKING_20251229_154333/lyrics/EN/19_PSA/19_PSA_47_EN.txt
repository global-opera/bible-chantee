Verse 1  
O clap your hands, all people near,  
Lift up your voice, let praises cheer.  
The Lord Most High, our King of grace,  
He reigns in power, we seek His face.  

Chorus  
Sing praises to our God, sing loud,  
With hearts united, we stand proud.  
For He is King, forever strong,  
In Him our hearts, they all belong.
