[TITLE]
In Your Presence, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In Judah, Lord, Your name is known,  
In Salem, where Your love has shone.  
Your tabernacle, holy ground,  
In Zion's heart, Your peace is found.  

Chorus  
O God of Jacob, we stand in awe,  
Your glory shines, we hear Your call.  
The mountains tremble, the earth will yield,  
In Your presence, our hearts are healed.  

Verse 2  
You break the arrows, still the fight,  
In darkest valleys, You are our light.  
The stouthearted are laid to rest,  
In Your embrace, we find our best.  

Chorus  
O God of Jacob, we stand in awe,  
Your glory shines, we hear Your call.  
The mountains tremble, the earth will yield,  
In Your presence, our hearts are healed.  

Bridge  
Who can stand before Your might?  
When You speak, the world takes flight.  
Your judgment comes, yet mercy flows,  
You save the meek, our hearts You know.  

Chorus  
O God of Jacob, we stand in awe,  
Your glory shines, we hear Your call.  
The mountains tremble, the earth will yield,  
In Your presence, our hearts are healed.  

Outro  
Vow to You, O Lord, we bring,  
With every breath, our praise we sing.  
You reign above, the kings must bow,  
In Your presence, we worship now.
