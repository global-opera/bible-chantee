[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
O give thanks to the Lord, for He is good,  
In every moment, His grace understood,  
From the dawn of creation, through the night skies,  
His mercy endures, our hearts testify.  

[Chorus]  
For His mercy endures, forever and ever,  
In the valleys and hills, His love will not sever.  
With a heart full of praise, we lift up His name,  
Forever His mercy, forever the same.  

[Verse 2]  
He who made the heavens, with wisdom so deep,  
Stretched out the earth, His promises we keep.  
The sun for the day, the moon and the stars,  
His mercy surrounds us, wherever we are.  

[Chorus]  
For His mercy endures, forever and ever,  
In the valleys and hills, His love will not sever.  
With a heart full of praise, we lift up His name,  
Forever His mercy, forever the same.  

[Bridge]  
He brought us from darkness, redeemed us in strife,  
Led us through the wilderness, giving us life.  
He remembers our low estate, hears every cry,  
In the shadow of His wings, we know He is nigh.  

[Verse 3]  
To Him who divided the sea with His might,  
Made a way for His people, brought them to light.  
He smote the great kings, with a powerful hand,  
His mercy endures, in this promised land.  

[Chorus]  
For His mercy endures, forever and ever,  
In the valleys and hills, His love will not sever.  
With a heart full of praise, we lift up His name,  
Forever His mercy, forever the same.  

[Outro]  
O give thanks to the God of heaven above,  
His mercy surrounds us, His infinite love.  
With hearts open wide, we sing of His grace,  
Forever His mercy, our refuge, our place.
