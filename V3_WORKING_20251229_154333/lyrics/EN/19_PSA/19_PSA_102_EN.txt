[TITLE]
Hear My Cry, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In my sorrow, Lord, I call,  
Hear my prayer, don’t let me fall.  
When the day is dark and long,  
Lift my heart, make me strong.  
Like the pelican, alone I stand,  
In the wilderness, I seek Your hand.  
My bones are weary, my spirit frail,  
But in Your mercy, I will not fail.  

Chorus:  
Hear my cry, O Lord, I plead,  
In this moment, meet my need.  
You endure forever, my hope, my song,  
In Your presence, I belong.  
Though the shadows fade away,  
Your light, O Lord, will guide my way.  

Verse 2:  
Ashes like bread, my tears I share,  
In Your anger, Lord, hear my despair.  
But I know You rise to show Your grace,  
In the time of favor, I seek Your face.  
Though my days like shadows wane,  
Your love, O Lord, will still remain.  
From the height of heaven, You look down,  
To the broken-hearted, You wear the crown.  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
You will build up Zion’s walls,  
In Your glory, every heart recalls.  
The captive’s chains will break apart,  
As we gather, Lord, with humble heart.  
For Your name, we will proclaim,  
In every nation, we lift Your fame.

Outro:  
You are the same, the years will end,  
But in Your love, we find our friend.  
Let the children rise and sing,  
In Your promise, our hearts take wing.
