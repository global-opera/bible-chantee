[TITLE]
Let All Creation Sing

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Praise the Lord from the heavens,  
In the heights, let praises rise.  
All His angels, join in chorus,  
Echo truth beyond the skies.  
Sun and moon, lift up Your voices,  
Stars of light, in harmony.  
He commands, and all creation,  
Sings His glory endlessly.

Chorus:  
Let them praise the name of the Lord,  
For His word has made the heavens soar.  
From the depths to the heights above,  
We declare His everlasting love.  
Praise the Lord, let every heart,  
Join the song, let worship start.  
For His name alone is excellent,  
In His presence, we are content.

Verse 2:  
Praise Him, ye mountains and valleys,  
Fruitful trees and cedars tall.  
Fire and hail, the stormy winds,  
Fulfill His word, they heed His call.  
All creation, young and old,  
Kings and nations, great and small,  
Let us gather, hearts united,  
In His name, we stand in awe.

Chorus:  
Let them praise the name of the Lord,

Bridge:  
He exalts the horn of His people,  
His glory shines through every land.  
Children of Israel, near to Him,  
In His arms, we firmly stand.  
With every breath, our hearts will sing,  
In His love, we find our everything.

Outro:  
Praise the Lord, let all creation sing,  
In His love, our hearts take wing.  
Forever, we will lift His name,  
Praise the Lord, forever the same.
