[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O sing unto the Lord a new song,  
Let all the earth lift voices strong.  
Bless His name, declare His grace,  
Show forth salvation, seek His face.  

Chorus  
For the Lord is great, greatly to be praised,  
In every heart, His love is raised.  
He reigns in glory, all the earth will see,  
In beauty and in holiness, we worship Thee.  

Verse 2  
Declare His wonders among the nations,  
His strength, His beauty, our jubilation.  
Honor Him, O kindreds, sing,  
Bring an offering, let our praises ring.  

Chorus  
For the Lord is great, greatly to be praised,  
In every heart, His love is raised.  
He reigns in glory, all the earth will see,  
In beauty and in holiness, we worship Thee.  

Bridge  
Let the heavens rejoice, let the earth be glad,  
Roar of the sea, all creation rad.  
Fields and trees in harmony, lift our song,  
For the Lord is coming, to Him we belong.  

Chorus  
For the Lord is great, greatly to be praised,  
In every heart, His love is raised.  
He reigns in glory, all the earth will see,

Outro  
O worship the Lord, in His holy light,  
With trembling hearts, we seek His might.  
He shall judge with truth, all people will see,  
In righteousness, He reigns eternally.
