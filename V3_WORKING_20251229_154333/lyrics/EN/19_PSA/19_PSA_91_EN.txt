[TITLE]
In the Secret Place

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the secret place of the Most High,  
I find my refuge, my fortress nigh.  
Under Your shadow, I rest and abide,  
In You, my Savior, my heart will confide.  

Chorus:  
You are my shield, my stronghold true,  
In every trial, I’ll trust in You.  
No fear of the night, nor arrows that fly,  
With You beside me, I will not cry.  

Verse 2:  
You deliver me from every snare,  
In the darkest hour, You are always there.  
With wings of protection, You cover my soul,  
Your truth is my armor, You make me whole.  

Chorus:  
You are my shield, my stronghold true,  
In every trial, I’ll trust in You.  
No fear of the night, nor arrows that fly,  
With You beside me, I will not cry.  

Bridge:  
A thousand may fall, but I will stand,  
Your angels surround me, they hold my hand.  
I call on Your name, and You answer me,  
In trouble, You’re with me, I’m set free.  

Chorus:  
You are my shield, my stronghold true,  
In every trial, I’ll trust in You.  
No fear of the night, nor arrows that fly,

Outro:  
With long life, You satisfy my soul,  
In Your salvation, I’m made whole.  
In the secret place of the Most High,  
Forever and always, Lord, You are nigh.
