[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
Give ear to my heart, O Lord, I pray,  
Consider my thoughts, as I seek Your way.  
Hearken to my cry, my King, my God,  
In the morning light, I lift my voice aloud.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, I will stand,  
With a heart so full, I raise my hands.  
In Your presence, I find my peace,  
Joyful in You, my soul’s release.  

Verse 2:  
For You do not delight in wickedness,  
Evil cannot dwell where You manifest.  
The foolish cannot stand before Your grace,  
Yet I come to worship, in this holy place.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, I will stand,  
With a heart so full, I raise my hands.  
In Your presence, I find my peace,  
Joyful in You, my soul’s release.  

Bridge:  
Lead me, O Lord, in paths of light,  
Make Your way straight, and guide my sight.  
Though the world may rage, and darkness fall,  
I’ll trust in You, my shelter, my all.  

Chorus:  
In Your mercy, I will stand,  
With a heart so full, I raise my hands.  
In Your presence, I find my peace,  
Joyful in You, my soul’s release.  

Outro:  
Let all who trust in You rejoice,  
In Your love, we lift our voice.  
For You bless the righteous with favor true,  
O Lord, our hearts are safe in You.
