[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
O praise the Lord, all ye nations,  
Lift high your voice, let joy arise.  
His mercies flow like boundless oceans,  
In Him we find our hope and prize.  

(Chorus)  
For His kindness is great toward us,  
His truth endures forevermore.  
With grateful hearts, we sing and trust,  
O praise the Lord, whom we adore.  

(Verse 2)  
In every trial, in every sorrow,  
His light will guide, we will not fear.  
His promises shine bright tomorrow,  
In every heart, He draws us near.  

(Chorus)  
For His kindness is great toward us,  
His truth endures forevermore.  
With grateful hearts, we sing and trust,  
O praise the Lord, whom we adore.  

(Bridge)  
All ye people, come and gather,  
Join in the song, let praises rise.  
For His mercy is our anchor,  
In His embrace, our spirits fly.  

(Chorus)  
For His kindness is great toward us,  
His truth endures forevermore.  
With grateful hearts, we sing and trust,  
O praise the Lord, whom we adore.  

(Outro)  
O praise the Lord, all ye nations,  
Praise Him, all ye people, too.  
His merciful kindness never wanes,  
In every breath, we worship You.
