[TITLE]
My Hope is in You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I’ll guard my heart and heed my ways,  
To speak no words that lead astray,  
In silence, Lord, my sorrow grows,  
Your truth ignites, my spirit glows.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, what wait I for?  
My hope is found in You,  
Deliver me, my heart implores,  
In all my trials, make me new.  

Verse 2:  
You hold my days within Your hands,  
A fleeting breath, like shifting sands,  
Each moment fades, yet I am Yours,  
In every doubt, Your love restores.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, what wait I for?  
My hope is found in You,  
Deliver me, my heart implores,  
In all my trials, make me new.  

Bridge:  
Hear my cry, O Lord, I plead,  
You know my heart, You know my need,  
As I wander, be my guide,  
In Your presence, I abide.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, what wait I for?  
My hope is found in You,  
Deliver me, my heart implores,  
In all my trials, make me new.  

Outro:  
O spare me, Lord, I long for grace,  
Before I leave this earthly place,  
Restore my strength, renew my soul,  
In Your embrace, I am made whole.
