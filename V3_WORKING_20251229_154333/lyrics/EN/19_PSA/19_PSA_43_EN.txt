[TITLE]
Psalm 43 - Light of My Joy
[LYRICS]
[Verse 1]
Vindicate me, O God, I implore,
Against the faithless, my voice rises once more.
Deliver me from the hands of deceit,
You, my protector, come rescue me, I entreat!

[Chorus]
Send forth Your light, shine through the night,
Your faithfulness, O Lord, guides me right.
To Your holy mountain, I long to ascend,
In You, my joy, I find peace without end.

[Verse 2]
Why have You rejected me in my pain?
Under oppression, I seek Your sweet refrain.
In my sorrow, I lift my eyes above,
To You, O my God, I find Your endless love.

[Chorus]
Send forth Your light, shine through the night,
Your faithfulness, O Lord, guides me right.
To Your holy mountain, I long to ascend,
In You, my joy, I find peace without end.

[Bridge]
Why so downcast, O my soul in distress?
Hope in God, find in Him your happiness.
I will praise Him, for He is my salvation,
In every trial, His grace is my foundation.

[Chorus]
Send forth Your light, shine through the night,
Your faithfulness, O Lord, guides me right.
To Your holy mountain, I long to ascend,
In You, my joy, I find peace without end.
