[TITLE]
Let God Arise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Let God arise, let enemies flee,  
As smoke is driven away, set the captives free.  
In Your presence, wickedness shall fall,  
But the righteous lift their voices, answering Your call.  

Chorus  
Sing unto God, lift praises high,  
Extol His name, the Lord who reigns on high.  
Father of the fatherless, judge of the weak,  
In Your holy habitation, it’s Your grace we seek.  

Verse 2  
You march before us, O God of might,  
The earth shakes, the heavens shine with light.  
You send the rain upon weary land,  
Confirming Your promise, with a gentle hand.  

Chorus  
Sing unto God, lift praises high,  
Extol His name, the Lord who reigns on high.  
Father of the fatherless, judge of the weak,  
In Your holy habitation, it’s Your grace we seek.  

Bridge  
Kings may flee, but we stand in grace,  
Your strength and power fill this sacred space.  
You lead us on, from depths to the skies,  
Blessed be the Lord, our salvation, arise!  

Chorus  
Sing unto God, lift praises high,  
Extol His name, the Lord who reigns on high.  
Father of the fatherless, judge of the weak,  
In Your holy habitation, it’s Your grace we seek.  

Outro  
Let God arise, let His glory shine,  
In every heart, let His love entwine.  
Blessed be God, forever we’ll sing,  
In Your presence, we find everything.
