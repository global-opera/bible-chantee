Verse 1:  
Praise the Lord, with all my heart,  
In His presence, I take my part.  
Blessed is the one who fears His name,  
Delights in His Word, forever the same.  
His children rise, in strength they stand,  
The upright blessed in this holy land.  

Chorus:  
Oh, how great is our God,  
His light shines in the dark.  
Compassion flows like a river wide,  
In His love, we find our guide.  
We will trust, we will stand,  
With hearts established in His hand.  

Verse 2:  
Wealth and riches fill his home,  
Righteousness shall forever roam.  
In the midst of trials, he will not fear,  
For his heart is anchored, the Lord is near.  
He shows His grace, he lends his hand,  
Guiding with wisdom, in faith he stands.
