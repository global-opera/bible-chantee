Verse 1:  
O sing unto the Lord, a new song arise,  
For He has done marvellous things, we lift our eyes.  
His right hand brought us victory, His arm is strong,  
In every heart, His salvation we will prolong.  

Chorus:  
Make a joyful noise, all the earth, proclaim,  
Rejoice and sing His praise, lift up His name.  
With harps and voices, let our praises swell,  
For His mercy and truth, in our hearts we dwell.
