[TITLE]
In Your Refuge, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear my voice, O God, I call to You,  
Preserve my heart, my soul, so true.  
From the shadows where the wicked hide,  
I seek Your light; in You, I abide.  

Chorus  
In Your refuge, I will find my rest,  
You are my shelter, my soul’s true quest.  
When the arrows fly, and the darkness grows,  
I trust in You, Lord, for You know.  

Verse 2  
They whisper lies, and their tongues are sharp,  
But I will cling to the hope in my heart.  
Though they lay their snares in secret,  
Your truth, O Lord, will never forget.  

Chorus  
In Your refuge, I will find my rest,  
You are my shelter, my soul’s true quest.  
When the arrows fly, and the darkness grows,  
I trust in You, Lord, for You know.  

Bridge  
You shall shoot Your arrows, swift and true,  
And every foe will know it’s You.  
With hearts uplifted, we’ll declare Your name,  
The righteous rejoice in the glory of Your fame.  

Chorus  
In Your refuge, I will find my rest,  
You are my shelter, my soul’s true quest.  
When the arrows fly, and the darkness grows,  
I trust in You, Lord, for You know.  

Outro  
So let my heart be glad in You,  
With every breath, in all I do.  
I’ll trust in You, Lord, my hope, my song,  
In Your embrace, forever strong.
