[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In Your presence, Lord, we bow,  
Subject to Your love, we vow.  
Gentle hearts, we speak Your name,  
No more strife, no more blame.  
Once we wandered, lost and blind,  
Deceived by lust, our hearts entwined.  
Yet Your mercy, like the dawn,  
Brought redemption, we've been drawn.  

Chorus  
Oh, the kindness of our Savior,  
Washing us in grace anew.  
Through the Holy Spirit’s favor,  
We rise in faith, our hope in You.  
Justified, we stand as heirs,  
Eternal life, Your love declares.  
In our hearts, let good works flow,  
As we walk in love, we grow.  

Verse 2  
Teach us, Lord, to heed Your call,  
To honor those who lead us all.  
In meekness, let us find our way,  
Speaking life, not words of clay.  
Foolish strife, we’ll cast aside,  
In Your truth, we will abide.  
With each step, may we be light,  
Shining bright through darkest night.
