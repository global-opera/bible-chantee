[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence, Lord, I hear,  
Your instruction draws me near,  
A wise son heeds his father's call,  
In Your truth, I stand, I shall not fall.  
The fruit of my lips, may it bring forth grace,  
In the shadows, let me seek Your face.

Chorus  
Oh, guide my heart, Lord, let me see,  
The light of righteousness, shining free.  
In the way of the wise, let my spirit dwell,  
In the echo of Your love, may all be well.

Verse 2  
The diligent hand shall reap the reward,  
Yet the sluggard's heart is never restored.  
Help me labor with faith each day,  
For in Your goodness, I find my way.  
To shun all lies and walk in truth,  
Bringing forth wisdom as a fountain of youth.

Chorus  
Oh, guide my heart, Lord, let me see,  
The light of righteousness, shining free.  
In the way of the wise, let my spirit dwell,  
In the echo of Your love, may all be well.

Bridge  
For pride brings strife, but humility wins,  
In the counsel of the wise, my heart begins.  
To seek Your kingdom, to walk in grace,  
In Your loving arms, I find my place.

Verse 3  
Hope deferred makes the heart grow weak,  
Yet, in Your promise, my soul shall speak.  
Accomplished desires, sweet to behold,  
In the path of righteousness, let my story unfold.  
A good man’s legacy, children’s delight,  
In the strength of Your Word, I find my light.

Outro
