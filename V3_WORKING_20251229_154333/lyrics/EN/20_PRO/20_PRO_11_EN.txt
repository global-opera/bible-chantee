[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In Your eyes, O Lord, we find our way,  
A balance just, where truth will stay.  
Pride leads to shame, but the humble see,  
In the quiet heart, wisdom's decree.  

Chorus  
Righteousness, our guiding light,  
Deliver us from darkest night.  
In Your mercy, our hope is found,  
Through the trials, we stand on solid ground.  

Verse 2  
The wicked fall, their plans undone,  
But the righteous shine like the morning sun.  
Integrity leads, the path is clear,  
In the strength of love, we cast out fear.  

Chorus  
Righteousness, our guiding light,  
Deliver us from darkest night.  
In Your mercy, our hope is found,  
Through the trials, we stand on solid ground.  

Bridge  
Scatter not in selfish gain,  
For the generous heart shall break the chain.  
Water the souls, and you shall see,  
The blessings flow abundantly.  

Verse 3  
The fruit of life from righteous trees,  
In Your hands, we find our peace.  
With every step, we seek Your face,  
In the midst of storms, we find our place.  

Chorus  
Righteousness, our guiding light,  
Deliver us from darkest night.  
In Your mercy, our hope is found,  
Through the trials, we stand on solid ground.  

Outro  
O Lord, may we walk in Your ways,  
In love and truth, through all our days.  
For the seed of the righteous shall be delivered,  
In Your grace, our hearts are forever tethered.
