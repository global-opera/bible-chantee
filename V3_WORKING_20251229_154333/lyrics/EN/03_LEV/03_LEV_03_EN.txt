Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, I come before You,  
With a heart that's laid bare, and my soul so true.  
I bring my sacrifice, a peace offering,  
Without blemish, pure, I lift my voice to sing.  

Chorus  
O Lord, I lay my hands upon the Lamb,  
In this sacred moment, I take my stand.  
Sprinkle the blood, let it cover my sin,  
In Your loving arms, may my journey begin.  

Verse 2

With every heartbeat, my praise I proclaim,  
In the presence of the altar, I lift Your name.
