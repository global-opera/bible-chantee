[TITLE]
Sanctified by Your Hand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows where the lost abide,  
You spoke to Moses, Your truth to guide.  
"Sanctify My people, keep them near,  
For I am holy, let there be no fear."  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, set us apart,  
With Your love, transform our hearts.  
In Your presence, we’ll abide,  
Holy One, our faithful guide.  

Verse 2  
The blood cries out, the price of sin,  
In the depths of darkness, let the light begin.  
For every heart that turns away,  
You call us back, O Lord, today.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, set us apart,  
With Your love, transform our hearts.  
In Your presence, we’ll abide,  
Holy One, our faithful guide.  

Bridge  
Let us walk in Your statutes true,  
As we seek to honor You.  
In a land that flows with grace,  
We find our home, in Your embrace.  

Verse 3  
No more to wander, no more to stray,  
You’ve severed darkness, You’ve shown the way.  
With every step, we’ll choose to stand,  
In holiness, we trust Your hand.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, set us apart,  
With Your love, transform our hearts.  
In Your presence, we’ll abide,  
Holy One, our faithful guide.  

Outro  
Sanctified by Your hand,  
We are Yours, we understand.  
In Your mercy, we will rise,  
Holy Lord, our sacrifice.
