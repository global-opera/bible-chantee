[TITLE]  
A Year of Jubilee  

[Verse 1]  
On Sinai high, the Lord did speak,  
To Moses bold, His promise deep,  
"Bring forth the word, to Israel's heart,  
In the land I give, let rest impart.  
For six years toil, your fields shall yield,  
But on the seventh, let peace be revealed.  

[Chorus]  
A year of jubilee, our hearts set free,  
In the grace of God, we find our peace.  
Return, return to what is right,  
In love and mercy, His light ignites.  

[Verse 2]  
In every harvest, my blessing flows,  
For every soul, my kindness shows.  
The land shall rest, for it is mine,  
In this holy time, let love entwine.  
When brothers falter, and shadows fall,  
Lift them in kindness, hear the call.  

[Chorus]  
A year of jubilee, our hearts set free,  
In the grace of God, we find our peace.  
Return, return to what is right,  
In love and mercy, His light ignites.  

[Bridge]  
Fear not the years, for I am near,  
In every trial, I’ll calm your fear.  
Count the blessings, let the trumpet sound,  
Liberty proclaimed, all around.  
My servants, my people, I set you free,  
In this year of jubilee, rejoice with me!  

[Chorus]  
A year of jubilee, our hearts set free,  
In the grace of God, we find our peace.  
Return, return to what is right,  
In love and mercy, His light ignites.  

[Outro]  
As we gather near, in unity,  
We’ll sing of hope, our jubilee.  
For in His presence, the blind will see,  
In every heartbeat, we’re set free.
