[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the silence of the night, You called to Moses,  
A voice like thunder, echoing Your plan.  
"Speak to My people, let them know their worth,  
In every vow they make, they shall understand."  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we are Yours, Lord, set apart,  
In silver and in sacred heart.  
Every promise, every seed we sow,  
In the year of jubilee, Your love will flow.  

(Verse 2)  
From twenty years to sixty, our lives You measure,  
In every soul, there's beauty and grace.  
A value placed upon us, a treasure,  
In the fields of promise, we will find our place.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we are Yours, Lord, set apart,  
In silver and in sacred heart.  
Every promise, every seed we sow,  
In the year of jubilee, Your love will flow.  

(Bridge)  
And if we falter, if we stray,  
You are our refuge, You guide our way.  
In the count of shekels, in the grains of wheat,  
We find redemption in Your holy beat.  

(Verse 3)  
Every beast and every heart devoted,  
Shall stand before You, holy and true.  
For every tithe, every life anointed,  
We give our all, Lord, in worship to You.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we are Yours, Lord, set apart,  
In silver and in sacred heart.  
Every promise, every seed we sow,  
In the year of jubilee, Your love will flow.  

(Outro)  
These are the words, from Sinai's height,  
A covenant written, guiding our light.  
As we gather, let our voices rise,  
In holy vows, our hearts will harmonize.
