[TITLE]
Covenant of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
We gather here, O Lord, in reverence,  
To lift our hearts, to seek Your face.  
You called us forth, from chains of darkness,  
In Your embrace, we find our place.  

Chorus  
You are our God, we are Your people,  
In Your love, our fears will cease.  
You give us rain, You bring the harvest,  
In Your arms, we find our peace.  

Verse 2  
No idols stand before Your glory,  
We bow our hearts, in holy awe.  
Your statutes guide, Your Word, our story,  
In every promise, we hear Your call.  

Chorus  
You are our God, we are Your people,  
In Your love, our fears will cease.  
You give us rain, You bring the harvest,  
In Your arms, we find our peace.  

Bridge  
Though we may stray, Your grace sustains us,  
With humble hearts, we confess our sin.  
Remember us, O God of mercy,  
Restore our souls, let new life begin.  

Verse 3  
When shadows fall, and trials surround us,  
You walk beside, we will not fear.  
Your covenant stands, forever faithful,  
Your presence here, our hearts draw near.  

Chorus  
You are our God, we are Your people,  
In Your love, our fears will cease.  
You give us rain, You bring the harvest,  
In Your arms, we find our peace.  

Outro  
So let us dwell, in Your sanctuary,  
With grateful hearts, we lift Your name.  
You are our God, forever faithful,  
In Your great love, we find our claim.
