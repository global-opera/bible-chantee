[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Holy, Holy, Is the Lord

[Verse 1]  
In the stillness of Your voice,  
You called us forth to be,  
A holy people set apart,  
Reflecting Your purity.  
You said, "Fear your mother, father,  
And keep My Sabbath day,"  
For in Your love and guidance,  
We find our righteous way.

[Chorus]  
Holy, holy, is the Lord,  
In every heart, Your truth restored.  
We seek Your face, we lift our praise,  
In sacred love, our lives embrace.

[Verse 2]  
Turn not to idols made of stone,  
For You alone are God,  
In every act of kindness shown,  
Our hearts shall bear Your rod.  
Leave corners of the field unclaimed,  
And gleanings for the poor,  
For in each gift of grace bestowed,  
You call us to restore.

[Chorus]  
Holy, holy, is the Lord,  
In every heart, Your truth restored.  
We seek Your face, we lift our praise,  
In sacred love, our lives embrace.

[Bridge]  
Let justice flow like rivers wide,  
With love that never fails,  
In unity, we stand and guide,  
Through storms and trials, prevail.  
For every neighbor, every kin,  
Your law of love we bear,  
In grace and truth, we rise again,  
In You, our burdens share.

[Chorus]  
Holy, holy, is the Lord,  
In every heart, Your truth restored.  
We seek Your face, we lift our praise,  
In sacred love, our lives embrace.

[Outro]  
So let us walk, in Your commands,  
With hearts that shine like stars,  
For You, O Lord, hold all our plans,  
Forever, we are Yours.
