[TITLE]
Strengthen My Hands

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of doubt, my enemies call,  
Whispers of fear, they rise, they fall.  
But I’m building a wall, with faith as my guide,  
For the work that You’ve given, I will not hide.  

Chorus  
Strengthen my hands, Lord, I will not cease,  
In the midst of the storm, I find my peace.  
Though they come with their lies, I’ll stand firm and true,  
For the work You’ve begun, Lord, I trust in You.  

Verse 2  
They sent me their words, meant to bring me low,  
But I know in my heart, Your truth will bestow.  
I will not retreat, for You are my shield,  
In the face of the threat, Your power revealed.  

Chorus

Though the world may conspire, I will not fear,  
For the walls that I build, Lord, You are near.

Outro  
So I’ll press on in faith, with courage anew,

Strengthen my hands, Lord, I trust in You.
