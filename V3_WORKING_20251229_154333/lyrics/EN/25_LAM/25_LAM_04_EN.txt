[TITLE]
From Ashes to Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
How has the gold become so dim,  
The purest hearts now lost in sin?  
O precious sons of Zion, weep,  
As hunger's grip our children keep.  
The streets are filled with cries of pain,  
Where once Your love would break the chain.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, arise, restore our sight,  
In darkness, be our guiding light.  
Your fury poured, yet hope remains,  
From ashes, Lord, bring forth new flames.  

Verse 2:  
The sea beasts nurture young with care,  
Yet we, like ostriches, despair.  
We hunger for the bread of life,  
But emptiness breeds endless strife.  
The righteous weep, forsaken now,  
While those who fed find no way out.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, arise, restore our sight,  
In darkness, be our guiding light.  
Your fury poured, yet hope remains,  
From ashes, Lord, bring forth new flames.  

Bridge:  
Our eyes grow dim, we mourn our fate,  
In shadows deep, we watch and wait.  
Yet still, we cling to Your embrace,  
For mercy flows, and grace we trace.  

Verse 3:  
The prophets fallen, priests in shame,  
Their blood cries out, we bear the blame.  
Yet through the fire, You refine,  
In brokenness, Your love will shine.  
O daughter Zion, lift your hands,  
For in Your midst, the Savior stands.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, arise, restore our sight,  
In darkness, be our guiding light.  
Your fury poured, yet hope remains,  
From ashes, Lord, bring forth new flames.  

Outro:  
The punishment is done, we rise,  
In faith we stand, with open skies.  
O daughter Zion, sing your song,  
For in His arms, we will belong.
