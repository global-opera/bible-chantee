[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows fell,  
Israel turned from idols’ spell,  
They broke the chains, they cut the ties,  
With hearts ablaze, they sought the skies.  
Hezekiah led with faith so strong,  
Restoring worship, where we belong.  

Chorus  
Return, O hearts, to the altar of grace,  
Bring your offerings, seek His face.  
In the gates of the Lord, let our praises rise,  
With every voice, we glorify.  

Verse 2  
From the fields, their harvest came,  
Firstfruits offered in His name,  
With joy and tears, they laid them down,  
In heaps of blessing, glory found.  
The priests and Levites took their stand,  
To serve the Lord across the land.  

Chorus  
Return, O hearts, to the altar of grace,  
Bring your offerings, seek His face.  
In the gates of the Lord, let our praises rise,  
With every voice, we glorify.  

Bridge  
With all our hearts, we seek You, Lord,  
In every act, let love be poured.  
Hezekiah’s call, we heed today,  
To worship You in the holy way.  

Verse 3  
In chambers prepared, the gifts laid bare,  
The faithful gathered, their hearts in prayer.  
With each commandment, truth restored,  
In unity, we love the Lord.  
Through every season, we’ll lift Him high,  
With every moment, our souls rely.  

Chorus  
Return, O hearts, to the altar of grace,  
Bring your offerings, seek His face.  
In the gates of the Lord, let our praises rise,  
With every voice, we glorify.  

Outro  
So let us serve with all our might,  
In every dark, we’ll shine Your light.  
With hearts united, we stand as one,  
In worship’s embrace, our victory won.  
Return, O hearts, to the altar of grace,  
In love and truth, we find our place.
