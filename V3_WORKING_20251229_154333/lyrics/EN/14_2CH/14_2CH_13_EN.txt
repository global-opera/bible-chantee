[TITLE]
With Us, Our God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, on mount Zemaraim,  
Abijah stood, amidst the valiant line.  
Four hundred thousand, brave in the fight,  
With Jeroboam's might, they faced the night.  
But he called out strong, to the hearts of men,  
"Remember His promise, His covenant!"  

Chorus:  
For the Lord God is with us, our captain and guide,  
In the face of the battles, we shall not hide.  
With trumpets we shout, and in faith we stand,  
For victory is ours, held firm in His hand.  

Verse 2:  
"Children of Israel, why turn from His grace?  
Forsaking the altar, you’ve lost your place.  
You’ve built up your idols, you’ve turned from His light,  
But we hold to the truth, and we’ll stand and fight.  
For the priests of the Lord, they burn sacrifice,  
Morning and evening, as a sweet incense rise."  

Chorus:  
For the Lord God is with us, our captain and guide,  
In the face of the battles, we shall not hide.  
With trumpets we shout, and in faith we stand,  
For victory is ours, held firm in His hand.  

Bridge:  
When the enemy surrounds, fear not, do not dread,  
For we cry out to God, on wings of prayer we tread.  
In the midst of the storm, His power we claim,  
With a shout of triumph, we’ll magnify His name.  

Chorus:  
For the Lord God is with us, our captain and guide,  
In the face of the battles, we shall not hide.  
With trumpets we shout, and in faith we stand,

Outro:  
So let us march forward, in His holy light,  
With the strength of the heavens, we’ll win every fight.  
For the Lord God is with us, our hearts ever true,  
In the name of Jesus, we’ll see our battles through.
