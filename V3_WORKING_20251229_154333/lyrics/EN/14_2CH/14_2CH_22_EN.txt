Verse 1  
In the city of Jerusalem's light,  
A young king rose, but walked in night,  
Ahaziah, led by wicked ways,  
Counsel of evil, through darkened days.  
Like Ahab's house, his heart did stray,  
In the sight of the Lord, he lost his way.  

Chorus  
Oh, God, Your judgment reigns,  
In the shadows, truth remains.  
With every battle fought in vain,  
Your mercy breaks through every chain.  
In the shadows of kings, we find our plea,  
For You are our light, O Lord, set us free.  

Verse 2  
He joined the war, with a heart so bold,  
Yet the wounds he bore, the tale unfolds.  
In Jezreel's grasp, his fate was sealed,  
With Jehu's sword, the truth revealed.  
Destruction came, as You had planned,  
The cries of the faithful, held in Your hand.
