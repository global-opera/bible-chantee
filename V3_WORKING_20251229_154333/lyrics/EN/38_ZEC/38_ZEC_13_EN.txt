[TITLE]
Fountain of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In that day, a fountain flows,  
For the broken, for the lost,  
A cleansing stream of mercy's grace,  
Washing sin, no matter the cost.  
Idols fall, their names erased,  
In Your presence, Lord, we stand embraced.  

Chorus:  
You are our God, we call on Your name,  
Refine our hearts, ignite the flame.  
Through the fire, we’re made anew,  
In the wounds of love, we find our truth.  

Verse 2:  
Prophets silent, visions fade,  
No more lies in Your holy name.  
With humble hearts, we seek Your face,  
In the shadows, we find our place.  
For every wound, a story told,  
In the house of friends, our hearts unfold.  

Chorus:  
You are our God, we call on Your name,  
Refine our hearts, ignite the flame.  
Through the fire, we’re made anew,  
In the wounds of love, we find our truth.  

Bridge:  
Awake, O sword, against the Shepherd,  
Scattered sheep, in need of grace.  
But through the trials, we will rise,  
Your people, Lord, we seek Your face.  
In the fire, we are purified,  
You hear our cries, our hearts abide.  

Chorus:  
You are our God, we call on Your name,  
Refine our hearts, ignite the flame.  
Through the fire, we’re made anew,  
In the wounds of love, we find our truth.  

Outro:  
Fountain opened, grace revealed,  
In Your presence, our hearts are healed.  
We are Yours, and You are ours,  
Forever held in love's sweet hours.
