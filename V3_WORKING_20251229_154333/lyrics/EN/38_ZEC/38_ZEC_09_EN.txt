[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land where shadows fall,  
The Word of the Lord calls,  
Eyes of man, all tribes in prayer,  
Lifted hearts, we seek You there.  
Damascus rests, as hope draws near,  
In the midst of our deepest fear.  

Chorus:  
Rejoice, rejoice, O Daughter of Zion,  
Your King comes with salvation,  
Lowly on a gentle steed,  
He speaks peace to every need.  
From sea to sea, His reign will be,  
In His arms, we find our plea.  

Verse 2:  
Stronghold built on silver's pride,  
But the Lord will turn the tide,  
Fire will consume the might of men,  
And in His mercy, we rise again.  
Ashkelon shall tremble, hearts will break,  
Yet in His love, we find our stake.

Bridge:  
Turn to the stronghold, prisoners of hope,  
Double for your troubles, rise up and cope.  
With arrows of lightning, He’ll lead the way,  
In the whirlwinds of grace, we’ll trust and stay.  
How great is His goodness, how great His grace,  
In the beauty of His presence, we find our place.

Outro:  
For how great is His beauty,

With hearts forever anchored,
