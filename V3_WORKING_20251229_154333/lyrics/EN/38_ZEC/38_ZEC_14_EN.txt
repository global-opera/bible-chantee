[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Behold, the day of the Lord is near,  
A time of battle, the nations will fear.  
In Jerusalem, the spoils will divide,  
Yet through the darkness, we find our guide.  

Chorus  
For He will fight for us, the Lord our King,  
In the valley of mountains, His praises we'll sing.  
Living waters flow from the heart of this place,  
In the light of His glory, we seek His face.  

Verse 2  
On Olives’ mount, His feet will stand,  
A great valley opens at His command.  
Flee like the days of the quake long ago,  
Our God will come, His power will show.  

Chorus  
For He will fight for us, the Lord our King,  
In the valley of mountains, His praises we'll sing.  
Living waters flow from the heart of this place,

Bridge  
One day, not day, nor night shall it be,  
The light will break forth, our eyes shall see.  
Holiness unto the Lord, we declare,  
In every heart, His presence will bear.  

Verse 3  
The wealth of the nations shall gather in grace,  
To worship the King, to find our place.  
No more destruction, we’ll dwell in peace,  
In the house of our Lord, our burdens release.  

Chorus

Outro  
In that day, we will rise and proclaim,  
Every pot shall be holy, exalt His name.  
The feast of our King, let all nations come,  
In the light of His mercy, our hearts are one.
