Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the night, we come before You,  
With our hearts wide open, Lord, we seek the truth.  
Teach us to pray, as the disciples did,  
To honor Your name, our Father in the skies.

Chorus:  
Our Father, hallowed be Your name,  
Your kingdom come, Your will, the same.  
Give us our daily bread, Lord, we plead,  
Forgive our sins, as we forgive those in need.
