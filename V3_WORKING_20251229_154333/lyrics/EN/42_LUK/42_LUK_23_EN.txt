[TITLE]
King of the Cross

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
They led Him to the trial, the multitude arose,  
With voices fierce and angry, they chose to expose.  
Accused of perverting, they sought to condemn,  
Yet Pilate found no fault in Him, the Son of Man.

Chorus  
Oh, King of the Cross, You bear our shame,  
With love unending, we call Your name.  
In darkness, You shine, the light of our way,  
Father, forgive them, You humbly pray.

Verse 2  
From Galilee to Calvary, they shouted with disdain,  
“Crucify this Savior!” their hearts were filled with pain.  
Yet in the midst of sorrow, He spoke of grace,  
“Daughters of Jerusalem, weep for your place.”

Chorus  
Oh, King of the Cross, You bear our shame,  
With love unending, we call Your name.  
In darkness, You shine, the light of our way,  
Father, forgive them, You humbly pray.

Bridge  
When the sun was darkened, and the veil was torn,  
You gave Your spirit, our salvation born.  
A righteous man stood witness to the pain,  
“Surely this was God’s own, the Lamb who reigns.”

Verse 3  
Two thieves beside You, one cried out in fear,  
“Remember me, Lord Jesus, when Your kingdom’s near.”  
With grace You answered, “Today you’ll be with Me,”  
In paradise forever, Your love sets us free.

Chorus  
Oh, King of the Cross, You bear our shame,  
With love unending, we call Your name.  
In darkness, You shine, the light of our way,  
Father, forgive them, You humbly pray.

Outro  
So let us not forget, Your sacrifice so true,  
In every tear and trial, we turn our hearts to You.  
Oh, King of the Cross, forever we will sing,  
For in Your death, we find our everything.
