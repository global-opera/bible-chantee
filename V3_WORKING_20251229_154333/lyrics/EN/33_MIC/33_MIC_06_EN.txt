[TITLE]
What Does the Lord Require?

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Hear the voice of the Lord, arise and stand,  
Mountains bear witness, let the hills understand.  
O my people, what have I done to you?  
I brought you from bondage, my love ever true.  

Chorus:  
What does the Lord require, but to do justly,  
To love mercy, walk humbly, and seek His glory?  
O my soul, remember His ways,  
In the light of His truth, let us lift our praise.  

Verse 2:  
I sent you the prophets, to guide on your way,  
Yet you turn from my counsel, your hearts led astray.  
Shall I bring you offerings, or rivers of oil?  
What good are the riches, if hearts are in toil?  

Chorus:  
What does the Lord require, but to do justly,  
To love mercy, walk humbly, and seek His glory?  
O my soul, remember His ways,  
In the light of His truth, let us lift our praise.  

Bridge:  
The Lord’s voice cries out, hear the call of the wise,  
Repent from the darkness, lift your eyes to the skies.  
For in the midst of the trials, His justice will shine,  
Return to the Father, your heart He’ll align.  

Chorus:  
What does the Lord require, but to do justly,  
To love mercy, walk humbly, and seek His glory?

Outro:  
In the stillness, we come, with hearts open wide,  
To do justly, love mercy, and walk by His side.  
O my people, hear now, the call of His grace,  
For in humble obedience, we find our true place.
