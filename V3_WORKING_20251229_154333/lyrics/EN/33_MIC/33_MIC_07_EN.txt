[TITLE]
In the Depths of Your Mercy

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Woe is me, lost in the night,  
Like summer fruit, I seek the light.  
The good have vanished from the earth,  
No upright heart, no hope, no worth.  
They hunt like wolves, with sharpened claws,  
In shadows deep, without a cause.  

Chorus  
But I will look to You, O Lord,  
In my despair, I’ll seek Your word.  
When I fall, I will arise,  
You are my light, my hope, my prize.  

Verse 2  
Trust not in friends, for they may fail,  
In the silence, hear my wail.  
Enemies rise from within my home,  
In the chaos, I feel so alone.  
Yet I bear the weight of my sin,  
Until Your mercy breaks within.  

Chorus  
But I will look to You, O Lord,  
In my despair, I’ll seek Your word.  
When I fall, I will arise,  
You are my light, my hope, my prize.  

Bridge  
Who is a God like You, so near?  
You pardon sin and calm my fear.  
You cast my shame into the sea,  
Your love and grace, they set me free.  
You will turn again, with compassion true,  
In the depths of mercy, I cling to You.  

Chorus  
I will look to You, O Lord,  
In my despair, I’ll seek Your word.  
When I fall, I will arise,  
You are my light, my hope, my prize.  

Outro  
Feed Your flock, O Shepherd King,  
In the days of old, Your praises sing.  
With every breath, I’ll lift my voice,  
In Your salvation, I will rejoice.  
In Your mercy, I will stand,  
Holding tightly to Your hand.
