Verse 1  
In the shadows of a night so still,  
God spoke to Abram, His voice fulfilled.  
“I am the Almighty, walk in My ways,  
A father of nations, your heart I raise.  
Your name shall be Abraham, a promise made,  
With every step, in faith, never afraid.”  

Chorus  
Covenant of promise, forever You stand,  
With every generation, we’re held in Your hand.  
From the heart of the desert to the shores of the sea,  
You’re the God of our fathers, and You’re faithful to me.  

Verse 2

“Isaac, your son, a testament true,  
From barren to blessed, I’ll bring life anew.”  
Kings shall arise, and nations will sing,  
In the tapestry woven, Your glory we bring.
