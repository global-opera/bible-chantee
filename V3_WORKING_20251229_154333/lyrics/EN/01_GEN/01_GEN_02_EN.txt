[TITLE]
In the Garden of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the dawn of creation, the heavens proclaimed,  
He spoke and the cosmos, by His voice were named.  
On the seventh day, He rested in peace,  
Blessed the hour of stillness, where all toil would cease.  

Chorus:  
In the garden of grace, where love intertwines,  
With the breath of His Spirit, life eternally shines.  
From the dust of the earth, we were formed by His hand,  
In the heart of creation, forever we stand.  

Verse 2:  
He planted a garden, where rivers would flow,  
Trees of life and knowledge, in beauty did grow.  
Every creature and flower, He called into being,  
In the midst of His wonders, His glory we're seeing.  

Chorus:  
In the garden of grace, where love intertwines,

Bridge:  
Oh, the deepness of His love, that made us His own,  
In the image of the Father, we are never alone.  
With a heart full of wonder, we rise to proclaim,  
In the garden of grace, we glorify His name.  

Chorus:  
In the garden of grace, where love intertwines,

Outro:  
And we’ll walk in His light, through the day and the night,  
In the garden of grace, we are woven in His might.  
Together as one, in His love we abide,  
In the garden of grace, forever our guide.
