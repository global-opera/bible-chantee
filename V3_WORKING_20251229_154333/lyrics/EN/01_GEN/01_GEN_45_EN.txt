[TITLE]
Come Near to Me

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the famine, where hope was lost in fear,  
Joseph stood before his brothers, his heart was drawing near.  
With tears that flowed like rivers, he called them to his side,  
“I am Joseph, your brother, in whom you can confide.  

Chorus:  
Come near to me, let your hearts be free,  
Though you sold me into darkness, God had a plan for me.  
He sent me forth to save you, to bring you home again,  
In the land of Goshen, we’ll find life without end.  

Verse 2:  
No need for grief or anger, for God’s hand was in it all,  
He brought me through the trials, He raised me from the fall.  
Now I stand as a father, to Pharaoh I’ve been made,  
For the sake of our salvation, this path was surely laid.

Bridge:  
See the wagons filled with blessings, provisions for the way,  
The good of all the land is yours, come, do not delay.  
Tell our father of my glory, the hope that’s come alive,  
For Joseph lives, and in this grace, our family will thrive.  

Verse 3:  
Together we will gather, under the starry skies,  
With Benjamin beside me, joy reflected in our eyes.  
So lift your heads, my brothers, for the past is washed away,  
In the arms of our Redeemer, we’ll rise again each day.

Outro:  
So come near to me, as our souls unite in grace,  
In the love of our Creator, we’ll forever find our place.  
Together in His promise, we are never far apart,  
For God has brought us home again, united heart to heart.
