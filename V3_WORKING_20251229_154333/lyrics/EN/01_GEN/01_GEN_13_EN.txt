[TITLE]
Promise in the Dust

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Out of Egypt, Abram rose,  
With his kin, and all he chose,  
From the south to Bethel's grace,  
To the altar, he sought Your face.  
Rich in cattle, silver, gold,  
Yet his heart, in You, was bold.  
Between the tents, a strife arose,  
But love prevailed; his kindness flows.

Chorus  
Lift up your eyes, behold the land,  
Every promise, held in His hand.  
Walk in the breadth, the length, the light,  
In the dust of the earth, our futures bright.  
For the seed of faith shall multiply,  
In the heart of the faithful, our God is nigh.

Verse 2  
Lot looked east, chose the plains so fair,  
But the wickedness lingered in the air.  
Abram stayed in Canaan's embrace,  
Trusting Your covenant, seeking Your grace.  
"Let there be peace," he humbly said,  
For we are brethren, in love we're led.  
The land before them, vast and wide,  
In every choice, You’re our guide.

Bridge  
Arise, walk through the earth,  
Every step, a new birth.  
Counting the dust, the stars above,  
In His promises, we find our love.  
For every journey, every trial faced,  
In His presence, we find our place.
