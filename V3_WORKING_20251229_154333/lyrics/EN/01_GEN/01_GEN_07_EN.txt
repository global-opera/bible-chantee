[TITLE]
The Ark of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In a world turned cold, where sin did abound,  
The Lord spoke to Noah, His voice like a sound.  
"Come, gather your family, into the safe place,  
For I see your heart, in this time and this space."  

Chorus:  
Oh, the waters will rise, but we will not fear,  
In the ark of Your promise, Lord, we draw near.  
Through the storms and the trials, Your love will remain,  
Forever we'll trust, in Your covenant name.  

Verse 2:  
Two by two they entered, the creatures of grace,  
Clean and unclean, they found their own space.  
Forty days of rain, but You held us secure,  
In the midst of the chaos, Your love is our cure.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the waters will rise, but we will not fear,  
In the ark of Your promise, Lord, we draw near.  
Through the storms and the trials, Your love will remain,  
Forever we'll trust, in Your covenant name.  

Bridge:  
You shut us in, Lord, in Your faithful embrace,  
As the flood raged around, we found rest in Your grace.  
Through the darkest of nights, Your light is our way,  
In the heart of the tempest, we will not stray.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the waters will rise, but we will not fear,  
In the ark of Your promise, Lord, we draw near.  
Through the storms and the trials, Your love will remain,  
Forever we'll trust, in Your covenant name.  

Outro:  
So let the rains fall, let the rivers swell high,  
For we are Your people, under Your sky.  
With every new dawn, we’ll sing and proclaim,  
In the ark of Your promise, forever the same.
