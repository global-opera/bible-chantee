[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
My heart trembles at Your voice,  
In awe of all You’ve done,  
You guide the lightning through the skies,  
And still the raging sun.  
From heavens high, Your wonders pour,  
You shape the clouds with grace,  
In whispers soft, You call us near,  
To seek Your holy face.

Chorus  
Oh, mighty God, we stand in awe,  
Your majesty surrounds,  
In every storm, in every peace,  
Your love and power found.  
You are the strength within our hearts,  
The light that breaks the night,  
With every breath, we lift Your name,  
You are our guiding light.

Verse 2  
In winter’s chill, You speak to snow,  
And rain from clouds above,  
You seal the hands of every man,  
Yet fill our hearts with love.  
The beasts retreat, the winds obey,  
Your voice, it shakes the ground,  
We marvel at Your wondrous works,  
In silence, we are found.

Bridge  
Teach us, Lord, to know Your ways,  
In darkness we will trust,

We know You are the same,  
In every trial, in every grace,  
We lift our hearts in praise.
