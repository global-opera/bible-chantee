Verse 1  
In the quiet of my sorrow,  
Words of comfort fade away,  
Miserable are the voices,  
Where's the peace they claim to bring?  
Yet, I long to lift you higher,  
With the strength that faith can sing,  
Though my heart is heavy laden,  
In Your arms, I find my wings.  

Chorus  
O, my God, hear my cry,  
Tears of anguish flow like rivers,  
In the shadows, I will sigh,  
Yet Your love remains forever.  
From the depths, I’ll call Your name,  
In the silence, You are near,  
With my heart, I’ll seek Your face,  
For You’re my refuge, Lord, I fear.  

Verse 2  
Though the storm around me rages,  
And the arrows pierce my soul,  
In my weakness, I am searching,  
For the truth that makes me whole.  
They have gathered all around me,  
Mocking words that cut so deep,  
But I’ll raise my voice in worship,  
For my hope in You I keep.

Bridge
