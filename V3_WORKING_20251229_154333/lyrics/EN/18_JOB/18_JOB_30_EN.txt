[TITLE]
In the Midst of Shadows

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
When the weight of sorrow’s near,  
I cry out, Lord, to You,  
Yet it feels like no one’s here.  
These hands that push against my soul,  
The laughter that surrounds my pain,  
Yet still, I seek Your light,  
In the storm, I call Your name.

Chorus  
You are my refuge, my strength in the fight,  
In the depths of despair, You are my guiding light.  
Though the world may turn and the shadows may grow,  
I’ll trust in Your mercy, in every high and low.

Verse 2  
Among the cries of broken hearts,  
Where the lost have found their shame,  
I weep for those who wander far,  
In the darkness, call their name.  
My spirit, Lord, is heavy now,  
With the struggle of the day,  
Yet I know You hear my prayer,  
And You guide me on my way.
