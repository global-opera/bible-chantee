[TITLE]
Hand of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence, I hear Your voice,  
Whispers of wisdom, in You I rejoice.  
The earth and the skies, they tell of Your might,  
In every creature, Your hand brings the light.  

Chorus  
Who knows the depths of Your grace,  
With every heartbeat, I seek Your face.  
You hold the world in Your gentle hand,  
Oh Lord, my God, in You I stand.  

Verse 2  
Though mockers may laugh, and trials may rise,  
Your truth is my anchor, my hope never dies.  
In the shadows of doubt, You shine like the dawn,  
With strength and with wisdom, You carry me on.  

Chorus  
Who knows the depths of Your grace,  
With every heartbeat, I seek Your face.  
You hold the world in Your gentle hand,  
Oh Lord, my God, in You I stand.  

Bridge  
You break the chains, and You lift the weak,  
In whispers of mercy, Your heart will speak.  
You lead through the darkness, You guide my way,  
In the wilderness, Lord, I trust and obey.  

Chorus  
Who knows the depths of Your grace,  
With every heartbeat, I seek Your face.  
You hold the world in Your gentle hand,  
Oh Lord, my God, in You I stand.  

Outro  
You’re the hope of the ages, the light of the day,  
In every moment, Lord, I’ll follow Your way.  
With every heartbeat, I seek Your face,  
Oh Lord, my God, I’m saved by Your grace.
