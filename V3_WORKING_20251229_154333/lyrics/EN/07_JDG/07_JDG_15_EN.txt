[TITLE]
Heaps Upon Heaps

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the time of harvest, a heart was torn,  
Samson came to love, but found his bride was gone.  
With fire in his spirit, he sought to reclaim,  
In the face of betrayal, he called on Your name.  

Chorus  
Oh, mighty God, who hears our cries,  
You turn our sorrow into victory skies.  
With the jawbone of an ass, he fought and stood,  
In the strength of Your power, he knew he could.  

Verse 2  
Three hundred foxes, he lit the flames,  
In the fields of the Philistines, he played their games.  
But vengeance is Yours, the battle is won,  
With courage and might, Your will be done.  

Chorus  
Oh, mighty God, who hears our cries,  
You turn our sorrow into victory skies.  
With the jawbone of an ass, he fought and stood,  
In the strength of Your power, he knew he could.  

Bridge  
When the cords bound him fast, Your Spirit came,  
Like fire in his bones, igniting the same.  
From a rock, You brought forth a well,  
In the dry and the weary, Your love does dwell.  

Verse 3  
Thirsty and weary, he called on Your grace,  
“Will I fall to the uncircumcised?” he faced.  
But You split the ground, and water did flow,  
In the depths of despair, Your mercy does show.  

Chorus  
Oh, mighty God, who hears our cries,  
You turn our sorrow into victory skies.  
With the jawbone of an ass, he fought and stood,  
In the strength of Your power, he knew he could.  

Outro  
He judged for the people, through trials and pain,  
In the heart of the battle, Your glory remained.  
With heaps upon heaps, he led with Your call,  
In the story of faith, You are Lord of all.
