[TITLE]
Journeys of His Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
From Rameses, we set our sights,  
With mighty hands, we walked in light.  
The Passover night, a blood-stained grace,  
The Lord's great hand, our hearts embraced.  
Through trials fierce, we faced the sea,  
In wilderness paths, He led us free.  

Chorus:  
Oh, we journey on, through night and day,  
With every step, our hearts will say,  
The Lord is with us, guiding our way,  
In the land of promise, we will stay.

Verse 2:  
In Marah's bitter, we found our need,  
Yet Elim’s fountains flowed with speed.  
Through valleys deep and mountains high,  
His presence leads; we will not die.  
With every camp, our spirits rise,  
In Sinai's shadow, we hear His cries.

Chorus:  
Oh, we journey on, through night and day,  
With every step, our hearts will say,  
The Lord is with us, guiding our way,  
In the land of promise, we will stay.

Bridge:  
When we cross the Jordan's tide,  
With faith our guide, we will abide.  
Drive out the fear that holds us tight,  
For in His hands, we find our light.  
The land is ours, a gift divine,  
In covenant love, His truth will shine.

Chorus:  
Oh, we journey on, through night and day,  
With every step, our hearts will say,  
The Lord is with us, guiding our way,  
In the land of promise, we will stay.

Outro:  
So here we stand, with hearts aglow,  
In His embrace, we’ll ever go.  
From Egypt’s chains, we’re set apart,  
For every journey starts in the heart.
