[TITLE]
Counted in His Grace

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the wilderness, You called us,  
Moses heard Your voice so clear,  
Gather all the tribes together,  
In each heart, Your love we fear.  
From Reuben to the sons of Judah,  
Each name, a story to unfold,  
In strength and unity we gather,  
In Your arms, we stand so bold.

[Chorus]  
Counted in Your grace, we rise,  
With every name, our spirits soar,  
Together we will praise,  
The One who guides forevermore.  
In covenant and love, we stand,  
Each tribe, each heart, a faithful band,  
Counted in Your grace, we rise,  
Together, Lord, we lift our eyes.

[Verse 2]  
From Simeon to the tribe of Gad,  
Every warrior, every heart,  
You have called us for Your purpose,  
In Your presence, we’ll not part.  
With each son and every daughter,  
We proclaim Your mighty name,  
In the shadow of Your glory,  
We will never be the same.

[Chorus]  
Counted in Your grace, we rise,

The One who guides forevermore.  
In covenant and love, we stand,

Counted in Your grace, we rise,

[Bridge]  
Oh, let the Levites guard Your presence,  
Surrounding all with holy fear,  
In this sacred space of worship,  
Lord, we draw so near.  
As we march in faith and unity,  
Let our hearts be true and bright,  
In the wilderness, You guide us,  
Leading us into the light.

[Outro]  
In the wilderness, we find our home,  
In Your love, we are never alone.  
Counted in Your grace, forever we will sing,  
Together in Your arms, Lord, our offering.
