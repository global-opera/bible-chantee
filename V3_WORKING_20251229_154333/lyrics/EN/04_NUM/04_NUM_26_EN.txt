[TITLE]
From Wilderness to Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the plains of Moab, the Lord spoke clear,  
To Moses and Eleazar, our hearts draw near.  
Counting the faithful, from twenty and bold,  
A legacy written in stories of old.  
Reuben and Simeon, their families stand,  
From each tribe gathered, united we band.

Chorus  
From wilderness to promise, we journey on,  
Inherit the land, where our hope is drawn.  
With every name called, the story unfolds,  
In the heart of the faithful, His love we behold.

Verse 2  
Gad and Judah, their numbers align,  
Issachar and Zebulun, in God’s grand design.  
Joseph’s sons, Ephraim and Manasseh,  
Together we rise, in His grace we’ll progress.  
Families of Asher, and Naphtali’s song,  
In the tapestry woven, we all belong.

Chorus  
From wilderness to promise, we journey on,  
Inherit the land, where our hope is drawn.  
With every name called, the story unfolds,  
In the heart of the faithful, His love we behold.

Bridge  
O Lord, You divide, by lot and by grace,  
Each tribe shall inherit, in this sacred place.  
Among the Levites, a holy decree,  
Your presence among us, forever we’ll be.  
Caleb and Joshua, the faithful remain,  
In the land of our promise, we’ll never be slain.

Chorus  
From wilderness to promise, we journey on,  
Inherit the land, where our hope is drawn.  
With every name called, the story unfolds,  
In the heart of the faithful, His love we behold.

Outro  
So let us remember, as we lift our song,  
The journey together, where we all belong.  
From wilderness to promise, our hearts in His hands,  
With faith as our anchor, united we stand.
