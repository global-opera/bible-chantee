[TITLE]
In the Wilderness of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, Israel cried,  
Discouraged by the journey, hearts filled with pride.  
Yet You heard their voices, Lord, in their despair,  
Promised them deliverance, love beyond compare.  

Chorus  
Oh, spring up, O well, let the waters flow,  
In the desert of our trials, Your grace will show.  
When we lift our eyes to the serpent high,  
We'll find our healing, in You we rely.  

Verse 2  
Fiery serpents biting, sin we can't deny,  
We have spoken against You, Lord, we sigh.  
But Moses prayed for mercy, You heard his plea,  
In the midst of our failures, Your love sets us free.  

Chorus  
Oh, spring up, O well, let the waters flow,  
In the desert of our trials, Your grace will show.  
When we lift our eyes to the serpent high,  
We'll find our healing, in You we rely.  

Bridge  
Fear not, says the Lord, for I am your guide,  
With every battle fought, I will be your side.  
From Arnon to Jabbok, Your hand leads the way,  
In the land of promise, we will sing and pray.  

Chorus  
Oh, spring up, O well, let the waters flow,  
In the desert of our trials, Your grace will show.  
When we lift our eyes to the serpent high,  
We'll find our healing, in You we rely.  

Outro  
In the wilderness of grace, we stand renewed,  
For every step You take, our hearts are imbued.  
Oh, spring up, O well, let the praises rise,  
In the land of Your mercy, we lift our eyes.
