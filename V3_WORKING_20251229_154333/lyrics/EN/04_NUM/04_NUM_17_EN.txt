[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, You spoke to Moses,  
“Gather rods of every tribe, let My voice be chosen.  
Each name upon the wood, a story to unfold,  
In the tabernacle's light, Your promise we behold.”  

Chorus  
Let the rod that blooms show Your hand,  
In the midst of doubt, in this barren land.  
You will silence the cries of the weary and lost,  
In the blossoming grace, we find peace at Your cost.  

Verse 2  
Twelve rods laid before You, in faith we place our plea,  
A token of Your presence, O Lord, we long to see.  
And in the morning light, the rod of Aaron shone,  
Buds and blossoms bursting forth, a miracle made known.  

Chorus  
Let the rod that blooms show Your hand,  
In the midst of doubt, in this barren land.  
You will silence the cries of the weary and lost,

Bridge  
O Lord, we tremble near Your holy place,  
In awe of Your power, we seek Your embrace.  
Take away our murmurs, our fear, and our strife,  
Let Your glory, O God, breathe new life.  

Chorus  
Let the rod that blooms show Your hand,  
In the midst of doubt, in this barren land.  
You will silence the cries of the weary and lost,

Outro  
So we gather in Your presence, with hearts open wide,  
Trusting in Your promise, our Savior, our guide.  
In every rod that blooms, Your faithfulness we see,  
In the beauty of Your love, forever we will be.
