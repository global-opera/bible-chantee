[TITLE]
In the Wilderness, We Cry

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the night, our voices rise,  
Tears like rivers fill our eyes.  
Did You bring us here to fall,  
To wander lost, to lose it all?  
Moses, Aaron, on their knees,  
"Lord, have mercy, grant us peace."  
In our hearts, rebellion grows,  
Yet Your faithfulness still shows.

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear our cry,  
In the wilderness, we sigh.  
Though we tremble, we believe,  
In Your promise, we shall cleave.  
Milk and honey, a land You’ve sworn,  
Lead us on, though we feel worn.  
In the wilderness, we cry,  
Lift us up, O Lord, on high.

Verse 2:  
Caleb, Joshua, hearts ablaze,  
"Fear not, for God’s love stays!"  
The giants may stand tall and fierce,  
But Your Spirit, Lord, will pierce.  
In our doubts, we seek the way,  
Yet Your glory lights the day.  
Forgive our fears, our wandering hearts,  
In Your mercy, new life starts.

Chorus:  
O Lord, hear our cry,

Bridge:  
Longsuffering, Your grace abounds,  
In the shadows, love surrounds.  
Though our journey feels so long,  
In the struggle, we are strong.  
You are faithful, we will stand,  
With our hearts, we lift our hands.  
Guide us through this desert land,  
In Your arms, we take our stand.

Outro:  
In the wilderness, we cry,  
O Lord, our hope, our guide.  
With the promise in our hearts,  
We will rise, we will not part.  
In the wilderness, we cry.  
In the wilderness, we cry.
