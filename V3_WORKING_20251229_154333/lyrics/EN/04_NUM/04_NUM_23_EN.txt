[TITLE]
The Word of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heights of the mountains, where shadows align,  
Balaam built altars, in faith he would find.  
Seven bulls and rams in a sacred embrace,  
He sought the Lord’s counsel, to know His grace.  

Chorus:  
Who can curse what the Lord has blessed?  
Who can defy, when He stands with the rest?  
From the dust of Jacob, a people arise,  
With a shout of victory, they lift to the skies.  

Verse 2:  
Balak stood waiting, his heart full of dread,  
"Come curse me this people," in whispers he said.  
But the Lord met Balaam, and filled him with truth,  
"Speak not your own words, but the words of My youth."

Bridge:  
God is not a man, He will not deceive,  
What He has spoken, we shall believe.  
No enchantment against us, no darkness can bind,  
For the Lord is our strength, our hearts intertwined.
