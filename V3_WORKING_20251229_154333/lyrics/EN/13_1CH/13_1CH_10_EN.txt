[TITLE]
Fallen Yet Redeemed

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the valley of shadows, the battle raged,  
Men of Israel fled, their hearts disengaged.  
Saul stood in the fray, with honor on his brow,  
But the weight of his choices led him to the end now.  

Chorus  
Oh, fallen king, in silence you lay,  
The echoes of your reign, now fading away.  
Yet in the dust, redemption calls,  
For every heart that stumbles, His grace restores us all.  

Verse 2  
Jonathan, beloved, lost in the fight,  
With Abinadab and Malchishua, gone from the light.  
The archers drew near, their arrows took aim,  
In the shadows of Gilboa, they whispered your name.  

Chorus  
Oh, fallen king, in silence you lay,  
The echoes of your reign, now fading away.  
Yet in the dust, redemption calls,  
For every heart that stumbles, His grace restores us all.  

Bridge  
In Jabesh, they mourned, the valiant stood tall,  
They honored the fallen, they answered the call.  
Seven days of fasting, a tribute of tears,  
For the king and his sons, through the pain we draw near.  

Verse 3  
He sought not the Lord, in his darkest hour,  
But grace still abounds, and love has the power.  
For from the ashes, a new light will rise,  
David, the shepherd, with faith in his eyes.  

Chorus  
Oh, fallen king, in silence you lay,  
The echoes of your reign, now fading away.  
Yet in the dust, redemption calls,  
For every heart that stumbles, His grace restores us all.  

Outro  
So let us remember, the lessons we learn,  
In the face of our battles, to His voice we return.  
For in every story, His mercy will gleam,  
Fallen yet redeemed, we rise in His dream.
