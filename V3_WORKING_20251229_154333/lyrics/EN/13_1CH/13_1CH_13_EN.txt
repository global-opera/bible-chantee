[TITLE]
Bring Back the Ark

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heart of Israel, a longing arose,  
David called the people, united in his pose.  
“Let us seek the Lord, let His presence return,  
With the Ark of God, our hearts will surely burn.”  

Chorus:  
Bring back the Ark, let the glory shine,  
In the midst of our praises, Your love entwined.  
We’ll gather together, with voices so strong,  
In the joy of Your presence, we will belong.  

Verse 2:  
From the fields of Shihor to Hemath's embrace,  
David led the people, a journey to grace.  
With harps and with timbrels, they sang out His name,  
In the joy of the Lord, hearts ignited aflame.  

Chorus:  
Bring back the Ark, let the glory shine,  
In the midst of our praises, Your love entwined.  
We’ll gather together, with voices so strong,  
In the joy of Your presence, we will belong.  

Bridge:  
But in the midst of the joy, a warning came,  
Uzza reached for the Ark, and he fell like a flame.  
David's heart was troubled, yet still he would seek,  
For the fear of the Lord, made the humble speak.  

Verse 3:  
In Obed-edom’s house, the blessing poured down,  
For three months of favor, in God’s love they found.  
So we’ll carry Your presence, with reverent hearts,  
In the story of faith, Lord, we’ll play our parts.  

Chorus:  
Bring back the Ark, let the glory shine,  
In the midst of our praises, Your love entwined.  
We’ll gather together, with voices so strong,  
In the joy of Your presence, we will belong.  

Outro:  
Oh, bring back the Ark, to our hearts and our land,  
In the arms of Your mercy, forever we’ll stand.  
With every song lifted, and every prayer we bring,  
We’ll honor Your presence, our praises will sing.
