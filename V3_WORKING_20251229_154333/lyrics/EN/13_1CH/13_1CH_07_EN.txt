[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, where the brave hearts stand,  
The sons of Issachar arose, led by God’s own hand.  
Tola and Uzzi, valiant men of might,  
Two and twenty thousand strong, they shone like stars at night.

Chorus  
We lift our voices, declare Your name,  
In battles fought, we'll praise the same.  
From every tribe, we rise as one,  
With hearts ignited by the Son.  
We stand in faith, our spirits soar,  
Mighty warriors, forevermore.

Verse 2  
The sons of Benjamin, brave and true,

Bridge  
Oh, hear the sound of victory’s song,  
As we march together, we are strong.  
In the name of Jesus, we will fight,  
With faith as our armor, we walk in light.

Verse 3  
From Ephraim’s lineage, to Asher’s grace,  
In every generation, we will seek Your face.  
With stories of triumph, our praises ascend,  
For in every battle, You are our friend.

Outro  
So let the banners wave, let the trumpets sound,  
In unity we gather, where Your love abounds.  
With each step we take, we’ll testify,  
For we are Your children, and to You we’ll fly.
