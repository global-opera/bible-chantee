[TITLE]
At the Threshingfloor

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of my doubt,  
Where my pride has led me wrong,  
A whisper calls within the night,  
The weight of sin, too great, too strong.  
O Lord, my heart is bare,  
In Your mercy, I despair,  
For I have numbered all my days,  
Yet lost in fear, I’ve lost my way.  

Chorus:  
At the threshingfloor, I kneel,  
Where grace and judgment meet,  
In the fire, You reveal,  
The cost of love, the price of peace.  
Let not the sword fall on this place,  
For I am guilty, Lord, I seek Your face.  
At the threshingfloor, I lay it down,  
In Your mercy, let my heart be found.  

Verse 2:  
O Gad, you bring the word of fate,  
Three paths to choose, a heavy weight,  
Yet in my heart, I cry to Thee,  
Let me fall in mercy’s sea.  
For man’s hand brings a heavy chain,  
But Your grace, it breaks the pain,  
With sackcloth on, I plead my case,  
O Lord, let me behold Your face.  

Chorus:  
At the threshingfloor, I kneel,  
Where grace and judgment meet,  
In the fire, You reveal,  
The cost of love, the price of peace.  
Let not the sword fall on this place,  
For I am guilty, Lord, I seek Your face.

Bridge:  
And there, You answered from above,  
With fire that ignites the heart,  
In surrender, I find Your love,  
This altar marks a brand new start.  
O Ornan, grant me this holy ground,  
For in my brokenness, Your grace abounds.

Outro:  
So here I stand, with hands held high,  
In the presence of the Lord Most High,  
At the threshingfloor, I’ll praise Your name,  
For in this place, I’m never the same.  
In Your mercy, I am found,  
At the threshingfloor, I turn around.
