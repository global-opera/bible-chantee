[TITLE]
Legacy of Praise

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the altar, where the Levites stand,  
Gershon, Kohath, Merari, a faithful band.  
From Amram to Aaron, the lineage unfolds,  
A story of devotion, through ages retold.  

(Chorus)  
We lift our voices high, in unity we sing,  
A legacy of praise, to our eternal King.  
From the heart of the faithful, to the hands of the brave,  
We honor those before us, their faith we will save.  

(Verse 2)  
Through trials and through triumphs, they carried the light,  
With each generation, they walked in His might.  
From Aaron to Phinehas, the offering made,  
In the holy of holies, His promise displayed.  

(Chorus)  
We lift our voices high, in unity we sing,  
A legacy of praise, to our eternal King.  
From the heart of the faithful, to the hands of the brave,  
We honor those before us, their faith we will save.  

(Bridge)  
With every note of worship, every heart that believes,  
We stand on the shoulders of the faithful who grieve.  
For in every city, and every sacred space,  
The echoes of their stories draw us to His grace.  

(Verse 3)  
From the mountains to the valleys, their cities remain,  
Hebron, Libnah, and Gath, forever proclaim.  
The God of our fathers, who leads us today,  
In the song of our hearts, we find our way.  

(Chorus)  
We lift our voices high, in unity we sing,  
A legacy of praise, to our eternal King.  
From the heart of the faithful, to the hands of the brave,  
We honor those before us, their faith we will save.  

(Outro)  
So let us carry forward, the torch of their flame,  
In the chorus of the ages, we glorify His name.  
For the sons of Levi, their song shall not cease,  
In the rhythm of His love, we find our peace.
