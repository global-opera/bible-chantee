### Title: "Sons of the Covenant"

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the altar, under Aaron’s watchful eye,  
The sons of promise gathered, their hearts lifted high.  
From Eleazar to Ithamar, they stand in sacred grace,  
Chosen for His purpose, to seek the Lord’s embrace.  

Chorus  
We are the sons of the covenant,  
Called to serve, to stand in faith.  
With hearts ablaze, we lift Your name,  
In unity, we seek Your face.  
O God, our strength, our guiding light,  
In this holy place, we worship You tonight.  

Verse 2  
Through the lots that fell before them, twelve tribes they represent,  
In the house of the Lord, their lives are truly spent.  
From Jehoiarib to Jedaiah, each name a sacred song,  
In the rhythm of the ages, they’ve been chosen for so long.

Bridge  
From every tribe, from every land,  
We unite, we take a stand.  
In reverence, we come as one,  
In the light of Your love, our journey's begun.  
O Lord, remember us, as we sing,  
In the beauty of Your grace, our praises ring.

Outro  
In the shadow of the altar, we will forever stand,  
Sons and daughters of the promise, held safely in Your hand.  
With every breath we take, in Your presence, we abide,  
Together in Your love, forever we will rise.
