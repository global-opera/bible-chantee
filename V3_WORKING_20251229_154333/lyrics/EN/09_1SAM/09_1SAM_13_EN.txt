[TITLE]
In the Valley of Giants

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, the people tremble and hide,  
A garrison of giants, their hearts filled with pride.  
But we gather in Your presence, Lord, in unity we'll stand,  
For You are our deliverer, with power in Your hand.

Chorus  
In the valley of giants, we lift our eyes,  
Our hope is found in You, O Lord on high.  
When all seems lost, and darkness surrounds,  
We'll trust in Your promise, our victory is found.

Verse 2  
Saul stood at Gilgal, waiting for the sign,  
But fear crept in like shadows, and he lost what was divine.  
With burnt offerings and pleading, he sought to break the strife,  
Yet the heart of God was searching, for a man to lead in life.

For You are our fortress, our refuge and care.  
With swords unsharpened, yet courage in our hearts,  
We will follow Your calling, Lord, together we’ll start.
