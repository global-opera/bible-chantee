[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Ashdod, the shadows fell,  
They brought in the ark, where Dagon dwells.  
But the morning light revealed a truth profound,  
Dagon lay broken, face to the ground.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the hand of the Lord is mighty and strong,  
He reigns over all, where the shadows belong.  
We lift up our voices, in worship we sing,  
For the God who is with us, our Savior, our King.
