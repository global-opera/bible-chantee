Verse 1  
In the valley, shadows loom,  
Israel trembles, facing doom.  
The Philistines gather near,  
Yet we cry out, Lord, draw near.  
Where is the strength that once we knew?  
Bring back the Ark, our hope renew.

Chorus  
Oh, let the earth resound with praise,  
As Your presence fills this place.  
In our battles, You are near,  
God of glory, calm our fear.  
Though the enemy may rise,  
We stand beneath Your mighty skies.

Verse 2

Woe to us, they cry in dread,  
With our God, we shall not tread.
