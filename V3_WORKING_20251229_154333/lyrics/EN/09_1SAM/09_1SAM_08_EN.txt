### Title: "A King to Rule Us"

Verse 1  
In the days of old, when Samuel grew weary,  
He called his sons, but they strayed from the way.  
They sought after gold, let judgment grow dreary,  
Turning from truth, their hearts led astray.  

Chorus  
Oh, hear our cry, we long for a leader,  
Like the nations round us, we seek a king.  
But in our hearts, You’re the one we need,  
Lord, reign in our lives, Your praises we sing.  

Verse 2  
The elders gathered, their voices resounding,  
“Give us a king, to lead us in might!”  
But Samuel grieved, for their choice was confounding,  
The Lord spoke to him, “They’ve turned from the Light.”  

Chorus  
Oh, hear our cry, we long for a leader,  
Like the nations round us, we seek a king.  
But in our hearts, You’re the one we need,  
Lord, reign in our lives, Your praises we sing.  

Bridge  
He’ll take from you, and your hearts will be burdened,  
Your sons will serve, and your daughters will toil.  
You’ll cry out in sorrow, your choice will be certain,  
But I am your King, I’ll defend and protect.  

Verse 3  
Yet the people refused, their hearts set on glory,  
“We want to be like them, we will not relent.”  
But God whispered softly, reminding the story,  
“Return to your first love, I am where you’re meant.”  

Chorus  
Oh, hear our cry, we long for a leader,  
Like the nations round us, we seek a king.  
But in our hearts, You’re the one we need,  
Lord, reign in our lives, Your praises we sing.  

Outro  
So lead us, O Lord, in Your truth we abide,  
In battles we face, we will stand by Your side.  
Our hearts are Your home, in You we confide,  
Forever our King, our hope and our guide.
