[TITLE]
Hannah's Cry

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of Shiloh, a heart filled with pain,  
Hannah weeps in silence, calling on Your name.  
With a spirit so bitter, she pours out her soul,  
Longing for a promise, to make her heart whole.  

[Chorus]  
O Lord of Hosts, remember me,  
In my affliction, set my spirit free.  
I will trust in You, my prayer will rise,  
With faith unbroken, I lift my cries.  

[Verse 2]  
Elkanah, faithful, with love in his eyes,  
As he comforts his longing, he hears her sighs.  
“Am I not enough?” he gently inquires,  
Yet the ache in her heart burns like holy fires.  

[Chorus]  
O Lord of Hosts, remember me,  
In my affliction, set my spirit free.  
I will trust in You, my prayer will rise,  
With faith unbroken, I lift my cries.  

[Bridge]  
From the depth of despair, her vow she declares,  
“If You grant me a son, I’ll offer him there.  
No razor shall touch him, he’ll serve You alone,  
In the house of the Lord, he will find his home.”  

[Verse 3]  
With a heart that is hopeful, she waits for the day,  
When the promise fulfilled will take sorrow away.  
And when Samuel comes, in joy she will sing,  
“I have asked of the Lord, my praises will ring!”  

[Chorus]  
O Lord of Hosts, remember me,  
In my affliction, set my spirit free.  
I will trust in You, my prayer will rise,  
With faith unbroken, I lift my cries.  

[Outro]  
So we gather in worship, like Hannah of old,  
With our cries and our hopes, in Your arms we behold.  
O Lord of Hosts, our hearts You embrace,  
In the story of faith, we find our place.
