[TITLE]
United in Christ

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
We are strong, we carry the weak,  
Lifting hearts, in love we speak,  
For Christ did not please Himself,  
He bore our pain, our sorrows felt.  

(Chorus)  
With one voice, we glorify,  
Our God of hope, who hears our cry,  
In patience, we stand, united in grace,  
Together we seek His holy face.  

(Verse 2)  
From the Scriptures, we find our way,  
In trials, His peace will stay,  
Gentiles rejoice, let praises rise,  
For mercy flows from heaven’s skies.  

(Chorus)  
With one voice, we glorify,  
Our God of hope, who hears our cry,  
In patience, we stand, united in grace,  
Together we seek His holy face.  

(Bridge)  
Oh, let us bear each other’s load,  
In the Spirit, love will grow,  
As we walk in faith, hand in hand,  
We’ll share His light across this land.  

(Chorus)  
With one voice, we glorify,  
Our God of hope, who hears our cry,  
In patience, we stand, united in grace,  
Together we seek His holy face.  

(Outro)  
Now may the God of peace abide,  
In every heart, forever guide,  
With joy and hope, our prayer we raise,  
In Christ alone, we find our praise.
