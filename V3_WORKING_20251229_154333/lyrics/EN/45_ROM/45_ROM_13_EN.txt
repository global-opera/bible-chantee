[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Let every soul bow down,  
To the powers God has crowned,  
For His hand is in control,  
In His grace, we find our role.  
Resist not the call of right,  
In His truth, we find our light,  
For rulers guide with His decree,  
Doing good, we shall be free.

Chorus:  
Oh, let us love, let us show,  
In every heart, His mercies flow.  
For love fulfills the law divine,  
In every soul, His light will shine.  
Awake, arise, the dawn is near,  
Cast off darkness, hold Him dear.

Verse 2:  
Render tribute to the just,  
Honor those in whom we trust,  
Fear and love, hand in hand,  
Guided by His perfect plan.  
For love works no ill, we sing,  
In His name, our praises ring,  
Awake from sleep, let hearts ignite,  
In the armor of His light.

Reflect His love in every place.
