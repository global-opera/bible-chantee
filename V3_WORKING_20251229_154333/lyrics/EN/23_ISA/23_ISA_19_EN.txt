[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Behold, the Lord rides on a swift cloud,  
His presence shakes the idols, brings them down.  
In the heart of Egypt, fear takes its hold,  
As brother fights brother, the truth unfolds.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, we cry out in the night,  
In our brokenness, You are the Light.  
You promise healing, You break the chains,  
In the midst of judgment, Your love remains.

Sacrifice and praise, to the Lord, we will rise.  
A highway of hope, where the nations unite,  
Blessed be Egypt, Your people in the light.
