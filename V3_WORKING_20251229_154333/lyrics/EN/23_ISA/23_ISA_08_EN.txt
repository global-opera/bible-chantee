[TITLE]
In the Midst of Darkness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of our fears,  
We hear the echoes of Your Word,  
You call us to behold, to see,  
The signs and wonders You have stirred.  
Mahershalalhashbaz, the name we bear,  
In the midst of trials, we lift our prayer.  

Chorus  
For You are with us, O Immanuel,  
In the storm, we will not dwell.  
Though darkness gather, our hearts will sing,  
Our refuge and strength, our everything.  

Verse 2  
The waters rise, the rivers roar,  
Yet we will stand on holy ground.  
You are the stone that we depend,  
In You, our hope, our lives are found.  
Though nations rage and fear take flight,  
We’ll trust in You, our guiding light.  

Chorus  
For You are with us, O Immanuel,  
In the storm, we will not dwell.  
Though darkness gather, our hearts will sing,  
Our refuge and strength, our everything.  

Bridge  
We will not fear what man may say,  
You are our rock, our firm foundation.  
In every trial, in every way,  
We find our peace in Your salvation.  
To the law and the testimony,  
We look to You, our only plea.  

Chorus  
For You are with us, O Immanuel,  
In the storm, we will not dwell.  
Though darkness gather, our hearts will sing,  
Our refuge and strength, our everything.  

Outro  
So bind up the truth, let it be known,  
In every heart, Your love be shown.  
We are Your signs, Your wonders bright,  
In the midst of darkness, we shine Your light.
