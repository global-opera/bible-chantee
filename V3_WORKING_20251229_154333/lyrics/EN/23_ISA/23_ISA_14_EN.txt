[TITLE]
The Day of His Rest

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O Lord, You show Your mercy,  
To Jacob, chosen, free.  
In the land where strangers gather,  
We find our unity.  
You break the chains of sorrow,  
Lift us from our fear,  
In the day of Your salvation,  
Your presence drawing near.  

Chorus:  
How has the oppressor ceased?  
The golden city falls,  
You reign, O Lord, in glory,  
Your love it breaks our walls.  
From ashes we will rise,  
In You we find our song,  
For in the day of Your great rest,  
We know where we belong.  

Verse 2:  
The staff of wicked rulers,  
You shatter with Your might,  
The nations tremble softly,  
As darkness turns to light.  
The earth begins to echo,  
With joy, a sacred sound,  
The trees rejoice in freedom,  
As hope is all around.

Bridge:  
Your hand is stretched out over all,  
The purpose shall not fail,  
In Zion, You have founded hope,  
Your love will ever prevail.  
No enemy can stand against  
The promise You have made,  
In every heart, Your kingdom comes,  
In every life, displayed.

Outro:  
We’ll trust in Zion’s promise,  
The poor will find their peace,  
In the day of His great rest,  
Our joy will never cease.
