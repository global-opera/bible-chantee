[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains high,  
A banner raised, we lift our cry,  
The Lord of Hosts, He calls us near,  
His mighty ones, let hearts draw near.  
From the heavens, His wrath descends,  
A day of reckoning, the end of sin.  

Chorus  
Howl, oh nations, for the day is near,  
The fierce anger of the Lord we fear.  
He shakes the heavens, the earth will quake,  
In His presence, all hearts will break.  

Verse 2  
The sun is darkened, stars lose their light,  
In pain and sorrow, we tremble in fright.  
The proud will fall, the haughty laid low,  
The beauty of kingdoms turned into woe.  
As Sodom fell, so shall it be,  
A desolation, a prophecy.  

Chorus

Bridge  
But in the midst of the darkened skies,  
A remnant rises, with faithful eyes.  
For though He punishes the wicked’s shame,  
He’ll make the humble call on His name.  
In the chaos, our hope remains,  
For in His mercy, our souls He gains.

Outro  
The day of the Lord, we stand in awe,  
Ready to hear His holy call.  
With trembling hearts, in reverence bow,  
For the glory of the Lord is here, right now.
