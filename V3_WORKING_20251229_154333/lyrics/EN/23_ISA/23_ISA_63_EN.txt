[TITLE]
Mighty to Save

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Who is this coming from Edom,  
With garments stained and bright?  
Glorious in His strength, oh,  
He speaks in righteousness and light.  
Trodden down the winepress,  
Alone He bore our shame,  
In the day of vengeance,  
He calls upon His name.  

Chorus  
Mighty to save, our Redeemer,  
In His love, we are made whole.  
With a heart that knows no anger,  
He will lead us to His fold.  
Oh, His mercies never falter,  
For His lovingkindness stays,  
We will sing of His salvation,  
Join the heavens in His praise.  

Verse 2  
In our affliction, He suffered,  
The angel of His grace drew near.  
He bore us through the valleys,  
With His Spirit, calm and clear.  
But we have strayed and wandered,  
And vexed His holy name,  
Yet still He longs to guide us,  
To restore us once again.

Bridge  
Look down from heaven’s glory,  
And see our hearts, our cry.  
Where is Your zeal, O Father?  
You are our hope on high.  
Though the enemy surrounds us,  
You are our refuge strong,  
Bring us back, O mighty Savior,  
In Your arms, we belong.
