[TITLE]
The Sound of Silence

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
O ships of Tarshish, hear the cry,  
For Tyre lies wasted, no place to abide.  
The merchants weep, their trade undone,  
The sea has spoken, no harvest to come.

[Chorus]  
Be still, O isle, in the shadow of the night,  
The pride of the nations fades from sight.  
The Lord of hosts has purposed this fall,  
To humble the glory, to answer the call.

[Verse 2]  
O Zidon, your strength has been laid bare,  
The waves of the sea whisper secrets of despair.  
Once you stood tall, a city of grace,  
Now the echoes of ruin, a haunting embrace.

[Chorus]  
Be still, O isle, in the shadow of the night,  
The pride of the nations fades from sight.  
The Lord of hosts has purposed this fall,  
To humble the glory, to answer the call.

[Bridge]  
Seventy years, you shall be forgotten,  
But hope will arise from the ashes of sorrow.  
Take up your harp, sing a new song,  
For the Lord will visit, and right every wrong.

[Verse 3]  
O daughter of Tarshish, pass through the land,  
No more strength remains, just a trembling hand.  
Yet in the stillness, a promise unfolds,  
Your treasures will be holy, your story retold.

[Chorus]  
Be still, O isle, in the shadow of the night,  
The pride of the nations fades from sight.  
The Lord of hosts has purposed this fall,  
To humble the glory, to answer the call.

[Outro]  
So howl, O ships, let your voices arise,  
For in the silence, redemption draws nigh.  
The Lord will remember, His mercy will flow,  
From the depths of despair, new life will grow.
