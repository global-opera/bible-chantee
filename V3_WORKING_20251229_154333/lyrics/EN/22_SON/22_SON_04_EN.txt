[TITLE]
Garden of My Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Behold, my love, so fair and bright,  
Your beauty shines, a pure delight.  
Doves’ eyes glisten, like the morning dew,  
In the locks of grace, I find my view.  
Your hair flows down like goats on hills,  
A gentle whisper, my heart it fills.  

Chorus  
Oh, how fair is Your love, my dear,  
Better than wine, I draw You near.  
In the garden of my heart, You reign,  
With every word, I call Your name.  

Verse 2  
Your lips, like scarlet threads entwined,  
Honey sweet, with grace defined.  
Your voice, a melody of calm,  
In every word, I find my balm.  
Your neck, a tower, strong and high,  
In Your embrace, my fears comply.  

Chorus  
Oh, how fair is Your love, my dear,  
Better than wine, I draw You near.  
In the garden of my heart, You reign,  
With every word, I call Your name.  

Bridge  
Awake, O winds, blow through the trees,  
Let the fragrance rise, let my soul be free.  
Come, my beloved, into this place,  
In the fountain of love, I seek Your grace.  

Verse 3  
A spring enclosed, a treasure rare,  
With fruits of joy beyond compare.  
In the orchard where Your spirit flows,  
I find my peace, my heart it knows.  
Until the dawn breaks, shadows flee,  
In Your presence, Lord, I long to be.  

Chorus  
Oh, how fair is Your love, my dear,  
Better than wine, I draw You near.  
In the garden of my heart, You reign,  
With every word, I call Your name.  

Outro  
Behold, my love, You’re all I see,  
In the garden of my heart, forever be.  
With every breath, I worship You,  
My heart, my love, forever true.
