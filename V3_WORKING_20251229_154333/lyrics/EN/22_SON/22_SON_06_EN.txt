[TITLE]
In the Garden of My Beloved

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Where has my Beloved gone, O fairest of all?  
In the silence, I seek Him, He answers my call.  
Down in His garden, among spices so sweet,  
He gathers His lilies, and my heart skips a beat.

Chorus  
I am my Beloved’s, my Beloved is mine,  
In the beauty of His presence, our hearts intertwine.  
Like the dawn breaking forth, like the moon in the night,  
He’s the joy of my soul, my everlasting light.

Verse 2  
You are beautiful, love, as the city of grace,  
Your eyes, like deep waters, I find my place.  
Your hair flows like rivers, your smile shines so bright,  
In the midst of the thorns, you are my delight.

Chorus  
I am my Beloved’s, my Beloved is mine,

Bridge  
Return, O Shulamite, return to my heart,  
In the garden of blessings, never to part.  
What will we see in the love that we share?  
As armies, we stand, in the light of His care.

Outro  
In the garden of my Beloved, I find my peace,  
As we dance through the flowers, our love will not cease.  
I am my Beloved’s, forever I'll be,  
In His arms, in His garden, my heart is set free.
