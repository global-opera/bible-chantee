[TITLE]
Faithful and Bold

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In days of old, the call was clear,  
To serve with love, to lend an ear,  
When needs arose, the hearts did cry,  
The widows' hopes, we can't deny.  
With wisdom sought, we gathered near,  
Chosen ones, the Spirit's cheer.  

Chorus:  
Faithful and bold, we rise in grace,  
United as one, we seek Your face.  
In prayer we stand, in Word we dwell,  
Your kingdom come, our hearts compel.  
Oh, let Your light forever shine,  
In every heart, Your love divine.  

Verse 2:  
Stephen stood, with courage bright,  
Full of power, a beacon of light.  
Though battles raged, his heart held true,  
In trials faced, his faith we knew.  
With hands laid on, the promise came,  
The message spread, in Jesus' name.

Bridge:  
When voices rise against Your Name,  
We stand with hope, we'll share the flame.  
With every heart, our spirits soar,  
For in our weakness, You are our roar.  
We’ll carry forth Your truth and grace,  
With love unyielding, we’ll seek Your face.
