[TITLE] Faithful in the Fire

[Verse 1]  
We were lost, tossed on the sea,  
In the storm, You rescued me.  
On Melita’s shore, Your grace was found,  
In the warmth of kindness all around.  
Though the viper strikes, I will stand tall,  
For in Your arms, I will not fall.  

[Chorus]  
Shake off the fear, shake off the pain,  
Your love surrounds me through the rain.  
You’re my healer, my hope, my song,  
In every trial, You make me strong.  
I will rise, I will proclaim,  
Your Kingdom come, forever reign.  

[Verse 2]  
Through the dark, I see Your light,  
With every step, You guide me right.  
From prison chains to the open door,  
You bring the lost to seek You more.  
With every heartbeat, I will declare,  
Your mighty works, Your endless care.
