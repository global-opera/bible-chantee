[TITLE]

[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Fear and Faith

[Verse 1]  
In the light of Your truth, we gather here,  
With hearts open wide, we draw near.  
Though shadows may fall, we stand in grace,  
With holy boldness, we seek Your face.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Ananias, oh, we hear Your call,  
To trust in the Lord, to give it all.  
No more holding back, no more disguise,  
We lay our burdens at Your feet, arise!

[Chorus]  
For we ought to obey, our voices proclaim,  
In the name of Jesus, we lift up His name.  
Fear may surround us, but faith is our song,  
With joy in our hearts, we’ll carry on strong.  

[Verse 2]  
From the depths of despair, You bring us light,  
In chains we are freed, our spirits take flight.  
The angels surround, in the night they guide,  
With courage, we stand, with You by our side.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Peter and the apostles, they taught with fire,  
Their hearts full of grace, ignited desire.  
In every temple, in every home,  
We share the good news, and never roam!  

[Chorus]  
For we ought to obey, our voices proclaim,  
In the name of Jesus, we lift up His name.  
Fear may surround us, but faith is our song,  
With joy in our hearts, we’ll carry on strong.  

[Bridge]  
Oh, let us not fear the trials we face,  
For in our weakness, You pour out Your grace.  
Revive us, renew us, ignite our hearts,  
In the mission You’ve set, we’ll play our parts.  

[Chorus]  
For we ought to obey, our voices proclaim,  
In the name of Jesus, we lift up His name.  
Fear may surround us, but faith is our song,  
With joy in our hearts, we’ll carry on strong.  

[Outro]  
Daily we rise, with hope in our eyes,  
Sharing Your love, to the ends of the skies.  
In every heartbeat, Your Spirit we’ll find,  
Together in unity, one heart and one mind.
