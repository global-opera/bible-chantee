[TITLE]
By His Name We Stand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
As they spoke unto the people,  
Boldly sharing truth divine,  
The priests and rulers gathered,  
Threatened to silence the light.  
But Peter, filled with power,  
Stood with courage in his heart,  
Declaring Christ, our Savior,  
Through Him, we will never part.  

Chorus  
By the name of Jesus, we are whole,  
In His love, He makes us bold.  
No other name can save our souls,  
In His grace, we find our hope.  
Oh, we will speak of all we've seen,  
With one voice, we lift our dreams,  
By His name, we stand redeemed,  
Forever in His victory!  

Verse 2  
The rulers tried to silence,  
The wonders they could not deny.  
A healing that was witnessed,  
By thousands who believed the cry.  
"Grant us boldness, Lord," we pray,  
To share Your love, to light the way,  
With signs and wonders on display,  
In Jesus’ name, we rise today.
