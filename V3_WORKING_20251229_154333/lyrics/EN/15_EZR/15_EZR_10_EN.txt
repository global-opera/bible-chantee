[TITLE]
A Covenant of Our Hearts

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, Ezra cried,  
With a heart torn in sorrow, he fell at Your side.  
We trembled in the rain, our spirits laid bare,  
Confessing our wrongs, lifting burdens in prayer.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, we return, with hope in our hearts,  
To turn from our ways, and make a new start.  
We come as Your people, in unity we stand,  
To renew our covenant, guided by Your hand.  

Verse 2  
In the streets of Jerusalem, we gathered as one,  
With tears in our eyes, we sought what’s undone.  
"Let us put away all that leads us astray,  
For Your grace is our strength, guiding us in the way."  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, we return, with hope in our hearts,  
To turn from our ways, and make a new start.  
We come as Your people, in unity we stand,  
To renew our covenant, guided by Your hand.  

Bridge  
With courage we rise, to follow Your call,  
In the shadow of our sins, still You love us through all.  
We’ll stand in the truth, with our hearts intertwined,  
For in You, Lord, a new life we will find.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, we return, with hope in our hearts,  
To turn from our ways, and make a new start.  
We come as Your people, in unity we stand,  
To renew our covenant, guided by Your hand.  

Outro  
So we lift up our voices, in reverence we sing,  
To the God of our fathers, our Savior, our King.  
In the light of Your mercy, we’ll walk in Your ways,  
Forever we'll honor You, for all of our days.
