[TITLE]
By Your Hand, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From the land of sorrow, we rise anew,  
Gathered by Your promise, Lord, we come to You.  
Through the river of Ahava, we seek Your face,  
In our hearts, O Father, fill this sacred space.  

Chorus  
By Your hand, O Lord, we are led,  
In the trials and the battles, we will not dread.  
For Your power and Your love will guide our way,  
With faith unshaken, we will seek and pray.  

Verse 2  
We proclaim a fast, and our hearts are bare,  
Seeking Your direction, Lord, we lift our prayer.  
With the weight of silver and the gold in hand,  
We are called as vessels, to fulfill Your plan.  

Chorus  
By Your hand, O Lord, we are led,  
In the trials and the battles, we will not dread.  
For Your power and Your love will guide our way,  
With faith unshaken, we will seek and pray.  

Bridge  
As we move toward the promise, we trust in You,  
For the enemy shall falter, with hearts brave and true.  
In unity we gather, our voices rise in song,  
With the hand of God upon us, we will carry on.  

Chorus  
By Your hand, O Lord, we are led,  
In the trials and the battles, we will not dread.  
For Your power and Your love will guide our way,

Outro  
So here we stand together, in Your grace we dwell,  
With the strength of our God, all will be well.  
By Your hand, O Lord, we journey on,  
In the light of Your glory, forever we belong.
