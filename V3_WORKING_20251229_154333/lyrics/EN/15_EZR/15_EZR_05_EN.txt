[TITLE]
The House of God Rebuilt

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Judah, the call rang clear,  
Haggai and Zechariah, the prophets near,  
"Rise, O people, this task is grand,  
The house of the Lord, we’ll rebuild by His hand."  

[Chorus]  
With courage, we stand, the eye of God on us,  
Through trials and doubt, in Him we trust.  
Oh, the stones may tremble, the walls may fall,  
But His promise remains, we’ll answer His call.  

[Verse 2]  
Zerubbabel and Jeshua, together they rise,  
With hearts full of faith, they reach for the skies.  
The governor questioned, “Who gave you this right?”  
“We serve the true King, in His name we fight.”  

[Chorus]  
With courage, we stand, the eye of God on us,  
Through trials and doubt, in Him we trust.  
Oh, the stones may tremble, the walls may fall,  
But His promise remains, we’ll answer His call.  

[Bridge]  
From the ashes of sorrow, new life will spring,  
As Cyrus decreed, let the praises ring.  
The vessels of glory, restored to their place,  
In the house of the Lord, we’ll sing of His grace.  

[Verse 3]  
The work may be slow, but our hearts are ablaze,  
For the God of the heavens, we offer our praise.  
Rebuilding the temple, our spirits unite,  
In the shadow of glory, we walk in His light.  

[Chorus]  
With courage, we stand, the eye of God on us,  
Through trials and doubt, in Him we trust.  
Oh, the stones may tremble, the walls may fall,  
But His promise remains, we’ll answer His call.  

[Outro]  
So let us be faithful, with every breath we take,  
Building up His kingdom, for His glory’s sake.  
In the heart of Jerusalem, our voices will soar,  
For the house of our God, forever we’ll build more.
