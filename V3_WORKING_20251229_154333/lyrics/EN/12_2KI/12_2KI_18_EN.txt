[TITLE]
Hezekiah's Trust

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the days of old, when kings would rise,  
Hezekiah stood with faith in his eyes.  
Twenty-five years, a heart so true,  
He sought the Lord, and in Him he grew.  
He broke the chains of idols and sin,  
Called the people back to where life begins.

[Chorus]  
Oh, trust in the Lord, our mighty shield,  
In battles we face, His power revealed.  
No king of the earth can stand in His way,  
Hezekiah’s heart, forever we’ll say.  
With faith unshaken, we lift our praise,  
For the God who delivers, our hearts ablaze.

[Verse 2]  
When the king of Assyria came with a boast,  
Rabshakeh shouted, "Your God is a ghost!"  
But the people stood silent, their hearts in despair,  
For Hezekiah prayed, casting burdens in prayer.  
"Lord, hear our cry, for we trust in Your name,  
Deliver this city, let Your glory reclaim."

[Bridge]  
He sent His angel, the night turned to dawn,  
The enemy fled, in victory we’d drawn.  
With every stronghold that crumbles away,  
We’ll stand in His promise, forever we’ll stay.  
For Hezekiah’s faith, a legacy bright,  
In the face of the storm, we walk in His light.

[Outro]  
So let us remember, as we sing this song,  
In the trials we face, to Him we belong.

In trust and in faith, our hearts will not die.  
With trust in the Lord, our mighty shield,
