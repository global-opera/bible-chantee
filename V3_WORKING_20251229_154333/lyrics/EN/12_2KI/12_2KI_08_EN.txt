[TITLE]
Restoration and Redemption

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
From the depths of despair, a mother cried,  
Her son, once lost, now alive by Your side.  
Elisha spoke, “Arise, take flight,  
For a famine is coming, take refuge tonight.”  
With faith in her heart, she gathered her kin,  
In the land of the Philistines, she found peace within.  

Chorus:  
Restoration comes, when hope seems lost,  
Your hand brings life, no matter the cost.  
In the shadows, You shine, a promise so true,  
Lord, we trust in Your love, for You make all things new.  

Verse 2:  
Seven years passed, with patience she stood,  
Returning to claim all the lost, as she could.  
She cried to the king, “Restore what is mine,”  
And the fruits of the field, in Your grace, they align.  
Gehazi proclaimed, “This is the one,  
The miracle lived, her journey’s begun.”  

Chorus:  
Restoration comes, when hope seems lost,  
Your hand brings life, no matter the cost.  
In the shadows, You shine, a promise so true,  
Lord, we trust in Your love, for You make all things new.  

Bridge:  
Oh, Elisha wept for the fate of the land,  
For the trials ahead, and the wicked man’s hand.  
Yet, through the pain, Your purpose unfolds,  
In the heart of the battle, Your story is told.  
You raise up the humble, You lift up the meek,  
In every dark valley, Your strength we seek.

Outro:  
So we lift up our hands, in surrender we stand,  
For the God who restores, holds our future in His hand.  
With every breath, we proclaim Your name high,  
In the midst of the storm, we will trust and rely.
