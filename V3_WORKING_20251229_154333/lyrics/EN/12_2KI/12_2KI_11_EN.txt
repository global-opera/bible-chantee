[TITLE]
Crowned in the Shadows

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the darkness of despair,  
A mother’s wrath, a kingdom bare,  
Yet a light was born, a child of grace,  
In the hidden place, he found his space.  
Jehosheba's heart, so brave and true,  
Stole away the hope, where no one knew,  
For six long years, in silence he’d stay,  
While shadows loomed, the light held sway.

Chorus  
Crowned in the shadows, a king will arise,  
With the sound of the trumpet, we lift up our cries.  
God save the king, let our voices proclaim,  
In the house of the Lord, we’ll glorify His name!

Verse 2  
Then came the day, the seventh year,  
Jehoiada called, the captains drew near,  
With spears in hand and hearts aligned,  
They gathered 'round, the truth to find.  
“Guard the king with all your might,  
Stand by his side, for he is the light,”  
They placed the crown on a young boy’s head,  
And the people rejoiced, all fear was shed.

Together we stand, as one in His grace,  
United in spirit, we seek His face.
