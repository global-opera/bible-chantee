Verse 1  
In the shadow of the night, when fear surrounds,  
King Hezekiah cries, his heart unbound.  
He rends his clothes, in sackcloth he bows,  
Before the Lord, he lays his troubles down.  
“God of Israel, hear my plea tonight,  
For the enemy blasphemes, and all seems lost in sight.”  

Chorus  
O deliverer, mighty and true,  
You stand with us, in battles we face,  
When the waves crash down, we look to You,  
O God, our refuge, our hiding place.  
Save us, Lord, that all may see,  
You are the One, who sets us free.  

Verse 2  
The letter of despair, he spreads before Your throne,  
“Look upon our plight, we are not alone.  
For the kings of men have fallen to their fate,  
But You, O Lord, are our hope, our strength innate.”  
He hears the promise, “Do not be afraid,  
The Assyrian’s pride shall be repaid.”

Bridge  
In the stillness of the night, Your angel came,  
A hundred thousand fell, never the same.  
You defend our hearts, O Lord, with grace,  
Turning back the enemy, we seek Your face.  
From Jerusalem, a remnant shall rise,  
To bear fruit and glory, we lift our eyes.
