[TITLE]
A Heart That Humbles

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of ancient kings,  
Josiah rose, a light to bring,  
At eight years old, he took the throne,  
With a heart for God, he stood alone.  
He walked the path of David’s grace,  
In every step, he sought Your face,  
No turning left, no turning right,  
He led his people into light.

Chorus  
Oh, a heart that humbles,  
A spirit that seeks,  
In the house of the Lord,  
Your voice speaks.  
Revive us, O God,  
In Your truth we stand,  
With open hands,  
We trust Your plan.

Verse 2  
In the temple’s silence, they found the scroll,  
The words of life, the truth to console.  
Shaphan read, and the king did weep,  
For their fathers’ ways had led them deep.  
He rent his clothes, his heart was bare,  
He sought Your will, he cried in prayer.  
“Great is Your wrath, O Lord,” he cried,  
“Turn from our sin, be our guide.”

Chorus  
Oh, a heart that humbles,  
A spirit that seeks,  
In the house of the Lord,  
Your voice speaks.  
Revive us, O God,  
In Your truth we stand,  
With open hands,  
We trust Your plan.

Bridge  
And You heard his cry,  
You saw his tears fall,  
“For the heart that is tender,  
I will answer the call.”  
“Gather him in peace,  
He shall not see the night,  
For a humble heart will shine,  
In Your glorious light.”

Chorus

Outro  
So lead us, O King,  
In Your ways we strive,  
With hearts that are humble,  
In You, we’re alive.  
Oh, Lord, we gather,  
Our voices we raise,  
For a heart that humbles,  
Will sing Your praise.
