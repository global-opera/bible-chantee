Verse 1  
In the land where shadows fall,  
Seventy sons in Samaria's hall,  
Jehu rose with a heart ablaze,  
To fulfill the word of God’s ways.  
He wrote a letter, his voice rang clear,  
A call to the rulers, a sound they could fear.  
“Choose your king, but know this truth,  
For the Lord’s decree shall break the youth.”

Chorus  
Oh, God of power, God of might,  
You stand with those who seek the light.  
With zeal for Your name, we will arise,  
In the battle, our hearts will cry.  
You’ve spoken, Your word will stand,  
We trust in You, we take our stand.

Verse 2  
Fear gripped their hearts, a tremor of doubt,  
Two kings had fallen, what was this about?  
Yet Jehu led with a righteous call,  
“Bring forth the worshippers, let none stand tall.”  
In the house of Baal, they gathered near,  
But Jehu’s heart was steady, his vision clear.  
With every sacrifice, he made a stand,  
To break the chains and reclaim the land.
