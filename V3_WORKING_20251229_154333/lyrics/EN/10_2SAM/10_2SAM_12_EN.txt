[TITLE]
The Heart of a King

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In a kingdom where riches reign,  
Two men dwelled in the same domain,  
One was rich, with flocks untold,  
The other poor, with a heart of gold.  
He loved his lamb, so gentle, so dear,  
A daughter in spirit, forever near.  
But a traveler came, seeking a feast,  
The rich man took what was not his, a thief at least.  

(Chorus)  
Oh Lord, have mercy on our hearts,  
In our weakness, let grace impart.  
For we are lost without Your light,  
Guide us back to what is right.  
Restore our souls, renew our ways,  
In the shadow of Your grace, we praise.  

(Verse 2)  
David's anger burned like fire,  
Against the man, the thief, the liar.  
Nathan spoke, "You are the one,  
Your sin has darkened the light of the sun."  
“You’ve taken life, despised the call,  
Yet still, God hears your desperate fall.”  
In brokenness, the king did weep,  
For the weight of sin, his soul did keep.  

(Chorus)

(Bridge)  
From ashes to beauty, You redeem,  
In our sorrow, You reign supreme.  
Though the child may not return,  
In Your arms, our hearts still yearn.  
You raise us up from deepest night,  
With love that shines, our guiding light.  

(Verse 3)  
He washed away his grief, stood tall,  
Into the house of the Lord, he called.  
With every breath, he chose to sing,  
In the face of loss, our praises ring.  
For in the trials, Your love remains,  
Through every storm, through all the pains.  
A son was born; the promise fulfilled,  
In the heart of a king, Your will instilled.

(Outro)  
In the heart of a king, we find our song,  
In Your love, Lord, we forever belong.  
With every trial, Your grace we see,  
Oh Lord, our hearts, forever free.
