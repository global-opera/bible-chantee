[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of Gilboa, the battle raged on,  
Saul and Jonathan, oh how they shone,  
Yet a messenger came with a heart full of dread,  
With torn clothes and earth upon his head.  

Chorus:  
Oh, mourn for the mighty, who fell by the sword,  
In the silence of sorrow, we lift up our Lord.  
Let not the proud triumph, let not their voices rise,  
For the beauty of Israel has fallen from the skies.  

Verse 2:  
“Tell me, my brother, how went the great fight?”  
He spoke of their courage, their strength, and their might.  
But anguish had seized them, the end was at hand,  
And the crown of the king slipped from his command.  

Chorus:  
Oh, mourn for the mighty, who fell by the sword,

Bridge:  
Jonathan, dear brother, your love was so true,  
In the depths of our sorrow, we remember you.  
Though the weapons of war have perished in vain,  
Your bond will sustain us through heartache and pain.

Outro:  
So we gather in mourning, with hearts that are raw,  
For the ones who have fought, who stood firm in the law.  
In the echoes of battle, their legacy lives,  
In the love of our Savior, our hearts He forgives.  
How the mighty have fallen, let their story be told,  
In the arms of our Father, their spirits unfold.
