[TITLE]
Return of the King

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the battle, the king weeps alone,  
For his son, Absalom, the heart he called his own.  
Victory turned to mourning, as silence fills the air,  
The people hid in sorrow, their hearts weighed down with care.  

(Chorus)  
O my son, O my son, the king's lamenting cry,  
Yet the heart of Judah longs to see their king arise.  
Bring him back, O bring him back, let the kingdom sing,  
For in the pain of loss, we still await our King.  

(Verse 2)  
Joab spoke with wisdom, “Lift your head, arise!  
For the lives of all your people, you must see with new eyes.  
Though the grief runs deep, remember who you are,  
The one who saved us from the night, our shining morning star.”  

(Chorus)  
O my son, O my son, the king's lamenting cry,  
Yet the heart of Judah longs to see their king arise.  
Bring him back, O bring him back, let the kingdom sing,  
For in the pain of loss, we still await our King.  

(Bridge)  
O Shimei, you came in shame, yet grace has made you free,  
For mercy flows like rivers, in the heart of our decree.  
Mephibosheth, in faithfulness, you sought the king's embrace,  
With every step to Jordan, we find our rightful place.  

(Verse 3)  
Barzillai, wise and aged, chose to bless the way,  
In every act of kindness, the heart of God displayed.  
So let us rise together, as one in faith we stand,  
For the king has come to gather, united hand in hand.  

(Chorus)  
O my son, O my son, the king's lamenting cry,  
Yet the heart of Judah longs to see their king arise.  
Bring him back, O bring him back, let the kingdom sing,

(Outro)  
Return, O King, return to us, let joy and peace abound,  
In every heart, a song of hope, let your glory resound.  
For though we face the valleys, and shadows may draw near,  
Our King is ever with us, and we have no fear.  
O my son, O my King, forever we will sing.
