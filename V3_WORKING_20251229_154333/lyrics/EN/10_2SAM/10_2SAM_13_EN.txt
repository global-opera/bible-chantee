[TITLE]
Shattered Promises

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the halls of David's throne,  
A tale of love turned bitter,  
Amnon's heart, a raging storm,  
For Tamar, his sister, he withered.  
But darkness whispered through a friend,  
A cunning scheme to break the bond,  
In shadows’ grip, the heart would bend,  
A love that led to sorrow's song.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we cry, our hearts in pain,  
For love that falters, for trust that’s slain.  
Guide us through the shadows, Lord,  
Restore our hearts, with Your mighty sword.  

Verse 2  
Tamar came with hope to share,  
To bring him comfort, bake him bread.  
But lust's cruel hand would lead to despair,  
A heart once pure, now filled with dread.  
"Do not force me, brother, hear my plea,  
For shame will follow, a curse unbound."  
Yet Amnon’s rage would set her free,  
In silence, broken, she was drowned.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we cry, our hearts in pain,  
For love that falters, for trust that’s slain.  
Guide us through the shadows, Lord,  
Restore our hearts, with Your mighty sword.  

Bridge  
We mourn for Tamar, her ashes weep,  
In the silence, wounds run deep.  
Yet in our hearts, Your love remains,  
To heal the broken, to break the chains.  
Absalom’s fury, a brother’s wrath,  
In the wake of sin, we find Your path.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we cry, our hearts in pain,  
For love that falters, for trust that’s slain.  
Guide us through the shadows, Lord,  
Restore our hearts, with Your mighty sword.  

Outro  
In every story, Your grace unfolds,  
From shattered promises, new life beholds.  
With every tear, we seek Your face,  
In our brokenness, we find Your grace.  
Amen.
