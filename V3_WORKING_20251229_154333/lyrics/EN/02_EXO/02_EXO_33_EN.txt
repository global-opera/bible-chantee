[TITLE]
Show Me Your Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
You called to Moses from the mountaintop,  
“Lead My people to the land I promised,  
A place of milk and honey, flowing free,  
But know, My presence must go with thee.”  
They mourned in silence, stripped their pride away,  
Seeking Your face, Lord, in the light of day.  

Chorus  
Show me Your glory, pass before my eyes,  
Let me behold You, in the clouded skies.  
You are my refuge, my heart's desire,  
In Your embrace, Lord, I’m set on fire.  

Verse 2  
Moses stood trembling, as You spoke so kind,  
“I know you by name, in grace you will find.  
Lead this nation, for they are My own,  
With You, I’ll walk, and make Your glory known.”  
The pillar of cloud descended, bright and bold,  
In Your presence, Lord, our hearts unfold.  

Chorus  
Show me Your glory, pass before my eyes,  
Let me behold You, in the clouded skies.  
You are my refuge, my heart's desire,  
In Your embrace, Lord, I’m set on fire.  

Bridge  
You said, “Stand on the rock, let My glory shine,  
I’ll cover you gently, My hand will be thine.  
Though you cannot see, you’ll feel My grace,  
In the cleft of the rock, you’ll find your place.”  

Chorus  
Show me Your glory, pass before my eyes,  
Let me behold You, in the clouded skies.  
You are my refuge, my heart's desire,  
In Your embrace, Lord, I’m set on fire.  

Outro  
As we stand in reverence, hearts open wide,  
We seek Your presence, our faithful guide.  
With every step forward, we trust in Your way,  
In the shadow of Your glory, we long to stay.
