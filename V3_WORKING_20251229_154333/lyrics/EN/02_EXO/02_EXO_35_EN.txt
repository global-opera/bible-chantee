[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Gathered in His presence, we come with open hands,  
The words of our Creator, the promise of His plans.  
Six days we toil and labor, but on the seventh day,  
We rest in holy silence, to worship and to pray.  

Chorus  
With a willing heart, we bring our gifts,  
To the altar of our God, our love uplifts.  
With gold and silver, threads of scarlet hue,  
For His house of glory, we give all that is true.  

Verse 2  
From every heart that stirs, we offer what we own,  
Fine linen and the spices, to build the sacred throne.  
In wisdom He has called us, to craft with skillful hands,  
To honor Him in beauty, as He leads our plans.

Bridge  
Oh, Bezaleel, filled with Spirit's grace,  
In every act of service, we find our place.  
Together we are woven, in the fabric of His love,  
As we build a holy dwelling, guided from above.

Outro  
So here we stand before You, with hearts forever stirred,  
Crafting with devotion, in each and every word.  
For in our acts of kindness, Your glory we proclaim,  
In this sacred gathering, we exalt Your name.
