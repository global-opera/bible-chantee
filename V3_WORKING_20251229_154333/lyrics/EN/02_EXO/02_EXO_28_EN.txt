[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Garments of Glory

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
You called us to this holy place,  
Aaron and his sons, chosen with care,  
To bear the names of Your people in prayer.  
With wisdom and skill, let the garments be spun,  
Threads of gold and colors, a work to be done.

[Chorus]  
Holy, holy, Lord of all,  
In the beauty of Your calling, we stand tall.  
With the names of Israel, on our hearts we bear,  
In the presence of the Lord, our lives laid bare.  
Garments of glory, woven with love,  
A tapestry of mercy, sent from above.

[Verse 2]  
Breastplate of judgment, stones shining bright,  
Each name a promise, a beacon of light.  
Pomegranates and bells on the robe’s gentle hem,  
A sound of our worship, a hymn to ascend.  
Oh, the weight of iniquity, lifted away,  
As we enter the holy, where Your presence stays.

[Chorus]  
Holy, holy, Lord of all,

[Bridge]  
Anointed and consecrated, we rise to serve,  
In every act of love, we find our worth.  
With every prayer, every song,  
We carry Your heart, where we belong.  
Oh, let us be vessels, pure and true,  
In the garments of glory, we worship You.

[Chorus]

[Outro]  
In the silence of the night, let our voices rise,  
With the hearts of the faithful, we reach for the skies.  
Clothed in Your promise, forever we’ll stand,  
In the garments of glory, held in Your hand.
