[TITLE]
Restoration in Your Hands

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of our hearts, we’ve wandered far away,  
A thief in the night, oh Lord, we’ve lost our way.  
Yet You call us to restore, to mend what’s been torn,  
To bring back the lost, as each new day is born.  

Chorus  
Restoration in Your hands, Lord,  
You make all things new.  
In the depths of our despair,  
Your grace will see us through.  
Let our hearts be filled with mercy,  
As we walk in Your light,  
In Your love, we find redemption,  
Turning darkness into bright.  

Verse 2  
When the fire breaks and consumes, leaving ashes behind,  
You remind us of the hope, in the pain we shall find.  
For every loss that we endure, You promise to restore,  
With the best of our fields, we’ll give to You once more.

Bridge  
Oh, how You hear the cries, of the widow and the weak,  
You call us to remember, the promises we seek.

With kindness and compassion, in every battle fought.

With every breath we take,  
We’ll praise You, Great I AM.
