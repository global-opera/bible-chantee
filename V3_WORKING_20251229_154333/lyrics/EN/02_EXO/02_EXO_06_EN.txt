[TITLE]
I Am the LORD

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the night,  
Under burdens, hearts in fright,  
You have heard our weary cries,  
You remember every sigh.  
Oh, deliverer, we call on You,  
With a strong hand, make all things new.  
From the chains that hold us tight,  
Bring us forth into Your light.

Chorus  
I am the LORD, I will set you free,  
With a mighty hand, I’ll bring you to Me.  
I am the promise made to Abraham,  
In the land of Canaan, you shall stand.  
Fear not, for I am here,  
Your God will wipe away every tear.  
I am the LORD, your heart's desire,  
In the midst of trials, I’ll lift you higher.

Verse 2  
Moses stood with trembling heart,  
Yet You spoke, “You’ll play your part.”  
To Pharaoh, boldly proclaim,  
“I am the LORD, I know your name.”  
For the groans of Israel rise,  
You remember every sigh.  
Through the darkness, through the pain,  
You will break the bonds of shame.

Bridge  
From the past, I bring you forth,  
To a future filled with worth.  
In my covenant, trust in Me,  
I will lead you to victory.  
Through the waters and the flame,  
I will never be the same.  
I am with you every day,  
In My love, you’ll find the way.
