Verse 1  
In the wilderness, Jethro came,  
With a heart full of praise for the God who reigns.  
He heard of the wonders, the signs of His hand,  
How the Lord delivered His people from the sand.  

Chorus  
Blessed be the Lord, who hears our cry,  
From the depths of despair, He lifts us high.  
In every trial, He’s our guiding light,  
Our strength, our refuge, through the darkest night.
