[TITLE]
On Eagles' Wings

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the wilderness, we stand in awe,  
Before the mount, we hear Your call.  
You brought us forth from the land of chains,  
In the shadows of fire, Your glory reigns.  

Chorus  
On eagles' wings, You carried us high,  
A peculiar treasure, beneath the sky.  
We’re a kingdom of priests, Your holy nation,  
With hearts awakened, in adoration.  

Verse 2  
Moses climbed the heights, to meet with You,  
In the thick cloud, Your voice rings true.  
You said, "Obey, keep my covenant near,"  
We tremble in reverence, for You are here.  

Chorus  
On eagles' wings, You carried us high,  
A peculiar treasure, beneath the sky.  
We’re a kingdom of priests, Your holy nation,  
With hearts awakened, in adoration.  

Bridge  
Thunder and lightning, the mountain quakes,  
In Your presence, our fears forsake.  
Prepare our hearts, let us draw near,  
In Your holiness, we lose all fear.  

Verse 3  
From Sinai’s peak, Your glory descends,  
A promise renewed, Your love never ends.  
We gather as one, our voices proclaim,  
All that You’ve spoken, we’ll honor Your name.  

Chorus  
On eagles' wings, You carried us high,  
A peculiar treasure, beneath the sky.  
We’re a kingdom of priests, Your holy nation,  
With hearts awakened, in adoration.  

Outro  
In the wilderness, we stand in grace,  
Forever Your people, we seek Your face.  
On eagles' wings, we rise and sing,  
To You, our Savior, our offering.
