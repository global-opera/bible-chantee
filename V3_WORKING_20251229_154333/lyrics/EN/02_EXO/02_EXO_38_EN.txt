(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the tabernacle, we gather near,  
With hearts laid bare, our burdens clear,  
The altar built with hands of grace,  
Of shittim wood, a sacred place.  

(Chorus)  
Come, let us bring our offerings,  
To the altar of our praise,  
In the fire of Your presence,  
Lord, we seek Your holy ways.
