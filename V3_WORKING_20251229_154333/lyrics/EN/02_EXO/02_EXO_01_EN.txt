[TITLE]
In the Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of Egypt, the children came,  
Jacob's sons united, calling out Your name.  
Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah stand,  
In the heart of the promise, the touch of Your hand.  
Though a new king arose, who knew not the past,  
He saw their strength growing, a nation amassed.

Chorus:  
Oh, we rise in Your power,  
Through the trials we face,  
In the depths of the struggle,  
We find Your embrace.  
You are with us, O God,  
In the midst of our pain,  
Through the fire, we’ll flourish,  
In Your love, we remain.

Verse 2:  
Taskmasters and burdens, they laid on our backs,  
But the more they afflict us, the more we won’t crack.  
In the bitterness of bondage, Your promise shines bright,  
The midwives who feared You, chose to stand for the right.  
They saved the sons of promise, by faith they stood tall,  
For Your hand was upon them, they trusted through it all.

Chorus:  
Oh, we rise in Your power,  
Through the trials we face,

We find Your embrace.

Bridge:  
So we sing of Your faithfulness,  
In the darkest of days,  
Even when shadows surround us,  
We lift up our praise.  
For You are the God who delivers,  
Your promises stand,  
In the land of our waiting,  
We trust in Your plan.

Outro:  
In the land of promise, we’ll stand and proclaim,  
With hearts full of worship, we’ll glorify Your name.  
For You are our Savior, our strength and our song,  
In the midst of the journey, with You we belong.
