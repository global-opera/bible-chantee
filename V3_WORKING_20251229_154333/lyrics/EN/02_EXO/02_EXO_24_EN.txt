[TITLE]
Come Up to the Mountain

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Come up to the mountain, O people of the Lord,  
Gather at His altar, where grace and truth accord.  
Moses heard the calling, the voice that spoke so clear,  
“Draw near, O my children, I am forever near.”  

Chorus  
We will do all You say, we’ll walk in Your way,  
By the blood of the covenant, we stand here today.  
With one voice we declare, our hearts we prepare,  
To worship You, O God, in awe and in prayer.  

Verse 2  
Twelve pillars standing strong, a testament of grace,  
The elders came together, to seek His holy face.  
And under sapphire skies, the glory shone so bright,  
They saw the God of Israel, a vision of pure light.  

Chorus  
We will do all You say, we’ll walk in Your way,  
By the blood of the covenant, we stand here today.  
With one voice we declare, our hearts we prepare,  
To worship You, O God, in awe and in prayer.  

Bridge  
In the midst of the cloud, His presence we behold,  
Forty days and nights, in the story to be told.  
Come up, O my servant, receive My sacred law,  
In the fire of Your glory, we stand in holy awe.  

Chorus  
We will do all You say, we’ll walk in Your way,  
By the blood of the covenant, we stand here today.  
With one voice we declare, our hearts we prepare,  
To worship You, O God, in awe and in prayer.  

Outro  
So come up to the mountain, with hearts that long to see,  
The wonders of Your promise, fulfilled in You and me.  
Together in Your presence, as one we lift our song,  
In the light of Your glory, Lord, we will carry on.
