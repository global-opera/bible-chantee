[TITLE]
A Brother Beloved

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the bonds of love we gather,  
In the grace of Christ we stand,  
Thankful hearts lift up our praises,  
For the work of His strong hand.  
Through the trials, through the shadows,  
Faithful friends, we are not alone,  
In this house of hope and mercy,  
We are family, His own.

Chorus  
Oh, receive one another,  
As Christ has welcomed me,  
In the love of the Father,  
We find our unity.  
Brother, sister, hand in hand,  
Together we will rise and stand,  
In the joy of our salvation,  
Bound in grace, forever free.

Verse 2  
Though we falter, though we wander,  
In our hearts, His love remains,  
For in weakness, He is stronger,  
In our struggles, He sustains.  
Let our faith be ever shining,  
Let His mercy guide our way,  
For the bowels of saints are refreshed,  
In the love we share today.

Bridge  
Let us hold each other closely,  
Bear the burdens, share the pain,  
In the name of our Redeemer,  
All is lost, yet all is gained.  
If we owe, let love repay us,  
In His kindness, we are whole,  
For in Christ, we are one body,  
With His Spirit in our souls.
