[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, we seek Your face,  
In the shadows of despair,  
As enemies draw near,  
We lift our hearts in prayer.  
Your word, a light in darkness,  
A promise we hold tight,  
With outstretched hands, You fight for us,  
In Your mercy, we find light.

Chorus  
You set before us life and death,  
In Your hands, we place our breath.  
We trust in You, our mighty King,  
In the storm, our voices sing.  
Your judgment, fierce, yet full of grace,  
We run to You, our hiding place.

Verse 2  
Though pestilence and famine rise,  
And shadows seek to claim our soul,  
You call us forth, You bid us come,  
To find our refuge, to be whole.  
O house of David, hear the call,  
Execute justice, stand for the weak,  
In the valley low, we hear Your voice,  
Your mighty hand, we dare to seek.
