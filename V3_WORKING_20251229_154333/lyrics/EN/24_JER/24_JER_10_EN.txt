[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear, O Israel, the voice of the Lord,  
Do not fear the signs that the world has stored.  
For the ways of the heathen are empty and vain,  
Their idols are lifeless, they bring only pain.  

Chorus  
For none is like You, O King of the nations,  
Great is Your name, filled with mighty foundations.  
You made the heavens, the earth by Your hand,  
We stand in Your presence, forever we’ll stand.  

Verse 2  
They cut down the trees, the work of their hands,  
Decked with silver and gold, on which darkness stands.  
But You are the living, the everlasting King,  
When You speak, the earth trembles, creation will sing.

Bridge  
O Lord, correct me, but not in Your wrath,  
Guide me in mercy, lead me in Your path.  
Pour out Your fury on those who don’t call,  
For we are Your people, You’re our all in all.

Outro  
O true God, our refuge, our fortress and guide,  
In the shadow of Your wings, forever we’ll bide.  
In the stillness, we worship, in the storm, we believe,  
For in You, O our Savior, we find all we need.
