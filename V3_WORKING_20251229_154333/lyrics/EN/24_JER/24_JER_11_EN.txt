[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Hear the words of the covenant,  
Spoken from the throne above,  
“Obey My voice, follow My ways,  
For I am your God, and you are My love.”  
From the land of Egypt, I brought you forth,  
Out of the furnace, I set you free,  
Yet you turned back, to the ways of your past,  
Chasing shadows, not seeing Me.

Chorus:  
Oh, Israel, why do you stray?  
Your gods of stone cannot save the day.  
In the streets of Jerusalem, there you cry,  
But only in My arms will you truly find life.  
Hear the call of the covenant,  
Turn your hearts back to Me,  
For I am faithful, I am true,  
I will lead you to victory.

Verse 2:  
I rose early to plead for your soul,  
But you heeded not the sound of My call.  
In the cities, you’ve set up your shame,  
Burning incense, forgetting My name.  
Yet I see you, beloved, like a tree of green,  
With fruit so good, but broken by sin.  
I will judge righteously, I will make it right,  
For I am the light that breaks through the night.

Chorus:  
Oh, Judah, why do you stray?  
Your gods of stone cannot save the day.  
In the streets of Jerusalem, there you cry,  
But only in My arms will you truly find life.  
Hear the call of the covenant,  
Turn your hearts back to Me,  
For I am faithful, I am true,  
I will lead you to victory.

Bridge:  
Come, return to the One who redeems,  
Lay down your burdens, embrace the dream.  
For every chain shall break in My name,  
And the lost will gather, never the same.  
I will pour out My Spirit, renew your heart,  
In the midst of the silence, hear My voice start.  
Let the heavens resound, let the earth proclaim,  
That I am the Lord, forever the same.

Chorus:  
Oh, Israel, why do you stray?  
Your gods of stone cannot save the day.  
In the streets of Jerusalem, there you cry,  
But only in My arms will you truly find life.  
Hear the call of the covenant,  
Turn your hearts back to Me,  
For I am faithful, I am true,  
I will lead you to victory.

Outro:  
So come, O people, return to the light,  
In the arms of your Father, find peace in the night.  
I am your God, and you are My own,  
In the covenant of love, you are never alone.
