[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the silence, voices rise,  
Pashur strikes with heavy hand,  
But Your word, O Lord, ignites,  
A fire deep within, I stand.  
Though the world may mock and jeer,  
Your truth, O God, I will declare,  
In the shadows, You are near,  
With a mighty heart, I bear.

(Chorus)  
Sing unto the Lord, O my soul,  
For You have set the captives free,  
In sorrow's night, Your light shines bold,  
With every tear, You comfort me.  
Though the trials come, I will not fear,  
Your justice reigns, oh, mighty King,  
In the face of pain, You draw me near,  
My heart will rise, Your praises sing.

(Verse 2)  
They whisper lies, they plot and scheme,  
Yet in the storm, I find my peace,  
For You, O Lord, are my strong beam,  
And in Your love, my fears release.  
Though the day seems dark and cursed,  
I trust in You, my soul's delight,  
In every trial, Lord, I’ll be immersed,  
For in Your arms, I find my fight.

(Bridge)  
Lift Your voice, declare His might,  
In the darkest hours, He is light,  
With fire in my bones, I will proclaim,  
The glory of His holy name.  
Though I walk through valleys deep,  
Your faithful love, my heart will keep,  
In every battle, Lord, I stand,  
With You beside me, hand in hand.
