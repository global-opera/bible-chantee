[TITLE]
Woe to Moab

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Against the land of Moab, the Lord has spoken,  
Woe unto you, Nebo, your pride is broken.  
In Heshbon, the whispers of evil devised,  
No more praise for the strong, for their glory is disguised.  

Chorus  
Cry out, O Moab, for judgment has come,  
Your cities lie desolate, for the battle is won.  
Flee to the mountains, let your heart take flight,  
For the God of Israel brings forth the night.  

Verse 2  
In Horonaim, the sound of weeping rises,  
Little ones lament as destruction surprises.  
Trust not in treasures, nor in strength of your hand,  
For Chemosh shall falter, the Lord’s mighty plan.  

Chorus  
Cry out, O Moab, for judgment has come,  
Your cities lie desolate, for the battle is won.  
Flee to the mountains, let your heart take flight,  
For the God of Israel brings forth the night.  

Bridge  
How can you stand, when the strong staff is torn?  
The mighty shall tremble, the proud now forlorn.  
Woe to the haughty, the day of reckoning calls,  
In the shadow of sorrow, the pride of Moab falls.  

Chorus  
Cry out, O Moab, for judgment has come,  
Your cities lie desolate, for the battle is won.  
Flee to the mountains, let your heart take flight,  
For the God of Israel brings forth the night.  

Outro  
Yet in the latter days, He will restore,  
The hearts of the broken, the lost He’ll restore.  
Hope for Moab, though the judgment is near,  
For the grace of the Lord will drive out all fear.
