### Title: "In the Depths of Despair"

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the city, where hope has lost its way,  
Your word is like a whisper, guiding through the fray.  
Though the sword and famine linger, and the pestilence draws near,  
You call us to surrender, to cast away our fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, let us rise and follow, to the place You lead us now,  
In the depths of our despair, we will trust and take a vow.  
For You hold our lives, our futures in Your hands,  
Let us go forth to the promise, to the hope that never ends.  

Verse 2  
In a dungeon deep and lonely, where the mire seeks to bind,  
You sent a faithful servant, a light for us to find.  
With rags of old compassion, he lifted up the lost,  
In the midst of our surrender, we will count the cost.  

Chorus

Bridge  
Speak to us, O Lord, let us hear Your call,  
In obedience, we stand, we will not fear the fall.  
Though the world may mock us, and the night be long,  
We will trust in Your promise, for in You we belong.  

Chorus

Outro  
So, we rise from the ashes, with hearts that beat as one,  
In the light of Your glory, our fears will come undone.  
In the depths of our despair, we find strength in Your grace,  
For You are our salvation, our refuge, our safe place.
