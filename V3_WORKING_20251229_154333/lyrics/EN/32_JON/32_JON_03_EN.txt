[TITLE]
Forty Days to Turn

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, Your voice was clear,  
You called to Jonah, spoke the truth we fear.  
“Arise, go forth to Nineveh’s great land,  
Proclaim My word, by My command.”  

Chorus  
Forty days, a warning cry,  
Turn from darkness, lift your eyes.  
With sackcloth and ashes, we seek Your grace,  
Oh Lord, in mercy, our hearts embrace.  

Verse 2  
Jonah walked through the streets so wide,  
Cried aloud, “Repent, let not pride abide.”  
From the king to the least, they fell to pray,  
In humility, they turned, they sought Your way.  

Chorus  
Forty days, a warning cry,  
Turn from darkness, lift your eyes.  
With sackcloth and ashes, we seek Your grace,  
Oh Lord, in mercy, our hearts embrace.  

Bridge  
Who can tell if You will turn,  
From anger fierce, to love we yearn?  
With faith we cry, “Oh God, relent,”  
Let hearts be changed, our lives You’ll mend.  

Chorus  
Forty days, a warning cry,  
Turn from darkness, lift your eyes.  
With sackcloth and ashes, we seek Your grace,  
Oh Lord, in mercy, our hearts embrace.  

Outro  
You saw their works, they turned away,  
From all their sin, they sought Your way.  
Oh God, in mercy, You heard their plea,  
In Your great love, we are set free.
