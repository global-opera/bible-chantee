Moyo Wangu Umeutulia

[Verse 1]  
Bwana, moyo wangu hauna kiburi,  
Macho yangu hayainuki,  
Sijishughulishi na mambo makuu,  
Nafsi yangu, Bwana, inakutafuta.

[Chorus]  
Ee Israeli, umtarajie Bwana,  
Tangu leo, milele,  
Ning'ara na neema Yako,  
Utupe amani, ulinzi wa milele.

Hakika nimeituliza nafsi yangu,

Ee Israeli, umtarajie Bwana,  
Tangu leo, milele,
