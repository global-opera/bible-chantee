[TITLE]
Hold Fast to Hope

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the morning, we gather 'round Your throne,  
Holy brethren, hearts united, in Your mercy we are known.  
You, our High Priest, forever, faithful in Your ways,  
Like Moses, who served You humbly, we lift our voice in praise.

Chorus:  
Oh, let us hold fast to our hope,  
With confidence, we come before You, Lord.  
Today, if we hear Your voice,  
We’ll not harden our hearts, we rejoice!  
In Your love, we find our rest,  
In Your grace, we are truly blessed.

Verse 2:  
You built the house of glory, every heart, a sacred space,  
The works of old remind us, of Your faithfulness and grace.  
Grieved were You with a generation that wandered in their doubt,  
Yet still, You call us gently, to believe and to shout.

With confidence, we come before You, Lord.

Bridge:  
We are partakers of Your promise,  
If we stand firm 'til the end,  
Exhort one another daily,  
In Your word, our hearts will mend.  
No more erring hearts of doubt,  
We will trust in all You say,  
For the path of life You show us,  
Leads us in Your holy way.

Outro:  
So today, hear our hearts,  
In the stillness, we remain,  
Faithful to the calling,  
In Your love, we are sustained.
