### Title: "In the Shadow of Your Promise"

Verse 1  
In the land where wanderers roam,  
Abraham journeyed, far from home,  
With a heart that trembled, filled with fear,  
He spoke of love, but the truth was near.  
“O Lord,” he cried, “They’ll take my life,  
For Sarah’s beauty, my beloved wife.”  
Yet in the night, Your voice did speak,  
To Abimelech, the righteous and meek.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your promise, we find our way,  
You guard our hearts, through night and day.  
Though we may falter, Your grace abounds,  
In the depths of trials, Your love surrounds.  

Verse 2  
You came in dreams, with words so clear,  
“Restore the man, for his prayers I hear.”  
In innocence, he sought to do right,  
Yet Your hand withheld him from the night.  
Abimelech, trembling, called on the wise,  
“What sin is this? Open our eyes!”  
But through the storm, Your mercy shown bright,  
You turned the darkness into light.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your promise, we find our way,  
You guard our hearts, through night and day.  
Though we may falter, Your grace abounds,  
In the depths of trials, Your love surrounds.  

Bridge  
You heal the broken, You mend the strife,  
With every heartbeat, You breathe new life.  
In every journey, where paths are unclear,  
You are our refuge, forever near.  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your promise, we find our way,  
You guard our hearts, through night and day.  
Though we may falter, Your grace abounds,  
In the depths of trials, Your love surrounds.  

Outro  
So we’ll walk in faith, with eyes on the prize,  
For in Your covenant, our hope will rise.  
Together we’ll stand, through valleys and heights,  
In the shadow of Your promise, our hearts ignite.
