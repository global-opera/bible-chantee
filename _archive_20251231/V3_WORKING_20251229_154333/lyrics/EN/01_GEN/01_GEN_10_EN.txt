[TITLE]
Generations of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
From the waters deep, with a promise anew,  
Noah's sons arose, in the light of the truth.  
Shem, Ham, and Japheth, a lineage so grand,  
From the ashes of the flood, they walked upon the land.  

Chorus:  
Generations of grace, woven through time,  
Every nation and tongue, a story divine.  
In the hearts of the lost, Your light will ignite,  
Through the dawn of creation, we rise in Your might.  

Verse 2:  
From Japheth came Gomer, and the shores of the sea,  
The tongues of the nations, each family set free.  
Ham bore mighty Cush, and the cities of old,  
In the shadows of Babel, Your purpose unfolds.  

Chorus:  
Generations of grace, woven through time,  
Every nation and tongue, a story divine.  
In the hearts of the lost, Your light will ignite,  
Through the dawn of creation, we rise in Your might.  

Bridge:  
Shem, the father, of all who believe,  
Eber’s sons in faith, the promise they weave.  
In the division of lands, Your hand was so clear,  
Through the ages and trials, You are always near.  

Verse 3:  
Peleg saw the earth, as the nations were spread,  
Each family in harmony, by Your Spirit led.  
Together we gather, from the corners of earth,  
In the tapestry of love, we proclaim our rebirth.  

Chorus:  
Generations of grace, woven through time,  
Every nation and tongue, a story divine.  
In the hearts of the lost, Your light will ignite,  
Through the dawn of creation, we rise in Your might.  

Outro:  
So let us remember, in unity stand,  
The generations of grace, by Your sovereign hand.  
From the flood to the promise, our hearts will proclaim,  
In the name of our Savior, forever the same.
