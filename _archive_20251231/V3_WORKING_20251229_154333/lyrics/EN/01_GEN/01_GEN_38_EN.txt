[TITLE]
The Call of Redemption

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the night, Judah wandered far,  
Seeking solace from the past, beneath the evening star.  
Met a woman of the land, her name was Shuah bright,  
In the silence of his heart, he lost his way from light.  

Chorus  
But Your grace, O Lord, it covers all our shame,  
In the depths of brokenness, You call us by our name.  
From the ashes of our sin, You weave a tale so grand,  
In Your love, we find our hope, forever we will stand.  

Verse 2  
Er was born, but wickedness took him from the land,  
Onan, too, would not obey, his heart refused to stand.  
Tamar waited, cloaked in grief, a widow’s silent plea,  
Yet in her heart, a promise held, for God’s design to see.

Bridge  
In the darkest hour, when all seems lost,  
You awaken dreams anew, no matter what the cost.  
Through the trials and the tears, Your mercy leads the way,  
In the lineage of our pain, redemption finds its say.  

Verse 3  
Judah saw her in the night, masked in a veil of strife,  
In the hiddenness of sin, a thread of hope for life.  
Two sons born from the sorrow, Pharez and Zarah’s grace,  
In the tapestry of ages, You reveal Your loving face.

Outro  
So we lift our voices high, in praise for all You’ve done,  
For in every story told, Your victory has won.  
We are woven in Your hands, a covenant so true,  
In the call of redemption, our hearts belong to You.
