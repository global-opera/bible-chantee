[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Grace

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In a land of hunger, where hope feels lost,  
A father’s heart is heavy, counting every cost.  
“Go again,” he says, “bring food for our kin,  
But take with you the promise, let our journey begin.”  

[Chorus]  
O Lord, show us mercy, in our darkest night,  
In our need and longing, be our guiding light.  
With hearts that are broken, we come seek Your face,  
In the shadow of grace, we find our place.  

[Verse 2]  
Judah speaks with courage, “Send the lad with me,  
For without him, father, we cannot set free.  
I’ll bear the blame forever, should I fail this task,  
Trust in the Lord’s provision, just this one thing ask.”  

[Chorus]  
O Lord, show us mercy, in our darkest night,  
In our need and longing, be our guiding light.  
With hearts that are broken, we come seek Your face,  
In the shadow of grace, we find our place.  

[Bridge]  
In the house of Joseph, they stood in awe,  
With grace overflowing, like an endless law.  
“Fear not, your God has given treasures in your sack,  
Come and dine together, you’re never turning back.”  

[Verse 3]  
And as they bowed in reverence, their hearts aligned,  
In the tears of reunion, love’s promise defined.  
Oh, heart that yearns for family, in the darkest hour,  
God’s hand is moving, revealing His power.  

[Chorus]  
O Lord, show us mercy, in our darkest night,  
In our need and longing, be our guiding light.  
With hearts that are broken, we come seek Your face,  
In the shadow of grace, we find our place.  

[Outro]  
So we rise in worship, with a spirit so free,  
In the love of the Father, we are meant to be.  
With every step forward, we embrace Your plan,  
In the shadow of grace, we find hope for man.
