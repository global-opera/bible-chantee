[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness after the storm,  
You called out, blessed us, made us warm.  
“Be fruitful, multiply,” Your voice resounds,  
In every heart, Your promise found.  
You gave us life, and all that breathes,  
A sacred bond, in You, we believe.  

Chorus  
Oh, we lift our eyes to the skies,  
The bow of Your covenant, a love that never dies.  
In the clouds, Your grace shines bright,  
Remembering us in every night.  
You are faithful, forever true,  
Lord, we stand in awe of You.  

Verse 2  
You spoke to Noah, a promise clear,  
“No more floods to bring us fear.”  
With every creature, we walk hand in hand,  
In every heart, Your love will stand.  
For You require not just our days,  
But the blood of our lives, we honor and praise.

Bridge  
With Shem, Ham, and Japheth’s call,  
Together we rise, together we fall.  
Cover our shame, with love profound,  
In our weakness, Your strength is found.  
Through the ages, You lead our way,  
In the light of Your promise, we will stay.
