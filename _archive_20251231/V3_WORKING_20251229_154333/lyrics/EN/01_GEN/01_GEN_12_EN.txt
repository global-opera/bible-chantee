[TITLE]
Journey of Blessing

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
You called my name, O Lord, in the night,  
To leave my home, to walk in Your light.  
From the land of my fathers, I rise and I go,  
To a promise unspoken, where Your blessings flow.  

Chorus:  
For I will make you a great nation,  
I will bless you and make your name known.  
In you shall all families find salvation,  
Through your faith, my love will be shown.  

Verse 2:  
In the heart of Canaan, Your voice I will hear,  
You promised the land, my spirit draws near.  
I build an altar, lifting praise to the skies,  
In moments of doubt, You are faithful, arise.  

Chorus:  
For I will make you a great nation,  
I will bless you and make your name known.  
In you shall all families find salvation,  
Through your faith, my love will be shown.  

Bridge:  
Even in famine, when shadows surround,  
I’ll trust in Your promise, Your grace will abound.  
Though trials may come, I will stand firm and true,  
In the depths of despair, Lord, I’ll turn my heart to You.  

Verse 3:  
Though I may falter, and fear may take hold,  
You reveal Your power, Your story unfolds.  
From Egypt’s embrace, You bring me back home,  
In the journey of blessing, I’ll never walk alone.  

Chorus:  
For I will make you a great nation,  
I will bless you and make your name known.  
In you shall all families find salvation,  
Through your faith, my love will be shown.  

Outro:  
So I journey onward, with hope as my guide,  
In the land of promise, Lord, forever abide.  
You called me by name, and I’ll follow Your way,  
In the journey of blessing, I’ll trust You each day.
