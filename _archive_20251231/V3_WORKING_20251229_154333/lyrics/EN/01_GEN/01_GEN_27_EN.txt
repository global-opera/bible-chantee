[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the twilight of his days, Isaac called his son,  
With eyes dimmed by the years, the time had surely come.  
“Bring me savory meat, that my soul may bless thee,  
For I know not the hour, my time's a mystery.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the weight of a blessing, so precious and profound,  
In the echo of the ages, God’s promise will resound.  
Though the heart may be torn, and shadows cast their light,  
The blessings of the Father shine through the darkest night.  

[Verse 2]  
Rebekah heard the words, and whispered to her child,  
“Go now, fetch the goats, in faith, be reconciled.  
Your brother's strength is wild, but the blessing's meant for you,  
In the hands of the Almighty, His will shall see you through.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the weight of a blessing, so precious and profound,  
In the echo of the ages, God’s promise will resound.  
Though the heart may be torn, and shadows cast their light,  
The blessings of the Father shine through the darkest night.  

[Bridge]  
When Jacob drew near, with trembling heart he stood,  
Deceit upon his lips, yet God saw all his good.  
“Who art thou, my son?” Isaac’s voice rang true,  
In the silence, the Spirit whispered, “This is what you’re due.”  

[Verse 3]  
And when Esau came in, with a cry of bitter pain,  
“Bless me too, my father! Don’t leave me in disdain.”  
But Isaac spoke the truth, and the blessing had been cast,  
In the tapestry of heaven, the shadows could not last.

[Outro]  
So we gather in Your presence, Lord, we seek Your face,  
In the story of our lives, let Your love embrace.  
For every blessing spoken, in the quiet and the strife,  
We find our hope in You, the giver of our life.
