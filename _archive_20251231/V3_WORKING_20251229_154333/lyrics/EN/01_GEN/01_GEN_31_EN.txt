[TITLE]
The God of My Fathers

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of my struggle,  
Where the winds of change now blow,  
I’ve walked through fields of labor,  
Under Laban’s heavy hand, so slow.  
Yet I’ve seen the hand of mercy,  
Guiding me through every trial,  
Though the face of man may falter,  
Your grace, Lord, makes me smile.

[Chorus]  
You are the God of my fathers,  
In the night, You call my name,  
Through the trials, You’re my shelter,  
In Your love, I’ll stake my claim.  
Lead me home, O faithful Father,  
In Your arms, I find my rest,  
With each step, I trust Your promise,  
In Your will, I am blessed.

[Verse 2]  
Rachel weeps for what is missing,  
Yet in You, our hope remains,  
For the riches of our father,  
Fade like shadows in the rain.  
You’ve seen our pain and heartache,  
You’ve heard our silent cries,  
Now, we rise in faith together,  
With our eyes fixed on the skies.

“Fear not, for I am with thee.”  
I will guide you, I will keep you,  
In your heart, you’ll find your peace.  
Raise the stones, let’s mark the promise,  
In this place, we make our vow,  
You are with us, now and always,  
In Your love, we stand right now.
