[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Two angels came to Sodom's gate,  
Lot bowed low, his heart did wait.  
“Come, my lords, stay in my home,  
Let your weary souls find rest, not roam.”  
He prepared a feast, with bread so pure,  
In the night, their safety was his cure.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the cries of mercy reach the skies,  
In the darkest places, Your grace will rise.  
Lead us from destruction, guide our feet,  
In the shadow of Your love, we find retreat.  

Verse 2:  
But the men of Sodom, fierce and bold,  
Surrounded Lot, their hearts were cold.  
“Bring forth the men, let us know their face,”  
Lot cried, “Do not speak of such disgrace.”  
He offered his daughters, in despair he stood,  
“Do nothing to them, for they are good.”  

Chorus:  
Oh, the cries of mercy reach the skies,  
In the darkest places, Your grace will rise.  
Lead us from destruction, guide our feet,  
In the shadow of Your love, we find retreat.  

Bridge:  
Then the angels pulled him in,  
With blinded eyes, they lost their sin.  
“Escape this place, for judgment’s near,  
The Lord has sent us, have no fear.”  
“Take your family, do not delay,  
Flee to the mountains, don’t look away.”  

Verse 3:  
Lot lingered still, his heart in doubt,  
“Let me flee to Zoar, don’t cast me out.”  
And grace was given, a city small,  
Where mercy rested, upon them all.  
But his wife looked back, her heart of stone,  
And in that moment, she stood alone.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the cries of mercy reach the skies,  
In the darkest places, Your grace will rise.  
Lead us from destruction, guide our feet,  
In the shadow of Your love, we find retreat.  

Outro:  
As smoke arose from Sodom’s plight,  
God remembered Abraham, in the night.  
Lot found refuge, in mercy’s embrace,  
Forever changed by grace upon grace.  
In the shadows, we seek Your face,  
Guide us, Lord, to that sacred place.
