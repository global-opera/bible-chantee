[TITLE]
Gathered in His Name

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Gather 'round, O sons of Jacob,  
Hear the words your father speaks,  
In the twilight of our journey,  
Wisdom found in ancient peaks.  
Reuben, firstborn, strength and might,  
Yet like water, you lose your fight.  
Simeon and Levi, fierce and bold,  
But in anger, darkness unfolds.

Chorus  
Gathered in His name, we stand,  
In the light of His command.  
From the heart of Israel,  
Voices rise, our praises swell.  
In the blessings and the strife,  
We find our purpose, our life.  
Gathered in His name, we sing,  
To the hope that He will bring.

Verse 2  
Judah, lion, strong and true,  
In your hands, the promise grew.  
Sceptre stays, till Shiloh's day,  
In your strength, we find our way.  
Zebulun by the ocean's shore,  
Issachar sees rest and more.  
Dan, the judge, with wisdom's eyes,  
Gad will rise, though he may cry.

Bridge  
Joseph, fruitful bough by the well,  
Strengthened by the One who fell.  
In the trials, Your grace abounds,  
In the shadows, our hope is found.  
Benjamin, swift as a wolf,  
In the night, you claim your spoils.  
All twelve tribes, united here,  
In His name, we cast away fear.

Outro  
So we gather, Lord, in grace,  
With our hearts, we seek Your face.  
In the cave, where rest shall be,  
We find our peace eternally.  
Gathered in His name, we stand,  
In Your love, we take our hand.  
Amen.
