Verse 1  
In the garden where life began,  
Two brothers born from one man's hand,  
Cain tilled the soil, Abel kept the sheep,  
Yet in their hearts, a secret did creep.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, hear our cries from the ground,  
Where shadows linger, and sorrow is found.  
Though we wander from Your perfect way,  
Your grace and mercy lead us back today.  

Verse 2  
Cain brought his fruits, but they fell short,  
Abel's offering, a sweet support.  
Wrath arose, and anger took flight,  
In the fields where darkness swallowed the light.  

Chorus  
Oh Lord, hear our cries from the ground,  
Where shadows linger, and sorrow is found.  
Though we wander from Your perfect way,

Bridge  
"Where is your brother?" the voice of the Lord,  
In his silence, the weight of his sword.  
Blood cries from earth, a haunting refrain,  
Yet in our failures, You still remain.  

Verse 3  
A mark of protection, though lost in our sin,  
You paved the path for new life to begin.  
From Seth to Enos, Your promise unfolds,  
In every story, Your heart we behold.  

Chorus

Outro  
From dust to destiny, we rise and proclaim,  
In the midst of our trials, we call on Your name.  
Redeemed and restored, we stand in Your grace,  
Forever united, we seek Your face.
