[TITLE]
Laughter of Promise

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the dusk of time, the Lord did call,  
To Sarah’s heart, He brought the dawn,  
A promise whispered, a child would be,  
In her old age, joy would be reborn.  
Abraham stood, with faith in his soul,  
Isaac was born, their hearts made whole.

[Chorus]  
Oh, laughter of promise, rise from the earth,  
God of the ages, You give us new birth.  
In the shadows of doubt, Your light breaks through,  
We stand in Your goodness, forever true.

[Verse 2]  
Yet in the wilderness, a mother wept,  
Hagar's heart heavy, in sorrow she kept.  
But the voice of the lad, reached Heaven’s height,  
God heard their cries, brought forth His light.  
“Fear not, dear Hagar, for I am near,  
A well of salvation, your heart I’ll clear.”

[Chorus]  
Oh, laughter of promise, rise from the earth,  
God of the ages, You give us new birth.  
In the shadows of doubt, Your light breaks through,  
We stand in Your goodness, forever true.

[Bridge]  
Covenants made under the sky,  
In Beersheba's land, where our spirits fly,  
With every heartbeat, we sing Your praise,  
Everlasting God, through all of our days.

[Verse 3]  
So let the nations hear of Your grace,  
From valleys low to the highest place.  
In every struggle, in every strife,  
You are our promise, our hope, our life.  
Laughter will echo, as we lift our song,  
In the arms of the Father, we truly belong.

[Chorus]  
Oh, laughter of promise, rise from the earth,  
God of the ages, You give us new birth.  
In the shadows of doubt, Your light breaks through,  
We stand in Your goodness, forever true.

[Outro]  
With hearts wide open, we bring You our praise,  
In laughter and promise, we’ll walk all our days.  
Oh, laughter of promise, forever we sing,  
For in Your great mercy, we find everything.
