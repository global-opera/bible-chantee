Verse 1:  
In the land of Canaan, where the promise stands,  
Abraham mourned for Sarah, held her in his hands.  
One hundred twenty-seven years, a life well-lived,  
In the cave of Machpelah, a resting place to give.  

Chorus:  
So we gather here, in this sacred space,  
To honor love that time cannot erase.  
In the shadows of sorrow, our hearts will sing,  
For in Christ, our hope is an everlasting thing.  

Verse 2:  
A stranger in this land, he sought a place,  
To lay his beloved, to find a resting grace.  
The sons of Heth, they bowed and offered wide,  
But Abraham chose to purchase, with honor as his guide.  

Chorus:

Through trials and heartache, we find our way,  
In the arms of the Father, where forever we’ll stay.
