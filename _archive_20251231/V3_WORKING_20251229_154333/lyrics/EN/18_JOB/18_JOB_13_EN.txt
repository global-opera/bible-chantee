[TITLE]
In Your Presence, I Stand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I have seen Your wonders, Lord,  
Heard the whispers of Your grace.  
In the quiet, I draw near,  
Longing for Your warm embrace.  
Though my heart may tremble,  
And the shadows close around,  
Still, I lift my voice to You,  
In Your love, I am found.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, I stand,  
Trusting in Your mighty hand.  
Though the trials may surround,  
I will never be unbound.  
For even when the night is long,  
In Your love, I find my song.

Verse 2:  
You know every struggle, Lord,  
All the burdens that I bear.  
Yet I seek Your gentle truth,  
With my heart laid open bare.  
If You search my hidden ways,  
And reveal what’s deep inside,  
I will trust in Your embrace,  
In Your mercy, I abide.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, I stand,

Bridge:  
Though You slay me, still I trust,  
In Your goodness, Lord, I must.  
Speak, O God, and I will hear,  
Your voice calms all my fear.  
Let Your light shine in my heart,  
In Your truth, I’ll never part.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, I stand,

Outro:  
So, I come before You now,  
With my heart and soul laid down.  
In Your presence, I will stay,  
Trusting You, come what may.
