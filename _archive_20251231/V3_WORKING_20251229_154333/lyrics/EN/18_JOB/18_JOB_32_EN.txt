[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence, I am waiting,  
Hearts are heavy, voices low,  
Wisdom flows from the ages,  
Yet, Your truth is what I seek to know.  
Elihu stood, with boldness in his heart,  
Though he was young, he played his part.  
For in the spirit of man, Your breath does dwell,  
Grant me understanding, Lord, I long to tell.  

Chorus:  
Speak to me, O Lord,  
Let Your voice be my guide,  
In the stillness, I will listen,  
In Your presence, I abide.  
Show me Your ways,  
Let my heart be renewed,  
In the light of Your wisdom,  
I find strength to pursue.  

Verse 2:  
Great men falter, the aged may not see,  
But Your truth, O Lord, shines clear to me.  
I’ve waited for answers, I’ve listened in vain,  
Yet You call me to speak, to break through the pain.  
With humility, I lay my heart bare,  
No flattering titles, just a soul in prayer.  
In the stillness of night, I’ll lift my plea,  
Let my voice rise, let my spirit be free.

Bridge:  
When the world is loud, and the answers are few,  
I’ll lean on Your promise, I’ll trust in You.  
For You are my refuge, my rock and my song,  
In Your arms of mercy, Lord, I know I belong.
