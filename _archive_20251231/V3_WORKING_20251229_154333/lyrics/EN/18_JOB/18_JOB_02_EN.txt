[TITLE]
In the Ashes

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence, when the shadows fall,  
Sons of God, they gather, answering the call.  
Satan walks, in the earth he roams,  
Seeking souls, far from their homes.  

Chorus  
Yet I will stand, my heart will sing,  
In the trials, I find my King.  
Though the storm may rage and the night be long,  
In the ashes, my faith is strong.  

Verse 2  
Job, a servant, blameless and true,  
In his suffering, he clings to You.  
Though the boils break, and the pain draws near,  
He lifts his voice, casting out fear.  

Chorus  
Yet I will stand, my heart will sing,  
In the trials, I find my King.  
Though the storm may rage and the night be long,  
In the ashes, my faith is strong.  

Bridge  
O Lord, You give, and You take away,  
In every moment, I choose to stay.  
For in my weakness, Your strength is near,  
In every tear, You wipe my fear.  

Verse 3  
Friends surround, in mourning they weep,  
Seven days of silence, a bond they keep.  
Yet in the silence, Your whispers flow,  
In the darkest hour, Your love I know.  

Chorus  
Yet I will stand, my heart will sing,  
In the trials, I find my King.  
Though the storm may rage and the night be long,  
In the ashes, my faith is strong.  

Outro  
In the ashes, I will rise,  
With my Savior, I’ll reach the skies.  
Though the world may falter, I’ll still believe,  
For in the ashes, my soul’s reprieved.
