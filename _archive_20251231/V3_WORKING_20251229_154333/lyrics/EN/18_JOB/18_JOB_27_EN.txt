[TITLE]
In Your Hands, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
As God lives, my heart will stand,  
Though trials come, I’ll trust Your hand.  
My breath is Yours, my spirit cries,  
With every tear, my soul will rise.  

Chorus:  
In Your hands, O God, I find my peace,  
Holding fast to truth, my doubts release.  
Though the storms may rage, I'll stand secure,  
In Your love, O Lord, I am made pure.  

Verse 2:  
My lips won’t speak of wicked ways,  
In darkest nights, I’ll sing Your praise.  
For what is hope, if not in You?  
My strength, my shield, my heart made new.  

Chorus:  
In Your hands, O God, I find my peace,  
Holding fast to truth, my doubts release.  
Though the storms may rage, I'll stand secure,  
In Your love, O Lord, I am made pure.  

Bridge:  
Though riches fade and shadows fall,  
Your promise stands, Your name I call.  
In the silence, I will wait,  
For You are good, and You are great.  

Chorus:  
In Your hands, O God, I find my peace,  
Holding fast to truth, my doubts release.  
Though the storms may rage, I'll stand secure,  
In Your love, O Lord, I am made pure.  

Outro:  
As I breathe, my heart will sing,  
In Your presence, my soul takes wing.  
Forever, Lord, I’ll lift Your name,  
In Your hands, I’ll never be the same.
