[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Uz, a man stood tall,  
Job, upright and faithful, he gave his all.  
With seven sons and daughters, joy in his home,  
In every feast, he called them, never alone.  

Chorus  
Blessed be Your name, O Lord on high,  
In the storms of life, You hear our cry.  
You give and take away, still I will say,  
Blessed be Your name, forever I'll stay.  

Verse 2  
When trials arose, and darkness fell near,  
He lost all he had, yet he held no fear.  
With torn robes and ashes, he fell to the ground,  
In worship he uttered, Your praise is profound.  

Chorus  
Blessed be Your name, O Lord on high,  
In the storms of life, You hear our cry.  
You give and take away, still I will say,  
Blessed be Your name, forever I'll stay.  

Bridge  
Through every loss, through every pain,  
Your love remains, my heart will sustain.  
No matter the trial, I choose to believe,  
Your grace is sufficient, my soul You’ll retrieve.  

Chorus

Outro  
Naked I came, and naked I’ll go,  
Yet in all things, Your faithfulness shows.  
Blessed be Your name, now and always,  
In every moment, my heart sings Your praise.
