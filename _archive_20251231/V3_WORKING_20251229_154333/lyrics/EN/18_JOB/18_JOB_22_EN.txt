[TITLE]
Return to the Almighty

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the stillness of the night,  
We seek Your presence, Lord, so bright.  
Can a man bring joy to Thee,  
In the depths of humility?  
For our ways may seem so wise,  
Yet in darkness, we disguise.  
Oh, reveal our hearts’ intent,  
Guide us back where love is spent.  

(Chorus)  
Return to the Almighty,  
In His arms, find peace anew.  
Lay aside our heavy burdens,  
Trust in Him, His grace will do.  
Lifting up our faces,  
In the light of all He’s done,  
In the presence of the Father,  
We find rest in the Son.  

(Verse 2)  
Have we turned away from truth,  
Broken bonds of love and youth?  
Withholding bread from hungry souls,  
Ignoring cries that make us whole.  
Yet Your mercy, Lord, abounds,  
In Your grace, our hope is found.  
Teach us kindness, show us love,  
As we seek the One above.  

(Chorus)  
Return to the Almighty,

(Bridge)  
Oh, we decree Your promises,  
Your light will shine upon our ways.  
When we call, You hear us, Lord,  
In our weakness, strength displays.  
With hands so pure, we seek Your face,  
Deliver us, O God of grace.  

(Chorus)

(Outro)  
Acquaint us now, O Lord divine,  
With every word, Your love will shine.  
In the quiet, let us be,  
In Your arms, we’re truly free.
