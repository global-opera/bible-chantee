[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Oh Lord, I come to seek Your face,  
In humility, I find my place.  
From afar, I lift my voice,  
To magnify Your righteous choice.  
You are mighty, strong and true,  
In Your hands, all things renew.  

Chorus:  
Behold, God, You are great,  
In Your power, we find our fate.  
You see the righteous, You hold them near,  
In every trial, You calm our fear.  
Oh, how You lift the poor in pain,  
With arms of mercy, You break every chain.  

Verse 2:  
You open ears to hear Your call,  
In our affliction, You see it all.  
With kings and thrones, You make our stand,  
Forever guided by Your hand.  
Yet if we stray, we lose our way,  
But in Your grace, we find the day.

Bridge:  
Who can teach like You, O Lord?  
Your wisdom flows, our hearts restored.  
We magnify the work You’ve done,

You gather clouds, the rain You send,  
Your love, O God, will never end.
