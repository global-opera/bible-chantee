[TITLE]
Awaken My Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness of the night,  
I lift my voice to You, O Lord,  
Your justice stands, unwavering light,  
In Your hands, my hope restored.  
Though my days are but a shadow,  
And my heart knows pain and strife,  
I will seek You in the sorrow,  
For You are my source of life.  

Chorus:  
Awaken my heart, Lord, make me whole,  
Fill my mouth with laughter, joy my soul.  
Though my beginnings may seem small,  
In Your hands, I will stand tall.  
You will not cast away the pure,  
In Your love, I am secure.  

Verse 2:  
Like the rush that needs the mire,  
I am rooted in Your grace,  
In the trials, I find desire,  
To seek the depths of Your embrace.  
You will rise and bless the humble,  
Make my dwelling place anew,  
In the storms, I will not stumble,  
For my trust is placed in You.  

Chorus:  
Awaken my heart, Lord, make me whole,  
Fill my mouth with laughter, joy my soul.  
Though my beginnings may seem small,  
In Your hands, I will stand tall.  
You will not cast away the pure,  
In Your love, I am secure.  

Bridge:  
Teach me, Lord, to walk in Your way,  
Show me grace, with each passing day.  
Though the wicked may face their shame,  
In Your mercy, I’ll praise Your name.  

Chorus:

Outro:  
With every breath, I’ll sing Your praise,  
In Your light, I find my days.  
Awaken my heart, Lord, make me whole,  
In Your presence, I am whole.
