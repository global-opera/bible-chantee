[TITLE]
In Your Hands, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
I hear Your whisper, soft and bright,  
You have lifted weary hearts,  
Strengthened those who fell apart.  
Yet now I tremble, Lord, I see,  
The weight of trials pressing me.  
But in my fear, I turn to You,  
My hope, my light, my refuge true.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, I find my peace,  
You uphold the weak, my fears release.  
Though the world may shake, I will not fade,  
In Your love, my trust is laid.  

Verse 2  
Who am I to question grace,  
When storms of life begin to race?  
Yet I remember, You are near,  
In every struggle, You are here.  
The righteous know, they will not fall,  
For in Your mercy, You hear our call.  
Though shadows loom, I’ll stand secure,  
In faith, my heart, forever pure.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, I find my peace,  
You uphold the weak, my fears release.  
Though the world may shake, I will not fade,  
In Your love, my trust is laid.  

Bridge  
Shall mortal man be just as You?  
Your wisdom far beyond our view.  
In houses made of dust and clay,  
Your strength prevails; You lead the way.  
So I will rest in what You’ve shown,  
In trials faced, Lord, I am not alone.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, I find my peace,  
You uphold the weak, my fears release.  
Though the world may shake, I will not fade,  
In Your love, my trust is laid.  

Outro  
In Your hands, O Lord, my heart will stay,  
With every breath, I’ll seek Your way.  
You are my strength, my hope, my song,  
In Your embrace, Lord, I belong.
