[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of our hearts, we seek Your face,  
Yet the world around us whispers, lost in grace.  
How long, O Lord, will we turn away,  
From the truth that calls us to walk in Your way?  

Chorus:  
Shine Your light, Lord, in the dark of night,  
For the wicked shall fade, and their fire lose sight.  
In Your arms, we find refuge and peace,  
Guide us, O Shepherd, let our doubts cease.  

Verse 2:  
With every step, let us not be ensnared,  
By the traps that lay, in shadows declared.  
The strength of the wicked, like dust in the wind,  
But in You, our Savior, new life will begin.  

Chorus:

For the wicked shall fade, and their fire lose sight.  
In Your arms, we find refuge and peace,  
Guide us, O Shepherd, let our doubts cease.  

Bridge:  
Terrors may surround, but we will not fear,  
In the valleys of shadows, You are still near.  
Your grace is our anchor; Your love is our song,  
In the depths of despair, You make us strong.  

Chorus:  
Shine Your light, Lord, in the dark of night,  
For the wicked shall fade, and their fire lose sight.

Outro:  
So we lift our voices, in worship we stand,  
Trusting Your promise, held safe in Your hand.  
Shadows may come, but we'll walk in Your way,  
For in You, O Lord, we will ever stay.
