### Title: "In Your Balance, Lord"

Verse 1  
I made a vow with my eyes, Lord,  
To guard my heart from deceit,  
What portion of You from above,  
If I stray from Your holy seat?  
In Your balance, weigh my soul,  
Count my steps and make me whole,  
For I long to walk in truth,  
In the light of Your grace renewed.

Chorus  
Oh, search my heart, Lord, know my ways,  
In Your presence, I want to stay.  
Let my life reflect Your love,  
As I seek the things above.  
In Your balance, pure and true,  
May my heart be close to You.

Verse 2  
If I've turned from the path of light,  
Or let my heart be led astray,  
Let my hands not hold to sin,  
For I long for Your guiding way.  
If I've withheld from those in need,  
Forgive my selfish, wandering greed,  
For You made us all the same,  
In Your eyes, we bear Your name.

Chorus  
Oh, search my heart, Lord, know my ways,  
In Your presence, I want to stay.  
Let my life reflect Your love,  
As I seek the things above.  
In Your balance, pure and true,  
May my heart be close to You.

Bridge  
If I've placed my hope in gold,  
Or found my worth in what is sold,  
Lord, keep me humble, keep me near,  
In Your mercy, cast out fear.  
When the world brings trials and strife,  
I will trust in You, the source of life.

Chorus  
Oh, search my heart, Lord, know my ways,  
In Your presence, I want to stay.

Outro  
In Your balance, Lord, I stand,  
Holding tightly to Your hand.  
Guide my steps in all I do,  
May my life bring glory to You.
