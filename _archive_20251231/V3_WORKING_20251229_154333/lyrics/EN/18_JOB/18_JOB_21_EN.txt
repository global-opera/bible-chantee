Verse 1  
Hear my cry, O Lord, I call,  
In my pain, I stand so tall.  
Mock me not, I plead tonight,  
For my spirit trembles in the light.  

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
Though the wicked rise, Your love won’t cease.  
You see my heart, my every fear,  
In Your arms, I draw so near.
