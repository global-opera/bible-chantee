[TITLE]
In Dust and Ashes

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I come before You, Lord, in humble prayer,  
With every burden, every weight I bear.  
I know that You can do all things,  
No thought is hidden from the King.  
In silence I have wandered, lost in my despair,  
But now I see You, Lord, my heart laid bare.

Chorus:  
In dust and ashes, I repent, I cry,  
For in Your presence, I find my reason why.  
You turn my sorrow into joy anew,  
With every blessing, I will worship You.

Verse 2:  
Job cried out to You, his heart in pain,  
Yet through his trials, Your glory remained.  
You spoke, and all his fears were laid to rest,  
Restoration came, a promise manifest.  
With friends beside him, they found grace to share,  
In love and mercy, You showed how much You care.

Chorus:  
In dust and ashes, I repent, I cry,  
For in Your presence, I find my reason why.  
You turn my sorrow into joy anew,  
With every blessing, I will worship You.

Bridge:  
You blessed his latter days, more than before,  
In every heartache, Your love we adore.  
For every trial, You bring forth a song,  
In every moment, Lord, with You I belong.

Chorus:  
In dust and ashes, I repent, I cry,  
For in Your presence, I find my reason why.  
You turn my sorrow into joy anew,  
With every blessing, I will worship You.

Outro:  
So here I stand, with faith held high,  
In Your great mercy, I will rely.  
For every tear, You’ve wiped away,  
In dust and ashes, I rise to pray.
