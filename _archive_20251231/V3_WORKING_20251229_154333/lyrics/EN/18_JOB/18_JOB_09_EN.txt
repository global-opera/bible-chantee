[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness of the night,  
I come to You, Lord, seeking light.  
Who can stand before Your throne,  
The just and mighty God alone.  
Mountains tremble at Your call,  
You alone, Lord, are over all.  
In Your wisdom, You do reign,  
Through the storm and through the pain.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
Though my heart is heavy, in You I release.  
You are my refuge, my hiding place,  
In the depths of sorrow, I seek Your grace.

Verse 2:  
Though the tempest rages on,  
I will trust in You, my song.  
If I cry out, will You hear?  
In my weakness, You are near.  
You’ve created the stars above,  
Yet You show me endless love.  
Though I feel lost, and my heart is sore,  
I know, my Savior, I’ll trust You more.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
Though my heart is heavy, in You I release.  
You are my refuge, my hiding place,  
In the depths of sorrow, I seek Your grace.

Bridge:  
You know my heart, You see my pain,  
In every trial, I’ll praise Your name.  
For even when I cannot see,  
I’ll trust Your ways, You’re here with me.  
No one can judge like You, O Lord,  
In Your hands, my life restored.

Chorus:  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
Though my heart is heavy, in You I release.  
You are my refuge, my hiding place,  
In the depths of sorrow, I seek Your grace.

Outro:  
In the silence, I will wait,  
For You are good, and I trust Your fate.  
In Your presence, Lord, I stand secure,  
In every season, Your love is pure.
