[TITLE]
In the Shadow of the King's Favor

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the halls of power, a queen stood bold,  
With a heart of courage, her story unfolds.  
"Grant me my petition, O King, hear my plea,  
For my people are lost, their fate rests with me."  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of the King’s favor,  
We rise, we rise, lifting our hearts in prayer.  
When the enemy stands, plotting in the night,  
Your grace will prevail, bring forth the light.  

Verse 2:  
Esther's voice trembled, but faith held her fast,  
Against wicked plans, a truth to outlast.  
"Who dares to harm us? The foe is revealed,  
In the face of danger, our fate is concealed."  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of the King’s favor,  
We rise, we rise, lifting our hearts in prayer.  
When the enemy stands, plotting in the night,  
Your grace will prevail, bring forth the light.  

Bridge:  
O mighty King, turn our sorrows to joy,  
With courage as our armor, fear cannot destroy.  
The gallows of Haman, turned on his own head,  
For those who seek justice, the righteous are fed.  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of the King’s favor,  
We rise, we rise, lifting our hearts in prayer.  
When the enemy stands, plotting in the night,  
Your grace will prevail, bring forth the light.  

Outro:  
So we gather together, with faith intertwined,  
In the story of Esther, Your power we find.  
With hearts full of hope, we sing out in praise,  
For the King of all kings, we lift up our days.
