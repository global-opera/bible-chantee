[TITLE]
Confident in Your Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In every prayer, I lift you high,  
With joy I thank my God above,  
For the fellowship we share in Christ,  
Bound together in His love.  
From the first day till now we stand,  
Confident in the work He’s done,  
For He who started this good plan,  
Will bring it forth till we are one.  

Chorus  
So let our hearts abound in love,  
In knowledge and in grace,  
Standing firm in one spirit,  
Shining bright in this place.  
For to live is Christ, we boldly proclaim,  
And in every trial, we lift His name.  

Verse 2  
In the bonds of faith, we find our strength,  
As we share in His grace and care,  
With courage, we’ll speak His truth,  
In every moment, He’ll be there.  
Though some may preach with envy’s sting,  
Still, Christ is lifted, hearts are free,  
In our struggles, hope will spring,  
For in our trials, we’ll see His victory.

Bridge  
With each breath, we lift You higher,  
In our lives, Your truth ignites,  
We stand together, hand in hand,  
Fighting boldly in Your light.  
For in our hearts, the Spirit flows,  
Filling us with joy and peace,  
We are Yours, and You are ours,  
In Your love, we find release.
