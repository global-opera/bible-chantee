### Called to Go

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, You called us,  
Twelve were chosen, hearts ablaze,  
With power to heal and to cast out,  
We carry Your love into the fray.  

Chorus  
We’re sent to the lost, the broken and low,  
Preaching Your kingdom, Lord, help us to go.  
Freely we’ve received, freely we’ll give,  
In Your name, we rise, for in You, we live.  

Verse 2  
Sheep among wolves, we walk with courage,  
Wise as serpents, gentle as doves,  
Though we may face trials and tribulation,  
Our hearts are anchored in Your love.  

Chorus  
We’re sent to the lost, the broken and low,  
Preaching Your kingdom, Lord, help us to go.  
Freely we’ve received, freely we’ll give,  
In Your name, we rise, for in You, we live.  

Bridge  
Fear not, for You are with us,  
Every hair on our heads, You know,  
We’ll speak Your truth in the shadows,  
And shine Your light wherever we go.  

Chorus  
We’re sent to the lost, the broken and low,  
Preaching Your kingdom, Lord, help us to go.  
Freely we’ve received, freely we’ll give,  
In Your name, we rise, for in You, we live.  

Outro  
He who receives us, receives You,  
In each small act, Your love shines through.  
With every step, we trust and believe,  
In the power of Your grace, we will achieve.
