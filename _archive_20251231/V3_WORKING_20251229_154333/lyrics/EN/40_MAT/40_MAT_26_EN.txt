[TITLE]
In the Garden of Sorrow

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
Your heart weighed down with pain,  
You prayed for strength and courage,  
Yet love remained the same.  
Betrayed by one who walked with You,  
A kiss sealed Your fate,  
Oh, in the garden, tears of anguish,  
You faced our sins, our hate.

Chorus  
Jesus, Savior, Lamb of God,  
You bore the cross alone.  
In our darkness, You are light,  
Love’s sacrifice was shown.  
Every tear, every cry,  
For our freedom, You would die,  
In the garden, You stood strong,  
With Your love, we now belong.

Verse 2  
The cup was filled with sorrow,  
Yet still, You chose to stay,  
"My Father, let Your will be done,"  
In anguish, You would pray.  
With every breath, a promise made,  
For the world, You gave it all,  
Through the shadows, we find hope,  
In Your name, we hear Your call.

Your love forever flows,  
In the garden, we find peace,  
In Your presence, love bestows.
