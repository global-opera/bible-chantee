[TITLE]
Star of Bethlehem

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness of the night, a promise shines so bright,  
Wise men traveled far and wide, led by heaven's light.  
"Where is He, the King of grace? We've seen His star in place,"  
In our hearts, we seek His face, to worship and embrace.

Chorus:  
O Star of Bethlehem, guide our way,  
In Your love, we find our stay.  
With hearts open wide, we come to adore,  
Our Savior, our King, forevermore.

Verse 2:  
In Bethlehem, the prophets spoke, a Governor to break the yoke,  
In a humble place, He came, our hearts forever changed.  
With gifts of gold and fragrant myrrh, we lay before Him,  
In awe we kneel, our praises stir, our hearts forever brim.

Chorus:  
O Star of Bethlehem, guide our way,  
In Your love, we find our stay.  
With hearts open wide, we come to adore,  
Our Savior, our King, forevermore.

Bridge:  
Though darkness may surround, His light will lead us home,  
With every step we take, we know we’re never alone.  
Through trials and through tears, He’s faithful, He’s so near,  
In the shadows of despair, His whispers calm our fear.

Chorus:  
O Star of Bethlehem, guide our way,  
In Your love, we find our stay.  
With hearts open wide, we come to adore,  
Our Savior, our King, forevermore.

Outro:  
So let us lift our voices high,  
In harmony, we'll testify,  
For in the silence, He will speak,  
Our hearts forever, He will seek.  
O Star of Bethlehem, we worship and pray,  
Our Savior, our King, foreverstay.
