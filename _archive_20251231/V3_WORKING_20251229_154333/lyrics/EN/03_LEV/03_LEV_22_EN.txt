[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Lord, You called to Moses, a voice from on high,  
To teach us Your ways, in obedience we sigh.  
Separate from the unclean, we seek to draw near,  
For in Your holy presence, we cast away fear.  

Chorus:  
Hallowed be Your name, O Lord, we lift You high,  
In the shadows of our sin, Your grace will abide.  
Sanctified and whole, our hearts will proclaim,  
We worship in spirit, hallowed be Your name.  

Verse 2:  
In the offerings we bring, let them be pure and right,  
No blemish will be given, in Your holy sight.  
For every vow we utter, every sacrifice made,  
With a heart that's devoted, our love will not fade.  

Chorus:  
Hallowed be Your name, O Lord, we lift You high,  
In the shadows of our sin, Your grace will abide.  
Sanctified and whole, our hearts will proclaim,

Bridge:  
From Egypt You have called us, to be Your chosen race,  
To honor and reflect You, to shine with holy grace.  
In every step we take, in every breath we breathe,  
We long to be Your vessels, in whom the lost believe.  

Chorus:  
Hallowed be Your name, O Lord, we lift You high,  
In the shadows of our sin, Your grace will abide.  
Sanctified and whole, our hearts will proclaim,

Outro:  
Forever we will praise You, forever we will sing,  
In the covenant You’ve given, our praises rise as wings.  
Lord, we stand united, in reverence we remain,  
With all of heaven's chorus, hallowed be Your name.
