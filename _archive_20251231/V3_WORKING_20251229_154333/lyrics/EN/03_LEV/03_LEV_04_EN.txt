[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the altar, we come to You,  
With hearts full of sorrow, for the sins we've done.  
When ignorance has led us, away from Your light,  
We seek Your forgiveness, Lord, restore us tonight.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lamb of God, our sacrifice,  
With blood that cleanses, with love so pure,  
You bear our burdens, our shame and strife,  
In Your embrace, we find our cure.  

Verse 2  
As Moses was commanded, so we lay our hands,  
On the head of our offering, Your mercy stands.  
With faith, we bring our hearts, each burden and care,  
Before the veil of mercy, we find solace there.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lamb of God, our sacrifice,

In Your embrace, we find our cure.  

Bridge  
Seven times the blood is sprinkled,  
In the presence of the Lord,  
Atonement made for every sin,  
Restoration through Your Word.  
We stand united, all as one,  
In this sacred place, we come undone.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lamb of God, our sacrifice,

Outro  
With grateful hearts, we lift our praise,  
For in Your mercy, we find our way.
