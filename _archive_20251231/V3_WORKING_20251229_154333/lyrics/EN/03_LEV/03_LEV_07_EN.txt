[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the altar, we come to lay our hearts,  
With the blood of our redemption, a new covenant imparts.  
Every sin, a heavy burden, now we cast aside,  
In the grace of our Redeemer, forever we abide.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, we lift our voices high,  
With thanksgiving, we will cry,  
For the peace that You have given,  
In Your love, we are alive.  
Let our offerings be holy,  
As we stand in awe of Thee,  
In the beauty of Your presence,  
We are one, we are free.  

[Verse 2]  
With unleavened cakes before You, mingled oil we bring,  
In the joy of our salvation, hearts rejoicing, we sing.  
Every vow and every promise, laid before Your throne,  
In the light of Your forgiveness, we are never alone.

[Bridge]  
Lord, You called us to remember,  
To partake in sacred grace,  
In the breaking of the bread,  
We find our rightful place.  
Every soul that seeks Your mercy,  
Cleansed and whole, we come,  
In the promise of Your kindness,  
We rejoice, we are one.

[Outro]  
In the wilderness, we gather,  
With our hearts, we bring our praise,  
In the bond of holy union,  
We will worship all our days.  
With every breath, we honor,  
In the love that sets us free,  
As we offer our hearts to You,  
Lord, we are one, we are Thee.
