[TITLE]
I Am the LORD Your God

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the silence of the night,  
The Lord calls out, “Hear my voice,  
I am the God who guides your way,  
In my statutes, you rejoice.  
Leave behind the lands of sin,  
Where the shadows cast their lies,  
Walk in light, let truth begin,  
For in me your heart shall rise.  

[Chorus]  
I am the Lord, your God,  
Walk with me, I’ll lead you home.  
In my love, you’ll find your place,  
In my grace, you are not alone.  
Keep my ways, hold them dear,  
In my promise, you are near.  
I am the Lord, your God,  
Forever in my love, you’re clear.  

[Verse 2]  
Boundaries set, for your own good,  
To protect the sacred trust,  
In the family, love should reign,  
In my ways, you shall find just.  
No deceit, no wickedness,  
Hold your heart in pure refrain,  
For I’ve called you to be blessed,  
In my name, you’ll know no shame.

I am holy, I am just,  
In my presence, you find trust.  
Seek my face, be set apart,  
In my kingdom, I’ll renew your heart.
