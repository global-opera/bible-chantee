Verse 1  
In the shadows of the night, I come with burdens deep,  
With wounds upon my skin, my heart begins to weep.  
The priest he looks with mercy, sees the scars of sin,  
Yet in hope I find my voice, for healing can begin.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, You cleanse the leper’s stain,  
With a touch, You break the chain.  
From the depths of sorrow, raise me high,  
In Your love, I find my life,  
Cleansed by grace, made whole in You,  
Forever faithful, ever true.

Verse 2  
When the darkness whispers doubts, and shame begins to rise,  
I will trust in Your promise, for Your love never lies.  
Through the trials that I face, You are with me, Lord,  
With each day I hear the call, to lift my heart in word.
