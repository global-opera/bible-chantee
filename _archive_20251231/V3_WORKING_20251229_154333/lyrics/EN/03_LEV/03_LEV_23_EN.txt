[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the silence of the night,  
God spoke to Moses, clear and bright,  
“Gather My people, let them hear,  
The feasts of the Lord, draw near.”  
Six days of labor, then the rest,  
The Sabbath calls, a holy quest.  
In every dwelling, let hearts unite,  
To honor the Lord, in His guiding light.  

[Chorus]  
We will feast in His presence,  
With joy we lift our song,  
Celebrating His goodness,  
In our hearts, we belong.  
From harvest to the altar,  
We bring our firstfruits here,  
In holy convocation,  
Our Savior’s love is near.  

[Verse 2]  
The Passover night, the lamb is slain,  
Unleavened bread, our hearts explain.  
Seven days we gather, in unity,  
A reminder of His grace, eternally.  
Count the days till the offering’s made,  
With thankful hearts, let none be swayed.  
The harvest is plenty, He blesses our hands,  
With love and mercy, our God understands.

[Bridge]  
Blow the trumpets, sound the call,  
Atonement, redemption, for one and for all.  
In the booths we dwell, remembering grace,  
He led us through trials, to a promised place.  
With palms in our hands, we rejoice in His name,  
For the feasts of the Lord are forever the same.

[Outro]  
These are the feasts, the Lord has declared,  
In every generation, His love is shared.  
So let us remember, with hearts open wide,  
The feasts of the Lord, in Him we abide.
