[TITLE]
Set Apart for You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Your holiness,  
You call us to stand, to be pure and blessed.  
We gather 'round, we lift our hands high,  
For the bread of life You give, we testify.  
Though the world may try to take us down,  
We are set apart, in Your love, we are found.  

Chorus  
Holy, holy, Lord God Almighty,  
In Your presence, we find our peace.  
You sanctify us, we’re made anew,  
Forever, Lord, we are set apart for You.  

Verse 2  
You speak to the heart, You guide our way,  
In the midst of trials, our voices we’ll raise.  
With every offering, we draw near,  
In reverence and awe, we cast out all fear.  
Though blemished and broken, we come as we are,  
Your grace restores, You shine like a star.  

Chorus  
Holy, holy, Lord God Almighty,  
In Your presence, we find our peace.  
You sanctify us, we’re made anew,  
Forever, Lord, we are set apart for You.  

Bridge  
Anointed and chosen, we wear Your crown,  
In the sanctuary of love, we’re never alone.  
With every heartbeat, we worship and sing,  
To the God who is holy, our offering.  

Chorus  
Holy, holy, Lord God Almighty,  
In Your presence, we find our peace.  
You sanctify us, we’re made anew,  
Forever, Lord, we are set apart for You.  

Outro  
Set apart for You, O Lord, we stand,  
In the grace of Your love, we trust Your hand.  
With every breath we take, we declare Your name,  
In the light of Your glory, we are forever changed.
