Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
A promise softly spoken,  
Moses heard the voice of God,  
With words of grace unbroken.  
"Speak to Israel, share the truth,  
A child brings joy and sorrow,  
For every birth, a path to walk,  
A hope for each tomorrow."

Chorus  
Cleansed by grace, we stand renewed,  
From every chain, our hearts imbued.  
With offerings of love and praise,  
We come before You, Lord, always.  
In the shadow of the Law,  
Your mercy shines, we’re made whole,  
In every trial, every fall,  
Your love redeems our very soul.

Verse 2  
For a son or daughter born,  
Seven days, then two weeks more,  
A time of waiting, purifying,  
To come to You, to seek and adore.  
Bring forth the lamb, the dove in hand,  
A sacrifice for all we’ve done,  
In the sanctuary, we find Your grace,  
Our hearts restored, our spirits run.
