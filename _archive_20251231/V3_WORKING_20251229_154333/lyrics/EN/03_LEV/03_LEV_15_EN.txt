[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Cleansing Waters

[Verse 1]  
In the stillness, I hear Your call,  
Moses spoke to the hearts of all,  
With a whisper of grace, You bring the light,  
To the unclean, You shine so bright.  
When the flesh is weary, when the heart is torn,  
In our struggles, Lord, we are reborn.  

[Chorus]  
Cleanse me, Lord, in the waters of grace,  
Wash me anew, in Your holy embrace.  
From every burden, every fear,  
Make me whole, Lord, I draw near.  

[Verse 2]  
Turtledoves at the altar lay,  
As our sins are lifted away,  
In the shadows, we find our peace,  
You redeem our hearts, our fears cease.  
The blood of the lamb, the sacrifice made,  
In Your presence, Lord, we are unafraid.  

[Chorus]  
Cleanse me, Lord, in the waters of grace,  
Wash me anew, in Your holy embrace.  
From every burden, every fear,  
Make me whole, Lord, I draw near.  

[Bridge]  
Seven days of waiting, then we rise,  
From the ashes, You open our eyes.  
On the eighth day, we come before,  
With grateful hearts, we adore.  
In Your mercy, we find our song,  
In Your love, Lord, we belong.  

[Chorus]  
Cleanse me, Lord, in the waters of grace,  
Wash me anew, in Your holy embrace.  
From every burden, every fear,  
Make me whole, Lord, I draw near.  

[Outro]  
Separate us from what holds us bound,  
In Your cleansing love, we are found.  
With every step, Lord, we proclaim,  
In the waters of grace, we bless Your name.
