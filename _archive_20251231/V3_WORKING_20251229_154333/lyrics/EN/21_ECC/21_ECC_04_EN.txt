[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadows where the tears fall,  
I see the cries of the oppressed,  
With no comfort from the mighty,  
Their hearts are heavy, souls distressed.  
Oh, how I long to lift them up,  
To share the burden they bear,  
But in this world of toil and strife,  
I find Your love is always near.

(Chorus)  
Better is one with a quiet heart,  
Than the riches of the vain,  
For in Your presence, Lord, we find,  
A peace beyond the pain.  
Two are better, hand in hand,  
Together we rise, in faith we stand,  
In the light of Your embrace,  
We find our strength, we find our place.

(Verse 2)  
I’ve seen the wise child in the dust,  
With eyes that seek the truth above,  
While kings and fools chase fleeting dreams,  
It’s wisdom that will show us love.  
For when we fall, we lift each other,  
In the warmth of a faithful friend,  
A cord of three, strong and unbroken,  
Our hearts entwined, our spirits blend.
