[TITLE]
In Every Season

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In every season, a time to be born,  
A time to embrace, a time to mourn.  
You’ve made all things beautiful in Your grace,  
In the silence, Lord, we seek Your face.  

Chorus  
Oh, every moment, every breath we take,  
You hold the purpose; our hearts awake.  
In joy and sorrow, in peace and strife,  
We find our meaning in this gift of life.  

Verse 2  
A time to heal, and a time to break,  
A time to weep, and a time to awake.  
In laughter and dancing, we lift our voice,  
In every season, Lord, we rejoice.  

Chorus  
Oh, every moment, every breath we take,  
You hold the purpose; our hearts awake.  
In joy and sorrow, in peace and strife,  
We find our meaning in this gift of life.  

Bridge  
For all that we toil, in Your hands we find,  
A holy calling, a love so kind.  
In every heartbeat, Your wisdom shines,  
In every shadow, Your light defines.  

Verse 3  
As we gather stones and cast away pain,  
In the ebb and flow, Your love remains.  
You judge the righteous, and You see our hearts,  
In every ending, a brand new start.  

Chorus  
Oh, every moment, every breath we take,  
You hold the purpose; our hearts awake.  
In joy and sorrow, in peace and strife,  
We find our meaning in this gift of life.  

Outro  
In every season, we trust in You,  
For all Your plans, Lord, are good and true.  
In every heartbeat, we find our peace,  
In the time of love, our souls release.
