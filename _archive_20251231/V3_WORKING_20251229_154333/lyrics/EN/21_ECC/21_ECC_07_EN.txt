[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of the night, where sorrow softly sighs,  
A good name is a treasure, more than gold that never dies.  
The end is better than the start, in the silence we will find,  
The heart that mourns, learns to be wise, leaving foolishness behind.  

[Chorus]  
So let us come and seek Your face,  
In the house of mourning, find our grace.  
With every tear, a lesson learned,  
In the heart’s deep ache, Your wisdom burns.  

[Verse 2]  
Better is the wise man’s rebuke, than the laughter of the fool,  
For in every bitter sorrow, there’s a path to deeper rule.  
Consider all Your work, O God, in the day of joy or pain,  
For wisdom, like a shelter, in the storm, will still remain.  

[Chorus]  
So let us come and seek Your face,

[Bridge]  
In the day of adversity, teach us to be still,  
For the proud and hasty spirit, never knows Your will.  
Help us find our refuge, in the quiet of the night,  
With each breath, we draw closer, to the truth, to the light.

[Outro]  
Lo, You made us upright, in the journey that we tread,  
In every twist and turn, Lord, let Your light be spread.  
For wisdom is our treasure, in the stillness, we remain,  
In the house of mourning, we find joy amidst the pain.
