[TITLE]
Faithful Oath of Heart and Hand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the city walls,  
Nehemiah stood with a heart so true,  
With priests and Levites, a sacred call,  
To walk in Your ways, to honor You.  
From the sons of Israel, we gather here,  
With knowledge and faith, we draw near,  
We’ve separated ourselves for the Law,  
Oh Lord, we pledge, hear our humble awe.

Chorus:  
We will walk in Your light,  
In the promise of Your grace,  
With our hearts and our hands,  
We will seek Your holy face.  
Through the trials and the strife,  
We will stand, we will fight,  
In the strength of Your might,  
We will walk in Your light.

Verse 2:  
We give our firstfruits, our lives laid bare,  
To the altar of love, our offerings shared,  
No compromise with the world outside,  
Together we vow, in You we abide.  
On the Sabbath, our hearts will rest,  
In the rhythm of grace, we are blessed,  
With our daughters and sons, we will teach,  
The ways of the Lord, His love we’ll preach.

Chorus:  
We will walk in Your light,  
In the promise of Your grace,  
With our hearts and our hands,  
We will seek Your holy face.  
Through the trials and the strife,  
We will stand, we will fight,  
In the strength of Your might,  
We will walk in Your light.

Bridge:  
Oh, we’ll bring the wood, we’ll bring the grain,  
For the work of Your house, we’ll sustain,  
With joy in our hearts, and songs of praise,  
In every season, we’ll lift Your name.  
And as we unite, in this oath we make,  
We will not forsake, for Your glory’s sake.

Chorus:  
We will walk in Your light,  
In the promise of Your grace,  
With our hearts and our hands,  
We will seek Your holy face.  
Through the trials and the strife,  
We will stand, we will fight,

Outro:  
In the faithful oath of heart and hand,  
Together we rise, oh Lord, we stand,  
As one in Your love, forever we’ll be,  
Walking in faith, our hearts set free.  
Walking in faith, our hearts set free.
