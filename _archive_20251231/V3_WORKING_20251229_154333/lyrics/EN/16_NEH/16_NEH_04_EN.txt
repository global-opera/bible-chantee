[TITLE]
Our God Will Fight for Us

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the shadow of the wall, we hear their scorn,  
Sanballat mocks our hearts, in despair we mourn.  
“Can these feeble hands revive the stones?” they cry,  
But in our hearts, we know, our God will hear our sigh.

(Chorus)  
So we build, we unite, with courage in our soul,  
For the Lord is our strength, He makes the broken whole.  
With swords by our side, we stand against the night,  
Our God will fight for us, He is our guiding light.

(Verse 2)  
Though the burdens weigh us down, and doubts may arise,  
We lift our eyes to heaven, where our true hope lies.  
“Fear not,” we hear Him say, “I am great and strong,  
Fight for your families, together we belong.”

(Chorus)  
So we build, we unite, with courage in our soul,  
For the Lord is our strength, He makes the broken whole.  
With swords by our side, we stand against the night,  
Our God will fight for us, He is our guiding light.

(Bridge)  
In the midst of the rubble, we rise and proclaim,  
With faith like our foundation, we’ll call on His name.  
When the trumpet sounds, we will gather as one,  
Our battles are His battles, the victory’s begun.

(Chorus)  
So we build, we unite, with courage in our soul,  
For the Lord is our strength, He makes the broken whole.  
With swords by our side, we stand against the night,  
Our God will fight for us, He is our guiding light.

(Outro)  
In the silence and the struggle, we will not despair,  
For with every stone we lay, we find His presence there.  
So we build, we unite, in the strength of His love,  
Our God will fight for us, He reigns from above.
