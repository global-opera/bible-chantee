[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Rebuilding the Walls

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the towers, the faithful rise,  
Eliashib leads with a heart that never dies.  
With brethren beside him, they lift their hands,  
Sanctifying gates, as the promise stands.  

[Chorus]  
We are rebuilding, the walls of our faith,  
Together we stand, in His mighty grace.  
Every stone we lay, in unity we find,  
A fortress of hope, in the love He designed.  

[Verse 2]  
Men of Jericho, side by side they toil,  
With strength in their hearts, they labor and soil.  
From the fish gate to the valley wide,  
With every repair, our spirits abide.  

[Chorus]  
We are rebuilding, the walls of our faith,  
Together we stand, in His mighty grace.  
Every stone we lay, in unity we find,  
A fortress of hope, in the love He designed.  

[Bridge]  
Oh, let us not falter, let us not cease,  
For God is our strength, our everlasting peace.  
With every heart willing, and every hand true,  
The walls shall rise up, as we worship You.  

[Verse 3]  
From the dung gate to the fountain’s flow,  
Each one has a part, in this work we sow.  
The Levites, the priests, with their voices raised,  
In the echoes of faith, our God be praised.  

[Chorus]  
We are rebuilding, the walls of our faith,  
Together we stand, in His mighty grace.  
Every stone we lay, in unity we find,  
A fortress of hope, in the love He designed.  

[Outro]  
So let the gates be opened, let His glory shine,  
In this holy city, our hearts align.  
With every prayer lifted, and every song sung,  
Together we rise, for His kingdom begun.
