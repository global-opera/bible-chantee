[TITLE]
Covenant of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of our hearts, we gather here,  
Fasting and in prayer, we draw near.  
With sackcloth on our souls, we confess,  
The sins of our past, Lord, we express.  
Your law we read, in humble awe,  
Your words of life, we stand in awe.

Chorus:  
O Lord, our God, forever reign,  
Your name exalted, through joy and pain.  
In mercy's arms, we find our place,  
Your covenant of love, our hearts embrace.

Verse 2:  
You called us out from the darkest night,  
With promises of hope, You are our light.  
From Egypt's chains, You brought us free,  
Through seas divided, our victory.  
With pillars guiding, both day and night,  
You led us onward, through every fight.

Chorus:  
O Lord, our God, forever reign,  
Your name exalted, through joy and pain.  
In mercy's arms, we find our place,  
Your covenant of love, our hearts embrace.

Bridge:  
Though we have wandered from Your ways,  
Your grace remains through all our days.  
You’ve shown us kindness, never let go,  
In every season, Your love will flow.  
We stand before You, hearts laid bare,  
In humble reverence, we find You there.

Verse 3:  
Through trials and storms, You've held our hands,  
In the wilderness, Your promise stands.  
You fed our hunger, quenched every thirst,  
A faithful Savior, in You we're immersed.  
Though we have strayed, Your mercy calls,  
You lift us up, when in darkness we fall.

Chorus:  
O Lord, our God, forever reign,  
Your name exalted, through joy and pain.  
In mercy's arms, we find our place,  
Your covenant of love, our hearts embrace.

Outro:  
Now we make a vow, to seek Your face,  
To live in the truth of Your holy grace.  
As we write our hearts in covenant love,  
We lift our voices to You, above.  
O Lord, our God, forever reign,  
In this sacred promise, we shall remain.
