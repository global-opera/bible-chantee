[LYRICS]
[TITLE] In The Midst of Opposition  

[Verse 1]  
In the land of promise, hearts once bound,  
Judah and Benjamin, in faith they found,  
Amidst the rubble, they raised their hands,  
To build a temple, to honor God's plans.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, we rise, we will not be moved,  
In the face of the storm, our faith renewed.  
Though the adversaries surround our way,  
We stand united, come what may.  

[Verse 2]  
Zerubbabel led, his voice rang so clear,  
"We build for the Lord, cast away all fear!"  
The enemy whispered, "Let us join the fight,"  
But their hearts were not true; they walked in the night.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, we rise, we will not be moved,  
In the face of the storm, our faith renewed.  
Though the adversaries surround our way,  
We stand united, come what may.  

[Bridge]  
When the hands grow weary, and the shadows fall,  
We will lift our eyes, oh Lord, hear our call.  
For in every battle, Your truth will prevail,  
Our hearts are Your temple, our courage will not fail.  

[Verse 3]  
Letters of accusation, they sought to discourage,  
But the promise of God is our strength and our courage.  
With every stone laid, we declare our trust,  
In the God of salvation, in His love we must.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, we rise, we will not be moved,  
In the face of the storm, our faith renewed.  
Though the adversaries surround our way,  
We stand united, come what may.  

[Outro]  
So let the winds blow, let the shadows fall,  
We build for the Kingdom, we answer the call.  
With hearts set ablaze, to worship and sing,  
In the midst of opposition, we rise as His kings.
