[TITLE]
Revive Us, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When the voices rise, and the hearts are torn,  
O Lord, we gather, in shame we mourn.  
From the lands of sin, we have turned away,  
In the shadow of our past, our souls betray.  
We’ve mingled with the darkness, lost our holy way,  
Our iniquities like mountains, rise to meet the day.  

Chorus  
Revive us, O God, in Your mercy, we plead,  
A remnant of grace, in our time of need.  
Though we’ve wandered far, Your love still remains,  
Lighten our eyes, break the chains of our pain.  
O Lord, we’re before You, in humility stand,  
Restore us, O Savior, with Your mighty hand.  

Verse 2  
In astonished silence, we lift our cries,  
For the transgressions, we can’t disguise.  
You’ve shown us mercy, though we’ve gone astray,  
We seek Your forgiveness, in our hearts we pray.  
A little space of grace, a nail in Your place,  
Reviving our spirits, with Your warm embrace.

Bridge  
What shall we say, with our hearts laid bare?  
We’ve forsaken Your commands, O Lord, we declare.  
Yet You’ve been righteous, though we are unclean,  
In the depths of our darkness, Your light still gleams.  
We will not break, nor join in their ways,  
For in Your presence, we long to stay.

Outro  
So let us rise, from the ashes we’ll sing,  
Of the hope You provide, and the freedom You bring.  
O Lord, we are Yours, forever we claim,  
In the name of Jesus, forever the same.  
Revive us, O God, in Your love we remain,  
Our hearts are Yours, in joy or in pain.
