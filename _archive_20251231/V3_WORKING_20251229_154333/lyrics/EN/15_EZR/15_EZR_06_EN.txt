[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Let Your House Be Built

[Verse 1]  
In the days of old, a decree was made,  
Darius the king, his heart unafraid.  
From the rolls of time, the truth was found,  
A promise of hope, in the sacred ground.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
Let the house of God arise,  
Where sacrifices rise to the skies,  
With every stone laid, and every prayer,  
We build up Your temple, Lord, we declare.

[Chorus]  
Let Your house be built, O God of grace,  
With joy, we gather in this holy place.  
Restore the vessels, the hearts, and the praise,  
We offer our lives for Your name’s embrace.

[Verse 2]  
Through the prophets' words, the elders stood tall,  
With faith in their hearts, they answered the call.  
From the ashes of sorrow, a new hope will soar,  
As we labor together, we’re stronger than before.

[Pre-Chorus]  
Let the work of God unfold,  
In unity, we stand bold.  
With every offering, we lift our hands,  
For the glory of Heaven, we make our plans.

[Chorus]  
Let Your house be built, O God of grace,  
With joy, we gather in this holy place.  
Restore the vessels, the hearts, and the praise,  
We offer our lives for Your name’s embrace.

[Bridge]  
With joy, we keep the feast,  
In the depths of our hearts, we are released.  
O God, You’ve turned our mourning to song,  
In Your presence, Lord, we know we belong.  

[Chorus]  
Let Your house be built, O God of grace,  
With joy, we gather in this holy place.  
Restore the vessels, the hearts, and the praise,  
We offer our lives for Your name’s embrace.

[Outro]  
We’ll build up Your house, we’ll stand in the light,  
With every prayer, we’ll fight the good fight.  
For the God of Israel, our hearts will sing,  
In Your mighty presence, our praises bring.
