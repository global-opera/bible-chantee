[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
As dear children, we will follow,  
In the light, our hearts will shine,  
Walking in love, as Christ has shown us,  
His sacrifice, a gift divine.  
No unclean thoughts, nor words of folly,  
In our hearts, let gratitude rise,  
For we were once in darkness,  
Now we stand as children wise.

Chorus  
Walk in love, let our hearts unite,  
Shining bright, as children of the light.  
Redeeming time in these days of strife,  
Filled with Your Spirit, Lord, we find our life.

Verse 2  
Awake, arise, let the light surround us,  
Reprove the darkness, let truth be known,  
In every moment, give thanks with gladness,  
In every heart, let Your love be sown.  
Not as fools, but wise in seeking,  
Understanding all Your will,  
With hymns and psalms, our voices lifting,  
In the silence, let our hearts be still.

Chorus  
Walk in love, let our hearts unite,  
Shining bright, as children of the light.  
Redeeming time in these days of strife,  
Filled with Your Spirit, Lord, we find our life.

Bridge  
Husbands love, as Christ loves the church,  
Wives, respect, in reverence we search,  
Together we stand, in unity we thrive,  
Reflecting Your love, our hearts come alive.

Outro  
In You, Lord, we find our song,  
With grateful hearts, we’ll carry on,  
In love, in light, forever strong,  
Together we walk, where we belong.
