[TITLE]
To Him Who Overcomes

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the midst of golden lights,  
You hold the stars in Your hand,  
You see our works, our weary fights,  
Yet call us to stand.  
You know our hearts, the trials faced,  
Through patience, we have trod,  
But Lord, we’ve lost our first embrace,  
Restore us, faithful God.

Chorus  
To Him who overcomes,  
We will rise, we will sing,  
Crowned with life, our victory,  
In Your name, we take wing.  
Fear not the trials that come,  
For You are by our side,  
Eternal life awaits,  
In You, our hearts abide.

Verse 2  
In Smyrna's pain, we lift our eyes,  
Though tribulation’s near,  
You call us rich, in faith we rise,  
Your promise, crystal clear.  
Even in the darkest night,  
With courage, we will stand,  
For You, O Lord, are our light,  
Our lives held in Your hand.

Bridge  
With every word, the sword of truth,  
We fight against the lies,  
Holding fast to all You’ve said,  
In faith, we will arise.  
We’ll cast aside the snares of sin,  
And cling to what is pure,  
For in Your strength, we’re born again,  
In You, we are secure.

Outro  
He who has an ear, let him hear,  
The Spirit calls us near,  
In every church, in every heart,  
Your love, our greatest cheer.  
To Him who overcomes,  
We lift our voices high,  
In the midst of Paradise,  
With You, we’ll never die.
