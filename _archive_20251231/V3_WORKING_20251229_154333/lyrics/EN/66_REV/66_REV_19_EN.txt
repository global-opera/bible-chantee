[TITLE]
Alleluia, The Lord Reigns

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heavens, a voice declares,  
"Alleluia! Our God, He cares.  
Salvation, glory, honor, and might,  
Righteous judgments in His sight."  
The great and mighty, He has made right,  
Judged the earth, brought forth His light.  

Chorus:  
Alleluia! The Lord omnipotent reigns,  
Let us rejoice, in His love, we remain.  
The marriage of the Lamb has come,  
Clothed in righteousness, we are one.  
Alleluia, forever we sing,  
To our Savior, our Lord, our King!  

Verse 2:  
Twenty-four elders bow in praise,  
With beasts of glory, their voices raise.  
"To our God, let all serve and fear,  
Both small and great, His love draws near."  
As thunder roars and waters crash,  
In His presence, our hearts will thrash.  

Chorus:  
Alleluia! The Lord omnipotent reigns,  
Let us rejoice, in His love, we remain.  
The marriage of the Lamb has come,  
Clothed in righteousness, we are one.  
Alleluia, forever we sing,  
To our Savior, our Lord, our King!  

Bridge:  
Faithful and True, on a white horse He rides,  
With fire in His eyes, our hope abides.  
Crowned with glory, His name we proclaim,  
KING OF KINGS, forever the same.  
With a sharp sword, He speaks with might,  
In the darkness, He brings the light.  

Chorus:  
Alleluia! The Lord omnipotent reigns,  
Let us rejoice, in His love, we remain.  
The marriage of the Lamb has come,  
Clothed in righteousness, we are one.  
Alleluia, forever we sing,  
To our Savior, our Lord, our King!  

Outro:  
Oh, Alleluia! The heavens resound,  
In our hearts, His love is found.  
Alleluia, forever we'll cry,  
To the Lamb of God, who reigns on high!
