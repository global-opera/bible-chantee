[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I saw a new heaven, a new earth arise,  
Where tears are wiped away, no more sorrow in our eyes.  
The holy city, adorned like a bride,  
Descending from Heaven, our hearts open wide.  

Chorus  
Behold, the glory of the Lamb so bright,  
In the light of God, we walk by faith, not sight.  
No more pain or darkness, forever we will dwell,  
In the New Jerusalem, where all is well.
