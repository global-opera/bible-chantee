[TITLE]
He Who Sees All

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I saw the Lord upon the altar high,  
Commanding all the posts to shake and cry,  
Though they flee to the depths or rise to the skies,  
No place can hide from His all-seeing eyes.  
He searches all the corners, every hidden place,  
In the depths of the sea, He knows our face.  

Chorus  
He who sees all, the Lord of hosts,  
With mercy and justice, He calls us close.  
In the shadow of sin, His hand will guide,  
Restoration awaits, in His love we abide.  

Verse 2  
He builds the heavens, the earth He commands,  
His mighty voice stirs the sea and lands.  
Though the sinners claim they’ll not face the sword,  
The eyes of the Lord are watching, adored.  
Yet in His wrath, a remnant remains,  
For the house of Jacob, His promise sustains.  

Chorus  
He who sees all, the Lord of hosts,  
With mercy and justice, He calls us close.  
In the shadow of sin, His hand will guide,  
Restoration awaits, in His love we abide.  

Bridge  
In that day, He’ll raise the fallen walls,  
Restoring the tabernacle, His grace enthralls.  
The plowman and reaper, their harvest will blend,  
Mountains will flow with wine, as His blessings descend.  
He’ll plant us again in the land of our birth,  
No more to be pulled from the soil of our worth.  

Chorus  
He who sees all, the Lord of hosts,  
With mercy and justice, He calls us close.  
In the shadow of sin, His hand will guide,  
Restoration awaits, in His love we abide.  

Outro  
So rise, O people, with hearts sincere,  
In the arms of the Father, His love draws near.  
He who sees all, our hope and our song,  
In His presence, forever, we belong.
