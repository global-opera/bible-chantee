[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, we hear Your voice, so clear,  
For three transgressions, judgment near.  
Moab’s fire shall rise and fall,  
In Kerioth, they’ll heed Your call.  
For Judah turned from laws divine,  
Their hearts in darkness, they crossed the line.  

Chorus  
We turn our hearts, O God, to You,  
In humble prayer, our spirits renew.  
With trembling hands, we seek Your face,  
Awake our souls, reveal Your grace.  

Verse 2  
O Israel, how have you strayed,  
Sold the righteous for silver laid?  
The meek are lost, the poor are shunned,  
Yet in Your mercy, Lord, we run.  
You brought us forth from Egypt's night,  
Through wilderness to promised light.  

Chorus  
We turn our hearts, O God, to You,  
In humble prayer, our spirits renew.  
With trembling hands, we seek Your face,  
Awake our souls, reveal Your grace.  

Bridge  
Press down upon us, Lord, we plead,  
As carts are heavy, we feel the need.  
In our weakness, come and save,  
Let not the mighty be left as slaves.  
We lay our pride before Your throne,  
Restore our hearts, let truth be known.  

Chorus

Outro  
So let the fire of Your love ignite,  
In every heart, bring forth the light.  
We seek Your mercy, Lord, today,  
In repentance, show us the way.
