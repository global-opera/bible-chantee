[TITLE]
The Good News of Peace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains,  
The Lord declares His might,  
Jealous for His people,  
In Him, we find our light.  
He rebukes the raging waters,  
And calms the stormy sea,  
His vengeance like a fire,  
Against all wickedness, He’s free.  

Chorus  
Oh, who can stand before His wrath?  
Who can bear the weight of sin?  
Yet in the day of trouble,  
Our refuge lies within.  
The Lord is good, a fortress strong,  
In Him, we place our trust,  
He breaks our chains, and leads us on,  
In His love, we find our rest.  

Verse 2  
The mountains quake before Him,  
The hills begin to melt,  
All creation bows in reverence,  
In His power, we are dealt.  
Though darkness may pursue us,  
His light will guide the way,  
With good news on the mountain,  
He brings peace to those who pray.

Bridge  
Let the echoes of His promise,  
Bring hope where fear has reigned,  
Though the foes may rise against us,  
In His name, we shall be saved.  
With the feet of those who bring,  
The tidings of His peace,  
We will gather in His presence,  
In His grace, our fears release.
