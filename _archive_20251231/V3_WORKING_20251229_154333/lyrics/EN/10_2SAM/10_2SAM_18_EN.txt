[TITLE]
Absalom, My Son

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the oak, where the battle raged,  
David stood with heavy heart, as the warriors engaged.  
He numbered all his men, commanded with great care,  
But his love for Absalom brought a sorrow rare.  

[Chorus]  
O Absalom, my son, my son,  
Though the arrows flew and the battles won,  
In my heart, you’re forever near,  
O Absalom, my son, my son,  
In the silence, I shed every tear.  

[Verse 2]  
The clash of swords, the cries of war,  
In the woods of Ephraim, we faced the score.  
Yet in the midst of strife, a father’s love remained,  
For even in this dark, his heart was pained.  

[Chorus]  
O Absalom, my son, my son,

[Bridge]  
How could I have wished to stand in your place?  
To shield you from the fate that you had to face.  
Though the world may rise against, I still would fight,  
For the love of a father shines through the night.  

[Verse 3]  
And when the news came through, my heart stood still,  
Cushi’s words like daggers, pierced my very will.  
“O King, your son is lost; the battle has been won,”  
But in my heart, the war raged on, my beloved son.

[Outro]  
So I weep upon the gates, my heart laid bare,  
In the echoes of the wood, I sense your air.  
For love transcends the battles we must face,  
O Absalom, my son, forever in grace.
