[TITLE]
Courage in the Battle

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When the king of Ammon passed away,  
A son named Hanun took his place to stay.  
David sent his kindness, a gesture of grace,  
But the hearts of the princes turned cold in their space.  

Pre-Chorus  
They questioned his honor, they mocked his intent,  
With shame they received what the king had sent.  
But David stood firm, in his heart he believed,  
In the strength of the Lord, the battles are achieved.  

Chorus  
Be of good courage, stand tall in the fight,  
For our God is with us, His truth is our light.  
We’ll play the men for our people, our kin,  
In the name of the Lord, let the victory begin.  

Verse 2  
Joab faced the armies, the foes in array,  
With wisdom and courage, he led them that day.  
Two brothers united, in battle they stood,  
For the cities of God, they would fight for the good.  

Pre-Chorus  
“Should the foes be too strong, I will call for your aid,  
In the strength of our God, let our fears be laid.”  
For when we are faithful, and trust in His plan,  
The enemies tremble, and our victory stands.  

Chorus  
Be of good courage, stand tall in the fight,  
For our God is with us, His truth is our light.  
We’ll play the men for our people, our kin,  
In the name of the Lord, let the victory begin.  

Bridge  
The Syrians fell, and the foes turned to flee,  
In the name of our God, we claim victory.  
For every battle fought, His power will reign,  
In the midst of the strife, we’ll lift up His name.  

Chorus  
Be of good courage, stand tall in the fight,  
For our God is with us, His truth is our light.  
We’ll play the men for our people, our kin,  
In the name of the Lord, let the victory begin.  

Outro  
So let us remember, with hearts filled with praise,  
In every battle fought, it’s His name we’ll raise.  
For the Lord is our shield, our comfort, our guide,  
With courage and faith, in His love we abide.
