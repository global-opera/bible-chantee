[TITLE]
A House of Praise

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In days of peace, O Lord, You’ve set me free,  
From shadows deep, You’ve walked beside me.  
A king in cedar, my heart now sings,  
Yet in a tent, Your presence clings.  

[Chorus]  
Who am I, O Lord, that You would choose,  
To dwell among us, our hearts to use?  
Your mercy flows like rivers wide,  
Establish my house, O Lord, abide.  

[Verse 2]  
You called me from the fields, so lowly,  
To lead Your people, You’ve made me holy.  
In every battle, Your hand was near,  
You’ve cut my foes, and calmed my fear.  

[Chorus]  
Who am I, O Lord, that You would choose,  
To dwell among us, our hearts to use?  
Your mercy flows like rivers wide,  
Establish my house, O Lord, abide.  

[Bridge]  
Now, O King of Glory, hear my cry,  
For generations, let Your name be high.  
Let the heavens echo, let the earth rejoice,  
In every promise, we lift our voice.  

[Verse 3]  
You’ve spoken truth, Your word stands tall,  
In every season, You’ve answered my call.  
With grateful hearts, we gather here,  
To magnify the One who draws us near.  

[Chorus]  
Who am I, O Lord, that You would choose,  
To dwell among us, our hearts to use?  
Your mercy flows like rivers wide,  
Establish my house, O Lord, abide.  

[Outro]  
Forever, O Lord, let Your kingdom stand,  
With every blessing, we lift our hands.  
In faith we trust, our hearts aligned,  
A house of praise, in You we find.
