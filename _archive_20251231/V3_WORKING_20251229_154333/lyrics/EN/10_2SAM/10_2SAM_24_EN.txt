[TITLE]
In Your Hands, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When David’s heart was heavy, he counted every soul,  
In pride, he sought the number, forgetting God’s control.  
Joab warned the king of folly, yet the word was set to move,  
A heart turned from the faithful, to the numbers he would prove.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, we fall,  
Your mercy reigns above it all.  
When shadows loom and fear takes flight,  
We trust in You, our guiding light.  

Verse 2  
From Dan to Beersheba, they roamed the land so vast,  
Eight hundred thousand valiant, their strength a fleeting cast.  
But when the dawn broke over, the truth began to sting,  
For sin had brought destruction, the weight of David’s king.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, we fall,  
Your mercy reigns above it all.  
When shadows loom and fear takes flight,  
We trust in You, our guiding light.  

Bridge  
“Let us fall in Your embrace,  
For Your mercies are enough.  
The battle is not ours to face,  
In Your grace, we rise and trust.”  

Verse 3  
The angel paused in sorrow, as judgment filled the night,  
David cried for mercy, “O Lord, make wrongs to right.”  
A threshing floor was chosen, a place to build an altar,  
With humble hearts we gather, Your love will never falter.  

Chorus  
In Your hands, O Lord, we fall,  
Your mercy reigns above it all.  
When shadows loom and fear takes flight,  
We trust in You, our guiding light.  

Outro  
So we raise our altars high,  
With offerings of love and praise,  
In every trial, we rely,  
On Your grace through all our days.  
In Your hands, O Lord, we fall,  
Your mercy reigns, our all in all.
