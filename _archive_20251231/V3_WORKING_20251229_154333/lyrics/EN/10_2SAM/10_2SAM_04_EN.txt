[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of the throne,  
Where hearts tremble and fear has grown,  
Saul's son fell, a tale of woe,  
Mephibosheth, lost in sorrow’s flow.  
The echoes of a battle cry,  
In the silence, the faithful sigh,  
Yet through the dark, Your light will rise,  
For in our weakness, Your strength is wise.  

[Chorus]  
O Lord, You are my refuge, my shield,  
In every storm, Your love revealed.  
From ashes to glory, You lift me high,  
With every heartbeat, I call to the sky.  
You redeem the broken, the lost, the slain,  
In Your embrace, I will rise again.  

[Verse 2]  
The hands of wickedness strike with deceit,  
But Your justice, O Lord, will not retreat.  
As David mourned the blood on his hands,  
He knew Your promise, Your perfect plans.  
For every battle, You stand by our side,  
In the valley of shadows, our hearts abide.  
You turn our sorrow into joyful songs,  
With You, O Lord, we forever belong.

[Bridge]  
So let the heavens declare Your name,  
In every heart, ignite the flame.  
From the depths of despair, we rise and sing,  
For You alone, O Lord, are our King.  
In every trial, Your love remains,  
You break the chains and heal our pains.
