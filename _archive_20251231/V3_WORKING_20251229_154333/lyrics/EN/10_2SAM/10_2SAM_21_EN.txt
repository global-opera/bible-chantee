[TITLE]
Atonement in the Harvest

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of David, famine spread,  
Year after year, the land lay dead.  
He sought the Lord, with humble heart,  
For the blood that cried, where did it start?  

Pre-Chorus:  
The Gibeonites came, with sorrowed plea,  
For justice and mercy, they sought to be free.  
“Not gold nor silver can heal our land,  
Atonement we seek, at Your command.”

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, hear our cry,  
In our brokenness, we lift our eyes.  
Through the shadows of sorrow, Your light will shine,  
In the depths of our struggle, Your love divine.  

Verse 2:  
Rizpah weeping, on the rock she lay,  
In sackcloth and ashes, she mourned each day.  
Protecting the fallen, her heart so true,  
A mother’s lament, forever anew.  

Pre-Chorus:  
David heard her call, and he took the way,  
To honor the past, to bring peace today.  
He gathered the bones, laid them to rest,  
In the heart of the land, where the faithful are blessed.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, hear our cry,  
In our brokenness, we lift our eyes.  
Through the shadows of sorrow, Your light will shine,  
In the depths of our struggle, Your love divine.  

Bridge:  
So when giants arise, and the battles seem lost,  
We stand in the promise, no matter the cost.  
With courage like David, we’ll fight and we’ll stand,  
For the Lord is our Savior, our hope is at hand.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of the harvest, hear our cry,  
In our brokenness, we lift our eyes.  
Through the shadows of sorrow, Your light will shine,  
In the depths of our struggle, Your love divine.  

Outro:  
Atonement in the harvest, restoration’s call,  
In the fields of His mercy, He gathers us all.  
From the ashes of mourning, new life will arise,  
In the arms of our Savior, our hope never dies.
