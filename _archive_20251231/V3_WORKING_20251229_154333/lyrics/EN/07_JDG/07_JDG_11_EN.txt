[TITLE]
A Vow of Valor

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Tob, a warrior stood,  
Jephthah the brave, though misunderstood.  
Cast out by kin, yet chosen by grace,  
In the shadows of doubt, he found his place.  

[Verse 2]  
The elders came pleading, in their time of need,  
"Come lead us to battle, for we trust in your creed."  
He raised his voice, with a heart full of pain,  
"Did you not cast me out? Why are you here again?"  

[Chorus]  
Through valleys of shadows, we stand in Your might,  
With courage unbroken, we’ll follow the light.  
O Lord, hear our vow, as we rise from the dust,  
In battles before us, we place all our trust.  

[Verse 3]  
He sent forth his message, the king of Ammon,  
"Why wage war against us? Our land was not won."  
With the Spirit upon him, he marched forth with pride,  
For the Lord had delivered; they stood side by side.  

[Chorus]  
Through valleys of shadows, we stand in Your might,  
With courage unbroken, we’ll follow the light.  
O Lord, hear our vow, as we rise from the dust,  
In battles before us, we place all our trust.  

[Bridge]  
And when victory came, with joy in his heart,  
His daughter approached, a scene torn apart.  
With timbrels and dances, she welcomed him home,  
Yet a vow to the Lord, he could not disown.  

[Verse 4]  
“Father, I see your heart, you’ve called on His Name,  
If the Lord's hand has moved, I’ll not bring you shame.”  
So she went to the mountains, to weep and to mourn,  
For in faith they both stood, a new hope was born.  

[Chorus]  
Through valleys of shadows, we stand in Your might,  
With courage unbroken, we’ll follow the light.  
O Lord, hear our vow, as we rise from the dust,  
In battles before us, we place all our trust.  

[Outro]  
So we sing of the faith, that breaks every chain,  
Of Jephthah and his daughter, their courage remains.  
In the stories of old, we find strength anew,  
For the Lord is our Captain, forever so true.
