[TITLE]
Deliverance in Our Midst

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows grow,  
The hearts of Israel turned away,  
Forgotten were Your mighty ways,  
They wandered from the light of day.  
But You saw their cries, O Lord,  
In the depths of their despair,  
You raised up men with courage bold,  
To lead them through the darkest air.  

Chorus  
Oh, deliverance in our midst,  
Your hand, O Lord, we will not miss,  
From valleys low to mountains high,  
With You, our battles will not die.  
In every trial, we will stand,  
For You, our God, hold out Your hand.  

Verse 2  
Othniel, brave, with Spirit's might,  
He fought the king of bitter night,  
And in his hand, a sword of truth,  
He brought the dawn, restored our youth.  
Yet time would pass, and hearts would stray,  
In cycles of our sin’s decay,  
But still, You heard our earnest plea,  
And sent as judge, our victory.  

Chorus  
Oh, deliverance in our midst,  
Your hand, O Lord, we will not miss,  
From valleys low to mountains high,  
With You, our battles will not die.  
In every trial, we will stand,  
For You, our God, hold out Your hand.  

Bridge  
Ehud came with purpose clear,  
A secret plan to quell our fear,  
In shadows deep, he took a stand,  
With faith and courage at his hand.  
The trumpet blew, the armies came,  
With hearts ignited in Your name,  
For every foe that dares to rise,  
We trust in You, our battle cries.  

Chorus  
Oh, deliverance in our midst,  
Your hand, O Lord, we will not miss,

In every trial, we will stand,  
For You, our God, hold out Your hand.  

Outro  
So let us rise, united strong,  
In every fight, we’ll sing our song,  
For You, our Savior, lead the way,  
In every battle, come what may.  
Oh, deliverance in our midst,  
We’ll trust in You; our hope persists.
