## 
Verse 1  
In the land where shadows fall,  
The trees sought a king to rule them all.  
Olive, fig, and vine stood tall,  
But none would leave their call.  
“Should I forsake my fruit so sweet,  
To wear the crown and taste defeat?”  
But bramble spoke, “Come trust in me,  
I’ll reign in darkness, can’t you see?”

Chorus  
Oh, Shechem, hear the warning cry,  
From the mount, let truth not die.  
Jotham’s voice calls through the night,  
Seek the light, oh seek the light.  
In the choice of kings, let hearts be true,  
For what you sow, you shall reap too.  
Oh, Shechem, hear the warning cry,  
Turn from shadows, lift your eyes high.

Verse 2  
Abimelech rose with silver bright,  
His brother’s blood stained the night.  
In the name of kin, he took the throne,  
But treachery ripened, seeds of disown.  
The hearts of men turned cold and dark,  
In the silence, echoed a spark.  
“Who is this king that you would serve?  
For his cruel hand has lost its nerve.”

Bridge  
Let fire come out and cleanse the land,  
As the bramble reigns with a hollow hand.  
But the faithful will rise, from ashes and pain,  
To seek the true King, forever to reign.  
In the battles we fight, in the choices we make,  
May our hearts align, for our faith's sake.  
For He who delivers, from shadows and strife,  
Is the One who gives hope, the breath of our life.
