[TITLE]
Awake, O Israel

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days when shadows fell,  
We cried out for a guiding light,  
Deborah arose, a mother's call,  
To lead us forth into the fight.  
O hear, O kings, give ear today,  
We sing of victories won,  
Praise the Lord, our hearts rejoice,  
For the battles fought are done.  

Chorus  
Awake, awake, O Israel,  
Lift your voice, let praises swell,  
For the Lord has fought our fight,  
In His power, we find our might.  
With courage bold, we stand as one,  
In His name, our victory’s won!  

Verse 2  
Mountains tremble, heavens weep,  
When our God goes forth in might,  
The river flows, the stars align,  
Against the darkness, shines His light.  
Oh, blessed be the hands that serve,  
With willing hearts, we take our stand,  
In the valleys, high places too,  
We trust in His commanding hand.

Bridge  
From the ashes, we will rise,  
Every heart ignited,  
For those who love the Lord shall shine,  
As the sun in all its brightness.  
Let our praises fill the skies,  
As we march on in His grace,  
With every step, we claim His name,  
In His love, we find our place.

Outro  
So let all thine enemies perish,  
O Lord, Your love will never cease,  
In Your hands, we trust our fate,  
As we rest in perfect peace.
