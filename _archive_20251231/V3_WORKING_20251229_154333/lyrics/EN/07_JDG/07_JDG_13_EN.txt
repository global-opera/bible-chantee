[TITLE]
A Nazarite's Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows dwell,  
Israel’s heart turned cold as stone,  
But a promise from on high,  
A child was born, not left alone.  
Manoah prayed with fervent heart,  
For guidance from the Lord above,  
An angel’s voice broke through the dark,  
A beacon of His endless love.

Chorus  
Shout, O heavens, rejoice anew,  
For a Nazarite is called to rise,  
With strength and purpose, he’ll break through,  
Deliverance from the darkest lies.  
O God, You move in mystery,  
Your plans unfold, we trust in Thee.

Verse 2  
With barren womb, hope seemed so far,  
Yet faith ignited with the Word,  
“Drink no wine, let not your heart stray,  
For in this child, My will is stirred.”  
The flame ascended, heaven kissed,  
Manoah fell, the wonder grew,  
“For we have seen the face of God,  
His promises forever true.”

Bridge  
In the camp of Dan, the Spirit flows,  
Empowering hearts where courage grows,  
From Zorah’s fields to battles won,  
With every breath, Your will be done.  
O Samson, rise, your strength is here,  
In faith we stand, we have no fear.

Outro

O God of wonders, hear our plea,  
In every moment, You are with me.
