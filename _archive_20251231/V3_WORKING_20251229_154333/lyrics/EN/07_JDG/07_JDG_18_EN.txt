[TITLE]
In the Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In days of old, no king to guide,  
The tribe of Dan sought lands to bide,  
With hearts of courage, they set their gaze,  
To find the place where hope ablaze.  
Through valleys low and mountains high,  
They searched for peace beneath the sky.

Chorus  
Oh, lead us, Lord, to the land of promise,  
Where Your grace flows like a river wide.  
In the quiet, we hear Your calling,  
To claim the blessings You provide.  
With faith as our shield, we shall not falter,  
In unity, we rise and stand.  
For in Your hands, our hope is anchored,  
Oh, guide us, Lord, to the promised land.

Verse 2  
They found a people living free,  
In careless ways, no fear to see.  
"Arise," they said, “the time has come,  
To take the land, let’s not be dumb."  
With hearts ignited by Your might,  
They marched ahead, into the night.

Bridge  
When shadows fall and fears surround,  
We’ll trust in You, our solid ground.  
For in the battles, You are near,  
Our hearts will sing, no room for fear.  
With every step, we’ll praise Your name,  
For by Your grace, we’ll never be the same.

Amen.
