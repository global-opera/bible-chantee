[TITLE]
Where You Go, I Will Go

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days when judges ruled the land,  
A famine struck, and hope slipped from our hands.  
Elimelech led his family far away,  
To Moab's shores, where they would stay.  
But grief would come, as shadows cast their gloom,  
Naomi wept, her heart filled with empty room.

Chorus:  
Oh, Ruth, your love, so steadfast and true,  
Where you go, I will go, I’ll follow you.  
Your people my people, your God my own,  
In the midst of trials, we will not be alone.  
Together we’ll journey, through the night and day,  
For the Lord’s hand guides us, He lights our way.

Verse 2:  
Orpah turned back, to her mother's embrace,  
But Ruth clung to Naomi, in this sacred place.  
"Don't urge me to leave you, my heart's intertwined,  
In life or in death, your fate will be mine."  
With tears they journeyed, through the dust and the pain,  
To Bethlehem, where hope would rise again.

Chorus:  
Oh, Ruth, your love, so steadfast and true,  
Where you go, I will go, I’ll follow you.  
Your people my people, your God my own,  
In the midst of trials, we will not be alone.  
Together we’ll journey, through the night and day,  
For the Lord’s hand guides us, He lights our way.

Bridge:  
Naomi returned, with a heart heavy and sore,  
"Call me Mara, for bitterness I bore."  
Yet the harvest was near, and the promise would bloom,  
In the emptiness, God brings life from the tomb.  
Oh, His grace will restore, what was lost in the night,  
With each step we take, He’ll turn wrongs into right.

Chorus:

Outro:  
So lift up your eyes, in the darkest of hours,  
For faith will lead us, and grace will empower.  
In the land of our trials, we shall find our rest,  
With our hearts intertwined, we are truly blessed.  
Where you go, I will go, forever I’ll stay,  
In the love of the Lord, we’ll find our way.
