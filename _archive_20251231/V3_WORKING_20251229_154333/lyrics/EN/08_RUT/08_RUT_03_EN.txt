### Title: "At Your Feet, My Redeemer"

Verse 1  
In the shadows, a mother’s heart,  
Sought for rest, a brand new start.  
With whispers soft, she guides her way,  
To the threshing floor, where hope would stay.  
Ruth, adorned, with courage bright,  
Anointed grace, she steps into the night.  

Chorus  
At Your feet, my Redeemer, I lay my life,  
In Your arms, my Savior, I find my peace.  
With every promise, You hold me near,  
In the stillness, I know You are here.  

Verse 2  
Boaz, a man of honor true,  
In the quiet, he sees her too.  
“Spread your wings, let love abound,  
For in this grace, our hope is found.”  
She draws near, the night is still,  
A heart of virtue, Your love to fulfill.  

Chorus  
At Your feet, my Redeemer, I lay my life,  
In Your arms, my Savior, I find my peace.  
With every promise, You hold me near,  
In the stillness, I know You are here.  

Bridge  
“Sit still, my daughter,” the mother says,  
For the Lord will work in ways we can’t assess.  
In the morning light, a new day will rise,  
With blessings poured out, beyond the skies.  
Fear not, my heart, for He is near,  
In every battle, He calms my fear.  

Chorus  
At Your feet, my Redeemer, I lay my life,  
In Your arms, my Savior, I find my peace.  
With every promise, You hold me near,  
In the stillness, I know You are here.  

Outro  
Oh, at Your feet, my Redeemer,  
In Your love, I am made whole.  
Resting in Your faithfulness,  
You are my heart, You are my soul.
