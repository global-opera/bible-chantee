[TITLE]
The Captains of Our Faith

[LYRICS]

VERSE 1:  
In the land of promise, where the brave hearts stand,  
Twenty-four thousand strong, united hand in hand.  
Jashobeam leads the way, in the first month’s light,  
With the children of Perez, they rise to win the fight.  

CHORUS:  
O Lord, we stand together, in Your mighty name,  
Through valleys and through mountains, we lift our hearts in flame.  
From every tribe and nation, Your strength we shall proclaim,  
In the captains of our faith, we find our hope, our aim.  

VERSE 2:  
Dodai in the second, with courage on his side,  
Benaiah the priest, in the third, our hearts abide.  
Asahel, Shamhuth, each one called to serve,  
A chorus of the faithful, with hearts that will not swerve.  

CHORUS:  
O Lord, we stand together, in Your mighty name,  
Through valleys and through mountains, we lift our hearts in flame.  
From every tribe and nation, Your strength we shall proclaim,  
In the captains of our faith, we find our hope, our aim.  

BRIDGE:  
In the shadows of the night, Your light will guide our way,  
For every trial we face, we trust in You, we say.  
No number can define us, like stars in heaven's grace,  
With David’s fierce devotion, we seek Your holy face.  

VERSE 3:  
From the treasures of the earth, to vineyards that we tend,  
In the fields of harvest, Your promises defend.  
Over flocks and herds, Your blessings overflow,  
In the strength of our unity, together we will grow.  

CHORUS:  
O Lord, we stand together, in Your mighty name,  
Through valleys and through mountains, we lift our hearts in flame.  
From every tribe and nation, Your strength we shall proclaim,  
In the captains of our faith, we find our hope, our aim.  

OUTRO:  
O Lord, we stand united, our banners raised on high,  
In the captains of our faith, we’ll soar into the sky.  
With every step we take, our hearts forever free,  
In the captains of our faith, we find our victory.
