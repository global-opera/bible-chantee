[TITLE]  
"Generations of Grace"

[Verse 1]  
In the book of ages, our names are found,  
Stories of the faithful, in glory they abound.  
From Judah’s line to Benjamin’s grace,  
Every heart, a story, in this holy place.  

[Chorus]  
O Lord, we stand in awe,  
Of Your love that never fades,  
Generations rise and fall,  
But Your promise ever stays.  
From the ruins, You restore,  
In our hearts, forever more,  
We are woven in Your plan,  
Faithful God, we take our stand.

[Verse 2]  
The priests and Levites, they served with pride,  
In the house of worship, where Your Spirit guides.  
With every offering, with every song,  
They lifted up our voices, where we all belong.  

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
And through the trials, through the night,  
You are our shepherd, our guiding light.  
In every lineage, in every name,  
Your hand is writing, we’re never the same.  
From Gibeon’s father to the sons of old,  
Your story’s told in hearts of gold.  

[Chorus]

[Outro]  
So let our lives be testaments,  
Of grace that leads us on,  
In every heart, in every song,  
Your love will carry on.  
O Lord, we stand in awe,  
Together we sing on and on.
