[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the days of David, a king full of grace,  
He gathered the people, all in their place.  
The Levites were numbered, their hearts set on high,  
To serve in the temple, beneath the sky.  

(Chorus)  
We are the servants, called by His name,  
In the house of the Lord, we praise the same.  
With instruments ringing, our voices we raise,  
Forever united, we sing of His grace.

(Verse 2)  
From the sons of Levi, a lineage true,  
With Aaron and Moses, their calling anew.  
In the courts of the holy, they stand day and night,  
Offering praise, bringing darkness to light.  

(Chorus)  
We are the servants, called by His name,  
In the house of the Lord, we praise the same.  
With instruments ringing, our voices we raise,  
Forever united, we sing of His grace.

(Bridge)  
Every morning we gather, to thank and adore,  
In the chambers of worship, our spirits will soar.  
For the Lord has given, His people sweet rest,  
In the heart of Jerusalem, we are forever blessed.  

(Chorus)  
We are the servants, called by His name,  
In the house of the Lord, we praise the same.  
With instruments ringing, our voices we raise,  
Forever united, we sing of His grace.

(Outro)  
So let us remember, the call we've received,  
To serve in His presence, in faith we believe.  
With hearts full of worship, we carry the light,  
In the house of the Lord, we shine ever bright.
