[TITLE]
In the Face of the Battle

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the fallen, a king's heart beats true,  
David sent his kindness, to comfort and renew.  
But whispers turned to doubt, in the land of Ammon's pride,  
They shamed the king's messengers, casting honor aside.  

Chorus  
Oh, be of good courage, let our hearts be strong,  
For the Lord is our fortress, in Him we belong.  
With valiant spirits rising, we stand for our King,  
In the face of the battle, His praises we sing.  

Verse 2  
A thousand talents silver, they sought to hire might,  
Chariots and horsemen, prepared for the fight.  
Yet Joab called the warriors, with courage in his voice,  
He set the ranks before them, in faith we will rejoice.

Bridge  
For the cities of our God, we fight in His name,  
With every clash of swords, our hearts aflame.  
When the enemy surrounds, we will not despair,  
For the Lord goes before us, His presence is our prayer.

Outro  
So we march on together, united in His grace,  
With faith as our armor, we will seek His face.  
In every trial and struggle, His victory we claim,  
For the battle belongs to the Lord, forever the same.
