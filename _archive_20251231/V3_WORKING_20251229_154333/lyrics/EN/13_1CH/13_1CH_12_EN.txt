[TITLE]
Mighty in Battle

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of Ziklag, David stood so strong,  
With mighty men around him, united in the throng.  
From the tribes of Judah, and the sons of Gad,  
They came to lift his spirit, in the fight for what he had.  

Chorus  
Mighty in battle, we stand as one,  
For the Lord is our refuge, our victory’s begun.  
With hearts united, we lift our voice,  
In the midst of our struggles, we will rejoice.  

Verse 2  
Faces like the lions, swift as roes on high,  
Armed with faith and courage, they would never cry.  
From Benjamin to Ephraim, they gathered at his call,  
To turn the kingdom’s tide, to God they’d give their all.  

Chorus  
Mighty in battle, we stand as one,  
For the Lord is our refuge, our victory’s begun.  
With hearts united, we lift our voice,  
In the midst of our struggles, we will rejoice.  

Bridge  
Oh, the spirit came upon them, as they marched in faith,  
With a perfect heart, they gathered, to proclaim His grace.  
In the face of the tempest, they stood side by side,  
For the joy of the Lord was their strength and their guide.  

Verse 3  
From the valleys to the mountains, their courage would not fade,  
With shields and spears before them, His power would invade.  
They feasted in His presence, with joy that knows no end,  
In unity and purpose, their hearts would now transcend.  

Chorus  
Mighty in battle, we stand as one,  
For the Lord is our refuge, our victory’s begun.  
With hearts united, we lift our voice,  
In the midst of our struggles, we will rejoice.  

Outro  
So let us come together, with faith to light the way,  
For the God of all creation, is with us every day.  
Mighty in battle, our hearts ablaze,  
In His love and mercy, we will always praise.
