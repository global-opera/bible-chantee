[TITLE]
The Call of the Valiant

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the days of old, the tales were told,  
Of Reuben's sons, brave and bold,  
From Gilead's heights to the valleys wide,  
With hearts aflame, they fought side by side.  
Through trials fierce, they raised their shields,  
In faith they stood, their fate revealed.  
For the Lord was with them, in every fight,  
Guiding their hands, their path was light.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we cry to You, our battle cry,  
With trusting hearts, we lift on high,  
In the face of giants, we will stand,  
For our God is mighty, we’re in His hand.  
We’ll fight the fight, with faith as our sword,  
For we are the valiant, we trust in the Lord.  

(Verse 2)  
From Bashan's heights to the land of foes,  
They took their stand, where the river flows,  
With courage deep, they sought Your face,  
In every struggle, they found Your grace.  
The Hagarites fell, the victory won,  
For in their hearts, the battle had begun.  
They cried out loud, their voices raised,  
And in their trust, their God was praised.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we cry to You, our battle cry,  
With trusting hearts, we lift on high,  
In the face of giants, we will stand,  
For our God is mighty, we’re in His hand.  
We’ll fight the fight, with faith as our sword,  
For we are the valiant, we trust in the Lord.  

(Bridge)  
When shadows fall and doubts arise,  
We’ll hold the line, we’ll lift our eyes,  
For You are with us, our guiding light,  
In every battle, You turn wrong to right.  
We’ll never falter, we’ll never yield,  
With hearts of valor, our fate revealed.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, we cry to You, our battle cry,  
With trusting hearts, we lift on high,  
In the face of giants, we will stand,  
For our God is mighty, we’re in His hand.  
We’ll fight the fight, with faith as our sword,

(Outro)  
So let the trumpets sound, let praises rise,  
For in our battles, we’ll hear the skies,  
The God of Israel, our hearts will claim,  
In every struggle, we’ll lift His name.
