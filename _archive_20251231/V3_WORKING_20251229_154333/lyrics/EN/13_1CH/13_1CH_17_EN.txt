[TITLE]
Your Promise, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of my heart, I hear Your call,  
As I dwell in this house, I long to see it all.  
You said, “Do not fear, my servant, I am near,  
For I’ve walked with you, my David, year by year.”

Chorus:  
O Lord, Your promise stands forever,  
A kingdom built by Your own hand.  
You will be our God, we will be Your people,  
In this sacred place, we will take our stand.  
Forever, forever, we will praise Your name,  
O Lord of hosts, our hearts shall never wane.

Verse 2:  
From the fields of sheep, You called me forth,  
To lead Your chosen ones, to spread Your worth.  
You’ve cast down enemies, Your grace is our guide,  
In every trial, through every tide.

Chorus:  
O Lord, Your promise stands forever,  
A kingdom built by Your own hand.  
You will be our God, we will be Your people,  
In this sacred place, we will take our stand.  
Forever, forever, we will praise Your name,  
O Lord of hosts, our hearts shall never wane.

Bridge:  
Who am I that You would lift me high?  
Your mercy flows like rivers in the sky.  
You’ve made me a vessel for Your glory,  
In every heartbeat, I will tell Your story.

Chorus:  
O Lord, Your promise stands forever,  
A kingdom built by Your own hand.  
You will be our God, we will be Your people,  
In this sacred place, we will take our stand.  
Forever, forever, we will praise Your name,  
O Lord of hosts, our hearts shall never wane.

Outro:  
Now, let it be established, let Your name arise,  
In the house of David, we lift our eyes.  
For You, O God, have spoken from above,  
In Your mighty arms, we rest in love.  
Forever, forever, we will praise Your name,  
O Lord of hosts, our hearts shall never wane.
