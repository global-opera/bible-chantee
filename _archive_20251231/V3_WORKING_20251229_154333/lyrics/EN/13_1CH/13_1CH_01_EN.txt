Verse 1  
From Adam's breath, the story unfolds,  
A lineage of faith, in hearts it's told.  
Seth, Enosh, and Kenan's call,  
Mahalaleel, Jered, they rise and fall.  
Through the ages, Your promise remains,  
In the bloodlines of hope, we find our names.  

Chorus  
Generations of grace, we stand in awe,

From Abraham's seed to the stars above,  
A legacy of faith, a story of love.

Through Peleg and Joktan, the earth divides,  
Yet Your Spirit guides, where Your love abides.
