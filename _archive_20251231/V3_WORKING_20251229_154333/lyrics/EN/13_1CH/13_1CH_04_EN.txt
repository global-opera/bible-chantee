[TITLE]
Echoes of Faith

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the line of Judah, a legacy stands,  
Pharez and Hezron, from ancient lands.  
With hearts full of sorrow, Jabez cried,  
"Bless me, O Lord, be my guide."  
Enlarge my borders, keep me from harm,  
Let Your hand be my shelter, my calm.  

(Chorus)  
Echoes of faith, through generations flow,  
In every battle, our trust will grow.  
From valleys of sorrow to mountains of grace,  
We stand in Your promise, we seek Your face.  

(Verse 2)  
Othniel and Caleb, the brave and the bold,  
In the heart of the fight, their stories unfold.  
Craftsmen and warriors, they labored with pride,  
Each name a reminder, with God as their guide.  
From cities of refuge to fields of the meek,  
Your faithfulness, Lord, is the hope that we seek.  

(Chorus)  
Echoes of faith, through generations flow,  
In every battle, our trust will grow.  
From valleys of sorrow to mountains of grace,  
We stand in Your promise, we seek Your face.  

(Bridge)  
In the days of Hezekiah, they rose up in might,  
With courage and boldness, they entered the fight.  
For the flocks that were scattered, they sought out the land,  
In the shadows of giants, they took their stand.  
You are our shepherd, our fortress, our song,  
In the echoes of history, to You we belong.  

(Chorus)  
Echoes of faith, through generations flow,  
In every battle, our trust will grow.  
From valleys of sorrow to mountains of grace,  
We stand in Your promise, we seek Your face.  

(Outro)  
So we lift up our voices, in unison praise,  
For the stories of old, and the hope of our days.  
In the line of Your promise, forever we’ll stand,  
Echoes of faith, held in Your hand.  
Echoes of faith, held in Your hand.
