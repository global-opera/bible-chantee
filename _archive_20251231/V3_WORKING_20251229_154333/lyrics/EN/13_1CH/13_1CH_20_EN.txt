[TITLE]
Victory in His Name

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the season when the kings arise,  
Joab led the brave to fight,  
Through the shadows, the courage lies,  
Against the foes, we claim the light.  
Rabbah fell beneath their might,  
As David wore the crown adorned,  
Gold and glory shining bright,  
In the battle, our hearts are warmed.

Chorus  
We sing of victory in His name,  
From the ashes He brings forth our praise.  
With every battle, we’re not the same,  
In His love, our fears are erased.  
Oh, the giants may roar, but we stand tall,  
With our Savior, we will never fall.  
In the name of Jesus, we rise and proclaim,  
Our battles are won, for His love remains.

Verse 2  
From Gezer’s fields to Gath's high walls,  
Sibbechai's hand was swift and true,  
Elhanan's strength, the giant falls,  
In the name of the Lord, we pursue.  
Jonathan stood with faith so bold,  
With every blow, the darkness fled,  
The tales of old, forever told,  
In our hearts, the promise spread.
