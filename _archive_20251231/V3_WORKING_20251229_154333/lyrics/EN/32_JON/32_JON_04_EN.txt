[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of my anger,  
I sought to flee Your grace,  
For I knew, O Lord, Your mercy,  
Could turn the hearts of this place.  
In my sorrow, I cried out,  
"Take my life, it's too much to bear,"  
But You spoke with gentle wisdom,  
"Jonah, do you even care?"

Chorus:  
Spare the city, Lord of mercy,  
With Your kindness, bring them near.  
In the depths of our despairing,  
Let Your love be ever clear.  
Teach us, Lord, to hold compassion,  
For the lost who cannot see,  
Spare the city, O our Savior,  
Let Your heart be shown through me.

Verse 2:  
I watched the gourd You gave me,  
As the sun beat down with heat,  
Yet I mourned for that small shelter,  
While the souls were lost in defeat.  
You remind me of Your kindness,  
Of the grace that I must share,  
For the ones who roam in darkness,  
Need Your light and loving care.

Bridge:

For in every life You cherish,  
Is the hope of a brighter day.
