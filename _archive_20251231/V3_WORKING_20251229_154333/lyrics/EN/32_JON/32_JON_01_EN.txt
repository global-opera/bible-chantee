[TITLE]
In the Depths, I Cry

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
You called to Jonah, rise and go,  
To Nineveh, where wickedness flows.  
But he turned away, fled from Your sight,  
Into the storm, lost in the night.  

Chorus  
O Lord of heaven, hear my plea,  
In the depths of darkness, set me free.  
Though I wander far, Your love will chase,  
In the tempest's roar, I seek Your grace.  

Verse 2  
The winds were raging, the sailors cried,  
Yet Jonah slept, in the ship he lied.  
Awakened by fear, they cast their lots,  
His heart was heavy with burdens he’s got.  

Chorus  
O Lord of heaven, hear my plea,  
In the depths of darkness, set me free.  
Though I wander far, Your love will chase,  
In the tempest's roar, I seek Your grace.  

Bridge  
Take me, cast me into the sea,  
For I know it’s my sin that brought this to be.  
Yet even in chaos, Your mercy surrounds,  
In the belly of despair, Your hope is found.  

Verse 3  
Three days in darkness, a moment so still,  
A prayer of repentance, I seek Your will.  
From the depths I cry, Your voice I hear,  
In the silence of sorrow, You draw me near.  

Chorus  
O Lord of heaven, hear my plea,  
In the depths of darkness, set me free.  
Though I wander far, Your love will chase,  
In the tempest's roar, I seek Your grace.  

Outro  
So here I stand, with a heart renewed,  
In the presence of grace, my soul subdued.  
With vows I offer, my life to Thee,  
O Lord of mercy, You’ve rescued me.
