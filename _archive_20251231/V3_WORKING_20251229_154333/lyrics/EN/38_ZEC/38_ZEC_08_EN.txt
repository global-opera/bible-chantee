[TITLE]
Return to Zion

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
The word of the Lord, it comes to me,  
With a passion for Zion, so fierce and free.  
In the heart of Jerusalem, He'll dwell once more,  
A city of truth, a holy shore.  

Chorus  
Return to Zion, where the old shall stand,  
With staff in hand, in this promised land.  
Boys and girls shall dance in the streets,  
In the love of the Lord, our joy completes.  

Verse 2  
He gathers His people from east and west,  
In truth and righteousness, we find our rest.  
Fear not, O children, your hands be strong,  
For in His mercy, we will belong.  

Chorus  
Return to Zion, where the old shall stand,  
With staff in hand, in this promised land.  
Boys and girls shall dance in the streets,  
In the love of the Lord, our joy completes.  

Bridge  
Speak the truth to one another,  
Let peace flow like a stream,  
In the light of His presence,  
We awaken from our dream.  
No more curses, we are blessed,  
In His arms, we find our rest.  

Chorus  
Return to Zion, where the old shall stand,  
With staff in hand, in this promised land.  
Boys and girls shall dance in the streets,  
In the love of the Lord, our joy completes.  

Outro  
Let us go speedily, to seek His face,  
In the heart of Jerusalem, we find our place.  
God is with us, let the nations see,  
In His truth and peace, forever free.
