[TITLE]
Path of the Righteous

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
As David faced the twilight of his days,  
He spoke to Solomon with wisdom’s gaze,  
“Be strong, my son, stand firm in the fight,  
Walk in the ways of the Lord; shine bright.  
Keep His commandments, hold fast to His ways,  
In truth and in love, let your heart sing praise.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, guide us, Lord, on the path of the righteous,  
With courage and strength, let our hearts be ignited.  
In every battle, through every storm,  
We’ll trust in Your promise, forever transformed.  

[Verse 2]  
Remember the kindness shown in the night,  
To Barzillai’s sons, let mercy take flight.  
And those who have cursed, may they find Your grace,  
For You hold our lives in a sacred embrace.  
In shadows of doubt, we’ll seek Your decree,  
Your throne established, forever shall be.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, guide us, Lord, on the path of the righteous,  
With courage and strength, let our hearts be ignited.  
In every battle, through every storm,  
We’ll trust in Your promise, forever transformed.  

[Bridge]  
With each step we take, Lord, we’ll honor Your name,  
In wisdom and love, we’ll never be the same.  
As Solomon rose, with a heart full of grace,  
We’ll walk in Your light, in this holy place.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, guide us, Lord, on the path of the righteous,  
With courage and strength, let our hearts be ignited.  
In every battle, through every storm,  
We’ll trust in Your promise, forever transformed.  

[Outro]  
So hear our cry, as we lift up our song,  
In faith and in hope, we know we belong.  
With hearts aligned, in Your presence we stand,  
Forever united, guided by Your hand.
