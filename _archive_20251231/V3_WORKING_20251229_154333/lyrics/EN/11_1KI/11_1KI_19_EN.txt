[TITLE]
In the Whisper of Your Voice

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the night, Elijah fled,  
With a heart weighed down by fear, he cried, "I’m dead."  
Underneath the juniper, he sought to find his peace,  
But You sent an angel, Lord, to bring his soul release.  

Chorus  
In the whisper of Your voice, I find my strength anew,  
When the storms rage all around, Lord, I will trust in You.  
Though the mountains quake and fall, and fire lights the skies,  
In the stillness of Your presence, my heart learns how to rise.  

Verse 2  
"Why are you here, my servant?" You asked him in the cave,  
He spoke of all his battles, of the hearts he longed to save.  
Yet You showed him a remnant, a faithful few so bold,  
In the face of every challenge, Your promise will unfold.

Bridge  
You are the God who sees me; You know my every fight,  
When I feel so alone, Lord, You’re my guiding light.  
Through the valleys and the shadows, Your love will carry me,  
In the quiet of the night, Your peace will set me free.

Outro  
So I’ll stand upon the mountain, in the promise of Your grace,  
With every breath within me, I’ll seek Your holy face.  
In the whisper of Your voice, my fears begin to cease,  
Forever in Your presence, I walk in perfect peace.
