[TITLE]
Fear Not, O Israel

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When the battle calls us near,  
With chariots loud and foes in fear,  
We will lift our eyes and see,  
The Lord our God, our victory.  
He brought us up from Egypt's night,  
His promise shines, our guiding light.  

Chorus  
Fear not, O Israel, for He is near,  
In every struggle, He calms our fear.  
With mighty hands, He fights for thee,  
Our hearts secure, in victory.  

Verse 2  
Oh, let not hearts grow faint or weak,  
For in His strength, our souls shall speak.  
What man is there who feels the dread?  
Return to peace, let fear be shed.  
For those who fight with hearts so bold,  
Will see the wonders He foretold.  

Chorus  
Fear not, O Israel, for He is near,  
In every struggle, He calms our fear.  
With mighty hands, He fights for thee,  
Our hearts secure, in victory.  

Bridge  
When cities rise against our way,  
Let us proclaim His peace today.  
If they reject, we’ll stand our ground,  
In Him, our hope and strength is found.  
Though trials rage and shadows loom,  
We trust in Him, He’ll guard our room.  

Chorus  
Fear not, O Israel, for He is near,  
In every struggle, He calms our fear.  
With mighty hands, He fights for thee,

Outro  
So raise your voice in faith and song,  
For in His love, we will stand strong.  
Fear not, O Israel, lift your gaze,  
In all our battles, we’ll sing His praise.
