[TITLE]
Be Strong and Courageous

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of promise, we stand today,  
Moses spoke to Israel, guiding our way.  
A hundred and twenty, his days were complete,  
Yet the Lord before us, His victory sweet.  

[Chorus]  
Be strong and courageous, do not be afraid,  
For the Lord our God is here, His love won’t fade.  
He goes before us, with power and grace,  
In every battle, we’ll seek His face.  

[Verse 2]  
Joshua leading, the people unite,  
The nations before us, they tremble in fright.  
The covenant promised, a land flowing free,  
With milk and honey, our hearts will agree.  

[Chorus]  
Be strong and courageous, do not be afraid,  
For the Lord our God is here, His love won’t fade.  
He goes before us, with power and grace,  
In every battle, we’ll seek His face.  

[Bridge]  
When trials surround us, and shadows draw near,  
We will not falter, for You are here.  
Your word is our anchor, our hope, and our song,  
In faith, we will follow, we’ll stand firm and strong.  

[Verse 3]  
Moses wrote the song, a witness to keep,  
For the hearts of our children, His promises deep.  
Though we may wander, and hearts may stray,  
The Lord’s love will find us, and lead us His way.  

[Chorus]  
Be strong and courageous, do not be afraid,  
For the Lord our God is here, His love won’t fade.  
He goes before us, with power and grace,  
In every battle, we’ll seek His face.  

[Outro]  
So gather together, all people of grace,  
With voices united, we’ll sing and embrace.  
For the Lord is our guide, through valleys and heights,  
In Him we find courage, in Him we find light.
