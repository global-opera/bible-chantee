[TITLE]
Journey to the Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the wilderness, we stand,  
Moses spoke, by God’s command,  
“Turn your hearts, rise and go,  
To the land I promised long ago.”  
Through the trials and the fear,  
The Lord our God is ever near,  
He’s gone before, He lights the way,  
In the dark, He’ll be our stay.  

Chorus:  
Oh, remember, He is faithful,  
Through the storms and in the night,  
He fights our battles, leads us onward,  
In His love, we find our light.  
He has set the land before us,  
Fear not, take hold of His grace,  
For the promise stands forever,  
In His arms, we find our place.  

Verse 2:  
Forty years in desert's sway,  
But His word will guide our way,  
He bore our burdens, lifted high,  
As a father with His child nearby.  
When giants rise and cities stand tall,  
The Lord our God will never fall,  
He’s promised us a land so fair,  
A place of rest, His love to share.

Bridge:  
So let us rise, let faith arise,  
With every step, we lift our eyes,  
To Him who goes before,  
In every valley, we’ll explore.  
For Caleb's heart, we'll follow true,  
In the face of giants, we’ll break through,  
His love is stronger, His truth will stand,  
We will take the promise land.
