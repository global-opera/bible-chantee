Verse 1  
In the land where shadows linger,  
A soul lies silent on the ground,  
The elders gather in their wisdom,  
With heavy hearts, they look around.  
They take the heifer, pure and gentle,  
Lead her down to the roughened vale,  
In this act of grace and mercy,  
They seek the truth behind the veil.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, be merciful, we pray,  
Cleanse our hearts from guilt and shame,  
In the valley of redemption,  
We call upon Your holy name.  
Lay not innocent blood upon us,  
Forgive us, Lord, we plead anew,  
In the valley of redemption,  
We find our hope, our strength in You.

Verse 2  
When the battle rages fiercely,  
And captives fall beneath our might,  
A woman stands in grief and longing,  
Her heart torn from the love of light.  
We bring her home, a gentle spirit,  
She mourns the life she’s left behind,  
In the sacred space of healing,  
New love can blossom, hearts entwined.

Bridge  
For the stubborn hearts, the wayward souls,  
We bring them forth to You, O Lord,  
In Your justice, let them see,  
The path of grace, the saving Word.  
And when the evening shadows fall,  
We bury sin beneath the tree,  
For in the darkness, hope shall rise,  
In Your embrace, we are set free.
