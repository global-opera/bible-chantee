Verse 1  
In the quiet of the evening, hear the call of grace,  
At the end of every season, there’s a sacred place.  
Let the burdens lift, let the chains fall free,  
For the Lord’s release, brings us peace eternally.  

Chorus  
Open wide your hand, let love overflow,  
To the brother in need, let compassion grow.  
For the poor shall never cease, in the land we roam,  
In our hearts, there’s a promise, we’re never alone.
