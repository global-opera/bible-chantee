Verse 1  
Oh, that You would rend the heavens,  
Come down, let Your glory flow,  
Mountains quake with Your presence,  
Let the nations tremble so.  
Fire burns, the waters boil,  
Make Your name known, Lord, we cry,  
You’ve done wonders we’ve not sought,  
In Your love, we lift our sigh.

Chorus  
Come, O Lord, we wait for You,  
You are faithful, strong and true.  
In our hearts, we long for grace,  
Show us mercy, seek Your face.  
As we stand in awe of Thee,  
We are clay, You are our plea.  
Father, hear our cry today,  
In Your arms, Lord, lead the way.

Verse 2  
Since the dawn of time, we’ve wandered,  
In our hearts, no one calls Your name.  
Yet You meet the hearts that seek You,  
In our darkness, still the same.  
We confess our sins before You,  
Like the leaves, we fade away.  
But in You, O Lord, we find hope,  
By Your grace, we’re here to stay.
