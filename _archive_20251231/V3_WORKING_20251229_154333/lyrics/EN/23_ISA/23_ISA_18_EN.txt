[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Woe to the land, where shadows take flight,  
Beyond the rivers, in the dark of night.  
Swift messengers sent on the waves,  
To a people scattered, the Lord still saves.  
Terrible and strong, from ages past,  
Their hearts are meted, but love holds fast.

Chorus  
Lift your eyes, behold the sign,  
The trumpet sounds, His love divine.  
In the harvest heat, He takes His rest,  
In the dew of His mercy, we are blessed.  
O dwellers of the earth, hear His call,  
The Ensign raised, for one and all.

Verse 2  
Like a cloud of dew, His grace will fall,  
The perfect bud awaits His call.  
He prunes the branches, cuts away the strife,  
That we may bear the fruit of life.  
Left together, for the fowls to see,  
Yet in His presence, we find our plea.

Bridge  
O Lord of hosts, we bring our gifts,  
From scattered lands, our spirit lifts.  
Though rivers spoil, and hearts grow weary,  
You reign in power, forever near me.  
At Mount Zion, Your name we raise,  
In solemn worship, our hearts ablaze.

Outro  
Woe to the land, but hope will arise,  
In every heart, His truth shall rise.  
With eyes wide open, we stand as one,  
In the light of the Savior, the victory's won.
