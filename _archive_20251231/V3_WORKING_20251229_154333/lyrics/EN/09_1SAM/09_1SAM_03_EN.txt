Verse 1  
In the silence of the night, a whisper calls my name,  
Like the child, Samuel, I long to seek Your face.  
When the lamp of hope burns low, and shadows fill the space,  
I will rise and come to You, Lord, fill my heart with grace.  

Chorus  
Speak, Lord, Your servant hears,  
In the stillness, calm my fears.  
With each word, may I draw near,  
Speak, Lord, Your servant hears.  

Verse 2  
Eli’s voice, it fades away, Your truth begins to shine,  
In the darkness, I am found, Your vision now is mine.  
Though the world may not perceive the call that’s deep within,  
I am here, O Lord, to learn, to trust, to rise again.  

Chorus  
Speak, Lord, Your servant hears,  
In the stillness, calm my fears.  
With each word, may I draw near,  
Speak, Lord, Your servant hears.  

Bridge  
Oh, let the heavens open wide,  
Reveal Your heart, let love abide.  
In every promise, I will stand,  
For I am Yours, held in Your hand.  

Verse 3  
As I walk this path of faith, I know You are my guide,  
In the trials and the storms, I will abide inside.  
All of Israel will see, Your glory through my life,  
With Your truth, I’ll stand as one, a beacon in the strife.

Outro  
So I lay my heart before You, Lord,  
With each breath, I seek Your word.  
In the shadows, light will break,  
Speak, Lord, Your servant hears, for Your sake.
