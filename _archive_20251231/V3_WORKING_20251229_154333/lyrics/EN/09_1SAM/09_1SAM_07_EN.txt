[TITLE]
Ebenezer: Hitherto the Lord has Helped Us

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows lingered,  
The Ark of God was kept in fear,  
Twenty years of silent longing,  
Israel cried, “O Lord, draw near.”  
Samuel called with hearts returning,  
“Put away the gods of stone,  
Prepare your hearts, embrace His mercy,  
Serve Him only; He’ll bring us home.”

Chorus  
Hitherto, the Lord has helped us,  
In our battles, He will stand.  
With a mighty hand, He saves us,  
Trust in Him, our Rock, our Lamb.  
Raise the stone of remembrance,  
Ebenezer, we proclaim,  
For the Lord has fought our battles,  
And forever bears our name.

Verse 2  
Gathered at Mizpeh, hearts were broken,  
Pouring out our tears like rain,  
We confess our sins before Him,  
Crying out, “O Lord, sustain!”  
As the Philistines draw nearer,  
Fear would seek to steal our peace,  
But Samuel’s prayers rose like thunder,  
And the heavens brought release.

And the storms begin to rage,  
We will stand upon His promise,  
In His love, we find our place.  
Every stone of help before us,  
Every trial turned to grace,  
With our hearts aligned in worship,  
We will seek His holy face.

Outro

In His name, we find our refuge,  
In His love, we are made whole,  
Hitherto, the Lord has helped us,
