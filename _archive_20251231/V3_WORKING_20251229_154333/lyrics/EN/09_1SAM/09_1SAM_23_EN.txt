[TITLE]
Deliverance in the Wilderness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Keilah, the Philistines rise,  
They rob the threshing floors, beneath darkened skies.  
David sought the Lord, "Shall I fight this day?"  
The voice from heaven answered, "Go, I’ll lead the way."  

Chorus:  
Oh, we will trust in You, Lord, our battles, You will fight,  
In the shadows of the wilderness, You bring us to the light.  
Fear not, for You are with us, Your hand will guide our way,  
In every storm and struggle, we lift our hearts and pray.  

Verse 2:  
Through the fear of his men, David stayed true,  
Again he sought the Lord, for the fight to ensue.  
"Arise, go down to Keilah, the victory is near,"  
With courage in his heart, he cast aside his fear.

Bridge:  
In the wilderness of Ziph, where shadows loom so wide,  
Jonathan came to strengthen, to stand right by his side.  
“Fear not, my friend, for you will reign,”  
In every trial and trouble, His promises remain.  

Verse 3:  
Saul sought him daily, to bring him to despair,  
Yet God was his defender, through every whispered snare.  
When the messenger came rushing, “The Philistines invade!”  
Saul turned from his pursuit, and a path for David made.

Outro:  
So we stand in faith, Lord, in valleys and the heights,  
With hearts that sing Your praises, through the darkest nights.  
In the wilderness, we’ll follow, wherever You may lead,  
For in You, our mighty Savior, we find all that we need.
