Verse 1  
In the land of Philistines, a burden heavy lay,  
The ark of the Lord, lost in shadows of the day.  
They called for their diviners, seeking what to do,  
"Send it not empty, return Him treasures true."

Chorus  
Oh, we lift our hearts to You,  
Return the glory, we seek You anew.  
In our darkest hours, Your light will shine,  
Holy Lord, we stand before Your divine.

Verse 2

A tribute to the One who reigns both low and high.  
They made a new cart, with kine they set apart,  
The path to Bethshemesh, guided by His heart.

Bridge  
Who can stand before this holy God?  
With trembling hearts, we come to You,  
For in Your presence, we find our peace,  
Oh, Lord of glory, our souls release.

Verse 3  
The harvesters rejoiced as they lifted up their gaze,  
The ark had come to greet them, bringing hope ablaze.  
They offered up their sacrifices, with hearts that were sincere,  
In the field of Joshua, they worshiped without fear.

Outro
