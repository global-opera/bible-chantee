[TITLE]
The Faith of Jonathan

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Upon a day, the courage stirred,  
Jonathan called his friend,  
“Let’s rise against the enemy,  
For God is with us till the end.”  
In shadows deep, where fear may dwell,  
He sought the garrison’s might,  
With hearts ablaze, they climbed the rocks,  
To face the darkness, bring the light.

Chorus:  
For there is no restraint to God,  
He saves by few or many,  
With faith as our battle cry,  
We stand, united, ready.  
Oh, the trembling earth beneath our feet,  
As victory draws near,  
With hearts aligned in trust and love,  
We shall not live in fear.

Verse 2:  
In secret paths, they made their way,  
To where the foes lay still,  
With signs from heaven guiding them,  
They climbed with steadfast will.  
“Come up to us!” the Philistines called,  
And Jonathan knew the sign,  
“Let’s fight with all our might today,  
For God’s the victory divine.”

Bridge:  
So let the world see, let the heavens declare,  
The power of faith, the promise of prayer,  
With every step, we follow the call,  
In battles of life, our God is our all.  
We rise like the dawn, with hope in our hearts,  
In the face of giants, we’ll never depart.

So lift your eyes, and sing the song,  
For God is with us, we’ll carry on.  
In every fight, through darkest night,  
With faith like Jonathan, we’ll shine His light.
