[TITLE]
Covenant of Souls

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the fields where the shepherds roam,  
Two hearts were bound, no longer alone,  
Jonathan’s love, a brother so true,  
Knit together in a bond that grew.  
A robe exchanged, a promise made,  
In the shadows, their faith displayed.  

Chorus  
For the Lord is with us, through every trial,  
In the midst of the battle, we'll stand reconciled.  
Though the giants may roar, and the darkness may come,  
With hearts intertwined, in His love we are one.  

Verse 2  
David returned, the songs filled the air,  
Women rejoiced, with joy everywhere.  
But Saul grew angry, his heart turned to fear,  
For the hand of the Lord, upon David was near.  
A javelin thrown, but David stood tall,  
In the midst of the storm, he would not fall.

Bridge  
From valleys of shadow, to mountains of grace,  
We walk in the light, in His warm embrace.  
With every struggle, and every fight,  
We rise with the strength of His glorious light.

So let us remember the covenant made,  
In the light of His presence, we will not fade.

In the love of our Savior, His truth we will find.
