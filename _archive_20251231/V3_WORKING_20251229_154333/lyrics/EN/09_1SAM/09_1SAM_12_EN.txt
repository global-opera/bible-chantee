[TITLE]
Behold Our King

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In days of old, the people cried,  
For a king to rule, their hearts denied,  
Yet the Lord, their God, had led them true,  
From Egypt’s chains, He brought them through.  
Oh, Samuel stood, with wisdom clear,  
“Witness against me, come draw near.  
Have I wronged you, taken what’s yours?  
In the light of God, my heart endures.”  

(Chorus)  
Behold our King, the Lord we praise,  
In every storm, through all our days.  
Fear not, O hearts, for He’s our guide,  
With truth and grace, forever abide.  

(Verse 2)  
Remember well the acts of grace,  
When He delivered from the darkest place.  
Though we have strayed, and lost our way,  
Still He’s our hope, our light, our stay.  
When foes surround and troubles rise,  
We cry to Him, with desperate sighs.  
“Forgive our sins, O Lord, we plead,  
In our hearts, Your truth we need.”  

(Chorus)  
Behold our King, the Lord we praise,  
In every storm, through all our days.  
Fear not, O hearts, for He’s our guide,  
With truth and grace, forever abide.  

(Bridge)  
Stand still and see the wonders He’ll do,  
In thunder and rain, He’ll make all things new.  
For the Lord will not forsake His own,  
Through trials faced, His love is shown.  
Only serve Him, with all your might,  
For He is our King, our guiding light.  

(Chorus)  
Behold our King, the Lord we praise,  
In every storm, through all our days.  
Fear not, O hearts, for He’s our guide,  
With truth and grace, forever abide.  

(Outro)  
So turn to Him, O people, rise,  
In faith we stand, with hopeful eyes.  
For the Lord is good, His promises sure,  
With hearts united, our faith endures.  
Behold our King, forever reign,  
In every heart, His love remains.
