Verse 1  
In the shadows of the night, I come to You,  
A heart in need of strength, Lord, I pursue.  
In the temple of Your grace, I seek to find,  
The bread of life, Lord, to fill my weary mind.  

Chorus  
Oh, give me hallowed bread, in this hour of need,  
Your presence, Lord, sustains me, my soul’s true feed.  
For in the trials faced, I’ll lift my voice and sing,  
You are my refuge, my Savior, my King.
