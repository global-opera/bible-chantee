[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In the silence of the heart, the fool declares,  
There is no God, in darkness he despairs.  
But You look down from heaven, Lord, to see,  
If any seek Your face, if any long to be free.  

Chorus:  
Oh, bring salvation from Zion,  
Let the captives rise and sing.  
In the midst of all our trials,  
You are our refuge, our King.  
In the darkness, You are shining,  
Hope and mercy, You will bring.  
Oh, bring salvation from Zion,  
Our hearts to You we cling.  

Verse 2:  
All have turned aside, none do good on their own,  
In the shadows of despair, we wander far from home.  
Yet in the fear of wickedness, Your light will show,  
In the generation of the righteous, Your love will flow.

Bridge:  
Lift up the weary, mend the broken hearts,  
You are the counsel, Lord, who never departs.  
In every cry of anguish, in every tear we shed,  
You are the hope of Israel, the promise never dead.

Oh, that salvation, Lord, would come,
