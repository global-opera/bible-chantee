[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
The Lord reigneth, let the earth behold,  
With cherubims, His majesty unfolds.  
In Zion’s heights, His greatness we proclaim,  
We lift our hearts to praise His holy name.  

Chorus  
Exalt the Lord our God,  
Worship at His feet,  
His love is everlasting,  
In His presence, we are complete.  
Holy, holy, is the King,  
Let our voices rise and sing,  
Exalt the Lord our God,  
In His grace, our spirits cling.  

Verse 2

In the shadow of Your wings, we find our hearts abide.
