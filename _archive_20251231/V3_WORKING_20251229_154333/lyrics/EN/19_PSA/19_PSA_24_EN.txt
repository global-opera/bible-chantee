[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
The earth is Yours, O Lord, we sing,  
And all that dwells, in joy we bring,  
You founded it upon the seas,  
In every wave, Your power frees.  

Chorus  
Lift up your heads, O gates, arise,  
The King of Glory draws nigh,  
Strong and mighty, battle's light,  
Come, let us worship, hearts unite.  

Verse 2  
Who shall ascend to Your holy place?  
The pure in heart, who seek Your face,  
With clean hands lifted, we stand in awe,  
In the shadow of Your love, we draw.  

Chorus  
Lift up your heads, O gates, arise,  
The King of Glory draws nigh,  
Strong and mighty, battle's light,  
Come, let us worship, hearts unite.  

Bridge  
This is the generation, Lord,  
That seeks Your face, in one accord,  
From every heart, we long to see,  
The beauty of Your majesty.  

Chorus

Outro  
Who is this King of Glory?  
The Lord of hosts, our story,  
Come, let us lift our praise and sing,
