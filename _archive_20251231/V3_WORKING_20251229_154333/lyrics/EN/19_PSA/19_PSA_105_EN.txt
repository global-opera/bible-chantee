[TITLE]
Remember His Deeds

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O give thanks unto the Lord,  
Call upon His mighty name,  
Make known His wondrous works,  
Let hearts rejoice in praise.  
Seek the Lord, His strength anew,  
In every trial, seek His face,  
For His promises are true,  
Forever, He’ll embrace.  

Chorus:  
We remember His deeds,  
The wonders of His ways,  
In the shadows, He is near,  
Our hearts lift in praise.  
He’s the God who leads us on,  
Through every night and day,  
With joy, we sing our song,  
In His love, we stay.  

Verse 2:  
He remembered His covenant,  
From Abraham to all His kin,  
A law to guide forevermore,  
In every loss, we’ve seen Him win.  
Through trials, He raised us high,  
In weakness, we find His grace,  
With every tear, He draws us nigh,  
In His arms, we find our place.

Through valleys deep, we’ll praise His name,  
For in Him, we find our sight.
