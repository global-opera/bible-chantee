[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
As the deer longs for the streams,  
My soul cries out, O Lord, it seems,  
I thirst for You, the living God,  
When will I stand where You are, my heart awed?  

Verse 2:  
Tears like rivers flow day and night,  
In the silence, I seek Your light,  
"Where is my God?" the whispers say,  
Yet in my heart, I long and pray.  

Chorus:  
Why so downcast, O my soul?  
Hope in God, He makes me whole.  
For I will praise Him, my heart's delight,  
In the dark, He is my light.  

Verse 3:  
I remember the joy of Your house,  
With a multitude, I sang aloud,  
But now my spirit feels so low,  
Yet I will trust, in You I’ll grow.  

Bridge:  
Deep calls to deep, Your waves surround,  
Your lovingkindness, my heart has found.  
In the night, Your song will be near,  
O my God, I know You hear.  

Chorus:  
Why so downcast, O my soul?  
Hope in God, He makes me whole.  
For I will praise Him, my heart's delight,  
In the dark, He is my light.  

Outro:  
I will say to my God, my Rock,  
You lift me up, You are my hope.  
Though the shadows may try to stay,  
I’ll trust in You, come what may.  
I’ll trust in You, come what may.
