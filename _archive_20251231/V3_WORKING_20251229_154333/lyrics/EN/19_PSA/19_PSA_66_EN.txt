[TITLE]
Come and See His Works

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Make a joyful noise, all the earth,  
Lift your voices, sing His worth.  
Honor to His glorious name,  
In His power, we proclaim.  
Terrible in deeds, our God so grand,  
Even the foes will bow at His command.  

Chorus  
Come and see the works of God,  
Through the sea He made a way,  
In the flood, we found our hope,  
He brings joy to every day.  
O bless our God, lift your praise,  
He holds our souls, in Him we stay.  

Verse 2  
Through the fire and through the night,  
In our trials, He is our light.  
You’ve proved us, Lord, like silver’s fire,  
In our affliction, we seek You higher.  
You’ve brought us forth from every test,  
Into a place of peace and rest.

Bridge  
I cried out to You, Lord, I was heard,  
In my troubles, I found Your Word.  
You’ve not turned Your heart away,  
Your mercy, Lord, is here to stay.  
So I will offer all I have,  
With lips that sing and hearts that laugh.
