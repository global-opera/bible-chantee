[TITLE]
Tremble at His Presence

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
When Israel walked from the chains of night,  
The house of Jacob, led by His light.  
Judah, His sanctuary, where grace abides,  
In the midst of the storm, our Savior guides.  

Chorus:  
Tremble, O earth, at the sound of His name,  
Mountains and valleys, we lift You the same.  
O sea, why do you flee? Jordan, stand still,  
At the presence of God, we bow to His will.  

Verse 2:  
The sea saw His power, and turned in despair,  
The Jordan was driven, by love laid bare.  
Little hills skipping, like lambs in the field,  
In awe of the One, our hearts are revealed.  

Chorus:  
Tremble, O earth, at the sound of His name,  
Mountains and valleys, we lift You the same.  
O sea, why do you flee? Jordan, stand still,  
At the presence of God, we bow to His will.  

Bridge:  
He turns rocks into rivers, flint into streams,  
In His mighty presence, we find our dreams.  
God of Jacob, our refuge, our song,  
In Your arms of mercy, to You we belong.  

Chorus:  
Tremble, O earth, at the sound of His name,  
Mountains and valleys, we lift You the same.  
O sea, why do you flee? Jordan, stand still,  
At the presence of God, we bow to His will.  

Outro:  
Tremble, O earth, at the presence of grace,  
In the power of love, we find our place.  
God of Jacob, forever we sing,  
In Your sanctuary, our praises take wing.
