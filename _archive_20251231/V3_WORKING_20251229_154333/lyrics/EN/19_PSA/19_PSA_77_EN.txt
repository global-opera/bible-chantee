[TITLE]
In the Night I Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I cry unto You, my God, with all my voice,  
In the day of trouble, Lord, I seek Your choice.  
My soul is restless, in the dark I weep,  
Yet still I long for You, my heart will not keep.  

Chorus:  
I remember Your wonders, the works of old,  
In the silence and shadows, Your mercy unfolds.  
Though my spirit feels heavy, and my heart is torn,  
I’ll trust in Your promise, through the night I am worn.  

Verse 2:  
Will You cast me away, will Your love cease to be?  
In the depths of my sorrow, I turn back to Thee.  
I recall all Your kindness, the strength of Your hand,  
You redeemed us, O Lord, with a love that will stand.

Yet in the stillness, You’re my guiding light.
