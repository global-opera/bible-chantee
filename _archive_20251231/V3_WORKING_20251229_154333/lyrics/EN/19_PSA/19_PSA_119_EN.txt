[TITLE]
In Your Ways, O Lord

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Blessed are those who walk in Your light,  
With hearts that seek You, day and night.  
In Your law, we find our way,  
O Lord, guide us, we pray.  

[Chorus]  
Teach me Your statutes, I long to know,  
Your word is my treasure, in my heart it will grow.  
When I wander, Lord, bring me back home,  
In Your love, I will never be alone.  

[Verse 2]  
With my whole heart, I seek Your face,  
You are my refuge, my hiding place.  
In every trial, Your truth is my song,  
In Your arms, Lord, I will be strong.  

[Chorus]  
Teach me Your statutes, I long to know,  
Your word is my treasure, in my heart it will grow.  
When I wander, Lord, bring me back home,  
In Your love, I will never be alone.  

[Bridge]  
Open my eyes to the wonders You hold,  
Your mercies surround me, Your promises bold.  
Even in darkness, Your light is my guide,  
Forever, O Lord, in Your truth I abide.  

[Chorus]  
Teach me Your statutes, I long to know,  
Your word is my treasure, in my heart it will grow.  
When I wander, Lord, bring me back home,  
In Your love, I will never be alone.  

[Outro]  
O Lord, keep me close, in Your ways I will stay,  
With every step forward, I’ll trust and obey.  
Your commandments, my joy, my strength, my delight,  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my light.
