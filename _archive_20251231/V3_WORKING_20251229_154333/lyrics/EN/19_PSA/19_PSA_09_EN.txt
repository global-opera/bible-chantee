[TITLE]
Forever Our Refuge

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I will praise You, Lord, with my whole heart,  
Declaring all Your wondrous works of art.  
In joy I lift my voice, I sing Your name,  
O Most High, forever I’ll proclaim.  

Chorus  
For You are my refuge, my strength in the night,  
In times of trouble, You lead me to light.  
Your judgment is righteous, Your mercy prevails,  
I will trust in You, for Your love never fails.  

Verse 2  
When foes surround me, they shall fall away,  
At Your presence, Lord, darkness turns to day.  
You hold my cause, You’re my defender true,  
In Your arms of grace, I find my peace in You.  

Chorus  
For You are my refuge, my strength in the night,  
In times of trouble, You lead me to light.  
Your judgment is righteous, Your mercy prevails,  
I will trust in You, for Your love never fails.  

Bridge  
Sing praises to the One who dwells in Zion,  
Let all the nations know, we lift our cry on.  
For the humble, the needy, find hope in Your grace,  
We rejoice in salvation, we stand in Your place.  

Chorus  
For You are my refuge, my strength in the night,  
In times of trouble, You lead me to light.  
Your judgment is righteous, Your mercy prevails,  
I will trust in You, for Your love never fails.  

Outro  
The wicked shall tremble, but we will remain,  
In the shadow of Your wings, we’ll call on Your name.  
Forever, our refuge, forever we’ll sing,  
To the God of our salvation, our hearts offering.
