Verse 1  
Bless the Lord, O my soul, lift Your name on high,  
You are clothed in glory, beneath the endless sky.  
With light as Your garment, You stretch the heavens wide,  
In majesty and honor, forever You abide.  

Chorus  
O Lord, my God, how great You are,  
Your wonders fill the earth, shining like a star.  
In every season, with every breath I take,  
I will sing Your praises, my heart awake.  

Verse 2

The creatures find their shelter, in the beauty of Your face.
