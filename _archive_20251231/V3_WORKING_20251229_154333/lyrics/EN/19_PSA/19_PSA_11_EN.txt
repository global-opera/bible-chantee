[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the night,  
Fear whispers to my soul,  
“Flee like a bird to the mountain high,”  
But in You, Lord, I am whole.  
Though the wicked aim their bows,  
And their arrows seek to smite,  
I will stand upon Your promise,  
You are my guiding light.

Chorus:  
In You, O Lord, I trust my heart,  
You are my hope, You won’t depart.  
When the foundations tremble and shake,  
Your love, O Lord, will never break.  
You reign in heaven, all is still,  
In the storm, I know Your will.  
For the righteous find their peace in You,  
O Lord, my strength, my refuge true.

Verse 2:  
Though the trials rage like fire,  
And the tempest roars with might,  
I will lift my eyes to heaven,  
You are my comfort, my delight.  
Your eyes behold the hearts of men,  
You know the paths we tread,  
In the silence, You are speaking,  
In Your truth, I’m gently led.
