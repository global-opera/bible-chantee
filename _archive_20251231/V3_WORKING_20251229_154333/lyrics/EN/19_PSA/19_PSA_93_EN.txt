[TITLE]
Majesty of the King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, You reign in majesty,  
Clothed in strength, in purity.  
The earth stands firm, it cannot sway,  
Your throne established, here to stay.  

Chorus  
You are mightier than the waves,  
Your voice, it calms the storms we face.  
In Your holiness, we find our place,  
Forever, Lord, we seek Your grace.  

Verse 2  
The floods may roar, their voices rise,  
Yet in Your peace, our hearts confide.  
For You are higher than the sea,  
Our refuge, Lord, eternally.  

Chorus  
You are mightier than the waves,  
Your voice, it calms the storms we face.  
In Your holiness, we find our place,  
Forever, Lord, we seek Your grace.  

Bridge  
From everlasting, You remain,  
In every trial, You are our gain.  
Your testimonies, sure and bright,  
Guide us through the darkest night.  

Chorus  
You are mightier than the waves,  
Your voice, it calms the storms we face.  
In Your holiness, we find our place,  
Forever, Lord, we seek Your grace.  

Outro  
O Lord, we worship, hearts in tune,  
In Your presence, we find our room.  
Majesty, our hearts proclaim,  
Forever, Lord, we lift Your name.
