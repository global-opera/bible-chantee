[TITLE]
A Deliverer's Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of despair, a mother’s heart did weep,  
For a child born of promise, her secret she would keep.  
In an ark of bulrushes, she placed him by the shore,  
A legacy of faith, a cry for hope restored.  

Chorus  
Oh, the river flows with grace,  
Through the tears and through the pain,  
God remembers every sigh,  
In His love, we rise again.  
A deliverer called by name,  
From the waters, hope remains,  
With compassion, He will lead,  
Every heartache, every need.  

Verse 2  
Pharaoh’s daughter found him, a babe with gentle cries,  
In her arms, she held the promise, love opened up her eyes.  
Sister stood in quiet faith, God’s plan was set in motion,  
Though he’d wander far away, he’d return with holy devotion.

Bridge  
In the land of Midian, where burdens lay concealed,  
Moses found a purpose, his heart began to heal.  
The cries of his people echoed in the night,  
And God, He heard them calling, their chains would soon take flight.

Outro  
Now we stand in faith and trust, for He hears our every plea,  
In the darkest of our trials, His light will set us free.  
For the promise still is living, in the hearts of those who seek,  
A deliverer for the broken, His strength is made perfect in the weak.
