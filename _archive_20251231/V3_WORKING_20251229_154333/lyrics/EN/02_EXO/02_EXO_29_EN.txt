[TITLE]
The Altar of Consecration

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Your grace, we gather here,  
With offerings pure, our hearts sincere.  
A young bullock, rams without a stain,  
We come to hallow, to lift Your name.  
In the waters we stand, as You wash us clean,  
Clothed in Your light, in Your love we glean.  

Chorus  
We lift our hands, in worship we bow,  
Before the altar, we honor You now.  
With every sacrifice, our hearts laid bare,  
In this holy place, we find You there.  

Verse 2  
Aaron and his sons, adorned in holy dress,  
Anointed with oil, in Your presence we confess.  
The blood upon the altar, a sweet aroma rise,  
In our brokenness, we seek Your skies.  
Each ram and loaf, our lives we bring,  
A wave offering, to our Savior King.  

Chorus  
We lift our hands, in worship we bow,  
Before the altar, we honor You now.  
With every sacrifice, our hearts laid bare,  
In this holy place, we find You there.  

Bridge  
You dwell among us, O Lord, our guide,  
In the tabernacle, forever abide.  
With every lamb we offer, our praises we sing,  
You are our refuge, our everything.  
We remember the promise, the blood that was shed,  
In Your holiness, we find our stead.  

Chorus  
We lift our hands, in worship we bow,  
Before the altar, we honor You now.  
With every sacrifice, our hearts laid bare,  
In this holy place, we find You there.  

Outro  
In the land of our journey, You lead us through,  
We are Your people, forever made new.  
As You sanctify our hearts, we stand in awe,  
O Lord, our God, we worship and draw.  
Amen.
