Verse 1  
In the desert's quiet cry,  
We wandered far, our spirits dry,  
With hearts that trembled, we called to Thee,  
“Is the Lord among us? Where can we be free?”  
Moses stood in the midst of our doubt,  
With a rod in hand, he cried out.

Chorus  
Oh, draw from the rock, let the waters flow,  
In our thirst, Lord, Your mercy we know.  
From the depths of our trials, Your grace will arise,  
In the wilderness, You hear our cries.

Verse 2  
At Rephidim, the battle grew,  
Against our foes, the fight was true.  
“Choose us men, we will stand tall,”  
With lifted hands, we will not fall.  
Aaron and Hur held him high,  
As the sun sank low in the evening sky.
