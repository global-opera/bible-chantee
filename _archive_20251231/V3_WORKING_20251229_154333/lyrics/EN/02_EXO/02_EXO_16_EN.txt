[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the wilderness, a journey begun,  
From Elim’s grace, to the land that You’ve won.  
Oh, we wandered lost, in hunger and fear,  
Crying out for mercy, Lord, You drew near.  

(Chorus)  
And You rained down bread from the skies,  
Manna of heaven, a sweet surprise.  
In our need, Your glory shines bright,  
You are our Savior, our guiding light.  

(Verse 2)  
With murmurs of doubt, they questioned Your plan,  
Longing for Egypt, as they took a stand.  
Yet Your voice spoke through, in a cloud so clear,  
“Gather my children, for I am near.”  

(Chorus)  
And You rained down bread from the skies,  
Manna of heaven, a sweet surprise.  
In our need, Your glory shines bright,  
You are our Savior, our guiding light.  

(Bridge)  
Forty years, through the night and the day,  
You fed Your people, in every way.  
Let us not gather more than we need,  
For in You, Lord, our hearts are freed.  

(Verse 3)  
On the sixth day, a double portion of grace,  
A Sabbath rest, in Your holy embrace.  
No need to wander, Your love will provide,  
In every moment, Lord, be our guide.  

(Chorus)  
And You rained down bread from the skies,  
Manna of heaven, a sweet surprise.  
In our need, Your glory shines bright,  
You are our Savior, our guiding light.  

(Outro)  
So we lift our hands, in this sacred space,  
Thank You for mercy, for love and for grace.  
In the wilderness, You’re faithful and true,  
Oh Lord, our hunger is satisfied in You.
