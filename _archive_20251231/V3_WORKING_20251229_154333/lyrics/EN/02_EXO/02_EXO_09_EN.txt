[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Let My People Go

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of oppression, a voice calls out,  
The God of the Hebrews, with a mighty shout.  
"Let my people go, they are bound no more,  
To serve me in freedom, to worship and restore."  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the chains fall down, hear the cry of the Lord,  
His hand is upon us, our hearts are restored.  
In the land of the weary, His power we see,  
For the God of the heavens has set His people free.  

[Verse 2]  
Through the trials of Egypt, the plagues He would send,  
With thunder and hail, His might would not end.  
Yet in Goshen’s embrace, His grace did abide,  
Preserving His children, He stands by their side.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the chains fall down, hear the cry of the Lord,  
His hand is upon us, our hearts are restored.  
In the land of the weary, His power we see,  
For the God of the heavens has set His people free.  

[Bridge]  
Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, yet God’s will remained,  
To show all the nations His righteous reign.  
For in every storm, in every trial we face,  
The Lord is our refuge, our hope, and our grace.  

[Verse 3]  
So rise up, O nations, behold His great might,  
The One who delivers us from darkness to light.  
“Let my people go,” echoes through the land,  
For we are His chosen, united we stand.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let the chains fall down, hear the cry of the Lord,  
His hand is upon us, our hearts are restored.  
In the land of the weary, His power we see,  
For the God of the heavens has set His people free.  

[Outro]  
Let my people go, let the story be told,  
Of freedom and grace, in His love we behold.  
With voices united, we worship and sing,  
For the God of our fathers is our everlasting King.
