[TITLE]
Altar of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountain, You gave us Your design,  
An altar made of shittim wood, a sacred place divine.  
Five cubits long and five cubits wide,  
With horns upon each corner, our hearts to You we guide.  

Chorus  
We lift our hands in worship, in the beauty of Your grace,  
With every prayer, we offer, in this holy, sacred space.  
Let the fire of Your presence, burn within our hearts anew,  
In the stillness, we remember, all we owe, we give to You.

Verse 2  
The brazen vessels shining, for ashes and for fire,  
The pans and shovels waiting, fulfill our heart's desire.  
A network of Your promise, beneath the altar's frame,  
As we gather in Your presence, we will bless Your holy name.

Bridge  
Pure olive oil, we bring to You,  
To light the lamp that burns so true.  
From evening to morning, our hearts align,  
In the tabernacle, Your love will shine.

Outro  
So here we stand together, in covenant with Thee,  
With every breath, we honor, the way You set us free.  
In this altar of Your mercy, we find our hearts’ embrace,  
With grateful hearts, we worship, in this holy, sacred space.
