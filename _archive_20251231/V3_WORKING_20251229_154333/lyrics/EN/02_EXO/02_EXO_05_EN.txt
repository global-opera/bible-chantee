[TITLE]
Let My People Go

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the taskmaster,  
With burdens on our backs we bear,  
We cry out to the God of Israel,  
In the desert, hear our prayer.  
Moses came with words of freedom,  
Yet Pharaoh's heart was cold as stone,  
"Who is this Lord?" he mocked in anger,  
But we are not forsaken, we're not alone.

Chorus  
Let my people go, to worship and to sing,  
In the wilderness, our hearts will rise and cling.  
Through trials and through sorrow, we lift our eyes in faith,  
For the Lord our God has come to lead us to our place.

Verse 2  
With every brick, our spirits weary,  
No straw to lift the weight we bear,  
Yet in the night, we dream of freedom,  
A feast of joy beyond compare.  
Pharaoh's voice is loud and daunting,  
But God, You hear our cries of pain,  
In every struggle, He is with us,  
His promises forever shall remain.

Chorus

Bridge  
Oh, Lord, we trust in Your deliverance,  
Though darkness seems to reign today,  
We will stand upon Your word and follow,  
For You are the light that leads the way.  
When the night is long and hope is fading,  
We’ll remember every promise made,  
In the desert, we will find our voices,  
And with gladness, we shall not be afraid.

Outro  
Let my people go, let our praises soar,  
We will worship in the night, and we’ll trust forevermore.  
With hands lifted high, we'll sing of freedom's song,  
For the Lord our God is with us; in His love, we belong.
