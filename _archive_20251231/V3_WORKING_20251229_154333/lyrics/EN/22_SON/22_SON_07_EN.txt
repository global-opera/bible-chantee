[TITLE]
In the Garden of Your Love

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
How beautiful are Your ways, O Lord,  
Like jewels crafted by a loving hand.  
Your grace flows like a river,  
In fields of promise where we stand.  
Your love, a fragrant bloom in spring,  
With every breath, my heart will sing.  

Chorus  
Come, my beloved, let us seek the vine,  
In the garden of Your love, our souls align.  
With tender whispers, You call me near,  
In Your embrace, I have no fear.  
In the sweetness of Your presence, I find my rest,  
In the beauty of Your love, I am truly blessed.  

Verse 2  
Your eyes shine bright like pools of peace,  
In the still of night, Your love won’t cease.  
Your joy, like clusters of ripe, sweet fruit,  
In every season, my heart takes root.  
You lead me to the places where life begins,  
With every step, I feel Your grace within.

Bridge  
I am my beloved’s, His desire for me,  
In the quiet moments, His heart I see.  
Let us rise together, let the morning glow,  
In the fields of promise, Your love will flow.
