[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Oh priests, hear the voice of the Lord,  
His commandment rings true in our hearts,  
Give glory to His name, for He is near,  
Yet we stumble, and fear has departed.  
In the shadow of blessings we stand,  
But curses may fall, if we do not heed,  
Let us return, with hearts laid bare,  
To the covenant of life, to the truth we need.

Chorus  
Restore us, O God, to the ways of the past,  
Let the law of truth be in our lips at last.  
We repent of our ways, our hearts we renew,  
Seeking mercy and grace, Lord, turning to You.

Verse 2  
Have we not one Father, created by grace?  
Why do we wander, forgetting our place?  
Judah has strayed, profaning the holy,  
In the tears of the altar, we stand so lowly.  
Yet in the midst of our cries, He remains,  
Witness to our vows, our covenant stains,  
Oh let us remember, our youth’s holy bond,  
Turn back to our first love, of which we are fond.

Chorus  
Restore us, O God, to the ways of the past,  
Let the law of truth be in our lips at last.  
We repent of our ways, our hearts we renew,  
Seeking mercy and grace, Lord, turning to You.

Bridge  
Hearts weary and worn, we come to Your throne,  
In the violence of silence, we find we’re alone.  
Yet hope rises up, from ashes we call,  
Lord, teach us Your ways, we surrender it all.

Outro  
Take heed to your spirit, let love be our guide,  
In the covenant of peace, let us abide.  
O Lord of hosts, come dwell in our hearts,  
With truth as our anchor, we’ll never depart.
