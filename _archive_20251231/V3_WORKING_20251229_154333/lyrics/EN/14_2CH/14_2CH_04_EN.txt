[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of Solomon, a vision was revealed,  
An altar made of brass, where hearts would be healed.  
Twenty cubits wide, pure and strong,  
Lift our voices high, let the praises belong.  

Chorus:  
We come to the house of glory,  
Where the sea flows deep and wide,  
With every prayer and story,  
In His presence, we abide.  
Twelve oxen stand in awe,  
Bearing the weight of grace,  
In the temple of the Lord,  
We find our sacred place.
