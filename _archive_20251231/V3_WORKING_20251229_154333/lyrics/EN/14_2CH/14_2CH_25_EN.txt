[TITLE]
In the Valley of Salt

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of Judah, a king arose,  
Amaziah, his heart sought to know,  
Right in the sight of the Lord he stood,  
Yet faltered in faith, as he misunderstood.  

Chorus  
Oh, lead us, Lord, in the battle's embrace,  
Strengthen our hearts, in Your holy grace,  
When shadows loom, and the giants call,  
We stand in Your power, we will not fall.  

Verse 2  
He gathered his warriors, a mighty throng,  
Three hundred thousand, where they all belonged,  
But a voice from Heaven warned him so clear,  
“Trust not in numbers, for I am near.”  

Chorus  
Oh, lead us, Lord, in the battle's embrace,  
Strengthen our hearts, in Your holy grace,  
When shadows loom, and the giants call,  
We stand in Your power, we will not fall.  

Bridge  
Yet pride did rise, as victories came,  
He turned to false gods, igniting Your flame,  
In the valley of salt, where the battle was won,  
Oh, lead us back, to the light of Your Son.  

Verse 3  
Amaziah’s heart, it turned from the way,  
He sought after idols, led his soul astray,  
A prophet spoke, “Why forsake the true?”  
Yet the king would not listen, his fate was set due.  

Chorus  
Oh, lead us, Lord, in the battle's embrace,  
Strengthen our hearts, in Your holy grace,  
When shadows loom, and the giants call,  
We stand in Your power, we will not fall.  

Outro  
We’ll fight in Your name, with faith as our shield,  
In every battle, Lord, we know You’ll yield,  
In the valley of salt, we’ll trust in Your might,  
Guide us, O Savior, through the darkest night.
