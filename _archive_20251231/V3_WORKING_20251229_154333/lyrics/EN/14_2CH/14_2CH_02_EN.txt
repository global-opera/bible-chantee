Verse 1  
In the heart of a king, with a vision so grand,  
Solomon sought to build, by God's holy hand.  
A house for the Name, for the glory divine,  
To honor the Lord, and in His light to shine.  

Chorus  
Great is our God, above all the earth,  
Heaven can't hold Him, yet He calls us His worth.  
With wood from the mountains, and gold from the skies,  
We build for His glory, our praises shall rise.  

Verse 2  
He called for the skilled, in the land far and wide,  
To craft with precision, with wisdom as guide.  
"Bring me cedar and fir, let the beauty unfold,  
For the house we shall raise, is a story retold."
