[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heart of Zion, where the promise dwells,  
Solomon gathered, the story tells.  
Gold and silver, all laid at Your feet,  
In the house of the Lord, where heaven and earth meet.  

Chorus:  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
In the cloud of His glory, we find our cures.  
Lifted voices, with trumpets we sing,  
Praise to the King, our hearts offering.  

Verse 2:  
Elders and Levites, in white linen adorned,  
Carrying the Ark, by the Spirit they’re formed.  
Sacrifices countless, in reverence they bow,  
In the presence of the Lord, we worship Him now.  

Chorus:  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
In the cloud of His glory, we find our cures.  
Lifted voices, with trumpets we sing,  
Praise to the King, our hearts offering.  

Bridge:  
The cherubim spread their wings, a sacred embrace,  
In the most holy place, we seek Your face.  
Fill this house, O Lord, with Your holy light,  
As we gather in unity, our souls ignite.  

Chorus:  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
In the cloud of His glory, we find our cures.

Outro:  
As the cloud fills the temple, we stand in awe,  
In the glory of the Lord, we feel the draw.  
Forever we’ll gather, in worship we’ll rise,  
In the house of the Lord, our praises arise.
