[TITLE]
King of Hearts

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of old, a king arose,  
At eight he took the throne,  
With a heart so pure, he sought the Lord,  
In the ways of David, he was known.  
He broke the chains of idols down,  
In the land where shadows lay,  
With courage bold, he turned the tide,  
And led the hearts to pray.

Chorus  
Oh, Josiah, king of hearts,  
You walked in truth, you played your part,  
With every breath, you sought the light,  
Restoring hope, igniting night.  
In your covenant, we find our way,  
With all our hearts, we too will say,  
Lord, we will follow, we will obey,  
Forever, forever, in Your grace we stay.

Verse 2  
He found the book, the sacred word,  
And rent his clothes in grief,  
For all the ways his people strayed,  
He sought the Lord for relief.  
With tender heart, he humbled low,  
A cry for mercy rose,  
The prophet spoke, the promise came,  
In peace, his spirit chose.

Bridge  
Gather the people, the young and old,  
Together we’ll rise, united and bold,  
In the temple, we’ll stand, hand in hand,  
For the God of our fathers, we take our stand.  
With every heart, we declare Your name,  
In faith we’ll walk, forever the same,  
As we remember, the story unfolds,  
In the light of Your love, our hearts behold.

In Your covenant, our souls aligned.  
Amen, Amen, we lift Your name high,  
In the echoes of heaven, our spirits will fly.
