[TITLE]
The Passover of Joy

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of Josiah, a king so bold,  
He gathered the people, their hearts to behold.  
With lambs and with offerings, they came as one,  
To remember the promise, the victory won.  

Chorus  
Oh, lift up your voices, let the praises ring,  
For the blood of the Lamb, our salvation we sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather today,  
With hearts full of joy, in His love we stay.  

Verse 2  
The priests stood in order, the Levites prepared,  
As the fire blazed brightly, their worship declared.  
From the depths of their hearts, they offered their song,  
In the shadow of grace, where we all belong.  

Chorus  
Oh, lift up your voices, let the praises ring,  
For the blood of the Lamb, our salvation we sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather today,  
With hearts full of joy, in His love we stay.  

Bridge  
Remember the goodness, the mercy so wide,  
As we feast in His presence, our Savior, our guide.  
From the ashes of sorrow, He brings forth our praise,  
In the light of His glory, we stand unafraid.  

Verse 3  
Through valleys and shadows, He walks by our side,  
In the fight for the truth, He’s our constant guide.  
For the battle is won, and the victory’s near,  
With our hearts united, we’ll cast out all fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, lift up your voices, let the praises ring,  
For the blood of the Lamb, our salvation we sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather today,  
With hearts full of joy, in His love we stay.  

Outro  
So we sing of His faithfulness, forever proclaimed,  
In the story of Josiah, His glory remains.  
With each breath, we honor, with each step, we stand tall,  
For our King is the Savior, the Lord over all.
