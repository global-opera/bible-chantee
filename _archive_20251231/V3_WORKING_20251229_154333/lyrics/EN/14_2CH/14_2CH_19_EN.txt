[TITLE]
Hearts Prepared to Seek You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In peace, King Jehoshaphat returned,  
To Jerusalem, where his heart had learned.  
He heard the seer's voice, a call to stand,  
To seek the Lord, with faith, not man’s command.  

Pre-Chorus  
Though wrath may linger for the paths we’ve trod,  
He found good in the heart that seeks our God.  
With every step from Beersheba's shore,  
He called the lost to know the Lord once more.  

Chorus  
Oh, prepare our hearts, Lord, to seek Your face,  
In every trial, in every place.  
With courage, we will stand, united in Your grace,  
For the fear of the Lord leads us to Your embrace.  

Verse 2  
Judges set in cities, wise and true,  
To guard the laws and guide us back to You.  
With reverence, they take their sacred stand,  
For we do not judge for man, but at Your command.  

Pre-Chorus  
No iniquity, no gifts can sway,  
In the fear of the Lord, we walk each day.  
So let us warn our brothers, hear our cry,  
For in Your love, we find the reason why.  

Chorus  
Oh, prepare our hearts, Lord, to seek Your face,  
In every trial, in every place.  
With courage, we will stand, united in Your grace,  
For the fear of the Lord leads us to Your embrace.  

Bridge  
Deal courageously, the promise is clear,  
The Lord is with us, casting out all fear.  
In every battle, through every storm,  
We find our strength, in Your love we are reborn.  

Chorus  
Oh, prepare our hearts, Lord, to seek Your face,  
In every trial, in every place.  
With courage, we will stand, united in Your grace,  
For the fear of the Lord leads us to Your embrace.  

Outro  
So we return, back to You, our King,  
With hearts prepared, our praises we will sing.  
In faith and unity, we rise above,  
For in Your presence, we find perfect love.  
Amen.
