[TITLE]
A House Restored

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of Joash, a child so young,  
He reigned with a heart where the praises were sung.  
With Jehoiada guiding, he walked in the light,  
Restoring God’s temple, restoring what’s right.  

Chorus  
Oh, gather the hearts, let the people rejoice,  
Bring in the treasures, let us lift up our voice.  
For the house of the Lord, we will faithfully stand,  
As we build up the altar, with our hearts and our hands.  

Verse 2  
He called for the Levites, instructed them clear,  
“Bring forth your offerings, let the time draw near.”  
The chest at the gate, filled with faith and with love,  
Together we’ll honor the One from above.  

Chorus  
Oh, gather the hearts, let the people rejoice,  
Bring in the treasures, let us lift up our voice.  
For the house of the Lord, we will faithfully stand,  
As we build up the altar, with our hearts and our hands.  

Bridge  
But in days that followed, they turned from His ways,  
Forsaking the truth, living lost in a haze.  
Yet God sent His prophets, with a call to return,  
To seek Him in sorrow, for His love we still yearn.  

Verse 3  
Oh, remember Jehoiada, the good he had done,  
His legacy lives on, though the battle is won.  
For the Spirit still moves, through the ages of time,  
In the hearts of His people, His love will always shine.  

Chorus  
Oh, gather the hearts, let the people rejoice,  
Bring in the treasures, let us lift up our voice.  
For the house of the Lord, we will faithfully stand,  
As we build up the altar, with our hearts and our hands.  

Outro  
So we rise in His presence, with courage and grace,  
Restoring His temple, seeking His face.  
In the name of the Father, the Spirit, the Son,  
With our hearts forever, let His will be done.
