[TITLE]
Eyes to See the Truth

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows loom,  
Your prophets cry, “There’s no more room.”  
To the river, let us go,  
To build a place where faith can grow.  
With axe in hand, they toil and sweat,  
But in the depths, the iron is lost yet.  
“Alas, my master, it’s borrowed,  
In the waters deep, my heart is sorrowed.”  

Chorus  
Open our eyes, Lord, let us see,  
The chariots of fire surrounding me.  
In battles fierce, when darkness falls,  
You’re with us, Lord, we hear Your call.  
Fear not, for those who stand with us,  
Are more than all the foes that rush.  
With every prayer, our hearts align,  
Your promises, our hope divine.  

Verse 2  
In Dothan’s night, they came in might,  
The servant trembled at the sight.  
“Alas, my master, what shall we do?”  
But Elisha answered, “Fear not, it’s true.”  
He prayed to God, “Lord, open his eyes,”  
And the mountains glowed with heavenly skies.  
With fire and light, Your army stands,  
In every trial, we trust Your plans.

Bridge  
In the midst of famine, when hope seems lost,  
We cry for mercy, no matter the cost.  
You bring provision, You grant us grace,  
In every trial, we seek Your face.  
The king may tremble, but we will stand,  
With faith unshaken, we trust Your hand.

Outro  
So let us rise, with courage bold,  
With hearts afire, Your truth we hold.  
For in Your presence, we shall find,  
The peace that passes, all humankind.  
Open our eyes, Lord, let us see,  
The victory won, eternally.
