[TITLE]
In the Ashes of Jerusalem

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the ninth year, the siege began,  
With mighty walls, they stood, they ran.  
No bread to feed the hungry hearts,  
The city weeps as hope departs.  

Chorus  
But You, O Lord, are our refuge,  
In the darkness, we call Your name.  
Though the fire consumes the temple,  
Your love remains, forever the same.  

Verse 2  
Zedekiah fled into the night,  
Chased by fear, lost from the light.  
In the plains, his fate was sealed,  
King of a land, no strength revealed.  

Chorus  
But You, O Lord, are our refuge,  
In the darkness, we call Your name.  
Though the fire consumes the temple,  
Your love remains, forever the same.  

Bridge  
In the ashes, You restore,  
Lift our heads, we’re not alone.  
From the ruins, You’ll raise a song,  
In our hearts, Your love is home.  

Verse 3  
The captives taken far away,  
Yet in their hearts, they long to pray.  
Gedaliah spoke with courage bright,  
"Fear not, for He will be our light."  

Chorus  
But You, O Lord, are our refuge,  
In the darkness, we call Your name.  
Though the fire consumes the temple,  
Your love remains, forever the same.  

Outro  
So through the trials, we will stand,  
With faith, we rise, in Your command.  
For even in the darkest night,  
You are our hope, our guiding light.
