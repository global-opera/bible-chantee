[TITLE]
A Day of Good Tidings

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of despair, we waited,  
Four lepers by the gate, hearts deflated.  
In the silence of our plight, we cried,  
"Why sit we here, when hope has died?"  

[Pre-Chorus]  
But a whisper from the Lord broke through the night,  
“Rise and go, for I will lead you to the light.”  

[Chorus]  
A day of good tidings, the promise is near,  
Where famine turns to plenty, and hope casts out fear.  
A measure of flour for a shekel, we will see,  
For the Lord has spoken, His word brings victory!  

[Verse 2]  
We ventured forth, in twilight’s embrace,  
To the camp of the enemy, seeking grace.  
No voice of man, no soldier in sight,  
The Lord had caused them to flee in fright.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
In the tents lay abundance, treasures untold,  
A feast of mercy, more precious than gold.  

[Chorus]  
A day of good tidings, the promise is near,  
Where famine turns to plenty, and hope casts out fear.  
A measure of flour for a shekel, we will see,  
For the Lord has spoken, His word brings victory!  

[Bridge]  
Oh, let us not be silent, share the news today,  
For the Lord has delivered, He’s made a way!  
We’ll proclaim His goodness, His grace will abound,  
In every heart, His love will be found.  

[Chorus]  
A day of good tidings, the promise is near,  
Where famine turns to plenty, and hope casts out fear.  
A measure of flour for a shekel, we will see,  
For the Lord has spoken, His word brings victory!  

[Outro]  
So rise, O weary hearts, take hold of His grace,  
In the midst of our struggles, we’ll seek His face.  
A day of good tidings, His promise rings true,  
In every season, His love will renew.  
A measure of flour, His faithfulness shines,  
In the gates of our lives, His victory aligns.
