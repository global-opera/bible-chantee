[TITLE]
Turn Your Hearts Again

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of old, when kings did reign,  
Israel wandered, lost in their pain.  
They turned from the way, from the God of their might,  
Chasing after idols, lost in the night.  

Chorus  
Turn your hearts again, O children of grace,  
Seek the Lord your God, find shelter in His embrace.  
From the ashes rise, let His truth be your song,  
For He is our refuge, where our hearts belong.  

Verse 2  
They built up high places, in secret they strayed,  
Provoking the Lord, in darkness they laid.  
Yet He sent His prophets, calling them near,  
“Turn from your evil, let My love draw you here.”  

Chorus  
Turn your hearts again, O children of grace,  
Seek the Lord your God, find shelter in His embrace.  
From the ashes rise, let His truth be your song,  
For He is our refuge, where our hearts belong.  

Bridge  
O Lord, You brought us up from bondage and strife,  
With a mighty hand, You’ve given us life.  
We will not forget, the covenant made,  
In the shadow of Your wings, our fears will fade.  

Verse 3  
But the nations came, with their gods of the land,  
Yet even in darkness, You still have a plan.  
Teach us, O Father, to fear and to love,  
For our hearts are set on the One above.  

Chorus  
Turn your hearts again, O children of grace,  
Seek the Lord your God, find shelter in His embrace.  
From the ashes rise, let His truth be your song,  
For He is our refuge, where our hearts belong.  

Outro  
So we lift our voices, in praise and in prayer,  
For the Lord our God, forever we’ll share.  
With every generation, we’ll stand and proclaim,  
“Turn your hearts again, in Jesus' name.”
