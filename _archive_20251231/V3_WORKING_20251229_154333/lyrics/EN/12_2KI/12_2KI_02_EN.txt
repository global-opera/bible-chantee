[TITLE]
The Mantle of Faith

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From Gilgal’s path we set our feet,  
Elijah calls, but I won’t retreat.  
As the Lord lives, I’ll stand with you,  
Through Bethel’s gates, our faith is true.  

Chorus  
Oh, the mantle falls, a spirit of fire,  
Double portion, Lord, my heart’s desire.  
With every step, I’ll follow You,  
In the whirlwind, my faith is renewed.  

Verse 2  
Through Jericho’s walls, whispers surround,  
Yet I hold fast, Your voice is profound.  
At Jordan’s edge, the waters divide,  
Together we stand, in You we abide.  

Chorus  
Oh, the mantle falls, a spirit of fire,  
Double portion, Lord, my heart’s desire.  
With every step, I’ll follow You,  
In the whirlwind, my faith is renewed.  

Bridge  
A chariot of fire, the heavens declare,  
As Elijah ascends, I’m left with a prayer.  
“Where is the Lord God of Elijah so bold?”  
With the mantle in hand, Your power unfolds.  

Verse 3  
The waters are healed, the barren restored,  
Your promises stand, forever adored.  
Though trials may come, I will not despair,  
With faith like Elisha, I’ll echo Your care.  

Chorus  
Oh, the mantle falls, a spirit of fire,  
Double portion, Lord, my heart’s desire.  
With every step, I’ll follow You,  
In the whirlwind, my faith is renewed.  

Outro  
Oh, the mantle falls, we rise and we soar,  
In every moment, Lord, we’ll trust You more.  
With hearts united, we’ll carry Your light,  
In the mantle of faith, we’ll shine through the night.
