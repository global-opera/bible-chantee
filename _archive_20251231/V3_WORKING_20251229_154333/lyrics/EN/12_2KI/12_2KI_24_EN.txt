[LYRICS]
 "In the Shadow of the Siege"

Verse 1  
In the days of kings who turned away,  
The land of Judah weeps and prays,  
For the innocent blood that stained the ground,  
In the silence of the night, a desperate sound.  

Chorus  
Lord, hear our cry, in our darkest hour,  
We seek Your face, Your steadfast power.  
Though the nations rise and the battles rage,  
We trust in Your Word, through every age.  

Verse 2  
Jehoiakim fell, his heart led astray,  
The prophets warned, but he chose his way.  
Now the Chaldeans march with a heavy hand,  
As Jerusalem trembles, like grains of sand.  

Chorus  
Lord, hear our cry, in our darkest hour,  
We seek Your face, Your steadfast power.  
Though the nations rise and the battles rage,  
We trust in Your Word, through every age.  

Bridge  
For the sins of the past, we plead for grace,  
In the shadow of the siege, we seek Your face.  
Though captives we may be, Your light will guide,  
With hope in our hearts, we will abide.  

Verse 3  
Zedekiah reigns, but his heart is blind,  
As the echoes of the past, in shadows we find.  
Yet in the ruins, You whisper our name,  
Through trials and fire, we’ll never be the same.  

Chorus  
Lord, hear our cry, in our darkest hour,  
We seek Your face, Your steadfast power.  
Though the nations rise and the battles rage,  
We trust in Your Word, through every age.  

Outro  
So we lift our hands, in surrender we stand,  
With faith in our hearts, we trust Your plan.  
In the shadow of the siege, Your love remains,  
Forever our refuge, through joy and pain.
