[TITLE]
Echoes of a Fallen King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the heart of Jerusalem, a young king arose,  
Twelve years he was, with a crown like a rose.  
But in shadows he wandered, turned from the Light,  
Built altars for idols, forsook what was right.  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, the echoes of hearts that turned far away,  
From the path of their fathers, lost in dismay.  
He raised up the high places, the groves and the shame,  
Provoked the Lord's anger, forgot His great name.  

Chorus  
We cry out, O Lord, hear our plea,  
Restore us, renew us, set our hearts free.  
From the sins of the past, we seek Your embrace,  
In the midst of our fall, show us Your grace.  

Verse 2  
In the darkness he led, with blood on his hands,  
Innocent cries filled the desolate lands.  
The prophets were speaking, but none would believe,  
A warning of judgment, a chance to retrieve.  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, the echoes of hearts that turned far away,  
From the path of their fathers, lost in dismay.  
He filled up the city with sorrow and pain,  
Yet hope still remained, though judgment would reign.  

Chorus  
We cry out, O Lord, hear our plea,  
Restore us, renew us, set our hearts free.  
From the sins of the past, we seek Your embrace,  
In the midst of our fall, show us Your grace.  

Bridge  
And when the night falls, and darkness surrounds,  
A remnant will rise, hear the hopeful sounds.  
For Josiah will come, with a heart pure and true,  
To lead us back home, to the One who makes new.  

Chorus  
We cry out, O Lord, hear our plea,  
Restore us, renew us, set our hearts free.  
From the sins of the past, we seek Your embrace,  
In the midst of our fall, show us Your grace.  

Outro  
Oh, echo our hearts, let us never forget,  
The lessons of old, in Your love we are met.  
In the story of Manasseh, we find our own way,  
To turn back to You, Lord, each and every day.
