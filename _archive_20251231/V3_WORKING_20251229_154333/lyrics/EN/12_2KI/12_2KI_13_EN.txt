Verse 1  
In the reign of Jehoahaz, a cry rose from the land,  
Oppression from the enemy, like dust beneath their hand.  
Yet he sought the face of God, in anguish and despair,  
The Lord, in love and mercy, heard his heartfelt prayer.

Chorus  
Arrows of deliverance, shoot them through the skies,  
Marked with grace and power, let the faithful rise.  
Chains of sin are breaking, freedom calls our name,  
With the strength of heaven, we will never be the same.

Verse 2  
Elisha, man of God, lay sick upon his bed,

“Take your bow and arrows, let your faith be bold,”  
For the Lord’s deliverance, is a story to be told.

Bridge  
Though the shadows gather, and the foes may seem so strong,  
We remember His compassion, where our hearts belong.

Verse 3  
Three times Joash struck the ground, a lesson learned in pain,  
For the victory is greater, when we trust in His name.  
So let us not forget, the mercy that He gives,  
For in every trial faced, it’s in Him that we live.
