[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Gathered in the house of the Lord,  
The elders came to hear His Word,  
Josiah stood, a promise made,  
To walk in truth, our hearts displayed.  
With all our soul, we pledge anew,  
To keep His ways, our lives pursue.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, we stand, our hearts unite,  
In covenant joy, we seek Your light.  
With every breath, we lift Your name,  
Revive our hearts, ignite the flame.  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
We’ll follow You, our rightful place.

From Geba's gates to Beersheba's shore,  
We cast aside what we abhor.
