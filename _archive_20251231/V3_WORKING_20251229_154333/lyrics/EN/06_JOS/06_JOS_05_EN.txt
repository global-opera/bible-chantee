[TITLE]
The Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the Jordan, the waters stood still,  
You parted every barrier, with Your mighty will.  
Kings of Canaan trembled, their hearts filled with fear,  
For Your people were rising, no longer held near.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the shame, let the past be gone,  
In the land of promise, we will carry on.  
With Your grace upon us, we will stand and fight,  
For the Captain of the host leads us into light.  

Verse 2:  
At Gilgal, You called us, to renew our hearts,  
A covenant of promise, a brand new start.  
Forty years in the wilderness, we wandered so long,  
But now we feast on Your goodness, and we sing Your song.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the shame, let the past be gone,  
In the land of promise, we will carry on.  
With Your grace upon us, we will stand and fight,  
For the Captain of the host leads us into light.  

Bridge:  
Holy ground beneath our feet,  
We bow before the King,  
With every breath we take in faith,  
Your victory we bring.  
Loose our shoes, we are Yours,  
In this sacred place,  
With every step, we follow close,  
To find our resting place.  

Chorus:  
Oh, roll away the shame, let the past be gone,  
In the land of promise, we will carry on.  
With Your grace upon us, we will stand and fight,  
For the Captain of the host leads us into light.  

Outro:  
So we march with courage, our hearts in Your hands,  
In the land of promise, we will take our stand.  
With faith as our banner, we’ll rise and proclaim,  
That the Lord of all glory is forever the same.
