Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the mountains,  
Where the weary find their way,  
You spoke to us, O Father,  
A refuge from the fray.  
Kedesh calls, and Shechem sings,  
In the heart of every soul,  
A place of grace, where mercy brings  
Restoration, makes us whole.

In the arms of Your embrace,  
We find our way to light.
