### Title: "Rise Up, O Israel"

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the past,  
Moses led with courage vast,  
But now a new day dawns,  
God's promise still lives on.  
Joshua, arise and go,  
Cross the Jordan, take the flow,  
Every step you take is blessed,  
In this land, you shall find rest.

Chorus  
Be strong and courageous, fear not the night,  
For the Lord your God is your guiding light.  
Wherever you wander, He'll be your stay,  
In every battle, He'll show you the way.  
Rise up, O Israel, claim what’s been foretold,  
With faith as your armor, be brave and bold.

Verse 2  
From the wilderness to the sea,  
All I promised shall be free,  
Every place your foot shall tread,  
This inheritance shall be spread.  
Meditate on His Word each day,  
In His truth, let your heart stay,  
For success will surely follow,  
In His strength, there’s no sorrow.

Bridge  
When the storms arise and shadows fall,  
Lift your eyes, He hears your call.  
He commands you now, do not be dismayed,  
For every promise, He has made.  
Together we stand, united as one,  
With hearts set on glory, our journey begun.

So rise up, O Israel, rise up today,  
With courage and strength, we’ll follow His way.  
His promise is true, our hearts will rejoice,  
In every moment, we’ll lift up our voice.
