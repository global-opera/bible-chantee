[TITLE]
Cleave to the Lord

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In days of old, when rest was found,  
The Lord had fought, His grace abound,  
Joshua stood, with wisdom shared,  
He called the tribes, for he had cared.  
“Remember all the battles won,  
For God is with you, His will be done,  
The lands before you, they shall be yours,  
His promise stands, forever endures.”

[Chorus]  
Cleave to the Lord, be strong and brave,  
He fights for you, your soul to save,  
No man can stand, where He commands,  
In faith we rise, and take our stand.  
Cleave to the Lord, His love prevails,  
Through every storm, His truth never fails,  
With courage bold, our hearts ignite,  
He is our strength, our guiding light.

[Verse 2]  
The nations linger, don’t turn aside,  
Hold fast to Him, let Him be your guide,  
For if you stray, the snares will bind,  
And thorns will pierce, the heart and mind.  
Yet He has promised, none shall oppose,  
A thousand shall fall, where His power flows,  
Take heed, take heart, love Him always,  
His faithfulness shines, through all your days.

[Bridge]  
As Joshua spoke, so we will stand,  
With hearts united, we join His band,  
For every promise, He’s ever made,  
Not one has fallen, His truth displayed.  
So let us turn, our eyes above,  
In every battle, we trust His love,  
For every victory, we lift our praise,  
Forever faithful, through all our days.
