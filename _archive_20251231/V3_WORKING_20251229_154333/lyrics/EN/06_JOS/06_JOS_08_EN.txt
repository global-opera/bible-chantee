[TITLE]
Victory in Your Hand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the dawn of hope, the Lord commands,  
"Fear not, my child, for I hold your plans.  
To Ai, go forth, rise up once more,  
The victory is yours; the battle's roar."  

Chorus:  
With our hands lifted high, we trust in Your might,  
You lead us through shadows, into the light.  
In every battle, our faith will stand,  
For You are our fortress, with victory in Your hand.  

Verse 2:  
Joshua stood, with valiant hearts,  
Thirty thousand strong, each played their parts.  
In the night they lay, in ambush near,  
For the Lord had spoken, “Be bold, have no fear.”  

Chorus:  
With our hands lifted high, we trust in Your might,  
You lead us through shadows, into the light.  
In every battle, our faith will stand,  
For You are our fortress, with victory in Your hand.  

Bridge:  
When the enemy comes, we’ll know just what to do,  
We’ll turn from the fight, and our trust will be true.  
As the smoke rises high, we’ll see Your plan unfold,  
For You are our refuge, our hearts will be bold.  

Verse 3:  
The city will fall, just as You said,  
With the sound of the trumpet, our fears will be shed.  
We’ll gather the spoils, and lift up Your name,  
For every victory won is forever the same.  

Chorus:  
With our hands lifted high, we trust in Your might,  
You lead us through shadows, into the light.  
In every battle, our faith will stand,  
For You are our fortress, with victory in Your hand.  

Outro:  
So we build an altar, in praise we unite,  
Remembering Your wonders, in day and night.  
For the Lord is our strength, our guide on this land,  
With faith unshaken, we stand in Your hand.
