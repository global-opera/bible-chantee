[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
Joshua stood, heart set right,  
The city of Jericho, closed tight,  
But the promise of the Lord shines bright.  
“See, I’ve given you the land,” He said,  
With faith and courage, we shall tread,  
The trumpets blow, let the people rise,  
For victory comes, as faith complies.

Chorus  
Walls fall down, when we shout His name,  
With every cry, His glory we proclaim.  
The mighty hand of God will lead us through,  
We lift our voices high, our faith made new.  
Walls fall down, oh, let the trumpets sound,  
With hearts united, we are glory-bound.

Verse 2  
Seven days, we marched in prayer,  
The priests with horns, the Ark laid bare,  
Round the city, we walked in trust,  
Obedience to God is a must.  
On the seventh day, we rose with the dawn,  
Seven times around, we pressed on,  
Then the sound of trumpets filled the air,  
A shout of praise, our victory declared.

Bridge  
So we trust in the promise, our hearts aligned,  
Through battles and trials, His love we find.  
With every step we take, we stand in grace,  
For the Lord goes before us, we seek His face.  
Rahab’s faith, a beacon of hope,  
In God’s great mercy, we learn to cope.  
From ashes to glory, His plan unfolds,  
In the heart of Jericho, His story told.

Outro
