Verse 1:  
In the silence of our hearts, we long for Your embrace,  
O Lord, we seek Your righteousness, we yearn for Your grace.  
With zeal we come, though lost in our own way,  
Teach us to believe, Lord, to trust and obey.  

Chorus:  
Whosoever calls upon Your name,  
Shall find salvation, never be the same.  
With our hearts, we believe, with our mouths, we confess,  
Jesus, You are Lord, our hope and righteousness.  

Verse 2:  
The word is in our hearts, it echoes in our souls,  
You are the end of striving, You make the broken whole.  
No difference in our standing, Your love knows no bounds,  
You reach the lost and weary, in grace we are found.
