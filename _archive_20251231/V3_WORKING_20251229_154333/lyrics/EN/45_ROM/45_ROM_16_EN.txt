[TITLE]
Grace and Fellowship

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the heart of the church, a sister stands true,  
Phoebe, a servant, in all that she’ll do,  
With love we receive her, as saints we unite,  
Supporting each other, in His glorious light.  

Chorus  
Oh, grace of our Lord, let it flow through our hearts,  
Binding us together, never to part,  
In love we labor, in faith we stand tall,  
Together in Christ, we are one and we’re all.  

Verse 2  
Priscilla and Aquila, they’ve laid down their lives,  
Helpers in Christ, where true love survives,  
Greet those who’ve toiled, and lifted His name,  
Through trials and triumphs, we’ll never be the same.  

Chorus  
Oh, grace of our Lord, let it flow through our hearts,  
Binding us together, never to part,  
In love we labor, in faith we stand tall,  
Together in Christ, we are one and we’re all.  

Bridge  
Mark those who cause division, with hearts that deceive,  
For we stand in obedience, in Him we believe,  
The God of all peace, will bruise every foe,  
His grace is our strength, in His love we will grow.  

Verse 3  
With a holy embrace, we greet one another,  
In the love of our Savior, we’re sisters and brothers,  
Each name that is mentioned, a story of grace,  
In the family of God, we all find our place.  

Chorus  
Oh, grace of our Lord, let it flow through our hearts,  
Binding us together, never to part,  
In love we labor, in faith we stand tall,  
Together in Christ, we are one and we’re all.  

Outro  
To God only wise, glory forever we bring,  
Through Jesus our Savior, our praises we sing,  
In the bonds of His love, we’ll stand hand in hand,  
Together in faith, united we’ll stand.
