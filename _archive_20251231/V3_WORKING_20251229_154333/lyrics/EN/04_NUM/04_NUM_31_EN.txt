[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, the Lord spoke to His friend,  
"Rise up, O mighty people, for your vengeance I command.  
A thousand from each tribe shall go forth to the fray,  
With Phinehas leading, in My name, they’ll find the way."

Chorus  
O Lord, we heed Your calling, our hearts are brave and true,  
With holy trumpets sounding, our strength comes forth from You.  
In the battle for Your glory, we pursue what’s just and right,  
We will stand, we will fight, guided by Your light.

Verse 2  
Through the valleys dark and deep, they marched with courage bold,  
As the kings of Midian fell, their stories now retold.  
But Moses, filled with righteous fire, sought to protect the pure,  
"Let not the wicked live, for their hearts shall not endure."

Bridge  
And from the spoils of battle, we bring our gifts to Thee,  
Gold and silver shining bright, as an offering humbly.  
For every soul that’s spared, we give our thanks in praise,  
In the shadow of Your mercy, we lift our hearts always.

Verse 3  
Moses shared the portion, dividing all in grace,  
To the Levites of the tabernacle, they found their rightful place.  
For the Lord commanded, and they followed every word,  
In the unity of spirit, His voice will be heard.

Outro  
So let us rise together, with our hearts and hands entwined,  
In the covenant of love, His purpose will we find.  
For the battle's not our own, it’s the Lord’s we now proclaim,  
In His name, forevermore, we will glorify His name.
