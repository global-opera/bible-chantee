[TITLE]
Vow of the Nazarite

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, You called my name,  
Set apart for You, Lord, I’ll never be the same.  
With my heart, I vow, a life of sacrifice,  
From the vine, I’ll turn away, seeking only Christ.  

Chorus  
Lord, I separate, to stand in Your grace,  
With every step I take, I seek Your holy face.  
Let the locks of my heart grow, in Your light I abide,  
In this vow of the Nazarite, I am Yours, my guide.  

Verse 2  
In the shadow of the dead, I will not draw near,  
My devotion is to You, my path is crystal clear.  
For my mother and my father, I will honor well,  
But my heart belongs to You, in Your love I dwell.  

Chorus  
Lord, I separate, to stand in Your grace,  
With every step I take, I seek Your holy face.  
Let the locks of my heart grow, in Your light I abide,  
In this vow of the Nazarite, I am Yours, my guide.  

Bridge  
When the days are fulfilled, I’ll come to You with praise,  
Offering my heart, Lord, in these sacred ways.  
With a lamb without a blemish, I’ll lay my burdens down,  
In the fire of Your presence, let my life be found.  

Chorus  
Lord, I separate, to stand in Your grace,  
With every step I take, I seek Your holy face.  
Let the locks of my heart grow, in Your light I abide,  
In this vow of the Nazarite, I am Yours, my guide.  

Outro  
The Lord bless me and keep me, let Your peace surround,  
Make Your face shine upon me, in Your love I’m found.  
As I walk this path, Lord, Your name I will declare,  
In the vow of the Nazarite, I’m forever in Your care.
