[TITLE]
Water from the Rock

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the desert, weary hearts,  
Gathered ‘round in troubled parts,  
Moses led with faithful grace,  
Yet doubt found a resting place.  
O Lord, where is our drink?  
In this wilderness, we sink.  
Would that we had stayed behind,  
In the chains we left, confined.  

[Chorus]  
But You, O God, will not forsake,  
From the rock, Your love will break.  
Water flows, a river wide,  
In our need, You will provide.  
We cry out, though we stray,  
In Your mercy, lead the way.  

[Verse 2]  
Moses fell before Your throne,  
In Your glory, he was shown.  
“Speak to the rock,” You called his name,  
Abundant life, we’ll never be the same.  
Yet in anger, he struck the stone,  
And the waters flowed, yet he stood alone.  
A lesson learned, a heart laid bare,  
In our trials, Lord, teach us to care.  

[Chorus]  
But You, O God, will not forsake,  
From the rock, Your love will break.  
Water flows, a river wide,  
In our need, You will provide.  
We cry out, though we stray,  
In Your mercy, lead the way.  

[Bridge]  
Through the valleys, through the pain,  
You are with us, we’ll not wane.  
Mourning hearts for Aaron’s loss,  
Yet in our grief, we bear the cross.  
Guide us, Lord, to higher ground,  
In Your promise, hope is found.  

[Chorus]  
But You, O God, will not forsake,  
From the rock, Your love will break.  
Water flows, a river wide,

[Outro]  
So we journey, hand in hand,  
Trusting You, O promised land.  
In the desert, we will sing,  
Of the life that You will bring.  
In Your presence, we will stand,  
Forever safe, forever planned.
