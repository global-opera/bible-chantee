Verse 1:  
In the stillness of the night,  
The Lord spoke to His own,  
“Bring forth a heifer pure,  
In the wilderness alone.  
Without spot, without blame,  
Set apart for sacred flame,  
In the sight of Eleazar,  
Bring her forth to bear the name.”

Chorus:  
Cleansing waters flow,  
From the ashes of the past,  
Purify our hearts, O Lord,  
In Your love, we are steadfast.  
In the third day, rise anew,  
Seventh day, we stand in You,  
Cleansing waters, grace bestowed,  
In Your presence, we’re made whole.

Water of separation flows,  
To redeem the heart that knows.
