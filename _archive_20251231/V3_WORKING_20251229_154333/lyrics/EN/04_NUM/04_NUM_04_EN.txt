[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Service of the Holy

[Verse 1]  
In the stillness of the night,  
The Lord called to Moses with light,  
“Gather the sons of Levi, come near,  
For a holy service, the purpose is clear.  
From the age of thirty, let them arise,  
To bear the burden, to serve and to wise.”

[Chorus]  
O, we lift up our hearts,  
In reverence and grace,  
For the work of Your hands,  
In this sacred space.  
Cover us, Lord, with Your love divine,  
As we offer our lives, in this holy design.

[Verse 2]  
Kohath, Gershon, and Merari too,  
Each with their calling, each with their view.  
The Ark of the Covenant, shrouded in blue,  
The bread of the presence, a promise so true.  
With badger skins covering, a veil of His might,  
In the heart of the tabernacle, shines forth His light.

[Chorus]  
O, we lift up our hearts,  
In reverence and grace,  
For the work of Your hands,  
In this sacred space.  
Cover us, Lord, with Your love divine,  
As we offer our lives, in this holy design.

[Bridge]  
When the camp stirs and the banners unfold,  
The sons will bear witness, the stories retold.  
With every step forward, we’ll carry Your name,  
In the service of glory, we’ll never be the same.  
O, let us not falter, let us not stray,  
For we walk in Your promise, in Your holy way.

[Chorus]  
O, we lift up our hearts,

[Outro]  
So we gather together, as one in His fold,  
The sons of the Levites, strong and bold.  
In the beauty of worship, our spirits align,  
For the service of the holy, forever we'll shine.  
Amen, amen, let our voices proclaim,  
In the service of the holy, we glorify Your name.
