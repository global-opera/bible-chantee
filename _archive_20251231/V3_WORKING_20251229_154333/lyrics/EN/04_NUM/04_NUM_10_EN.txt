[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the wilderness,  
Your voice calls forth the sound,  
Two silver trumpets echoing,  
Gathering us all around.  
With the journey of Your people,  
We step into the light,  
Guided by Your presence,  
Through the day and through the night.

Chorus  
Rise up, O Lord, let Your glory shine,  
Lead us on this journey, be our guide divine.  
In the battles we will face, we will call Your name,  
In joy and in sorrow, forever the same.

Verse 2  
As we march with hearts united,  
Following the cloud and fire,  
Each tribe and every standard,  
In Your love, we shall aspire.  
With the Ark of Your covenant,  
Going forth before our eyes,  
You drive away the darkness,  
With Your strength, our spirits rise.

Bridge  
Come, Hobab, join our mission,  
For the Lord has spoken good,  
Together we will journey,  
In the promise of His wood.  
What goodness He has shown us,  
We will share with those we meet,  
In the wilderness of longing,  
Let our hearts and hopes repeat.

Outro  
When the trumpets sound, we gather,  
In the presence of our King,  
Rise up, O Lord, among us,  
Let the heavens hear us sing.  
Return, O Lord, to Israel,  
With Your love, our hearts ignite,  
In the journey of Your people,  
You are our hope, our light.
