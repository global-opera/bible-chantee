[TITLE]
In the Shadow of the Cloud

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the heart of the desert, where whispers collide,  
Moses led with a meekness, in the Lord he confides.  
But Miriam and Aaron, in shadows they spoke,  
Questioning the calling, with hearts that were broke.  

Chorus  
Oh, hear the voice of the Lord,  
In the stillness, His truth restored.  
He speaks to the humble, to those who obey,  
In the shadow of the cloud, we find our way.  

Verse 2  
“Come forth,” said the Lord, in the pillar of grace,  
To His chosen servant, He reveals His face.  
With visions and dreams, He speaks to the wise,  
But Moses, my faithful, beholds with his eyes.  

Chorus  
Oh, hear the voice of the Lord,  
In the stillness, His truth restored.  
He speaks to the humble, to those who obey,  
In the shadow of the cloud, we find our way.  

Bridge  
Forgive us, O Father, for words that divide,  
Restore us in mercy, let love be our guide.  
Moses cried for healing, with faith that stood tall,  
In the depths of our weakness, Your grace covers all.  

Verse 3  
Seven days she was silent, in the camp far away,  
But the people did not move, till they welcomed her sway.  
The Lord’s hand of mercy brings healing anew,  
In the shadow of the cloud, His promise is true.  

Chorus  
Oh, hear the voice of the Lord,  
In the stillness, His truth restored.  
He speaks to the humble, to those who obey,  
In the shadow of the cloud, we find our way.  

Outro  
So let us walk forward, with hearts intertwined,  
In the journey together, His love we will find.  
With the cloud as our covering, we’ll follow His light,  
For in the shadow of the cloud, His grace shines bright.
