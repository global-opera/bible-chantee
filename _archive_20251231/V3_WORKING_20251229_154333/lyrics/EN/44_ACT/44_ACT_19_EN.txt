[TITLE]
In the Name of Jesus

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In Ephesus, the Spirit came,  
With hands laid down, we call His name.  
From John's baptism to Christ we turn,  
The fire of faith, within us burns.  
Twelve hearts ablaze, we gather near,  
With tongues of praise, our voices clear.

Chorus:  
In the name of Jesus, we stand and declare,  
Miracles and wonders, His glory we share.  
Mighty is the Word, it prevails through the night,  
In the name of Jesus, we walk in His light.

Verse 2:  
Paul spoke boldly, the truth unveiled,  
Against the darkness, our hope prevailed.  
The shadows trembled, the spirits fled,  
In the name of Jesus, new life is spread.  
Fear fell upon them, the name was known,  
As hearts repented, His love was shown.

Chorus:  
In the name of Jesus, we stand and declare,  
Miracles and wonders, His glory we share.  
Mighty is the Word, it prevails through the night,  
In the name of Jesus, we walk in His light.

Bridge:  
Great is our God, the One we adore,  
He shakes the heavens, opens every door.  
With unity and love, we rise and proclaim,  
The kingdom of God, forever His name.

Chorus:  
In the name of Jesus, we stand and declare,  
Miracles and wonders, His glory we share.  
Mighty is the Word, it prevails through the night,  
In the name of Jesus, we walk in His light.

Outro:  
So let us gather, as one we will sing,  
In the name of Jesus, our praises we bring.  
With hearts united, we’ll shine ever bright,  
In the name of Jesus, we walk in His light.
