[TITLE]
Sent by Your Spirit

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In Antioch, they gathered, prophets and teachers,  
With hearts aligned, they sought the Lord's treasure.  
Fasting and praying, they heard the call,  
"Separate Me Barnabas and Saul."  

[Chorus]  
Sent by Your Spirit, we rise and go,  
With the truth of Your Word, let Your love overflow.  
To the ends of the earth, we carry Your light,  
In the name of Jesus, we shine through the night.  

[Verse 2]  
Through Cyprus they traveled, proclaiming Your grace,  
In every synagogue, they found a holy space.  
With courage they stood, against darkness they fought,  
For the sake of salvation, Your truth they sought.  

[Chorus]  
Sent by Your Spirit, we rise and go,  
With the truth of Your Word, let Your love overflow.  
To the ends of the earth, we carry Your light,  
In the name of Jesus, we shine through the night.  

[Bridge]  
Oh, we’re calling the lost, we’re bringing them home,  
In the joy of the Gospel, we’ll never be alone.  
Through trials and storms, we stand firm and bold,  
For the promise You’ve given, through ages foretold.  

[Verse 3]  
In every city, with joy we'll proclaim,  
The risen Messiah, forever the same.  
Forgiveness and hope, through Jesus, we find,  
A love that unites, a vision aligned.  

[Chorus]  
Sent by Your Spirit, we rise and go,  
With the truth of Your Word, let Your love overflow.  
To the ends of the earth, we carry Your light,  
In the name of Jesus, we shine through the night.  

[Ending]  
Filled with the Spirit, we’ll share what we’ve seen,  
Declaring salvation, a new life redeemed.  
With hearts full of courage, we’ll never back down,  
For we are Your people, with Your grace, we are crowned.
