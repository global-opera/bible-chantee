[TITLE]
A People Zealous for Good Works

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the morning,  
We gather here in grace,  
With hearts that long for wisdom,  
To seek Your holy face.  
The aged men, they lead us,  
In faith and love so true,  
With patience in the trials,  
Reflecting You anew.

Chorus:  
O Lord, we lift our voices,  
To walk in Your delight,  
In sound doctrine we rejoice,  
Your truth, our guiding light.  
A people zealous for good works,  
In all things we will stand,  
Together in Your love, O Lord,  
United by Your hand.

Verse 2:  
The aged women teach us,  
With grace in every word,  
To love our homes and families,  
In You, our hope assured.  
Young men, rise up in courage,  
With minds that seek the right,  
In patterns of good living,  
We'll shine Your love so bright.

Chorus:  
O Lord, we lift our voices,  
To walk in Your delight,  
In sound doctrine we rejoice,  
Your truth, our guiding light.  
A people zealous for good works,  
In all things we will stand,  
Together in Your love, O Lord,

Bridge:  
For the grace of God has come,  
Teaching us to live in peace,  
Denying all ungodliness,  
In You, we find release.  
We look for hope eternal,  
As we seek Your holy face,  
With hearts that long for Jesus,  
We find our resting place.

Chorus:  
O Lord, we lift our voices,

Outro:  
These things we speak and cherish,  
With authority and grace,  
Let no one look down upon us,  
As we run this sacred race.  
In every act of kindness,  
In every word we share,  
We honor You, our Savior,  
In love, beyond compare.
