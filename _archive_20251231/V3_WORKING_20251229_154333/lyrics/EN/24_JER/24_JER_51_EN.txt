[TITLE]
Fall of Babylon

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, You’ve spoken, the winds arise,  
Against the heart of Babylon, Your judgment cries.  
The land of sin, the cup of shame,  
The nations tremble at the sound of Your name.  

Chorus  
Flee from her midst, O people of the light,  
For the time has come, the day turns to night.  
Your vengeance is mighty, Your truth stands tall,  
In the shadows of Babylon, let the faithful call.  

Verse 2  
The arrows are ready, the watchmen awake,  
Your purpose is certain, the ground starts to shake.  
From the heights of her towers, her treasures laid bare,  
You bring forth the justice, for all who despair.  

Chorus  
Flee from her midst, O people of the light,  
For the time has come, the day turns to night.  
Your vengeance is mighty, Your truth stands tall,  
In the shadows of Babylon, let the faithful call.  

Bridge  
You are the power, the strength of our fight,  
With Your battle axe, we stand in the light.  
The kingdoms will tremble, the strong will fall down,  
In the glory of Zion, Your victory’s crown.  

Verse 3  
The cries of the wounded, the tears of the lost,  
Echo through ages, for sin has its cost.  
Yet we are not forsaken, Your hand guides our way,  
In the midst of destruction, we lift up our praise.  

Chorus  
Flee from her midst, O people of the light,  
For the time has come, the day turns to night.  
Your vengeance is mighty, Your truth stands tall,  
In the shadows of Babylon, let the faithful call.  

Outro  
O Babylon, your time has come near,  
In the sound of the trumpet, we stand without fear.  
The Lord of Hosts reigns, His promises true,  
In the fall of the mighty, His grace will renew.  
In the fall of the mighty, His grace will renew.
