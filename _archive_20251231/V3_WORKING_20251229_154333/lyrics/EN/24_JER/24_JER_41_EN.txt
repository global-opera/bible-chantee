[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Betrayal

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the seventh month, a tale unfolds,  
Of a prince who came with a heart so cold.  
In Mizpah's land, where bread was shared,  
A darkness rose, for none had prepared.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, in the shadow of betrayal,  
We cry out, hear our hearts prevail.  
In the valley of death, Your light we seek,  
From ashes and pain, we find the meek.  

Verse 2:  
Ishmael wept as he met the throng,  
With offerings brought, but the deed was wrong.  
In the midst of sorrow, the blood was shed,  
The cries of the fallen, oh, how they spread.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, in the shadow of betrayal,  
We cry out, hear our hearts prevail.  
In the valley of death, Your light we seek,  
From ashes and pain, we find the meek.  

Bridge:  
But in the darkness, there's hope in sight,  
Johanan rose, a warrior of light.  
He gathered the lost, brought them back home,  
In the midst of despair, we are never alone.  

Verse 3:  
Though Ishmael fled, his grip did not last,  
The remnant was saved, from the shadows cast.  
Oh, Lord, we stand, united in grace,  
In the midst of our battles, we find our place.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, in the shadow of betrayal,  
We cry out, hear our hearts prevail.  
In the valley of death, Your light we seek,  
From ashes and pain, we find the meek.  

Outro:  
So we lift our voices, in worship we rise,  
In the face of betrayal, we look to the skies.  
For Your love surrounds us, Your mercy will flow,  
In the shadow of betrayal, we’ll always know.
