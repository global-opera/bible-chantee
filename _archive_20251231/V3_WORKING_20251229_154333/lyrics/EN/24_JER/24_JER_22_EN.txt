[TITLE]
Hear the Word of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
O King of Judah, hear His call,  
The throne of David stands for all.  
Execute justice, lift the weak,  
Deliver the broken, it’s Him we seek.  

Chorus:  
Hear the word of the Lord,  
Our hearts cry out for Your grace.  
In judgment and righteousness,  
We find our rightful place.  
O God, we turn from our ways,  
Restore us, we humbly pray.  
Hear the word of the Lord today.

Verse 2:  
The widow’s heart, the fatherless,  
In Your love, we find our rest.  
Do no wrong, let mercy reign,  
For the innocent, we bear their pain.  

Chorus:  
Hear the word of the Lord,  
Our hearts cry out for Your grace.  
In judgment and righteousness,  
We find our rightful place.  
O God, we turn from our ways,  
Restore us, we humbly pray.  
Hear the word of the Lord today.

Bridge:  
Woe to those who build on lies,  
In unrighteousness, their glory dies.  
But You, O Lord, You know our plight,  
In our darkness, be our light.  
We call on Heaven, hear our plea,  
Revive our hearts, set our spirits free.

Chorus:

Outro:  
O earth, earth, hear His voice,  
In His presence, we rejoice.  
With every prayer, with every song,  
We’ll heed the call, we’ll carry on.  
Hear the word of the Lord today.
