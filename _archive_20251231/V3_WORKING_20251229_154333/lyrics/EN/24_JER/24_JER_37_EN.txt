[TITLE]
Words of the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the city, the cries of despair,  
King Zedekiah’s heart was heavy, no hope in the air.  
Jeremiah stood before him, with truth to proclaim,  
“Pharaoh's help will falter, the Chaldeans still remain.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we seek Your voice, in silence, we will wait,  
Though the world may turn against us, our hearts shall not shake.  
For Your words are like a river, flowing deep and wide,  
In the midst of our affliction, we will abide.  

Verse 2  
In the prison’s dark embrace, where hope seems to fade,  
Jeremiah cried for mercy, a plea unafraid.  
“Where are the prophets now, who spoke of peace and light?  
Yet I stand here in chains, holding fast to Your might.”  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we seek Your voice, in silence, we will wait,  
Though the world may turn against us, our hearts shall not shake.  
For Your words are like a river, flowing deep and wide,  
In the midst of our affliction, we will abide.  

Bridge  
Speak, Lord, for Your servants are listening,  
In the chaos, we will trust in Your decree.  
Even when the night is closing in,  
Your promises will be our victory.

Outro  
So we lift our hands in worship, in Your presence, we will stand,  
Holding on to every promise, trusting in Your plan.  
In the darkness, You are with us, faithful till the end,  
Words of the Lord, our refuge, our Savior and our friend.
