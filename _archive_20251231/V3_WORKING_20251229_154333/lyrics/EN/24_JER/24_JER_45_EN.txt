[TITLE]
In the Shadows of Your Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence, I cry out,  
Woe is me, my heart's in doubt,  
Grief upon my weary soul,  
Where's the peace that makes me whole?  
Yet You whisper through the night,  
“Fear not, child, I am your light.”  
Though I wander, I am found,  
In Your love, my hope is bound.

Chorus:  
In the shadows of Your grace,  
I will seek Your holy face.  
Though the world may break apart,  
I find refuge in Your heart.  
You have promised, You will stay,  
In the darkness, lead my way.  
All my burdens, I release,  
In Your arms, I find my peace.

Verse 2:  
You have built and You will break,  
Every sorrow, every ache.  
What is planted, You will claim,  
Yet my life, You know my name.  
Great things I will not pursue,  
For my heart belongs to You.  
In the midst of storms I see,  
You are with me endlessly.

Bridge:  
I will trust in every word,  
In the chaos, I have heard,  
Though the land may fade away,  
In Your hands, my soul shall stay.  
You give life where hope seems lost,  
In Your love, I count the cost.  
Every trial, every tear,  
You are with me, always near.
