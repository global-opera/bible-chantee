Verse 1  
Lord, we gather in Your presence,  
With hearts that seek Your face,  
You call us to remember,  
The path we’ve lost in grace.  
In the valley where the shadows,  
Speak of sorrow and despair,  
We hear the words of warning,  
In the silence of the air.

Chorus  
Break us, Lord, like pottery,  
Shatter every prideful heart,  
From the ashes we will rise,  
In Your mercy, find our part.  
We won't turn from Your calling,  
In the valley of the slain,  
Awaken us to Your promise,  
Let us never be the same.

Verse 2  
For the blood of the innocent,  
Covers the ground we tread,  
In the fires of the idols,  
Where our hearts have often bled.  
You speak of desolation,  
Of a city turned away,  
Yet Your love still beckons gently,  
Calling us to seek Your way.

Bridge
