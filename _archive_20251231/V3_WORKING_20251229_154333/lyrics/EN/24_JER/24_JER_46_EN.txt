[TITLE]
Fear Not, O Israel

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
The word of the Lord came, like thunder in the night,  
Against the nations rising, in their fierce might.  
Egypt’s pride like a flood, roaring through the land,  
But the hand of our Savior shall make a stand.  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, O Israel, for I am with you,  
Though the storms may gather, my love will see you through.  
In the shadow of darkness, I’ll be your light,  
Fear not, O Israel, I will fight your fight.  

[Verse 2]  
The mighty have stumbled, their courage turned to dust,  
For the day of the Lord is coming, in Him we trust.  
Their chariots are trembling, their shields cast aside,  
In the face of our God, their pride cannot abide.  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, O Israel, for I am with you,  
Though the storms may gather, my love will see you through.  
In the shadow of darkness, I’ll be your light,  
Fear not, O Israel, I will fight your fight.  

[Bridge]  
O daughter of Egypt, prepare for the day,  
For the sword shall devour, the call shall not sway.  
But I will deliver, my promise stands true,  
From the north, I will save you, in all that you do.  

[Chorus]  
Fear not, O Israel, for I am with you,  
Though the storms may gather, my love will see you through.  
In the shadow of darkness, I’ll be your light,  
Fear not, O Israel, I will fight your fight.  

[Outro]  
So arise, O my people, lift your voices high,  
For the Lord of all hosts, hears our battle cry.  
Fear not, O Israel, for I am with you,  
In the midst of the chaos, my grace will renew.
