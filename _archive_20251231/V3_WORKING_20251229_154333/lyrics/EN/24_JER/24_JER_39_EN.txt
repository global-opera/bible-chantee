[TITLE]
Deliverance in the Darkness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the city, the walls begin to fall,  
Zedekiah trembles deeply, he hears the enemy’s call.  
Fleeing through the night, he ran from what was true,  
But Your hand, O Lord, was guiding him anew.  

Chorus  
Though the night may be long, and the darkness deep,  
You are the light, O Lord, in whom we can keep.  
In the midst of our sorrow, You whisper to our hearts,  
"Trust in Me, My child, for I will never part."  

Verse 2  
The flames of Babylon consumed the homes of men,  
Yet in the midst of ruin, You spoke of hope again.  
To the captive and the broken, Your promise still remains,  
"Fear not, for I am with you, through the trials and the pains."

Bridge  
Ebedmelech, hear the word of hope,  
In the trials of this life, I will help you cope.  
I will deliver you, I will set you free,  
For those who trust in Me, shall find victory.

Outro  
So lift your eyes to heaven, in the midst of despair,  
For the Lord is our refuge, our Savior, always there.  
In the ruins of our hearts, He rebuilds what was lost,  
In the arms of our Redeemer, we count not the cost.
