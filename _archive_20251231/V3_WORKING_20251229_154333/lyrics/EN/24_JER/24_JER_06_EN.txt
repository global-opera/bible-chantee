[TITLE]
O Daughter of Zion

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
O daughter of Zion, the call has been made,  
Gather your hearts, in the shadows we wade.  
Blow the trumpet, let the warning be clear,  
For evil approaches, in the darkness, we fear.  

(Chorus)  
Hear, O Earth, the sound of His voice,  
Turn back to the Lord, let Him be our choice.  
Stand in the ways, seek the old paths of grace,  
In the depths of our sorrow, He’ll meet us in this place.  

(Verse 2)  
The shepherds will come, with flocks to surround,  
Pitching their tents, where His mercy is found.  
But woe unto us, if we turn from the light,  
For the day is a-fading, and shadows steal sight.  

(Chorus)  
Hear, O Earth, the sound of His voice,  
Turn back to the Lord, let Him be our choice.  
Stand in the ways, seek the old paths of grace,  
In the depths of our sorrow, He’ll meet us in this place.  

(Bridge)  
Gird yourself with sackcloth, ashes on your face,  
Cry out in the night, for the Lord is our grace.  
He has set watchmen, yet we turn away,  
But His love calls us back to the light of the day.  

(Verse 3)  
From the north, they come, with weapons in hand,  
A great nation rising, like waves on the sand.  
Yet in our anguish, we lift up our plea,  
For the Lord is our fortress, our hope, and our plea.  

(Chorus)  
Hear, O Earth, the sound of His voice,  
Turn back to the Lord, let Him be our choice.  
Stand in the ways, seek the old paths of grace,  
In the depths of our sorrow, He’ll meet us in this place.  

(Outro)  
O daughter of Zion, arise and believe,  
In the midst of the storm, our God will not leave.  
His promise is sure, His heart ever true,  
O daughter of Zion, He’s calling for you.
