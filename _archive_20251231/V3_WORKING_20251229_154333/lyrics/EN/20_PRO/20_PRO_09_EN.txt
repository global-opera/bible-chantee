[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heart of the city, wisdom calls,  
She’s built her house, with pillars tall.  
Come, taste the bread, the wine she shares,  
A feast of life, for all who dare.  

Chorus:  
O come, O come, to wisdom's way,  
Forsake the foolish, let truth lead the day.  
With every bite, and every sip,  
In the fear of the Lord, our hearts will lift.  

Verse 2:  
She cries to the simple, “Turn in to me,  
Find understanding, and you will see.  
For the fear of the Lord is where it starts,  
A treasure of wisdom, for open hearts.”  

Chorus:  
O come, O come, to wisdom's way,  
Forsake the foolish, let truth lead the day.  
With every bite, and every sip,  
In the fear of the Lord, our hearts will lift.  

Bridge:  
Be wise, be wise, let knowledge grow,  
In the light of His truth, our spirits flow.  
Rebuke the scorner, but love the wise,  
In wisdom's embrace, our lives will rise.  

Chorus:  
O come, O come, to wisdom's way,  
Forsake the foolish, let truth lead the day.  
With every bite, and every sip,

Outro:  
So heed the call, let your heart be still,  
For wisdom’s path is the path of His will.  
In grace we gather, in love we stand,  
With wisdom’s feast, held in His hand.
