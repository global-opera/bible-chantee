[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
My heart, O Lord, I offer thee,  
To hide Your words, to set me free.  
Incline my ear to wisdom’s call,  
In understanding, may I stand tall.  

Chorus  
Oh, let me seek like silver bright,  
In search of truth, in Your pure light.  
For wisdom flows from You, my King,  
In every heart, Your praises sing.  

Verse 2  
If I cry out for knowledge rare,  
Lift up my voice in humble prayer.  
You guard my path, You light my way,  
In righteous truth, I choose to stay.  

Chorus  
Oh, let me seek like silver bright,  
In search of truth, in Your pure light.  
For wisdom flows from You, my King,  
In every heart, Your praises sing.  

Bridge  
When wisdom enters, joy will rise,  
Discretion leads, my spirit flies.  
Deliver me from paths of night,  
Keep me, O Lord, in Your pure light.  

Verse 3  
The wicked stray, their hearts mislead,  
But in Your ways, I find my creed.  
For those who walk in righteous grace,  
Shall dwell with You, in Your embrace.  

Chorus  
Oh, let me seek like silver bright,  
In search of truth, in Your pure light.  
For wisdom flows from You, my King,  
In every heart, Your praises sing.  

Outro  
So guide my steps, O Lord, I pray,  
In wisdom’s path, I’ll find my way.  
Forever anchored in Your love,  
I seek Your wisdom from above.
