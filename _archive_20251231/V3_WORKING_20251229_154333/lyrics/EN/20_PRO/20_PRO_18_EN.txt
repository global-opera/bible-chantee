[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence, we draw near,  
Seeking wisdom, Lord, we hear,  
With desire, our hearts entwined,  
In Your presence, truth we find.  
Fools may boast, but we will seek,  
In Your light, we are made meek.  

Chorus  
Your name, O Lord, our strong tower,  
In Your arms, we find our power.  
Righteous run, and we are safe,  
In Your grace, we find our place.  

Verse 2  
Words like waters, deep and true,  
Let our speech reflect on You.  
In the shadows, lies may creep,  
But Your wisdom, Lord, we keep.  
Though the haughty hearts may rise,  
In humility, our hope lies.

Bridge  
Life and death within our tongue,  
Let our praises be unsung.  
With the fruit of love we speak,  
In Your presence, we are meek.  
Friends like brothers, loyal, true,  
Closer, Lord, we cling to You.  

Chorus

Outro  
So we gather in Your light,  
Seeking wisdom, hearts in flight.
