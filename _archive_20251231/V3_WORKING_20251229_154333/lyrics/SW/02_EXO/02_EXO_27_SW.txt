[LYRICS]
Kichwa: Madhabahu ya Ibada

[NYIMBO]

Kifungu 1  
Wewe uliye juu, Bwana wa majeshi,  
Ukatupa amri, ujenzi wa madhabahu,  
Mti wa mshita, ukakufanya wewe,  
Ili ibada yetu, iwe ya kweli.

[Chorus]  
Madhabahu, madhabahu,  
Tukufu mbele yako,  
Pembe za shaba,  
Tukufu ni jina lako.  
Madhabahu, madhabahu,  
Iwe ya milele,  
Tuwalete mafuta,  
Mbele yako, Bwana wetu.
