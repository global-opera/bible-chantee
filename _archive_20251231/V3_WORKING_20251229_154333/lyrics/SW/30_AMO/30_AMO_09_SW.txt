Bwana, Tazama Neno Lako

[Verse 1]  
Nalimwona Bwana, akisimama,  
Karibu na madhabahu, akisema,  
Watakimbia wapi, hawatakimbia,  
Neno lako, Bwana, ni kweli, si uongo.

[Chorus]  
Bwana, Mungu wa majeshi,  
Uigusa nchi, ikayeyuka,
