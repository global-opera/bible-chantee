[Verse 1]  
Na ahimidiwe Bwana, mwamba wangu,  
Unifundishaye mikono yangu vita,  
Vidole vyangu kupigana,  
Mhisani wangu, boma langu,  
Ngome yangu, mwokozi wangu.
