### Title: Bwana, Nisamehe

Verse 1:  
Ninasema, nitatunza,  
Njia zangu, Ee Bwana,  
Nisije nikakosa,  
Kwa ulimi wangu,  
Ninapokabiliana na majaribu.  

Chorus:  
Bwana, unijulishe mwisho wangu,  
Nijue jinsi nilivyo dhaifu,  
Matumaini yangu ni kwako,  
Ee Mfalme, nisaidie.  

Verse 2:  
Ninanyamaza, sina faraja,  
Maumivu yangu yanazidi,  
Moyo wangu ni moto,  
Katika kuwaza, ninakushangilia.  

Chorus:  
Bwana, unijulishe mwisho wangu,  
Nijue jinsi nilivyo dhaifu,  
Matumaini yangu ni kwako,  
Ee Mfalme, nisaidie.  

Bridge:  
Umefanya siku zangu,  
Kama mashubiri,  
Kila mwanadamu ni ubatili,  
Nisamehe, uniepushe,  
Na maasi yangu yote,  
Usinifanye laumu,  
Mimi ni mgeni,  
Msafiri kama baba zangu.  

Chorus:  
Bwana, unijulishe mwisho wangu,  
Nijue jinsi nilivyo dhaifu,  
Matumaini yangu ni kwako,  
Ee Mfalme, nisaidie.  

Outro:  
Uniachilie, nikunjuke uso,  
Kabla sijaondoka,  
Nisiwepo tena,  
Bwana, nisaidie,  
Nisamehe, Ee Bwana.
