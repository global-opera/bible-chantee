[TITLE]  
Wewe Ndiye Ngome Yangu

[Verse 1]  
Ee Mungu wangu, uniponye,  
Katika adui zangu, niwe salama,  
Uniponye na maovu,  
Wanaoshindana nami, nishike mkono.

[Chorus]  
Mimi nitakuimba, asubuhi,  
Fadhili zako, ziko kwangu,  
Wewe ni ngome yangu,  
Mungu wa fadhili zangu,  
Nitatangulia sifa,  
Kwa maana wewe ni mfalme.

[Verse 2]  
Wakati wa jioni, wanalilia,  
Wakitafuta chakula, wanakosa,  
Lakini mimi nitakungoja,  
Wewe, Bwana, nguvu zangu.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
Uamke, Bwana, uwashughulike,  
Hawana nguvu mbele yako,  
Kwa dhambi zao, na maneno yao,  
Uwakomeshe, wajue wewe ni Mungu.  

[Chorus]

[Outro]  
Ee nguvu zangu, nitakuimba,  
Mungu wa fadhili zangu,  
Nitatangulia sifa,  
Wewe ndiwe ngome yangu.
