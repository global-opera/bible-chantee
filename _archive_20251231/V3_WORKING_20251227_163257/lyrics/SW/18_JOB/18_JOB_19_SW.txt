[TITLE]  
Mkombozi Wangu Yuko Hai

[Verse 1]  
Mimi ni mgeni, nikiangalia,  
Nafsi yangu inalia, Mungu, nisaidie,  
Maneno yenu yamekuwa magumu,  
Lakini najua, huwezi kuniacha.

[Chorus]  
Mkombozi wangu, yuko hai,  
Hatimaye atasimama,  
Nitaona uso wako,  
Moyo wangu unakutafuta,  
Ning’ara na utukufu wako.

[Verse 2]  
Nimepoteza njia zangu,  
Nikiwa gizani, nakusihi,  
Ndugu zangu wamenisahau,  
Lakini kwa wewe, nitaweza kuishi.

[Chorus]  
Mkombozi wangu, yuko hai,  
Hatimaye atasimama,  
Nitaona uso wako,  
Moyo wangu unakutafuta,  
Ning’ara na utukufu wako.

[Bridge]  
Nihurumieni, rafiki zangu,  
Kwa maana mkono wa Mungu umenigusa,  
Bila wewe, siwezi kusimama,  
Ninangojea, ninangojea,  
Nitamwona Mungu, nitamwona.

[Chorus]  
Mkombozi wangu, yuko hai,  
Hatimaye atasimama,  
Nitaona uso wako,  
Moyo wangu unakutafuta,  
Ning’ara na utukufu wako.

[Outro]  
Nitamwona, nitamwona,  
Nitamwona Mungu wangu,  
Mkombozi wangu, yuko hai,  
Nitatembea na wewe milele.
