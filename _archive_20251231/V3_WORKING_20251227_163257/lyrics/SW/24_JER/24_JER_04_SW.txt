Rudi Kwangu, Ee Israeli

[Verse 1]  
Ee Israeli, rudi, rudi kwangu,  
Mimi ni Bwana, nisiyeondoa,  
Tazama, machukizo yako, yasiwe mbele,  
Nitaondoa ghadhabu yangu, niwe nawe.
