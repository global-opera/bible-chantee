[TITLE]  
Mfalme wa Israeli

[Verse 1]  
Samweli alizungumza, sauti ya Bwana,  
"Sauli, nakutuma, enda ukawapige,  
Waamaleki waovu, wamesimama mbele,  
Ning’ang’anie, ili uonyeshe uaminifu."

[Chorus]  
Mfalme, mfalme, umekosea,  
Umeacha hai, umepotea njia,  
Kutii ni bora, kuliko dhabihu,  
Ee Bwana, tusaidie, tuwe waaminifu.

[Verse 2]  
Sauli alijibu, "Nimekishika neno,"  
Lakini sauti za watu, zilimshinda,  
Kondoo na ng'ombe, walileta nyara,  
Lakini Bwana, unataka mioyo safi.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
Samweli alilia, usiku kucha,  
Bwana akasema, "Nimegairi,  
Sauli, umepoteza, ufalme wako,  
Kukataa neno langu, ni dhambi kubwa."

[Chorus]  
Mfalme, mfalme, umekosea,  
Umeacha hai, umepotea njia,

[Verse 3]  
Katika wingu la ushindi, tutaimba,  
Kama Samweli, tutaomba kwa bidii,  
Tukumbuke, Bwana, uaminifu wako,  
Katika vita vyote, tusimame na wewe.

[Outro]  
Tutakushukuru, Bwana wa majeshi,  
Tukiwa watiifu, tutashinda vita,  
Ee Mfalme wetu, tuwe na moyo safi,  
Katika neema yako, tutaishi milele.
