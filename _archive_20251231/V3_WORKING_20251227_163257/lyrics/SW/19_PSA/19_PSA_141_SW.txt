[LYRICS]
### NITAKUITA, EE BWANA

[Verse 1]  
Ee Bwana, nimekuita, unijie hima,  
Sauti yangu inakuita, umesikia sala.  
Kama uvumba wa jioni, ipae mbele zako,  
Mikono yangu inainuka, dhabihu kwako.

[Chorus]  
Ninakuja, Ee Bwana, unilinde,  
Ukiwa mlinzi, nitatulia.  
Moyo wangu unakuelekea,  
Usiniache, unifanye huru.

[Verse 2]  
Uwe mlinzi kinywani pangu,  
Nisijikite kwenye maovu ya dunia.  
Mwenye haki anipige, itakuwa fadhili,  
Nitakumbatia, Ee Bwana, fadhili zako.

[Chorus]  
Ninakuja, Ee Bwana, unilinde,  
Ukiwa mlinzi, nitatulia.  
Moyo wangu unakuelekea,  
Usiniache, unifanye huru.

[Bridge]  
Macho yangu yanakuelekea,  
Nilijua, Bwana, nitatoka salama.  
Wabaya waanguke, watatumbukia,  
Mimi nitatembea, na wewe ni mwanga.

[Chorus]  
Ninakuja, Ee Bwana, unilinde,  
Ukiwa mlinzi, nitatulia.  
Moyo wangu unakuelekea,  
Usiniache, unifanye huru.

[Outro]  
Ee Bwana, nimekuita, unijie hima,  
Sala yangu ipae, kwa jina lako.  
Nitatembea kwa amani, na wewe ni ngao,  
Nitatumikia milele, Ee Bwana, asante.
