Kichwa: Kumbuka, Ee Bwana

Verse 1:  
Sikieni, enyi mbingu,  
Neno lako linanitafuta,  
Wewe uliye Mtakatifu,  
Tukufu, Bwana wa majeshi.  
Watoto wako wameasi,  
Wameacha njia zako,  
Ng'ombe amjua bwana wake,  
Lakini Israeli hawajui.
