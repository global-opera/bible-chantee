[TITLE] UPO KWETU, EWE BWANA

[Verse 1]  
Katika giza, nimesimama,  
Moyo wangu umekata tamaa,  
Lakini nakuomba, Ee Bwana,  
Nikumbuke, nakusihi, nisaidie.  

[Chorus]  
Upo kwetu, Ee Bwana,  
Nimeona machozi yangu,  
Unitendee, na uniponye,  
Nitatangaza wema wako.  

[Verse 2]  
Hezekia alijigeuza,  
Ukatuma neno lako,  
Ukamwambia, “Nimekusikia,  
Nitazidisha siku zako."  

[Chorus]  
Upo kwetu, Ee Bwana,  
Nimeona machozi yangu,  
Unitendee, na uniponye,  
Nitatangaza wema wako.  

[Bridge]  
Kuzimu hakuwezi kukusifu,  
Lakini mimi nitaimba,  
Aliye hai, atakukumbuka,  
Baba, watoto watakujua.  

[Verse 3]  
Kwa amani, umeniponya,  
Dhambi zangu umenitupa,  
Nitakuimba, siku zote,  
Nyumbani mwako, nitakutumikia.  

[Chorus]  
Upo kwetu, Ee Bwana,  
Nimeona machozi yangu,  
Unitendee, na uniponye,  
Nitatangaza wema wako.  

[Outro]  
Ee Bwana, nitakutukuza,  
Kwa vinubi, nyimbo zangu,  
Katika nyumba yako, nitakaa,  
Milele, Ee Bwana, ni wewe.  

(Repeat Chorus as desired)
