[TITLE]  
Eliya na Elisha

[Verse 1]  
Katika Gilgali, Bwana alituma,  
Eliya akamwambia, Elisha, simama.  
“Ka hapa, mpenzi, kwa mapenzi Yake,  
Nitatembea, siwezi kuondoka nawe.”

[Chorus]  
Eliya, Eliya, wa mfalme wa Israeli,  
Roho yako inang'ara, inatuongoza.  
Katika vichaka na katika milima,  
Bwana wetu, twakutukuza, tufuate, oh!

[Verse 2]  
Wana wa manabii, walikujia,  
“Jua, bwana wako atachukuliwa, si wewe?”  
Elisha alijibu, “Nyamazeni, nashikilia,  
Sitaondoka, Bwana, ni wewe nipe nguvu.”

[Chorus]  
Eliya, Eliya, wa mfalme wa Israeli,  
Roho yako inang'ara, inatuongoza.

[Bridge]  
Maji yamegawanyika, njia ilifunguka,  
Eliya akapanda, mbinguni akatoweka.  
Elisha na mavazi, alikumbuka,  
“Yuko wapi, Bwana, Mungu wa Eliya?”

[Chorus]

[Verse 3]  
Mji wa Yeriko, walimwita,  
Maji ya chemchemi, Bwana alitenda.  
Chumvi ililetwa, akasema, “Nimeponya,  
Maji haya sasa, hayatazaa maumivu.”

[Outro]  
Eliya na Elisha, wa imani kuu,  
Tunaimba, tunasifu, tunasonga mbele.  
Mungu wetu, utukufu ni wako,  
Katika historia, tutakutukuza!
