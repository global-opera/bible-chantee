[TITLE]
Covenant at the Door

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness, the Lord spoke clear,  
To Moses, His servant, words to revere.  
“Gather My people, bring forth the signs,  
Anointing oil and offerings divine.”  
With Aaron and sons, they stood in the light,  
Clothed in the garments, prepared for the rite.

Chorus:  
At the door of the tabernacle, we stand,  
In reverence and awe, we lift up our hands.  
Consecration and glory, our hearts now restored,  
We come as one body, united, adored.  
O Lord, hear our cry, as we worship and sing,  
In the shadow of mercy, our praises we bring.

Verse 2:  
With water and blood, Moses cleansed every part,  
The altar was sanctified, a pure work of heart.  
The ram of the offering, a sweet-scented flame,  
In obedience to You, we gather in Your name.  
Anointed with oil, the covenant sealed,  
Together in faith, our hearts are revealed.

Chorus:  
At the door of the tabernacle, we stand,

Consecration and glory, our hearts now restored,  
We come as one body, united, adored.

Bridge:  
For seven days we linger, in Your presence, we dwell,  
Keeping watch at the door, where Your love casts a spell.  
With the bread of Your promise, and the flesh of our praise,  
We feast on Your goodness, through all of our days.

Chorus:  
At the door of the tabernacle, we stand,

Outro:  
So let us remember, as we echo this call,  
In the covenant made, we surrender it all.  
At the door of Your presence, forever we’ll stay,  
In the joy of Your mercy, we find our way.
