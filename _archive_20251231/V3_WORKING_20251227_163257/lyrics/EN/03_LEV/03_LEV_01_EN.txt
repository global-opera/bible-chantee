[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of Your presence, Lord,  
You call us near, our hearts restored.  
With offerings from our hands we bring,  
A sacrifice, our praises sing.  
From the herds and flocks we choose,  
A male without blemish, we must not lose.  
With willing hearts, we stand today,  
At the door of grace, we humbly pray.  

Chorus  
Burn, oh fire, rise to the throne,  
A sweet aroma, You make us Your own.  
In the ashes, our burdens lay,  
As we offer ourselves in this sacred way.  
Lord, we bring our hearts anew,  
A living sacrifice, holy and true.  
With every breath, we long for You,  
In the fire of love, our spirits renew.  

Verse 2  
As the priests prepare the altar's flame,  
Blood of atonement, we call Your name.  
In the order of sacrifice, we find our place,  
Washed in mercy, embraced by grace.  
With inwards cleansed, our hearts laid bare,  
Upon this altar, Lord, we share.  
With turtledoves and pigeons in flight,  
We seek Your mercy, O God of light.

Bridge  
Every offering, great or small,  
From the depths of our hearts, we give it all.  
As the flames ascend, may our voices rise,  
In the beauty of surrender, our spirits fly.  
Lord, ignite our hearts with holy fire,  
To be a living sacrifice, our one desire.
