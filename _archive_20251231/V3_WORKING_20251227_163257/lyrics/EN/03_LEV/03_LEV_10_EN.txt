### Title: "The Fire of Holiness"

Verse 1  
In the silence of the tent, the incense rose,  
Yet strange fire was offered, and the heart knows,  
The God of glory demands a sacred place,  
For His name is holy, a consuming grace.  

Chorus  
Sanctify, O Lord, in all we bring,  
In our hearts and lives, let Your praises ring.  
Teach us to discern, the clean from the unclean,  
In reverent awe, before Your holy scene.  

Verse 2  
Moses spoke with wisdom, to Aaron’s pain,  
"Hold your peace, for glory shall remain."  
With heavy hearts, they bore the cost of sin,  
In the sacred space, where the light begins.  

Chorus  
Sanctify, O Lord, in all we bring,  
In our hearts and lives, let Your praises ring.  
Teach us to discern, the clean from the unclean,  
In reverent awe, before Your holy scene.  

Bridge  
Let us not forget, the weight of Your decree,  
To honor You, O Lord, in all humility.  
For the blood that covers, for the grace that flows,  
In Your presence, Lord, our spirit knows.  

Verse 3  
In the offering of fire, we seek Your face,  
With every sacrifice, we find our place.  
May our hearts be pure, as we come to You,  
In the shadow of Your grace, we are made new.  

Chorus  
Sanctify, O Lord, in all we bring,  
In our hearts and lives, let Your praises ring.  
Teach us to discern, the clean from the unclean,  
In reverent awe, before Your holy scene.  

Outro  
O Lord, our God, to You we come,  
With hearts wide open, Your will be done.  
In the fire of holiness, let our lives proclaim,  
The beauty of Your mercy, forever Your name.
