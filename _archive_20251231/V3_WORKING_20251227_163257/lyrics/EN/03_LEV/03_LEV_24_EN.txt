[TITLE]
Eternal Light and Covenant Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, You spoke to Moses,  
"Bring pure oil, let the lamps shine bright,  
In the tabernacle, a holy place of closeness,  
Let the fire burn, a beacon in the night."  
With every flame, a promise kept,  
In the heart of Your people, Your presence met.  

Chorus  
Eternal light, O Lord, we seek,  
In the shadows, Your voice we hear,  
With every offering, our hearts we bring,  
In covenant grace, we draw near.  
Your love, a testament, forever true,  
In the light of Your mercy, we’re made new.  

Verse 2  
Twelve loaves on the table, pure and fine,  
With frankincense rising, a sweet perfume,  
In remembrance of the bond, we intertwine,  
Your spirit guiding us, in the holy room.  
For every heart that strays away,  
You call us back, Your love displays.

Bridge  
And though we falter, and sometimes stray,  
Your law of love, it lights our way.  
With grace unending, Your mercy flows,  
In the depths of our hearts, Your truth bestows.  
We stand united, one and all,  
In the name of Jesus, we heed Your call.
