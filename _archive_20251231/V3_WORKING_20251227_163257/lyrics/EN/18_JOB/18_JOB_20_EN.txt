[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
I ponder all Your ways,  
The triumph of the wicked,  
Fades like morning haze.  
For they rise upon the clouds,  
With pride that leads to fall,  
But their joy is fleeting,  
Oh, Lord, You see it all.

Chorus  
You are the just and faithful,  
You hold the scales of grace,  
In the shadow of our trials,  
Your love is our embrace.  
Though the wicked seem to prosper,  
Their end is not in sight,  
In Your presence, we find refuge,  
You are our guiding light.

Verse 2  
Though they feast on empty wealth,  
And hide their hearts in shame,  
Their riches turn to bitterness,  
Their pride, a fleeting name.  
For every hand that's lifted,  
In greed's unholy dance,  
Will face the wrath of Heaven,  
In Your holy glance.

Bridge  
Oh, Lord, reveal our hearts,  
In Your mercy, we abide,  
Let us seek the humble path,  
With You as our guide.  
Though the earth may tremble,  
And the skies may roar,  
We will stand in Your promise,  
Forever, evermore.
