### 
Verse 1  
In the quiet, I call on You,  
When the storms of life surround,  
I seek Your face, my refuge true,  
In Your grace, my hope is found.  
Though the foolish may take root,  
And the envious may rise,  
You, O Lord, are my pursuit,  
In Your love, my spirit flies.

Chorus  
You do great things, unsearchable,  
Marvelous beyond all we know.  
You lift the low, restore the soul,  
In Your hands, my heart will grow.  
Though troubles come like sparks in flight,  
I trust in You, my guiding light.

Verse 2  
When the hungry seek their way,  
And the robbers steal the night,  
You deliver, You don't delay,  
In Your mercy, I find light.  
You take the wise in their own schemes,  
And turn their plans to dust,  
But in You, my heart redeems,  
In Your promise, I will trust.
