[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Your Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Is there not a time for man,  
Days like a fleeting shadow,  
As a servant seeks the sun,  
Longing for the dawn to show.  
In the nights of weariness,  
I cry out for the light,  
When will dawn bring me rest,  
And chase away the night?

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, my days are fleeting,  
Like a whisper in the breeze,  
Yet I find my hope in You,  
In Your grace, my spirit frees.  
Though the earth may fade away,  
In Your love, I find my way,  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
I’ll seek You all my days.

Verse 2:  
My flesh is worn, my heart is tired,  
Life feels like a distant dream,  
But Your eyes, they’re ever near,  
In my anguish, hear me scream.  
What is man that You should care,  
Visit him with tender grace?  
Though I falter on this path,  
In Your arms, I find my place.

Though the night may seem so long,  
In Your light, I am made strong.
