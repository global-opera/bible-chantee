Verse 1  
Hear my heart, O Lord, I come to You,  
With the words of my soul, I long to be true.  
In the silence of night, when dreams begin to flow,  
You open my ears, revealing what I know.  

Chorus  
For You are God, greater than all man,  
In Your hands, my life, You hold in Your plan.  
From the shadows, You call me to light,  
Deliver my soul, Lord, and guide me through night.
