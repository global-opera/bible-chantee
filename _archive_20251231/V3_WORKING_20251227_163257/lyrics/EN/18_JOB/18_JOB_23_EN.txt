[TITLE]
In the Silence You Are Near

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows, my heart is heavy,  
I cry out, Lord, where can I find?  
I long to stand before Your presence,  
To share my heart, to know Your mind.  
Though I seek You in every direction,  
I cannot see, but I know You’re there,  
For You know the path that I wander,  
And in my trials, Your love I bear.

Chorus  
In the silence, You are near,  
You hold my heart, You calm my fear.  
Through the darkness, I will stand,  
For I am Yours, held in Your hand.  
When I’m refined like gold, I’ll rise,  
In Your embrace, my spirit flies.

Verse 2  
I hold Your steps, I seek Your wisdom,  
Esteeming Your words more than my need.  
In the quiet, my soul is listening,  
For in Your truth, my heart is freed.  
Though the storms may come around me,  
And the world may shake, I will not sway,  
For You are steadfast in my struggle,  
Your promises guide me day by day.

Bridge  
Lord, You are in one mind,  
And who can change Your way?  
You perform the plans You’ve set,  
I trust in You today.  
Though I tremble in Your presence,  
And my heart is soft and bare,  
I will lift my voice in praise,  
For You are always there.

Outro  
In the silence, Lord, You are near,  
Forever with me, my heart You steer.  
Through every trial, I will trust,  
For in Your love, Lord, I find my rest.  
In You, my soul will be at peace,  
In Your presence, my fears will cease.
