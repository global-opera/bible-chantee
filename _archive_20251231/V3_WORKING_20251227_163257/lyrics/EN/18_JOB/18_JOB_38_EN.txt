[TITLE]
In the Whirlwind

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the whirlwind, You spoke to Job,  
Who are we to question, who are we to know?  
You laid the earth's foundation, with wisdom profound,  
In the depths of creation, Your glory resounds.  

Chorus:  
You are the God of all the skies,  
The morning stars sing, to You they rise.  
Who can fathom the depths of Your might?  
In the whirlwind, You bring forth light.  

Verse 2:  
Where were we when You commanded the sea?  
When You set the boundaries, declared it to be?  
You cloth the sky with clouds, and darkness does yield,  
In Your hands, every promise is sealed.  

Chorus:  
You are the God of all the skies,  
The morning stars sing, to You they rise.  
Who can fathom the depths of Your might?  
In the whirlwind, You bring forth light.  

Bridge:  
In the treasures of snow, Your wonders unfold,  
You guide the lightning, Your story is told.  
Oh, the lions find their prey, in Your sovereign grace,  
In every moment, Lord, we seek Your face.  

Chorus:  
You are the God of all the skies,  
The morning stars sing, to You they rise.  
Who can fathom the depths of Your might?  
In the whirlwind, You bring forth light.  

Outro:  
So we stand in awe, in reverent fear,  
For the God of creation, our hearts draw near.  
In the whirlwind, we find our peace,  
In the depth of Your love, our worries cease.
