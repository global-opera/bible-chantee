[TITLE]
In the Whisper of Your Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Born of woman, just a breath,  
Days are fleeting, filled with depth.  
Like a flower, cut down in bloom,  
Yet in Your hands, there's hope in gloom.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You see me, You know my ways,  
In the shadow of my troubles, I seek Your face.  
Though my days are numbered, I find my rest,  
In the whisper of Your grace, I am blessed.  

Verse 2:  
Like a tree that falls, yet sprouts anew,  
In the scent of water, Your promise rings true.  
Though my body fades, my spirit will rise,  
For in Your presence, my heart never dies.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You see me, You know my ways,  
In the shadow of my troubles, I seek Your face.  
Though my days are numbered, I find my rest,  
In the whisper of Your grace, I am blessed.  

Bridge:  
When I lie down, I trust in You,  
In the silence, Your love breaks through.  
Though the mountains fall and the waters dry,  
You hold my heart, my Savior, my guide.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, You see me, You know my ways,  
In the shadow of my troubles, I seek Your face.  
Though my days are numbered, I find my rest,  
In the whisper of Your grace, I am blessed.  

Outro:  
So I will wait, till my change shall come,  
With hope in my heart, I'll rise with the sun.  
For You call my name, and I will respond,  
In the whisper of Your grace, I am drawn.
