[TITLE]
Faithful Servants of the Lord

[LYRICS]

VERSE 1  
In the house of our God, they stand so strong,  
Meshelemiah, with sons, where they belong.  
Zechariah leads, wise and true,  
In the gates of the Lord, they rise anew.  
Obededom’s sons, valiant and brave,  
In service and honor, their hearts they gave.  

CHORUS  
O lift your voice, let praises soar,  
For the faithful ones who serve and adore.  
In every gate, where the light shines bright,  
They guard the truth, in the day and night.  

VERSE 2  
From the east to the west, their lots were cast,  
Divisions of porters, steadfast and vast.  
Shemaiah’s might, a legacy bold,  
In the treasures of heaven, their stories unfold.  
With strength in their hands and faith in their hearts,  
They gather together, each playing their part.  

CHORUS  
O lift your voice, let praises soar,  
For the faithful ones who serve and adore.  
In every gate, where the light shines bright,  
They guard the truth, in the day and night.  

BRIDGE  
For every battle won, for every song sung,  
In the service of the King, our hearts are young.  
Hashabiah leads with courage so grand,  
Together we rise, united we stand.  
In the treasures of faith, our hope will remain,  
We’re mighty in spirit, through joy and through pain.  

CHORUS  
O lift your voice, let praises soar,  
For the faithful ones who serve and adore.  
In every gate, where the light shines bright,  
They guard the truth, in the day and night.  

OUTRO  
So here we gather, as one in His grace,  
In the house of the Lord, we find our place.  
With hearts of valor, we’ll carry the light,  
Together forever, in His love, we unite.
