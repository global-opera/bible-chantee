[TITLE]
The Lord Preserves

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the valley, where giants used to stand,  
David rose with courage, by the Lord’s mighty hand.  
He smote the Philistines, their stronghold brought to dust,  
In every trial and battle, he placed his faith and trust.  

Chorus  
For the Lord preserves, wherever we may go,  
He fights our every battle, His power we will show.  
From mountains high to valleys low, His grace will lead the way,  
In the name of Jesus, we rise and we will say.  

Verse 2  
Moab bowed before him, brought forth gifts in praise,  
Hadarezer fell before him, in the Lord’s mighty gaze.  
With chariots and horsemen, he claimed the land so wide,  
Through every storm and struggle, the Lord was by his side.

Bridge  
With gold and silver offerings, we dedicate our lives,  
From every nation gathered, His glory will arise.  
O King of all the heavens, we lift our hearts in song,  
For You have won the victory, in You we all belong.

Outro  
So let the banners wave, let the praises fill the air,  
For the Lord preserves His people, in His love, we stand and share.  
With every step of faith we take, we trust in His embrace,  
In the heart of every battle, we find His strength and grace.
