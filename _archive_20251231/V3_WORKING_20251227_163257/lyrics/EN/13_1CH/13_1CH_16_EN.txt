[TITLE]
In Your Presence, We Rejoice

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
We gather in Your holy name,  
Before the Ark, we lift our praise.  
With offerings of bread and peace,  
Our hearts unite in love’s embrace.  
You’ve shown us wonders, mighty deeds,  
Your faithfulness our souls have freed.  
From Abraham to Jacob’s line,  
Your promises, O Lord, divine.

Chorus  
In Your presence, we rejoice,  
We sing of all Your wondrous works.  
Let the heavens and the earth proclaim,  
Great is the Lord, forever reigns.  
We give thanks, O God of might,  
Your mercy holds us through the night.  
With every heart, we lift our voice,  
In Your presence, we rejoice.

Verse 2  
From nation to nation, You guide our way,  
Reproving kings, You keep us safe.  
O blessed Savior, gather us near,  
Deliver us, cast out our fear.  
We seek Your strength, we seek Your face,  
In every trial, we find our place.  
Your glory shines, our hearts ignite,  
We worship You, the source of light.

Bridge  
O give thanks unto the Lord,  
For He is good, His love endures.  
With every trumpet, every song,  
We declare Your glory, where we belong.  
Let the trees and fields, all sing out,  
For in Your presence, we have no doubt.  
You are our Savior, our guiding light,  
In Your embrace, we find our fight.

Outro  
So let us gather, hearts entwined,  
In unity, Your love we find.  
O Lord, our God, we bless Your name,  
Forever and ever, we’ll proclaim.  
Amen, Amen, we sing in grace,  
In Your presence, Lord, we find our place.
