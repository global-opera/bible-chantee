[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land where rivers flow,  
Joshua called the faithful ones,  
Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh,  
Your hearts have followed, your work well done.  
You stood beside your brothers true,  
Obeyed the voice of the Lord anew,  
Now the rest has come, His promise clear,  
Return to your tents, for the time is here.

[Chorus]  
O love the Lord your God,  
Walk in all His ways,  
Keep His commandments,  
Serve Him all your days.  
With all your heart, with all your soul,  
Cleave to Him, let His peace make you whole.  
O love the Lord your God,  
Witness from the start,  
Together we stand, united in heart.

[Verse 2]  
But at Jordan’s edge, an altar rose,  
A sign of faith, where love still flows.  
Fear not, dear brothers, we’ll not rebel,  
For our hearts are one, and in Him we dwell.  
A witness built for generations to see,  
That our bond in the Lord shall ever be free.  
So let not the river divide us apart,  
For the Lord is God, He knows every heart.

Our altar speaks, a promise made,
