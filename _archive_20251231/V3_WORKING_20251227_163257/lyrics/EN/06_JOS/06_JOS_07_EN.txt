[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land where shadows fall,  
A tale of pride, we heed the call.  
Achan’s heart, a secret kept,  
In the midst of Israel, he wept.  
He took the cursed, he turned away,  
And in his sin, our hope did sway.  

[Chorus]  
Lord, we come, our faces low,  
In the valley where sorrow flows.  
Cleanse our hearts, renew our song,  
In Your grace, we will be strong.  
Lead us forth, through every fight,  
In Your name, we find our light.  

[Verse 2]  
Joshua cried, “O Lord, we plead,  
Why have You brought us here to bleed?”  
Yet in the dark, Your voice rang clear,  
“Rise up, my people, cast out your fear.”  
For in the midst of sin we stand,  
With heavy hearts, we seek Your hand.
