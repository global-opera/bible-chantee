[LYRICS]
[TITLE] In Your Promised Land

[Verse 1]  
Together we gather, in the land of our dreams,  
The Levites are calling, from the heart of our themes.  
You’ve given us cities, with spaces to dwell,  
In Your grace and Your mercy, we rise and we tell.  

[Chorus]  
You’ve given us rest, in the promise so true,  
Every word that You spoke, Lord, we find it in You.  
With the strength of our fathers, we stand hand in hand,  
In the cities of refuge, in Your promised land.  

[Verse 2]  
From the hills of Judah, to the valleys so wide,  
You’ve blessed us with harvest, and You walk by our side.  
With every provision, we lift up Your name,  
For the battles You’ve won, we will never be the same.  

[Chorus]  
You’ve given us rest, in the promise so true,  
Every word that You spoke, Lord, we find it in You.  
With the strength of our fathers, we stand hand in hand,  
In the cities of refuge, in Your promised land.  

[Bridge]  
And though the storms may rise, we will not be afraid,  
For with You, Lord, we’re anchored, in the plans that You’ve laid.  
You delivered our foes, not a word fell to the ground,  
In the light of Your glory, our victory is found.  

[Chorus]  
You’ve given us rest, in the promise so true,  
Every word that You spoke, Lord, we find it in You.  
With the strength of our fathers, we stand hand in hand,  
In the cities of refuge, in Your promised land.  

[Outro]  
With each step that we take, Lord, we honor Your name,  
For the hope that You’ve given, forever the same.  
In the cities of refuge, we’ll forever abide,  
In the arms of our Savior, our hearts open wide.
