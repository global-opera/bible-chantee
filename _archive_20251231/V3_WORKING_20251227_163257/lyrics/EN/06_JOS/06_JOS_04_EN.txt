Verse 1  
When the people crossed the Jordan,  
With the ark before their way,  
The waters parted, grace unbroken,  
Leading us to brighter days.  
Twelve men called from every tribe,  
Each a stone upon their shoulder,  
A memorial for all time,  
Of the God who makes us bolder.  

Chorus  
These stones, a sign of faith,  
For the children who will ask,  
"What do these stones mean for us?"  
We’ll tell of love, we’ll tell of grace,  
Of the mighty hand of God,  
Who led us through the raging waves.  

Verse 2  
In the midst of every battle,

With every stone, our hearts will run,  
To declare His wondrous light.
