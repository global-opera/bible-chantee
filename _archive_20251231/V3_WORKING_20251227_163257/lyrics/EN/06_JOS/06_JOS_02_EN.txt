[TITLE]
The Scarlet Thread

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of Jericho, two men did tread,  
Sent to spy the promised land, where the faithful led.  
In a harlot’s humble home, they found a place to hide,  
Rahab’s heart was brave and bold, with the truth inside.  

Pre-Chorus:  
She knew the Lord had spoken, His power in the night,  
The fear of God had fallen, their hearts lost in fright.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the scarlet thread, a promise to the free,  
A sign of grace and mercy, for all who dare to believe.  
In the depths of darkness, His light will shine anew,  
With the blood of the covenant, He will rescue you.  

Verse 2:  
“Save my family, hear my plea,” she cried with all her might,  
“Bind this thread upon my door, let it be my guiding light.”  
The men declared, “We’ll honor this, for your kindness shown,  
When the walls come crashing down, you’ll never be alone.”  

Pre-Chorus:  
So she let them down in faith, with a cord of red so bright,  
Their lives exchanged for hers, in the stillness of the night.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the scarlet thread, a promise to the free,  
A sign of grace and mercy, for all who dare to believe.  
In the depths of darkness, His light will shine anew,  
With the blood of the covenant, He will rescue you.  

Bridge:  
From the ashes of the past, He’s making all things new,  
In the ruins of despair, His love will carry you.  
For the Lord your God is with you, with power from above,  
In the face of every battle, you stand in His great love.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the scarlet thread, a promise to the free,  
A sign of grace and mercy, for all who dare to believe.  
In the depths of darkness, His light will shine anew,  
With the blood of the covenant, He will rescue you.  

Outro:  
So raise the scarlet thread, lift your voice in praise,  
For the God who fights for us, in all our broken days.  
In the story of Rahab, His faithfulness we see,  
With a scarlet thread of promise, He has set us free.
