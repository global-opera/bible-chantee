[TITLE]
Inheritance of the Brave

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of promise, where the giants roam,  
Manasseh's children seek a place called home.  
With hearts of courage and a spirit so bold,  
They rise to claim the gifts that God foretold.  

Chorus:  
Inheritance of the brave, we rise in faith,  
Your promises remain, O Lord, our strength and grace.  
Though the battles rage and the giants stand tall,  
With You beside us, we will conquer all.  

Verse 2:  
Daughters of Zelophehad, their voices raised high,  
In a world that tried to silence, they would not comply.  
"Give us our portion, for we are Your own,  
In the family of faith, we claim our throne."  

Chorus:  
Inheritance of the brave, we rise in faith,  
Your promises remain, O Lord, our strength and grace.  
Though the battles rage and the giants stand tall,  
With You beside us, we will conquer all.  

Bridge:  
From the hills to the valleys, we will not fear,  
With every step forward, Your presence is near.  
Though chariots of iron may try to deceive,  
In the power of Your name, we will believe.  

Verse 3:  
Joshua spoke with wisdom, urging them on,  
"Take the mountains, cut the wood, the battle's not won.  
For you're a great people, with strength in your hands,  
Drive out the darkness, claim the promised lands."  

Chorus:  
Inheritance of the brave, we rise in faith,  
Your promises remain, O Lord, our strength and grace.  
Though the battles rage and the giants stand tall,  
With You beside us, we will conquer all.  

Outro:  
So we lift our voices, declaring Your might,  
In the face of the storms, we walk in Your light.  
With hearts full of courage, we stand and proclaim,  
Inheritance of the brave, we glorify Your name.
