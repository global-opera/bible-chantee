[TITLE]
Inheritance of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where shadows fall,  
We gather, hearts entwined,  
At Shiloh, we heed Your call,  
To seek the place You’ve lined.  
The tribes await their promised share,  
Yet some stand still, unsure,  
O Joshua, with wisdom rare,  
Lead us to the land secure.  

Chorus  
We will rise and take our stand,  
Claim the gifts You’ve laid at hand.  
Inherit all You’ve made for me,  
In the promise, Lord, we believe.  
With every step, we’ll walk in grace,  
In Your presence, we find our place.  
We will trust and never fear,  
For our inheritance is near.  

Verse 2  
Through the valleys, mountains high,  
We’ll journey in Your name,  
Describing lands beneath the sky,  
For Your glory, we proclaim.  
The Levites dwell in sacred grace,  
Yet we are called to fight,  
To carve our homes in this wide space,  
With faith, we claim our right.

Bridge  
So cast the lots before our eyes,  
Let our hearts be bold and true.  
From Judah’s hills to Benjamin’s skies,  
We’ll trust in all that You will do.  
In the cities, we’ll declare Your name,  
From Jericho to Jerusalem’s heart,  
In unity, we’ll rise, unashamed,  
And play the role that’s set apart.
