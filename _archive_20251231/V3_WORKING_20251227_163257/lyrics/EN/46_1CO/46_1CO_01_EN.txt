### Title: "Glory in the Lord"

Verse 1  
In the calling of His grace,  
We are sanctified in faith,  
Together we gather here,  
In the name of Jesus, dear.  
No division in our hearts,  
Joined in spirit, set apart,  
For the truth that we proclaim,  
Is the power of His name.  

Chorus  
Let us glory in the Lord,  
He is wisdom, He’s our sword,  
In our weakness, He is strong,  
In His love, we all belong.  
Waiting for the coming King,  
To our hearts, His praises sing,  
In the fellowship we find,  
Christ unites us, heart and mind.  

Verse 2  
From the wise and from the meek,  
God has chosen those who seek,  
Not in riches, nor in pride,  
But in Jesus, we abide.  
For the cross brings life anew,  
In its foolishness, we’re true,  
Every gift He gives to share,  
In His grace, we find our prayer.

Bridge  
Every heart and every soul,  
In His presence, we are whole,  
Blameless in the day to come,  
In His love, we are made one.  
Called to preach the cross of grace,  
In His power, we embrace,  
Jews and Greeks, all stand as one,  
In the Savior, God’s own Son.

In the victory He’s begun.
