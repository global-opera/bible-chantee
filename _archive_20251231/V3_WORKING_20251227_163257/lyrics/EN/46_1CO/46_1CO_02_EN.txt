Verse 1  
In weakness, I come with trembling heart,  
Not seeking the wisdom of this world’s part.  
For in the shadow of the cross I stand,  
Declaring Your truth, Lord, by Your hand.  
Jesus Christ, the crucified,  
The power of God, my hope, my guide.

Chorus  
Reveal to us, O Spirit, deep and wide,  
The mysteries of grace where love abides.  
Eye has not seen, nor ear has heard,  
The glory of our God, His holy Word.  
We have the mind of Christ, our hearts ignite,  
In Your wisdom, Lord, we find our light.

Verse 2  
Not in enticing words, but in Your might,  
We share the gospel, shining through the night.  
The wisdom of the ages, hidden yet clear,  
In every promise, Lord, we draw near.  
For what the world can’t see, we now embrace,  
The depths of love revealed in Your grace.
