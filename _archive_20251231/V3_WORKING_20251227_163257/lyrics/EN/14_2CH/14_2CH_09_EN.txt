[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of Sheba, a queen did arise,  
To seek out the wisdom, beneath the skies.  
With treasures of gold and spices so rare,  
She journeyed to Solomon, her heart laid bare.  

Chorus  
Oh, blessed be the LORD, who reigns on high,  
In the throne of wisdom, where truth will never die.  
From the depths of our hearts, we come and we seek,  
For the light that guides us, the strength of the weak.  

Verse 2  
With questions unspoken, she sought to know,  
The depth of his wisdom, the grace he would show.  
He answered her queries, nothing hid from sight,  
In the house of the LORD, her spirit took flight.  

Chorus  
Oh, blessed be the LORD, who reigns on high,  
In the throne of wisdom, where truth will never die.  
From the depths of our hearts, we come and we seek,

Bridge  
See the golden vessels, glisten in the light,  
A kingdom adorned, in glory and might.  
The nations will gather, to hear of the King,  
For the wisdom of Solomon, the hope that he brings.  

Verse 3  
And with every blessing, her heart filled with praise,  
She knew in his presence, her fears would erase.  
For the God who delights, in justice and grace,  
Has chosen a ruler, to lead in His place.  

Chorus

Outro  
So let us remember, the story of old,  
Of a king named Solomon, with wisdom untold.  
In the journey of seeking, may we find our way,  
In the light of His goodness, forever we’ll stay.
