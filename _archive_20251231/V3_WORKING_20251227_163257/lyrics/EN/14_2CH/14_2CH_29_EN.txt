[TITLE]
Return to the Lord

[LYRICS]

VERSE 1  
In the days of Hezekiah, a king so young,  
He sought the heart of God, the song that must be sung.  
With open doors of worship, he called the Levites near,  
"Sanctify the temple, let our hearts be clear!"

CHORUS  
Return to the Lord, with praises we will bring,  
With burnt offerings and joy, our voices rise and sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather as one,  
With hearts laid bare before Him, our worship has begun.

VERSE 2  
The altar was restored, the incense filled the air,  
The blood of sacrifice poured out, a sign of love and care.  
The Levites played their cymbals, the trumpets rang out loud,  
As the people bowed in worship, a holy, joyful crowd.

CHORUS  
Return to the Lord, with praises we will bring,  
With burnt offerings and joy, our voices rise and sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather as one,  
With hearts laid bare before Him, our worship has begun.

BRIDGE  
Oh, let our hearts be turned, like the days of old,  
To seek the face of mercy, to honor and behold.  
With every sacrifice, we lay our lives anew,  
For God has made a way, His promises are true.

CHORUS  
Return to the Lord, with praises we will bring,  
With burnt offerings and joy, our voices rise and sing.  
In the house of the Lord, we gather as one,  
With hearts laid bare before Him, our worship has begun.

OUTRO  
Hezekiah rejoiced, the people filled with grace,  
In the presence of the Lord, we find our resting place.  
So let us come together, with hearts forever true,  
In the house of the Lord, we will worship anew.
