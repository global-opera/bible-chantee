[TITLE]
In Your Dwelling Place

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the thick of darkness, You reside,  
Yet in my heart, I’ve built a place to hide.  
A house of praise, a holy ground,  
Where Your glory, Lord, is always found.  
You chose Jerusalem, a city so bright,  
Your name upon it, a beacon of light.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, hear our cry, we lift our hands high,  
In this dwelling place, we seek Your face.  
You keep Your promises, faithful and true,  
In Your dwelling place, we worship You.

[Verse 2]  
From Egypt’s bondage, You set us free,  
In every promise, Your hand we see.  
With David's heart, we long to build,  
A sanctuary where hope is fulfilled.  
When we call on You, hear our humble plea,  
Forgive our sins, restore our unity.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, hear our cry, we lift our hands high,  
In this dwelling place, we seek Your face.  
You keep Your promises, faithful and true,  
In Your dwelling place, we worship You.

[Bridge]  
When the heavens shut, and the rain won’t fall,  
In our despair, Lord, we heed Your call.  
With every prayer, we turn back to You,  
Restore our hearts, make us new.  
To the stranger, to the lost, bring them near,  
In this house of prayer, let them know You’re here.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, hear our cry, we lift our hands high,  
In this dwelling place, we seek Your face.  
You keep Your promises, faithful and true,  
In Your dwelling place, we worship You.

[Outro]  
Now arise, O Lord, dwell among us still,  
Let Your mercy flow, and Your purpose fulfill.  
As we stand united, in spirit and grace,  
We find our refuge, in Your dwelling place.  
In Your dwelling place, we worship You.  
In Your dwelling place, we worship You.
