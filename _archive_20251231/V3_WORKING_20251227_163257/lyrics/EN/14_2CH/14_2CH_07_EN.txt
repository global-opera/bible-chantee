[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the silence of the night, Solomon prayed,  
With a heart full of faith, he sought Your face.  
Then fire fell from heaven, consuming all,  
The glory of the Lord, in this sacred place.  

[Chorus]  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
We bow before You, our hearts secure.  
In the house of the Lord, we lift our praise,  
Forever we worship, our voices raise.  

[Verse 2]  
All of Israel saw the fire descend,  
With faces to the ground, they called Your name.  
In unity we offered, our hearts laid bare,  
Twenty-two thousand oxen, all in Your fame.  

[Chorus]  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
We bow before You, our hearts secure.  
In the house of the Lord, we lift our praise,  
Forever we worship, our voices raise.  

[Bridge]  
If my people, humble and true,  
Turn from their ways, I will hear you.  
I have chosen this place, my name will abide,  
With open eyes and heart, I’ll be your guide.  

[Verse 3]  
As Solomon stood, with faith in his heart,  
You promised Your presence, never to part.  
Oh, guide us, Lord, as we seek Your light,  
In the battles we face, be our strength and might.  

[Chorus]  
For He is good, His mercy endures,  
We bow before You, our hearts secure.  
In the house of the Lord, we lift our praise,  
Forever we worship, our voices raise.  

[Outro]  
So let our lives be a sacrifice,  
In the glory of the Lord, we find our life.  
Together we stand, in awe of Your grace,  
Forever and always, we seek Your face.  

[End]
