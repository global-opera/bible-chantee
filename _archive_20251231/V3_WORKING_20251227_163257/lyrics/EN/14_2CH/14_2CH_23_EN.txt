[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the silence of the shadows, where the darkness tried to reign,  
Jehoiada stood with courage, calling forth a holy name.  
Gathered were the faithful, Levites strong and true,  
In the house of God they made a vow, their hearts anew.

Chorus  
God save the King! Let praises rise,  
With trumpets and with voices, we lift our cries.  
For in the courts of heaven, our joy will not remain,  
In unity we worship, God save the King!

Verse 2  
A crown upon His head, the promise had come true,  
The son of David rising, with grace and strength anew.  
The people stood rejoicing, their hearts in one accord,  
With every instrument of praise, they magnify the Lord.

Chorus  
God save the King! Let praises rise,  
With trumpets and with voices, we lift our cries.  
For in the courts of heaven, our joy will not remain,  
In unity we worship, God save the King!

Bridge  
Break down the altars, let the idols fall,  
In the name of Jesus, we will stand for all.  
With every weapon holy, we stand in faith and fight,  
For the kingdom of our Savior, we walk in His light.

Chorus  
God save the King! Let praises rise,  
With trumpets and with voices, we lift our cries.  
For in the courts of heaven, our joy will not remain,

Outro  
So let the people gather, let their hearts be one,  
In the house of the Lord, our victory's begun.  
With every heart surrendered, we proclaim His name,  
Forever we will worship, God save the King!
