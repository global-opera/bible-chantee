[TITLE]
The Battle Belongs to the Lord

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the face of the storm, we gather in prayer,  
A multitude rising, we cast all our cares.  
O Lord God of our fathers, we seek Your embrace,  
With trembling hearts, we stand in this place.  

[Chorus]  
For the battle belongs to the Lord,  
With faith we will rise, in His strength we stand sure.  
Though the enemy comes, we will not be afraid,  
With our eyes fixed on You, our fears are laid.  

[Verse 2]  
From the cliffs of Ziz, they approach with intent,  
Yet You are our refuge, our hearts You have lent.  
“Do not fear, do not doubt,” Your promise rings true,  
For the fight is not ours, it belongs unto You.  

[Chorus]  
For the battle belongs to the Lord,  
With faith we will rise, in His strength we stand sure.  
Though the enemy comes, we will not be afraid,  
With our eyes fixed on You, our fears are laid.  

[Bridge]  
We lift up our voices, in worship we sing,  
Praise to our Savior, our Lord and our King.  
He has fought for us, He has won the day,  
In the valley of blessings, we gather and say,  

[Chorus]  
For the battle belongs to the Lord,  
With faith we will rise, in His strength we stand sure.  
Though the enemy comes, we will not be afraid,  
With our eyes fixed on You, our fears are laid.  

[Outro]  
So we walk in Your victory, our hearts filled with joy,  
For with You, O Lord, we know we’ll never be destroyed.  
In the valley of Berachah, our praises ascend,  
In the presence of the Lord, our battles will end.
