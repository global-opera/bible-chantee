Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, a king arose,  
Jotham, brave and faithful, in the path he chose.  
With a heart set on the Father, he walked in His light,  
Building walls and cities, standing strong in the fight.  

Chorus  
Mighty in the Lord, he prepared his ways,  
Fighting for the kingdom, through the darkest days.  
With each step he took, he called on Heaven's grace,  
Jotham, faithful servant, in the Lord's embrace.  

Verse 2  
Though the people wandered, their hearts turned away,  
He held fast to the promise, did not sway from the way.  
In the battles with the Ammonites, he raised his sword,  
Victory was his anthem, he trusted in the Lord.  

Chorus  
Mighty in the Lord, he prepared his ways,  
Fighting for the kingdom, through the darkest days.  
With each step he took, he called on Heaven's grace,  
Jotham, faithful servant, in the Lord's embrace.  

Bridge  
Oh, let us rise like Jotham, with courage in our hearts,  
Building up His kingdom, playing our part.  
In the face of trials, let us not despair,  
For our God is with us, always there, always there.  

Chorus  
Mighty in the Lord, he prepared his ways,  
Fighting for the kingdom, through the darkest days.  
With each step we take, we’ll call on Heaven's grace,  
Together as His children, in the Lord's embrace.  

Outro  
So we’ll stand together, in unity and praise,  
Walking in His promise, for all of our days.  
Mighty in the Lord, we’ll prepare our ways,  
Faithful in our calling, lifting up His name.
