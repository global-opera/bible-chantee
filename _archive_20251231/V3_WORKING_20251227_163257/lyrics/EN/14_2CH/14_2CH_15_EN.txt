[TITLE]
Seek the Lord, He Will Be Found

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of our doubts,  
When our hearts are torn apart,  
The Spirit calls us to arise,  
To seek His face with all our hearts.  
Hear the voice of truth today,  
Asa’s courage we embrace,  
For the Lord is ever near,  
When we seek Him, He is here.

Chorus:  
Seek the Lord and He will be found,  
In our trials, let His grace abound.  
With our voices lifted high,  
We will call upon His name,  
In His presence, we are changed,  
Seek the Lord, He will remain.

Verse 2:  
In the days of empty ways,  
Without the law, we’ve lost our way,  
But when we turn and lift our eyes,  
His mercy meets us in our cries.  
No more idols, we will stand,  
With our hearts in His command,  
Gathered here, we choose to vow,  
To seek the Lord, we humbly bow.

Chorus:  
Seek the Lord and He will be found,  
In our trials, let His grace abound.  
With our voices lifted high,  
We will call upon His name,  
In His presence, we are changed,  
Seek the Lord, He will remain.

Bridge:  
With a loud voice, we will proclaim,  
In unity, we lift His name.  
For in our hearts, a covenant made,  
To seek Him with all love displayed.  
Let our hands be strong,  
For we know where we belong,  
In the peace of God, we stand,  
United by His mighty hand.

Chorus:  
Seek the Lord and He will be found,  
In our trials, let His grace abound.  
With our voices lifted high,  
We will call upon His name,

Outro:  
So let the nations hear our song,  
In the Lord, we will be strong.  
With our hearts, we seek His face,  
In His love, we find our place.  
Seek the Lord and He will be found,  
In His grace, our hope is crowned.  
Seek the Lord, forever true,  
In His presence, we are new.
