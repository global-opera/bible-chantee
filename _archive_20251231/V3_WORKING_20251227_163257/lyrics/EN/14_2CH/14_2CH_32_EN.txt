[TITLE]
More With Us

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the walls,  
Fear whispers in the night,  
Sennacherib stands tall,  
But our hearts hold to the Light.  
Hezekiah calls the brave,  
Gathered in the gate,  
“Fear not the arm of flesh,  
Our God shall guide our fate.”

Chorus:  
For there are more with us,  
Than those who stand against,  
In the battle, in the storm,  
We find our confidence.  
With You, O Lord, we rise,  
In faith, we lift our cries,  
You fight our battles true,  
Our hope forever You.

Verse 2:  
They taunt with words of doubt,  
Mocking every prayer,  
But we stand on holy ground,  
Our trust is in Your care.  
With every stone and shield,  
We prepare for the fight,  
For You are our defense,  
Our everlasting Light.

Bridge:  
An angel sent from heaven,  
With power to defend,  
He cut off every foe,  
Your promise shall not end.  
We humble ourselves, O Lord,  
In gratitude we stand,  
Your grace has brought us victory,  
In the palm of Your hand.

Outro:  
So let our hearts be bold,  
With courage, let us sing,  
For in You, we are whole,  
Our Savior, our King.  
In the face of every trial,  
Our faith shall never cease,  
With You, we stand united,  
In Your love, we find peace.
