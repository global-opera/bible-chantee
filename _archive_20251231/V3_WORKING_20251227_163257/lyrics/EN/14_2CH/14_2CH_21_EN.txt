[TITLE]
The Light of David

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of Jehoshaphat, a king so wise,  
He walked in Your ways, Lord, under heaven’s skies.  
Gifts of silver and gold, a heritage bestowed,  
But the crown passed to Jehoram, a dark path he chose.  

Chorus:  
Oh, remember the covenant, the promise You made,  
To keep a light shining where shadows invade.  
Though kings may falter and hearts may stray,  
Your love endures forever, in darkness, You stay.  

Verse 2:  
With sword in hand, he turned from the true,  
Slew his own brethren, a heart cold as stone.  
He followed the kings who brought ruin and shame,  
Forsaking the God of his fathers, he walked in vain.  

Chorus:  
Oh, remember the covenant, the promise You made,  
To keep a light shining where shadows invade.  
Though kings may falter and hearts may stray,  
Your love endures forever, in darkness, You stay.  

Bridge:  
Elijah’s warning, a voice from on high,  
“Turn back to the Lord, or watch your love die.”  
Yet the plague came swiftly, a judgment so clear,  
In the end, he departed, with no one to cheer.  

Verse 3:  
The Edomites rose, rebellion in their hearts,  
The treasures of Judah, torn apart.  
But Your promise remains, through the trials and pain,  
For the house of David, Your grace will sustain.  

Chorus:  
Oh, remember the covenant, the promise You made,  
To keep a light shining where shadows invade.  
Though kings may falter and hearts may stray,  
Your love endures forever, in darkness, You stay.  

Outro:  
So we lift our voices, in praise to Your name,  
For the light of David, forever the same.  
Through the trials of life and the battles we face,  
You’re our hope and our strength, our eternal embrace.
