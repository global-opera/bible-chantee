[TITLE]
A Choice of Hearts

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In Shechem gathered all the land,  
A new king sought to take his stand.  
Jeroboam from Egypt returned,  
With heavy hearts, the people yearned.  
"O Rehoboam, hear our plea,  
Ease the yoke, let our souls be free."  

Chorus  
Oh, the weight of the crown can blind the wise,  
In the call of the people, the truth shall rise.  
Will you lead with love or with a heavy hand?  
A choice of hearts, a kingdom to stand.  

Verse 2  
He sought the counsel of the old,  
Words of wisdom, pure and bold.  
"Be kind to those who serve your name,  
Speak gentle truths, and you'll gain their fame."  
But the voice of youth rang loud and clear,  
"Make it heavier, let them fear!"  

Chorus  
Oh, the weight of the crown can blind the wise,  
In the call of the people, the truth shall rise.  
Will you lead with love or with a heavy hand?  
A choice of hearts, a kingdom to stand.  

Bridge  
But the heart of the king turned away,  
In stubborn pride, he chose to sway.  
The people cried, "What have we gained?  
No portion left, in David’s name."  
So they turned to their tents and left the throne,  
In rebellion fierce, they stood alone.  

Verse 3  
Yet in the shadows, God’s plan unfolds,  
Through trials and tears, His grace extols.  
Though kings may falter, and hearts may stray,  
The faithful find hope in a brighter day.  
For every yoke that burdens the soul,  
In God’s embrace, we are made whole.  

Chorus  
Oh, the weight of the crown can blind the wise,  
In the call of the people, the truth shall rise.  
Will you lead with love or with a heavy hand?  
A choice of hearts, a kingdom to stand.  

Outro  
So we lift our voices, and we seek Your face,  
In every trial, we find Your grace.  
Guide our hearts, Lord, in all we do,  
In the choice of love, we will follow You.
