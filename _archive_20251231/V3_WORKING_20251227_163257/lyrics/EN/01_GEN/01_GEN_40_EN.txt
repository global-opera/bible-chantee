[TITLE]
Dreams in the Darkness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadowed halls of sorrow,  
Two men bound by fate’s cruel hand,  
A butler and a baker,  
In the prison, where dreams stand.  
They shared their hearts in silence,  
Where hope seemed far away,  
But Joseph came to offer light,  
A glimpse of breaking day.

Chorus  
Oh, the dreams in the darkness,  
They’re whispered by the night,  
For in every trial, God is near,  
He turns our pain to light.  
Lift your eyes, for He is with us,  
In the depths, He hears our cries,  
From the prison to the promise,  
He’s the One who never lies.

Verse 2  
"Tell me of your visions,  
For interpretations belong to God,"  
Joseph spoke with faith unshaken,  
In the silence, His heart was awed.  
The butler saw the blossoms,  
And the grapes that filled his hand,  
Three days of hope and freedom,  
A new life from the sand.

Bridge  
But as the sun rose brightly,  
The butler forgot his friend,  
Yet God’s plan was still unfolding,  
His purpose does not end.  
In the waiting, there's a promise,  
In the silence, there’s a song,  
Though we may not see the answer,  
In His love, we still belong.

Outro  
So we’ll trust in every moment,  
Holding close His gentle hand,  
In the dreams, He gives us courage,  
For His ways we may not understand.  
From the prison to the promise,  
He will lead us through the night,  
With every dream that’s whispered,  
He will turn our dark to light.
