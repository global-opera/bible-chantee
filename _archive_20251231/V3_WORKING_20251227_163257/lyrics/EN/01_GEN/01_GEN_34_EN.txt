### Title: "The Heart of a Father"

Verse 1  
In the land of promise, a daughter went to see,  
Amidst the daughters of the land, her heart was wild and free.  
But darkness came upon her, a shadow crossed her way,  
In the heart of Shechem, love turned to dismay.  

Chorus  
Oh, the cries of a father, the pain of his heart,  
In the silence of night, when the world fell apart.  
What is right, what is just, when the innocent bleed?  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, for in You we believe.  

Verse 2  
Hamor spoke of peace, of union and of ties,  
But the anger of the brothers raged like fire in the skies.  
"For our sister's honor, we cannot turn away,  
If you seek to be one, this price you must pay."

Bridge  
In the midst of the storm, when the darkness surrounds,  
We remember Your promise, our hope still abounds.  
For the heart of a father, in the journey we take,  
In the trials and the battles, Your love will awake.

Outro  
So we gather our voices, in a bond of His grace,  
For the love of our sister, we will stand in this place.  
Oh, Lord, guide our hearts, let justice be our song,  
In the heart of the Father, together we belong.
