[TITLE]
Dreamer in the Pit

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the land of Canaan, where the shadows fall,  
Jacob loved his favored son, Joseph, above them all.  
With a coat of many colors, shining bright and bold,  
But envy brewed among his kin, a story to be told.

(Chorus)  
Oh, Dreamer, in the pit, where hope seems lost and grim,  
Though your brothers cast you out, your light will never dim.  
For every wound and tear you bear, God’s purpose will remain,  
In the silence and the sorrow, He will turn your pain.

(Verse 2)  
Joseph shared his visions, of sheaves that bowed in grace,  
But hatred grew among his brothers, they could not see his face.  
"Come now, let us slay him," they plotted in the night,  
But Reuben spoke for mercy, to save him from their spite.

(Chorus)  
Oh, Dreamer, in the pit, where hope seems lost and grim,  
Though your brothers cast you out, your light will never dim.  
For every wound and tear you bear, God’s purpose will remain,  
In the silence and the sorrow, He will turn your pain.

(Bridge)  
In the darkness, you will rise, from the depths of despair,  
The dreams that God has given you, they’re alive beyond compare.  
So hold on to the promise, though the road is long and steep,  
For the Father sees your journey, in His love, you’ll find your peace.

(Chorus)  
Oh, Dreamer, in the pit, where hope seems lost and grim,  
Though your brothers cast you out, your light will never dim.  
For every wound and tear you bear, God’s purpose will remain,  
In the silence and the sorrow, He will turn your pain.

(Outro)  
So lift your eyes, oh Dreamer, for the dawn will break anew,  
In the tapestry of heaven, God is weaving hope in you.  
From the pit to the palace, He will guide you through the night,  
Oh, Dreamer, keep on dreaming, for your future’s filled with light.
