[TITLE]
The Well of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the twilight, by the well,  
Abraham's servant, with a tale to tell,  
He prayed for guidance, for a sign from above,  
To find a bride for Isaac, in the grace of love.  

Chorus  
O Lord, lead me on this journey,  
In Your mercy, let Your will be done.  
From the waters, let her answer,  
A heart of kindness, Your chosen one.  

Verse 2  
He stood in faith, as the women drew near,  
With a humble heart, calling out in prayer,  
"Let her give me water, and my camels too,  
Let this be the sign, that I seek from You."  

Chorus  
O Lord, lead me on this journey,  
In Your mercy, let Your will be done.  
From the waters, let her answer,  
A heart of kindness, Your chosen one.  

Bridge  
And as he spoke, behold, she came,  
Rebekah, fair and blessed with grace,  
She filled his pitcher, and the camels drank,  
In awe he bowed down, and gave thanks.  

Verse 3  
"Who are you, dear maiden? Whose daughter can you be?"  
"I am of Bethuel, from Nahor’s family."  
He worshiped the Lord for the path that was shown,  
In the way of the faithful, the seeds had been sown.  

Chorus  
O Lord, lead me on this journey,  
In Your mercy, let Your will be done.  
From the waters, let her answer,  
A heart of kindness, Your chosen one.  

Outro  
And they blessed her, sent her on her way,  
To be a mother of nations, in Your light she'll stay.  
With Isaac, she found love, in the promise divine,  
In the heart of their story, Your faithfulness shines.
