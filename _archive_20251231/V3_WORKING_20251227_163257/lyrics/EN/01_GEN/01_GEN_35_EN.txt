[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of the night,  
You called my name, O Lord,  
"Arise, go to Bethel,  
Where I’ll meet you once more."  
With a heart full of longing,  
I gather all I hold,  
To leave behind the strange gods,  
And walk in faith so bold.

Chorus  
We will rise and go,  
To the place where You dwell,  
Build an altar to Your glory,  
In the presence of Emmanuel.  
In our hearts, we lay down,  
Every burden, every fear,  
At Bethel, You will meet us,  
O God, draw near, draw near.

Verse 2  
Through the trials and the tears,  
You were with me all the way,  
In my darkest hour,  
You turned the night to day.  
As I lay my stones of offering,  
Pour out my praise anew,  
In this sacred place, my Savior,  
I find my hope in You.

Bridge  
You called me Israel,  
A promise to fulfill,  
From the dust You lift me,  
With Your love, I am still.  
Be fruitful, multiply,  
A nation from my seed,  
In the land that You have given,  
In Your grace, I am freed.

Outro  
So we journey on together,  
With our eyes set on the prize,  
In the shadow of Your promise,  
We will trust, we will rise.  
At Bethel, You will meet us,  
Your presence, our delight,  
In this holy place of worship,  
We find our peace tonight.
