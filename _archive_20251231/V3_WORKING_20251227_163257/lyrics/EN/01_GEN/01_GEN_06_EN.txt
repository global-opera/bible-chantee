[TITLE]
Grace in the Ark

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, when the earth was fair,  
Men multiplied, yet darkness filled the air.  
Sons of God saw beauty in mortal eyes,  
But hearts turned to evil, beneath the skies.  
The Spirit grieved, for wickedness had grown,  
Imaginations led to paths unknown.  

Chorus:  
But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord,  
A just man walking, faithful to His word.  
In a world of chaos, he stood firm and strong,  
Building an ark, where the righteous belong.  

Verse 2:  
The earth was filled with violence, hearts turned to sin,  
Yet God called to Noah, “A new world to begin.”  
“Make for yourself an ark, of gopher wood so pure,  
In this storm of judgment, my promise will endure.”  
Two of every kind, they shall come to thee,  
To keep them safe within, for I will set them free.  

Chorus:  
For Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord,  
A just man walking, faithful to His word.  
In a world of chaos, he stood firm and strong,  
Building an ark, where the righteous belong.  

Bridge:  
Oh Lord, we seek Your mercy, in the shadows of the night,  
Guide us through the waters, lead us to the light.  
With every heartbeat, may we trust in Your plan,  
For Your covenant is faithful, through the ages, You will stand.  

Chorus:  
For Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord,  
A just man walking, faithful to His word.  
In a world of chaos, he stood firm and strong,  
Building an ark, where the righteous belong.  

Outro:  
So let us remember, through trials we may face,  
There is always hope, for we dwell in His grace.  
With hearts united, we will sing His praise,  
For in the ark of mercy, our lives will be raised.
