[TITLE]
In the Shadow of His Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Egypt, where the sun beats down,  
A dreamer sold to shadows, a lost and lonely crown.  
Yet the Lord was with Joseph, His hand upon his life,  
In the house of Potiphar, he rose above the strife.  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stand,  
When the trials surround me, I’ll trust His plan.  
Though the storms may rage, and the night seems long,  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stay strong.  

Verse 2:  
With favor from his master, all was placed in his care,  
But temptation whispered softly, a voice beyond compare.  
“Lie with me,” she beckoned, yet he turned away in fear,  
For to sin against his God was a path he wouldn’t near.  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stand,  
When the trials surround me, I’ll trust His plan.  
Though the storms may rage, and the night seems long,  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stay strong.  

Bridge:  
Though the prison gates may close, and the world may turn away,  
The Lord was with Joseph, a light within the gray.  
In every chain and sorrow, His mercy would unfold,  
For He turns our darkest moments into stories of gold.  

Verse 3:  
He faced the lies and trials, cast down but not alone,  
In the depths of every struggle, His faithfulness was shown.  
With grace that never falters, and love that never ends,  
In the heart of every valley, our Savior still defends.  

Chorus:  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stand,  
When the trials surround me, I’ll trust His plan.  
Though the storms may rage, and the night seems long,  
In the shadow of His grace, I will stay strong.  

Outro:  
So let the world remember, in our weakness He’ll arise,  
With Joseph’s heart as witness, we’ll find strength in His skies.  
In the shadow of His grace, we will rise and sing,  
For in every darkened moment, His light is everything.
