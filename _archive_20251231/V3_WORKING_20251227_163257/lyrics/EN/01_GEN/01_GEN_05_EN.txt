(Verse 1)  
In the beginning, You breathed life,  
In Your image, we were formed,  
Male and female, blessed in Your light,  
Called us Adam, in Your love adorned.  
From the dust, You shaped our frame,  
With a promise, our hearts aflame.  
Through the ages, Your hand has traced,  
The generations of Your grace.

(Chorus)  
Oh, we walk in the light of Your story,  
From Adam to Noah, we see Your glory.  
Each life a testament, a legacy,  
In every heartbeat, Your love sets us free.  
You are the God of the living,  
In every age, our hope You’re giving.  
Through trials and triumphs, we find our place,  
In the generations of Your grace.

In every daughter, Your promise holds.  
Though years may pass and life may wane,  
Your covenant stands, forever the same.
