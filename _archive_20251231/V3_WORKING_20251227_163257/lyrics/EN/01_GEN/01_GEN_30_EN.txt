[TITLE]
In the Midst of Waiting

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In shadows deep, a longing heart,  
Rachel cried, "Give me a part,  
Of life, of hope, give me a son,  
Or I shall fade, my race undone."  
But Jacob turned, with furrowed brow,  
"Am I in place of God somehow?"  
Yet in despair, she sought a way,  
To hold the promise, come what may.  

Chorus  
In the midst of waiting, Lord, I trust,  
You hear my cries, in You I must.  
Through every trial, my heart will sing,  
For You are my hope, my everything.  

Verse 2  
Bilhah bore a child, the first of grace,  
Dan was named, a sign of faith's embrace.  
Then Naphtali came with wrestlings fierce,  
A sister's strife, the heart it pierce.  
Leah, too, with hunger for love,  
Bore Gad and Asher, blessings above.  
Yet in the struggle, God's hand was near,  
He saw their hearts, He held their tears.  

Chorus  
In the midst of waiting, Lord, I trust,  
You hear my cries, in You I must.  
Through every trial, my heart will sing,  
For You are my hope, my everything.  

Bridge  
God remembered Rachel, He heard her plea,  
In the silence, He was there,  
He opened her womb, the gift set free.  
Joseph came, a promise rare,  
"God has taken away my shame,"  
She named him right, in His great name.  
With each new dawn, our hearts will rise,  
In every moment, our God supplies.  

Chorus  
In the midst of waiting, Lord, I trust,  
You hear my cries, in You I must.  
Through every trial, my heart will sing,  
For You are my hope, my everything.  

Outro  
So let us wait with hearts ablaze,  
For in the waiting, You show Your ways.  
With every tear and whispered prayer,  
We find Your love, forever there.  
In the midst of waiting, Lord, I trust,  
You are my hope, my everything, my just.
