[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the morning light, the journey starts anew,  
Men with heavy hearts, their burdens to pursue.  
With sacks filled with blessings, yet shadows loom near,  
For the silver cup of truth, they tremble in fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, wherefore have we done this wrong?  
We’ve seen the grace, we’ve seen the song.  
In our hearts, we long to mend,  
With Your mercy, Lord, our hearts transcend.  

Verse 2  
Joseph’s words like whispers, cutting through the night,  
"Why do you repay my good with darkened spite?"  
In our weakness, Lord, we stand before You,  
In the depths of our sin, Your love shines through.  

Chorus  
Oh, wherefore have we done this wrong?  
We’ve seen the grace, we’ve seen the song.  
In our hearts, we long to mend,  
With Your mercy, Lord, our hearts transcend.  

Bridge  
Judah speaks for all, a plea from the heart,  
"Let me take his place, Lord, I will not part.  
For the life of the lad is bound to our own,  
In our father’s sorrow, we are not alone."  

Verse 3  
As the cup is revealed, the truth comes to light,  
In the shadows of shame, love breaks through the night.  
For in every failure, Your grace does abound,  
Restoration awaits, in Your mercy we’re found.  

Chorus  
Oh, wherefore have we done this wrong?  
We’ve seen the grace, we’ve seen the song.  
In our hearts, we long to mend,  
With Your mercy, Lord, our hearts transcend.  

Outro  
So let us return with our heads held high,  
For in every trial, You hear our cry.  
With the cup of grace, we stand redeemed,  
In Your love, O Lord, forever we dream.
