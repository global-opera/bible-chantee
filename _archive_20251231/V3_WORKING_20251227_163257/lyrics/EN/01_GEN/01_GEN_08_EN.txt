[TITLE]
The Promise of the Ark

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In a world of storm and strife,  
God remembered Noah's life,  
Every creature, great and small,  
He held them in His arms through it all.  
The winds arose, the waters ceased,  
In the heart of chaos, He brought peace.  
From the depths, a whisper came,  
"Trust in Me, call upon My name."  

Chorus  
Oh, the promise of the ark,  
A beacon in the dark,  
Through the trials, we will stand,  
Held secure in His mighty hand.  
With every wave that crashes down,  
He brings us hope, He wears the crown.  
In the silence, He’s our song,  
The God who keeps us, we belong.  

Verse 2  
Forty days, the dove took flight,  
Searching for the signs of light,  
In her beak, an olive leaf,  
A promise born of sweet relief.  
Noah’s heart began to soar,  
As the waters ebbed to shore.  
Every creature, every kin,  
Stepping forth, new life begins.  

Chorus

Bridge  
And as the altar rises high,  
A fragrant offering to the sky,  
God’s heart spoke, a vow anew,  
"I’ll never forsake you, I’ll see you through."  
While the earth remains, His seasons flow,  
In every heart, His love will grow.

Outro  
So we rise, with faith ablaze,  
In His presence, we give praise.  
From the ashes, life restored,  
In His promise, we are assured.  
The promise of the ark, forever true,  
Our hearts, our lives, belong to You.
