Verse 1:  
In the heat of the day, a promise drew near,  
Three men in the shade, their purpose made clear.  
Abraham, he ran with a heart full of grace,  
“Stay with me, my Lord, let me seek Your face.”  

Chorus:  
Is anything too hard for the Lord?  
In the silence of doubt, You speak Your Word.  
From the ashes of hope, new life will arise,  
In the tent of my heart, I hear Your cries.  

Verse 2:  
He called for Sarah, the joyful surprise,  
Though years had gone by, still I hear the skies.  
“Where is your Sarah?” the promise was cast,  
“Within the tent door, your future is vast.”  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
O Lord, I draw near, though I am but dust,  
In Your mighty hand, I place all my trust.  
For the cries of the righteous, You will not ignore,  
In the shadows of Sodom, Your mercy will soar.  

Verse 3:  
Ten hearts in the city, You counted each plea,  
For the sake of the faithful, You’d spare them, You’d see.  
Abraham stood firm, in the face of despair,  
With a heart full of courage, he lifted his prayer.

So I lift my eyes high, where my promise will stay,
