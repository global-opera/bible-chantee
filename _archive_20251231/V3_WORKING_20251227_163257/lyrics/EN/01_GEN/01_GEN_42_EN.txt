Verse 1  
In the land where hunger cries,  
Jacob looked with weary eyes,  
“Why do you stand and gaze in fear?  
There’s corn in Egypt, hope is near.”  
Ten brothers set their hearts to roam,  
To buy the bread that leads them home.  

Chorus  
O Lord, in seasons dark and wide,  
Your grace abounds, our hearts abide.  
In the promise of Your name,  
We rise from ashes, free from shame.  
Though trials come, we’ll trust and stand,  
You’re with us in this weary land.  

Verse 2  
Joseph knew the pain they bore,  
Their hearts were troubled, guilt they wore.  
In the shadows, he concealed his face,  
Yet love remained, a hidden grace.  
“Bring forth your brother, prove your claim,  
Then I will know you’re not the same.”

Verse 3  
As sacks were opened, hearts were stirred,  
“What is this that we have heard?”  
Fear gripped their souls, but still they cried,  
“God’s hand is here, He will provide.”  
But Jacob mourned, his heart was torn,  
A father's love, in sorrow’s dawn.
