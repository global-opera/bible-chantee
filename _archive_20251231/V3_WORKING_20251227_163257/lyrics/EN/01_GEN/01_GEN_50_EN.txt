[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the land, where we mourn,  
Joseph wept for his father, the love reborn.  
With a heart full of sorrow, he sought to fulfill,  
The promise of the grave where his father lay still.  

Chorus  
O, carry me home to the land of my rest,  
Where the promise of God is forever blessed.  
Through the valley of shadows, Your light will guide,  
In the land of promise, where hope will abide.  

Verse 2  
With chariots and horsemen, a great company,  
They traveled through grief, a sight to see.  
Canaan's fields were calling, a legacy true,  
In the cave of Machpelah, their bond ever new.  

Chorus  
O, carry me home to the land of my rest,  
Where the promise of God is forever blessed.  
Through the valley of shadows, Your light will guide,  
In the land of promise, where hope will abide.  

Bridge  
Forgive and restore, as Joseph did call,  
For what man intends for evil, God uses for all.  
Fear not, my brethren, for love has the key,  
To nourish our hearts, in unity we’ll be.  

Verse 3  
As time gently whispers, the journey goes on,  
God will visit His people, a new day will dawn.  
With faith in our hearts, we’ll rise from the dust,  
For His covenant promise, in Him we will trust.  

Chorus  
O, carry me home to the land of my rest,  
Where the promise of God is forever blessed.  
Through the valley of shadows, Your light will guide,  
In the land of promise, where hope will abide.  

Outro  
So let us remember, the love that remains,  
In the heart of the Father, our hope never wanes.  
In the land of promise, together we stand,  
With faith as our anchor, held firm in His hand.
