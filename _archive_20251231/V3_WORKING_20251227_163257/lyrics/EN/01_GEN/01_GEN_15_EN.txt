[TITLE]
Promise of the Stars

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the night, Your word came to me,  
"Fear not, Abram, I am your shield,  
Your reward, exceeding, beyond all we see,  
Trust in My promise, let your heart be healed.  
Childless, I ponder, where is my heir?  
But You spoke of the stars, a count I can't bear.  

Chorus  
So shall your seed be, as the stars in the sky,  
A covenant unbroken, Your faithfulness will never die.  
From the river of Egypt to the great Euphrates,  
In the heart of the darkness, Your light always radiates.  

Verse 2  
You brought me out, from Ur's distant land,  
To give me this promise, a future so grand.  
Yet in the shadows, I feel the weight of fear,  
But You, O Lord, assure me, Your voice I will hear.  
In the deep of the night, You laid forth the plan,  
A fire and a furnace, Your glory I span.  

Chorus  
So shall your seed be, as the stars in the sky,  
A covenant unbroken, Your faithfulness will never die.  
From the river of Egypt to the great Euphrates,  
In the heart of the darkness, Your light always radiates.  

Bridge  
In the years of waiting, I will trust and believe,  
For every promise spoken, in Your love I receive.  
Through trials and through darkness, I will stand in Your grace,  
For the iniquity is measured, yet Your mercy we embrace.  

Chorus  
So shall your seed be, as the stars in the sky,  
A covenant unbroken, Your faithfulness will never die.  
From the river of Egypt to the great Euphrates,

Outro  
O Lord, I will follow, through every unknown way,  
In the promise of tomorrow, I will choose to stay.  
In the stars, I see Your love, in the night, I find my song,  
With You as my promise, my heart will carry on.
