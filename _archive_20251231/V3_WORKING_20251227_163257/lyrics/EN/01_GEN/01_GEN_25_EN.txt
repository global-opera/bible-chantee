### [TITLE]
Generations of Promise

### [Verse 1]
In the land where the covenant was made,  
Abraham stood, a faithful heart displayed.  
With Keturah by his side, he walked in grace,  
From his love, new nations would find their place.  

### [Chorus]  
Generations of promise, we sing Your praise,  
From Isaac to Jacob, through all our days.  
Your hand upon us, guiding our way,  
In the story of love, forever we’ll stay.  

### [Verse 2]  
Isaac prayed for Rebekah, barren and worn,  
The Lord heard his cry, new life would be born.  
Twins in her womb, a struggle divine,  
Two nations destined, in Your plan they align.  

### [Chorus]  
Generations of promise, we sing Your praise,  
From Isaac to Jacob, through all our days.

### [Bridge]  
Oh, the birthright traded, a lesson to learn,  
In the silence, Your wisdom, we yearn.  
From the dust of the earth, to the stars up above,  
You weave our stories, in mercy and love.  

### [Verse 3]  
Abraham’s legacy, in the cave where he lay,  
With Isaac and Ishmael, they honored his way.  
Through valleys and mountains, Your promise remains,  
In the hearts of the faithful, Your love still sustains.  

### [Chorus]

### [Outro]  
Generations of promise, Your faithfulness shines,  
In every heartbeat, Your love intertwines.  
With hearts wide open, we’ll walk in Your light,  
Generations of promise, our hope shining bright.
