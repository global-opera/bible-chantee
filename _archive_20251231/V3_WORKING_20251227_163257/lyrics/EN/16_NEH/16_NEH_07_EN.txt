[TITLE]
Rebuild Our Walls

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the city, where the ruins lay,  
Nehemiah saw the heartache, heard the people pray.  
With a faithful heart he gathered, nobles by his side,  
Together they would rise up, with the Lord as their guide.  

Chorus  
Rebuild our walls, O Lord, let Your strength arise,  
In the heart of every soul, let our spirits rise.  
From the ashes we will gather, by Your grace we stand,  
With our voices raised in worship, united hand in hand.  

Verse 2  
For the gates were closed in sorrow, but the sun would shine,  
As the watchmen took their places, guarding what is Thine.  
With the Levites and the singers, lifting praises high,  
In the midst of every struggle, You will never lie.  

Chorus  
Rebuild our walls, O Lord, let Your strength arise,  
In the heart of every soul, let our spirits rise.  
From the ashes we will gather, by Your grace we stand,  
With our voices raised in worship, united hand in hand.  

Bridge  
We remember all Your promises, written in our hearts,  
From the captives You’ve delivered, a brand new start.  
As we walk in faith together, every step we take,  
With the light of Your salvation, we will not forsake.  

Chorus  
Rebuild our walls, O Lord, let Your strength arise,  
In the heart of every soul, let our spirits rise.  
From the ashes we will gather, by Your grace we stand,

Outro  
So we’ll sing as one, in joy and in pain,  
With our hearts forever loyal, we will praise Your name.  
Rebuild our walls, O Lord, let Your strength arise,  
In the heart of every soul, let our spirits rise.
