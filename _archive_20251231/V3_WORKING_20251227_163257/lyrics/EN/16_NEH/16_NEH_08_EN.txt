[TITLE]
Joy of the Lord is Our Strength

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
As one we gather, hearts aligned,  
In unity, we seek Your face,  
With open ears and spirits kind,  
Your Law, O Lord, our saving grace.  
Ezra reads, the truth proclaimed,  
From morning light till midday's glow,  
We stand in awe, our hearts aflame,  
In reverence, we bow low.  

Chorus  
Lift up your hands, O people, sing,  
The joy of the Lord is our strength today!  
With every heart, let praises ring,  
In His Word, we find our way.  
From sorrow deep, to joy anew,  
In Him we stand, our hope is true.  

Verse 2  
The Levites guide, our hearts they mend,  
“Do not weep, for this day is holy,”  
With joy and mirth, our prayers ascend,  
In His presence, we walk boldly.  
Go forth, rejoice, share with the meek,  
For in His love, we find our peace,  
With booths we dwell, Your grace we seek,  
In every heart, let joy increase.  

Chorus

Bridge  
From the streets to the mountain high,  
Branches of praise we raise to the sky,  
In every season, Your truth remains,  
In every trial, Your love sustains.  
We'll gather again, our voices rise,  
With hearts united, we lift our eyes.

Outro  
So let us dwell in joy and grace,  
In booths of love, we find our place.  
For the joy of the Lord is our strength,  
Forevermore, we’ll sing His praise!  
Forevermore, we’ll sing His praise!
