[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the sanctuary, I find my peace,  
When the world around me seems to never cease.  
I’ve wandered through shadows, lost in despair,  
But in Your presence, Lord, I’m met with care.  

Chorus  
Oh, draw me nearer, Lord, to You I cling,  
You are my strength, my heart’s true King.  
When my flesh is weak, and my heart may fail,  
In Your loving arms, I will prevail.  

Verse 2  
The wicked prosper, their chains made of pride,  
Yet their fleeting joys will not abide.  
I’ve been tempted, Lord, to envy their gain,  
But in Your truth, I find joy in the pain.  

Chorus  
Oh, draw me nearer, Lord, to You I cling,  
You are my strength, my heart’s true King.  
When my flesh is weak, and my heart may fail,  
In Your loving arms, I will prevail.  

Bridge  
There’s none in heaven or on earth I desire,  
But You, my Savior, my heart’s true fire.  
You hold me close, guide me with Your hand,  
Forever I’ll trust, in Your perfect plan.  

Chorus

Outro  
Goodness and mercy follow me,  
In Your presence, Lord, my soul is free.
