[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
I will extol You, O Lord, my King,  
For You have lifted me high on the wings.  
You turned my sorrow into joy divine,  
In my darkest hour, Your light will shine.  

Chorus  
Sing, O saints, and give Him praise,  
For His mercy is new with each passing day.  
Weeping may linger, but joy will arise,  
In the dawn of Your love, my soul will rise.  

Verse 2  
O Lord, my God, I cried out to Thee,  
You healed my heart, set my spirit free.  
From the shadow of death, You called me near,  
In Your presence, I’ll cast away all fear.  

Chorus  
Sing, O saints, and give Him praise,  
For His mercy is new with each passing day.  
Weeping may linger, but joy will arise,  
In the dawn of Your love, my soul will rise.  

Bridge  
You turned my mourning into dancing,  
You clothed me with gladness, my heart is advancing.  
Forever I’ll sing of Your grace and Your truth,  
With every breath, I will give thanks to You.  

Chorus  
Sing, O saints, and give Him praise,

Outro  
O Lord my God, forever I’ll proclaim,  
In every season, I’ll bless Your name.  
You’ve turned my sorrow into joy, it’s true,  
With all of my heart, I will worship You.
