[TITLE]
The Righteous Hand of God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
From my youth, afflictions came,  
Many times, I’ve borne the shame,  
Yet Your mercy, Lord, held fast,  
In the trials, I am steadfast.  
Plowers came and furrows deep,  
But Your love, my soul will keep.  

Chorus:  
The Lord is righteous, He sets me free,  
Chains are broken, my heart can see.  
Though the enemy surrounds,  
In Your grace, my hope abounds.  
Let them stumble, let them fall,  
In Your name, I stand tall.  

Verse 2:  
Confounded, those who hate Zion,  
Like the grass that fades at dawn.  
They will not claim the harvest here,  
For Your blessing, Lord, is near.  
In the shadows, in the night,  
You are with me, my guiding light.
