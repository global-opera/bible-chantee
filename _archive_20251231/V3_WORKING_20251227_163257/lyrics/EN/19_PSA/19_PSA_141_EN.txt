[TITLE]
My Eyes Are Turned to You

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
LORD, I cry out to You,  
Make haste, hear my plea,  
As incense rises high,  
Let my prayer be,  
With hands lifted up,  
Like the evening's grace,  
In Your presence, I long,  
For Your sweet embrace.

Chorus:  
My eyes are turned to You,  
O God, my trust and light,  
Keep my heart from the snares,  
Guide me through the night.  
In the trials I face,  
Let Your mercy flow,  
With every breath I take,  
To You, I will go.

Verse 2:  
Set a watch on my lips,  
Guard my words, I pray,  
Keep me from the wicked,  
In their deceptive ways.  
Let the righteous rebuke,  
In kindness, let it be,  
For even in their sorrow,  
I’ll find You with me.

Bridge:  
When the judges fall,  
And the wicked stray,  
I will stand in Your truth,  
And trust in Your way.  
Let their nets entangle,  
But I will be free,  
For my heart is anchored,  
In Your love for me.

Outro:  
LORD, I cry out to You,  
In faith, I will stand,  
With my eyes upon You,  
Holding on to Your hand.  
In every moment,  
You’re my refuge, my song,  
In Your love, I find peace,  
With You, I belong.
