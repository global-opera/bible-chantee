[TITLE]
In Your Mercy I Will Trust

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
How long, O Lord, will You hide Your face?  
In sorrow's grip, I seek Your grace.  
Each whisper of doubt, a shadow in my heart,  
Yet still I long for You, my Savior from the start.  

Chorus  
I trust in Your mercy, my soul finds rest,  
In the depths of my struggle, You know what’s best.  
I will sing of Your love, my heart shall proclaim,  
For You have dealt bountifully, I bless Your name.  

Verse 2  
The enemy surrounds, their voices loud and near,  
But in the quiet night, I feel You drawing near.  
Lighten my eyes, O Lord, let hope arise,  
In every fear I face, I lift my gaze to the skies.  

Chorus  
I trust in Your mercy, my soul finds rest,  
In the depths of my struggle, You know what’s best.  
I will sing of Your love, my heart shall proclaim,

Bridge  
Though troubles may come, and shadows may fall,  
Your light breaks the darkness, You hear my call.  
With every breath I take, I’ll lift my voice high,  
For in Your embrace, I will never die.

Outro  
How long, O Lord, I know You are here,  
In every moment, You calm all my fear.  
I will sing of Your goodness, my heart shall rejoice,  
In the mercy You give, I find my voice.
