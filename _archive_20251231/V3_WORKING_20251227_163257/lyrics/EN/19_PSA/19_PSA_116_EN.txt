[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
I love You, Lord, for You have heard my cry,  
In my distress, You drew so nigh.  
The sorrows weighed, my heart was low,  
But in Your name, my hopes did grow.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, I call upon Your grace,  
In every trial, I seek Your face.  
You’ve delivered me, my soul restored,  
I’ll walk with You, my faithful Lord.  

Verse 2:  
You heard my voice, You set me free,  
From depths of sorrow, Your mercy’s key.  
With grateful heart, I lift my song,  
In the land of the living, where I belong.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, I call upon Your grace,  
In every trial, I seek Your face.  
You’ve delivered me, my soul restored,  
I’ll walk with You, my faithful Lord.  

Bridge:  
What can I give for all You've done?  
I take the cup of salvation,  
In the presence of Your people, Lord,  
I lift my vows, my heart’s devotion.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, I call upon Your grace,  
In every trial, I seek Your face.  
You’ve delivered me, my soul restored,  
I’ll walk with You, my faithful Lord.  

Outro:  
In the courts of Your house, I’ll praise Your name,  
For in Your love, I’ll never be the same.  
With thankful heart, I’ll sing forevermore,  
In Your presence, Lord, my soul will soar.
