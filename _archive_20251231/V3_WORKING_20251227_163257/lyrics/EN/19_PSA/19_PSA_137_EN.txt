[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
By the rivers where we wept,  
In a land that feels so far,  
We hung our harps on willows,  
Underneath the evening star.  
Captors called for joyful songs,  
But our hearts were filled with pain;  
How can we sing our Savior’s praise,  
In a land of grief and shame?

Chorus:  
Oh, if I forget, Jerusalem,  
Let my hands lose their way.  
If I do not remember, Lord,  
Let my tongue in silence stay.  
You are my joy, my heart's delight,  
In every shadow, You are light.  
I’ll sing your song, O Lord, my King,  
In this strange land, I will cling.

Verse 2:  
Remember, Lord, the cries of old,  
The pain of those who fell.  
The heartache of our broken past,  
In Babylon’s cruel spell.  
Yet in the midst of sorrow,  
Your promise still remains,  
A hope that stirs within my heart,  
Through all my tears and pain.

Bridge:  
Even when the night is long,  
I will lift my voice in song.  
For You’re the reason I can stand,  
My heart will always trust your plan.  
In every trial, You are near,  
O Lord, I hold You dear.  
With every breath, I will proclaim,  
Your love, my hope, my strength, my name.
