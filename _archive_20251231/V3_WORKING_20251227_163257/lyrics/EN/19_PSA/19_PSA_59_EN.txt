[TITLE]
My Refuge, My Strength

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of the night,  
Enemies surround my soul,  
But I lift my heart to You,  
O God, make my spirit whole.  
They lie in wait, their whispers loud,  
Yet I trust in Your embrace,  
Awake, O Lord, hear my cry,  
Defend me by Your grace.

Chorus:  
I will sing of Your power,  
I will sing of Your mercy,  
In the morning light, I will rise,  
For You are my defense, my shield,  
In You alone, my heart finds peace,  
O God, my refuge, my strength.

Verse 2:  
Though they gather at the dawn,  
Like dogs they roam the streets,  
With swords upon their tongues,  
Their pride and rage they repeat.  
But You, O Lord, shall laugh,  
For their plans will come to naught,  
In Your hands, I find my rest,  
The battle is already fought.

Chorus:  
I will sing of Your power,  
I will sing of Your mercy,  
In the morning light, I will rise,  
For You are my defense, my shield,  
In You alone, my heart finds peace,  
O God, my refuge, my strength.

Bridge:  
Let them wander, let them roam,  
But I know where my heart belongs,  
You are with me in the storm,  
Your love, it carries me along.  
Consume their pride, for they shall see,  
That You alone reign eternally.

Chorus:  
I will sing of Your power,  
I will sing of Your mercy,  
In the morning light, I will rise,  
For You are my defense, my shield,  
In You alone, my heart finds peace,  
O God, my refuge, my strength.

Outro:  
With each breath, I will declare,  
Your goodness, Lord, beyond compare,  
In every trial, You are there,  
My refuge, my strength, my prayer.  
Amen.
