Verse 1  
The heavens declare Your glory, O Lord,  
In every star, Your handiwork is poured.  
Day speaks of Your grace, night whispers Your name,  
In every language, we lift up the same.  

Chorus  
O God, we behold Your wonders,  
In the skies and in our hearts,  
Your law is perfect, it revives our souls,  
In Your embrace, we find our roles.  
Let the words of my mouth and the thoughts of my heart,  
Be pleasing to You, my strength, my part.  

Verse 2  
Your commandments rejoice, bringing light to our eyes,

More precious than gold, more sweet than the hive,  
In Your truth, O Lord, we come alive.
