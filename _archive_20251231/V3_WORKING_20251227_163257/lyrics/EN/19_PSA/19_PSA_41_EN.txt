[TITLE]
Blessed Are the Poor

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the stillness, Lord, I come,  
With a heart that's worn and weak,  
You see the ones who suffer,  
And the pain that they can't speak.  
You promise, Lord, to lift us high,  
To deliver in our hour of need,  
For the humble, You will provide,  
In Your mercy, we are freed.

(Chorus)  
Blessed are the poor,  
For in You, they find their peace,  
Lord, deliver us,  
And let our burdens cease.  
In our weakness, You are strong,  
Hold us close, where we belong,  
We will trust in You, our song,  
Forever, we are Yours.

(Verse 2)  
When my enemies surround me,  
Whispers come like shadows deep,  
Yet Your kindness stays beside me,  
In Your arms, my soul shall weep.  
You know my heart, You see my tears,  
Raise me up, O Lord, I plead,  
In my trials, calm my fears,  
In Your presence, I am freed.

(Bridge)  
Your love sustains, it never fails,  
In the darkness, hope prevails,  
You uphold my integrity,  
Set my heart before Your face,  
Oh, Lord, I call, be merciful,  
Heal my soul, I long for grace.

Amen, and Amen,  
Forever, we are Yours.
