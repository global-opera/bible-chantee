[TITLE]
Righteous King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, give wisdom to the king,  
In justice, let his heart take wing.  
He shall judge the poor with grace,  
In righteousness, our fears erase.  
Mountains high shall bring us peace,  
In Your embrace, our troubles cease.

Chorus  
We lift our hands, we bow our knees,  
To the King of kings, our hearts' release.  
Forever, Lord, Your name we praise,  
In every heart, Your love ablaze.

Verse 2  
Like rain upon the mown grass,  
Your mercy flows, our burdens pass.  
The righteous flourish in Your light,  
Abundance of peace, the day and night.  
From sea to sea, Your reign extends,  
In every heart, Your love transcends.

Chorus  
We lift our hands, we bow our knees,  
To the King of kings, our hearts' release.  
Forever, Lord, Your name we praise,  
In every heart, Your love ablaze.

Bridge  
Blessed be Your glorious name,  
Forevermore, we’ll sing the same.  
In every nation, hearts will call,  
"Blessed be the One who reigns o’er all!"

Chorus  
We lift our hands, we bow our knees,  
To the King of kings, our hearts' release.  
Forever, Lord, Your name we praise,  
In every heart, Your love ablaze.

Outro  
O Lord, let glory fill the earth,  
In every life, reveal Your worth.  
Blessed be the God of might,  
Forever, Lord, our guiding light.
