[TITLE]
The Lord Reigns in Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
The Lord reigns, let the earth rejoice,  
In His presence, we lift our voice,  
Clouds and darkness surround His throne,  
Righteousness and judgment, our cornerstone.  

Chorus:  
Rejoice in the Lord, O righteous souls,  
His light breaks through, and our hearts behold,  
He is high above, all gods He surpasses,  
In His glory, we find our peace that lasts.  

Verse 2:  
A fire goes forth, consuming the night,  
His lightnings flash, bringing hope and sight,  
The hills melt away, in His mighty grace,  
Every heart trembles, in His embrace.  

Chorus:  
Rejoice in the Lord, O righteous souls,  
His light breaks through, and our hearts behold,  
He is high above, all gods He surpasses,  
In His glory, we find our peace that lasts.  

Bridge:  
Zion rejoices, the daughters sing,  
In His judgments, our praises ring,  
He preserves the souls of those who believe,  
In His holiness, we find reprieve.  

Chorus:  
Rejoice in the Lord, O righteous souls,  
His light breaks through, and our hearts behold,  
He is high above, all gods He surpasses,  
In His glory, we find our peace that lasts.  

Outro:  
Light is sown for the just, gladness within,  
We stand in His presence, our Savior, our King,  
Rejoice in the Lord, lift your voice in praise,  
For His love and mercy, we will always raise.
