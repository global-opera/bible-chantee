### Title: Walk in Integrity

Verse 1  
O Lord, I come before You,  
With a heart that's pure and true,  
In Your lovingkindness, I abide,  
In Your truth, I will confide.  
Examine me, Lord, search my soul,  
In Your light, I am made whole.  
I have walked in integrity,  
In Your presence, I am free.  

Chorus  
I will bless You in the gathering,  
With a heart of thanks, I sing,  
For Your wondrous works surround me,  
In Your house, my spirit clings.  
Redeem me, Lord, be merciful,  
In Your love, I find my role,  
Together we will lift Your name,  
In integrity, I’ll proclaim.  

Verse 2  
I have turned from those who stray,  
And the wicked I won't sway,  
With clean hands, I approach Your throne,  
In Your altar, I find home.  
I will not sit with those who scheme,  
In Your truth, I find my dream.  
Gather not my soul with sin,  
Lord, my victory's found in Him.
