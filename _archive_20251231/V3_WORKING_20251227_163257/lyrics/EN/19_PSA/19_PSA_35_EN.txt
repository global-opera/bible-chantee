[TITLE]
O Lord, My Salvation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Lord, I plead my cause today,  
Stand with me, as shadows sway.  
Against the foes who seek my soul,  
Lift Your shield, make my heart whole.  

Chorus  
For You are my salvation, my refuge strong,  
In the battle, Lord, to You I belong.  
Let my heart rejoice, let my spirit sing,  
In Your righteousness, I’ll find everything.  

Verse 2  
Confound the ones who plot my fall,  
Let their schemes be turned to call.  
As chaff before the winds they flee,  
With angelic might, You fight for me.  

Chorus  
For You are my salvation, my refuge strong,  
In the battle, Lord, to You I belong.  
Let my heart rejoice, let my spirit sing,  
In Your righteousness, I’ll find everything.  

Bridge  
How long, O Lord, will You be still?  
Awake to my cry, fulfill Your will.  
Judge me with mercy, let truth prevail,  
In the great assembly, my heart shall hail.  

Verse 3  
When falsehood rises, I will stand,  
In prayer and fasting, I lift my hands.  
Though they rejoice at my despair,  
I’ll trust in You, O Lord, in prayer.  

Chorus  
For You are my salvation, my refuge strong,  
In the battle, Lord, to You I belong.  
Let my heart rejoice, let my spirit sing,  
In Your righteousness, I’ll find everything.  

Outro  
With every breath, I’ll praise Your name,  
In the midst of trials, I’ll never be the same.  
Let them shout for joy, let them sing aloud,  
For You take pleasure in Your faithful crowd.
