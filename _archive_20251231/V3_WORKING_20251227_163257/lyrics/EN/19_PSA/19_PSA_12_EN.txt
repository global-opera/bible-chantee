[TITLE]
In Your Safety, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Help us, Lord, for the faithful fade,  
In a world where truth is betrayed.  
With lips that flatter, hearts that deceive,  
In silence we sigh, longing to believe.  

Chorus  
You are our refuge, our hope in the night,  
Preserve us, O Lord, in Your steadfast light.  
Your words are pure, like silver refined,  
In the chaos around, Your peace we will find.  

Verse 2  
The proud may rise with their boastful tongues,  
But You hear the cries of the least among.  
For the oppressed, You will stand and defend,  
In Your loving arms, we find our true friend.  

Chorus  
You are our refuge, our hope in the night,  
Preserve us, O Lord, in Your steadfast light.  
Your words are pure, like silver refined,  
In the chaos around, Your peace we will find.  

Bridge  
Lord, arise, bring safety to the meek,  
In the shadows of doubt, it’s Your voice that we seek.  
Though the wicked may walk, we will stand in Your grace,  
For You are our stronghold, our safe resting place.  

Chorus  
You are our refuge, our hope in the night,  
Preserve us, O Lord, in Your steadfast light.  
Your words are pure, like silver refined,  
In the chaos around, Your peace we will find.  

Outro  
Help us, Lord, for the faithful fade,  
In Your arms, our fears invade.  
With hearts that trust, we lift up our praise,  
Forever, O Lord, we’ll sing of Your ways.
