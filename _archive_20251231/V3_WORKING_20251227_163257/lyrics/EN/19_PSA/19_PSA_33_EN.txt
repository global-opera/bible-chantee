[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Rejoice in the Lord, O righteous ones,  
Your praises rise like morning sun.  
With harp and psaltery, we lift our voice,  
In His goodness alone, we will rejoice.  

Chorus:  
Sing a new song to Him, lift your heart,  
Play with skill, let your worship start.  
For His word is right, His ways are true,  
In all His goodness, we find our view.  

Verse 2:  
He spoke, and the heavens were formed with grace,  
The breath of His mouth, the stars in place.  
He gathers the waters, He knows our plight,  
In His loving kindness, we find our light.  

Chorus:  
Sing a new song to Him, lift your heart,  
Play with skill, let your worship start.  
For His word is right, His ways are true,  
In all His goodness, we find our view.  

Bridge:  
The eye of the Lord is upon those who fear,  
In His mercy and love, we have nothing to fear.  
Our souls wait for Him, our shield in the night,  
With hearts full of trust, we bask in His light.  

Chorus:  
Sing a new song to Him, lift your heart,  
Play with skill, let your worship start.  
For His word is right, His ways are true,  
In all His goodness, we find our view.  

Outro:  
Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us today,  
As we hope in Your name, guide us on our way.  
Rejoice in the Lord, with hearts full of grace,  
In His love everlasting, we find our place.
