[TITLE]
In Your Name, O Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Save me, O God, by Your mighty name,  
Judge my heart, let Your strength proclaim.  
Hear my prayer, Lord, I lift my voice,  
In the shadows, I find my choice.  

Chorus:  
For You are my helper, my refuge, my guide,  
In the midst of the storm, You are right by my side.  
Though foes may surround me, I will not despair,  
In Your truth and Your mercy, I find my prayer.  

Verse 2:  
Strangers rise up, seeking to devour,  
But I look to You, my strength and my tower.  
They’ve not set You before them, it’s clear,  
Yet I will stand firm, for my God is near.  

Chorus:  
For You are my helper, my refuge, my guide,  
In the midst of the storm, You are right by my side.  
Though foes may surround me, I will not despair,  
In Your truth and Your mercy, I find my prayer.  

Bridge:  
I will freely sacrifice, lift my praise on high,  
For You have delivered, oh, I will testify.  
Your love is unchanging, my heart will sing,  
You’ve seen my desire, and You’re everything.  

Chorus:  
For You are my helper, my refuge, my guide,  
In the midst of the storm, You are right by my side.  
Though foes may surround me, I will not despair,  
In Your truth and Your mercy, I find my prayer.  

Outro:  
Oh, I will praise Your name, O Lord, it is good,  
For You’ve delivered me, just as You said You would.  
With my eyes on Your promise, I’ll stand and declare,  
In Your love, I find hope, in Your name, I share.
