[TITLE]
In Your Presence

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Hear my cry, O Lord, I call to You,  
From the depths of my heart, I seek what's true.  
Your eyes behold the paths I tread,  
In Your loving kindness, Lord, I'm led.  

Chorus:  
Keep me as the apple of Your eye,  
Under the shadow of Your wings, I’ll lie.  
With every step, I trust in Thee,  
In Your presence, Lord, I long to be.  

Verse 2:  
You’ve tested my heart, in the night You’ve come,  
Your gentle whisper, it breaks the numb.  
I will not stray from the way You've shown,  
In Your grace, I find my home.  

Chorus:  
Keep me as the apple of Your eye,  
Under the shadow of Your wings, I’ll lie.  
With every step, I trust in Thee,  
In Your presence, Lord, I long to be.  

Bridge:  
Arise, O Lord, deliver me,  
From the hands that rise against me.  
Your right hand saves, my fortress strong,  
In Your embrace, I will belong.  

Chorus:  
Keep me as the apple of Your eye,  
Under the shadow of Your wings, I’ll lie.  
With every step, I trust in Thee,  
In Your presence, Lord, I long to be.  

Ending:  
I will behold Your face in righteousness,  
Awake in Your likeness, my heart will rest.  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
Forever in Your love, my soul's release.
