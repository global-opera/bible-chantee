[TITLE]
Redeemed from the Grave

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear, O people, all around,  
Rich and poor, let truth be found.  
Wisdom speaks, my heart will say,  
In the shadows, I will pray.  
Why should I fear, when trials come,  
In the dark, He guides me home.  

Chorus  
For God will redeem my soul,  
From the power of the grave,  
In His arms, I find my whole,  
He is mighty, He will save.  
Though the riches fade away,  
His love remains forever true,  
In His light, I’ll find my way,  
O Lord, I trust in You.  

Verse 2  
They trust in wealth, but it will fail,  
No ransom bought, no earthly tale.  
The wise and fool, they all must die,  
Yet in His grace, my spirit flies.  
Man's glory fades, like morning mist,  
But in His love, I will persist.  

Chorus

Bridge  
So I will lay my burdens down,  
In the peace that I have found,  
No fear in death, I’ll walk with grace,  
For in His arms, I’ve found my place.  
From the ashes, He will raise,  
My heart will sing, my life will praise.

Outro  
Redeemed from the grave, I rise anew,  
In every moment, Lord, I trust in You.  
Your promise stands, forever sure,  
In Your embrace, my heart is pure.
