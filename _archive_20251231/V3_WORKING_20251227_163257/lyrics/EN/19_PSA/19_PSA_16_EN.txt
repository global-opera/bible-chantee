[TITLE]
In Your Presence, Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Preserve me, O God, I trust in You,  
My heart, it finds its home, in all that You do.  
You’re my Lord, my joy, my soul’s delight,  
In the saints of the earth, Your love shines bright.

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
My heart is glad, my fears release.  
With You beside me, I shall not sway,  
You’re my inheritance, my hope each day.

Verse 2  
Their sorrows multiply, who chase the vain,  
But I lift my voice, I’ll call Your name.  
You guide my steps, through night and day,  
Your counsel comforts, in every way.

Chorus  
In Your presence, Lord, I find my peace,  
My heart is glad, my fears release.  
With You beside me, I shall not sway,  
You’re my inheritance, my hope each day.

Bridge  
You won’t leave my soul in despair,  
For in Your light, I find You there.  
You show me the path that leads to life,  
In Your embrace, I’m free from strife.

Chorus

Outro  
At Your right hand, pleasures evermore,  
In Your presence, Lord, I long to explore.  
With joy overflowing, I’ll sing Your praise,  
Forever and always, my heart You’ll raise.
