[TITLE]
In Your Name We Stand

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the day of trouble, Lord, hear my cry,  
Defend me with the strength of Jacob’s God on high.  
From Your sanctuary, send Your help to me,  
In the shadows of the night, let Your light set me free.  

Chorus:  
We will rejoice in salvation’s song,  
In the name of our God, to You we belong.  
With banners lifted high, we proclaim Your grace,  
Lord, fulfill our hearts, in Your love we embrace.  

Verse 2:  
Remember our offerings, accept our praise,  
In the fire of sacrifice, we seek Your ways.  
Grant us according to the desires of our hearts,  
In every counsel spoken, may Your will impart.  

Chorus:  
We will rejoice in salvation’s song,  
In the name of our God, to You we belong.  
With banners lifted high, we proclaim Your grace,  
Lord, fulfill our hearts, in Your love we embrace.  

Bridge:  
Some trust in chariots, some in worldly might,  
But we trust in Your name, Lord, our guiding light.  
Though the world may falter, we will rise and stand,  
Saved by Your right hand, we are held in Your plan.  

Chorus:  
We will rejoice in salvation’s song,  
In the name of our God, to You we belong.  
With banners lifted high, we proclaim Your grace,  
Lord, fulfill our hearts, in Your love we embrace.  

Outro:  
Save us, O Lord, when we call on Your name,  
In the depths of our hearts, may Your truth remain.  
With every breath we take, we will lift You high,  
In the name of the Lord, we will never die.
