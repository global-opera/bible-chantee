### Title: "O Lord, My Refuge"

Verse 1  
O Lord, to whom vengeance belongs,  
Reveal Yourself, where justice longs.  
Lift up Your hand, judge of the earth,  
Render reward, give pride its dearth.  

Chorus  
How long, O Lord, shall the wicked thrive?  
In the silence of night, I call, I strive.  
For You know our hearts, our every thought,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find what we sought.  

Verse 2  
They break in pieces, Your people, O King,  
The widow and stranger, their voices ring.  
Yet still they say, “The Lord does not see,”  
But wisdom calls, “Open your eyes, be free.”  

Chorus  
How long, O Lord, shall the wicked thrive?  
In the silence of night, I call, I strive.  
For You know our hearts, our every thought,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find what we sought.  

Bridge  
Blessed is the man whom You teach and chasten,  
In the days of trouble, Your grace is our haven.  
Though the throne of iniquity stands tall,  
You’re my defense, my rock through it all.  

Chorus  
How long, O Lord, shall the wicked thrive?  
In the silence of night, I call, I strive.  
For You know our hearts, our every thought,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find what we sought.  

Outro  
The Lord is my help, He holds me up high,  
In the multitude of thoughts, to You I cry.  
With every breath, I’ll trust and believe,  
For in Your love, O Lord, I shall cleave.
