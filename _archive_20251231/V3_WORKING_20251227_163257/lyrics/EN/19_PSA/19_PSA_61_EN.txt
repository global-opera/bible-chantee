Verse 1  
Hear my cry, O Lord, I call to Thee,  
From the ends of the earth, my soul’s in need.  
When my heart is overwhelmed, Lord, I seek,  
Lead me to the Rock, Your strength I plead.

You are my shelter, strong tower divine,
