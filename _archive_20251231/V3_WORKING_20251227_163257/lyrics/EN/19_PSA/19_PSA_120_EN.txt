[TITLE]
In My Distress I Cry

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In my distress, I cry to You,  
O Lord, I seek Your face anew.  
From lying lips, deliver me,  
From tongues that bring deceit so free.  

Chorus  
Hear my prayer, O Lord, I call,  
In Your mercy, catch my fall.  
Though sharp arrows may surround,  
In Your love, I am found.  

Verse 2  
Woe is me, I dwell in strife,  
In a land where peace is rife.  
But I will stand and seek Your grace,  
For in Your arms, I find my place.  

Chorus  
Hear my prayer, O Lord, I call,  
In Your mercy, catch my fall.  
Though sharp arrows may surround,  
In Your love, I am found.  

Bridge  
I am for peace, though they bring war,  
In Your strength, I will endure.  
With every breath, I lift Your name,  
In the silence, I’ll proclaim.  

Chorus  
Hear my prayer, O Lord, I call,  
In Your mercy, catch my fall.  
Though sharp arrows may surround,  
In Your love, I am found.  

Outro  
In my distress, I cry to You,  
O Lord, my heart is ever true.  
With every tear, I trust in grace,  
Forever, Lord, I seek Your face.
