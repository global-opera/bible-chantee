[TITLE]
In Your Presence, O God

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In Zion, O Lord, our hearts will sing,  
With praises that rise, to You we bring.  
You hear our prayers, our cries ascend,  
All flesh shall come, on You depend.  

Chorus:  
Blessed are those whom You choose to draw,  
Into Your courts, we stand in awe.  
Satisfaction flows from Your holy place,  
In Your goodness, we find our grace.  

Verse 2:  
Though iniquities weigh heavy and strong,  
Your mercy prevails, where we belong.  
You cleanse our hearts, our transgressions fade,  
We trust in Your love, our fears allayed.  

Chorus:  
Blessed are those whom You choose to draw,  
Into Your courts, we stand in awe.  
Satisfaction flows from Your holy place,  
In Your goodness, we find our grace.  

Bridge:  
You calm the seas, the chaos inside,  
With strength like mountains, in You we abide.  
Your tokens of love bring joy to the morn,  
In every valley, new life is born.  

Verse 3:  
You water the earth, with rivers so wide,  
In seasons of drought, You’re ever our guide.  
Crowning the year with Your goodness and care,  
We lift up our voices, in gratitude, prayer.  

Chorus:  
Blessed are those whom You choose to draw,  
Into Your courts, we stand in awe.  
Satisfaction flows from Your holy place,  
In Your goodness, we find our grace.  

Outro:  
In Your presence, O God, we find our rest,  
With hearts overflowing, we know we are blessed.  
Forever we’ll sing, in joy and in peace,  
In Your arms of love, our souls find release.
