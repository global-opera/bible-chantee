[TITLE]
Taste and See

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
I will bless the Lord at all times,  
His praise shall fill my heart and soul,  
When I sought Him, He heard my cries,  
He delivered me, He made me whole.  

[Chorus]  
O magnify the Lord with me,  
Let us lift His name on high,  
Taste and see, the Lord is good,  
In His love, we will abide.  

[Verse 2]  
The humble hear and they rejoice,  
He surrounds the broken and the meek,  
The angel of the Lord stands near,  
In every trouble, He will speak.  

[Chorus]  
O magnify the Lord with me,  
Let us lift His name on high,  
Taste and see, the Lord is good,  
In His love, we will abide.  

[Bridge]  
Keep your tongue from evil's way,  
Seek the peace, let love be your guide,  
The Lord is close to all who pray,  
In His presence, we shall hide.  

[Verse 3]  
Many are the trials that we face,  
But He will rescue, our hearts He'll mend,  
Trust in Him, for He is near,  
His faithfulness will never end.  

[Chorus]  
O magnify the Lord with me,  
Let us lift His name on high,  
Taste and see, the Lord is good,  
In His love, we will abide.  

[Outro]  
O fear the Lord, you saints of grace,  
In His arms, we'll find our place,  
The Lord redeems, His love will stay,  
Forevermore, we'll sing His praise.
