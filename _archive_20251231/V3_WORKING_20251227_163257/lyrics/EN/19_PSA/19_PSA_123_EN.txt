[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness of this hour,  
We lift our eyes on high,  
To You, O Lord, our shelter,  
In the heavens, You abide.  
As servants watch their masters,  
And maidens seek their guide,  
Our hearts are set on mercy,  
In You alone, we bide.

Chorus:  
Have mercy, Lord, we cry,  
Our souls are longing, hear our sighs.  
With every tear, we come to You,  
In our weakness, make us new.  
Lift our eyes, O Lord, we plead,  
In Your presence, we are freed.

Verse 2:  
Filled with scorn and heavy burdens,  
We bear the weight of shame,  
In the midst of proud contempt,  
We call upon Your name.  
Though the world may mock and scorn us,  
Still, we choose to stand,  
For in Your arms, we find our refuge,  
In Your grace, we’re planned.
