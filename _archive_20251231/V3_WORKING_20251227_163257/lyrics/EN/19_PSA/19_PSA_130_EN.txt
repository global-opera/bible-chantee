Verse 1  
Out of the depths, O Lord, I call,  
Hear my voice, attend my plea,  
In my sorrow, I stand small,  
Yet in You, my hope will be.  
If You marked our iniquities,  
Who could stand before Your gaze?  
But You offer sweet forgiveness,  
In Your love, my heart will praise.

Chorus  
I wait for You, O Lord, my soul,  
More than watchmen for the dawn.  
In Your Word, my hope's made whole,  
With You, Lord, I will carry on.  
Let Israel put their trust in Thee,  
For mercy flows and grace is free.  
You redeem us from all sin,  
In Your arms, our lives begin.

Verse 2  
In the silence, I seek Your face,  
You are my light in darkest days.  
With each heartbeat, I seek Your grace,  
Guide my steps in all Your ways.  
For with You, there’s plenteous grace,  
And mercy, Lord, is ever near.  
In my weakness, I find my place,  
In Your love, I will not fear.
