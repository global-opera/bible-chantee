[TITLE]
My Light and Salvation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
The Lord is my light, my hope in the night,  
Whom shall I fear when I walk in His sight?  
Though enemies rise, their plans will not stand,  
I trust in His strength, He holds my hand.  

Chorus  
I will dwell in His house, forever I'll seek,  
To behold all His beauty, my heart will not speak.  
In the secret of His love, I find my peace,  
He lifts me above, my fears will cease.  

Verse 2  
In trouble, He hides me, a shelter so strong,  
He sets me upon a rock, where I belong.  
With joy in my heart, I offer my praise,  
In His holy presence, my spirit will raise.  

Chorus  
I will dwell in His house, forever I'll seek,  
To behold all His beauty, my heart will not speak.  
In the secret of His love, I find my peace,  
He lifts me above, my fears will cease.  

Bridge  
When my father and mother may turn away,  
The Lord will take me up, my guiding stay.  
Teach me Your ways, Lord, lead me each day,  
In courage, I wait, for You show me the way.  

Chorus  
I will dwell in His house, forever I'll seek,  
To behold all His beauty, my heart will not speak.  
In the secret of His love, I find my peace,  
He lifts me above, my fears will cease.  

Outro  
Wait on the Lord, be of good courage,  
He'll strengthen my heart, my soul will flourish.  
With every breath, I will trust and believe,  
In the goodness of God, I shall receive.
