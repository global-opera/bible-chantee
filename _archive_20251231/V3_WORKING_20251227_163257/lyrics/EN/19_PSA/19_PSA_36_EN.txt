Verse 1:  
In the silence, my heart knows,  
The wicked tread where no fear grows.  
Flattering words, they weave their lies,  
Yet in Your truth, my hope shall rise.  
For Your mercy, Lord, fills the skies,  
And Your faithfulness, it never dies.

Chorus:  
Under Your wings, I find my place,  
In the shadow of Your endless grace.  
You are my refuge, my heart's delight,  
In Your light, Lord, I see the light.  
In the river of pleasures, I drink from You,  
Your lovingkindness, ever true.

Verse 2:  
Though the proud may rise and seek to claim,

I stand secure in Your embrace,  
For You preserve both man and grace.  
Your righteousness, like mountains high,  
In the depths of love, You hear my cry.
