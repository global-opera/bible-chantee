[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Have mercy on me, Lord, I plead,  
In Your lovingkindness, set me free.  
Blot out my transgressions, wash me clear,  
In the depths of my heart, I hold You near.  

Chorus  
Create in me a clean heart, O God,  
Renew a spirit that’s pure and true.  
Restore to me the joy of salvation,  
I long to be close, I long to be with You.  

Verse 2  
I acknowledge my sins, they weigh me down,  
Against You alone, I wear the crown.  
In the hidden depths, teach me Your ways,  
In Your tender mercy, let me praise.  

Chorus  
Create in me a clean heart, O God,  
Renew a spirit that’s pure and true.  
Restore to me the joy of salvation,  
I long to be close, I long to be with You.  

Bridge  
Purge me with hyssop, make me whole,  
Hide Your face from my sins, cleanse my soul.  
Open my lips to sing of Your grace,  
I’ll share Your goodness, I’ll seek Your face.  

Chorus  
Create in me a clean heart, O God,  
Renew a spirit that’s pure and true.  
Restore to me the joy of salvation,  
I long to be close, I long to be with You.  

Outro  
With a broken spirit, I come in prayer,  
A contrite heart, You will not spare.  
In Your good pleasure, let Zion rise,  
I’ll offer my life, and lift it high.
