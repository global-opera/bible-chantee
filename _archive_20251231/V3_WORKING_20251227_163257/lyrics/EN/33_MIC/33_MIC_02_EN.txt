[TITLE]
Awake, O Jacob

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Woe to the hearts that plot in the night,  
In shadows of greed, they seek their delight.  
With power in hand, they rise with the dawn,  
But the cry of the broken, it lingers on.  

Chorus:  
Awake, O Jacob, hear the call,  
The Lord is speaking, He sees it all.  
Turn from the darkness, rise from the dust,  
In Him is our refuge, in Him we trust.  

Verse 2:  
They covet the fields, take by their might,  
Oppressing the weak, they hide from the light.  
But the justice of God will not be delayed,  
He gathers the remnant, His promise displayed.  

Chorus:  
Awake, O Jacob, hear the call,  
The Lord is speaking, He sees it all.  
Turn from the darkness, rise from the dust,  
In Him is our refuge, in Him we trust.  

Bridge:  
Arise and depart, for this is not rest,  
Pollution surrounds, but we are blessed.  
The King of all glory leads us ahead,  
With courage and truth, we’ll follow instead.  

Verse 3:  
Oh, cast off the robes of deceitful lies,  
For the Spirit of God, it never denies.  
He gathers His people, unites in His name,  
From the ashes of shame, we rise to proclaim.  

Chorus:  
Awake, O Jacob, hear the call,  
The Lord is speaking, He sees it all.  
Turn from the darkness, rise from the dust,  
In Him is our refuge, in Him we trust.  

Outro:  
The breaker has come, we’re free to go,  
With the Lord as our leader, our hearts overflow.  
Awake, O Jacob, let your voice be heard,  
In His love and mercy, we stand on His Word.
