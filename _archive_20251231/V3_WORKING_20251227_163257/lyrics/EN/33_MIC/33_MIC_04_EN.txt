[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the last days, the mountain shall rise,  
Above all the hills, reaching to the skies.  
People will gather, from nations afar,  
To the house of our God, our shining star.  
He’ll teach us His ways, we’ll walk in His light,  
In paths of His justice, we’ll find what is right.  

Chorus:  
O Daughter of Zion, arise, take your stand,  
In the name of our Lord, we’ll walk hand in hand.  
No more will we fear, for He reigns from above,  
In peace and in hope, we dwell in His love.  

Verse 2:  
Swords turned to plowshares, the nations will cease,  
In the shadow of Zion, we’ll find our peace.  
Every vine and fig tree, a place to abide,  
With none to affright us, in Him we confide.  
Though nations may gather, their plans will fall down,  
For the thoughts of the Lord, in glory, abound.  

Chorus:  
O Daughter of Zion, arise, take your stand,  
In the name of our Lord, we’ll walk hand in hand.  
No more will we fear, for He reigns from above,  
In peace and in hope, we dwell in His love.  

Bridge:  
Gathered together, the lost and the weak,  
He’ll make us a remnant, His promise we seek.  
From the fields of our labor, to the heart of our King,  
We’ll sing of His mercy, and praises we’ll bring.  

Chorus:  
O Daughter of Zion, arise, take your stand,  
In the name of our Lord, we’ll walk hand in hand.  
No more will we fear, for He reigns from above,  
In peace and in hope, we dwell in His love.  

Outro:  
So, rise, O Daughter of Zion, shine bright,  
For the Lord of all hosts is our guiding light.  
We’ll walk in His name, forever we’ll be,  
In the mountain of grace, we are set free.
