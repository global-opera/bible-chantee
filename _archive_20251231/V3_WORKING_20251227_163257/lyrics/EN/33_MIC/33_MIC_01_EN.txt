[TITLE]
The Call from the Temple

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Hear, O people, the word of the Lord,  
From His holy temple, let truth be restored.  
Mountains tremble, valleys break,  
As He comes down, our hearts awake.  
For the sins of Jacob, we bow in shame,  
In the shadow of Jerusalem, we call His name.

Chorus  
O Lord, come forth, tread upon the high,  
Melt our hearts like wax, let our pride die.  
In Your presence, we lay bare,  
All our idols, we lift in prayer.  
Bring us back from our wayward ways,  
O God of glory, we seek Your face.

Verse 2  
In the wailing, we find our plea,  
For the wounds of Judah, we mourn and see.  
Roll in the dust, we confess our sin,  
As the gates of sorrow call us in.  
Lachish, Saphir, hear our cry,  
For the grief of our hearts, Lord, we rely.

Chorus

Bridge  
In the silence, we hear Your call,  
To turn from our ways, to surrender all.  
The glory of Israel, arise anew,  
We yield our hearts, Lord, we wait for You.
