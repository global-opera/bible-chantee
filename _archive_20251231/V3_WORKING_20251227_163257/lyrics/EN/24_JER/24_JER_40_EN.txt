Verse 1:  
From the ashes of our sorrow,  
A voice calls out in the night,  
Jeremiah, bound yet hopeful,  
In chains but seeking the light.  
The Lord has spoken, His will made clear,  
In the land of judgment, we shall not fear.

Chains unbound, we rise again,
