[TITLE]
Girdle of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O Lord, You spoke to me, a call to wear,  
A linen girdle, pure, a symbol to bear.  
In the depths of obedience, I found my place,  
Yet I hid it by Euphrates, lost in Your grace.  

Chorus:  
As the girdle clings to the waist,  
So You cling to us, in love and in grace.  
But we wander far, in pride we fall,  
O Lord, hear our cry, we need Your call.  

Verse 2:  
Days turned to shadows, the word came again,  
“Rise up, take the girdle, for it’s marred by sin.”  
I dug in the river where I’d laid it low,  
Profitable for nothing, like hearts turned to stone.  

Chorus:  
As the girdle clings to the waist,  
So You cling to us, in love and in grace.  
But we wander far, in pride we fall,  
O Lord, hear our cry, we need Your call.  

Bridge:  
We’ve chased after gods, in darkness we roam,  
But Your voice calls us back, to turn and come home.  
Humble our hearts, let us bow down in fear,  
Before the darkness, let Your light draw near.  

Verse 3:  
Hear, O Jerusalem, the cry of the Lord,  
Your beauty is fading, your heart’s grown bored.  
Yet there’s hope in the sorrow, in the tears that we shed,  
For You long to cleanse us, to rise from the dead.  

Chorus:  
As the girdle clings to the waist,  
So You cling to us, in love and in grace.  
But we wander far, in pride we fall,  
O Lord, hear our cry, we need Your call.  

Outro:  
Lift up our eyes, let us see the way,  
For You are the truth, our light, our stay.  
In the depths of our hearts, may we ever find,  
A girdle of grace, forever entwined.
