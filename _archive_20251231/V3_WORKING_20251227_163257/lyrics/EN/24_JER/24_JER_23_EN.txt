Verse 1  
Woe to the shepherds, who scatter the sheep,  
In the shadows of darkness, their watch they do keep.  
But the Lord speaks with power, His promise is clear,  
He gathers the remnant, His love draws us near.

Chorus  
He’s the Lord, our Righteousness,  
In Him we find our rest.  
No fear, no dismay,  
He leads us on the way.  
From the north and the south,  
He calls us from our doubt,  
With the heart of a shepherd,  
He gathers us now.
