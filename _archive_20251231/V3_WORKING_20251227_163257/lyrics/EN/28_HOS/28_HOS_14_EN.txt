### Return to the Lord

Verse 1  
O Israel, return to the Lord your God,  
For iniquity has brought you low.  
Take with you words, and turn to His grace,  
He’ll take away your sin, and make you whole.  

Chorus  
We will lift our hearts, like calves of our lips,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find our rest.  
No more will we trust in idols or kings,  
For in You alone, our hope takes wing.  

Verse 2  
I will heal your backsliding, love you free,  
My anger turned away, now you’re with Me.  
Like dew on the morning, I’ll bring you life,  
You’ll grow like the lily, in the light.  

Chorus  
We will lift our hearts, like calves of our lips,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find our rest.  
No more will we trust in idols or kings,  
For in You alone, our hope takes wing.  

Bridge  
Under Your shadow, we will revive,  
As corn and the vine, we come alive.  
What have we to do with the works of our hands?  
From You, O Lord, our fruit will stand.  

Chorus  
We will lift our hearts, like calves of our lips,  
In Your mercy, Lord, we find our rest.  
No more will we trust in idols or kings,  
For in You alone, our hope takes wing.  

Outro  
Who is wise to see the ways of the Lord?  
The just will walk in paths forevermore.  
Return to the Lord, with hearts open wide,  
In His love and grace, forever abide.
