Verse 1  
In trembling hearts, we rise, O Lord,  
Exalted once, now lost in pride;  
We've turned to idols, crafted lies,  
In silver dreams, our souls have died.  
Yet You are God, from Egypt's land,  
No savior but Your mighty hand.

Chorus  
O Israel, turn back to Me,  
In Your darkness, I will be  
The light that breaks the chains of sin,  
In Your sorrow, let joy begin.  
I am the King, where else to flee?  
In repentance, find your plea.

Verse 2  
In wilderness, I knew your name,  
In drought and trials, still the same.  
Yet in the bounty, hearts grew cold,  
Forgotten grace, our stories told.  
As a lion, fierce and true,  
I seek the lost, I'll come for you.
