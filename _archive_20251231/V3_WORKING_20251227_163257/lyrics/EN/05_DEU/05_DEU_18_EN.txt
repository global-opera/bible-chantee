[TITLE]
The Lord Is Our Inheritance

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Your presence, we gather here to stand,  
The Levites serve before You, in the promise of Your hand.  
No earthly claim or portion, but our hearts are set on high,  
For the Lord, He is our treasure, in His love we will abide.

Chorus  
The Lord is our inheritance, our refuge and our song,  
In the fire of Your offerings, we find where we belong.  
You raise up a prophet, Your truth will be our guide,  
We listen for Your whisper, in our hearts, You will reside.

Verse 2  
When the nations call in darkness, we will not follow their ways,  
You have chosen us, O Father, to walk in holy praise.  
No divination nor enchantments shall lead us far astray,  
In the light of Your salvation, we will trust and not dismay.

Bridge  
Speak to us, O mighty Savior, with words that bring us peace,  
In the quiet, in the stillness, let our fears and doubts release.  
We will walk in Your commandments, with hearts that long to know,  
The promises You’ve spoken, in the love You freely show.

Outro  
So we gather in Your presence, Lord, our hearts forever true,  
In the covenant of mercy, we find our strength in You.  
For the Lord is our inheritance, our song that never ends,  
In the grace of Your salvation, we are Yours, our faithful friend.
