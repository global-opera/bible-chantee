[TITLE]
In the Place You Choose

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land You’ve given, we shall stand,  
With hearts aligned, we heed Your command.  
To cast away the idols of old,  
And seek the truth that Your Word has told.  

[Chorus]  
In the place You choose, we gather near,  
With offerings of love, we draw You here.  
We rejoice in grace, in all that we do,  
For every blessing, Lord, we give back to You.  

[Verse 2]  
We’ll raise our voices, lift our hands high,  
With every sacrifice, we testify.  
In unity we come, our hearts entwined,  
To seek Your presence, Lord, our souls aligned.  

[Chorus]  
In the place You choose, we gather near,  
With offerings of love, we draw You here.  
We rejoice in grace, in all that we do,  
For every blessing, Lord, we give back to You.  

[Bridge]  
You are our refuge, our everlasting light,  
In You we find our rest, our hope shines bright.  
As we break the bread, and pour the wine,  
We remember Your love, forever divine.  

[Chorus]  
In the place You choose, we gather near,  
With offerings of love, we draw You here.  
We rejoice in grace, in all that we do,  
For every blessing, Lord, we give back to You.  

[Outro]  
So lead us, O Lord, to the place You’ve planned,  
With faith as our guide, we’ll follow Your hand.  
For in every moment, our hearts will sing,  
To the glory of our God, our everlasting King.
