[TITLE]
Hear Our Cry, O Israel

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
O Israel, hear the words of the Lord,  
His statutes and judgments, a righteous accord.  
To live in His promise, the land that He gives,  
In faith we will follow, for in Him we live.  

Chorus  
Keep His commandments, hold fast to His grace,  
For our God is near, in every place.  
In the fire and darkness, His voice breaks the night,  
With love everlasting, He guides us in light.  

Verse 2  
Remember the wonders, the signs of His might,  
He brought you from bondage, He led you to light.  
Cling to His covenant, let not your hearts stray,  
Teach your sons and daughters to walk in His way.  

Chorus  
Keep His commandments, hold fast to His grace,  
For our God is near, in every place.  
In the fire and darkness, His voice breaks the night,  
With love everlasting, He guides us in light.  

Bridge  
What nation is great, with a God so near?  
In every heart's longing, He'll calm every fear.  
If we seek Him with all, in sorrow or joy,  
He’ll show us His mercy, His love to employ.  

Verse 3  
In trials and troubles, His promise stands true,  
Through valleys of shadows, He’ll carry us through.  
Remember His goodness, His power displayed,  
In the covenant made, our hope will not fade.  

Chorus  
Keep His commandments, hold fast to His grace,  
For our God is near, in every place.  
In the fire and darkness, His voice breaks the night,  
With love everlasting, He guides us in light.  

Outro  
O Israel, hear the call of the King,  
With hearts intertwined, let our voices sing.  
For the Lord is our God, our fortress and guide,  
In the land of His promise, in Him we abide.
