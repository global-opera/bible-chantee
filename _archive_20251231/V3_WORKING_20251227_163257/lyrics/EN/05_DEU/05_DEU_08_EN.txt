Verse 1:  
In the wilderness, we wandered far,  
Through trials and hunger, beneath the stars.  
Yet in our longing, You showed the way,  
With manna from Heaven, our hearts You sway.  
Forty years we walked, our raiment new,  
In every step, Lord, we learned of You.

Chorus:  
Oh, remember, oh, remember,  
The path that leads to life,  
In Your commandments, we find our treasure,  
In Your presence, we rise.  
You brought us through the fire,  
You led us through the night,  
Oh, remember, oh, remember,  
In Your love, we find our light.

Verse 2:  
A land of promise, where blessings flow,  
Fountains and valleys where Your grace will grow.  
Wheat and honey, our tables lay,  
In fullness, we gather, our hearts to say.  
When we are full, let our hearts not stray,  
For You are our power, our guide, our stay.

It’s You who gives power, it’s You who provides,  
In every moment, Lord, be our guide.
