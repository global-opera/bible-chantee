[TITLE]
In the Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land where Your commandments flow,  
You teach us, Lord, the way to go.  
To fear Your name, to walk in grace,  
With hearts of love, we seek Your face.  
From generation to the next, we share,  
Your statutes, Lord, our hearts laid bare.  
With every step, we choose to stand,  
In the truth of Your mighty hand.  

Chorus  
Oh, hear, O Israel, our God is one,  
With all our hearts, we will love the Son.  
In every moment, we’ll speak Your word,  
In our homes and lives, let Your voice be heard.  
You brought us out, with a mighty hand,  
You lead us forth to the promised land.  

Verse 2  
You gave us cities we did not build,  
With blessings poured, our hearts You filled.  
In the fields of plenty, we gather grace,  
Yet still we seek, Lord, Your embrace.  
For if we forget the path You’ve shown,  
We wander far from the love we’ve known.  
So bind Your word upon our hearts,  
In every breath, let new life start.

Bridge  
When our children ask, “What does this mean?”  
We’ll tell of wonders, the unseen.  
From bondage lost to freedom gained,  
Your faithfulness, forever sustained.  
In every trial, in every test,  
We trust in You, Lord, for we are blessed.  
Your commandments guide us day by day,  
In Your love, Lord, we will stay.

Outro  
So we will walk, in Your light, O Lord,  
With faithful hearts, we lift our sword.  
In the land of promise, we find our rest,  
Forever guided, forever blessed.
