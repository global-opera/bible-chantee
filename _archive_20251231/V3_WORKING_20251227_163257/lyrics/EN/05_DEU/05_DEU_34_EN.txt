[TITLE]
The Mountain of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
From Moab’s plains, he climbed so high,  
To Pisgah’s peak beneath the sky.  
The Lord revealed the land so wide,  
A promise held, where dreams abide.  
Gilead’s grace, and Judah’s song,  
In every heart, where we belong.  
Yet Moses saw but could not tread,  
His journey here was nearly said.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the land of promise, shining bright,  
Given to the faithful, a glorious sight.  
Though he could not enter, he stood in awe,  
For the love of the Father, his heart would draw.  
In every whisper, in every prayer,  
The promise remains, our God is there.  

Verse 2:  
Moses, the servant, faithful and true,  
In valleys deep, his spirit grew.  
A hundred years, yet strong was he,  
With eyes that saw the victory.  
For thirty days, the people wept,  
In mourning deep, their promises kept.  
But God raised Joshua, wise and bold,  
To lead the way, as the story unfolds.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the land of promise, shining bright,  
Given to the faithful, a glorious sight.  
Though he could not enter, he stood in awe,  
For the love of the Father, his heart would draw.  
In every whisper, in every prayer,  
The promise remains, our God is there.  

Bridge:  
Face to face, he knew the Lord,  
In signs and wonders, the path was poured.  
With mighty hand, He led them through,  
Faithful forever, His words are true.  
We stand in awe of the tales of old,  
The covenant love that never grows cold.  

Chorus:  
Oh, the land of promise, shining bright,  
Given to the faithful, a glorious sight.  
Though he could not enter, he stood in awe,  
For the love of the Father, his heart would draw.  
In every whisper, in every prayer,  
The promise remains, our God is there.  

Outro:  
So let us climb, to heights unknown,  
With hearts united, His love our own.  
For every promise, we shall proclaim,  
In faith we walk, and glorify His name.
