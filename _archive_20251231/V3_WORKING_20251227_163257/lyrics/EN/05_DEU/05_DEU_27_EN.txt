[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From the mountains high, we lift our eyes,  
To the land of promise, where our hope lies.  
With mighty hands, we build with care,  
Great stones of faith, in this land we share.  
We’ll write the words, His law so true,  
In our hearts forever, Lord, we’ll follow You.

Chorus  
We are Your people, O Lord our God,  
With every command, we’ll walk where You trod.  
In the flow of milk and honey, we will sing,  
With joy in our hearts, our praises we bring.  
Amen, amen, we lift our voice,  
In covenant love, we rejoice!

Verse 2  
On Mount Ebal, we hear the call,  
To heed Your voice, Lord, we give our all.  
Curses and blessings echo through time,  
In Your holy presence, our hearts align.  
With stones of remembrance, we stand so bold,  
In the shadow of glory, Your promises told.

Bridge  
Cursed be the ways that lead us astray,  
We’ll stand in Your truth, Lord, day after day.  
With joy, we will offer our hearts as a song,  
Guided by Your love, where we belong.

The faithfulness of God, as His promise stands.

We are Your people, O Lord, guide our way.
