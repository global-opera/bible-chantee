[TITLE]
In the Midst of Us

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
Woe to the heart, that turns away,  
In the shadows, lost and led astray.  
Princes roar like lions fierce,  
Judgment calls, our souls it pierce.  
Yet, O Lord, You stand so near,  
Injustice fades, Your love draws near.  

[Chorus]  
Sing, O daughter of Zion, rise,  
Rejoice in the truth, lift up your eyes.  
Fear not, for the Lord is here,  
Mighty Savior, drawing near.  
He takes away our shame and strife,  
In His love, we find our life.  

[Verse 2]  
In the midst of pain, where sorrow dwells,  
You gather the broken, hear their cries and yells.  
A remnant pure, in trust we stand,  
With faithful hearts, we lift our hands.  
Your judgment shines like morning light,  
Bringing hope to the darkest night.  

[Chorus]  
Sing, O daughter of Zion, rise,  
Rejoice in the truth, lift up your eyes.  
Fear not, for the Lord is here,  
Mighty Savior, drawing near.  
He takes away our shame and strife,  
In His love, we find our life.  

[Bridge]  
You will gather all the lost,  
Undo the chains, no matter the cost.  
With joy, You will sing over us,  
In Your arms, Lord, we place our trust.  
Every tear we’ve cried, You’ll wipe away,  
In Your presence, we will stay.  

[Chorus]  
Sing, O daughter of Zion, rise,  
Rejoice in the truth, lift up your eyes.

[Outro]  
For in that day, we shall proclaim,  
Your love, O Lord, forever the same.  
With hearts united, we call Your name,  
In the midst of us, we’ll never be the same.
