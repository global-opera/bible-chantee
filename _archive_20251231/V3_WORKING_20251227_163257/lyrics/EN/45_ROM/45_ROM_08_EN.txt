[TITLE]
No Condemnation

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In Christ, there’s no condemnation,  
We walk by Spirit’s grace,  
The law of life has freed us,  
From sin's dark, grasping place.  
Your Son came down in likeness,  
To bear our every sin,  
Condemning death within us,  
New life in Him begins.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, we are led by Your Spirit,  
Sons and daughters of the King,  
In the hope of Your salvation,  
Our hearts together sing.  
With every trial, every sorrow,  
We rise, unshaken, bold,  
For nothing can separate us,  
From the love that makes us whole.  

[Verse 2]  
The flesh leads us to death’s shadow,  
But Spirit brings us peace,  
In every groan and longing,  
Your presence brings release.  
We are heirs of Your promise,  
Adopted by Your grace,  
Together in Your suffering,  
We wait to see Your face.

We’re more than conquerors, love’s our lot.
