[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the heart of Israel, Your promise stands,  
You have not cast away, but hold our hands.  
Through the shadows of doubt, we still believe,  
A remnant You’ve kept, in grace we receive.  

Chorus:  
O the depths of Your riches, wisdom divine,  
Your mercy, unending, forever we find.  
In the fall of Your people, salvation arose,  
Through the branches we’re grafted, Your love overflows.  

Verse 2:  
Elias cried out, alone in despair,  
Yet You whispered hope, with a faithful prayer.  
Seven thousand hearts, untouched by the night,  
You are our Deliverer, our guiding light.  

Chorus:  
O the depths of Your riches, wisdom divine,  
Your mercy, unending, forever we find.  
In the fall of Your people, salvation arose,  
Through the branches we’re grafted, Your love overflows.  

Bridge:  
For who has known the mind of the Lord?  
Who can counsel the One who holds all?  
From Him, through Him, to Him, all things belong,  
To Him be the glory, forever our song.  

Chorus:  
O the depths of Your riches, wisdom divine,  
Your mercy, unending, forever we find.  
In the fall of Your people, salvation arose,  
Through the branches we’re grafted, Your love overflows.  

Outro:  
All Israel shall be saved, as it is proclaimed,  
By Your grace, we are one, forever unashamed.  
O the depths of Your grace, how unsearchable still,  
In Your wisdom and mercy, we seek to fulfill.
