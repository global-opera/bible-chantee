[TITLE]
Called to His Purpose

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Here I stand, a servant true,  
Called by grace to worship You.  
From the seed of David’s line,  
You are Lord, forever mine.  
Your gospel, Lord, I proclaim,  
In Your power, I lift Your name.  

Chorus  
I’m not ashamed of the gospel light,  
For it is the power, my heart ignites.  
From faith to faith, Your truth unfolds,  
In every nation, Your love retold.  

Verse 2  
Thankful, Lord, for faith that shines,  
Across the earth, Your love entwines.  
In prayer, I lift each heart to You,  
Desiring grace, in all we do.  
Together, Lord, we share the call,  
In mutual faith, we rise and fall.  

Chorus  
I’m not ashamed of the gospel light,  
For it is the power, my heart ignites.  
From faith to faith, Your truth unfolds,  
In every nation, Your love retold.  

Bridge  
Creation sings of all You’ve made,  
Your glory shines, never to fade.  
Though hearts may wander, we seek Your face,  
In every trial, we find Your grace.  

Chorus  
I’m not ashamed of the gospel light,  
For it is the power, my heart ignites.  
From faith to faith, Your truth unfolds,  
In every nation, Your love retold.  

Outro  
So let us stand, in love unite,  
Proclaiming grace, in darkest night.  
For we are called, beloved and free,  
To share Your truth, eternally.
