[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the law, I was bound,  
A heart heavy, lost, and drowned.  
But You came, Lord, to set me free,  
In Your grace, I found my plea.  

Chorus  
I am free, free in Christ,  
Dead to the law, I’ve found new life.  
No more in chains, I rise and sing,  
In the Spirit, my heart takes wing.  

Verse 2  
The law was holy, just, and good,  
But it showed my sins where I once stood.  
Yet You brought light to my darkest place,  
Drawing me close, wrapped in Your grace.  

Chorus  
I am free, free in Christ,  
Dead to the law, I’ve found new life.  
No more in chains, I rise and sing,  
In the Spirit, my heart takes wing.  

Bridge  
O wretched man, who could save?  
You, Jesus, took my place,  
In my weakness, You are strong,  
With Your love, I belong.  

Verse 3  
Now I serve with a heart renewed,  
In the Spirit, my soul is imbued.  
What once was death, now brings forth fruit,  
In Your love, my roots take root.  

Chorus  
I am free, free in Christ,  
Dead to the law, I’ve found new life.  
No more in chains, I rise and sing,  
In the Spirit, my heart takes wing.  

Outro  
Thank You, Lord, for grace so true,  
In this freedom, I worship You.  
With every breath, my heart will cry,  
In You, my Savior, I will abide.
