[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
Oh Lord, I stand before Your grace,  
With humble heart, I seek Your face.  
In judging others, I find my shame,  
For I too walk in sin’s own name.  
Your truth, it pierces through the night,  
Reminds me, Lord, to walk in light.

Chorus:  
Inwardly Yours, my heart, my soul,  
Teach me Your ways, to make me whole.  
Not just a hearer, but a doer too,  
In Your goodness, Lord, I’ll follow You.

Verse 2:  
Your kindness leads me to repent,  
In mercy’s arms, my heart's content.  
No one’s exempt from Your holy call,  
Each deed will rise, we’ll answer all.  
For glory waits for those who seek,  
In patient love, Your truth we speak.

Chorus:  
Inwardly Yours, my heart, my soul,  
Teach me Your ways, to make me whole.  
Not just a hearer, but a doer too,  
In Your goodness, Lord, I’ll follow You.

Bridge:  
Oh, cleanse my heart, Lord, make it new,  
With every breath, I long for You.  
No more to judge, but to reflect,  
Your love, O God, I will protect.  
For in the secret, You will see,  
The truth revealed, You set me free.

Chorus:  
Inwardly Yours, my heart, my soul,  
Teach me Your ways, to make me whole.  
Not just a hearer, but a doer too,  
In Your goodness, Lord, I’ll follow You.

Outro:  
So let my life speak of Your grace,  
In every moment, I’ll seek Your face.  
Inwardly Yours, forever true,  
With every step, I’ll walk with You.
