[TITLE]  
“The Call to Obedience”

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of the valley, a voice calls me near,  
The Lord sent word to Samuel, a mission crystal clear.  
Anoint the one I’ve chosen, to lead with holy grace,  
Yet a heart turned from obedience, has lost its rightful place.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, to obey is better than the finest sacrifice,  
To hearken to Your whisper, Lord, is our true price.  
In the battles that we face, let our hearts be aligned,  
For rebellion is a shadow, that darkness leaves behind.

[Verse 2]  
Saul gathered all the people, two hundred thousand strong,  
But in the heat of conquest, he let the spoils prolong.  
“Spare the best,” he reasoned, “to offer up in praise,”  
Yet the echo of the Lord’s command, he chose to disobey.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, to obey is better than the finest sacrifice,  
To hearken to Your whisper, Lord, is our true price.  
In the battles that we face, let our hearts be aligned,  
For rebellion is a shadow, that darkness leaves behind.

[Bridge]  
“Saul, why did you falter? The Lord's command was clear,  
To wipe away the wicked, and cast away your fear.  
But you feared the voice of man, and turned your heart away,  
Now the kingdom's torn asunder, for you have lost your way.”  

[Verse 3]  
With tears Samuel cried out, the weight of grief was great,  
For the King had turned from heaven, and sealed his own fate.  
Yet in the midst of sorrow, redemption finds a way,  
For even in our failures, His love will still display.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, to obey is better than the finest sacrifice,  
To hearken to Your whisper, Lord, is our true price.  
In the battles that we face, let our hearts be aligned,  
For rebellion is a shadow, that darkness leaves behind.

[Outro]  
So let us heed the calling, with hearts that seek to serve,  
In every fight and struggle, may faith be our reserve.  
For the Lord will not abandon, His purpose will prevail,  
In obedience and worship, our hearts shall never fail.
