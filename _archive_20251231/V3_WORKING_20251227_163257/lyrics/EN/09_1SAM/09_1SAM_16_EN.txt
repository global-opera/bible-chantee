[TITLE]
The Heart of a King

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the night, Lord, you spoke to Samuel,  
“Fill your horn with oil, for I have chosen well.  
Do not mourn for what is lost, for I have a plan,  
A king will rise from Jesse’s line, by My own hand.”  

Chorus:  
You look beyond the outward, You see the heart within,  
In the fields and in the valleys, Your chosen ones begin.  
From the humble and the lowly, You raise the mighty high,  
In the heart of a king, Lord, let Your Spirit fly.  

Verse 2:  
The elders trembled at the sight, afraid of what would come,  
“Peaceably,” Samuel replied, “the sacrifice is done.”  
With each son that passed before him, eyes upon the height,  
You whispered to Your servant, “This is not the light.”  

Chorus:  
You look beyond the outward, You see the heart within,  
In the fields and in the valleys, Your chosen ones begin.  
From the humble and the lowly, You raise the mighty high,  
In the heart of a king, Lord, let Your Spirit fly.  

Bridge:  
Bring in the one who watches sheep, the young and the unknown,  
Anoint him with Your presence, let his faith be shown.  
For in the quiet moments, You prepare him for the fight,  
With a heart that knows Your love, he’ll walk in Your light.  

Verse 3:  
When the shadows fell on Saul, and darkness filled his soul,  
A skillful hand was sought to make the broken whole.  
David came with melody, a harp to calm the storm,  
In the presence of the King, Your peace began to form.  

Chorus:  
You look beyond the outward, You see the heart within,  
In the fields and in the valleys, Your chosen ones begin.  
From the humble and the lowly, You raise the mighty high,  
In the heart of a king, Lord, let Your Spirit fly.  

Outro:  
So let us rise and follow, where Your Spirit leads the way,  
In the heart of every shepherd, Your glory we display.  
Anointed for Your purpose, we’ll stand and testify,  
In the heart of a king, Lord, let Your Spirit fly.
