[TITLE]
Return to Peace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the valley where shadows loom,  
The armies gather, a sense of doom.  
But David stands, a heart so true,  
Faithful servant, he’ll see it through.  
Oh, the lords of the land, they raise their voice,  
“Make him turn back, let him make his choice.”  
Yet in the silence, he knows his fate,  
For the Lord is with him, he’ll not hesitate.

Chorus  
Return to peace, where the waters flow,  
In the midst of battle, Your love we know.  
When the giants rise and the shadows fall,  
In You, my Savior, I will stand tall.  
Return to peace, Your strength I’ll find,  
In every struggle, Your love will bind.  
Though the world may rage and the night may flee,  
In the arms of my Savior, I’m forever free.

Verse 2  
Achish spoke, “I’ve found no blame,  
You’ve walked with honor, you’ve earned your name.”  
Yet fear surrounds, the lords decree,  
“Let him not fight; he’s not ours to be.”  
But David cried, “What have I done?  
To stand beside me, is this not the one?”  
In every heartache, in every tear,  
The Lord’s hand guides, He’s always near.

Bridge  
So rise, O heart, in the morning light,  
With faith unshaken, we’ll conquer night.  
For the Lord of hosts, He goes before,  
In every challenge, we’ll rise and soar.  
With every step, His promise guides,  
In battle’s fury, with Him we abide.

Outro  
Return to peace, oh, return to grace,  
In the arms of my Savior, I find my place.  
With every battle, my heart will sing,  
Forever trusting in my King.
