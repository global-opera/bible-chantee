[TITLE]
The Anointed's Heart

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadows of the cave, where the wild goats roam,  
Saul sought to take my life, yet I stood alone.  
With my heart heavy, I saw him near,  
The one who chased my soul, gripped by fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, You are my refuge, my strength indeed,  
In the face of my enemy, You plant the seed.  
Though I could take revenge, I choose to stay my hand,  
For Your anointed, I will trust in Your command.  

Verse 2  
The whispers of my men, they urged me on,  
"Now is the time, your chance to be strong."  
But I remembered the promise, the call You gave,  
To honor the anointed, to love, not enslave.  

Chorus

Bridge  
In the midst of the battle, I see Your grace,  
Mercy over judgment, I’ll seek Your face.  
When wickedness surrounds, I will stand my ground,  
For the Lord will judge between us, in Him I'm found.  

Verse 3  
Saul wept as I spoke, "My lord, hear my plea,  
For I am not your foe, I seek to be free.  
Look at the skirt in my hand, I could have brought harm,  
But I chose the way of peace, with Your mighty arm."

Outro  
So let the world see, Your love reigns supreme,  
In battles and trials, I’ll cling to the dream.  
Though I walk through the valley, I’ll follow Your light,  
For I know, Lord, You are my guiding sight.
