Verse 1  
In the night, a whisper calls,  
Jonathan stands, defying the fall,  
With courage bold, he speaks the truth,  
To save a heart, to shield the youth.  
“Take heed, dear friend, the danger is near,  
But fear not, for God’s love is here.”  

Chorus  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
We find refuge, we find our place,  
When the storms of life arise,  
You’re our shelter, our safe skies.  
Though the javelins may fly,  
You lift us high, O Lord, we cry,  
In the shadow of Your grace,  
We are held, we are embraced.  

Verse 2  
As the war drums sound, the battle ignites,  
David stands strong, in the face of the fight.  
With faith unshaken, he meets the foe,  
The Lord of hosts, His power flows.  
Yet in the dark, a treacherous plan,  
But Michal’s heart, like a shield, will stand.
