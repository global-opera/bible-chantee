[TITLE]
Chosen for His Purpose

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the land of Benjamin, a man of might,  
Kish was his name, in the shadows of night.  
He sought for the lost, the treasures of old,  
And a son named Saul, with a heart brave and bold.  

Chorus:  
Chosen for His purpose, called to lead the way,  
In the quiet whispers, hear the Lord today.  
From the heights of promise, to the valleys low,  
He will guide the chosen, wherever they go.  

Verse 2:  
Through the hills of Ephraim, they wandered alone,  
Saul and his servant, their courage was shown.  
“Let’s seek the seer,” the servant did say,  
A man of God, leading us on the way.  

Chorus:  
Chosen for His purpose, called to lead the way,  
In the quiet whispers, hear the Lord today.  
From the heights of promise, to the valleys low,  
He will guide the chosen, wherever they go.  

Bridge:  
When Samuel saw Saul, the Lord spoke with grace,  
“This is the one, who will take his place.  
Fear not, oh my people, for I have heard your cry,  
Through the smallest of tribes, my love will not die.”  

Verse 3:  
In the high place they gathered, blessings to find,  
Saul took his seat, with the faithful aligned.  
A portion prepared, just for him to receive,  
In the heart of the humble, God’s plans we believe.  

Chorus:  
Chosen for His purpose, called to lead the way,  
In the quiet whispers, hear the Lord today.  
From the heights of promise, to the valleys low,  
He will guide the chosen, wherever they go.  

Outro:  
So rise up, O chosen, hear His voice anew,  
For in every journey, He is leading you.  
From the lost and the lonely, to the kings on high,  
He calls forth the faithful, with a love that won’t die.
