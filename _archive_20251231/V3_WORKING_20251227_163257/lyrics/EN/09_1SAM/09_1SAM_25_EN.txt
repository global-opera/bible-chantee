[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of sorrow, Samuel lay down,  
The people gathered ‘round, a heartbroken crown.  
But in the wilderness, David heard the call,  
For Nabal was unyielding, his heart so small.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
He sent his young men, with peace upon their lips,  
But Nabal answered harshly, with pride in his grips.  
Yet Abigail, with grace, saw the danger near,  
A woman of wisdom, she knew not to fear.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed be the Lord, who leads us in the night,  
With courage in our hearts, He turns wrong into right.  
When the battles rage, and darkness starts to fall,  
He brings forth a voice, a whisper through it all.  

[Verse 2]  
Abigail made haste, with gifts in her hands,  
Two hundred loaves of bread, and wine from distant lands.  
She rode down the hill, with purpose in her stride,  
To meet the King of hearts, and turn the tide.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
She fell before him, her humble spirit bowed,  
“Let not my lord shed blood, nor wear a vengeful shroud.  
For Nabal is a fool, and folly is his name,  
But let your heart be free, and rise above the shame.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed be the Lord, who leads us in the night,  
With courage in our hearts, He turns wrong into right.  
When the battles rage, and darkness starts to fall,  
He brings forth a voice, a whisper through it all.  

[Bridge]  
“Forgive the trespass, let peace be your way,  
For the Lord will fight your battles, each and every day.  
Your soul is bound in life, held by His great hand,  
A promise to the faithful, a hope we understand.”  

[Chorus]  
Oh, blessed be the Lord, who leads us in the night,  
With courage in our hearts, He turns wrong into right.  
When the battles rage, and darkness starts to fall,  
He brings forth a voice, a whisper through it all.  

[Outro]  
So let us rise in faith, with Abigail's grace,  
For wisdom leads us forward, to the Savior's embrace.  
In every trial faced, may our hearts be bold and true,  
For the Lord of all the earth, will always see us through.
