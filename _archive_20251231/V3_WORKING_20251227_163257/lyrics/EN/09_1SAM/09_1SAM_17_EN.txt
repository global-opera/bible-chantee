[TITLE]
In the Valley of Giants

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the valley where shadows loom,  
The armies stand with hearts in gloom,  
A giant roars, his voice like thunder,  
Fear grips the hearts, pulls them asunder.  
But in the silence, a shepherd hears,  
With faith unshaken, he casts out fears,  
"Who is this foe, that dares defy,  
The living God, who reigns on high?"

[Chorus]  
In the valley of giants, we will stand,  
With our eyes on the promise, in His hand,  
For the battle is Yours, O Lord divine,  
In the face of our giants, Your strength will shine.  
With a sling and a stone, we rise to fight,  
For the victory's ours, through Your might.

[Verse 2]  
David ran, with courage bold,  
The weight of the world, he would not hold.  
He spoke with the fire of heaven's breath,  
"For I come in the name of life, not death."  
With every step, the ground did shake,  
The hearts of the faithful began to wake.  
The giant laughed, but his pride would fall,  
For God would deliver, God would call.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
When the fears surround, and darkness looms,  
In the name of Jesus, we break through the gloom.  
Every chain is shattered, every wall must fall,  
For the Lord of Hosts is our battle call.  
So we lift our voices, we raise our song,  
In the face of the giants, we sing along.

[Outro]  
So let the world see, let the nations know,  
In the valley of giants, Your power will show.  
We stand unafraid, with hearts set free,  
For the battle belongs, O Lord, to Thee.
