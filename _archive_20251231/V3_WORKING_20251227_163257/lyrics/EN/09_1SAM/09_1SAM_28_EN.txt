[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of old, the armies drew near,  
The Philistines gathered, instilling great fear.  
Saul sought the counsel, but heaven was still,  
The echoes of silence, his heart did fulfill.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, in the valley of doubt,  
When shadows surround us, and fear calls us out.  
Though we may falter, our faith remains strong,  
In the shadows of Endor, we’ll sing our song.  

Verse 2  
Disguised in the darkness, he sought for the light,  
A woman of spirits brought forth the night.  
“Bring me Samuel,” he cried in despair,  
For the voice of the Lord was no longer there.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, in the valley of doubt,  
When shadows surround us, and fear calls us out.  
Though we may falter, our faith remains strong,  
In the shadows of Endor, we’ll sing our song.  

Bridge  
Though the kingdom has fallen, and darkness has grown,  
You, Lord, are our refuge, our heart’s steady stone.  
In battles we face, You give strength to the weak,  
In whispers of silence, it’s Your voice that we seek.  

Verse 3  
Samuel spoke words that echoed through time,  
“God has departed, for you’ve crossed the line.”  
Yet mercy abounds, even in the night,  
For those who are broken, He’ll bring forth the light.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, in the valley of doubt,  
When shadows surround us, and fear calls us out.  
Though we may falter, our faith remains strong,  
In the shadows of Endor, we’ll sing our song.  

Outro  
So rise up, O people, in faith we will stand,  
For the Lord is our Savior, our guide, our command.  
In every dark moment, His light will appear,  
In the shadows of Endor, our hearts will draw near.
