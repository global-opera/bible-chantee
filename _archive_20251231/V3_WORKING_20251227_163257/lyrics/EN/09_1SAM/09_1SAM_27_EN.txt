[TITLE]
In the Land of Refuge

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In shadows deep, where fear takes hold,  
David fled, his heart so bold,  
A king pursued, a life to spare,  
He sought a refuge, a place of care.  
To Gath he came, with men so brave,  
In the land of foes, his heart was saved.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, Lord, You are my fortress strong,  
In trials fierce, I sing my song.  
When the giants rise, and hope seems lost,  
I find my peace beneath Your cross.  
In the land of refuge, You are near,  
With every battle, I will not fear.  

[Verse 2]  
He sought a town, a humble place,  
To dwell in grace, to seek Your face.  
Achish granted, Ziklag’s ground,  
In foreign lands, Your love was found.  
Through every raid, with strength anew,  
You led him forth, his heart was true.

[Bridge]  
Though enemies surround, and darkness falls,  
Your light will guide me through it all.  
With every step, I trust in You,  
In every storm, You see me through.  
You turn my sorrows into song,  
In Your embrace, where I belong.
