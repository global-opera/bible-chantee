[TITLE]
The Roar from Zion

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From the hills of Tekoa, a voice breaks through,  
The Lord is calling, hear His truth anew.  
In the days of kings, in the shadows of might,  
He speaks of justice, His judgment is right.  

Chorus  
Oh, the Lord will roar from Zion,  
His voice shakes the land,  
He calls us to repentance,  
With a mighty hand.  
In the hearts of the weary,  
Let His word take flight,  
For the Lord will roar from Zion,  
Bringing darkness to light.  

Verse 2  
For the sins of nations, His fire will descend,  
From Gaza to Damascus, His message won't bend.  
Three transgressions counted, the scales are weighed,  
In His holy presence, the proud will be laid.  

Chorus

Bridge  
Hear the cries of the captives,  
Feel their silent plea,  
Lord, ignite a fire,  
In our hearts, make us free.  
With our eyes turned to heaven,  
Let our spirits ascend,  
For the roar of the Lord,  
Is a call to amend.

Outro

And His love covers all.
