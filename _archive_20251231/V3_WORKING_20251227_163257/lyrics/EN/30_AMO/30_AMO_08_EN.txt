[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
O Lord, I see a vision clear,  
A basket full of summer’s cheer,  
Yet heavy lies this solemn weight,  
For judgment comes upon our fate.  
The songs once sung in joyful praise,  
Turn into howls in darker days.  
The needy cry, their voices faint,  
While greed and pride our hearts do taint.

Chorus:  
Oh, let us mourn, let our hearts break,  
For every soul, Lord, for Your sake.  
We’ve wandered far from Your embrace,  
In silence now, we seek Your face.  
A famine not of bread we find,  
But hunger deep within our mind,  
Lord, speak to us, we long to hear,  
Restore our hearts and draw us near.

Verse 2:  
The sun shall set upon the noon,  
As darkness falls, we face our ruin.  
Our feasts turned bitter, joy replaced,  
With sackcloth on, we seek Your grace.  
The land, it trembles, shakes with fear,  
For every sin that brought us here.  
O hear our cries, we plead and pray,  
Revive our hearts, show us the way.

Bridge:  
From sea to sea, we search in vain,  
For Your Word, Lord, to break our chains.  
Let not our hearts be led astray,  
But guide us back to You, we pray.  
In every valley, in every night,  
Let Your truth be our guiding light.
