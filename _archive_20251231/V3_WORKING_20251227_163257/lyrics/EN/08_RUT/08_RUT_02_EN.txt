[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the fields of grace, where the harvest flows,  
A stranger comes, with a heart that knows,  
Naomi’s hope, in the land of her kin,  
With every step, new life begins.  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, Boaz, mighty man, from Bethlehem you stand,  
With kindness in your eyes, and a gentle hand.  
You see the one who’s lost, and you welcome her in,  
A story of redemption, where love will begin.  

Chorus  
Under His wings, where mercy reigns,  
In the arms of grace, we will never be the same.  
Though I am a stranger, I find my place,  
In the shelter of His love, I am saved by grace.  

Verse 2  
With every glean, I gather hope anew,  
Among the sheaves, I see the light of You.  
“Fear not,” you say, “stay close by my side,  
Drink from the well, in My love abide.”  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, Boaz, you provide, in the darkest night,  
The bounty of Your goodness, shines so bright.  
For every tear I’ve sown, a harvest will arise,  
In the fields of promise, I’ll lift my eyes.  

Chorus  
Under His wings, where mercy reigns,  
In the arms of grace, we will never be the same.  
Though I am a stranger, I find my place,  
In the shelter of His love, I am saved by grace.  

Bridge  
Blessed be the Lord, who sees our weary hearts,  
In every act of kindness, His love imparts.  
Ruth in the fields, with faith she will stand,  
In the hands of the Redeemer, we find our land.  

Chorus  
Under His wings, where mercy reigns,  
In the arms of grace, we will never be the same.  
Though I am a stranger, I find my place,  
In the shelter of His love, I am saved by grace.  

Outro  
So let us glean, from the fields of His love,  
With grateful hearts, we will rise above.  
In the story of Ruth, our faith will ignite,  
Under His wings, we walk in the light.
