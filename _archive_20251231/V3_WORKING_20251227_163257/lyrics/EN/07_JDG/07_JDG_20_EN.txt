## Title: As One We Stand

### Verse 1
From Dan to Beersheba, we gather as one,  
In the shadow of Gibeah, our battle's begun.  
With hearts united, we cry out in need,  
For the wickedness rising, we seek to be freed.  

### Chorus  
O Lord, guide our hands, lead us through the night,  
In the face of our trials, be our strength and light.  
Together we stand, as Your people we fight,  
With faith as our armor, we’ll trust in Your might.  

### Verse 2  
The Levite’s lament, his voice filled with pain,  
Tells of hearts broken, and honor lost in vain.  
We won’t turn away, our resolve is so clear,  
To cast out the darkness, to bring in the fear.

### Bridge  
In the heat of the battle, we come to Your throne,  
We weep for our brothers, no one stands alone.  
With prayers as our weapons, we seek Your command,  
For victory’s coming, united we stand.

### Outro  
So let the flames rise, let the smoke fill the sky,  
For in every struggle, Lord, You are nearby.  
As one we will march, with Your love as our guide,  
In the name of our Savior, in You we abide.
