[TITLE]
Rise Up, O Israel

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the land of promise, we stood so tall,  
But we turned from Your light, and we started to fall.  
Forsaking our Savior, we wandered astray,  
With hearts full of idols, we chose our own way.  

(Chorus)  
Rise up, O Israel, hear our cry,  
We’ve sinned against Heaven, we call to the sky.  
In the midst of our troubles, Lord, we seek Your embrace,  
Deliver us, Father, restore us by grace.  

(Verse 2)  
For eighteen long years, we were sorely oppressed,  
By the hands of our foes, in our time of distress.  
Yet we lifted our voices, with hearts broken free,  
Confessing our failings, we longed to be free.  

(Chorus)  
Rise up, O Israel, hear our cry,  
We’ve sinned against Heaven, we call to the sky.  
In the midst of our troubles, Lord, we seek Your embrace,  
Deliver us, Father, restore us by grace.  

(Bridge)  
You brought us from bondage, from darkness to light,  
With Your mighty hand, You’ve led us through the night.  
Remember Your promise, Your covenant true,  
We’ll turn from our idols, and worship anew.  

(Verse 3)  
In the face of the battle, we gather in prayer,  
With courage and faith, we rise up, aware.  
For the Lord is our strength, our refuge and guide,  
In the heart of the storm, we will stand by Your side.  

(Chorus)  
Rise up, O Israel, hear our cry,  
We’ve sinned against Heaven, we call to the sky.  
In the midst of our troubles, Lord, we seek Your embrace,  
Deliver us, Father, restore us by grace.  

(Outro)  
So we stand united, our hearts in one place,  
With the hope of redemption, we bask in Your grace.  
Rise up, O Israel, let our voices proclaim,  
Our faith is our fortress, forever Your name.
