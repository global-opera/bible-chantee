Verse 1  
In the land where shadows linger,  
Samson's heart was drawn to her,  
A woman from the Philistines,  
A choice that made his parents stir.  
“Is there none among our kind?”  
Yet the Lord had plans divine,  
For in the depths of danger,  
A victory He would design.

Chorus  
Out of the strong comes sweetness,  
From the lion's roar, a feast,  
In the darkness, light is shining,  
In our battles, You are peace.  
Though the world may challenge boldly,  
With Your Spirit, we will rise,  
Out of the strong comes sweetness,  
In Your grace, our hope survives.

Verse 2  
Through the vineyards, he walked on,  
A lion's might, fierce and bold,  
But the Spirit came upon him,  
Strength to conquer, brave and bold.  
He found honey in the carcass,  
A sign of life from what was slain,  
With joy, he shared the sweetness,  
In the struggle, joy remains.
