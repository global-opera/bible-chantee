[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
When Israel turned from Your ways,  
In shadows deep, they lost their place,  
Cried out to You, O Lord of might,  
In bondage held, they sought the Light.  
You raised a voice, a prophetess,  
Deborah stood, in faithfulness.  
With courage bold, she called the brave,  
To mount Tabor, their hearts to save.

Chorus:  
O Lord, You go before us,  
In battles fierce, we are not alone.  
With faith, we rise and trust Your Word,  
For You deliver, our hearts made whole.  
A mighty hand, against the foe,  
Your victory, the story told.  
In every struggle, we shall stand,  
For our Redeemer leads our band.

Verse 2:  
Ten thousand strong, they took their place,  
With Deborah, they sought Your grace.  
The chariots of iron roared like thunder,  
But You, O God, would tear asunder.  
"Up now!" she cried, "The Lord has come,  
This day, the battle shall be won."  
With swords ablaze and hearts aflame,  
They marched ahead, in Jesus’ name.

Bridge:  
Jael the brave, in silence crept,  
With courage strong, while Sisera slept.  
The nail in hand, she struck with might,  
And darkness fell, to dawn’s new light.  
O Lord, You bring our enemies down,  
In You, we find our solid ground.  
We sing of battles fought and won,  
For in our hearts, Your will be done.

Outro:  
So let us lift our voices high,  
In every trial, we shall rely.  
For God who fought, and won the day,  
In Him alone, we find our way.  
O Lord, our strength, our guiding light,  
In every battle, You are our might.  
We stand in faith, forever true,  
Our hearts rejoice, for we trust You.
