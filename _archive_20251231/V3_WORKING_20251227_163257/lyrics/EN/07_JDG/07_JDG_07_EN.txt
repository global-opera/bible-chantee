[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the valley low, where the shadows creep,  
Gideon stood with a heart so deep,  
The Midianites like grasshoppers lay,  
But the Lord spoke, guiding the way.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
“Fear not, my child, for I am near,  
With a mighty hand, I will draw near.  
You have too many; let the fearful go,  
Trust in my strength, let your courage grow.”  

[Chorus]  
With the sword of the Lord and Gideon’s might,  
We stand in the battle, ready to fight.  
Raise your trumpets high, let the darkness flee,  
For our God is with us, we claim victory!  

[Verse 2]  
Three hundred strong, against the tide,  
With pitchers and lamps, and faith as our guide.  
The dream was revealed, the fear was cast,  
In the midst of the night, our hope held fast.  

[Pre-Chorus]  
“Arise, my people, the time is here,  
With joy in your hearts, let go of your fear.  
The enemy trembles, their end is near,  
For I am your God; I am always here.”  

[Chorus]  
With the sword of the Lord and Gideon’s might,  
We stand in the battle, ready to fight.  
Raise your trumpets high, let the darkness flee,  
For our God is with us, we claim victory!  

[Bridge]  
Through the night we march, in His name we trust,  
Every wall must fall, every fear turns to dust.  
In our hearts, a fire, ignited by grace,  
With faith as our anchor, we boldly embrace.  

[Chorus]  
With the sword of the Lord and Gideon’s might,  
We stand in the battle, ready to fight.  
Raise your trumpets high, let the darkness flee,  
For our God is with us, we claim victory!  

[Outro]  
So let the world know, in His name we stand,  
With the sword of the Lord, we take back the land.  
Gideon’s story lives on through the ages,  
In the heart of the faithful, His power engages.
