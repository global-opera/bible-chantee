[TITLE]
Hearts of Israel

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the gate, Absalom stood so bold,  
Chariots and horses, a heart that turned to gold.  
He spoke to those in need, with a voice so sweet and fair,  
“Your matters are of justice, but where's the king to care?”  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, when the hearts of men are swayed,  
In the battles of our trust, where the light begins to fade.  
Guide us through the storm, when our spirits start to roam,  
For in Your hands, O Lord, we find our way back home.  

Verse 2:  
Forty years had passed, he sought to claim the throne,  
In Hebron's sacred ground, his vow began to grow.  
With whispers of a trumpet, the people gathered round,  
But the truth lay hidden, as their faith was unbound.  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
David wept in silence, barefoot on the way,  
With the ark of the covenant, he prayed for brighter days.  
“Turn the counsel to foolishness,” his heart cried out to Thee,  
In the wilderness he worshipped, seeking Your decree.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, when the hearts of men are swayed,  
In the battles of our trust, where the light begins to fade.

Outro:  
So let us lift our voices, in worship and in grace,  
For the battle belongs to You, as we seek Your face.  
In the echoes of our hearts, Your truth will ever reign,  
Through the trials and the triumphs, Your love will not be vain.
