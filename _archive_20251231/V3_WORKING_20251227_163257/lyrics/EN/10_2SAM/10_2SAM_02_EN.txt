Verse 1:  
In the land of Judah, a call from above,  
David sought wisdom, led by Your love.  
“Shall I rise to the cities, O Lord, guide my way?”  
You answered his heart, “Go up to Hebron today.”

King of our hearts, reign over us,

Through trials and shadows, You lead us still,
