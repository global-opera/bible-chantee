[LYRICS]
[TITLE] The Dance of the Ark

[Verse 1]  
From Baale of Judah, we rise,  
With chosen hearts, we seek the skies,  
To bring the Ark, our sacred prize,  
The LORD of Hosts, our battle cries.  
Upon a cart, we carry forth,  
The presence of our God, our worth,  
With instruments of joy and praise,  
We lift His name in endless ways.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, we dance before You,  
With every heart, we sing anew,  
In the midst of joy, our spirits soar,  
Together, Lord, forevermore.  
Blessed are we in Your embrace,  
As we behold Your holy face,  
The Ark of God, our guiding light,  
In every step, we find our might.

[Verse 2]  
But at the threshing floor, a hand,  
To steady the Ark, a grave command.  
Your holy wrath, we cannot stand,  
For in our hearts, we understand.  
Yet, in the house of Obededom,  
Your blessings flow, a welcome home.  
For three long months, Your grace did shine,  
And now, we come, our hearts align.

[Chorus]  
O Lord, we dance before You,  
With every heart, we sing anew,  
In the midst of joy, our spirits soar,  
Together, Lord, forevermore.  
Blessed are we in Your embrace,  
As we behold Your holy face,  
The Ark of God, our guiding light,  
In every step, we find our might.

[Bridge]  
With every six paces, we lift our praise,  
A sacrifice of love, our hearts ablaze.  
David danced in linen, unashamed and free,  
For it’s in Your presence, Lord, we long to be.  
Though others may scorn, we will not hide,  
For in the joy of the Lord, our hearts abide.  
With every beat, our spirits rise,  
In the dance of the Ark, we find our prize.

[Chorus]

[Outro]  
So we will gather, hand in hand,  
In reverence, united we stand,  
With joyful hearts, we sing Your song,  
In the dance of the Ark, we all belong.
