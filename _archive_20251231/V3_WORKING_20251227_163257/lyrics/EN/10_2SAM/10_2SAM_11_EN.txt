[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the twilight of the king's great hall,  
David wandered, a heart to enthrall.  
With power and glory, he stood so tall,  
Yet in the shadows, he began to fall.  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, Bathsheba, a beauty so rare,  
From the roof he gazed, lust filled the air.  
In his restless heart, temptation grew,  
What was once a king, now lost and untrue.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, for we’ve strayed away,  
In the battles of our hearts, we need Your grace today.  
Restore our brokenness, heal our deepest wounds,  
For in the ruins of our hearts, Your love breaks through.  

Verse 2  
Uriah, loyal, stood firm in the fight,  
While David's deceit cast a shadow of night.  
With a letter of death, betrayal he penned,  
A warrior's life lost, a friendship's end.  

Pre-Chorus  
Oh, Bathsheba, we weep for the cost,  
For in every sin, a soul can be lost.  
In the silence of night, the cries go unheard,  
Yet mercy awaits, through the dark, it is stirred.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, for we’ve strayed away,  
In the battles of our hearts, we need Your grace today.  
Restore our brokenness, heal our deepest wounds,  
For in the ruins of our hearts, Your love breaks through.  

Bridge  
In the depths of despair, we lift our eyes high,  
For Your mercy, oh Lord, can never run dry.  
Though we wander like sheep, lost in our pride,  
Bring us back to the fold, where Your love will abide.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cry, for we’ve strayed away,  
In the battles of our hearts, we need Your grace today.  
Restore our brokenness, heal our deepest wounds,

Outro  
With each step we take, may we seek Your face,  
In the story of grace, let us find our place.  
For though we stumble, Your light will renew,  
In the ruins of our hearts, Lord, we turn to You.
