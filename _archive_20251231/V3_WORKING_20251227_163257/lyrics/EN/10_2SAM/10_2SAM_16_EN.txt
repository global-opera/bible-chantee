Verse 1  
In the shadow of the hill, O King,  
With bread and wine, Ziba came to bring,  
A humble heart, he sought your grace,  
While whispers of betrayal filled the place.  
But David stood, though weary and worn,  
With faith in the Lord, his spirit reborn.  

Chorus  
In the midst of trials, we’ll lift our eyes,  
Though the storms may rage, our hope will rise.  
For the Lord is with us, through every fight,  
His love will lead us, our guiding light.  

Verse 2  
And Shimei cursed, casting stones in scorn,  
Yet David’s heart, in mercy was adorned.  
“Let him speak,” he said, “for God has willed,  
In my affliction, His purpose is fulfilled.”  
With every curse, he sought the peace,  
In the midst of chaos, his soul found release.
