[TITLE]
Restoration of the House

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of Jehoash, a king so true,  
Guided by the priest, with wisdom anew.  
He saw the breaches, the temple in need,  
A heart for the house, he took the lead.  

Chorus:  
Oh, restore the house, let our praises rise,  
With every offering, we lift to the skies.  
In faith we gather, united we stand,  
To mend the broken, with a faithful hand.  

Verse 2:  
The treasures of old, laid at Your feet,  
Every heart's desire, in this holy seat.  
From the chest they gathered, the gold and the grace,  
Rebuilding the altar, a sacred place.  

Chorus:  
Oh, restore the house, let our praises rise,  
With every offering, we lift to the skies.  
In faith we gather, united we stand,  
To mend the broken, with a faithful hand.  

Bridge:  
When the storms arise, and the battles rage,  
We will not falter; we'll turn the page.  
For in Your promise, we find our strength,  
Restoration flows, from Your love's great length.  

Chorus:  
Oh, restore the house, let our praises rise,  
With every offering, we lift to the skies.  
In faith we gather, united we stand,  
To mend the broken, with a faithful hand.  

Outro:  
So let the walls be filled with songs of grace,  
As we seek Your heart, in this sacred space.  
In the name of Jesus, we rise and proclaim,  
The restoration of Your house, forever Your name.
