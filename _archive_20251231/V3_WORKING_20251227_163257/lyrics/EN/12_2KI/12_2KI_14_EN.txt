[TITLE]
In the Shadow of Kings

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the days of old, when kings would rise,  
Amaziah took his place beneath the skies.  
Twenty-five years, a heart made bold,  
Yet in the high places, the truth untold.  
He fought for the land, he stood for the right,  
But shadows lingered in the fading light.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we lift our hands to You,  
In every battle, we trust in what is true.  
Your Word, our guide through every storm and night,  
In the shadow of kings, we seek Your light.

Verse 2  
Jehoash called him forth, a challenge laid,  
“Do not let pride lead you to be dismayed.”  
But Amaziah’s heart, it would not yield,  
To the wisdom spoken, his fate was sealed.  
At Bethshemesh they met, the clash was fierce,  
But Judah fell, and Israel pierced.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we lift our hands to You,  
In every battle, we trust in what is true.  
Your Word, our guide through every storm and night,  
In the shadow of kings, we seek Your light.

Bridge  
Fathers and sons, we stand in grace,  
For every sin, we seek Your face.  
In battles fought and lessons learned,  
Your mercy reigns, our hearts returned.  
Though kingdoms rise and kingdoms fall,  
Your love remains, it conquers all.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we lift our hands to You,  
In every battle, we trust in what is true.  
Your Word, our guide through every storm and night,  
In the shadow of kings, we seek Your light.

Outro  
From the ashes of strife, we rise again,  
With faith in our hearts, we’ll trust ‘til the end.  
For every story told, His promise we cling,  
In the shadow of kings, our praises we sing.
