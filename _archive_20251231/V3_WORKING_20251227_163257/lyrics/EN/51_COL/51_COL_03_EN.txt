[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
When we are risen with the Son,  
We set our hearts on what is won,  
In glory with our Savior’s name,  
Where Christ, our life, forever reigns.  
Let go of chains that held us fast,  
Put off the old, embrace at last,  
For in His love, we are renewed,  
In Him alone, our souls are viewed.

Chorus:  
Seek the things that are above,  
Where mercy flows and peace is love.  
In Christ, we find our home,  
With hearts united, we are known.  
Let His word dwell deep within,  
In every song, our lives begin.  
We give thanks, we lift our praise,  
For in His light, our spirits blaze.

Verse 2:  
No anger, wrath, or bitter strife,  
But kindness, love, and holy life.  
Forgiving as He forgave,  
In every heart, His grace we crave.  
In unity, we stand as one,  
No Jew, no Greek, we all are done,  
With love that binds, our hearts will soar,  
In Christ, our hope forevermore.

Bridge:
