[TITLE]
Hope in You, Our Glory

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the grace from God our Father,  
Peace flows like a river wide,  
We give thanks for faith unshaken,  
Love that binds us side by side.  
Oh, the hope laid up in heaven,  
A truth we hold so dear,  
In the Word of life eternal,  
Your promise draws us near.  

Chorus  
Christ in us, our hope of glory,  
In Your light, we stand renewed.  
By Your blood, we find redemption,  
In Your love, our hearts imbued.  
We walk worthy, filled with wisdom,  
Bearing fruit in all we do,  
Strengthened by Your mighty power,  
Lord, our hope is found in You.  

Verse 2  
Epaphras, a faithful servant,  
Brings a message from the heart,  
Joyful prayers we lift before You,  
Longing, Lord, for wisdom's art.  
In all patience, we find courage,  
Your Spirit leads us through the night,  
In the body of Your church, Lord,  
You are our guiding light.

Bridge  
You’ve delivered us from darkness,  
Into the kingdom of Your Son,  
For by Him, all things were created,  
In Him, our victory is won.  
Present us, Lord, unblameable,  
In Your sight, we find our place,  
Grounded in Your truth forever,  
Filled with hope and boundless grace.

All our hope is found in You.
