[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the stillness of Your presence, Lord,  
We find the peace that You have stored,  
A promise left for those who seek,  
To enter in, so bold and meek.

Chorus  
Oh, let us hear Your voice today,  
And not harden hearts, but choose to stay,  
For in Your rest, our souls are free,  
We cease from striving, Lord, in Thee.

Verse 2

But we who believe, we find our place,  
In You, our refuge, we seek Your grace.

Chorus  
Oh, let us hear Your voice today,  
And not harden hearts, but choose to stay,  
For in Your rest, our souls are free,  
We cease from striving, Lord, in Thee.

Bridge  
Great High Priest, we come to Thee,  
Touched by our pain, You set us free.  
Boldly we approach Your throne of grace,  
Finding mercy in Your warm embrace.

Chorus  
Oh, let us hear Your voice today,

Outro  
Today we enter, today we find,  
The rest that’s promised, peace of mind.  
In You, our Savior, forever blessed,
