Verse 1  
In the shadows of the crowd, a heart so lost and low,  
Zacchaeus climbed the sycamore, longing just to know.  
He sought to see the Savior, though the world stood in his way,  
But in the branches high above, he found his hope today.  

Chorus  
Come down, Zacchaeus, for I must abide,  
In your humble home, I’ll be your guide.  
Salvation’s drawing near, joy will fill this place,  
Oh, the Son of Man has come to save by grace.  

Verse 2  
With murmurs all around him, they questioned who he was,  
A sinner in their eyes, but Jesus saw his cause.  
“Half of all I own, Lord, I’ll give to the poor,  
And if I’ve wronged a soul, I’ll restore and more.”

Outro  
So climb your tree of doubt, and reach for heaven’s light,  
For Jesus calls you down, to walk with Him tonight.
