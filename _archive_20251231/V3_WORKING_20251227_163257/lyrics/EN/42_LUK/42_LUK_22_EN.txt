[TITLE]
In the Upper Room

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the night,  
Gathered ‘round the table,  
With a longing in Your heart,  
You shared the truth so stable.  
You broke the bread, You blessed the cup,  
For us, You laid Your life down,  
In this moment, love poured out,  
A Savior’s grace in the crown.  

Chorus  
In the upper room we find,  
A love that knows no bounds,  
In the whisper of Your voice,  
Our hope and peace resound.  
Though betrayal waits outside,  
Your spirit still remains,  
In this sacred place we bow,  
Forever we proclaim.  

Verse 2  
Judas kissed with treachery,  
But Your love would never fade,  
You prayed for us in agony,  
For the price of sin was paid.  
Though the darkness closed around,  
You stood firm in the night,  
With each tear and every prayer,  
You would bring us into light.
