[TITLE]
Wisdom's Call

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night, I seek Your face,  
With a heart that longs for truth, Lord, show me grace.  
For the path of the wise is a treasure to behold,  
In Your word, my spirit finds the riches of old.

Chorus  
Oh, lead me, Lord, in the ways of Your might,  
Through the shadows, You will be my guiding light.  
In integrity, I’ll walk, my heart pure and true,  
With every step I take, I will trust in You.

Verse 2  
Let me cease from strife, let my soul find peace,  
In the counsel of the wise, may my worries cease.  
For a faithful man is rare, a jewel in Your sight,  
Help me to be steadfast, shining Your light.

Chorus  
Oh, lead me, Lord, in the ways of Your might,  
Through the shadows, You will be my guiding light.  
In integrity, I’ll walk, my heart pure and true,  
With every step I take, I will trust in You.

Bridge  
Who can cleanse the heart, who can make it whole?  
Only You, O Lord, can renew my soul.  
With mercy and truth, You uphold the throne,  
In Your loving arms, I am never alone.

Verse 3  
When the tempter calls, and deceit draws near,  
I’ll wait on You, Lord, for I know You hear.  
Through the trials I face, let my faith be strong,  
In the wisdom of the ages, I will sing my song.

Chorus  
Oh, lead me, Lord, in the ways of Your might,  
Through the shadows, You will be my guiding light.  
In integrity, I’ll walk, my heart pure and true,  
With every step I take, I will trust in You.

Outro  
In the stillness of my heart, let Your wisdom flow,  
For in You, Lord Jesus, my spirit will grow.  
With each breath I take, I will honor Your name,  
In the journey of my life, forever the same.
