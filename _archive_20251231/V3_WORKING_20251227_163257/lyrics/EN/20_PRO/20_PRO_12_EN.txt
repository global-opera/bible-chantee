Verse 1  
In the heart that loves instruction,  
Knowledge flows like living streams,  
A good man finds favor in Your sight,  
While the wicked chase their schemes.  
The roots of the righteous stand so firm,  
In Your grace, Lord, we find our term.

Chorus  
Guide us on the path of righteousness,  
Where Your truth will lead us home,  
In the light of Your wisdom,  
We will never walk alone.  
In the way of life, we find our peace,  
In the pathway of righteousness, we cease.
