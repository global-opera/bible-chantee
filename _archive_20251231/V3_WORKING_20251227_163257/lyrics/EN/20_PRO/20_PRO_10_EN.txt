[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the quiet of the morning light,  
A wise son brings a father's delight,  
But a foolish heart brings sorrow to the day,  
Oh, Lord, guide our hearts, show us the way.  

Chorus:  
Righteousness, O Lord, it leads to life,  
Your blessings fill our souls, dispel our strife.  
In the way of the just, our hearts shall find,  
A well of life, Your wisdom intertwined.  

Verse 2:  
Treasures of wickedness fade away,  
But the diligent hand finds wealth to stay.  
In the summer's harvest, let us not sleep,  
For the fruits of Your labor, Lord, we seek.  

Chorus:  
Righteousness, O Lord, it leads to life,  
Your blessings fill our souls, dispel our strife.  
In the way of the just, our hearts shall find,  
A well of life, Your wisdom intertwined.  

Bridge:  
Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all,  
In the lips of the wise, let our praises call.  
Teach us, Lord, to walk in Your light,  
In the way of the righteous, we’ll stand upright.  

Verse 3:  
The memory of the just is blessed and bright,  
While the name of the wicked fades into night.  
Let our hearts be pure, our lips speak true,  
In the fear of the Lord, we trust in You.  

Chorus:  
Righteousness, O Lord, it leads to life,  
Your blessings fill our souls, dispel our strife.  
In the way of the just, our hearts shall find,  
A well of life, Your wisdom intertwined.  

Outro:  
As the whirlwind passes, the wicked shall cease,  
But the righteous stand firm, in Your peace.  
Guide us in wisdom, in love and grace,  
Lord, lead us forever in Your embrace.
