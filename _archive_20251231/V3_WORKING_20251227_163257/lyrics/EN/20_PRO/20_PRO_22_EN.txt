Verse 1  
A name worth more than riches, Lord,  
In humble hearts, we find our way,  
For treasures fade, but love endures,  
In grace, we seek to walk each day.  

Chorus  
Oh, teach us, Lord, to sow in love,  
To lift the poor, to share our bread,  
In wisdom’s light, we rise above,  
Your truth, our guide, where'er we tread.

Let not our hearts be led astray,  
From paths of peace and pure delight,  
In quiet trust, we humbly pray,  
Preserve our souls, O Lord, our Light.

That they may stand when trials come,  
In all our seeking, Lord, we praise,  
For in Your presence, we are home.
