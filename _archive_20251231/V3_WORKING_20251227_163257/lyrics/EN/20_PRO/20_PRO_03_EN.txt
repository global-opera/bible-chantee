[TITLE]
Paths of Wisdom

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Oh, my heart, don’t forget His ways,  
In His law find peace and grace.  
Length of days and life anew,  
In His truth, He’ll guide us through.  

Chorus  
Trust in the Lord with all your heart,  
Lean not on your own understanding.  
In all your ways, acknowledge Him,  
He’ll direct your paths, His love commanding.  

Verse 2  
Bind His mercy round your neck,  
Write His truth upon your heart.  
Favor found in God’s embrace,  
In His wisdom, we’ll find our place.  

Chorus  
Trust in the Lord with all your heart,  
Lean not on your own understanding.  
In all your ways, acknowledge Him,  
He’ll direct your paths, His love commanding.  

Bridge  
Happy is the one who seeks,  
In wisdom’s arms, we find our peace.  
More precious than gold and rubies bright,  
In her ways, we walk in light.  

Verse 3  
When shadows fall, He’s our delight,  
No fear shall come, His love is right.  
Sleep in peace, for He’s our stay,  
In the night, He lights our way.  

Chorus  
Trust in the Lord with all your heart,  
Lean not on your own understanding.  
In all your ways, acknowledge Him,  
He’ll direct your paths, His love commanding.  

Outro  
With every step, we’ll follow true,  
In wisdom’s grace, we’ll renew.  
Honor Him with all we do,  
In His love, we are made new.
