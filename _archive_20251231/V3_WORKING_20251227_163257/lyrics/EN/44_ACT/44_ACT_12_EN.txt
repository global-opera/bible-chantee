### Title: "Chains Fall Away"

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
In the darkness, hope takes flight,  
Peter bound, yet faith remains,  
The church is praying through the pain.  
With every cry, a heart's refrain,  
Lord, deliver, break these chains.  

Chorus  
Chains fall away, when we call Your name,  
You send Your light to break the night,  
In the midst of fear, You draw us near,  
Our God, our Savior, forever reigns.  
Chains fall away, with a mighty hand,  
You lift the broken, You take our stand,  
In our darkest hour, in Your power,  
We rise again, forever free to stand.  

Verse 2  
An angel's touch, a whispered grace,  
“Arise, my child, it’s time to face,  
The gates of doubt, the walls of despair,  
I’ve set you free, My love is there.”  
Through the night, the path is clear,  
With every step, Your presence near.

Bridge  
We gather in Your name, united in prayer,  
Like Peter at the door, we know You’re always there.  
With hearts of joy, we lift our voice,  
In every trial, we will rejoice.  
The Word of God grows, it will never cease,  
In every heart, Your love brings peace.

Our God is here, our chains fall away.
