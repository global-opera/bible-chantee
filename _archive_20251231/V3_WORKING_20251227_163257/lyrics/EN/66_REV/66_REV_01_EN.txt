[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the silence of Patmos, I heard a voice so clear,  
A trumpet call of glory, drawing all to hear.  
“Write the words of prophecy, for the time is at hand,  
Blessed are those who listen, who keep the Lord’s command.”  

Chorus:  
Alpha and Omega, beginning and the end,  
The One who loves and washes, our Savior and our Friend.  
Every eye will see Him, in clouds of majesty,  
He reigns as King forever, our hope and victory.  

Verse 2:  
From the throne flows grace and peace, to all the faithful ones,  
In the midst of golden candlesticks, the righteous Light, He shone.  
His eyes like flames of fire, His voice like mighty seas,  
He holds the keys to death and hell, our hearts are set at ease.

Bridge:  
Fear not, for I am with you, I am the first and last,  
I lived and died for you, and I hold the future fast.  
Kings and priests before Him, in His grace we stand,  
To Him be all the glory, united in His plan.

Outro:  
He is the Almighty, who was and is to come,  
In every heart, we gather, proclaiming Christ, our Son.  
Alpha and Omega, forever we will sing,  
To Him be all the glory, our Savior, Lord, and King.
