[TITLE]
The Fall of Babylon

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From the heavens, an angel descends,  
With power that brightens the earth's darkened ends,  
He proclaims with a voice of thunderous might,  
“Babylon's fallen, consumed by the light.”  

Chorus  
Oh, come out, my people, from her wicked ways,  
Do not partake in her darkened days.  
For her sins have reached to the skies above,  
Yet in His mercy, He calls us to love.  

Verse 2  
Her riches are fleeting, her splendor a lie,  
Kings mourn her downfall, they weep and they sigh.  
Once clothed in glory, now ashes and smoke,  
In one hour, her empire is broke.  

Chorus  
Oh, come out, my people, from her wicked ways,  
Do not partake in her darkened days.  
For her sins have reached to the skies above,  
Yet in His mercy, He calls us to love.  

Bridge  
Rejoice, O heavens, for justice is near,  
The cries of the faithful, our voices we clear.  
Her sorceries shattered, her power undone,  
In the light of the Savior, our victory's won.  

Verse 3  
No more the sound of the harpers will play,  
The bride and the bridegroom have turned from the fray.  
In her was the blood of the prophets and saints,  
Now we raise our songs, our hearts free from chains.  

Chorus  
Oh, come out, my people, from her wicked ways,  
Do not partake in her darkened days.  
For her sins have reached to the skies above,  
Yet in His mercy, He calls us to love.  

Outro  
So we stand in His glory, our voices as one,  
In the light of His truth, our new life begun.  
Babylon's fallen, and we shall be free,  
Forever in His presence, in victory.
