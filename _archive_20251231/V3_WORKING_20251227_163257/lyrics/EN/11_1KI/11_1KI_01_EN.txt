[TITLE]
The King's Promise

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the twilight of David's reign,  
As the shadows start to fall,  
A promise made, a heart unchained,  
To his son, he heeds the call.  
Bathsheba bows, her heart in prayer,  
“Lord, remember what You’ve spoken,  
For Adonijah’s reign fills the air,  
Yet Your word shall not be broken.”

(Chorus)  
God save the king, the chosen one,  
Anointed by Your hand,  
With joy we sing, let Your will be done,  
In this sacred land.  
From the throne of David’s line,  
Solomon shall rise and shine,  
God save the king, our hearts align,  
In Your promise divine.

(Verse 2)  
Zadok brings the oil of grace,  
Nathan’s voice, a guiding light,  
The people gather, hearts embrace,  
In Gihon, all is made right.  
With the trumpet’s sound, the earth rejoices,  
A new dawn breaks, the heavens sing,  
United in our faithful choices,  
We lift our praise to the King of Kings.

(Bridge)  
Fear not, O Israel, for the Lord is near,  
In every battle, He calms our fear.  
Adonijah trembles, but we stand secure,  
For God’s own plan, will ever endure.  
With mighty men and hearts ablaze,  
We trust in You, through all our days.

As the sun sets on this sacred night,  
We declare Your faithfulness, shining bright.  
With every heart, we lift our voice,  
In Your kingdom, we rejoice!
