### Title: "The Brook and the Widow"

Verse 1  
In the land where drought had come,  
Elijah stood before the throne,  
With a word from God, he proclaimed the truth,  
No dew, no rain, by his word alone.  
So he fled to Cherith's stream,  
Where the ravens brought their feast,  
In the silence of the wilderness,  
God's provision never ceased.

Chorus  
Oh, the Lord provides, when we trust and abide,  
In our darkest hour, His hand will guide.  
From the brook to the widow’s need,  
Faith will rise, and we will see,  
The miracle of grace in every heart that believes.

Verse 2  
To Zarephath, he was sent,  
A widow gathering sticks in despair,  
He called her to bring a morsel,  
For her faith was the answer to prayer.  
In her hands, a handful of meal,  
Yet she chose to give, and thus revealed,  
The barrel won’t waste, the oil won’t cease,  
In her trust, the Lord’s peace.

Bridge  
When the son fell sick and lost his breath,  
She cried out in sorrow, facing death.  
Elijah prayed, “O Lord, hear my plea,  
Bring back this child, restore him to me.”  
And the Lord heard the voice of His man,  
Reviving the boy by His mighty hand.  
Now she knows, with her heart set free,  
The truth of the Lord lives eternally.

Outro  
So we stand in awe, with our voices raised high,  
For the God of Elijah hears every cry.  
In the trials we face, His love remains,  
With faith as our anchor, we’ll never be the same.  
Oh, the Lord provides, in every season,  
Trust in His word, for He is our reason.
