[TITLE]
The God Who Answers

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In a land where the drought had come,  
Elijah stood, a voice for the One.  
“Gather all, let the truth be known,  
The fire of God will prove His throne.”  
In the shadows, the prophets cried,  
But their cries fell silent, their gods denied.  
Yet with faith, he built the altar strong,  
Twelve stones of promise, where we belong.

Chorus  
Oh, hear us, Lord, we call Your name,  
In the midst of doubt, ignite the flame.  
Let the fire fall, let Your glory be,  
The God who answers, we will see!  
The God who answers, we will see!

Verse 2  
They shouted loud, from dawn till noon,  
But Baal was silent, a fading tune.  
Then Elijah prayed with a heart so bold,  
“Show them, Lord, let Your truth unfold.”  
He drenched the altar, a sacrifice prepared,  
With every drop, our hearts laid bare.  
“Let it be known, You’re the God of grace,  
Turn our hearts back, let us seek Your face.”

Chorus  
Oh, hear us, Lord, we call Your name,  
In the midst of doubt, ignite the flame.  
Let the fire fall, let Your glory be,  
The God who answers, we will see!  
The God who answers, we will see!

Bridge  
And when the fire fell, they knelt in awe,  
The God of Israel, we stand in awe.  
You are the light that breaks the night,  
In every battle, You are our fight.  
We lift our hands, we lift our voice,  
In the presence of the Lord, we rejoice.

Chorus

Outro  
So let the rain come, let it pour down,  
In the heart of the desert, Your grace abounds.  
With every heartbeat, our spirits rise,  
The God who answers, forever our prize.
