[TITLE]
A Heart for the Lord

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of kings, a tale unfolds,  
Abijam reigned, but his heart grew cold,  
Walking in shadows, the sins of his kin,  
Yet grace shone bright, for David’s heart within.  

Chorus:  
Oh Lord, our God, we seek Your face,  
In every battle, in every place.  
With hearts of worship, we lift Your name,  
For in Your mercy, our hope remains.  

Verse 2:  
Then came Asa, with courage so bold,  
He turned from the idols, let Your truth be told.  
With fire and passion, he sought the right way,  
Restoring Your temple, his heart would not sway.  

Chorus:  
Oh Lord, our God, we seek Your face,  
In every battle, in every place.  
With hearts of worship, we lift Your name,  
For in Your mercy, our hope remains.  

Bridge:  
Through every war, through every strife,  
You are our refuge, our source of life.  
With silver and gold, we bring our praise,  
For in Your presence, our hearts ablaze.  

Verse 3:  
Asa stood strong, though foes would arise,  
He called on You, lifting heavenward eyes.  
You heard his cry, brought victory near,  
In every struggle, Lord, You are here.  

Chorus:  
Oh Lord, our God, we seek Your face,  
In every battle, in every place.  
With hearts of worship, we lift Your name,  
For in Your mercy, our hope remains.  

Ending:  
So let our hearts be perfect like David’s song,  
In every moment, Lord, with You we belong.  
With faith as our banner, we rise and proclaim,  
In all of our battles, we praise Your great name.
