[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the glory of your reign, oh Solomon,  
With wisdom like the dawn, but a heart undone.  
You walked in paths of peace, yet turned away,  
Chased after strange loves that led you astray.  

Chorus  
Oh, guard my heart, Lord, keep it true,  
From the whispers of idols that pull me from You.  
In the shadow of your grace, let me find my way,  
A heart devoted, forever I’ll stay.  

Verse 2  
Seven hundred brides, and a thousand pleas,  
But their voices led you down to foreign seas.  
Ashtoreth and Chemosh, they stole your gaze,  
Your heart once pure now lost in a haze.  

Chorus  
Oh, guard my heart, Lord, keep it true,  
From the whispers of idols that pull me from You.  
In the shadow of your grace, let me find my way,  
A heart devoted, forever I’ll stay.  

Bridge  
But You, O Lord, were faithful still,  
Through shadows deep, You guide my will.  
In every choice, in every fight,  
I seek Your face, my guiding light.  

Verse 3  
You stirred up foes as a warning sign,  
Yet still, Your mercy calls me to align.  
For David’s sake, You held a tribe dear,  
A promise of love that casts out fear.  

Chorus  
Oh, guard my heart, Lord, keep it true,  
From the whispers of idols that pull me from You.  
In the shadow of your grace, let me find my way,  
A heart devoted, forever I’ll stay.  

Outro  
So let my heart be steadfast, my spirit soar,  
In the light of Your love, forever I’ll adore.  
With every breath, I’ll sing Your name,  
In the battle of faith, I’ll never be the same.
