[TITLE]
Forever in Your House

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
When Solomon built the sacred place,  
A house for You, O Lord, filled with grace.  
You came down as a flame that night,  
Your name hallowed, shining bright.  
You whispered to him, "I hear your call,  
This house I bless, I’ll be with you all."  

Chorus  
Forever in Your house, we will stand,  
In awe of Your mercy, in the palm of Your hand.  
With hearts aligned, we’ll walk Your way,  
O Lord, our God, hear us as we pray.  

Verse 2  
If we walk in truth, as David did,  
With hearts of integrity, our sins are hid.  
Your promise stands, a kingdom to reign,  
But turn from You, and we’ll bear the pain.  
Oh, let our eyes not stray from Your light,  
Keep us steadfast, Lord, through the night.  

Chorus  
Forever in Your house, we will stand,  
In awe of Your mercy, in the palm of Your hand.  
With hearts aligned, we’ll walk Your way,  
O Lord, our God, hear us as we pray.  

Bridge  
And when the world may ask, “Why this fate?”  
We’ll declare Your glory, Your love so great.  
For we forsook not, our God divine,  
You brought us forth, through Your sacred sign.  
So let our hearts echo with praise,  
In this holy place, we’ll lift Your name always.  

Chorus  
Forever in Your house, we will stand,  
In awe of Your mercy, in the palm of Your hand.  
With hearts aligned, we’ll walk Your way,  
O Lord, our God, hear us as we pray.  

Outro  
In every whisper, in every song,  
We’ll build Your kingdom, where we belong.  
Forever in Your house, we will sing,  
To You, our Savior, our God, our King.
