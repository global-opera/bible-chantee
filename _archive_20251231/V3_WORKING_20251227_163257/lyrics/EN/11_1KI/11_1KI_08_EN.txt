[TITLE]
In Your Holy Presence

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the heart of Zion, the elders came,  
Gathered 'round King Solomon, calling His name.  
The Ark of the Covenant, a promise so true,  
In the house of the Lord, we lift praise anew.  

(Chorus)  
O Lord, in Your holy presence, we stand,  
Your glory fills this place, our hearts in Your hand.  
From the depths of our worship, we rise and we sing,  
Forever we’ll honor, our Savior and King.  

(Verse 2)  
With sacrifices given, the multitude praised,  
A cloud of Your glory, in worship we stayed.  
Your mercy, O God, is steadfast and sure,  
You keep every promise, our hearts You restore.  

(Chorus)  
O Lord, in Your holy presence, we stand,  
Your glory fills this place, our hearts in Your hand.  
From the depths of our worship, we rise and we sing,  
Forever we’ll honor, our Savior and King.  

(Bridge)  
When we turn from our ways, and cry out Your name,  
You hear from the heavens, our hearts You reclaim.  
In battles and trials, we trust in Your grace,  
For You are our refuge, our hiding place.  

(Verse 3)  
Remember Your servants, Lord, hear our plea,  
As we walk in Your statutes, our hearts set free.  
With each generation, Your love will abide,  
In the midst of Your people, forever our guide.  

(Chorus)  
O Lord, in Your holy presence, we stand,  
Your glory fills this place, our hearts in Your hand.  
From the depths of our worship, we rise and we sing,  
Forever we’ll honor, our Savior and King.  

(Outro)  
Blessed be the Lord, who gives us His rest,  
In the arms of His mercy, we are truly blessed.  
With hearts intertwined, in Your love we remain,  
Forever united, we’ll sing out Your name.
