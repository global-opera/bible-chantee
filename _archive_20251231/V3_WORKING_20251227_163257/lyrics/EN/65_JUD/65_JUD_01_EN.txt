[TITLE]
Keep Us in Your Love

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
Servants called by grace divine,  
Sanctified, we seek to shine,  
In mercy, peace, and love we stand,  
Preserved in Christ, held by Your hand.  

Chorus  
Oh, keep us in Your love,  
As we contend for faith,  
With hearts united, we arise,  
In the light of Your embrace.  
Oh, keep us in Your love,  
With strength to persevere,  
Guided by the Holy Spirit,  
We draw ever near.  

Verse 2  
Though darkness seeks to lead astray,  
And mockers rise to seize the day,  
We remember words of old,  
In unity, Your truth we hold.
