Verse 1  
In the still of the night, the king could not sleep,  
He called for the scrolls, for the records to keep.  
Mordecai's name, in the pages was found,  
A guardian of truth, in the silence profound.  

Chorus  
Oh, lift up your voice, for the King shall declare,  
Honor and glory for those who declare,  
Faith in the shadows, God’s light will break through,  
His justice will reign, and His love will renew.  

Verse 2  
Haman stood waiting, his heart filled with pride,  
But the King sought the one whom the crown would abide.  
“What shall be done?” the King asked with delight,  
And Haman proclaimed, “Let him shine in the light!”

Bridge  
From the gates of despair, to the heights of His grace,  
Mordecai rides forth, in the King’s warm embrace.  
When battles arise, and the foe seems so near,  
Trust in His promise, for the King’s word is clear.
