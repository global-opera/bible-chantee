[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadow of despair,  
Enemies drew near, hearts filled with fear,  
But You turned the tide, O Lord,  
With courage and strength, we stand restored.  
Gathered in our cities, we will rise,  
With faith unyielding, reaching for the skies.  

Chorus:  
From sorrow to joy, we lift our voice,  
In the face of darkness, we rejoice.  
You’ve turned our mourning into praise,  
A day of feasting, we sing Your ways.  
In every battle, we find our song,  
You are our strength, to You we belong.  

Verse 2:  
Mordecai’s name echoed through the land,  
With wisdom and honor, You took a stand.  
Esther’s heart was bold, she took her place,  
In the face of peril, she sought Your grace.  
You turned the plot of the wicked one,  
Now we dance in freedom, our victory won.  

Chorus:

Bridge:  
Seventy-five thousand foes fell to the ground,  
By Your mighty hand, we were glory-bound.  
With every letter sent forth in love,  
You remind us, Lord, You reign above.  
Generations will remember this day,  
Your faithfulness, forever we will say.

Outro:  
So we gather together, hearts intertwined,  
In the joy of our Savior, His love defined.  
Forever we’ll celebrate, forever we’ll sing,  
For You have turned our mourning into everything.  
From sorrow to joy, we lift our voice,  
In Your presence, O Lord, we rejoice.  
Amen.
