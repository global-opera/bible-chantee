[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Seeds of Your Kingdom

[Verse 1]  
By the shores, You spoke in grace,  
Tales of soil and heart's embrace,  
A sower went to cast the seed,  
In every heart, Your Word we need.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let us hear, oh, let us see,  
The treasures found in You, in me.  
From stony ground to fertile land,  
Your kingdom grows, we take a stand.  

[Verse 2]  
Some seeds fell on the path of doubt,  
The fowls of night, they came about.  
In shallow hearts, the sun did blaze,  
But still, Your love, it calls and stays.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let us hear, oh, let us see,  
The treasures found in You, in me.  
From stony ground to fertile land,  
Your kingdom grows, we take a stand.  

[Bridge]  
Blessed are we who hear Your Word,  
In the quiet, our hearts stirred.  
You teach us all the mysteries,  
Your voice, a gentle melody.  

[Verse 3]  
Among thorns, the cares of life,  
Choking hope, and breeding strife.  
Yet in good soil, Your truth takes root,  
Bearing fruit, a harvest, absolute.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, let us hear, oh, let us see,  
The treasures found in You, in me.  
From stony ground to fertile land,  
Your kingdom grows, we take a stand.  

[Outro]  
So gather us, O Lord, we pray,  
In Your arms, forever stay.  
With every seed that we have sown,  
Your love, our hearts, forever known.  

[END]  
(Repeat Chorus as desired)
