[TITLE]
Awake to His Coming

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the night, the lamps are burning,  
Wise and foolish, hearts are yearning.  
Some have oil, their lights are bright,  
While others sleep, lost in the night.  
Oh, the cry rings out at midnight,  
“Behold the Bridegroom, come to light!”

Chorus  
Awake, awake, O hearts that slumber,  
Ready your lamps, let love encumber.  
For He comes, the King of Glory,  
In His arms, we'll tell the story.  
Faithful servants, rise and shine,  
In His presence, we will dine.

Verse 2  
With our talents, gifts bestowed,  
Let us share what we’ve been showed.  
Trading all for joy and grace,  
In the harvest, find our place.  
When He comes, He’ll ask our name,  
“Did you labor for My gain?”

Bridge  
When the nations gather near,  
He will separate the dear.  
Sheep on His right, goats on the left,  
Faithful hearts will find their rest.  
“Did you love? Did you care?  
In the least, My heart was there.”

Outro  
So let us stand with hope and grace,  
Awaiting Him, our saving place.  
With every breath, our hearts align,  
In the light of love divine.  
Awake, awake, O hearts that slumber,  
For soon we’ll see Him, our great wonder.
