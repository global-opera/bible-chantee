[TITLE]
Shake the Heavens

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the days of old, they saw Your glory,  
In the temple's light, the stories told,  
But now, O Lord, we stand in longing,  
For the greater joy, for the greater fold.  
Zerubbabel, be strong, take heart,  
For You’re with us still, Lord, never depart.  

Chorus:  
Shake the heavens, shake the earth,  
Bring Your glory, bring new birth.  
Fear not, my people, for I am here,  
In this place, I’ll bring my peace near.  

Verse 2:  
From the fields we’ve toiled, our hands are weary,  
Yet Your promise stands, You’ll bless our days.  
Consider now, from this day onward,  
Your Spirit moves, igniting praise.  
With every vine and fig tree’s yield,  
From this day forth, our hearts are healed.  

Chorus:  
Shake the heavens, shake the earth,  
Bring Your glory, bring new birth.  
Fear not, my people, for I am here,  
In this place, I’ll bring my peace near.  

Bridge:  
O Lord of hosts, we lift our cries,  
You’ll overthrow the kingdoms, we see the skies.  
Your signet chosen, a promise declared,  
In the shaking, Lord, we know You care.  

Chorus:  
Shake the heavens, shake the earth,  
Bring Your glory, bring new birth.  
Fear not, my people, for I am here,  
In this place, I’ll bring my peace near.  

Outro:  
In the quiet, we will stand,  
Trusting in Your mighty hand.  
From this day forth, Lord, we believe,  
In Your glory, we shall receive.
