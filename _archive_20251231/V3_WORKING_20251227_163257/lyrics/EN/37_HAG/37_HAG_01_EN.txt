[TITLE]
Consider Your Ways

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:
In the quiet of the morning, Lord, we seek Your face,  
Your voice breaks through the silence, calling us to grace.  
You’ve seen our hearts distracted, our homes built high,  
While Your house lies in ruins, we hear Your gentle sigh.

Chorus:  
Consider Your ways, O my people,  
Turn back to the heart of the Lord.  
We’ve sown in vain, but You’re calling,  
To build Your house, our true reward.  
With faith we rise, in unity,  
We’ll work for You, our hearts restored.

Verse 2:  
We’ve chased after the shadows, seeking what won’t last,  
But in Your arms, O Father, our future’s held fast.  
You’ve promised to be with us, to guide our every step,  
As we gather wood and build, we’ll find our hearts are blessed.
