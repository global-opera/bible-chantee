Verse 1  
In the wilderness, God spoke to me,  
“Send forth the men, let them see.  
A land of promise, flowing with grace,  
Canaan awaits, our destined place.”  
With courage rising, we set our eyes,  
On hills and valleys beneath the skies.  
The names of leaders, brave and true,  
We march together, our hearts renewed.

Chorus  
Oh, the land is good, it flows with milk and honey,  
Fruits of faith, abundant and sweet.  
Yet giants rise, their shadows loom before us,  
But in His strength, we will not retreat.  
Courage, O hearts, for the Lord is with us,  
His promise stands, never shall we fall.  
With every step, we claim our inheritance,  
We are His people, He hears our call.

Verse 2  
Through the mountains, we journeyed far,  
Brought back the fruit, a witness to our scar.  
Clusters of grapes, a taste of the land,  
Yet fear crept in, and they could not stand.  
“Look at the walls, they are tall and strong,  
The giants roam, and we feel so wrong.”  
But Caleb stood firm, his voice rang clear,  
“Let us go up! The victory is near!”
