[TITLE]
In the Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
When You spoke to Moses, calling forth the tribe,  
“Bring your hearts and offerings, let your faith abide.  
In the land I give you, where blessings shall flow,  
Raise a sweet aroma, let Your praises grow.  

Chorus:  
In the land of promise, we will lift our voice,  
With our hearts surrendered, in Your love rejoice.  
From the first of our harvest, to the last of our days,  
We’ll offer up our lives, in the sweetest praise.  

Verse 2:  
With each grain and drink offering, we humbly bring,  
A tenth of our labor, to honor You, our King.  
For the stranger among us, Your grace still applies,  
One law for all nations, beneath Your skies.  

Chorus:  
In the land of promise, we will lift our voice,  
With our hearts surrendered, in Your love rejoice.  
From the first of our harvest, to the last of our days,  
We’ll offer up our lives, in the sweetest praise.  

Bridge:  
If we stumble in darkness, by ignorance we stray,  
You call us to repentance, to turn and find the way.  
Atonement is our anchor, Your mercy is our song,  
In the shadow of Your presence, Lord, we shall belong.  

Chorus:  
In the land of promise, we will lift our voice,  
With our hearts surrendered, in Your love rejoice.  
From the first of our harvest, to the last of our days,  
We’ll offer up our lives, in the sweetest praise.  

Outro:  
So let us wear our fringes, a reminder in our sight,  
To keep Your commandments, to walk in holy light.  
For You are our God, who led us from despair,  
In the land of promise, we find our refuge there.
