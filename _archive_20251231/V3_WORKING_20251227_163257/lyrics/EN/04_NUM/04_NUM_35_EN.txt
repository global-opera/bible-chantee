[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the plains where Moses stood,  
The Lord spoke clear, His voice a flood,  
"Give the Levites their place to dwell,  
In cities of refuge, where grace compels.  
Forty-eight cities with suburbs wide,  
For the lost and weary, let them abide."

Chorus  
O Lord, You are our shelter,  
In the storms that rage within,  
A place of peace and mercy,  
Where new life can begin.  
In Your arms, we find our refuge,  
In Your love, we are set free,  
In the cities of refuge,  
You are our sanctuary.

Verse 2  
When shadows fall and darkness calls,  
A heart in need, Your grace enthralls,  
Six cities stand, a call to flee,  
For the broken, the lost, a path to be.  
From the avenger, they find their way,  
In the arms of mercy, where hope will stay.
