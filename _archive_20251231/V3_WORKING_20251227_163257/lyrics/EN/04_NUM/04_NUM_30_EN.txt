[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the hush of the night, we gather here,  
With promises whispered, we draw near.  
Father, You see every vow we make,  
In our hearts, our souls, for Your name's sake.  

Chorus  
We will not break our word, O Lord,  
In the silence, let our hearts be stored.  
Every promise, a sacred bond,  
In Your faithfulness, we respond.  

Verse 2  
As a daughter in her youth, she binds her soul,  
Under the watch of a father, her heart made whole.  
If his silence stands, then her vow shall hold,  
In the light of Your grace, her story unfolds.  

Chorus  
We will not break our word, O Lord,  
In the silence, let our hearts be stored.  
Every promise, a sacred bond,  
In Your faithfulness, we respond.  

Bridge  
And if a husband hears, yet holds his peace,  
Every vow made shall find its release.  
In love’s gentle strength, the spirit will grow,  
In the unity of hearts, Your blessings will flow.  

Verse 3  
For the widow and the lost, her heart shall stand,  
In the depths of her soul, You hold her hand.  
Every word that she speaks, a prayer to the skies,  
In the stillness of trust, her spirit will rise.  

Chorus  
We will not break our word, O Lord,  
In the silence, let our hearts be stored.  
Every promise, a sacred bond,  
In Your faithfulness, we respond.  

Outro  
So we come with our vows, in reverence we stand,  
Under covenant grace, forever we’re planned.  
Lord, keep our hearts true, let our voices raise,  
In the echoes of love, we give You our praise.
