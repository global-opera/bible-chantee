[TITLE]
The Land of Promise

[LYRICS]

(Verse 1)  
In the fields of Gilead, where the cattle roam,  
The children of Reuben sought a place called home.  
They came with hearts of longing, their voices bold,  
Moses, hear our story, let our dreams unfold.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, lead us, Lord, to the land of promise bright,  
Where Your grace and mercy shine, guiding us by night.  
We will stand in battle, our hearts forever true,  
For the gift of our inheritance, we claim it all in You.  

(Verse 2)  
“Shall your brothers go to war, and you stay behind?  
Discourage not the heart, leave no fear confined.  
The land is ours to conquer, as I spoke before,  
With faith we will advance, trusting in Your core.”  

(Chorus)  
Oh, lead us, Lord, to the land of promise bright,  
Where Your grace and mercy shine, guiding us by night.  
We will stand in battle, our hearts forever true,  
For the gift of our inheritance, we claim it all in You.  

(Bridge)  
We will build our cities, for our children’s peace,  
But armed we will march forth, our courage will not cease.  
With every step together, united we will stand,  
For the glory of Your kingdom, we enter this land.  

(Verse 3)  
As the tribes came together, in one heart, one soul,  
The call of the Almighty made their spirits whole.  
With shields and swords uplifted, they crossed the raging sea,  
For the land of promise waited, where His love sets free.  

(Chorus)  
Oh, lead us, Lord, to the land of promise bright,  
Where Your grace and mercy shine, guiding us by night.  
We will stand in battle, our hearts forever true,  
For the gift of our inheritance, we claim it all in You.  

(Outro)  
So we march in faith, with a promise in our heart,  
For the Lord who goes before us, will never let us part.  
In the land of Gilead, where our dreams are born anew,  
With every breath we take, Lord, we worship You.
