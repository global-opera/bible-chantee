[TITLE]
Lights of the Sanctuary

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the stillness of the night, O Lord, You spoke,  
To Moses and to Aaron, Your words evoke,  
"Light the lamps, let them shine, over all the land,  
With a golden candlestick, as I have planned."  

Chorus:  
Light of the sanctuary, shine within our hearts,  
Illuminate the darkness, where Your love imparts.  
With hands laid upon them, we gather in Your name,  
For the Levites are Yours, forever we proclaim.  

Verse 2:  
"Take the Levites, cleanse them, with purifying rain,  
Sprinkle water of mercy, wash away their stain.  
Bring forth the offerings, in humble sacrifice,  
Atonement for Your people, in Your grace, we rise."  

Chorus:  
Light of the sanctuary, shine within our hearts,  
Illuminate the darkness, where Your love imparts.  
With hands laid upon them, we gather in Your name,  
For the Levites are Yours, forever we proclaim.  

Bridge:  
From every firstborn, You called them to be,  
Set apart, consecrated, for all eternity.  
In the tabernacle, they serve with joy and fear,  
Bringing forth our offerings, drawing ever near.  

Chorus:  
Light of the sanctuary, shine within our hearts,  
Illuminate the darkness, where Your love imparts.  
With hands laid upon them, we gather in Your name,  
For the Levites are Yours, forever we proclaim.  

Outro:  
So let us walk in Your ways, O Lord, we plead,  
In the light of Your presence, our every need.  
With the Levites before You, we offer our praise,  
In the sanctuary's glow, we lift up our days.
