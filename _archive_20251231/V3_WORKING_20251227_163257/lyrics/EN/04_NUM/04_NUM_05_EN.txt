Verse 1  
In the desert, the Lord spoke to Moses,  
"Cast out the unclean, let them be free.  
For in the midst of My people,  
Holiness dwells, and they must see."  
With trembling hearts, they obeyed the command,  
For the Lord is a refuge, a guiding hand.  

Chorus  
Cleansed and redeemed, we lift our voices high,  
In Your presence, Lord, we find our reply.  
From ashes to beauty, You wash us anew,  
In Your mercy, O God, we are made true.  

Verse 2  
When sin creeps in, and shadows draw near,  
Confession brings light, dispelling our fear.  
With offerings of sorrow, we come to the throne,  
To recompense our trespasses, we’re never alone.  
The priest stands before us, interceding in grace,  
With every confession, we find our true place.

In the power of Your name, we are forever changed.
