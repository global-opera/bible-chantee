[TITLE]
Called to the Sanctuary

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadow of Sinai, where Your voice did resound,  
You spoke to Moses, Your promise profound.  
From the heart of the wilderness, a people You claim,  
The Levites, Your servants, in holy name.

[Chorus]  
We are called to the sanctuary,  
To lift our hearts in praise,  
With every breath, we honor You,  
In the sacred space, we stay.  
O Lord, we offer our lives today,  
In service, love, and grace,  
We are called to the sanctuary,  
In Your presence, we find our place.

[Verse 2]  
Aaron and his sons, anointed with care,  
To serve in Your presence, to stand in prayer.  
Though Nadab and Abihu fell in the light,  
Your mercy reminds us, in darkness, You guide.

[Chorus]

[Bridge]  
O take the firstborn, redeem them to You,  
In their place, the Levites, faithful and true.  
Each name and each family, woven in grace,  
In the tapestry of worship, we seek Your face.

[Outro]  
In the shadow of Sinai, where Your voice did resound,  
We are called to Your service, in glory, we are found.  
In the sanctuary’s embrace, we lay our burdens down,  
Forever in Your love, our hearts will wear the crown.
