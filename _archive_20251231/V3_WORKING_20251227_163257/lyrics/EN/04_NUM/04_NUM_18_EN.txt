### 
Verse 1  
In the shadow of the altar, where the incense fills the air,  
The Lord called unto Aaron, with a promise so rare.  
"Thou and thy sons shall bear the weight,  
Of the sanctuary’s iniquity, as I navigate."

Chorus  
O holy Lord, we stand in awe,  
Your presence guides us, forevermore.  
In every offering, in every prayer,  
We seek Your grace, we find You there.

Verse 2  
With the Levites gathered, united in their role,  
To keep the charge of worship, to minister to the soul.  
No stranger shall draw near, for sacred is this place,  
Through the blood of the Lamb, we find Your saving grace.

Chorus  
O holy Lord, we stand in awe,  
Your presence guides us, forevermore.  
In every offering, in every prayer,  
We seek Your grace, we find You there.

Bridge  
You are our inheritance, our portion, and our prize,  
In the land of promise, beneath the endless skies.  
We lift our gifts before You, as a sweet and fragrant sign,  
In the heart of Your sanctuary, our spirits intertwine.

Verse 3  
Every heave offering, from the best of our hands,  
We bring to You, Lord, as You’ve commanded in Your plans.  
For the tithes of Your people, we offer with delight,  
To build the house of worship, to shine Your holy light.

Chorus  
O holy Lord, we stand in awe,  
Your presence guides us, forevermore.  
In every offering, in every prayer,  
We seek Your grace, we find You there.

Outro  
So we gather in Your name, in unity we stand,  
With hearts prepared for worship, in the promise of Your hand.  
Your covenant of salt, forever we will keep,  
In the charge of the sanctuary, Your love runs deep.
