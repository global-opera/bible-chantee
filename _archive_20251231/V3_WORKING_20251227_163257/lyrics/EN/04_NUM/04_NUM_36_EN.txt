[TITLE]
Inheritance of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the land of promise, by Your word we stand,  
Chief fathers gathered, seeking Your command.  
The daughters of Zelophehad, their voices rise,  
Claiming their promise, with faith in their eyes.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, let our hearts be true,  
Inherit the grace that You’ve given anew.  
From tribe to tribe, let us hold fast,  
To the legacy of love that forever will last.

Verse 2  
Moses spoke the truth, as You guided his hand,  
“Let them marry in faith, united we’ll stand.”  
For every daughter, a purpose divine,  
In the family of their fathers, Your love we find.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, let our hearts be true,  
Inherit the grace that You’ve given anew.  
From tribe to tribe, let us hold fast,  
To the legacy of love that forever will last.

Bridge  
Even in the plains of Moab, near Jericho,  
Your commandments guide us, through valleys below.  
Let our inheritance, be rooted in You,  
For every promise fulfilled, our hearts will renew.

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, let our hearts be true,  
Inherit the grace that You’ve given anew.  
From tribe to tribe, let us hold fast,  
To the legacy of love that forever will last.

Outro  
In the story of our lives, we find our place,  
Holding tightly to the gift of Your grace.  
Inherit the promise, as we lift up our voice,  
Together in unity, we rejoice, we rejoice.
