[TITLE]
Heaven's Throne, Our Heart's Desire

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
From heaven’s throne, You call our name,  
The earth beneath, Your footstool, the same.  
Where can we build a house for You?  
For all we are, Lord, is made anew.  
Your hand has formed the skies above,  
Yet still, You seek the heart in love.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, we tremble at Your word,  
In our brokenness, we’ve heard.  
You lift the lowly, comfort the meek,  
In Your presence, Lord, it’s You we seek.  
Rejoice, rejoice, all who believe,  
In Your glory, our hearts receive.  

Verse 2  
You see the choices we have made,  
In our abominations, we’ve strayed.  
But when we call, You hear our cry,  
With arms of mercy, You draw nigh.  
Before the pain, You bring the birth,  
A nation born from the depths of earth.

Bridge  
As a mother comforts her child,  
So You gather us, tender and mild.  
Peace like a river, flowing free,  
Your love, O Lord, is all we need.  
From every nation, we will come,  
To declare Your glory, Your kingdom’s home.
