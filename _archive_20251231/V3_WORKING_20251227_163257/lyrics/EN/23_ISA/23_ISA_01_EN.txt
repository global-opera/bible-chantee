Verse 1  
O heavens, hear the cry,  
The earth, give heed to the call,  
Our hearts have wandered far away,  
In darkness, we’ve begun to fall.  
You’ve raised us as Your children,  
Yet we’ve turned our backs in shame,  
A sinful nation, laden deep,  
With iniquity, we bear the blame.

Chorus  
Come, let us reason together,  
Though our sins be red as the night,  
You will wash us, make us clean,  
In Your mercy, we find our light.  
O Lord, we seek Your redemption,  
In Your grace, our hearts will mend,  
Restore us, mighty One of Israel,  
Let Your righteousness ascend.

Verse 2  
Our cities lie in ruin,  
Strangers invade our sacred land,  
The daughter of Zion weeps alone,  
With no one left to take a stand.  
You’ve called us to seek justice,  
To care for the widow and the weak,  
Yet we’ve offered vain oblations,  
Hollow words, our hearts don’t speak.
