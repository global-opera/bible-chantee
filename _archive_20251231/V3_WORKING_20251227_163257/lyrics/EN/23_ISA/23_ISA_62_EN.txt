[TITLE]
City of Salvation

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
For Zion’s sake, I will not be still,  
In the silence, my heart finds its will.  
Righteousness shines, like the morning light,  
Salvation burns bright, dispelling the night.  

Chorus:  
O Jerusalem, arise and sing,  
The glory of the Lord, your heart shall bring.  
No longer forsaken, you’re called by His name,  
Hephzibah, beloved, forever the same.  

Verse 2:  
Watchmen stand guard, proclaiming His grace,  
Day and night, we seek His face.  
Gather the stones, prepare the way,  
The Lord has declared, “Salvation’s here to stay.”  

Chorus:  
O Jerusalem, arise and sing,  
The glory of the Lord, your heart shall bring.  
No longer forsaken, you’re called by His name,  
Hephzibah, beloved, forever the same.  

Bridge:  
Lift the standard high, let hope resound,  
In the courts of holiness, His praise is found.  
The harvest is plenty, the laborers rejoice,  
In the love of our Savior, we lift our voice.  

Chorus:  
O Jerusalem, arise and sing,  
The glory of the Lord, your heart shall bring.  
No longer forsaken, you’re called by His name,  
Hephzibah, beloved, forever the same.  

Outro:  
Behold, your salvation comes,  
A city not forsaken, the heart of the One.  
Redeemed and restored, forever we’ll reign,  
In the love of our Savior, we sing His name.
