[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of our fear,  
A prophet walks, he draws us near,  
With sackcloth torn, and feet laid bare,  
He shows the truth, the weight we bear.  
Oh, the cries of Egypt's shame,  
Their glory lost, no place to claim.  
With every step, he bears the sign,  
Of hope and grace, the Lord divine.  

Chorus:  
Naked and unashamed, we stand,  
Before Your throne, O mighty hand.  
In every trial, our hearts will seek,  
The strength to rise when we are weak.  
Our hope is in the One who saves,  
Through every storm, our soul He braves.  

Verse 2:  
The king of Assyria draws near,  
Yet in our hearts, we will not fear.  
For every chain, You break away,  
In darkest nights, You are our day.  
The captives cry, but we lift praise,  
To You, O Lord, our voices raise.  
With every loss, we find our gain,  
In You alone, we’ll never wane.

Bridge:  
Oh, hear the cry of the broken,  
In every heart, Your Word is spoken.  
We flee to You, our refuge and might,  
In the darkest hour, You are our light.  
Though shame may come, we’ll not despair,  
For in our weakness, You are there.

Outro:  
So let us walk, with heads held high,  
In faith we trust, our spirits nigh.  
With every step, we’ll lift Your name,  
Naked and unashamed, we’ll proclaim.
