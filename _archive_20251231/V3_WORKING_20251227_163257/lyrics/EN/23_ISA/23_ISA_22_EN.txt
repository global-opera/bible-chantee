[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the valley of vision, where shadows fall,  
A tumultuous city, once so proud, now small.  
The cries of the broken, the burden we bear,  
We weep for our people, in sorrow and despair.  

Chorus:  
Lord, hear our lament, as we lift our eyes,  
In the midst of our mourning, we seek Your skies.  
For the walls may be broken, but You are our strength,  
In the valley of vision, we trust You at length.  

Verse 2:  
You’ve called us to weeping, to mourning and pain,  
Yet joy fills the hearts, while we’re bound in chains.  
With chariots of darkness, they gather around,  
But Your promise still echoes, Your glory profound.

Bridge:  
You’ve chosen a servant, Eliakim stands tall,  
With the key of David, he’ll answer Your call.  
Though the nails may be fastened, and burdens weigh down,  
You are the foundation, our hope will abound.

Outro:  
In the valley of vision, we wait for Your light,

In the valley of vision, we’ll sing to the skies.
