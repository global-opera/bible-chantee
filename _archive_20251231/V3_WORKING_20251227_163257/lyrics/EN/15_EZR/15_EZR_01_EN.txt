[TITLE]

[LYRICS]
 Rise Up to Build

VERSE 1  
In the heart of a kingdom, a word was proclaimed,  
Cyrus stood bold, in the Lord’s holy name.  
“Go up to Jerusalem, let the faithful arise,  
Build a house for the Lord, lift your voices high.”  

CHORUS  
Rise up, rise up, all you hearts that are stirred,  
The Spirit of God calls us forth with His word.  
With silver and gold, with joy we will bring,  
Together we’ll build, for our God is the King.  

VERSE 2  
From the shadows of Babylon, hope takes its flight,  
Judah’s sons gathered, ignited by light.  
With courage and purpose, they answered the call,  
To restore what was broken, to build up the wall.  

CHORUS  
Rise up, rise up, all you hearts that are stirred,  
The Spirit of God calls us forth with His word.  
With silver and gold, with joy we will bring,  
Together we’ll build, for our God is the King.  

BRIDGE  
Oh, Lord of the heavens, Your promise stands true,  
In every provision, we see You renew.  
From the ashes of sorrow, Your glory will shine,  
As we gather together, our hearts intertwine.  

VERSE 3  
So let us be willing, let our hands be strong,  
In unity we rise, where our hearts belong.  
With vessels of honor, we’ll carry Your grace,  
Building up Your temple, we seek Your face.  

CHORUS  
Rise up, rise up, all you hearts that are stirred,  
The Spirit of God calls us forth with His word.  
With silver and gold, with joy we will bring,  
Together we’ll build, for our God is the King.  

OUTRO  
In the heart of Jerusalem, our praise will ascend,  
With each stone we lay, on Your love we depend.  
Oh, rise up, rise up, for the Lord is our guide,  
In the house of our God, forever we’ll abide.
