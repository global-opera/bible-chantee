[TITLE]  
"Ezra's Journey"

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Babylon, a heart set free,  
Ezra sought the law of God, with faith he would decree.  
From the king's own hand, a message came so clear,  
To gather all the faithful, to bring their offerings near.

[Chorus]  
Oh, the hand of the Lord is upon us,  
Guiding every step we take.  
With silver and gold for the altar,  
In His name, our hearts awake.  
We'll rise up to Jerusalem,  
With joy, we’ll sing His praise.  
For the God of our fathers leads us,  
In His love, we find our ways.

[Verse 2]  
Through the shadows of the night, he journeyed on,  
With priests and Levites, the faithful ones were drawn.  
In the fifth month of grace, they reached the holy place,  
To restore the house of worship, to seek His holy face.

In the light of His glory, we’ll stand as one,
