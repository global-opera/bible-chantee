[LYRICS]
[TITLE] Return to the Promised Land

[Verse 1]  
From the ashes of our sorrow,  
We arise, we rise anew,  
Led by hearts of faith and courage,  
To the land that You once drew.  
Zerubbabel, he leads the way,  
With a promise shining bright,  
Through the shadows, through the night,  
We reclaim what’s ours by right.

[Chorus]  
We are the children of the promise,  
Gathered in Your name,  
In the city of our worship,  
We will lift our praise,  
For You brought us from the darkness,  
Into Your glorious light,  
Together we will stand,  
In the love that shines so bright.

[Verse 2]  
From the lands of our captivity,  
We are called to rise again,  
Every tribe and every nation,  
Joined as one, forever friends.  
In the heart of Jerusalem,  
We will build our hope anew,  
With each stone, with each prayer,  
We proclaim our faith in You.

[Chorus]  
We are the children of the promise,  
Gathered in Your name,  
In the city of our worship,  
We will lift our praise,  
For You brought us from the darkness,  
Into Your glorious light,  
Together we will stand,  
In the love that shines so bright.

[Bridge]  
Oh, let the trumpets sound,  
Let the banners wave on high,  
In the midst of our rejoicing,  
We will never say goodbye.  
For the past is in Your hands,  
And the future’s in Your plan,  
With our hearts aligned in worship,  
We will rise and take our stand.

[Chorus]  
We are the children of the promise,  
Gathered in Your name,  
In the city of our worship,  
We will lift our praise,

Together we will stand,

[Outro]  
Together we will stand,  
In the love that shines so bright,  
As we journey back to You, Lord,  
In our hearts, forever right.
