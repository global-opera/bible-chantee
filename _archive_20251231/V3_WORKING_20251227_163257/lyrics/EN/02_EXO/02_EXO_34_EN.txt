[TITLE]
Tablets of Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the morning light, I rise to seek Your face,  
On this holy mount, I come to find my place.  
You called me forth, as I hew these stones anew,  
Your words of life, O Lord, I long to hear from You.

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are merciful, Your grace abounds in me,  
In the shadow of Your glory, I find my victory.  
With every breath, I worship, in the stillness, I will wait,  
For You are the covenant, my heart’s eternal fate.

Verse 2:  
You stood before me, in the cloud of Your embrace,  
Proclaiming all Your love, and the wonders of Your grace.  
Forgiving all my sins, though I am stiff-necked and blind,  
You call me to Your heart, where mercy I shall find.

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are merciful, Your grace abounds in me,  
In the shadow of Your glory, I find my victory.  
With every breath, I worship, in the stillness, I will wait,  
For You are the covenant, my heart’s eternal fate.

Bridge:  
You drive out every fear, as I walk in Your light,  
I’ll not bow to false gods, for You are my delight.  
With my hands raised high, I surrender all I am,  
In this sacred moment, I will trust Your perfect plan.

Verse 3:  
Forty days and nights, with no bread to sustain,  
Yet in Your holy presence, I am forever changed.  
Moses’ face was shining, reflecting all Your grace,  
In Your love and mercy, I find my resting place.

Chorus:  
O Lord, You are merciful, Your grace abounds in me,  
In the shadow of Your glory, I find my victory.  
With every breath, I worship, in the stillness, I will wait,  
For You are the covenant, my heart’s eternal fate.

Outro:  
So I’ll stand upon this mountain, with my heart in Your hands,  
Declaring Your great love, as I obey Your commands.  
For You are my salvation, my future and my song,  
In the tablets of Your grace, forever I belong.
