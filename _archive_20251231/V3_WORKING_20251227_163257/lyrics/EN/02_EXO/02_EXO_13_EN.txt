[TITLE]
By Strength of Hand

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the shadows of the night,  
Pharaoh's heart was hard as stone,  
But the Lord with mighty might,  
Brought His people to His own.  
From the house of bondage set free,  
In a land of promise, flowing like the sea,  
Remember this day, lift your voice in praise,  
For the Lord has shown His ways.  

[Chorus]  
By strength of hand, He leads us through,  
From the chains of sin, He makes all things new.  
In the wilderness, His light will shine,  
With every step, we are His design.  
Oh, remember, remember, the promise He made,  
In His love and mercy, we’re never afraid.  

[Verse 2]  
Seven days we’ll break the bread,

All that’s first belongs to You.  
Raise a banner of His grace,  
For the Lamb was slain to take our place,  
In the night, His light will guide,  
With every breath, our hearts abide.
