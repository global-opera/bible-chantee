[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
In the land of Egypt, a Word was declared,  
A month of beginnings, the promise we shared.  
Take for your families, a lamb pure and bright,  
Without blemish, our hope in the night.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the blood on the doorposts, a sign of our grace,  
When He sees the sacrifice, we find our place.  
In the shadows of judgment, we stand secure,  
The Passover Lamb, our hearts He’ll ensure.  

[Verse 2]  
With bitter herbs and unleavened bread,  
We remember the night when the firstborn fled.  
Girded and ready, we eat in haste,  
For in His protection, we find our rest and grace.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the blood on the doorposts, a sign of our grace,  
When He sees the sacrifice, we find our place.  
In the shadows of judgment, we stand secure,  
The Passover Lamb, our hearts He’ll ensure.  

[Bridge]  
Generations will gather, their stories will tell,  
Of the night of deliverance, where we were compelled.  
From bondage to freedom, He brought us through,  
In the feast of remembrance, we worship anew.  

[Verse 3]  
Now we walk in promise, our journey unfolds,  
With faith as our anchor, His love we behold.  
For the Lamb that was slain, now lives in our hearts,  
A covenant written, where mercy imparts.  

[Chorus]  
Oh, the blood on the doorposts, a sign of our grace,  
When He sees the sacrifice, we find our place.  
In the shadows of judgment, we stand secure,  
The Passover Lamb, our hearts He’ll ensure.  

[Outro]  
So let us remember the price that He paid,  
For the Lamb of salvation, our debts He has laid.  
With joy in our hearts, let us sing His refrain,  
In the promise of freedom, forever we’ll reign.
