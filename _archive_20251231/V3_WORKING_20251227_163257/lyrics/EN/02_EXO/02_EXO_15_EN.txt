[TITLE]

[LYRICS]

[Verse 1]  
I will sing unto the Lord,  
For He has triumphed gloriously,  
The horse and rider thrown in the sea,  
The depths have covered them, lost in history.  
His right hand, O Lord, so glorious in might,  
Dashing the enemy, turning darkness to light.  

[Chorus]  
Who is like You, O Lord, among the gods?  
Fearful in praises, doing wonders,  
You led forth Your people, redeemed with love,  
In the shadow of Your wings, we rise above.  
The Lord shall reign forever and ever,  
In the mountain of inheritance, we gather.  

[Verse 2]  
In the wilderness, we wandered and cried,  
Bitter waters of Marah, our hopes denied.  
But You showed us the tree, with Your healing grace,  
Turning sorrow to joy, in this holy place.  
You guide us with mercy, through trials we face,  
In Your strength we find refuge, our resting place.

[Bridge]  
Miriam sang with her timbrel in hand,  
All the women rejoiced, united we stand.  
“Sing ye to the Lord, for He has won the fight,  
The horse and rider cast into the night.”  
Fear and dread fall upon the land,  
But we walk on dry ground, held by His hand.

[Outro]  
Forever and ever, O Lord, we declare,  
In Your holy sanctuary, we find our prayer.  
With every breath, let our hearts proclaim,  
The triumph of the Redeemed, forever Your name.
