[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of the mountains, where Your glory shines,  
You called us forth to gather, among the sacred signs.  
Fine linen woven gently, in colors rich and bright,  
With cherubim in splendor, reflecting heaven's light.  

Chorus  
O Lord, in the tabernacle, Your presence we will seek,  
In the whispers of Your mercy, we find our hearts so meek.  
With every stitch and every seam, Your covenant we claim,  
In the beauty of Your promise, forever we’ll proclaim.  

Verse 2  
Fifty loops of gold entwined, together we are one,  
A tapestry of faithfulness, where every battle’s won.  
The curtains of the goats’ hair, a covering for the soul,  
In the depths of Your protection, we find our hope made whole.

Bridge  
Veil of blue and purple, the boundary of the grace,  
In the most holy presence, we behold Your face.  
With the mercy seat before us, our prayers rise like the dawn,  
In this sacred dwelling, our hearts forever drawn.

Outro  
So we gather in Your spirit, as one in love we stand,  
A holy habitation, united in Your hand.  
In the shadow of Your wings, we find our resting place,  
In the heart of the tabernacle, we embrace Your grace.
