[TITLE]
Signs of His Faithfulness

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the quiet of the night,  
Moses heard Your call,  
"Who am I to lead Your people,  
When they doubt and stand so tall?"  
With a rod in hand, a simple sign,  
You showed him wonders, divine.  
From serpent's bite to healing grace,  
In Your presence, he found his place.

Chorus  
You are the God of Abraham,  
The One who calls us by name.  
In our weakness, You are strong,  
Lead us, Lord, through every storm.  
With signs and wonders, we will see,  
Your faithfulness, our victory.  
We trust in You, our hearts will sing,  
For You are God, our everything.

Verse 2  
"Put your hand within your bosom,  
Bring forth life from death," You said.  
In the leprous flesh, he trembled,  
Yet Your words, they brought him stead.  
“Who made the mouth of man to speak?  
I will guide you, so don’t be weak.”  
Through Aaron’s voice, Your truth declared,  
With every step, Your love was shared.

Bridge  
In the land of Egypt, chains will break,  
Your heart for freedom, we will take.  
With every sign, the world will know,  
Your love is mighty, Your power flows.  
In every shadow, in every doubt,  
You are our promise, we will shout.

So we bow our heads in worship,  
For the One who sets us free,
