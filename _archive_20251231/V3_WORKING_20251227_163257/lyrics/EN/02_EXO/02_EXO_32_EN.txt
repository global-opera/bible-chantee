[TITLE]
In the Shadow of the Calf

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
On the mountain high, where the glory shone,  
Moses sought Your face, Lord, all alone.  
But the people cried, their hearts led astray,  
Fashioning their gods, they turned from Your way.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cries, as we wander and roam,  
In our darkest nights, we long for our home.  
Forgive us our sins, as we seek Your embrace,  
Lead us back to You, Lord, in Your saving grace.  

Verse 2  
Aaron took the gold, and the calf was made,  
In the heart of the camp, their faith began to fade.  
They danced and they played, in the shadows of shame,  
Yet Your love remained, calling out our name.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cries, as we wander and roam,  
In our darkest nights, we long for our home.  
Forgive us our sins, as we seek Your embrace,  
Lead us back to You, Lord, in Your saving grace.  

Bridge  
Moses cried out, "Lord, remember Your own,  
For the sake of Your promise, let mercy be shown."  
In the face of our sin, Your compassion poured out,  
You turned from Your wrath, and we lift up our shout.  

Verse 3  
In the valley below, where the shadows fall,  
Your people stood lost, yet You heard their call.  
"Who is on the Lord's side?" rang out through the night,  
In the midst of our failures, You shine as our light.  

Chorus  
Oh, Lord, hear our cries, as we wander and roam,  
In our darkest nights, we long for our home.  
Forgive us our sins, as we seek Your embrace,  
Lead us back to You, Lord, in Your saving grace.  

Outro  
So we gather in faith, with hearts open wide,  
In the shadow of the calf, we seek to abide.  
Forever we’ll praise, as we walk in Your ways,  
In the love of our Savior, our voices will raise.
