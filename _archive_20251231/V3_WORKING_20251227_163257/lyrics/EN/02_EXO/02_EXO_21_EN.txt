[TITLE]
Servants of the Covenant

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the shadows of the law, we find our way,  
A servant bound for six years, then to break the chain,  
If he came alone, he walks free,  
But if love holds him close, he stays for eternity.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of mercy, guide our hearts,  
In the echoes of Your judgment, let love impart,  
We stand in the light of covenant grace,  
With every choice, we seek Your face.  

Verse 2:  
A daughter sold, her future in the hand,  
Yet You call for justice, a loving command,  
If her heart's not cherished, let her be redeemed,  
In the eyes of the Father, her worth is esteemed.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of mercy, guide our hearts,  
In the echoes of Your judgment, let love impart,  
We stand in the light of covenant grace,  
With every choice, we seek Your face.  

Bridge:  
Life for life, and eye for eye,  
In the weight of our choices, we learn to rely,  
On the wisdom of the ages, Your truth stands tall,  
In every encounter, we’ll heed Your call.  

Verse 3:  
If the ox stirs the waters, let the owner know,  
For the path of our actions, in Your light, we grow,  
In the tales of our struggle, let justice prevail,  
For in love’s true reflection, we will never fail.  

Chorus:  
Oh, Lord of mercy, guide our hearts,  
In the echoes of Your judgment, let love impart,  
We stand in the light of covenant grace,  
With every choice, we seek Your face.  

Outro:  
So we gather in Your presence, our hearts entwined,  
In the story of Your mercy, our lives aligned,  
With each step in the journey, we find our way,  
As servants of the covenant, forever we’ll stay.
