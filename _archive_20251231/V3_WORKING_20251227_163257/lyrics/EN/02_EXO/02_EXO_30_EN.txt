[TITLE]
The Altar of Incense

[LYRICS]

Verse 1  
In the shadow of Your glory,  
Where the mercy seat resides,  
We gather, hearts laid open,  
Before the veil, our prayers arise.  
Crafted from the wood of shittim,  
Golden crowns adorned with grace,  
An altar made for sweet communion,  
In this sacred, holy place.  

Chorus  
Lift our incense to the heavens,  
As a fragrant offering,  
In the stillness, hear us calling,  
Lord, we bow before our King.  
Let our hearts be pure and holy,  
As we seek Your face anew,  
In the presence of Your mercy,  
Lord, we worship, Lord, we worship You.  

Verse 2  
With our hands washed in the water,  
Cleansed by grace, we come to Thee,  
To offer up our lives as living,  
Sacrifices, Lord, set free.  
Each breath a prayer, each thought a longing,  
For Your presence, we desire,  
In the depths of every moment,  
Light our hearts, ignite the fire.

Bridge  
Anoint us with Your holy oil,  
Set apart for Your delight,  
We are vessels of Your promise,  
Shining through the darkest night.  
With every prayer and every tear,  
You draw near, You draw near,  
In the quiet, we remember,  
You are here, Lord, You are here.
