### Title: "A Cry in Egypt"

Verse 1  
In the silence of the night,  
The Lord spoke with a mighty hand,  
“Yet one more plague shall come,  
And freedom shall fill this land.”  
To the people, He gave favor,  
Jewels of gold and silver bright,  
In the shadows of their sorrow,  
Hope would rise, breaking the night.

Chorus  
Oh, hear the cry in Egypt,  
A sound like none before,  
But for His chosen people,  
He stands and opens doors.  
No fear shall touch the children,  
For His promise guides our way,  
In the midst of darkness,  
His light will lead us today.

Verse 2  
Moses stood before the throne,  
With a heart of righteous flame,  
“Against the firstborn of the land,  
The Lord shall pass His name.”  
A great cry will fill the heavens,  
Yet His hand will shield us near,  
For He puts a bold distinction,  
In His love, we have no fear.

Chorus  
Oh, hear the cry in Egypt,

Bridge  
Pharaoh’s heart may be hardened,  
Yet our God is on the throne,  
He will part the waves before us,  
And His glory shall be known.  
Every chain shall be broken,  
Every bondage laid to rest,  
For the Lord will fight our battles,  
In His love, we are blessed.

Outro  
In the night, a dawn is breaking,  
With a hope that will not cease,  
For the Lord, He is our Savior,  
In His arms, we find our peace.  
A cry in Egypt, a song of grace,  
In every heart, His love we trace.  
With every step, we walk in faith,  
Our God delivers, our souls embrace.
