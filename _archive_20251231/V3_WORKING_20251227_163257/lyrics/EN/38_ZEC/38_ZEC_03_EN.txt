[TITLE]
Clothed in Grace

[LYRICS]

Verse 1:  
In the presence of the Lord, I stand,  
Filthy rags upon my weary frame,  
But the angel speaks with a gentle hand,  
“Take away the shame, call my name.”  
With a heart laid bare, I come to Thee,  
The accuser whispers, yet I am free.  

Chorus:  
O Lord, rebuke the foe,  
You’ve chosen me, I know,  
A brand plucked from the fire,  
In Your love, I will aspire.  
You’ve removed my iniquity,  
Clothed me in Your purity,  
In the vine and under the tree,  
I will dwell in grace, I will be free.  

Verse 2:  
Look upon me, Lord, I am Yours,  
With a crown of mercy, my heart restores.  
You’ve engraved my name upon the stone,  
In Your presence, I am never alone.  
Let the garments fall, let the chains be gone,  
In the light of Your truth, I rise up strong.

Bridge:  
In that day, oh Lord, we shall see,  
Every neighbor, together, in unity.  
Your servant, the Branch, shall rise and reign,  
Removing iniquity, breaking every chain.  
Here in Your courts, I will walk by faith,  
In Your presence, I find my place.
