Privacy Policy

Sung Bible (Bible Chantee) is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application.

Data Collection

Sung Bible does not collect any personal data. We do not request or store:

  • Your name or email address
  • Your location
  • Your contacts
  • Your payment information (payment is handled by Google Play)
  • Any other personal information

Locally Stored Data

The application may store locally on your device:

  • Your favorite chapters
  • Your language preferences
  • Your listening progress

This data remains only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following services:

  • Cloudflare R2: to host audio files
  • Netlify: to host the website

These services may collect anonymous technical data (IP address, browser type) in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Cookies

The application does not use tracking cookies. Only essential technical cookies may be used for the proper functioning of the site.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your information to third parties. No data is shared with advertisers or business partners.

Security

Although we do not collect personal data, we take security seriously. Our site uses HTTPS protocol to secure all communications.

Children

Sung Bible is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new update date.

Contact

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, contact us at:
